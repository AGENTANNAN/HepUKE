On ψ(3770), study e+e− → e+e− η′ via single-tag two-photon fusion, with η′ → π+π−γ (Mode I) and η′ → π+π−η, η → γγ (Mode II). Use the 20.3 fb−1 real data at √s = 3.773 GeV and the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two η′ decay modes. Event selection requires exactly three charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge ±1; photons must have >25 MeV (barrel) or >50 MeV (endcap) energy, shower time in [0,700] ns, and be more than 10° from the nearest charged track, with at least one good photon in Mode I and at least two in Mode II. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, high-momentum tracks (p>0.5) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.8), π/K separation, requiring one π+ and one π−, while the remaining track is the tagged e±. Mode I performs a 1C kinematic fit to γ π+π− e_tag e_miss, treating the untagged lepton as missing with electron mass and applying a loose χ²<200 in BOSS (tight χ²_1C<70 in ROOT), and in ROOT requires cosθ_miss>0.99 for the quasi-real missing photon. Mode II first does a Kalman fit of γγ to the η mass, then a 2C fit to γγ π+π− e_tag e_miss with the M(γγ)=m_η constraint and the same missing-lepton treatment, applying loose χ²<200 (tight χ²_2C<200 in ROOT) and requiring at least one η candidate, with the same cosθ_miss>0.99 ROOT cut. Both modes share the same three-track, photon, and PID baseline.
