At the 22 BESIII scan energies from 2.000 to 3.080 GeV, study e+e− → ωη with ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ, η → γγ, and e+e− → ωπ0 with ω → π+π−π0 and both π0 → γγ, both giving π+π−4γ final states. Use the real data and inclusive MC at all 22 points; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes, both matched to the 2.125 GeV data set. Event selection requires exactly one positive and one negative charged track with |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm, |Vz|<10 cm, identified as pions by the probability method against kaon and proton hypotheses, plus at least four photons with E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) / E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), EMC time [0,700] ns, and photon-track angle >10°. For ωη, require one γγ pair within 20 MeV of the π0 mass and one γγ pair within 30 MeV of the η mass, each from a Kalman fit with χ²<25, choose the π0-η pairing minimizing the combined π0/η mass χ² and require it below the π0π0 and ηη alternatives, apply a veto χ²(π+π−4γ)<χ²(π+π−5γ) and |Eγ3−Eγ4|/pη<0.9 against ωπ0π0 and ωγISR, then perform a 4C fit to π+π−π0η with χ²<70, nominal. For ωπ0, require two γγ pairs each within 20 MeV of the π0 mass from Kalman fits with χ²<25, select the π0π0 pairing by smallest χ² and take the π0 whose π+π−π0 mass is closest to mω as the ω daughter, then perform a 4C fit to π+π−π0 with χ²<70, nominal. Both modes share the same charged-track, photon, and PID preselection.
