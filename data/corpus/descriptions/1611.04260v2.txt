At the ψ(3770) peak (√s = 3.773 GeV), measure the single-tag branching fractions of D+ → K_S0 K_S0 K+, D+ → K_S0 K_S0 π+, D0 → K_S0 K_S0 and D0 → K_S0 K_S0 K_S0, with K_S0 → π+π−, using 2.93 fb⁻¹ of real data and the corresponding inclusive MC, plus 300k-event exclusive MC for each of the four modes (Mode II generated as a mixture of 90% D+ → K_S0 K*(892)+, K*(892)+ → K_S0 π+, and 10% direct three-body phase space). Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with mode-dependent multiplicities: 3 positive and 2 negative tracks with net charge +1 for the two D+ modes, 2 positive and 2 negative with net charge 0 for D0 → K_S0 K_S0, and 3 positive and 3 negative with net charge 0 for D0 → K_S0 K_S0 K_S0. Particle ID uses the probability method (prob cut 0.001) with combined dE/dx and TOF confidence levels (CL_K vs CL_π): the prompt K+ in Mode I is identified against pions, the prompt π+ in Mode II against kaons, and the K_S0 daughters are used without PID in all modes. Each K_S0 is reconstructed from two oppositely charged tracks with daughter |Vz|<20 cm, constrained to a common vertex, requiring |M(π+π−) − M(K_S0)| < 12 MeV/c² and L/σ_L > 2, with the corresponding number of K_S0 candidates per mode. A 4C kinematic fit to the D candidate (K_S0 K_S0 K+, K_S0 K_S0 π+, K_S0 K_S0, or K_S0 K_S0 K_S0) is performed with χ² < 200. Mode-dependent ΔE windows (±3σ from data and MC fits, e.g. data (−17,+19) MeV for Mode I and MC (−16,+16) MeV) are applied, and when multiple candidates survive only the one with minimum |ΔE| is retained; D yields are later extracted from M_BC fits with 2D/3D K_S0 sideband subtraction (sideband 0.020 < |M(π+π−) − M(K_S0)| < 0.044 GeV/c², ARGUS endpoint 1.8865 GeV/c²).
