At the J/ψ, study J/ψ → γ η_c, η_c → Ξ0 Ξbar0, with Ξ0 → Λ π0 (and charge conjugate), Λ → p π−, π0 → γγ, giving a final state of five photons and four charged tracks. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and matching inclusive MC, plus 500k-event exclusive MC generated with the full decay chain (phase space for Ξ0/η_c and hyperonic weak decay for Λ). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, at least two positive and two negative tracks with total ≥4 and net charge 0; photons with EMC TDC 0–14, 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, at least five photons, each at least 10° from any charged track, and at least five isolated photons at least 20° from any primary track. PID by the probability method (prob > 0.001), identifying protons against kaons and pions and pions against kaons, with charged tracks above 0.247 GeV/c treated as protons and below as pions. Form π0 from γγ pairs constrained to the nominal π0 mass (χ² < 200, at least two π0, M(γγ) window 0.098–0.165 GeV/c²), reconstruct Λ → p π− and Λbar → pbar π+ by secondary-vertex fits minimizing the mass difference (χ² < 200, mass window 1.111–1.120 GeV/c²) and remove used particles, then perform a nominal 4C fit on γ Λ Λbar π0 π0 with χ² < 200, also storing the χ² of the competing 4C hypothesis without the radiative photon for background suppression; Ξ0/Ξbar0 candidates use the best Λπ0 pairing within 1.296–1.331 GeV/c², with helix-parameter correction applied to all charged tracks before the fit.
