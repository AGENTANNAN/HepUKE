At 4.600 GeV (567 pb⁻¹) measure the absolute branching fraction for inclusive semileptonic Λ_c⁺ → X e⁺ ν_e via a double-tag technique with anti-Λ_c⁻ hadronic tags. Use the 4.600 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive MC events for e⁺e⁻ → Λ_c⁺ anti-Λ_c⁻ with the same phase-space decay card to model the two tag modes. Reconstruct the tag anti-Λ_c⁻ through its charge-conjugated decays anti-Λ_c⁻ → p̄ K_S⁰ and anti-Λ_c⁻ → p̄ K⁺ π⁻, while the signal side requires at least one positron and a missing neutrino (massless) in the final state. Positron PID uses CL(e)>0.001, CL(e)/(CL(e)+CL(π)+CL(K)+CL(p))>0.8, and E_e/p_e>0.8, with hadron misidentification handled by a PID efficiency unfolding matrix later in ROOT. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the tag system, positron, and missing neutrino to the lab-frame center-of-mass energy, with χ²<200, and save the fitted missing mass, unfitted missing mass, and q² observables. No explicit charged-track |cosθ|, |Vz|, or |Vr| cuts are set in the DSL; tracking efficiency, low-momentum positron extrapolation below 200 MeV/c, and wrong-sign/secondary-positron subtraction are applied in the subsequent ROOT analysis.
