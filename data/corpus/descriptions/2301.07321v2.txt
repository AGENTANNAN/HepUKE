Study e+e− → D*0 D*− π+ using partial reconstruction with two tagging methods: the D0-tag reconstructs D*0 → D0π0 plus a bachelor π+ and leaves the D*− missing (D0 → K−π+, K−π+π0 or K−π+π+π−), while the D−-tag reconstructs D*− → D−π0 plus a bachelor π+ and leaves the D*0 missing (D− → K+π−π−), with π0 → γγ. Use real data and inclusive MC at 86 c.m. energy points from 4.189 to 4.951 GeV (37 XYZ plus 49 scan points, 17.9 fb−1 total), and generate 200k-event exclusive MC per energy point for e+e− → D*0D*−π+ with D*0 → D0π0, D*− → D−π0, D0 → K−π+, D− → K+π−π−, π0 → γγ. Select charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks and at least five tracks total; require at least two photons with E > 25 MeV barrel / > 50 MeV endcap, angle to nearest track > 10°, and EMC TDC 0–14; use probability-based PID with probability > 0.001 and K±/π± separation. Reconstruct π0 from γγ with a 1C Kalman fit to the nominal π0 mass, χ² < 25. Perform partial reconstruction with missing-D* recoil mass in 1.80–2.20 GeV, apply D0 windows K−π+ 1.835–1.887, K−π+π0 1.827–1.882, K−π+π+π− 1.855–1.874 GeV, D− window K+π−π− 1.856–1.883 GeV, D* windows M(D0π0) 2.004–2.009 GeV with P*(π0) outside 0.025–0.050 GeV and M(D−π0) 2.008–2.013 GeV with P*(π0) outside 0.030–0.055 GeV, and require M(π+D0) > 2.02 GeV. A 3C fit constrains the π0, D and D* masses with χ² < 50 and keeps the best combination; when both tags survive, retain the D0-tag.
