On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → K− Λ anti-Ξ+ and ψ(3686) → γ K− Λ anti-Ξ+, with Λ → p π−, anti-Λ → anti-p π+, and anti-Ξ+ → anti-Λ π+. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the two decay modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 mm (10 cm), Vr<10 mm (1 cm), at least three positive and three negative tracks, and net charge 0; for the radiative mode also select photons with TDC 0–14, angle to any track >10°, 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy thresholds, and at least one photon. Identify particles by the probability method with CL>0.001, separating π/K/p, requiring at least one proton and one anti-proton, keeping only the kaon with the highest PID CL (extra kaons treated as pions, likewise for protons), and requiring the K− to originate from the IP. Reconstruct Λ → p π−, anti-Λ → anti-p π+, and anti-Ξ+ → anti-Λ π+ through secondary vertex fits that minimize the mass difference, then perform a 4C kinematic fit: for K− Λ anti-Ξ+ with χ²<200 and Λ/anti-Λ mass windows 1.110–1.121 GeV/c²; for γ K− Λ anti-Ξ+ with χ²<100, the same Λ/anti-Λ windows, and anti-Ξ mass 1.315–1.330 GeV/c², selecting the photon with the smallest χ² if several are present. In the radiative mode, veto ψ(3686) → K− Σ0 anti-Ξ+ background by requiring M(γΛ)>1.21 GeV/c² on the 4C-corrected quantities at the ROOT stage.
