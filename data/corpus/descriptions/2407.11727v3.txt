On the ψ(4260) energy region, measure the leptonic decays D_s^+ → μ^+ν_μ and D_s^+ → τ^+ν_τ (with τ^+ → e^+ν_e ν̄_τ, μ^+ν_μ ν̄_τ, π^+ν̄_τ and ρ^+ν̄_τ, ρ^+ → π^+π^0) via a double-tag method in e^+e^- → D_s^{*+}D_s^{*-}, where the D_s^{*-} → γD_s^- tag is reconstructed in D_s^- → K^+K^-π^- and the signal D_s^+ recoils against it. Use 17 real-data samples spanning 4.237–4.699 GeV (10.64 fb^-1 in total) together with the matching inclusive MC at every energy point, and generate 1M-event exclusive MC at all points for each of the five signal modes. The tag side employs 13 D_s^- decay modes (K^+K^-π^-, K^+K^-π^-π^0, π^+π^-π^-, K_S^0K^-, K_S^0K^-π^0, K^+π^-π^-, K_S^0K_S^0π^-, K_S^0K^+π^-π^-, K_S^0K^-π^+π^-, π^+η, π^+η'π^+π^-, π^+η'ρ^0γ, π^+π^0η), with per-mode ΔE windows keeping only the candidate of smallest |ΔE| per mode and charge, single-tag yields obtained from an M_BC fit, no extra charged tracks beyond the signal track, and a K_S^0 peaking veto |M(π^+π^-) − m(K_S^0)| < 0.03 GeV/c² applied for the π^+π^-π^- and K^+π^+π^- tags. The signal side requires exactly one additional charged track (μ^+, e^+, or π^+), net charge +1, and a missing neutrino; muons are identified using MUC depth requirements (mu_a, with a mu_b cross-check), and the ρ mode additionally requires two photons with a minimum photon angle of 10° plus one π^+. Each mode is fitted with a four-momentum constraint and χ² < 200, with the γγ invariant mass in the ρ channel further constrained to the nominal π^0 mass, the four τ sub-modes being fitted simultaneously.
