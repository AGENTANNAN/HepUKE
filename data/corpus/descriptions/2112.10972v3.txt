At √s = 3.773 GeV on the ψ(3770), search for the baryon-number-violating decays D0 → pbar e+ and D0 → p e− with a double-tag method in which the anti-D0 is reconstructed in the hadronic modes K+π−, K+π−π0 and K+π−π+π−. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC together with a 600k-event exclusive MC sample of ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar. The signal side requires one antiproton plus one positron (Alg I) or one proton plus one electron (Alg II), with net charge zero. Electron identification combines dE/dx, TOF and EMC into confidence levels, requiring CLe > 0.001, CLe/(CLe+CLπ+CLK) > 0.8 and E/p > 0.85c, while the (anti)proton must satisfy CLp > 0.001 with CLp > CLK and CLp > CLπ. Final-state-radiation recovery adds EMC clusters within 10° of the positron to its four-momentum, and a missing-energy veto on U_miss outside (−0.15, 0.15) GeV suppresses D0 → K− e+ νe. A four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ² < 200 is applied, and the yield is counted in the M_BC^sig vs ΔE^sig signal region 1.860–1.872 GeV/c² and −0.028–0.018 GeV, with charge-conjugate channels implied throughout.
