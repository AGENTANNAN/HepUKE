At the ψ(2S) (3.686 GeV), study ψ(2S)→γχ_cJ/η_c(2S) with χ_cJ/η_c(2S)→p pbar, using the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, together with a 100k-event exclusive signal MC sample in which the ψ(2S) decays with equal 25% rates to γχ_c0, γχ_c1, γχ_c2, and γη_c(2S), each followed by p pbar. Event selection requires exactly one positive and one negative charged track with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm, and at least one photon with E>25 MeV in the barrel or E>50 MeV in the endcap within TDC 0–14. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions and requiring at least one proton and one antiproton. The photon must be isolated, with opening angle greater than 20° to the positive track and greater than 30° to the negative track. A 4C kinematic fit to γ p pbar is performed with a vertex fit on the two tracks, nominal flag, and χ²<60, while a competing 4C fit to p pbar is also stored; the downstream analysis requires χ²_4C(γ p pbar)<χ²_4C(p pbar) to suppress ψ(2S)→p pbar with a fake/FSR photon. No explicit invariant-mass window is imposed in the selection; a 3C fit with floating photon momentum, applied downstream, provides M_ppbar_3C for background separation. The same selection is applied to the real data, inclusive MC, and exclusive signal MC.
