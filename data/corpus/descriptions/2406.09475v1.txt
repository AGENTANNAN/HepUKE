On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → ω K+K-η with ω → π+π-π0, π0 → γγ and η → γγ, searching for X(1870) → K+K-η. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes, J/ψ → ω K+K-η phase space and J/ψ → ω X(1870), X(1870) → K+K-η, both with ω → π+π-π0, π0 → γγ, η → γγ, and simulating ω with a Dalitz distribution and X(1870) as a Breit-Wigner. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC timing 0–14, angle to nearest track >10°, at least four photons. PID is by the probability method (probability >0.001), with K/π separation against each other and protons, requiring 2 K and 2 π. Perform a 5C kinematic fit to K+K-π+π-4γ with four-momentum conservation plus one γγ constrained to the π0 mass, χ²<80, flagged nominal. After the fit, require |M(π+π-π0)−m(ω)|<0.02 GeV/c² and |M(γγ)−m(η)|<0.02 GeV/c², veto η' with |M(π+π-η)−m(η')|>0.025 GeV/c², then search for X(1870) in the M(K+K-η) distribution.
