On ψ(3770) at √s=3.773 GeV, measure the e+e− → D0D0bar and e+e− → D+D− cross sections using the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC. Generate 100k-event exclusive MC for D0D0bar (D0→K−π+, anti-D0→K+π−) and for D+D− (D+→K−π+π+, D−→K+π−π−, with π0→γγ and K_S0→π+π−). Tag one charmed particle with charm +1 and the other with charm −1; the D0 tag modes are K−π+, K−π+π0, K−π+π+π−, and the D+ tag modes are K−π+π+, K−π+π+π0, K_S0π+, K_S0π+π0, K_S0π+π+π−, K+K−π+, with no photons on the signal side. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<10 mm, Vz<100 mm; PID uses combined TOF and dE/dx information, taking the highest-probability particle hypothesis, K_S0 daughters are exempt from impact-parameter and PID requirements, and the D0→Kπ mode is subject to cosmic/QED vetoes using TOF, EMC, and muon-counter information. Photons must have E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.8) or E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.84<|cosθ|<0.92), EMC TDC in 0–700 ns, and no track association; π0→γγ candidates require mass 115–150 MeV, at least one barrel photon, and are refit with a π0 mass constraint, while K_S0→π+π− candidates require a secondary-vertex fit χ²<100 and mass 487–511 MeV. Tag candidates use mode-dependent asymmetric ΔE windows (±3σ about the mean, extended to −4σ on the low side for π0 modes) and MBC signal regions 1.858–1.874 GeV/c² for D0 and 1.8628–1.8788 GeV/c² for D+; double-tag events require opposite net charge, opposite charm, and no shared tracks. Perform a kinematic fit constraining four-momentum and both tag masses to the nominal D0 or D+ mass with χ²<200; cross sections are extracted from the double-tag/single-tag yield ratio with efficiency cancellation, including a −0.2% quantum-correlation correction for D0D0bar and MC-determined peaking-background subtraction.
