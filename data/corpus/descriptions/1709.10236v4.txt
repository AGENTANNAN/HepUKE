Study e+e− → Λ Λ̄ at √s = 2.2324 GeV (just above threshold), 2.400, 2.800 and 3.080 GeV to determine the Λ effective form factor. Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at all four energies; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for threshold mode I (Λ→pπ−, Λ̄→p̄π+) and mode II (Λ̄→n̄π0, π0→γγ), and a 200k-event ConExc signal sample at each of 2.400, 2.800 and 3.080 GeV (Λ→pπ−, Λ̄→p̄π+). At threshold mode I select exactly two opposite-charge tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge 0; identify both as pions by probability PID (prob>0.001, π/K/p separation), require one π+ and one π− and pion momenta in 0.08–0.11 GeV/c, reconstructing only the two pions with the Λ and Λ̄ untagged and no kinematic fit. At threshold mode II select at most one good track (same |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm) and at least three photons with E>25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) and no track within 10°; combine photons into a π0 with a 1C kinematic fit to the π0 mass, χ²<20, requiring at least one π0, and reconstruct only n̄π0 with the recoil Λ untagged. At the higher energies select two positive and two negative tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<30 cm, Vr<10 cm and net charge 0; identify one p+, one p−, one π+ and one π− (probability PID, prob>0.001, p/π/K separation), build Λ→pπ− and Λ̄→p̄π+ candidates by secondary vertex fits with a Λ mass window |M(pπ)−MΛ|<0.01 GeV/c², then perform a 4C kinematic fit to the initial e+e− four-momentum with χ²<200, require ΛΛ̄ opening angles above 170°, 176° and 178° at 2.400, 2.800 and 3.080 GeV, respectively, and count signal in 0.98 < M(ΛΛ̄)/√s < 1.02.
