On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → Λ Λ̄ ω, with Λ → p π−, Λ̄ → p̄ π+, ω → π+ π− π0, and π0 → γ γ. Use 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for ψ(3686) → Λ Λ̄ ω with the same sub-decays. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring at least six tracks, and PID by the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying protons against pions/kaons, pions against kaons/protons, and anti-protons against pions/kaons, assigning p, p̄, π+, π−. Photons must have E>25 MeV, EMC time 0–700 ns, and open by more than 10° from any charged track. Reconstruct Λ from pπ− and Λ̄ from p̄π+ via secondary-vertex fits with |M−M_Λ|<5 MeV, and π0 from γγ in the 115–150 MeV mass window. Perform a 5C kinematic fit constraining four-momentum conservation and the γγ invariant mass to the nominal π0 mass, with χ²<200, flagged nominal.
