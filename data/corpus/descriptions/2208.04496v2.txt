Search for a massless dark photon γ′ in Λ_c+ → p γ′ using a double-tag method, with the ST Λ_c− reconstructed in ten hadronic tag modes (pKπ, Λπ, Λπππ, and pKπππ families) and the DT signal side required to be a proton plus an invisible massless γ′. Use the seven c.m. energy points 4.600, 4.612, 4.628, 4.641, 4.661, 4.682, and 4.699 GeV (4.5 fb^−1 total) real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive MC events for Λ_c+ → p γ′. ST selection requires M_BC ∈ (2.275,2.310) GeV/c² with mode-dependent asymmetric ΔE requirements; ST yields are extracted from unbinned ML fits to M_BC (MC signal convolved with a Gaussian plus ARGUS background), summing to 105,244 ± 384. DT selection requires exactly one tight track recoiling against the ST Λ_c−, PID-identified as a proton, with no loose tracks, and vetoes extra showers by E_max < 0.3 GeV and E_sum < 0.5 GeV to suppress π0 background; the signal region is M²_rec(Λ_c− p) ∈ (0.0,0.1) GeV²/c⁴. Apply a 4C (four-momentum-constrained) kinematic fit with χ² < 200. The main backgrounds are peaking Λ_c+ → p K_L⁰ and Λ_c+Λ_c−; N_obs = 13, N_bkg = 14.6 ± 1.5, yielding B(Λ_c+ → p γ′) < 8.0×10^−5 at 90% CL.
