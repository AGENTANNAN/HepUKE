On the J/ψ at 3.097 GeV, search for the weak decays J/ψ → D_s^{(*)−} e+ ν_e + c.c., with D_s*− → D_s− γ and D_s− reconstructed in four modes: K+K−π−, K+K−π−π0, K_S0 K−, and K_S0 K+π−π− (K_S0→π+π−, π0→γγ). Use 2.25×10^8 J/ψ real data and matching inclusive MC, plus a 478 pb−1 ψ(4040) data sample at 4.009 GeV as a D_s-efficiency control sample that is not used in the signal selection. Generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the eight signal modes (D_s and D_s* channels × four D_s decays), using the dedicated weak-interaction c→s generator. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; four tracks (2 positive, 2 negative) for the K+K−π−, K+K−π−π0 and K_S0K− modes, and six tracks (3 positive, 3 negative) for K_S0K+π−π−; photons with E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) or E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), >20° from any track, EMC timing 0–700 ns, requiring at least 1 photon for the D_s* modes except ≥3 when a π0 is present, and ≥2 photons for the D_s K+K−π−π0 mode; extra neutral energy below 0.15–0.30 GeV depending on mode and channel. Identify exactly one positron by the probability method with P(e)>0.001, P(e)>P(K), P(e)>P(π), and 0.80<E/p<1.05, removing it from the charged list, then require kaons with P(K)>0.001, P(K)>P(π) and pions with P(π)>0.001, P(π)>P(K), with final charged multiplicities 1K+1K−1π−, 1K+1K−1π−, 1K−1π+1π−, or 1K+1π+3π− for the four modes respectively. Reconstruct K_S0 via a secondary vertex fit with decay length >2σ and 0.487<M(π+π−)<0.511 GeV/c², and π0 via a mass-constrained γγ fit with χ²<100 and 0.115<M(γγ)<0.150 GeV/c²; for D_s* combine the D_s with a photon and require 0.125<ΔM=m(D_sγ)−m(D_s)<0.150 GeV/c². Because the neutrino is undetected, no full 4C fit is applied; instead reconstruct the missing four-momentum, require |p_miss|>50 MeV, and use U_miss=E_miss−|p_miss| as the signal variable, with the D_s mass window at ±3σ and a simultaneous unbinned fit over the four D_s modes in −0.2<U_miss<0.2 GeV/c² performed offline.
