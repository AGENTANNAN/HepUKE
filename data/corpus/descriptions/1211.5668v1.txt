On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ X(1810) with X(1810) → ω φ, ω → π+π−π0, φ → K+K−, and π0 → γγ. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for γ ω φ with X(1810) phase space, ω Dalitz, φ VSS, and π0 → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy threshold, at least three photons, more than 10° from any charged track, and EMC TDC in 0–14. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying kaons against pions/protons and pions against kaons/protons, requiring exactly 1 K+, 1 K−, 1 π+, and 1 π−. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γγγ K+K− π+π− with χ² < 200 (a tighter χ² < 40 is applied later), plus two competing 4C fits to 2γ K+K− π+π− and 4γ K+K− π+π− without χ² cuts. Reconstruct π0 from the γγ pair closest to the nominal π0 mass, requiring |M(γγ)−Mπ0| < 20 MeV/c²; select ω and φ signal regions with |M(π+π−π0)−Mω| < 40 MeV/c² and |M(K+K−)−Mφ| < 15 MeV/c², and veto M(γπ+π−π0) < 1.0 GeV/c² to remove J/ψ → φη′ background.
