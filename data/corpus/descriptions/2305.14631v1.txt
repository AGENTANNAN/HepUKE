At the single 4.178 GeV energy point, using 3.19 fb−1 of real data and the corresponding inclusive MC, study e+e− → Ds*+Ds−, D*0 anti-D0, and D*+D− with partial reconstruction of only one D*(s) per event. Generate 500k-event exclusive MC samples for each mode: Ds*+ → γDs+, Ds+ → K_S0K+ (with Ds− → K+K−π− in the card but not reconstructed); D*0 → D0π0, D0 → K−π+, anti-D0 → K+π−; and D*+ → D+π0, D+ → K−π+π+, D− → K+π−π−, with K_S0 → π+π− and π0 → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; PID by the probability method with cut 0.001, kaon/pion separation, assigning K+, K−, π+, π−. Photons require energy >25 MeV and EMC time 0–700 ns. Reconstruct K_S0 from π+π− by a secondary-vertex fit with mass window 0.487–0.511 GeV and π0 from γγ with mass window 0.115–0.150 GeV. Partial reconstruction then applies ΔE and D(s)-mass cuts (Table 1), with signal in M(D(s)π0/γ) versus recoil-mass M(D(s)) bands, rejecting overlaps and keeping background below 8%. A kinematic fit constrains the D(s) mass and the D(s)π0(γ) recoil mass to their known values, with a χ² cut and updated four-momenta for the helicity analysis. This unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to θ0, θ1, φ1, m12 uses tag and recoil samples, tests J^P = 1−, 2+, 3−, subtracts background with inclusive MC, and normalizes with PHSP MC and efficiency, confirming J^P = 1− with >10σ significance.
