Study the single-tag decay Λ_c+ → n π+ η (with Λ π+ η and Σ0 π+ η also present) recoiling against a hadronically reconstructed anti-Λ_c−, using real data at 4.600–4.843 GeV (11 points) and matching inclusive MC for ten of those points; generate 500k-event exclusive signal MC per energy point with Λ_c+ → X π+ η (X = n, Λ, Σ0 in equal parts), anti-Λ_c− → anti-p K+ π−, and η → γγ / π+π−π0 / π0π0π0. The tag anti-Λ_c− is built from 11 hadronic modes, and two separate signal-side chains are used for η → γγ and η → π+π−π0. For η → γγ, require one positive signal track with loose |Vz|<20 cm and |cosθ|<0.93, net charge +1, two photons with minimum angle 10° and energy >0.025 GeV, and a missing neutron; perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation and γγ constrained to the η mass, χ²<200, require M(γγ) in [0.505,0.575] GeV/c² with a 1C η-mass fit χ²<20, and veto M_recoil(η) in (1.13,1.25) GeV/c² against Λ_c+ → Σ+η. For η → π+π−π0, require two π+ and one π− (net charge +1) plus two photons with the same photon criteria and a missing neutron; fit with four-momentum conservation, γγ constrained to π0, and π+π−π0 constrained to η, χ²<200, require M(γγ) in [0.115,0.150] GeV/c² with 1C χ²<200 and M(π+π−π0) in [0.505,0.575] GeV/c² with 1C χ²<20, and veto M_recoil(η) in (1.16,1.22) GeV/c² and M_recoil(π+π−π+) in (1.10,1.13) GeV/c². The missing mass M_miss = sqrt(E_miss² − |p_miss|²) is the extraction observable for the simultaneous n, Λ, Σ0 components, and a post-BOSS Particle Transformer ensemble of 50 networks further suppresses Λ_c+ and non-Λ_c+ backgrounds with score cuts below 0.05 and 0.1.
