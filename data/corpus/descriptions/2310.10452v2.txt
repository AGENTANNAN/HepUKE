Measure the e+e− → ηπ+π− cross section from √s = 2.000 to 3.080 GeV using 19 R-scan real-data points (2.000, 2.050, 2.100, 2.125, 2.150, 2.175, 2.200, 2.232, 2.309, 2.386, 2.396, 2.644, 2.646, 2.900, 2.950, 2.981, 3.000, 3.020, and 3.080 GeV), plus inclusive MC at 2.125, 2.396, and 2.900 GeV. Generate 100k-event exclusive ConExc MC for each scan point for e+e− → ηπ+π− with η → γγ (ConExc mode 37; vhdr → π+π−η PHSP). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge zero; photons with TDC 0–14, 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, at least two photons, each more than 10° from any charged track. Identify pions by the probability method with prob>0.001 against kaons and protons, requiring exactly one π+ and one π−; remove any charged pion with E/p≥0.8 as Bhabha background. Reconstruct η → γγ with a 1C fit constraining M(γγ) to the η nominal mass, χ²<25, requiring at least one η, then perform a 4C fit to π+π−η with a vertex constraint on the two charged tracks and a nominal four-momentum constraint, χ²<100. Apply an η mass window of [0.523, 0.573] GeV/c² with sidebands [0.478, 0.503] and [0.593, 0.618] for background estimation, and a photon helicity-frame cut |cosθγ|<0.95 on the corrected four-momenta from the nominal fit to suppress mis-combination; a partial-wave analysis extracts the intermediate contributions. Note that ConExc mode 37 covers √s 1.025–2.975 GeV, so the 3.000, 3.020, and 3.080 GeV points may lie beyond the table range.
