At √s = 3.773 GeV on the ψ(3770), measure the semileptonic decays D0 → π−π0 e+ νe and D+ → π−π+ e+ νe with a double-tag (hadronic tag plus missing neutrino) technique, using the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC and generating 200k exclusive-MC events for each signal mode. For D0 → π−π0 e+ νe, tag the opposite-charm anti-D0 (charm −1) via K+π−, K+π−π0, K+π−π0π0, K+π−π−π+, or K+π−π−π+π0, and require on the signal side two photons, one π−, one e+, net charge 0, and a missing νe, then reconstruct π0 from the two photons and perform a four-momentum-constrained fit with the γγ invariant mass additionally constrained to the nominal π0 mass and χ² < 200. For D+ → π−π+ e+ νe, tag D− (charm −1) via K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, KS0π−, KS0π−π0, KS0π−π+π−, or K+K−π−, and require one π−, one π+, one e+, net charge 1, and a missing νe, with a four-momentum-constrained fit and χ² < 200. The encoded extra criteria are an extra-photon veto of Eγ,max < 0.25 GeV, Bremsstrahlung/FSR recovery for the e+, a KS0 veto for π+π− masses within 70 MeV/c² of the KS0 in the D+ mode, and a |Umiss| < 0.06 GeV window for the PWA sample, where Umiss ≡ Emiss − |Pmiss|. No explicit |cosθ|, |Vz|, |Vr|, photon energy-threshold, or PID/π-K/lepton-separation cuts are present in this encoding; the signal particle content is fixed only by the multiplicities and net charges listed above. The simultaneous 5D amplitude analysis uses mππ, q², θe, θπ, and χ.
