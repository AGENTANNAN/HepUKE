On the ten XYZ energy points from 4.190 to 4.420 GeV, study e+e− → π0π0 J/ψ (Zc(3900)0), with J/ψ → e+e− / μ+μ− and π0 → γγ. Use the real data and inclusive MC at all ten points, and generate 200k-event exclusive signal MC at each energy point according to ψ(4260) → π0π0 J/ψ phase space with J/ψ → e+e−/μ+μ− (50/50) and π0 → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, angle to any track >5°, at least four photons; PID by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, high-momentum tracks (p>1.0) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6), electrons required to have E/p>0.7 and muons E/p<0.3 with at least one muon having hits in more than six MUC layers. Reconstruct π0 from photon pairs with a 1C Kalman fit to the nominal π0 mass (χ²<200), requiring at least two π0, a loose 100 < M(γγ) < 160 MeV/c² window (tightened to 120–150 MeV/c² to reject extra combinations), and selecting the π0π0 pair with the smallest χ²_1C+χ²_1C+χ²_4C that shares no photons. Then perform the nominal 4C kinematic fit to the π0π0 l+l− hypothesis, followed by a 7C fit adding the two π0 masses and the J/ψ mass, both with χ²<200, keeping 2.95 < M(l+l−) < 3.2 GeV/c², and apply a Bhabha/two-photon veto requiring the two-track opening angle <175° for any e+/e− with |cosθ|>0.5.
