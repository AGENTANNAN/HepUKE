At the seven c.m. energy points 4.600, 4.612, 4.628, 4.641, 4.661, 4.682 and 4.699 GeV, study single-tag Λc+ production from ψ(4260) → Λc+ Λc− with the Λc+ fully reconstructed in the reference mode Λc+ → Σ+ π+ π− and in the signal modes Λc+ → Σ+ K+K−, Σ+ φ (φ → K+K−), Σ+ K+ π− and Σ+ K+ π− π0, where Σ+ → p π0 and π0 → γγ. Use the real data and inclusive MC at 4.600 GeV, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC both for the Σ+ π+ π− reference and for the Σ+ K+K− signal mode. The signal side requires exactly two photons (the π0 daughters), one K+, one K− and one proton, with net charge +1. The intermediate resonances are selected with M(p π0) in [1.174, 1.200] GeV/c² for Σ+, M(γγ) in [0.115, 0.150] GeV/c² for π0, and vetoes on Λ (M(p π−) outside [1.11, 1.12] GeV/c²) and K_S0 (M(π+π−) outside [0.48, 0.52] GeV/c²). A kinematic fit is applied with four-momentum conservation, the γγ invariant mass constrained to the nominal π0 mass, and a χ² cut of 200. Mode-dependent ΔE windows (e.g. [−0.017, 0.008] GeV for Σ+ K+K−, [−0.040, 0.032] GeV for the reference) are used, keeping the candidate with the smallest |ΔE|, and signal yields are extracted from unbinned maximum-likelihood fits to M_BC with a Gaussian-convolved MC signal shape and an ARGUS background.
