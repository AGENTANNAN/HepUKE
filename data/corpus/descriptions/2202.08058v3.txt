On the ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ → γ Ξ Ξ̄ via partial reconstruction in two channels, Ξ⁻Ξ⁺ and Ξ⁰Ξ̄⁰. Use the 3.686 GeV real data (~448M events) together with its inclusive MC, plus a 200k-event exclusive signal MC of ψ(2S) → γχ_cJ, χ_cJ → Ξ⁻Ξ̄⁻, Ξ⁻ → Λπ⁻, Λ → pπ⁻ (hyperon weak decay), with Ξ⁰ → Λπ⁰, π⁰ → γγ used for the neutral mode. For Ξ⁻Ξ⁺, require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and at least two positive and two negative; photons above 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap), at least one, with ≥10° separation from tracks and TDC 0–14; proton identification by the probability method (prob>0.001, p vs K/π) requiring ≥1 proton, which is then removed from the positive-track list, with the remaining positives/negatives assigned as π⁺/π⁻; reconstruct Λ → pπ⁻ and Ξ⁻ → Λπ⁻ by secondary-vertex fits, then partial-reconstruct γ Ξ⁻ choosing the best Ξ mass (1.32171 GeV) and requiring the recoil mass against γΞ⁻ within 1.2717–1.3717 GeV (m_Ξ ± 50 MeV), inferring the Ξ⁺ from recoil. For Ξ⁰Ξ̄⁰, require ≥1 positive and ≥1 negative track and ≥3 photons, apply the same proton PID and Λ → pπ⁻ vertex fit, then a π⁰ → γγ Kalman fit constraining the two photons to the nominal π⁰ mass with χ²<25 and ≥1 π⁰; partial-reconstruct γ plus Ξ⁰ (Λπ⁰), best combination by Ξ⁰ mass (1.31486 GeV) with recoil mass within 1.2899–1.3399 GeV (m_Ξ⁰ ± 25 MeV). In the downstream ROOT stage, apply Λ and Ξ mass windows (|M_pπ⁻−m_Λ|<5 MeV, |M_Λπ−−m_Ξ⁻|<10 MeV, |M_Λπ⁰−m_Ξ⁰|<10 MeV after a χ²<20 1C fit), select the radiative photon minimizing |M_recoil(γΞ)−m_Ξ|, and identify χ_cJ from M_recoil(γ) in 3.3–3.6 GeV.
