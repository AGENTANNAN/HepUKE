On ψ(3686), study inclusive K_S0 production, K_S0 → π+π−, using real data and inclusive MC at the ψ(3686) energy point (dataset 709_3686) and fitting 22 energy points from 3.640–3.701 GeV; also generate 1,000,000 exclusive MC events for ψ(4260) → K_S0 X, K_S0 → π+π− in phase space. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<10 cm, and at least three good tracks; pions are identified by the probability method (prob>0.001) against kaons and protons, with one positive and one negative pion assigned, and pion candidates must satisfy E/p<0.9 to reject electrons. Reconstruct K_S0 from the π+π− pair with zero total charge via a secondary vertex fit, choosing the candidate that minimizes the mass difference and has the longest decay length (L>0.4 cm), and require at least one additional good track with R_xy<1 cm and R_z<10 cm. Perform a 4C kinematic fit on the K_S0 with a nominal four-momentum constraint and χ²<200, using the K_S0 mass signal window |M(π+π−)−M_K_S0|<11 MeV/c² and sidebands 21–42 MeV/c² for background estimation.
