Measure the center-of-mass energy at 16 scan points from 4130 to 4440 MeV through e+e− → μ+μ−, using the real data and inclusive MC available at each point. For every energy point, generate a 1-million-event exclusive MC sample of the di-muon final state, with a ψ(4260) → μ+μ− phase-space decay card. Event selection requires exactly one positive and one negative charged track, net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm. Muon identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut and pion rejection, treating high-momentum tracks above 1.0 GeV/c as leptons (electron if the EMC energy exceeds 0.6 GeV), and requires one μ+ and one μ−. A nominal four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit to the μ+μ− pair is applied with χ²<200. The μ+μ− mass peak is then fitted with a Gaussian in the (−1σ, +1.5σ) window, with ISR/FSR mass-shift and momentum-calibration corrections, the latter derived from ISR J/ψ→μ+μ− in the same data.
