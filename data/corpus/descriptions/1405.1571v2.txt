Study ψ(3686) → ω K+ K− with ω → π+ π− π0 and π0 → γγ, using the 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) data and inclusive MC, plus 44.49 pb−1 of 3.65 GeV continuum data and its inclusive MC for subtraction. Generate exclusive MC with 100k ψ(3686) → ω K+ K− events at 3.686 GeV, 50k at 3.65 GeV, and 50k ψ(3686) → γ η_c(2S), η_c(2S) → ω K+ K− for the peaking-background study. Select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring two positive and two negative tracks and zero net charge; select photons with EMC hit time in 0–700 ns, angle to any charged track >20°, energy >25 MeV in the barrel and >50 MeV in the endcap, and at least two photons. Identify particles by the probability method with P>0.001, separating K/π against pions, kaons, and protons, and require at least one K+, one K−, one π+, and one π−. Reconstruct π0 candidates from two photons with a mass-constrained fit (χ²<25, at least one π0). Then perform a 4C kinematic fit to K+ K− π+ π− π0 with a vertex fit of the four charged tracks, requiring 0.752 < M(π+π−π0) < 0.812 GeV and χ²<200, followed by a 5C fit with the π0 mass constraint in 0.11 < M(γγ) < 0.15 GeV and χ²<200; the final analysis applies the tighter 5C χ²<90, and signal efficiencies are determined with a data-driven BODY3 generator at both energy points.
