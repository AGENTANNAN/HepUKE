At the J/ψ(3097), measure the four related channels J/ψ → Λ̄ π+ Σ− and its charge conjugate plus J/ψ → Λ̄ π− Σ+ and its charge conjugate, reconstructing Λ/Λ̄ → pπ∓ while the Σ is not reconstructed and is inferred from the recoil mass. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and its inclusive MC; generate 500,000 exclusive MC events for J/ψ → Λ̄ π+ Σ−, Λ̄ → p̄ π+, Σ− → n π− in phase space. Event selection requires at least three charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93; PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against pions/kaons and pions against kaons/protons. The Λ/Λ̄ is formed from pπ∓ via a secondary-vertex fit with |M(pπ∓) − M(Λ)| < 5 MeV, decay-length significance > 2, and the best candidate chosen by the minimum [M(pπ∓) − M(Λ)]²; the prompt pion must satisfy DCA < 10 cm in z and < 1 cm in xy. The Σ is selected via the recoil mass of the (Λ/Λ̄)π system in [1.14, 1.24] GeV/c². For the PWA efficiency, a full Σ → n π reconstruction is used with a 1C kinematic fit to the missing neutron (χ² < 30) and a Λ vertex fit χ² < 30, yielding ~31–35% efficiency after data/MC corrections.
