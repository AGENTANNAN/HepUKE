On the J/ψ(3097), study J/ψ → γπ0π0 with π0 → γγ, using the 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data and inclusive MC plus a 3.080 GeV continuum data sample for background; generate 100k-event exclusive-MC samples for the signal (phase-space J/ψ→γπ0π0), J/ψ→γη (η→π0π0π0), and J/ψ→γη′ (η′→ηπ0π0) modes. Event selection applies a charged-track veto with no tracks satisfying |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and zero positive, negative, and net charge, with no further PID beyond this veto. Photons must satisfy an EMC timing window of 0–14, be at least 10° from any charged track, have E>25 MeV in the barrel or >50 MeV in the endcap, with at least five good photons. Four photons are paired into two π0 candidates with each photon-pair mass constrained to the nominal π0 mass in a Kalman fit (χ²<25, requiring at least two π0), then a nominal kinematic fit of γπ0π0 constrains the final-state four-momentum to the initial J/ψ four-momentum; together these give the 6C fit, with a loose BOSS-pass χ²<200 while the published mass-dependent χ²<20 below and <60 above the KK threshold is applied subsequently. For multiple photon permutations the minimum-χ² combination is retained, and photon pairs assigned to a π0 are required to lie within 13 MeV/c² of the π0 mass.
