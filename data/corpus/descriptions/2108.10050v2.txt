On the ψ(4260) at √s = 4.178 GeV, study e+e− → Ds*+ Ds−, with Ds*+ → Ds+ γ, Ds+ → π+π−π+ (signal), and Ds− → K+K−π− (tag). Use the 4.178 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k exclusive MC events for the full decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly three tracks with at least one positive and one negative; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC in EMC 0–14, angle to nearest track >10°, at least one photon; PID by the probability method (prob>0.001), pions identified against kaons and protons, requiring at least one π+ and one π−. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to π+π−π+ with Ds+ mass constraint, χ²<200. Additional requirements: veto pions from K_S0→π+π− (mass window ±12 MeV, decay length >2× vertex resolution) and photons from π0→γγ (mass 0.125–0.145 GeV); tag Ds*+ → Ds+ γ via direct |M_rec − m(Ds*+)|<0.02 GeV or indirect ΔM = M(Ds+γ)−M(Ds+) in [0.135,0.15] GeV; apply TMVA neural-net background suppression; select Ds+ mass in [1.900,2.0535] GeV with signal region |m(π+π−π+)−m(Ds+)|<12 MeV for the QMIPWA amplitude analysis.
