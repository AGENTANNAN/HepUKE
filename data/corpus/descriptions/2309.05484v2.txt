At seven energy points from 4.600 to 4.699 GeV (4.5 fb⁻¹), search for the double-tag process e⁺e⁻ → Λc⁺ Λc⁻ with the signal Λc⁺ → Σ⁻ K⁺ π⁺, Σ⁻ → n π⁻ (neutron treated as missing), tagging Λc⁻ in ten hadronic modes (pK⁺π⁻, pKS0, pK⁺π⁻π0, pKS0π0, pKS0π⁺π⁻, Λπ⁻, Λπ⁻π0, Λπ⁺π⁻π⁻, Σ0π⁻). Use the real data and inclusive MC at each point, plus 100k-event exclusive MC for the Λc⁺ → Σ⁻ K⁺ π⁺ (Σ⁻ → n π⁻) signal generated from e⁺e⁻ → Λc⁺Λc⁻. Charged tracks require |cosθ|<0.93, with the K⁺ and π⁺ candidates having |Vz|<10 cm and |Vr|<1 cm while the π⁻ from Σ⁻ is allowed |Vz|<20 cm; the three charged particles (K⁺, π⁺, π⁻) must sum to charge +1, no additional charged tracks may pass the track quality, and PID uses combined dE/dx and TOF with K⁺/π⁺ identified by PID probability and a vertex fit applied to the K⁺ and π⁺. A kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation and the Λc mass constraint is required with χ²<200. Backgrounds are suppressed by the recoil mass against Λc⁻ in (2.275, 2.310) GeV/c², the recoil mass against H⁺ outside (1.15, 1.24) GeV/c² (against Λc⁺ → Σ⁺ K⁺ π⁻), M(π⁺π⁻) outside (0.48, 0.52) GeV/c² (KS0 veto), and M_rec(H⁻) > 1.15 GeV/c².
