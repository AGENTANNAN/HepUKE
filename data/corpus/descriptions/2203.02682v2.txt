On 34 energy points from 3.773 to 4.701 GeV, study e+e− → ωπ0 and e+e− → ωη, with ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ, and η → γγ. Use real data at all 34 points and inclusive MC for a subset of them; generate exclusive MC for each channel using the corresponding decay chain. Event selection: exactly one positive and one negative charged track, total two tracks, net charge zero, with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; at least four photons with barrel energy threshold 25 MeV and endcap 50 MeV, each at least 10° from any charged track; identify pions by the probability method, separating π± from K± and protons with a probability cut of 0.001. For the ωπ0 mode, perform two mass-constrained Kalman fits on γγ pairs to reconstruct the π0 from ω and the bachelor π0; for the ωη mode, reconstruct the π0 from ω and the η. Then apply a 6C kinematic fit to π+π−π0(ω)π0(bachelor) or π+π−π0(ω)η with four-momentum conservation plus nominal π0 (and η) mass constraints, χ² < 200, and a competing 5C fit without the bachelor π0 or η mass constraint, χ² < 200. Both channels share the same selection chain except for the final-state particle assignment.
