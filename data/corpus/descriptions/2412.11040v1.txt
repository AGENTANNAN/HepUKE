On the ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, study ψ(3770) → D+ D− with the tag D− → K+ π− π− and the fully reconstructed signal D+ → K− π+ π+ π0, π0 → γγ. Use the 7.93 fb−1 real data and inclusive MC at this energy, and generate 1,000,000 exclusive signal MC events for the same D+ → K− π+ π+ π0 plus D− tag in phase space, later reweighted by the amplitude-analysis result for efficiency. For the tag, select D− → K+ π− π− and require tag-side ΔE in [−0.025, 0.024] GeV. For the signal, require one K− and two π+ with total charge +1, and two photons with energy above 25 MeV in the barrel / 50 MeV in the endcap and opening angle to charged tracks greater than 10°; reconstruct π0 with γγ invariant mass in [0.115, 0.150] GeV/c² and a 1C mass-constrained fit to the nominal π0 mass with χ² < 30, requiring at least one photon in the barrel EMC. The selection specifies charged final-state multiplicities but no track |cosθ|, |Vz|, |Vr| or PID criteria. Perform a 6C kinematic fit imposing four-momentum conservation, the π0 mass, and the D+ tag mass, retaining χ² < 100. Use the D+ signal region 1.863 < M_BC < 1.879 GeV/c², additional ΔE cuts of [−0.062, 0.034] GeV for D+ and [−0.025, 0.025] GeV for D−, and veto D0D0bar background by rejecting events with 1.863 < M_BC^W < 1.867 GeV/c² under the D0D0bar mispartition hypothesis.
