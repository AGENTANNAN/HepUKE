On the ψ(3686) (3.686 GeV, 448.1M events), study ψ(3686) → K⁻ Λ Ξ̄⁺ + c.c. for a partial wave analysis of Ξ* hyperons, with Ξ̄⁺ → Λ̄ π⁺, Λ̄ → p̄ π⁺, and the Λ → p π⁻ reconstructed via the recoil mass RM(K⁻ Ξ̄⁺). Use the real data and inclusive MC for the 709 ψ(3686) sample; generate 1M-event exclusive MC for the full decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least 4 tracks with ≥2 positive and ≥2 negative; PID by the probability method requiring exactly one proton (against K/π), one K⁻ (against π), and two π⁺ (against K). Reconstruct Λ̄ from a p̄ π⁺ secondary vertex fit with mass window [1.110,1.121] GeV/c², and Ξ̄⁺ from Λ̄ π⁺ with mass window [1.315,1.330] GeV/c² and decay length >0.5 cm; Ξ̄⁺ daughter tracks use looser |Vz|<15 cm and Vr<10 cm cuts. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to K⁻ Ξ̄⁺, treating the Λ as missing, with χ² < 200 and nominal settings, and require recoil mass RM(K⁻ Ξ̄⁺) in [1.080,1.140] GeV/c² to select prompt Λ candidates. Apply this selection to the data, inclusive MC, and exclusive MC.
