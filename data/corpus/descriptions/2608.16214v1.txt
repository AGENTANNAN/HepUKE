On the J/ψ(3.097 GeV) and ψ(3686)(3.686 GeV) resonances, study the decay to Σ0 Σ̄0 η, with Σ0 → γΛ, Σ̄0 → γΛ̄, Λ → pπ−, Λ̄ → p̄π+, and η → γγ. Use the corresponding real data samples ((10087±44)×10^6 J/ψ and (2712±14)×10^6 ψ(3686) events) together with their inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the two resonances using the same decay chain. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm and Vr<10 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons must have energy above 25 MeV (barrel) or 50 MeV (endcap) with EMC TDC in [0,14] and at least four good photons. Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating protons from kaons and pions and pions from kaons and protons, requiring at least one proton, one antiproton, one π+ and one π−. Secondary vertex fits reconstruct Λ from pπ− and Λ̄ from p̄π+ by minimizing the mass difference, removing the used tracks from the candidate list. A nominal 4C kinematic fit to Λ Λ̄ γγγγ is applied with χ²<200 at the BOSS level (tightened in ROOT to 60 for J/ψ and 40 for ψ(3686)), together with an alternative 4C fit to Λ Λ̄ γγγ used to reject one-fewer-photon background. Both resonances share the same selection chain, with ROOT-level requirements including a π0 veto |M(γγ)−m(π0)|>20 MeV, Σ0/Σ̄0/η candidate selection via a joint χ² with mass windows of 15 MeV for Σ0 and Σ̄0 and 25 MeV for η, and for ψ(3686) an additional J/ψ veto |M(Σ0Σ̄0)−m(J/ψ)|>30 MeV.
