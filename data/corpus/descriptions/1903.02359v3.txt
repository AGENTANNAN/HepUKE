Measure e+e− → ω χc0 at nine energy points from 4.180 to 4.280 GeV, with χc0 → π+π− or K+K−, ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ, using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC samples plus KKMC-generated exclusive MC for both χc0 decay modes at each point. The center-of-mass energy is taken run-by-run from the measured value. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm, |Vz|<10 cm, four positive-track and zero negative-track counts, and net charge 0; photons are selected with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy thresholds, 2–20 photons, EMC timing 0–700 ns, and at least 10° separation from tracks. A γγ Kalman fit constrains the two photons to the nominal π0 mass, followed by a 5C kinematic fit to π0 plus the four charged daughters imposing four-momentum conservation and the π0 mass constraint, with χ²<100 and nominal flag. Because charged kaons and pions are clearly separated in momentum, tracks with p>1 GeV/c are assigned to the χc0 daughters and p<1 GeV/c to the ω daughters; the ππ and KK hypotheses are reconstructed separately, and the final choice of the smaller 5C χ² is made in the ROOT stage, with both decay modes sharing the same selection chain.
