Measure the Born/dressed cross sections of e+e− → p K− Λ̄ (+c.c.), with Λ̄ → p̄ π+, across the BESIII R-scan. Use the 37 real-data energy points from 4.009 to 4.951 GeV (BOSS 703–707) together with inclusive MC at 4.178 GeV (about 40× the data luminosity), and generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC at every energy point for e+e− → p K− Λ̄, Λ̄ → p̄ π+ (phase-space placeholder for the full amplitude model). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93 and |Vz|<20 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks with net charge zero, with no PID applied since momenta are high and the p/p̄/π± assignment is resolved by the Λ vertex fit and the 4C fit. Reconstruct the Λ from a p π− pair via a secondary-vertex fit that minimizes the mass difference, keep exactly the best candidate within 1.10 < M(pπ−) < 1.13 GeV/c², and veto the kaon when |cosθ_K|>0.83 to suppress beam-induced backgrounds. Then perform a 4C kinematic fit on the Λ p K− final state with χ²<100, flagged nominal; both charge-conjugate modes share the same chain.
