On the 4.178 GeV data sample, search for Ds+ → γ e+ ν_e using a modified double-tag technique: single-tag a Ds− in hadronic modes, then reconstruct the signal Ds+ → γ e+ ν_e. Use the 4.178 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus 100k-event exclusive MC for ψ(4260) → Ds+ Ds*−, Ds*− → Ds− γ, Ds− → K+K−π−, Ds+ → γ e+ ν_e. Tag the Ds− (charm = −1) in 13 hadronic modes: K+K−π−, K+K−π−π0, KsK, πη, πππ, Kππ, KsK−ππ, KsK+ππ, KsKsπ, KsKπ0, Ksπ, πππη, and ππ0η, with η/η′ and soft-γ from Ds* decays reconstructed internally by the tag algorithm. On the signal side require exactly one photon with energy above 25 MeV and angle greater than 10°, exactly one e+ with net charge +1, and a missing ν_e. Perform a kinematic fit with a four-momentum constraint and χ² < 200, resolving the four possible Ds+ production hypotheses at the ROOT level, and keep the analysis blinded until selection optimization on inclusive MC.
