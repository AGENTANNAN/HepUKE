At e+e− centre-of-mass energies from 4128.8 to 4337.9 MeV, using the 16 real-data points of the 4.13–4.34 GeV scan (11.5 fb−1) and the corresponding inclusive MC, study e+e− → γ χc1(3872), χc1(3872) → π+π−η via two η decay modes: η → γγ (Mode I) and η → π+π−π0 with π0 → γγ (Mode II); 100k-event exclusive MC is generated for each mode at every energy point. In both modes charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm, and photons must have EMC TDC 0–14 with barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, be at least 10° from any track, and number at least three. Mode I requires exactly one positive and one negative track, taken as π+π−, then performs a 5C fit to γ π+π−γγ constraining the two photons to the η mass together with four-momentum conservation, χ²<200, with the nominal fit hypothesis; it also rejects π0→γγ mis-combinations when |M(γ_rad γ_1(2))−m(π0)|<20 MeV/c² and radiative dimuon background when cosθ_ππ<−0.96. Mode II requires exactly two positive and two negative tracks and uses probability-based PID to identify pions against kaons, requiring at least one π+ and one π−, followed by an intermediate γγ mass-constrained π0 fit with χ²<25 and at least one π0, and then a 5C fit to π+π−π+π−π0γ constraining M(π+π−π+π−π0) to the η mass plus four-momentum conservation with χ²<200; it additionally vetoes events with M(γ_rad γ_1) in [125.6,150.0] MeV/c² and M(γ_rad γ_2) in [115.7,160.0] MeV/c² to suppress e+e−→π+π−π+π−π0.
