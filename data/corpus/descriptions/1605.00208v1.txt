At √s = 3.773 GeV, measure the absolute branching fraction of D+ → K0 e+ νe with K0 → K_S0 → π0 π0 using a double-tag technique on 2.93 fb−1 of ψ(3770) real data together with the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive MC events for the full ψ(3770) → D+D−, D+ → K0 e+νe, K0 → K_S0 → π0π0 chain. Reconstruct the tag D− in six hadronic single-tag modes (K+π−π−, K_S0π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S0π−π0, K_S0π+π−π−, K+K−π−): charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93 and, unless they come from K_S0, V_xy<1 cm and |V_z|<10 cm; K/π separation from combined dE/dx and TOF confidence levels (CL_K>CL_π for a kaon, CL_π>CL_K for a pion); K_S0 daughters with |V_z|<20 cm, assigned π+π− without PID, forming a common vertex with |M(π+π−)−m_K_S0|<12 MeV/c² and L/σ_L>2; photons with shower time within 700 ns of the event start, E>25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), and opening angle >10° to the closest charged track; and π0 from γγ with M(γγ) in (0.115, 0.150) GeV/c² plus a mass-constrained fit. For each tag mode keep only the combination with minimum |ΔE|, requiring |ΔE| within ±25 MeV for the K+π−π−, K_S0π−, K_S0π+π−π− and K+K−π− final states and within (−55,+40) MeV for K+π−π−π0 and K_S0π−π0, with the ST signal region taken as 1.863 < M_BC < 1.877 GeV/c². On the signal side require exactly one good charged track not used by the tag, carrying charge opposite to the tag D− and identified as an electron via combined dE/dx, TOF and EMC information (CL_e>0.001 and CL_e/(CL_e+CL_π+CL_K)>0.8), with photons within 5° of the electron direction added to its four-momentum to partially recover FSR and bremsstrahlung, plus at least four good photons. The K0 → K_S0 → π0 π0 candidate must have M(π0π0) in (0.45, 0.51) GeV/c², selecting the combination with the smallest sum of the two π0 mass-constrained χ² values. Perform a 4C kinematic fit of the tag D−, the four photons, the e+ and a massless neutrino to the lab energy, constraining both π0 masses to their nominal values, with χ²<200; a veto on extra photons with E_max^extra γ<0.300 GeV and the U_miss fit for the undetected neutrino are applied at the ROOT stage.
