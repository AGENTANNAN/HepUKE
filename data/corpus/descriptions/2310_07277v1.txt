On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), search for five semileptonic-tagged D-meson weak decays: J/ψ → D0π0, D0η, D0ρ0, D−π+ and D−ρ+ (with charge conjugates), where the D0 is tagged via D0 → K+e−ν̄e and the D− via D− → K_S0 e−ν̄e, with the π0/η → γγ, ρ0 → π+π−, ρ+ → π+π0 and K_S0 → π+π−. Use the 708_3097 J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive signal MC for each of the five modes (all at E_cms = 3.097 GeV). Charged tracks are required to satisfy |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm and Vr < 1 cm; the two-track modes (D0π0, D0η) require exactly two tracks (K+e−), while the four-track modes (D0ρ0, D−π+, D−ρ+) require exactly four tracks with net charge zero. Photons are selected with the EMC timing window 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, an angle to any track > 10°, and at least two photons for the modes containing a π0 or η. Particle identification uses the probability method (CL cut 0.001) with kaon identified against pion, electron against pion and kaon, and pion against kaon and proton; the electron is further required to satisfy E/p > 0.8. In the π0/η modes a 1C kinematic fit constrains the two photons to the nominal π0 (or η) mass with χ² < 20 (requiring ≥1 π0/η, mass windows 0.115–0.150 GeV for π0 and 0.50–0.57 GeV for η), whereas the D− modes first reconstruct a K_S0 through a secondary vertex fit requiring |M(π+π−) − m_K_S0| < 12 MeV, decay length > 2σ from the IP, K_S0 daughter |Vz| < 20 cm and best χ² combination. The undetected neutrino is handled by the missing four-momentum U_miss = E_miss − c|p_miss|, with the best candidate chosen by mass, and a nominal D-meson recoil-mass window of 1.80–1.95 GeV/c² is imposed against the π0/η/ρ/π+ system to further suppress background.
