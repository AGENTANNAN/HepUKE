At ψ(3770), study the semileptonic decay D+ → K1(1270)0 e+ ν_e with K1(1270)0 → K−π+π0, π0 → γγ, using the double-tag technique ψ(3770) → D+ D−. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 200k exclusive signal-MC events for D+ → K−π+π0 e+ ν_e with the tag D− → K+π−π− (signal modeled with ISGW2 and a relativistic Breit-Wigner for the K1). Reconstruct the tag D− in six hadronic modes (K+π−π−, Ksπ−, K+π−π−π0, Ksπ−π0, Ksπ−π−π+, K+K−π−), and on the signal side require exactly two photons with opening angle >10°, one K−, one π+, one e+ and a missing ν_e. Positron PID by CLe > 0.001 and CLe/(CLe+CLπ+CLK) > 0.8 together with E/p > 0.8, and π0 selection from two photons with momentum >0.15 GeV/c, |cosθ_decay| < 0.8 and M(γγ) in [0.115, 0.150] GeV. Perform a 5C kinematic fit (four-momentum constraint plus the γγ mass constrained to the nominal π0), χ² < 200, and recover FSR/bremsstrahlung photons within 5° of the positron. Veto the hadronic D+ → K−π+π+π0 background with M(K−π+π0 e+) < 1.78 GeV, and veto K*(892)0 e+ν with a fake π0 by requiring U'_miss outside (−0.09, 0.03) GeV. Extract the yields from a 2D unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to M(K−π+π0) versus U_miss, with the signal shape taken from signal MC and the background shape from inclusive MC.
