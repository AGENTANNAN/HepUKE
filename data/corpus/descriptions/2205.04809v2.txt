This is a detector-performance measurement of the CMS energies and luminosities of e+e− collision data taken between √s = 4.61 and 4.95 GeV, using twelve real-data energy points (BOSS 706: 4.610–4.700 GeV; BOSS 707: 4.740–4.946 GeV) plus inclusive MC. The three independent measurements are: CMS energies from Λc+Λc− partial reconstruction with Λc+ → p K− π+ (a ψ(4260) → Λc+Λc− phase-space decay card is supplied); luminosity from large-angle Bhabha scattering e+e− → (γ)e+e−; and a di-photon cross-check e+e− → (γ)γγ. A common track-quality selection is applied to all datasets, requiring charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm and Vr < 1 cm, with PID by the probability method (prob cut 0.001) separating K, π and p against one another. The Bhabha path selects exactly two oppositely charged tracks with momentum above 2 GeV/c and |cosθ| < 0.8 in the EMC barrel, using the EMC energy deposit to correct saturation effects at high energy; the di-photon cross-check instead requires two photon candidates in the EMC with energy and angle cuts. No kinematic fit is performed at the BOSS level — the energy and luminosity extractions are done offline in ROOT.
