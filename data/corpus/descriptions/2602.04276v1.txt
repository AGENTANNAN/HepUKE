At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → Λ Λ̄ with the Λ tagged via Λ → p π− and the Λ̄ annihilating at rest on a proton in the beam-pipe cooling oil, for six signal channels: Λ̄p → K_S0 π+, K+ π+ π−, K_S0 2π+ π−, K+ 2π+ 2π−, K+ 3π+ 3π−, and 2K+ K−. Use the 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data and the corresponding inclusive MC, together with a 200k-event exclusive MC sample generated for each of the six modes. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ| < 0.93, with per-mode multiplicities and charges: (≥2 positive, ≥1 negative, 3 total) for K_S0 π+, (≥2, ≥2, 4) for K+ π+ π−, (≥3, ≥2, 5) for K_S0 2π+ π−, (≥3, ≥3, 6) for K+ 2π+ 2π−, (≥4, ≥4, 8) for K+ 3π+ 3π−, and (≥3, ≥2, 5) for 2K+ K−. Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating p from K/π, K from π, and π from K as required by the mode. A tagged Λ is built from the p π− pair with smallest |M(pπ−) − m_Λ|, with signal window |M − m_Λ| < 0.003 GeV/c² (sideband 0.006–0.009 GeV/c²), and a K_S0 from π+π− with smallest |M(π+π−) − m_K_S0| and window 0.015 GeV/c², in the modes containing it. The full final state is then fitted with four-momentum conservation and the Λ (and K_S0) mass constraints, χ² < 200. Additional criteria are the Λ recoil-mass window |M_recoil(Λ) − m_Λ̄| < 0.020 GeV/c², an annihilation vertex 3.0 ≤ R_xy ≤ 3.5 cm, and |p_oil| < 0.04 GeV/c to reject annihilations on bound Au/Be/C nucleons.
