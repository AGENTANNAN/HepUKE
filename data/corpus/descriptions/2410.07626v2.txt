At √s = 3.773 GeV (ψ(3770)), measure D+ → μ+νμ with the double-tag method, using the 20.3 fb⁻¹ real data sample and the matching inclusive MC. Generate a 1,000,000-event exclusive signal MC sample for ψ(3770) → D+D−, D+ → μ+νμ, D− → K+π−π−. Reconstruct the single-tag D− in eight hadronic modes: K+π−π−, K_Sπ−, K+π−π−π0, K_Sπ−π0, K_Sπ+π−π−, K+K−π−, π+π−π−, and K+π−π+π−π−. On the signal side require exactly one μ+ with charge +1, no photons, and an undetected neutrino, with no additional charged track allowed. Muon identification uses hit depth versus momentum and cosθ, the candidate EMC energy must lie in 0.00–0.35 GeV, and the maximum energy of any unused shower must be below 0.3 GeV. Perform a kinematic fit constraining the tag, μ+, and missing neutrino to the lab-frame four-momentum, with χ² < 200, and apply this selection to the real data, inclusive MC, and exclusive signal MC.
