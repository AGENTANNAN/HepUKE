At √s = 3.773 GeV (ψ(3770)), measure the CP-even fraction F_+^{4π} of D⁰ → π⁺π⁻π⁺π⁻ in quantum-correlated D⁰D̄⁰ events, using a double-tag method in which one D is reconstructed in a CP-eigenstate tag and the other in the π⁺π⁻π⁺π⁻ signal. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC samples, with tag candidates taken from the pre-stored DTagAlg vocabulary: CP-even tags K⁺K⁻ and K_S⁰π⁰π⁰, and CP-odd tags K_S⁰π⁰, K_S⁰η and K_S⁰η′, each ranked by invariant mass. The signal side requires exactly four charged pions (2 π⁺ and 2 π⁻) with net charge 0, zero photons, and exactly four additional tracks all identified as pions. Veto K_S⁰ on the signal side by rejecting events where any π⁺π⁻ pair has invariant mass in [0.481, 0.514] GeV/c² with L/σ_L > 2, and require the signal-side ΔE within [−0.026, 0.023] GeV. Perform a kinematic fit imposing four-momentum conservation and constraining the tag invariant mass to the nominal D⁰ mass, with χ² < 200. Tag modes not available in the DTagAlg vocabulary (K_L⁰π⁰, K_L⁰ω, π⁰π⁰, K_S⁰ω, K_S⁰φ, K_L⁰π⁰π⁰) and the quasi-CP-even π⁺π⁻π⁰ and phase-space-binned K_S,L⁰π⁺π⁻ tags are handled in separate algorithms; single-tag yields come from M_BC fits with an ARGUS background shape, and double-tag yields from M_BC (fully reconstructed) or missing-mass fits, with peaking backgrounds estimated from MC including quantum-correlation corrections.
