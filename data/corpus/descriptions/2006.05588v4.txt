On ψ(3686), study inclusive multiplicity distributions (N_ch, N_sh, N_pi0) for ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ with χ_cJ → anything (and χ_cJ → γ J/ψ), extracting χ_cJ and J/ψ yields from fits to the shower-energy (E_sh) distribution rather than exclusive reconstruction. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive ψ(3686) MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for ψ(2S) → γ χ_c0, χ_c0 → anything (phase space). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring at least one track (relaxed for the N_ch=0 study); photons with EMC TDC in [0,14] and energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), at least one neutral shower; PID by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying pions against kaons and protons. For the N_ch=0 case, veto |Px_neu|>1.0 GeV/c and |Py_neu|>1.0 GeV/c, and apply non-ψ(3686) and ψ(3686)→ππJ/ψ background filters. π0 candidates are γγ pairs with 0.120 < M_γγ < 0.145 GeV/c². No kinematic fit is performed; yields come from fitting the E_sh spectrum per multiplicity bin.
