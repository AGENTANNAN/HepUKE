On the 4.600–4.700 GeV data (seven energy points), study e+e− → Ds*+ DsJ−, with Ds*+ → γ Ds+, Ds+ → K+K−π+, and DsJ− → π+π−π0 for the three DsJ− states Ds0*(2317)−, Ds1(2460)− and Ds1(2536)−; use the corresponding inclusive MC and generate a 500k-event exclusive signal MC for all seven energy points. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring two positive and one negative track (net charge +1); photons with EMC timing 0–14, energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), separation from tracks >20°, and at least one photon. PID uses the probability method with probability >0.001, K+ versus π and π+ versus K separation, requiring exactly one K+, one K−, and one π+. Reconstruct Ds*+ partially from γK+K−π+ (DsJ− missing), choosing the combination closest to the nominal Ds*+ mass 2.1122 GeV/c2, requiring the recoil mass in 2.0–2.8 GeV/c2, and accepting events that satisfy at least one Ds+ submode (φπ+ with |M(K+K−)−mφ|<9 MeV/c2 or anti-K*0K+ with |M(K−π+)−mK*0|<84 MeV/c2). A 2C kinematic fit is applied to γK+K−π+ constraining the masses to the nominal Ds+ (1.96834 GeV/c2) and Ds*+ (2.1122 GeV/c2), with χ²<10, before the partial reconstruction.
