At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), perform a detector trigger-efficiency study rather than a decay analysis, using the 2018 real data and the matching inclusive MC sample at this energy. Three control samples are defined: Bhabha e+e− events with two EMC clusters of opening angle >166° and |E_emc(e+)+E_emc(e−)−3.097|/3.097 ≤ 0.10 plus two opposite-charge good tracks with opening >175°; dimuon μ+μ− events with two opposite-charge good tracks, opening ≥178°, each track p<2 GeV/c and E_EMC<0.7 GeV, and total four-momentum in (2.8,3.3), (−0.1,0.1), (−0.1,0.1), (−0.2,0.2) for (E,px,py,pz); and generic hadronic events with at least two good tracks in the MDC, requiring opening <170° when exactly two tracks to suppress Bhabha and dimuon. The common preselection requires good charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track, and net charge zero. Trigger efficiency is computed as N(selected and trigger condition satisfied for the channel) / N(selected), with the global efficiency obtained by inclusion–exclusion over three groups of trigger channels, and Clopper–Pearson 68.27% CL intervals assigned to the results.
