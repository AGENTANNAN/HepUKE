On the ψ(3770), study e+e− → p pbar π0 with π0 → γγ, measuring the Born cross section at 3.773 GeV, 3.650 GeV, and the ψ(3770) line-shape scan (3.736–3.813 GeV, merged into seven luminosity-weighted points; a 3.780 GeV scan point is used here). Use real data at 3.773 GeV (2.9 fb−1), 3.650 GeV (44 pb−1), and the scan point, plus inclusive MC at 3.773 and 3.650 GeV. Generate 200k-event exclusive signal MC for e+e− → ψ(3770) → p pbar π0, π0 → γγ, phase-space, at each energy point, and 20k-event exclusive background MC for ψ(3770) → p pbar π0 γ, π0 → γγ, at each point. Event selection: exactly one positive and one negative charged track with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and net charge zero; photons with EMC timing 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV (|cosθ|<0.8), endcap >50 MeV (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), more than 10° from the proton track, and at least two candidates. PID uses the probability method from dE/dx and TOF with confidence >0.001, identifies the proton against π and K, requires at least one proton and one antiproton, and removes protons or antiprotons with pT<0.3 GeV/c. Require isolated photons with angle to proton >10° and to antiproton >30°, at least two photons, then perform a nominal 5C kinematic fit to p pbar γγ with four-momentum conservation plus a π0 mass constraint on the photon pair, iterating all γγ combinations and keeping the smallest χ², with χ²<50, and require the π0 candidates within ±3σ of the nominal π0 mass.
