On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γη'η' in two independent η' decay modes using the real J/ψ data and inclusive MC (10.09×10⁹ J/ψ events); generate 500k-event exclusive MC for Mode I, where both η' → ηπ⁺π⁻ and η → γγ (final state 5γ 2π⁺2π⁻), and for Mode II, where one η' → γπ⁺π⁻ and the other η' → ηπ⁺π⁻ with η → γγ (final state 4γ 2π⁺2π⁻). Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero; photons must have EMC TDC 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, angle to any track >10°, and at least five photons in Mode I or at least four in Mode II. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying all four charged tracks as pions against kaons and protons, requiring 2π⁺ and 2π⁻. Mode I reconstructs two η → γγ with a 1C Kalman fit (χ²<25, at least two η), selects the best η' pair from ηπ⁺π⁻ by minimizing the sum of |M−m_η'|² and requires |M(ηπ⁺π⁻)−m_η'|<0.01 GeV/c², then performs a nominal 6C fit (4C plus two η mass constraints) with χ²<85 and applies a photon-number veto using competing 4C fits under 4γ/5γ/6γ hypotheses (accepting 5γ if χ²_5γ < χ²_4γ and χ²_5γ < χ²_6γ). Mode II reconstructs at least one η → γγ with the same 1C Kalman fit (χ²<25), selects the best η' pair by minimizing the weighted sum of |M−m_η'|², with one η' from γπ⁺π⁻ satisfying |M−m_η'|<0.02 GeV/c² and the other from ηπ⁺π⁻ satisfying |M−m_η'|<0.01 GeV/c², vetoes π⁰ by requiring |M(γγ)−m_π⁰|>0.02 GeV/c² for all γγ pairs excluding the η pair, requires M(π⁺π⁻) in [0.4,0.85] GeV/c² for the γπ⁺π⁻ η', applies M(η'η')<3.0 GeV/c², then performs a nominal 5C fit (4C plus one η mass constraint) with χ²<75 and a photon-number veto using competing 4C fits under 3γ/4γ/5γ hypotheses.
