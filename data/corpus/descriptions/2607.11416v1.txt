On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → γ η_c, η_c → p p̄ η, η → γγ, giving a p p̄ γγγ final state. Use the 709_3686 real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for the signal chain with default cross section. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track and net charge 0; photons with EMC timing 0–14, more than 10° from any track, 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, at least three photons. PID by the probability method with 0.001 probability cut, identifying exactly one proton and one antiproton against kaons and pions, plus an isolated-photon requirement (>20° from the primary tracks, ≥3 photons). Perform a 4C kinematic fit to p p̄ γγγ with χ²<200 (nominal), and also fit the 2γ and 4γ hypotheses to store their χ² for background suppression, requiring at analysis level χ²(3γ)<65 and χ²(3γ) below both alternative hypotheses. Further suppress background by choosing the η from the two lower-energy photons with M(γγ) in (0.4,0.7) GeV/c² (otherwise the next pair), vetoing π⁰ (0.12–0.15 GeV/c²) and ψ(3686)→η J/ψ recoil-mass (3.072–3.125 GeV/c²) windows, and keeping the η signal window (0.520,0.570) GeV/c² with sidebands (0.475–0.500)∪(0.600–0.625) GeV/c².
