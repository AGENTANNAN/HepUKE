On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, search for the ΔS=2 nonleptonic decays Ω− → Σ0 π− and Ω− → n K− using a double-tag method in ψ(3686) → Ω− Ω+, with the Ω+ tag reconstructed through Ω+ → Λ K+, Λ → p π− (charge conjugate). Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1.27 million exclusive MC events for each of the two signal modes (Ω− → Σ0 π− with Σ0 treated inclusively, and Ω− → n K− with the neutron not reconstructed). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; for Ω− → Σ0 π− require at least two positive and two negative tracks with net charge 0, while for Ω− → n K− require exactly four charged tracks (two positive, two negative) and net charge 0. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying at least one proton against kaon/pion, and at least one K+ (Σ0 π− mode) or one K+ plus one K− (n K− mode) against pion/proton, then assigning remaining positive tracks as π+ and negative tracks as π−. Reconstruct the tag-side Λ → p π− (charge conjugate) via a secondary vertex fit, building a virtual Λ and removing used tracks, then perform a 4C kinematic fit on Λ K+ π− (Σ0 π− mode) or Λ K+ K− (n K− mode) with χ² < 200, flagged nominal. Apply Ω+ tag mass windows M(Λ K+) ∈ [1.664, 1.680] GeV/c² and recoil mass ∈ [1.652, 1.695] GeV/c² at the ROOT level. No photon selection is required.
