On the seven c.m. energy points 4.600, 4.612, 4.628, 4.641, 4.661, 4.682 and 4.699 GeV (4.5 fb−1 total), use real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for the signal Λc+ → p η′. Use the double-tag method: reconstruct Λc− in ten hadronic tag modes (including Σ modes; the four family-level topologies Λc−→pK−π−, Λπ−, Λπ−π+π−, pK−π−π+π− are used with finer modes handled in ROOT) with tag-side charm −1, requiring M_BC in (2.275,2.310) GeV/c2 and asymmetric ΔE per mode. On the signal side, select exactly three tight charged tracks recoiling against the ST Λc−, identified by PID as p, π+ and π−, plus two photons, veto Λ via M(pπ−)>1.125 GeV/c2 and K_S0 via M(π+π−) outside (0.490,0.510) GeV/c2. For η′→π+π−η (η→γγ), treat the η as missing and require the recoil mass M_rec(Λc− p π+π−) to lie in the η window; for η′→π+π−γ, use the photon from unused photons with a ΔE selection. Apply a four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ²<200, and extract the result from a simultaneous unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to M_rec(Λc− p) and M(π+π−γ).
