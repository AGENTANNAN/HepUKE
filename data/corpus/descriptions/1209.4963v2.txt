Study ψ(3686) → π0 h_c, h_c → γ η_c, with the η_c reconstructed in 16 exclusive hadronic modes: pp, 2(π+π−), 2(K+K−), K+K−π+π−, ppπ+π−, 3(π+π−), K+K−2(π+π−), K+K−π0, ppπ0, K_S0 K±π∓, K_S0 K±π∓π+π−, π+π−η, K+K−η, 2(π+π−)η, π+π−π0π0, and 2(π+π−)π0π0. Use the 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data (106M events) and inclusive MC, plus 42 pb^-1 of continuum data at 3.65 GeV with its inclusive MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the 16 η_c decay modes. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge zero, and mode-specific numbers of positive/negative tracks matching the final state. Photons are EMC showers with E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.8) and E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), at least 10° from any track and inside the EMC timing window (0–14), with mode-dependent multiplicities of ≥3, ≥5 or ≥7; PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating protons (≥1 or ≥2), pions (≥3 or ≥4) and kaons (≥1, ≥2 or ≥3) from the other species where required, while the K_S0-containing, π+π−η, 2(π+π−)η and multi-π0 modes have no PID requirement. Soft π0 and η candidates are formed from γγ pairs within 15 MeV/c² of the nominal mass through a 1C mass-constrained fit (χ²<25), K_S0 → π+π− is reconstructed via a secondary-vertex fit (mass window 20 MeV/c², decay length ≥2σ), and a 4C kinematic fit under the ψ(3686) → π0 γ X_i hypothesis is applied (nominal constraint, loose χ²<200 in BOSS, with the paper's channel-dependent χ²_4C of 30–70 applied in ROOT), followed by the η_c window 2.900–3.050 GeV/c² and E1 photon energy 0.450–0.550 GeV; when several h_c candidates remain, the one with the smallest combined χ² (4C + 1C + PID + vertex) is retained.
