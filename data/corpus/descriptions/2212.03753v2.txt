Measure the absolute branching fraction of inclusive semileptonic Λc+ → X e+ νe via a tag-and-missing technique on seven energy points of real data (4.600, 4.612, 4.628, 4.640, 4.661, 4.682, 4.699 GeV), each with its matching inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive signal MC per point for Λc+ → X e+ νe (X inclusive, phase space). Tag side: a hadronic Λc− (anti-charm) reconstructed in the hadronic mode group, explicitly including the p K π and p KS modes (the 12 hadronic tag modes of the paper being approximated by this group). Signal side: exactly one positron of charge +1, identified by E/p > 0.8 together with PID likelihoods L′e > 0.001 and L′e/(L′e+L′π+L′K) > 0.8, plus one missing massless neutrino. The positron momentum is binned in 50 MeV/c steps from 200 to 1000 MeV/c, with wrong-sign subtraction for charge-symmetric backgrounds and PID/tracking-efficiency unfolding deferred to the ROOT stage rather than the BOSS selection. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the tag Λc−, the e+ and the νe to the centre-of-mass energy (ECMS = 4.640 GeV), accepting χ² < 200.
