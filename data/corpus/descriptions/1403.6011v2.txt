Study e+e− → p pbar in the vicinity of ψ(3770), extracting the cross section and separating the resonant ψ(3770) → p pbar amplitude from the continuum through their interference. Use real data at 3.650 GeV (44.5 pb−1), 3.773 GeV (2917 pb−1), and the ψ(3770) line-shape scan points covering 3.74–3.90 GeV, together with inclusive MC at 3.650 and 3.773 GeV. Generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC for e+e− → p pbar at each centre-of-mass energy point (3.650, 3.773, and every scan point), and a 500k-event ISR background sample ψ(4260) → γ ψ(3686), ψ(3686) → p pbar associated with the 3.773 GeV data. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, and net charge 0; no photon selection is applied. Identify particles by the probability method with probability >0.001, separating protons from kaons and pions and requiring exactly one proton and one antiproton; both tracks must satisfy |p − p_exp| < 40 MeV/c with p_exp = sqrt(s/4 − m_p^2) (1.637 GeV/c at 3.773 GeV), the per-scan-point momentum window being applied by re-running with the corresponding CM energy. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the p pbar system to the e+e− CM energy, with χ² < 200, flagged nominal. An additional back-to-back cut requiring the CM proton–antiproton opening angle to be greater than 179° is noted but has no dedicated implementation.
