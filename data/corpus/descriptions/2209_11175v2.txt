At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ K_S0 K_S0 π0 with K_S0 → π+π− and π0 → γγ, using the corresponding real data (10.09×10⁹ J/ψ events) and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive-MC events for the J/ψ → γ K_S0 K_S0 π0 phase-space decay with the same intermediate decays. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; photons must have barrel energy >25 MeV or endcap energy >50 MeV, EMC timing in [0,14] (50 ns units), angle to the nearest track >10°, and at least three photons. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying pions against kaons and protons and requiring at least two π+ and two π−. Two K_S0 candidates are reconstructed from π+π− secondary-vertex fits that minimize the π+π− mass difference, with used tracks removed, and at least one π0 is required from a two-photon fit constraining the γγ mass to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<25. A final kinematic fit to γ K_S0 K_S0 π0 imposes four-momentum conservation plus π0 and both K_S0 mass constraints (7C), with χ²<200, flagged nominal.
