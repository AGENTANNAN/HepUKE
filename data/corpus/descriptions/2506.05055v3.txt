At the J/ψ(3097) resonance, study J/ψ → γ π⁰π⁰π⁰ with π⁰ → γγ. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k exclusive MC events for this decay. Require no charged tracks; photons must have energy above 25 MeV in the barrel or 50 MeV in the endcap and TDC in [0,14], with at least 7 photons. First perform a 1C fit pairing photons to the nominal π⁰ mass, χ²<10, requiring at least three π⁰ candidates. Then perform a 7C fit to γ π⁰π⁰π⁰ with nominal masses and four-momentum conservation, χ²<40; also test the competing γ η π⁰π⁰ hypothesis (η→γγ) with a four-momentum constraint for background rejection. Further veto |M(γπ⁰)−M(ω)|<0.06 GeV/c² and |M(γ_rγ)−M(π⁰)|<0.02 GeV/c² for radiative-photon miscombination, require at least one π⁰π⁰ pair in the f₀(980) window [0.89,1.09] GeV/c², and perform a partial-wave analysis for M(π⁰π⁰π⁰)<1.6 GeV/c² with η(1405), f₁(1285), f₁(1420), f₁(1510), and nonresonant 0⁻⁺ and 1⁺⁺ PHSP.
