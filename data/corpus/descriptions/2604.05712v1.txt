At the ψ(3770) resonance (√s = 3.773 GeV), we measure the CKM angle γ via quantum-correlated D0D0bar decays using an unbinned optimal Fourier method on 8 fb−1 of BESIII data (2010–2011 and 2021–2022). We use the real data and inclusive MC samples, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes: D0 → K_S0 π+π− and D0 → K_S0 K+K−, both modeled with a Dalitz-plot amplitude, with the opposite D0bar → K+π− (phase space) and K_S0 → π+π−. Two double-tag selections are performed, one per signal mode: the signal D (charm +1) is tagged against the other D (charm −1) reconstructed in flavor-specific, CP-even, CP-odd, and self-conjugate modes including D0 → K−π+, K−π+π0, K−π+π+π−, K+K−, π+π−, K_S0π0π0, π+π−π0, K_S0π0, K_S0η, K_S0η′, K_S0ω, K_S0π+π−, and K_S0K+K−, ranked by invariant mass. Final-state particle selection follows the previous BESIII binned strong-phase measurement (JHEP 06 (2025) 086). A kinematic fit constrains four-momentum and the D, K_S0, and K_L0 masses to their nominal values, with χ² < 200. For K_L0 h+h− signal (and K_L0 tags), the K_L0 is treated as missing and the missing mass squared is used at the ROOT stage; optimal per-event Fourier weights (M_π=2, M_K=1) are applied in the joint BESIII+LHCb fit. Dominant peaking backgrounds (D → π+π−h+h− for K_S0 h+h− and D → K_S0(→π0π0)h+h− for K_L0 h+h−) are handled in the ROOT fit.
