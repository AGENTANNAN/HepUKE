At the ψ(3770), for the improved measurement of the strong-phase difference δ_D^{Kπ} in quantum-correlated D0D0bar decays, select events with one D0 reconstructed in a CP tag mode and the other in the signal mode K−π+. Use 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC (dataset 712_3773); no exclusive MC generation is specified. The decay model is ψ(3770)→D0D0bar, with D0→K−π+ and anti-D0→K+π− in phase space. Two separate tag selections are processed: CP-even tags K+K−, π+π−, π0π0, K_S0π0π0; and CP-odd tags K_S0π0, K_S0η, K_S0η′ (via π+π−η), both using pre-stored tag candidates ranked by invariant mass. On the signal side require zero photons, exactly one K− and one π+, and net charge zero. Each candidate is fitted with four-momentum conservation and the tag invariant mass constrained to the nominal D0 mass, keeping χ²<200. Other tag modes, including K_L0X, K_S0ω/φ, π+π−π0, and K_S,L0π+π−, are noted as not in the tag vocabulary and handled separately.
