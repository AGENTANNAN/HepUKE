Study e+e− → π+π− ψ(3686) at the 16 c.m. energy points from 4.008 to 4.600 GeV, using the corresponding real data (5.1 fb−1 total) and inclusive MC generated at 4.258 and 4.358 GeV; the signal is generated with ConExc mode 91 (ψ(2S)π+π−) at every point, with 100k-event exclusive samples for mode I [ψ(3686) → π+π−J/ψ, J/ψ → e+e−/μ+μ−], mode II [ψ(3686) → π0π0J/ψ, π0J/ψ, ηJ/ψ, γγJ/ψ, J/ψ → l+l−], and the ISR background e+e− → π+π−J/ψ. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm; PID uses the probability method (prob>0.001), treating p>1.0 GeV/c tracks as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV) and the remaining tracks as pions with π/K separation. For the six-track mode I selection require 3 positive and 3 negative tracks with net charge 0 and PID counts 2π+, 2π−, 1l+, 1l−, then perform a 4C fit to π+π−π+π−l+l− with 3.05<M(l+l−)<3.15 GeV/c² and χ²<60, followed by a 5C fit constraining M(l+l−) to the nominal J/ψ mass. The five-track mode I selection requires exactly five tracks with ≥2 positive and ≥2 negative, PID counts ≥1π+, ≥1π−, 1l+, 1l−, a 1C fit with one undetected pion and the same J/ψ mass window with χ²<15, and a subsequent 2C fit adding the J/ψ mass constraint. For mode II require 2 positive and 2 negative tracks with net charge 0 and at least two photons with E>25 MeV (barrel) or >50 MeV (endcap), EMC timing 0–14 (0–700 ns) and >10° isolation from tracks; PID requires 1π+, 1π−, 1l+, 1l−, and no kinematic fit is applied, the ψ(3686) being selected through the π+π− recoil mass in a loose 3.63–3.75 GeV/c² window. The best ψ(3686) candidate choice, J/ψ mass window, MUC-hit requirement, background vetoes and angular cut are applied at the later ROOT stage.
