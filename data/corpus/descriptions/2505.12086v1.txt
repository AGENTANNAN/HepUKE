At ψ(3770), √s = 3.773 GeV, study D+ → π+ηη with η → γγ, tagging the D− (charm −1) in six hadronic modes: K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S π−, K_S π−π0, K_S π−π−π+, and K+K−π−. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate a 100k-event exclusive MC sample for ψ(3770) → D+D− with D+ → π+ηη, D− → K+π−π−, η → γγ. On the signal side require exactly one π+ and four photons, with the minimum photon angle cut set to 10°. Tag candidates are selected within the ST windows M_BC ∈ [1.860, 1.880] GeV and |ΔE| < 0.040 GeV, with background suppression by a BDTG classifier (the constraint of M(π+ηη) to the nominal D+ mass is applied at the ROOT stage). Perform a 4C kinematic fit with both η masses constrained to their nominal values, requiring χ² < 200, and run the same selection over data, inclusive MC, and the signal exclusive MC.
