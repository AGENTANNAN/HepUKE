At 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → Σ+ Σ̄− with entangled Σ+Σ̄− pairs to test CP symmetry and measure the decay parameters α+ and ᾱ−. Use the 708_3097 real data and matching inclusive MC at this single energy point, and generate 500k exclusive signal MC events for J/ψ → Σ+ Σ̄− with Σ+ → n π+ and Σ̄− → n̄ π− (phase space). Event selection requires exactly two charged tracks with zero net charge, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<30 cm, Vr<10 cm; PID by the probability method (>0.001) separates pions from kaons/protons, requiring one π+ and one π−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to the π+π− pair with four-momentum conservation, χ²<200 and nominal configuration. Neutrons and anti-neutrons are reconstructed from EMC showers with E>600 MeV, lateral moment>20, angle to any charged track >10°, EMC time 0–700 ns, taking the most energetic candidate. A second fit with free neutron momenta imposes four-momentum conservation plus Σ+ and Σ− mass constraints, using anti-neutron directions with floated energy, applied in ROOT. The total J/ψ sample is (1.0087±0.0044)×10^10 events; additional Σ+→p π0 and Σ̄−→p̄ π0 channels are handled in ROOT for ratio measurements with π0→γγ mass-constrained fits.
