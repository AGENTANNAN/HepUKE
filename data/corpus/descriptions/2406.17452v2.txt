Study double-tagged e+e− → Ds*+Ds*−, with Ds*± → γDs± (93.5%) or π0Ds± (6.5%), the tag Ds− reconstructed in seven single-tag modes (KsK, KKπ, KKππ0, KsKππ, π η(γγ), π η′(→ππη,γγ), Kππ) and the signal Ds+ → π+π+π−π0. Use the seven real-data energy points from 4.128 to 4.226 GeV (705 at 4.130/4.160, 703 at 4.180–4.220) together with the matching inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC (e+e− → Ds*+Ds*−, Ds* → γ/π0 Ds, Ds+ → π+π+π−π0, π0 → γγ) at each point. On the signal side require exactly 2 photons with opening angle above 10°, 2 π+ and 1 π−, giving net charge +1. A 7C kinematic fit is then applied: four-momentum conservation plus the π0, tag-Ds− and Ds+ signal invariant-mass constraints, with χ² < 200 (an 8C variant including the Ds+ signal-mass constraint is used for the amplitude analysis stage). Tag-side selection additionally uses M_BC > 1.85 GeV/c² and D0→K−π+, D+→K−π+π+ vetoes, applied later in the ROOT stage.
