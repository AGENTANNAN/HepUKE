At √s = 3.773 GeV (ψ(3770)), use a double-tag method to measure absolute branching fractions of D0 and D+ → KKππ decays, with e+e− → ψ(3770) → D Dbar and the tag Dbar reconstructed in the D0bar → K+π−, K+π−π0, K+π−π−π+ or D− → K+π−π−, KS0π−, K+π−π−π0 modes. Run on the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for four representative signal modes: D0 → K+K−π0π0, D0 → KS0KS0π+π−, D+ → K+K−π+π0, and D+ → KS0KS0π+π0 (a subset of the nine paper modes). Signal-side requirements are: D0 → K+K−π0π0, four photons plus one K+ and one K− with net charge 0; D0 → KS0KS0π+π−, three π+ and three π− with net charge 0; D+ → K+K−π+π0, two photons, one K+, one K−, one π+, net charge +1; D+ → KS0KS0π+π0, two photons, three π+, three π−, net charge +1. No charged-track |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr, photon-energy threshold, or PID method/π-K separation values are specified, so those are left to the standard BOSS defaults and particle assignments rely on the required multiplicities and charges; the KS0 modes use secondary-vertex fits to the remaining π+π− pairs with a KS0 mass window of 0.486–0.510 GeV/c² and decay length greater than 2σ of the vertex resolution. Each channel is fitted with a four-momentum constraint, each γγ pair assigned to a π0 is constrained to the nominal π0 mass, and χ² < 200 is required; the D0 → K+K−π0π0 fit has two π0 mass constraints, the two D+ modes each have one, and the D0 → KS0KS0π+π− fit has none extra.
