On the 4.23–4.70 GeV energy range, study e+e− → π0π0ψ2(3823) with ψ2(3823) → γχc1, χc1 → γJ/ψ, J/ψ → ℓ+ℓ−, and π0 → γγ, using partial reconstruction that allows one photon from the ψ2(3823)→γχc1 decay to be missing. Use the 20 listed real-data points together with inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC per point with this decay chain. Event selection requires exactly one positive and one negative charged track with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, plus at least five photons with EMC TDC in [0,14], barrel energy >25 MeV, endcap energy >50 MeV, and angle to any track >10°. Lepton identification uses the high-momentum strategy, treating tracks with p>1.0 GeV as leptons (electron if EMC energy >1.1 GeV, otherwise muon), and requires one l+ and one l−. A 4C kinematic fit is then performed on l+l− plus five photons, with the missing photon constrained to zero mass, the l+l− mass constrained to the J/ψ, two γγ pairs constrained to the π0 mass, χ²<15, flagged nominal. Post-fit selections applied downstream include the χc1 window 3.49–3.53 GeV/c², ψ(2S) veto 3.665–3.700 GeV/c², an ηJ/ψ veto requiring M(γγπ0π0)>0.70 GeV/c², and Nγ≤6 suppression against π0π0π0π0J/ψ; the ψ2(3823) mass is measured as M(γγℓ+ℓ−) − M(ℓ+ℓ−) + m(J/ψ).
