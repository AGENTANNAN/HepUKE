On ψ(3686), study χ_c1 production in ψ(3686) → γ χ_c1 with χ_c1 → φ φ η (η→γγ), χ_c1 → φ φ η' in two modes (η'→π+π−γ and η'→π+π−η, η→γγ), and χ_c1 → φ K+K− η (one φ, η→γγ), all with φ → K+K−. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 1,000,000-event exclusive MC for each of the four modes (φφη, φφη' with η'→π+π−γ, φφη' with η'→π+π−η, and φ K+K− η). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; photons with 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC timing 0–14, more than 10° from any track, requiring at least 2 (η'→π+π−γ) or at least 3 (all other modes) photons. PID uses the probability method (prob>0.001) with K/π separation, requiring at least 2 K+ and 2 K−; the φφη channel has 2 positive and 2 negative tracks with no pions, the φ K+K− η channel has 2 positive and 2 negative tracks with no pion requirement, and the two η' modes have 3 positive and 3 negative tracks with at least one π+ and one π−; all have net charge 0. Perform a 4C kinematic fit on the selected particles (with a γγ mass constraint to the η in the φφη, φφη' Mode II, and φ K+K− η channels), χ²<200 loose (χ²<38 in the paper, tight cut at ROOT), flagged nominal. Apply the final mass windows at ROOT: φ 1.005–1.035 GeV/c² (two K+K− pairs; for φ K+K− η one φ in this window and the other K+K− pair >1.09 GeV/c²), η 0.51–0.57 GeV/c² in φφη, η' |M(π+π−γ)−0.958|<0.015 GeV/c² (Mode I) or |M(π+π−η)−0.958|<0.015 GeV/c² (Mode II), plus a π0 veto |M(γγ)−mπ0|>0.015 GeV/c² for Mode I and a J/ψ veto |M(K+K+K−K−)−M_J/ψ|>0.015 GeV/c² for φ K+K− η.
