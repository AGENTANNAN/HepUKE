On the ψ(2S) resonance at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(2S) → γ χcJ with χcJ → 4π0 (J=0,1,2), each π0 → γγ, using the 709_3686 real data and inclusive MC and generating 500k-event exclusive MC samples for each of the three χcJ modes (ψ(2S)→γχc0, γχc1, γχc2, all with χcJ→4π0). Event selection requires zero charged tracks, no positive or negative tracks, with track-quality cuts |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm, and exactly nine photons with EMC timing 0–14 and energy above 25 MeV in the barrel or 50 MeV in the endcap. Photon pairs are constrained to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<25, requiring at least four π0 candidates and a π0 mass window of 110–150 MeV/c²; among the nine photons the best combination of one radiative γ plus four π0 is chosen by minimizing χ²_4π = Σ_i (m_γγ,i − m_π0)²/σ² with σ=6.5 MeV/c² and requiring χ²_4π<15. The selected γπ0π0π0π0 system is then subjected to a nominal four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ²<200. Further suppression uses the total energy of the nine photons required to lie in 3.45–3.80 GeV, a J/ψ veto rejecting any di-π0 recoil mass within 100 MeV/c² of m_J/ψ, and a K_S K_S veto for χc0/χc2 rejecting sqrt((m12−m_KS)²+(m34−m_KS)²)<100 MeV/c² for any assignment of the four π0 into two di-π0 pairs; the same selection chain is used for all three χcJ modes.
