On the ψ(3770) (√s = 3.773 GeV), study inclusive D → φX decays with φ → K⁺K⁻, using a tag-and-signal method where the opposite charm D is reconstructed hadronically. Use the 2.93 fb⁻¹ ψ(3770) real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of two representative signals, D⁺ → φπ⁺ (from ψ(3770) → D⁺D⁻) and D⁰ → φK⁻π⁺ (from ψ(3770) → D⁰D̄⁰), both with φ → K⁺K⁻, to determine efficiencies for the inclusive φX signal. Two separate tag analyses are run: for D⁺ → φX, tag the D⁻ in the modes K⁺π⁻π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰π⁻, K_S⁰π⁻π⁰ and K_S⁰π⁻π⁻π⁻; for D⁰ → φX, tag the D̄⁰ in K⁺π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁰ and K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁺. On the signal side require exactly one K⁺ and one K⁻ with net charge 0 (the φ → K⁺K⁻ pair), with the signal side treated as inclusive φX so that all known D → φX decays are covered by the exclusive MC for efficiency. Apply a four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ² < 200 to each tag (and signal) combination, and note that the quantum-correlation correction factor is taken as unity for the charged D⁺ tags but applied for the neutral D⁰ tags from external inputs.
