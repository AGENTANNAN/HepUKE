On the 4.260 GeV data, study e+e− → π±(D D*)∓ via partial reconstruction in two isospin channels: π+ D0 D*− with D0 → K−π+, and π− D+ D*0 with D+ → K−π+π+, detecting only the bachelor pion plus one D meson and inferring the D* from energy-momentum conservation. Use the 4.260 GeV real data (525 pb−1) and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and at least three well-reconstructed tracks; PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying K against π/p and π against K/p, with at least one K−, at least two π+, and for the π−D+ tag also at least one π−. Perform a kinematic fit on the K−π+π+ (K−π+π+π−) system, constraining M(K−π+) to m(D0) [M(K−π+π+) to m(D+)] with χ²<200 as a loose default, the published two-constraint fit χ²<30 being applied later, and keep the smallest-χ² combination; D candidates must satisfy |M(K−π+)−m(D0)|<15 MeV/c² or |M(K−π+π+)−m(D+)|<15 MeV/c², and for the π+D0 tag a post-fit M(π+D0)>2.02 GeV cut rejects D*+D*− background. Both channels share the same selection chain.
