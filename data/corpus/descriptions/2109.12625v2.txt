On the J/ψ peak at 3.097 GeV, search for the radiative decay J/ψ → γ A0 with A0 → μ+μ−. Use the 708_3097 real data set and its inclusive MC, and generate 120k exclusive MC events for J/ψ → γ A0, A0 → μ+μ− (phase-space decay). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring at least one positive and one negative track; photons with TDC 0–14, angle to track >10°, energy >25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), at least one photon. Identify leptons by the probability method: tracks with p>0.5 GeV/c are treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon), requiring at least one l+ and one l−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to the γ l+l− system, flagged nominal, with χ²<200. The same selection chain is applied to data, inclusive MC, and exclusive MC.
