Study e+e− → D_s*+ D_s− → γ D_s+ D_s− at √s = 4.178–4.226 GeV, tagging the D_s− through seven hadronic modes (K+K−π−, K_S0 K−, K+K−π−π0, K_S0 K−π+π−, π+π−π−, π−η′, K+π−π−; the eighth mode, K_S0 K+ π− π−, is not available) and reconstructing the signal D_s+ → K_S0 K+ π0 with K_S0 → π+π− and π0 → γγ, including the D_s*+ transition photon in the tag. Use the six real-data energy points 4.178, 4.190, 4.200, 4.210, 4.220 and 4.226 GeV with the matching inclusive MC, and generate 2M exclusive-MC events for the full decay chain. Selection: K_S0 pions with |Vz|<20 cm and |cosθ|<0.93, vertex-fit χ²<100, K_S0 flight significance >2σ, and π+π− mass in (0.492,0.503) GeV/c²; photons with E>25 MeV (barrel, |cosθ|<0.80) or >50 MeV (endcap, 0.86<|cosθ|<0.92) and timing <700 ns, requiring two photons forming a π0 through a 1C γγ mass-constrained fit with χ²<10, plus one K+, one π+, and one π− with net charge +1. Tag candidates require M_tag in [1.930,1.990] GeV/c², with the best single tag chosen by recoil mass closest to the known D_s*+ mass and the best double tag by average (M_tag+M_sig)/2 closest to the known D_s mass. Perform an 8C kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation and mass constraints on K_S0, π0, the D_s− tag and D_s*+, then an added D_s+ mass constraint, χ²<200, keeping the best candidate, and require M_sig in (1.930,1.990) GeV/c². Suppress combinatorics with M(K+K−)<1.05 GeV, |M(K+π−)−M(K*(892))|<70 MeV and |M(π−π0)−M(ρ)|<150 MeV, and require D_s+ momentum >0.1 GeV/c to remove soft pions from D*+ decays.
