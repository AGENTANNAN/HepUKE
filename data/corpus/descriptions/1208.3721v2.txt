On the ψ(2S), study ψ(2S) → γ χ_cJ with χ_cJ → p n̄ π⁻ and its charge conjugate p̄ n π⁺, plus the same two channels with an extra π⁰ → γγ, for J = 0, 1, 2, with each charge-conjugate mode treated as its own selection. Use the 3.686 GeV ψ(2S) real data (1.06×10⁸ events) and inclusive MC, together with 3.65 GeV continuum data and its inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the twelve signal modes (χ_c0, χ_c1, χ_c2 in p n̄ π⁻, p̄ n π⁺, p n̄ π⁻ π⁰ and p̄ n π⁺ π⁰). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<5 cm, Vr<0.5 cm, exactly one proton and one π⁻ (or one p̄ and one π⁺), net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14 and at least 10° from any track, requiring at least one photon (at least three for the π⁰ modes), the radiative photon isolated by >30° from the (anti)proton track and all photons with E<80 MeV removed. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 confidence level, separating p from K/π and π from K/p, and requiring exactly one proton and one pion candidate. Each candidate is fitted with a 1C kinematic fit under the γ p n̄ π⁻ (or γ p̄ n π⁺) hypothesis, constraining the missing (anti)neutron to the nominal neutron mass and applying χ²<200 at BOSS level; the π⁰ modes fit all three-photon combinations (retaining the smallest χ²) and additionally store the χ² of the two- and four-photon competing hypotheses for a later veto.
