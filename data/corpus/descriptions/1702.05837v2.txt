On ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV (2.93 fb−1), study D+→γe+νe in a double-tag analysis using real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for the signal D+→γe+νe and 100k for the dominant D+→π0e+νe background (π0→γγ), both from ψ(3770)→D+D− with D−→K+π−π−. Reconstruct the tag D− in the six hadronic single-tag modes K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, KS0π−, KS0π−π0, KS0π+π−π− and K+K−π− (charge-conjugate modes implied); charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm and |Vz|<10 cm, with π/K separation by PID likelihoods (Lπ>LK for pions, LK>Lπ for kaons), KS0→π+π− candidates require |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, no Vr/PID, 0.487<M(π+π−)<0.511 GeV/c² and decay length >2σ, photons require E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) or E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.84<|cosθ|<0.92) and a shower time within 700 ns, and π0→γγ candidates require 0.115<M(γγ)<0.150 GeV/c², a 1C mass-constrained fit, and are rejected if both photons are in the endcaps. If several tags occur in a mode, keep the one with smallest |ΔE|, with ST M_BC in 1.8628–1.8788 GeV/c² and mode-dependent ΔE windows (K+π−π−: −27–25 MeV; K+π−π−π0: −62–34; KS0π−: −25–25; KS0π−π0: −73–41; KS0π+π−π−: −33–30; K+K−π−: −23–20). On the signal side, require exactly one leftover track, identified as an electron opposite the tag by dE/dx, TOF and EMC with L(e)>0 and L(e)/(L(e)+L(π)+L(K))>0.8, and at least one remaining photon with energy >10 MeV and angle >10° from the track, using the highest-energy photon as the radiative candidate and treating the neutrino as a massless missing particle. Add final-state-radiation photons with E>50 MeV within a 5° cone around the electron, veto any photon pair with π0 1C fit χ²<20, and require the radiative photon lateral moment in (0.0,0.3). Perform a 4C kinematic fit of the tag, γ, e+ and νe to the lab energy with χ²<200, and store the fitted missing mass, U_miss and q² for the final analysis.
