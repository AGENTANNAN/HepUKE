At the ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV, sample 712_3773, 2.93 fb⁻¹), measure inclusive D⁰/D⁺ → π⁺π⁺π⁻X branching fractions with a tag-based technique using real data and inclusive MC. For D⁰, use the flavor tag anti-D⁰ → K⁺π⁻ with signal D⁰ → π⁺π⁺π⁻X and generate 1,000,000 exclusive-MC events, applying a quantum-coherence correction for the C = −1 ψ(3770) tag correlation. For D⁺, use the hadronic tag D⁻ → K⁺π⁻π⁻ with signal D⁺ → π⁺π⁺π⁻X and another 1,000,000 exclusive-MC events, with no quantum-correlation correction. On the signal side require at least two π⁺ and one π⁻ with net charge +1, veto K_S⁰ by rejecting any π⁺π⁻ pair with invariant mass in [0.485, 0.510] GeV/c², and perform a four-momentum-constrained fit with χ² < 200. Tag candidates are selected with M_BC and ΔE windows, taking the best candidate by minimum |ΔE|. Yields are unfolded for bin migration in M_3π using an SVD method with L-curve regularization: 9 bins from 0.50–2.05 GeV/c² for D⁰ and 10 bins from 0.55–2.15 GeV/c² for D⁺, and the D⁰ and D⁺ results are combined for isospin-averaged partial branching fractions.
