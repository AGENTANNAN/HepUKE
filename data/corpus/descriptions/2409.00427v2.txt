Measure the Born cross section of e+e− → Ξ⁰Ξ̄⁰ with the single-baryon-tag method, Ξ⁰ → π⁰Λ, Λ → pπ⁻, π⁰ → γγ (charge conjugate included), using the 45 CM energy points from 3.51 to 4.95 GeV (~30 fb⁻¹ total; a representative subset of the scan points is listed) of real data with the matching inclusive MC, and generating 100k-event exclusive signal MC at every scan point (continuum production with a ψ(4260) top mother and flat phase space). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 and Vr<10, at least two positive and one negative track with net charge zero; photons with EMC timing 0–14, at least 10° away from any charged track, energy threshold 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), at least two photons. PID by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions and requiring at least one proton and one antiproton; the proton-identified tracks are then removed from the charged-track list and the remaining positive/negative tracks are taken as π+/π−. Reconstruct Λ → pπ⁻ with a secondary-vertex fit minimizing the mass difference (used tracks removed), and π⁰ → γγ with a 1C Kalman kinematic fit constraining the two-photon mass to m_π⁰ and χ²<20, requiring at least one π⁰. No kinematic fit is applied to the Ξ⁰, which is unmeasured: the Ξ⁰ candidate is chosen by minimizing |M(π⁰Λ)−m_Ξ⁰|, with the signal window |M(π⁰Λ)−m_Ξ⁰|<10 MeV/c² and the recoil-mass window |M_recoil−m_Ξ⁰|<60 MeV/c², the Λ window |M(pπ⁻)−m_Λ|<5 MeV/c², and a four-region two-dimensional sideband background estimate, all applied in the downstream ROOT stage.
