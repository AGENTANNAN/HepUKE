On data at √s = 4.918 and 4.950 GeV (plus the matching inclusive MC), study e+e− → anti-Λc− Λc(2625)+ with Λc(2625)+ → Λc+ π+π−, using partial reconstruction in which the anti-Λc− and its daughters are left unreconstructed; generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC at each energy point for each of three Λc+ tag modes, Λc+ → pK−π+, Λc+ → pKS0 (KS0 → π+π−), and Λc+ → Λπ+ (Λ → pπ−). Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm and net charge 0, with at least 4 positive and at least 2 negative tracks for the pK−π+ mode and at least 4 positive and 4 negative tracks for the two modes containing a secondary vertex; photons are accepted with EMC TDC in 0–14, energy above 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) and more than 10° from any track, with no minimum photon multiplicity. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut and explicit proton, kaon and pion separation. For the pKS0 and Λπ+ modes, a secondary-vertex fit of the π+π− or pπ− pair builds the KS0 or Λ by minimizing the mass difference and the used daughters are removed from the candidate list. The Λc(2625)+ is then formed by partial reconstruction, choosing the combination whose mass is closest to 2.62811 GeV/nucleus and requiring the recoil mass against the missing anti-Λc− to lie in 2.000–2.800 GeV; signal extraction (ΔM = M(Λc+π+π−) − M(Λc+)) is done downstream in ROOT.
