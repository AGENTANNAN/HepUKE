At √s = 3.097 GeV, study the doubly radiative decay J/ψ → γ η′ with η′ → γ γ π0 and π0 → γ γ, giving exactly five photons and no charged tracks. Use the J/ψ real data and inclusive MC at 3.097 GeV; for signal and background modelling generate exclusive MC of 1,000,000 events for J/ψ → γ η′, η′ → γ γ π0 and 500,000 events each for η′ → γ ω (ω → γ π0), η′ → π0 π0 η (η → γ γ), η′ → 3π0, J/ψ → γ π0 π0, and J/ψ → ω η (ω → γ π0, η → γ γ). Event selection requires zero charged tracks, so no |cosθ|, |Vz| or Vr track cuts are applied. Exactly five photon candidates are required with energy above 25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ| < 0.80) and above 50 MeV in the end-cap (0.86 < |cosθ| < 0.92), each more than 10° from any charged track. No charged-particle PID is needed because the final state is fully neutral. A 5C kinematic fit to the five photons is performed with four-momentum conservation and a π0 mass constraint on one photon pair, keeping the smallest-χ² combination and using a loose χ² < 200 in BOSS (the paper’s χ² < 30 is applied later outside BOSS). After the fit, the most energetic photon is taken as the radiative photon, the remaining two photons plus the π0 form the η′ candidate, and the signal window is |M(γ γ π0) − M_η′| < 25 MeV; events are vetoed if any photon pair other than the π0 candidate has |M(γ γ) − M_π0| < 18 MeV. The η′ → γ ω and nonresonant components are separated by a fit to M(γ π0) over 0.20–0.92 GeV.
