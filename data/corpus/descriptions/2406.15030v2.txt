On the 4.914 and 4.946 GeV data points, search for e+e− → φχc1(3872) with φ → K+K−, χc1(3872) → ρ0J/ψ, ρ0 → π+π−, and J/ψ → ℓ+ℓ− (ℓ=e or μ). Use the real data and inclusive MC at both energies, and generate 50k-event exclusive MC for the full signal chain. Reconstruct two event classes: a 6-track final state K+K−π+π−ℓ+ℓ− requiring three positive and three negative tracks with zero net charge, and a 5-track class corresponding to one missing kaon, requiring at least two positive and two negative tracks, five tracks total, and zero net charge. All charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; photons are selected with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, TDC 0–14, at least 10° from any track, and no minimum photon multiplicity. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut for K/π separation, while high-momentum tracks above 1.0 GeV are treated as leptons, classified as electrons if their EMC energy exceeds 0.6 GeV and otherwise as muons. Apply a 4C kinematic fit to K+K−π+π−ℓ+ℓ− for the 6-track class with χ²<150, and a 1C fit with a missing kaon for the 5-track class, testing both K+ and K− hypotheses and selecting the best χ², with χ²<20; the J/ψ and φ mass windows, |M(ℓ+ℓ−)−mJ/ψ|<0.04 GeV and |M(K+K−)−mφ|<0.015 GeV, are applied after the fit.
