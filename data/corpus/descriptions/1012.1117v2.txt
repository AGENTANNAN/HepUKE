On the J/ψ(3097), study J/ψ → γ η′, η′ → η π+π−, η → γγ. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and its inclusive MC; generate 500k exclusive signal MC events for the single mode J/ψ → γ η′ with η′ → η π+π− via Dalitz decay and η → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with EMC TDC 0–14, isolation angle ≥20° from charged tracks, energy threshold 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), at least three photons. PID by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, pions identified against kaons, requiring at least one π+. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γγγπ+π− using nominal masses, χ²<200, choosing the photon combination with minimum χ² among all photon candidates. The η is taken from the γγ pair whose invariant mass is closest to nominal mη, with an η signal window of 0.518–0.578 GeV/c² applied at the ROOT level; the same selection is applied to data, inclusive MC, and exclusive signal MC.
