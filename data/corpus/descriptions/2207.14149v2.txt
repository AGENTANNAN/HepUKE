On the seven c.m. energy points from 4.600 to 4.699 GeV (total 4.5 fb⁻¹), study Λc+ → Λ e+ νe with the double-tag method: ST Λc− → 14 hadronic tag modes (including pK−π+, Λπ−, Λπ−π+π−, pK−π+π−π+), DT Λc+ → Λ e+ νe, Λ → p π−. Use real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for the signal mode. Event selection: ST tags via M_BC and ΔE cuts (total ST yield 122,268 ± 474); DT requires Λ → p π− vertex fit with |M − M(Λ)| < 5 MeV, e+ identification with CL_e > 0.001, CL_e > CL_π, CL_e > CL_K, one e+, one proton, one π−, and a missing neutrino, with U_miss = E_miss − |p_miss| used for signal extraction. Perform a 4-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ² < 200. A 4D ML fit to q², cosθ_e, cosθ_p, χ within −0.06 < U_miss < 0.06 GeV extracts five form-factor parameters (α1_gperp, α1_fperp, r_f+, r_fperp, r_g+), yielding a0_gperp = 0.54 ± 0.04 ± 0.01 (χ²/ndof = 0.85). |V_cs| is determined as 0.936 ± 0.017(BF) ± 0.024(LQCD) ± 0.007(τ_Λc).
