At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), measure three charm decays by the double-tag method: D0 → η π+π− with η → γγ, tagged by an anti-D0 reconstructed in Kπ, Kππ0, and Kπππ modes; D+ → η η π+ with both η → γγ, tagged by D− in six hadronic modes (Kππ, K_Sπ, Kπππ0, K_Sππ0, K_Sπππ, and KKπ); and D+ → η π+π0 with η → γγ and π0 → γγ, using the same D− tag modes. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the three signal modes. Signal-side requirements are two photons plus one π+ and one π− for the D0 mode, four photons plus one π+ for the first D+ mode, and four photons plus one π+ for the second D+ mode. Complete the selection with a 4C kinematic fit with χ² < 200, applying an η mass constraint for D0 → η π+π−, two η mass constraints for D+ → η η π+, and η plus π0 mass constraints for D+ → η π+π0. The code does not encode separate charged-track |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr cuts, photon-energy thresholds, or a PID strategy; the tag reconstruction and the listed final-state particle multiplicities define the remaining selection.
