Search for lepton-number-violating D_s+ → h− h0 e+ e+ decays at c.m. energies 4.128–4.226 GeV (7.33 fb−1) with the single-tag method, requiring the recoil D_s− → K+K−π−; use the eight real data sets at 4.128/4.157/4.178/4.189/4.199/4.209/4.219/4.226 GeV and matching inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC (at 4.178 GeV) for each of six signal modes: φπ−e+e− (φ→K+K−), φK−e+e−, K_S0π−e+e−, K_S0K−e+e− (K_S0→π+π−), π−π0e+e−, and K−π0e+e− (π0→γγ). Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vxy<1 cm, with net charge +1 and mode-dependent multiplicities (e.g. ≥3 positive/≥2 negative for the φπ− mode, ≥2/≥2 for the K_S0 and π0 modes). Photons are accepted with E>25 MeV in the barrel or >50 MeV in the endcap, TDC 0–700 ns, and >10° from any track, with at least two photons for the π0 modes. PID uses the probability method (prob_cut 0.001) with K/π separation and e+ identified against K and π, requiring the appropriate numbers of K±, π±, and exactly two e+, and both positrons (except the φK− mode, which needs only one) must pass the L_e/(L_e+L_π+L_K)>0.8 electron criterion including an E/p cut. Neutral mesons are reconstructed via secondary-vertex fitting for K_S0 (M_π+π− in [0.487, 0.511] GeV/c², |Vz|<20 cm, L/σ_L>2) and via a Kalman/1C fit of the two photons to the π0 mass (χ²<50, M_γγ in [0.115, 0.150] GeV/c²), with φ selected in M_K+K− ∈ [1.00, 1.05] GeV/c². Each mode then undergoes a 4C kinematic fit on (h−, h0 or π0, e+) with χ²<200. Backgrounds are suppressed by cosθ(e+e−)<0.95, cosθ(e+π−)<0.98, L_π/σ_L<3 (K_S0 veto), L/σ_L>2 (fake-K_S0 veto) and E(π0)>0.17 GeV, with the M_rec–ΔM signal region optimized per energy point by Punzi FOM (not applied to the φK− mode).
