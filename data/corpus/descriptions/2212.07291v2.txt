At sqrt(s)=4.661, 4.682, 4.699, 4.740, 4.750, 4.781, 4.843, 4.918 and 4.951 GeV, study e+e− → ω X(3872), X(3872) → π+π− J/ψ, J/ψ → l+l− (l=e, μ), ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ; use the real data and inclusive MC at all nine points, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC per energy point from a ψ(4260)-mother decay card. Two topologies are treated with separate selections: a 6-track (all-charged) and a 5-track (one pion missed) chain. Charged tracks require |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with exactly 6 tracks and net charge 0 in the 6-track topology or 5 tracks in the 5-track topology; photons require TDC 0–14, 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold and >10° separation from tracks, at least 1 (6-track) or ≥2 (5-track). PID uses the probability method against π/K, with the two highest-momentum (>1 GeV/c) opposite-charge tracks taken as the J/ψ lepton pair, electrons identified by EMC>0.8 GeV and muons by EMC<0.4 GeV with at least one muon penetrating >3 MUC layers. For the 5-track chain a π0 is reconstructed from γγ with a mass constraint (χ²<200, ≥1 π0). Perform a 1C kinematic fit for the 6-track topology (recoil mass of 4π+ll against e+e− constrained to the π0 mass, χ²<15) and a 2C fit for the 5-track topology (γγ→π0 mass plus recoil mass of 3π+π0+ll to the π mass, χ²<25), both flagged nominal. Mass windows: J/ψ [3.07,3.13] GeV/c² with sidebands [2.96,3.05] and [3.15,3.24], η veto [0.52,0.58], ψ(2S) veto [3.680,3.692], ω [0.75,0.81], and X(3872) signal region [3.86,3.88] GeV/c².
