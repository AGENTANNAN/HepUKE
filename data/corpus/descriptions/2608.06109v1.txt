On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ η′, η′ → e± μ∓ (charge conjugate included). Use the real J/ψ data (≈9 billion events) and inclusive MC; generate 600k-event exclusive MC for the signal. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel, |cosθ|<0.80) / 50 MeV (endcap, 0.86<|cosθ|<0.92) energy threshold, shower time in [0,700] ns, angle to closest track >10°, at least one photon. PID by the probability method, high-momentum tracks (p>0.5 GeV/c) treated as leptons, electron candidates requiring P_e(e)/(P_e(e)+P_e(π)+P_e(K))>0.8, P_e(e)>0.001, E_EMC/p>0.8 and dE/dx cuts, muon candidates requiring dE/dx, 0.1<E_EMC<0.3 GeV and MUC penetration depth, with exactly one positive and one negative lepton. The highest-energy photon (~1.4 GeV) is taken as the radiative photon, and a 4C kinematic fit (γ e μ) is performed with χ²<200 in BOSS (tightened to χ²<100 in ROOT); a photon recoil mass window of (0.932, 0.982) GeV/c² (5σ around the η′ mass) is applied in ROOT.
