On ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → 3K_S^0 K^±π^∓ (J=0,1,2), with K_S^0 → π+π−. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate one 500k-event exclusive MC sample for the three χ_cJ signal modes with the same decay chain. Event selection: at least eight charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; at least one photon with 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC TDC 0–14, and at least 10° from any charged track. Apply probability-based PID with π/K separation, identifying K+ against π−, K− against π+, π+ against K−, and π− against K+ (zero probability cut), and reconstruct K_S^0 from two oppositely charged tracks without PID, with |Vz|<20 cm, common vertex χ²<200, decay length >2σ from the IP, and a π+π− mass window |M−0.498|<0.012 GeV/c² (3σ); sidebands 0.020<|M−0.498|<0.044 GeV/c² are used for background subtraction. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γ K_S^0 K_S^0 K_S^0 K+π− with χ²<50, taking the radiative photon as the highest-energy photon candidate and choosing the best combination by minimum χ², and require the three K_S^0 candidates to lie in the signal mass region.
