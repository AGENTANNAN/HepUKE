Measure the cross section of e+e− → η φ, with φ → K+K− and η → γγ, at 23 centre-of-mass energies from 3.773 to 4.600 GeV. Use the corresponding real data and matching inclusive MC at each energy point, together with ConExc-generated exclusive signal MC (mode 23, vpho → φ η) of 500k events per point. Event selection requires exactly two charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, one positive and one negative, net charge zero, and at least two photons with EMC timing 0–700 ns and energy thresholds of 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap). Kaons are identified by the probability method against π and p (CL(K)>CL(π), CL(K)>CL(p), probability cut 0.001), requiring one K+ and one K−. A 4C kinematic fit to K+K−γγ with four-momentum conservation is performed with χ²<100, flagged nominal. The φ is selected with |M_KK − M_φ| < 9.8 MeV (≈2σ) and the η by requiring the γγ opening angle < 1.0 rad for 0.4 < M_γγ < 0.5 GeV to suppress ISR φγ background; the signal yield is extracted from an unbinned fit to M_γγ.
