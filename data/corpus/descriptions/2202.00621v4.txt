On the J/ψ(3097), study J/ψ → γηη′ with η → γγ and two η′ decay modes: η′ → ηπ+π− (η → γγ), giving a 5γπ+π− final state, and η′ → γπ+π−, giving a 4γπ+π− final state. Use the 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data sample 708_3097, corresponding to 10.09×10^9 J/ψ events, and its inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two η′ modes. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, and net charge 0. Photons must satisfy EMC TDC 0–14 and be more than 10° from any track, with barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, and at least five photons in the ηπ+π− mode or at least four in the γπ+π− mode. Pions are identified by the probability method with prob>0.001, π+ and π− against K and proton, requiring one π+ and one π−; no lepton identification is imposed. η candidates are reconstructed from γγ with the invariant mass constrained to the nominal η mass and χ²<25, requiring at least two η in the ηπ+π− mode and at least one in the γπ+π− mode, and η′ candidates are formed with best-combination selection in the relevant ηπ+π− or γπ+π− invariant-mass window. A 5C kinematic fit to γηηπ+π− (Mode I) or a 4C fit to γγηπ+π− (Mode II) is then applied, each with four-momentum conservation, nominal masses, and χ²<200.
