At ψ(3770), perform a double-tag analysis of D0 → K+K−π0π0 against three anti-D0 tag modes: K+π−, K+π−π0, and K+π−π−π+. Use the 712_3773 ψ(3770) real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive MC events for the signal decay D0 → K+K−π0π0, with the generator card decaying the anti-D0 to K+π−. On the signal side require one K+, one K−, net charge zero, and exactly four photons with energy above 25 MeV and photon opening angle above 10°; no explicit |cosθ|, |Vz|, or Vr cuts are encoded, and no specific PID method is specified beyond the kaon/pion content. Fit each candidate with a four-momentum constraint plus two π0 mass constraints to the nominal π0 mass, with χ² < 200. In addition, π0 candidates must satisfy 0.115 < M(γγ) < 0.150 GeV/c² with at least one barrel photon and a 1C Kalman π0 fit χ² < 50; veto Ks0 by excluding M(π0π0) in [0.460, 0.525] GeV/c² and require the D0–anti-D0 opening angle > 167°. Tag-side ΔE windows are K+π−: (−0.025, 0.025) GeV, K+π−π0: (−0.055, 0.040) GeV, K+π−π−π+: (−0.025, 0.025) GeV, while the signal-side ΔE window is (−0.020, 0.020) GeV; best candidates are chosen by minimum |ΔE|. The amplitude-analysis region is 1.861 < MBC < 1.870 GeV/c² for both signal and tag.
