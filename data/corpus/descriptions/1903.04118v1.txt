At √s = 4.178 GeV, using the 3.19 fb⁻¹ real data set plus inclusive MC, perform a double-tag analysis of D_s⁺ → π⁺ π⁰ η with the tag D_s⁻ reconstructed in seven single-tag modes (K_S K⁻, K⁺K⁻π⁻, K_S K⁻π⁰, K⁺K⁻π⁻π⁰, K_S K⁺π⁻π⁻, π⁻η, π⁻η′), the tag side taken as charm −1 and the signal side D_s⁺ → π⁺ π⁰ η with π⁰→γγ and η→γγ giving four photons, exactly one π⁺ and net charge +1. Generate exclusive MC with the conexc generator using a D*⁻ → γ D_s⁻, D_s⁺ → π⁺ π⁰ η phase-space decay card. Perform a 7C kinematic fit combining four-momentum conservation with constraints on the π⁰ and η invariant masses (each from the appropriate photon pair) and the D_s mass, including the direct photon from D*⁻ → γ D_s⁻, selecting the photon giving the smallest 7C χ² as γ_dir, with a χ² cut of 1000 and fitted momenta stored; the 7CA variant additionally constrains γ_dir plus D_s± to the D_s* mass. A BDT using M(γγ)_η, the low-energy photon momentum and p(γ_dir) is applied later in ROOT, retaining 77.8% of signal and rejecting 73.4% of background, events with cos θ_η < 0.998 are vetoed, and the amplitude analysis is run on the accepted DT candidates.
