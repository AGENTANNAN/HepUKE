Measure the cross sections for e+e− → p p̄ η and e+e− → p p̄ ω at the 17 BESIII scan points from 3.7730 to 4.5995 GeV, using the real data and matching inclusive MC at each energy. Generate 100k-event exclusive MC at every point for both modes: e+e− → p p̄ η via ConExc continuum mode 51 with η → γγ, and e+e− → p p̄ ω via KKMC plus ψ(4260) with ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ. For both channels, select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm, and photons with energy thresholds of 25 MeV in the barrel and 50 MeV in the endcap, EMC timing 0–14, at least 10° away from any track, and at least two photons. The η channel requires one positive and one negative track with net charge zero and, using probability PID with a 0.001 cut, one proton and one antiproton, separating protons from kaons and pions. The ω channel requires two positive and two negative tracks with net charge zero and, with the same PID, one p, one p̄, one π+, and one π−, separating protons and pions from kaons. In the η channel, constrain the two photons to the η nominal mass (χ²<25, at least one η), then perform a 4C kinematic fit to p p̄ η with nominal masses (χ²<200), and apply |M(γγ)−mη|<3σ. In the ω channel, constrain the two photons to the π0 mass (χ²<25, at least one π0), then perform the 4C fit to p p̄ π+π−π0 with nominal masses (χ²<200), and apply |M(π+π−π0)−mω|<30 MeV/c²; K_S0 → π+π− and Λ → pπ− peaking backgrounds are vetoed, and the signal is extracted from a simultaneous fit to the M(p p̄) recoil-mass spectra.
