Study e+e− → Λ Σ0 + c.c. at the 14 BESIII scan points from 2.3094 to 3.0800 GeV, using the real data and matching inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive ConExc MC per point for e+e− → Λ Σ0 + c.c. with Λ → pπ−, Σ0 → γΛ, and the charge-conjugate decays. At the near-threshold 2.3094 GeV point use the indirect method: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track; probability PID (dE/dx only, prob>0.001) identifies pions against kaons and protons and requires exactly one π+ and one π−. Require a two-pion vertex within 2 cm of the beam line, π− momentum in 0.08–0.12 GeV/c, and at least two additional secondary tracks from a common vertex 1–5 cm from the beam line (antiproton annihilation), with the signal extracted from the π+ momentum fit. At the other 13 points use the single-Λ tag: tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<30 cm, Vr<10 cm, at least one positive and one negative track; probability PID (prob>0.001) requires exactly one proton (against kaon/pion) and one π− (against kaon). Reconstruct Λ → pπ− with a secondary-vertex fit (decay length >2σ_vtx, summed χ²<50), build the Λ candidate by minimizing the mass difference in the window [1.11,1.12] GeV/c², and infer the Σ0 from the recoil mass using E_Σ0 = (s + m_Σ0² − m_Λ²)/(2√s); no kinematic fit is applied, and yields are extracted from unbinned maximum-likelihood fits to the recoil-mass spectrum including ΛΛ and Σ0Σ0 backgrounds and Λ sidebands at [1.095,1.105] and [1.125,1.135] GeV/c².
