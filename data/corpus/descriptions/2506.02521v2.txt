At ψ(3770) (√s = 3.773 GeV), using the 20.3 fb⁻¹ real data and inclusive MC, measure the double-tag decays D⁺ → η ℓ⁺ ν_ℓ (ℓ = e, μ) with six hadronic D⁻ tag modes: K⁺π⁻π⁻, K_Sπ⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰, K_Sπ⁻π⁰, K_Sπ⁺π⁻π⁻, and K⁺K⁻π⁻. Generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the four signal modes—D⁺ → η(γγ)e⁺ν_e, D⁺ → η(π⁺π⁻π⁰)e⁺ν_e, D⁺ → η(γγ)μ⁺ν_μ, and D⁺ → η(π⁺π⁻π⁰)μ⁺ν_μ—with the D⁻ decaying through the same six tag modes. The signal side requires two photons with no explicit signal-photon energy threshold, a missing neutrino, and either one e⁺ (γγ or 3π), one μ⁺ (γγ or 3π), or in the 3π modes additionally one π⁺ and one π⁻; no explicit |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr, or net-charge cuts are encoded, but extra good charged tracks are vetoed and the maximum extra photon energy must be below 0.25 GeV, with no extra π⁰ allowed in the muon channel. Electron PID uses L_e > 0.8(L_e+L_π+L_K), L_e > 0.001, and E_EMC/p > 0.8; muon PID uses L_μ > L_K, L_μ > L_e, L_μ > 0.001, E_EMC ∈ (0.101, 0.282) GeV, and probability thresholds prob_μ ≥ 0.001, prob_μ > prob_e, prob_μ > prob_K, and prob_μ/(prob_μ+prob_π+prob_K) ≥ 0.43. A kinematic fit constrains four-momentum conservation with χ² < 200 and, for the γγ modes, also constrains the γγ invariant mass to the nominal η mass; the η windows are M(γγ) ∈ (0.50, 0.57) GeV/c² and M(π⁺π⁻π⁰) ∈ (0.53, 0.57) GeV/c², while the muon channel vetoes M(ημ⁺) < 1.722 (γγ) or < 1.710 (3π) GeV/c² against D⁺ → ηπ⁺ peaking background. Tag-side ST yields come from an M_BC fit in the D⁻ window (1.863, 1.877) GeV/c², and the DT signal is extracted from an unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to U_miss ≡ E_miss − |p_miss|c, with the D⁺ momentum reconstructed as p_D⁺ = −p_D⁻ × sqrt(E_beam²/c² − m_D⁻²c²).
