At the J/ψ(3.097 GeV) resonance, measure the absolute branching fractions of J/ψ → γη′ via five exclusive η′ decay modes. Use the 1310.6×10⁶-event J/ψ real-data sample together with its inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of η′→γπ+π−, η′→ηπ+π− (η→γγ), η′→ηπ0π0 (η,π0→γγ), η′→γω (ω→π+π−π0, π0→γγ) and η′→γγ. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm and net charge 0, with exactly one π+ and one π− in the charged modes, while photons must satisfy TDC 0–14, be more than 10° from any track, and pass 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) thresholds, with at least 2 (γπ+π−), 3 (ηπ+π−, γω, γγ) or 5 (ηπ0π0) photons. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut and π/K separation, requiring 1 π+ and 1 π− where charged pions are present. Kinematic fitting: the modes containing an η or π0 first apply a Kalman fit constraining γγ pairs to the η or π0 nominal mass with χ²<25, requiring ≥1 η (ηπ+π−), ≥1 η and ≥2 π0 (ηπ0π0), or ≥1 π0 (γω); every mode then undergoes a 4C kinematic fit with nominal constraints and χ²<200 on γγπ+π−, γηπ+π−, γηπ0π0, γγπ+π−π0, or γγγ. The inclusive η′ tag uses the photon-conversion method (the radiative photon from J/ψ→γη′ converting to e+e−, with a π0 conversion veto), selecting the highest-energy photon (E≈1.4 GeV) as the radiative candidate, normalized with BF(J/ψ→γη′)=(5.27±0.03±0.05)×10⁻³ and an efficiency correction of 1.0085±0.0050.
