At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → ω η π⁺π⁻ with ω → π⁺π⁻π⁰, η → γγ and π⁰ → γγ, to investigate the f₁(1285), η(1405) and X(1870) in the ηπ⁺π⁻ system. Use the J/ψ real data and the corresponding inclusive MC (2×10⁸ J/ψ); generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC for the ωηπ⁺π⁻ Dalitz chain, a 2M-event phase-space MC of the same final state without the ω resonance (for the phase-space correction curve), and 100k-event exclusive MC for the peaking b₁(1235)a₀(980) background (b₁ → ωπ, a₀ → ηπ). Event selection: four charged tracks (2 positive, 2 negative, net charge 0) with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 20 cm, Vr < 2 cm, all treated as pions with no PID; at least four photons with EMC energy > 25 MeV (barrel) / > 50 MeV (endcap), TDC 0–14, and at least 10° from any track. Two Kalman fits to γγ pairs constrain one pair to the π⁰ mass and another to the η mass, each with χ² < 25, requiring at least one π⁰ and one η; then a nominal 4C kinematic fit on π⁺π⁻π⁺π⁻π⁰η with an IP-constrained vertex fit of the four charged tracks (χ²_V < 100) and a four-momentum constraint, with χ²_4C < 50. Post-fit, apply γγ windows η (524–572) MeV and π⁰ (122–148) MeV, choose the ω candidate minimizing |M(π⁺π⁻π⁰) − m_ω| and requiring it below 28 MeV, and retain the combination with the smallest χ²_4C; background is estimated from ω and a₀(980) mass sidebands and the b₁(1235)a₀(980) peaking background is vetoed.
