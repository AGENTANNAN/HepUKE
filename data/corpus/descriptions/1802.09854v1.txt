At the ψ(3686) (E_c.m.=3.686 GeV), study ψ(3686)→π+π−J/ψ with J/ψ→3γ via J/ψ→γη, η→γγ, for two-photon decays of pseudoscalar mesons. Use the ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive MC for ψ(2S)→π+π−J/ψ, J/ψ→γη, η→γγ. Event selection: require exactly one positive and one negative charged track with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, assigned as π+ and π− without further PID; photons must have EMC TDC 0–14, energy >25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), be >15° from any track, with at least three photons. Perform a 4-momentum-constrained kinematic fit to π+π−γγγ with χ²<50, nominal. After the fit, apply |M(3γ)−m_J/ψ|<0.08 GeV/c²; extract π⁰/η/η′/η(1405)/η(1475)/η(1760)/X(1835) signals from M(γγ) using unbinned ML fits with sideband subtraction and angular reweighting, and subtract the J/ψ→γπ⁰π⁰ peaking background estimated from dedicated MC.
