At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV) we search for two Cabibbo-suppressed/favored channels: J/ψ → Ds− ρ+ with Ds− → φ e− νe, φ → K+K−, ρ+ → π+ π0, π0 → γγ, and J/ψ → D0bar K*0bar with D0bar → K+ e− νe and K*0bar → K− π+. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus 600k-event exclusive MC for each of the two decay modes. Event selection requires four charged tracks with net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm and Vr<2 cm, and photons with EMC timing 0–700 ns, more than 20° from any track, and energy thresholds of 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), with at least two photons in mode 1. PID uses the probability method (cut 0.001): high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) are treated as leptons (electron if EMC E>0.6), with K/π separation against protons, requiring at least one K+, one K−, one π+, and one e−; electron candidates additionally need E/p>0.8 and |cosθ|<0.8, and missing momentum must exceed 0.1 GeV/c with |Umiss|<0.05 GeV (mode 1) or <0.02 GeV (mode 2). In mode 1, a Kalman fit to the two photons constrains them to the nominal π0 mass (χ²<200, ≥1 π0), followed by a kinematic fit to K+K−π+π0e− with a missing ν, four-momentum constraint, φ mass window 1.01–1.03 GeV, ρ+ mass window 0.62–0.95 GeV, and χ²<200. In mode 2, the γγ Kalman fit to the π0 mass uses χ²<20 as a veto against π0 backgrounds, then a kinematic fit to K+K−π+e− with a missing ν, four-momentum constraint, K*0bar mass window 0.82–0.98 GeV, and χ²<200.
