Measure the branching fractions of Λc+ → Σ+ η and Λc+ → Σ+ η' relative to the reference modes Λc+ → Σ+ π0 and Λc+ → Σ+ ω (ω → π+π−π0), with Σ+ → p π0 and π0 → γγ, using seven e+e− data points from 4.600 to 4.699 GeV (≈4.5 fb⁻¹) together with the matching inclusive MC samples. Generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each signal mode, all decaying via Λc+ → Σ+ η(η'), Σ+ → pπ0: Σ+η with η→γγ, Σ+η with η→π+π−π0, and Σ+η' with η'→π+π−η, η→γγ. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with at least one positive and one negative track for the η→γγ final state and at least two of each when three charged pions are present. Photons must satisfy an EMC energy threshold of 25 MeV in the barrel or 50 MeV in the endcap, lie within the 0–14 TDC window and be more than 10° from any track, with at least four photons required. Particle identification uses the probability method with a probability cut of 0.0, separating protons against kaons/pions, kaons against pions and pions against kaons. Intermediate γγ pairs are constrained to the π0 mass (χ²<100) and, where applicable, to the η mass (χ²<100), followed by a 4C fit on the pπ0 plus η(η') system with the pπ0 invariant mass constrained to the Σ+ nominal mass, using χ²<17 for both Σ+η modes and χ²<30 for Σ+η'. The signal windows are M(pπ0) ∈ (1.174, 1.200) GeV/c² for Σ+, M(γγ) ∈ (0.500, 0.560) GeV/c² for η→γγ, M(π+π−π0) ∈ (0.535, 0.560) GeV/c² for η→3π, and M(π+π−η) ∈ (0.946, 0.968) GeV/c² for η', with a pK_S^0 veto rejecting M(π0π0) in (0.440, 0.520) GeV/c².
