Study ψ(3686) → γ η_c(2S), with η_c(2S) → K_S^0 K^± π^∓ and η_c(2S) → K^+ K^- π^0. Use 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data (106 M events, 156 pb^-1) and 3.65 GeV continuum data (42 pb^-1), together with inclusive MC for both energies; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each signal mode (K_S^0 K^+ π^-, K_S^0 K^- π^+, K^+ K^- π^0) and for background modes including fake-photon K_S^0 K π and K K π^0, FSR K_S^0 K π, ω K^+ K^-, π^0 K_S^0 K π, π^0 K^+ K^- π^0, π^+π^- J/ψ with J/ψ → K^+K^-/leptons, η J/ψ with η → 3π/γγ, and continuum γ* → K_S^0 K π. Charged tracks must have |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm; the K_S^0 K^± π^∓ modes require exactly two positive and two negative tracks with net charge zero, while the K^+ K^- π^0 mode requires exactly one positive and one negative track with net charge zero. Photons must have E > 25 MeV in the barrel or > 50 MeV in the endcap, EMC timing in [0,14], be at least 10° from any charged track, and number at least one (K_S^0 modes) or at least three (K^+K^-π^0 mode). PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut and kaon identification against π/p, requiring at least one K^+ and one K^- for the K_S^0 modes or exactly one K^+ and one K^- for the K^+K^-π^0 mode. For the K_S^0 modes, K_S^0 → π^+π^- candidates are formed from all opposite-charge pairs by the best secondary-vertex fit, with |M(π^+π^-) − m(K_S^0)| < 7 MeV/c^2 and a vertex at least 0.5 cm from the IP, the remaining tracks must not form another good K_S^0, and the recoil mass of all π^+π^- pairs is required to be < 3.05 GeV/c^2. A 4C kinematic fit of γ K_S^0 K π is performed with a loose χ² < 200 (the paper requires χ²_4C < 50 later), selecting the best combination by χ² = χ²_4C + χ²_PID(K) + χ²_PID(π), while the K^+K^-π^0 mode uses a 5C fit of γ K^+ K^- γγ with the π^0 mass constrained, loose χ² < 200 (paper requires χ²_5C < 30), choosing the best photon and π^0 by smallest χ²_5C; an invariant mass of the two charged tracks under the muon hypothesis < 2.9 GeV/c^2 vetoes J/ψ and η J/ψ backgrounds.
