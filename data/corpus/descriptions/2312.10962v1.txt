On the BESIII energy scan from 3.51 to 4.95 GeV, study e+e− → K_S0 K_L0, with K_S0 → π+π− and K_L0 inferred from missing momentum, using the ConExc generator for the continuum and ψ(3770). Use real data and inclusive MC at the scan points (representative sets at 3.773, 4.180, 4.230, 4.914, and 4.946 GeV, from the full ~50-point scan); generate matching exclusive MC at the same points with e+e− → K_S0 K_L0 in the VSS model and K_S0 → π+π− phase space. Select events with exactly two charged tracks of net charge zero (effectively one π+ and one π−; no additional track |cosθ|, |Vz|, or Vr cuts are encoded), photons with at least 25 MeV and at least 20° from charged tracks, and pion identification by the probability method requiring both tracks to be π± over K±, p±, e±, and μ± with probability >0.001, plus electron rejection via L(π)>L(e) and E/p < 1.2 GeV/(GeV/c). Reconstruct K_S0 through a secondary-vertex fit to the π+π− pair with mass window 0.478–0.518 GeV/c², decay length > 2 cm, and χ² < 15, then apply a 1C kinematic fit constraining the π+π− mass to the K_S0 nominal mass with χ² < 12. K_L0 is not explicitly reconstructed but is identified by missing momentum; neutral clusters in a 20° cone opposite the K_S0 direction with second moment > 20 cm² are used as a validation check. Suppress backgrounds by rejecting any γγ combination with M(γγ) ∈ 0.123–0.144 GeV/c², requiring total neutral energy outside the K_L0 cone below 0.2 GeV against K*0(892)K0+c.c., and accepting EMC timing within 0–700 ns; signal is extracted in ROOT from X = E(K_S0)/E_beam in [0.98,1.02] with an unbinned likelihood fit including signal, e+e−→γISR ψ(3686), K*0(892)K0+c.c., and exponential continuum, convolved with a Gaussian for data–MC resolution.
