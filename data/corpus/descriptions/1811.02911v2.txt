On the 4.178 GeV data set (3.19 fb⁻¹), search for the semileptonic D_s⁺ decays D_s⁺ → K⁰e⁺ν_e (K⁰ → K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻) and D_s⁺ → K*⁰e⁺ν_e (K*⁰ → K⁺π⁻), produced via e⁺e⁻ → D_s*⁺D_s⁻ with D_s*⁺ → γD_s⁺. Use the real 4180 data and its inclusive MC, and generate 2M-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes. Reconstruct the tag side as a single D_s⁻ in any of 13 hadronic ST modes (K_S K, KKπ, K_S Kπ⁰, KKππ⁰, K_S K⁻ππ, πππ, πη, ππ⁰η, πe⁺ππη, πe⁺ρ γ and related). On the signal side require one π⁺, one π⁻ and one e⁺ (or one K⁺, one π⁻ and one e⁺ for the K*⁰ mode) with net charge +1, and treat the neutrino as missing. Apply a 4-momentum (4C) kinematic constraint with χ² < 200. Both modes are processed with the same selection chain and run over data, inclusive MC and their respective exclusive MC samples.
