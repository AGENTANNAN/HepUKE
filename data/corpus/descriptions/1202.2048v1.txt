On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV) and ψ(2S) (3.686 GeV) resonances, study J/ψ → π+π−π0 and ψ′ → π+π−π0 using the corresponding real data samples (2.25×10^8 J/ψ and 1.06×10^8 ψ′ events) together with inclusive MC, and generate 1M-event exclusive MC for each mode: J/ψ → ρ0π0 with ρ0 → π+π− and π0 → γγ, and ψ′ → π+π−π0 phase space with π0 → γγ. Event selection requires exactly one positive and one negative charged track with net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, plus at least two photons with EMC timing 0–14, more than 10° from any track, and energy thresholds of 25 MeV in the barrel and 50 MeV in the endcap. The charged tracks are assigned as π+π− without hadron PID; for the ψ′ sample, charged tracks with associated EMC energy above 0.8 GeV are removed to suppress electrons, and analysis-level vetoes require MUC penetration depth less than 40 cm and m(π+π−)<3 GeV/c^2. A Kalman fit constrains the photon pair to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<50 and requires at least one π0, keeping the best pair, after which the event is fitted with the nominal π+π−π0 hypothesis using a 4C four-momentum constraint plus the π0 mass constraint, i.e. a 5C fit with χ²<50, and a competing K+K−π0 fit is performed so that events with a smaller kaon-hypothesis χ² are rejected from the stored χ². The π0 candidate is finally required to satisfy 0.11 < m(γγ) < 0.15 GeV/c^2 after the fit. Both J/ψ and ψ′ selections share the same chain, except for the additional ψ′ electron, muon and π+π− mass vetoes.
