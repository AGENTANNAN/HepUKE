At ψ(3770), 3.773 GeV, study D0 → ρ− μ+ ν_μ with ρ− → π− π0 using a double-tag (ST + missing) technique, where the tag side is a hadronic anti-D0 (charm −1) reconstructed in three modes: K+π−, K+π−π0, and K+π−π−π+. Use the 712_3773 ψ(3770) real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1,000,000 exclusive-MC events for ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar with D0 → ρ− μ+ ν_μ, ρ− → π−π0. The tag-side selection requires ΔE ∈ (−0.055, 0.040) GeV for Kππ0 and (−0.025, 0.025) GeV for Kπ and Kπππ, M_BC ∈ (1.859, 1.873) GeV/c², choosing the best candidate per mode per charge by minimum |ΔE|. On the signal side require two photons (π0 → γγ), one π− and one μ+, net charge 0, and a massless missing ν_μ, with M(π−π0) ∈ (0.625, 0.925) GeV/c²; identify muons using dE/dx+TOF+EMC probabilities (no MUC) via CL_μ > 0.001, CL_μ > CL_e, CL_μ > CL_K, with no μ/π requirement, and require E_μ,EMC ∈ (0.125, 0.275) GeV. Veto K_S^0 by M(π−π+) outside 0.458–0.538 GeV/c² and recoil M(π+π−) outside 0.468–0.528 GeV/c², require M(ρ−μ+) < 1.5 GeV/c², extra-photon energy < 0.25 GeV, |M²_miss(π→K)| > 0.05 GeV²/c⁴, and no extra charged track or π0. Perform a kinematic fit constraining the tag plus signal (π−, μ+, 2γ→π0) plus ν_μ to the ψ(3770) four-momentum, with M(γγ) constrained to the nominal π0 mass, χ² cut 200, and extract the signal from the missing-mass-squared distribution.
