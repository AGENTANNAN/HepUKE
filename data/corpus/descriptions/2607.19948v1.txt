At the J/ψ energy point (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → p pbar with possible secondary elastic pp scattering on beam-pipe material. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive signal-MC events for J/ψ → p+ anti-p− phase space, with Geant4 simulation applying secondary scattering of the generated proton on beam-pipe material. Event selection uses only charged tracks: |cosθ| < 0.93, exactly three tracks, two positive and one negative; no photon, Vz/Vr, or global nC kinematic-fit criterion is encoded. Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions and requiring at least two proton candidates and at least one antiproton. A secondary elastic pp-scattering vertex fit is performed on the two final-state protons, requiring the vertex at Rxy 3.0–3.5 cm (mineral-oil layer) or 6.0–6.8 cm (MDC inner wall), i.e. ±4σ around the two scattering surfaces. The incident proton is reconstructed by recoil as p_p = p_e+ + p_e− − p_pbar, the target proton from four-momentum conservation, with |p_target| < 50 MeV/c to suppress quasi-free nuclear collisions and an antiproton recoil-mass window 0.85–1.02 GeV/c². Proton spin precession in the 1.0 T solenoid between production and the scattering vertex (Rxy = 3.2 or 6.3 cm) is simulated with Geant4 and treated as a helicity-frame rotation, and the SAID analyzing power A_N(theta) is applied event-by-event in the downstream unbinned maximum-likelihood fit of sin(ΔΦ).
