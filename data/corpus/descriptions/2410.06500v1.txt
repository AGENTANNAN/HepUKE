On the ψ(3770) at √s = 3.773 GeV, search for D+ → γρ+ with ρ+ → π+π0 and D+ → γK*+ with K*+ → K+π0 (π0 → γγ) using a double-tag technique. Use the 20.3 fb−1 real data at 3.773 GeV and its inclusive MC, and generate 1 million exclusive MC events for each signal mode, with the tag D− decaying to K+π−π−. The tag side selects six hadronic D modes—D → Kππ, Kπππ0, KKπ, K_Sπ, K_Sππ, K_Sπππ—with charm −1, applying the standard TagAnalysis charged-track quality cuts (|cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm), 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap photon thresholds, and probability-based PID. The signal side requires three photons with a minimum photon angle of 10°, plus exactly one π+ for the ρ+ mode or exactly one K+ for the K*+ mode. A kinematic fit imposes a 4C constraint and constrains the two photons from π0 to the nominal π0 mass, with χ² < 200. The two signal modes use the same tag-side selection and fit procedure, each run over data, inclusive MC, and its exclusive MC.
