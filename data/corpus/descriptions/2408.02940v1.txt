On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ η_c(2S) with η_c(2S) → K+K−η, η → γγ, using the 2.712-billion-event ψ(3686) real data set and its inclusive MC, together with 3.650 GeV continuum real data and inclusive MC; generate a 100k-event exclusive phase-space MC sample for the full signal chain (ψ(2S) → γ η_c(2S), η_c(2S) → K+K−η, η → γγ). Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with exactly two tracks of net charge zero and at least one positive and one negative. Photons must have energy above 25 MeV in the barrel and 40 MeV in the endcap (the low threshold retaining the M1 radiative photon), lie more than 10° from any track, satisfy the EMC TDC window 0–14, and number between 3 and 6. Kaons are identified by the probability method (prob>0.001) against pions and protons, requiring exactly one K+ and one K−. A 4C kinematic fit of K+K−γγγ is applied with χ²<20, alongside a competing 4C fit of the K+K−γγγγ hypothesis to veto ψ(3686) → γγ K+K−η via χ²(3γ)<χ²(4γ), and a nominal 5C fit imposing the η mass on the two photons with χ²<200; the η signal window is 0.51<M(γγ)<0.57 GeV/c², the lowest-energy photon is taken as the radiative photon, and further background suppression uses a 2D π⁰ veto, an η′ photon-energy veto outside [0.156,0.196] GeV, M(3γ)>0.6 GeV/c², M(K+K−)<3.0 GeV/c² and outside [1.007,1.033] GeV/c², and track momentum ≤2 GeV/c.
