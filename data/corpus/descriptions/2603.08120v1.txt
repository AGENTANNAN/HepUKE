On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ η′, η′ → e⁺e⁻ ω, ω → π⁺π⁻π⁰, π⁰ → γγ. Use the 708_3097 real data set together with its inclusive MC, and generate a 5M-event exclusive signal MC sample with J/ψ → γη′ (helicity amplitude), η′ → e⁺e⁻ω, ω → π⁺π⁻π⁰ (DALITZ), π⁰ → γγ. Event selection: four charged tracks with |cosθ|≤0.93, Vr<2 cm, |Vz|<20 cm, two positive and two negative, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–700 ns, more than 10° from any track, and at least three photons. PID uses the probability method, requiring at least 1 π⁺, 1 π⁻, 1 e⁺, 1 e⁻, with high-momentum tracks (p>1.0) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6, else muon) and π/K/e separation. A Kalman fit constrains two photons to the π⁰ nominal mass (χ²<200, ≥1 π⁰), then a nominal 4C fit on γ e⁺e⁻ π⁺π⁻ π⁰ is applied with χ²<70 on the combined 4C+PID χ², selecting the best combination by minimum χ², alongside a competing 4C fit to J/ψ → π⁺π⁻π⁺π⁻γγγ whose χ² is stored and used to reject events where it is smaller (e/π mis-ID veto). Additional requirements: the most energetic photon must have E>1 GeV; |M(γγ)−m_π⁰|<0.015 GeV/c² for the two lowest-energy photons; a photon-conversion veto (retain Rxy<2 cm, otherwise reject if cosθ_eg>0 and −1.0<Δxy<0.8 cm); suppression of π⁰→γe⁺e⁻; and a veto on 0.52<M(γe⁺e⁻)<0.58 GeV/c² with M(γe⁺e⁻)<0.5 GeV/c² against η→γe⁺e⁻. The η′ candidate M(π⁺π⁻π⁰e⁺e⁻) must lie in (0.90, 1.02) GeV/c², tightened for the TFF fit to [0.94, 0.98] GeV/c² with M(π⁺π⁻π⁰e⁺e⁻)−M(π⁺π⁻π⁰) in [0.14, 0.20] GeV/c².
