On the ψ(3770), ψ(3686) and J/ψ, study six exclusive charmonium channels: ψ(3770) → ΛΛ̄π⁺π⁻, ΛΛ̄π⁰, ΛΛ̄η, Σ⁺Σ̄⁻, Σ⁰Σ̄⁰, Ξ⁻Ξ̄⁺ and Ξ⁰Ξ̄⁰; ψ(3770) → γη_c(2S) with η_c(2S) → K_S⁰K±π∓; ψ(3770) → γχ_c1,2 with χ_cJ → γJ/ψ, J/ψ → e⁺e⁻ or μ⁺μ⁻; ψ(3686) → γχ_c0,2 with χ_c0,2 → π⁰η_c, η_c → K_S⁰K±π∓; ψ(3686) → π⁺π⁻J/ψ with J/ψ → γγ or γφ, φ → K⁺K⁻; and J/ψ → π⁰φ. Use 2.92 fb⁻¹ of ψ(3770), 106 M ψ(3686) and 1.31 B J/ψ real data, with 42 pb⁻¹ of 3.65 GeV continuum for QED background and inclusive MC at each energy. Generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each decay mode — the seven baryonic channels, γη_c(2S), γχ_c1→γe⁺e⁻, γχ_c1→γμ⁺μ⁻, γχ_c2→γe⁺e⁻, χ_c0→π⁰η_c, χ_c2→π⁰η_c, π⁺π⁻J/ψ→γγ and π⁺π⁻J/ψ→γφ — and 500k for J/ψ→π⁰φ. All charged tracks require |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge 0, and photons require EMC timing 0–14, ≥20° from any track, and E>25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), with multiplicities set per mode. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut: the baryonic modes identify one proton against K/π (1p, 1p̄) and build Λ/Λ̄ through secondary vertex fits; the kaon/pion modes identify K vs π and π vs K; the charmonium radiative modes use the high-momentum lepton selector (p>1.0 treated as a lepton, electron if EMC energy >0.6) requiring 1l⁺1l⁻. Reconstruct π⁰ (and η) from photon pairs with a mass-constrained Kalman fit, χ²<25, and K_S⁰ or J/ψ from vertex or mass-constrained fits (K_S⁰ window 0.487–0.511 GeV/c²), then perform a 4C kinematic fit with χ²<200.
