At 4.178 GeV (BOSS 703, 3.19 fb−1), study D_s^+ → π^+π^+π^−X inclusively in a double-tag measurement, with the tag side D_s^− reconstructed in either K_S^0 K^− (K_S^0 → π^+π^−) or K^− K^+ π^−. Use the 4.178 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k exclusive MC events for each of the two tag modes. On the signal side require two π^+ and one π^− with net charge +1, veto K_S^0 by rejecting any π^+π^− pair with mass in [0.485, 0.510] GeV/c^2, and veto electrons via E/p < 0.8, with no further photon or hadron-PID cuts specified. Select single-tag D_s^− candidates in the two modes, requiring M_BC > 2.05 GeV/c^2 for the K_S^0 K^− mode and choosing the best tag by minimum |ΔE|. Perform a 4C kinematic fit (four-momentum constraint) with χ² < 200. Unfold the inclusive π^+π^+π^−X yield in 11 M_3π bins from 0.55 to 2.15 GeV/c^2 using an 11×11 efficiency matrix and SVD with L-curve optimization.
