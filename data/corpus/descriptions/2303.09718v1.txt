At 24 center-of-mass energies from 4.009 to 4.680 GeV, study continuum e+e− → π+π−ω with ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ, for a helicity amplitude analysis involving f0(500), f0(980), f2(1270), f0(1370), b1(1235)±, and ρ(1450)± states fitted simultaneously over the energy points. Use the corresponding 24 real-data samples and inclusive MC at 4.180 and 4.260 GeV; generate 100k-event exclusive ConExc MC for each energy point with e+e− → π+π−ω, ω → π+π−π0 (Dalitz), π0 → γγ. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero. Photons must have energy above 25 MeV in the barrel or 50 MeV in the endcap, be at least 10° from any charged track, and be at least two in number. Pion identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating pions from kaons and protons, requiring at least two π+ and two π−. Reconstruct π0 from photon pairs by constraining the γγ invariant mass to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<25, requiring at least one π0; then perform a 5C kinematic fit to π+π−π+π−π0 with four-momentum and π0-mass constraints, χ²<60, flagged nominal, and also test a competing π+π−π+π−π0γ hypothesis with a four-momentum constraint for background suppression. Apply E_EMC/p<0.9 for pion candidates from non-ω decays, veto |M(π+π−)−M(K_S0)| in (0.49,0.51) GeV/c2 and |M(π+π−π0)−M(χ_c0)| in (3.39,3.44) GeV/c2, select the ω candidate closest to the nominal ω mass, and use the ω signal region (0.76,0.82) GeV/c2 with sidebands (0.68,0.74) and (0.84,0.90) GeV/c2; charge conjugation is implied throughout.
