At √s = 3.773 GeV on the ψ(3770), study D+ → K̄0 e+ ν_e with K̄0 → K_S0 → π+π− and D+ → π0 e+ ν_e with π0 → γγ, using a D-tag technique against the recoil D− → K+π−π−. Use the 2.93 fb−1 real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each signal mode, with PHOTOS/ISGW2 for the semileptonic decay. Tag one D with charm −1 in nine hadronic modes (Kππ, K_Sπ, K_SK, KKπ, Kπππ0, πππ, K_Sππ0, Kππππ, K_Sπππ). On the signal side, for K̄0 e+ ν_e require one π+, one π−, one e+, net charge +1, a missing neutrino, and a minimum photon angle of 10°; for π0 e+ ν_e require one e+, net charge +1, two photons with a minimum angle of 10° and energy above 25 MeV (barrel; 50 MeV endcap), and a missing neutrino. Perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation and a mass constraint to K_S0 (π+π−) or π0 (γγ), χ² < 200; for π0, the γγ mass must be in (0.110, 0.150) GeV/c² before the 1-C fit and the best π0 is chosen by minimum 1-C χ². Include FSR recovery by adding photons within 5° of the positron to the positron four-momentum, reject events with an unused photon of E_γ,max > 300 MeV, and, for the π0 mode, require photon showers within 700 ns of the event start. No explicit track |cosθ|, Vz, Vr, or PID method is encoded in the selection.
