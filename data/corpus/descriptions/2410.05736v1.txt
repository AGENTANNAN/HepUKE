On the ψ(3686) at √s = 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → φ η η' with φ → K+K−, η → γγ, and η' → γπ+π−, using the 709_3686 real data and inclusive MC and generating 1,000,000 exclusive signal MC events with that decay chain. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero; photons with EMC TDC in 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV and endcap energy >50 MeV, at least 10° from any charged track, and at least three photons. Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating K from π/p and π from K/p, and requires exactly one K+, one K−, one π+, and one π−. A pre-selection 4C fit to K+K−π+π−γγγ is performed with χ²<50; competing 4-photon (K+K−π+π−γγγγ) and 2-photon (K+K−π+π−γγ) hypotheses are fitted for veto, and the nominal 5C fit adds an η→γγ mass constraint, with χ²<200. Additional vetoes reject χ_cJ by |M(K+K−π+π−γγ)−M(χ_cJ)|>0.010 GeV and J/ψ or ηJ/ψ by |M(γγγK+K−)−M(J/ψ)|>0.030 GeV and |M(γπ+π−K+K−)−M(J/ψ)|>0.030 GeV, with helix-parameter corrections applied to simulated charged tracks for the 4C fit. The same selection chain is applied to data, inclusive MC, and exclusive signal MC.
