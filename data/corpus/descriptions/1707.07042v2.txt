On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_c0,2 with χ_c0,2 → η'η' and χ_c0,2 → ηη', where the η' decays as η' → γπ+π− or η' → ηπ+π− with η → γγ and the η decays as η → γγ. Use the 448.1×10⁶ ψ(3686) events at 3.686 GeV (real data and inclusive MC), together with 48 pb⁻¹ of 3.65 GeV continuum real data and its inclusive MC as a background control; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the five modes: both η' → γπ+π−, both η' → ηπ+π−, one η' of each type, ηη' with η' → γπ+π−, and ηη' with η' → ηπ+π−. Five independent selection chains are used, distinguished by the final-state multiplicity: 4 charged tracks + ≥3 photons (both η' → γπ+π−), 4 tracks + ≥5 photons (both η' → ηπ+π−), 4 tracks + ≥4 photons (mixed), 2 tracks + ≥4 photons (ηη', η' → γπ+π−), and 2 tracks + ≥5 photons (ηη', η' → ηπ+π−). All charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with 2 positive and 2 negative tracks plus net charge 0 for the η'η' modes, or 1 positive and 1 negative track with net charge 0 for the ηη' modes; no PID is applied and every charged track is assumed to be a pion. Photons are required to have an EMC time in 0–700 ns and energy above 25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.8) or 50 MeV in the endcaps (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92). Where an η is present it is built from a photon pair mass-constrained (Kalman) to the nominal η mass with χ²<25, requiring at least one η (mixed and ηη' with η' → γπ+π− modes) or at least two η (both η' → ηπ+π− and ηη' with η' → ηπ+π− modes). Finally a nominal 4C kinematic fit of the full final state (the transition γ plus the relevant photons, η's and π±) to the beam four-momentum is applied with χ²<200, the published decay-mode-dependent χ² cut (25–90) being applied afterwards.
