At √s = 4.600 GeV, measure the absolute branching fraction of the inclusive decay Λc+ → K_S^0 X with the double-tag technique, using the 4600 real data and inclusive MC, plus 500k-event exclusive MC for ψ(4260) → Λc+ Λc−, Λc− → pbar K+ π−, Λc+ → K_S^0 X, K_S^0 → π+π−. The single-tag side reconstructs Λc− (charm −1) through eight hadronic modes: pbar K_S^0, pbar K+π−, pbar K_S^0 π0, pbar K_S^0 π+π−, pbar K+π−π0, Λπ−, Λπ−π0, and Λπ−π+π− (the paper's pbar Σ0 π−, Σ− π0, Σ− π+π− modes are unavailable). On the signal side, require exactly one π+ and one π− with net charge 0 and a massless missing particle X0 to account for the undetected remainder of the Λc+ decay. K_S^0 candidates are formed from the remaining tracks with a π+π− vertex fit χ² < 100, decay vertex displaced from the interaction point by >2σ, |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 20 cm, and no Vr constraint, keeping the smallest-χ² candidate if multiple. A 4C kinematic fit is applied with an effectively unbounded χ² cut (999999), the K_S^0 mass being selected instead by a two-dimensional unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to beam-constrained mass versus M(π+π−). Background vetoes reject Λ (M(pbar π+) in 1.110–1.120 GeV) for the pbar K_S^0 π0 and pbar K_S^0 π+π− modes, K_S^0 (M(π+π−) or M(π0π0) in 0.480–0.520 GeV) for Λπ−π+π−, and Σ− (M(pbar π0) in 1.170–1.200 GeV) for pbar K_S^0 π0. No explicit PID or photon selection is encoded in this specification.
