On the 4.600 GeV data (567 pb⁻¹, dataset 703_4600) and matching inclusive MC, study e⁺e⁻ → Λ_c⁺ anti-Λ_c⁻ with partial reconstruction of one Λ_c⁺ in the pK_S⁰ mode, K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻; the paper also notes Λπ⁺, Σ⁺π⁰, and Σ⁰π⁺ modes, but only pK_S⁰ is encoded here. Generate 500k exclusive MC events for Λ_c⁺ → pK_S⁰, K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻, with the anti-Λ_c⁻ side left unreconstructed. Select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and one negative track, and net charge +1; no photon requirement is imposed. Identify at least one proton by the probability method (cut 0.001) against pion/kaon hypotheses, remove it from the charged list, and treat the remaining positive and negative tracks as π⁺ and π⁻. Form K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻ via a secondary-vertex fit minimizing the mass difference, then reconstruct Λ_c⁺ from p + K_S⁰, using the best candidate closest to the nominal Λ_c⁺ mass 2.28646 GeV/c² and requiring the recoil mass against the missed anti-Λ_c⁻ to be in the M_BC window 2.278–2.294 GeV/c². No kinematic fit, nC constraint, or χ² cut is applied in this BOSS selection; the vertex fit and mass windows provide the constraints.
