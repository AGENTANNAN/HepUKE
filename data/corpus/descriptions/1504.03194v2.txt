On the J/ψ resonance at 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → φ π0 with φ → K+K− and π0 → γγ, using the 1.311×10^9-event J/ψ real data sample and matching inclusive J/ψ MC. Generate exclusive MC samples: 1,000,000 events for the signal J/ψ → φπ0 → K+K−γγ, 1,000,000 for the coherent background J/ψ → K+K−π0, and 500,000 each for J/ψ → φπ0π0, φγγ, γη_c(1S) with η_c → K+K−π0, γK+K−, γπ0K+K−, π0π0K+K−, and φη with η → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks exactly one positive and one negative with net charge 0, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; photons at least two with energy >25 MeV in the barrel and >50 MeV in the endcap, angle to the nearest charged track >20°, and EMC timing 0–14. Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying kaons against pions and protons and requiring one K+ and one K−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to the K+K−γγ final state with four-momentum constraint, keeping the combination with smallest χ² when more than two photons are present, with a loose χ²<200 applied in this selection; the published χ²<30 and the π0 mass window 0.115 < M(γγ) < 0.155 GeV/c², together with sidebands 0.055–0.095 and 0.175–0.215 GeV/c², are applied after the fit.
