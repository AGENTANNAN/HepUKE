On the J/ψ(3097), study J/ψ → γ a, a → γγ, giving a purely neutral three-photon final state. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 1,000,000 exclusive MC events for J/ψ → γa, a → γγ. Select events with zero charged tracks and at least three photons, so no PID is applied. Photons must be reconstructed in the barrel with |cosθ|<0.80 and E>25 MeV, with endcap reconstruction disabled, and must satisfy a photon–track separation angle >10°, an EMC TDC window 0–14, and a photon-pair timing requirement |ΔT|<500 ns. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to three photons with χ²<30, and require its χ² to be smaller than those of the 2-, 4-, and 5-photon 4C fits to suppress QED and J/ψ→γπ⁰π⁰ backgrounds. Further reject background using ΔE₁₃<1.46 GeV, ΔE₂₃<1.41 GeV for photons ordered by decreasing energy, |Δφ₃₁|>1 rad, and by vetoing any event with a di-photon invariant mass in the π⁰(0.11–0.16), η(0.52–0.56), η′(0.92–0.99), or η_c(2.92–3.04) GeV/c² windows.
