On the ψ(4260) at √s = 4.226 and 4.257 GeV, study e+e− → D+ D*− π0 with D*− → D̄0 π− (Mode I) and e+e− → D0 D̄*0 π0 with D̄*0 → D̄0 π0 (Mode II), where the D+ decays via K−π+π+, K−π+π+π0, K_S0π+, K_S0π+π0, or K_S0π+π+π−, the D0/D̄0 decays via Kπ, Kππ0, or Kπππ, and K_S0 → π+π−, π0 → γγ. Use the 4.226 GeV (1092/pb) and 4.257 GeV (826/pb) real data sets with their corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, and Vr < 1 cm, with at least two positive and one negative track in Mode I, or at least one positive and one negative track in Mode II; photons must have E > 25 MeV in the barrel or > 50 MeV in the endcap, with EMC timing 0–14, and at least two photons are required. Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating kaons from pions/protons and pions from kaons/protons. In Mode I, a γγ kinematic fit constrains M(γγ) to m(π0) with χ² < 200 and at least one π0; a fit to K−π+π+ and K+π−π0 requires both masses in 1.840–1.880 GeV and constrains them to the D+ and D̄0 masses with χ² < 100; then a nominal fit to D+ D̄0 γγ constrains M(γγ) = m(π0) and the missing mass of D+ D̄0 γγ to m(π−) with χ² < 200, keeping the best DD combination by smallest χ²_D+χ²_D̄, with M(γγ) checked in 0.120–0.150 GeV before the 2C fit, M(Dπ0) > 2.1 GeV, |RM(Dπ0) − m(D*)| < 36 MeV/c², and a common D vertex χ² < 100. In Mode II, a γγ fit constrains M(γγ) to m(π0) with χ² < 200 and at least one π0; a K−π+ / K+π− fit requires both masses in 1.840–1.880 GeV and constrains them to the D0 and D̄0 masses with χ² < 100; a nominal D0 D̄0 γγ fit constrains M(γγ) = m(π0) and the recoil mass of D0 D̄0 γγ to m(π0) with χ² < 60, while a competing D̄*0 → D̄0γ hypothesis constrains that recoil mass to 0, with a radiative veto requiring χ²_2C(π0) < 60 and χ²_2C(γ) > 20, again keeping the best DD combination by smallest χ²_D+χ²_D̄, and requiring the same M(γγ) window, M(Dπ0) > 2.1 GeV, and |RM(Dπ0) − m(D*)| < 36 MeV/c².
