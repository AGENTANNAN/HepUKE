Measure the Born cross section for e+e− → K_S^0 K_L^0 at 15 R-scan center-of-mass energies from 2.000 to 3.080 GeV, using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC samples at each point plus 100k-event exclusive MC per point generated with ConExc mode 46 (K_S K_L). Select events with exactly one positive and one negative charged track (net charge 0), requiring |cosθ|<0.93 and |Vz|<20 cm (no Vr cut), and assign the positive track as π+ and the negative as π−; no photon requirement or dedicated PID is used, but an electron veto demands E/p<0.8 for every charged track. Reconstruct K_S^0 → π+π− with a secondary-vertex fit that minimizes the π+π− mass difference, requiring decay-length significance >2 and |m_{π+π−}−m_{K_S^0}|<35 MeV/c², and requiring the π+π− momentum to match the expected K_S momentum at √s/4 within 15 MeV/c. Then perform a kinematic fit with K_L^0 treated as missing and a four-momentum constraint, χ²<200, flagged nominal. Note that the ConExc mode 46 table covers only up to 2.14 GeV, so the higher-energy points require validation.
