On the ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, study ψ(3770)→D⁰ D̄⁰ double tags for a D⁰→K_L⁰ π⁺π⁻ amplitude analysis with the K_L⁰ missing, and D⁰→K_S⁰ π⁺π⁻ with K_S⁰→π⁺π⁻ fully reconstructed; the hadronic flavor tags are K⁺π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁺π⁻, and K⁺π⁻π⁰. Use the 712_3773 real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1,000,000-event exclusive signal MC for each mode with ψ(3770)→D⁰ D̄⁰, the signal D⁰→K_L⁰π⁺π⁻ or K_S⁰π⁺π⁻, D̄⁰→K⁺π⁻, and K_S⁰→π⁺π⁻ in the K_S⁰ sample. For the K_L⁰ mode, require one π⁺ and one π⁻ on the signal side with net charge 0, treat the K_L⁰ as missing via M_miss² = (E_beam − E_π⁺ − E_π⁻)² − |p_tag + p_π⁺ + p_π⁻|², and perform a four-momentum-conservation fit with χ² < 200. For the K_S⁰ mode, require exactly four charged tracks and zero photons, reconstruct K_S⁰ from π⁺π⁻ with a secondary-vertex fit using mass window [0.485,0.510] GeV/c² and flight significance L/σ_L > 2, form a D⁰ candidate from K_S⁰ π⁺π⁻, and perform a 6C fit imposing total four-momentum conservation plus nominal D⁰ and K_S⁰ masses, with χ² < 200 and nominal fit flag. Both modes apply π⁰ and η vetoes rejecting any γγ pair in [0.095,0.165] GeV/c² or [0.48,0.58] GeV/c², respectively, and tag-side ΔE and M_BC windows are ±3σ with the best tag chosen by minimum |ΔE|. The encoded selection does not specify |cosθ|, |Vz|, or Vr cuts, nor photon energy thresholds or a PID method/lepton–π–K separation beyond the track/charge multiplicities and photon vetoes. This BOSS stage performs the event selection, vertex fit, and kinematic fits; the amplitude/isobar fit is done later on ROOT.
