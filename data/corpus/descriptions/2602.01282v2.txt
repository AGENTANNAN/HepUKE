At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study Λbar p annihilation on protons at rest in the beam-pipe cooling-oil layer, J/ψ → Λ Λbar with Λ → p π− tagging one side and the other side reconstructing Λbar p → K+ π+ π− + k π0 (k = 1, 2, 3), plus the K*(892)+ → K+ π0 sub-process with K*(892)+ π+ π− for k = 1. Use the 3.097 GeV real J/ψ data and its inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the four modes (K+π+π−π0, K+π+π−2π0, K+π+π−3π0 and K*+π+π−). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, requiring at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons within TDC 0–14 with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap thresholds, at least 2, 4 or 6 for k = 1, 2, 3; PID by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions, kaons against pions and pions against kaons. π0 candidates are formed from γγ with a 1C mass constraint, χ² < 200, keeping at least 1, 2 or 3 π0 (the best combination minimizing the summed 1C χ²). The tag-side Λ is built from p π− via a secondary-vertex fit minimizing the mass difference, with tracks above (below) 500 MeV/c taken as proton (pion) candidates, requiring M(pπ−) ∈ [1.110, 1.121] GeV/c² (best vertex χ², ndf = 1) and a single-tag recoil-mass window RM_Λ ∈ [1.071, 1.160] GeV/c², keeping only the p π− used in the Λ. The final state (Λ K+ π+ π− [π0…]) is fitted with a nominal 4-momentum constraint plus the Λ mass constraint, χ² cut 200; the annihilation vertex must lie at R_xy ∈ [2.9, 3.6] cm and the inferred proton momentum satisfy |p_oil| < 0.05 GeV/c.
