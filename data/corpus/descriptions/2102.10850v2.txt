On the ψ(3770) at √s = 3.773 GeV, study the double-tag process ψ(3770) → D0 D̄0, with the tag D̄0 → K+π−, K+π−π0, or K+π−π−π+ and the signal D0 → K1(1270)− e+νe, K1(1270)− → K−π+π−. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 400k exclusive MC events for the full decay card, with the three tag modes in equal fractions and the semileptonic signal decay. Event selection: all charged tracks satisfy |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, and Vr < 1 cm, with tracks from K_S0 decays not used; the signal side requires one K−, one π+, one π−, and one e+, net charge 0, and one missing νe, while the tag side uses anti-D0 candidates in the three hadronic modes with charm −1. Photons must have E > 25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ| < 0.80) or E > 50 MeV in the endcap (0.86 < |cosθ| < 0.92), be separated from extrapolated tracks by >10°, and have shower time within 700 ns; π0 candidates are formed from two photons with 0.115 < M(γγ) < 0.150 GeV/c2 and a 1C mass-constrained fit to the nominal π0 mass. PID combines dE/dx and TOF for K/π separation, requiring L(K) > L(π) for kaons and L(π) > L(K) for pions, while electrons combine dE/dx, TOF, and EMC with E/p > 0.8 and L′(e)/(L′(e)+L′(π)+L′(K)) > 0.8. A 4C kinematic fit is performed on the tag D plus K−π+π−e+νe system with χ² < 200, and the tag modes use mode-dependent ΔE windows (K+π−: [−0.029, 0.027] GeV; K+π−π0: [−0.069, 0.038] GeV; K+π−π−π+: [−0.031, 0.028] GeV), an M_BC window of (1.858, 1.874) GeV/c2, and the candidate with the smallest |ΔE| per tag mode.
