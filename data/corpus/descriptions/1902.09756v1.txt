On the ψ(3686), study ψ(2S) → p p̄ φ with φ → K+ K−, yielding a p p̄ K+ K− final state. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for ψ(2S) → p̄ p+ φ (phase space) and φ → K+ K− (VSS). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; PID by the probability method with prob > 0.001, identifying protons against kaons and pions and kaons against pions and protons, requiring at least one proton, one antiproton, and at least one kaon (K+ or K−), with remaining tracks assigned as pions. Veto events where any pπ− or p̄π+ invariant mass lies within 3 MeV/c² of the Λ mass. Perform a 1C kinematic fit under ψ(2S) → p p̄ K+ K− with a missing K+ or K−, constraining four-momentum and flagged nominal, keeping the best χ² when both kaons are detected.
