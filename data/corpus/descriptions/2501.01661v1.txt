Using the 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data sample and its inclusive MC, search for the radiative decays ψ(3686) → γ η_c(2S) and ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ, all followed by η_c(2S)/χ_cJ → p p̄ K⁺K⁻ (final state γ p p̄ K⁺K⁻). Generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the four radiative signal modes (η_c(2S), χ_c0, χ_c1, χ_c2, all produced via VSP_PWAVE) and 200k events for the non-radiative ψ(3686) → p p̄ K⁺K⁻ background. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks with net charge zero; at least one photon with E > 25 MeV in the barrel or E > 50 MeV in the endcap and TDC in [0,700] ns. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut: first identify one proton and one anti-proton (against kaon and pion hypotheses) and remove those tracks, then identify one K⁺ and one K⁻ (against pion) from the remaining tracks. Perform a 3C kinematic fit on γ p p̄ K⁺K⁻ with a vertex fit on the four charged tracks, the photon energy floating, χ² < 200, and flagged nominal. The analysis also applies fake-photon resampling, FSR and helix corrections, a GPR-based efficiency curve, a 1 + α cos²θ_γ photon angular distribution, a damping function in the signal lineshape, and MC–data resolution smearing.
