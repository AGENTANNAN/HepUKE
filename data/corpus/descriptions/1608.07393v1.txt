At the J/ψ(3097), study e+e− → γ η π0 with η → γγ and π0 → γγ, i.e. a five-photon, all-neutral final state. Use the 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data and matching inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of seven modes: phase-space γηπ0, γa0(980) with a0 → ηπ0, γa2(1320) with a2 → ηπ0, ηω, ηφ, γη′ with η′ → 2π0η, and γη′ with η′ → γω. Event selection: require no reconstructed charged tracks, with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, and zero positive plus zero negative tracks; photons must satisfy 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy thresholds, an EMC timing window 0–14 (within 50 ns of the most energetic shower), a >10° angle to any track, and at least five photons, with no PID applied since the final state is neutral. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to five photons under four-momentum conservation, with a loose χ² < 200 in BOSS and the published χ² < 30 applied later in ROOT. Choose the η and π0 photon pairs by minimising Δ = sqrt[(M(g1g2)−mη)² + (M(g3g4)−mπ0)²], require |M(γγ)−mη| < 0.024 GeV/c² and |M(γγ)−mπ0| < 0.015 GeV/c², and use π0 sidebands 0.030 < |M(γγ)−mπ0| < 0.045 GeV/c². Reject two-π0-like combinations with sqrt[(M(g1g2)−mπ0)² + (M(g3g4)−mπ0)²] < 0.05 GeV/c², and veto ω by requiring |M(γπ0)−mω| > 0.07 GeV/c².
