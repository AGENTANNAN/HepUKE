On the e+e− scan from 4.0076 to 4.6984 GeV, study e+e− → π+π− ψ(3686) with ψ(3686) → π+π− J/ψ (charged Mode I) or ψ(3686) → neutrals + J/ψ (neutral Mode II), J/ψ → e+e−. Use the real data and inclusive MC over that energy range, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes. For both modes, charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least two positive and two negative tracks; Mode I selects 5 charged tracks with net charge ±1 (allowing one missed pion) or 6 with net charge 0, while Mode II requires four charged tracks with net charge 0 and at least two photons with EMC timing 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, and opening angle to any track >10°. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001: tracks with p>1.0 GeV/c are leptons, electron if EMC energy >0.7 (otherwise muon), with at least one e+ and one e−; tracks with p<0.65 (0.80) GeV/c for √s below (above) 4.465 GeV are treated as pions, and same-flavour opposite-charge lepton pairs are required. Mode I performs a nominal 4C fit to π+π−π+π−e+e− with χ²<60 for six tracks, with the documented five-track 1C missing-pion fit (χ²<15), J/ψ mass window [3.05,3.15] GeV/c², and subsequent 5C/2C fit after the J/ψ mass constraint. Mode II performs no kinematic fit, applies a J/ψ mass window [3.05,3.15] GeV/c², and vetoes cosθ(π+π−)<0.9, |M_rec(π+π−e+e−)|>63 MeV/c², |M_corr(ψ(3686))−M(ψ(3686))|>8 MeV/c², and |M(γγπ+π−)−M(η)|>50 MeV/c².
