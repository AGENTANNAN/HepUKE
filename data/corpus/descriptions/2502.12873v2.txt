At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), measure the strong-phase difference between D0 and D0bar → K+K−π+π− in bins of phase space using quantum-correlated DDbar pairs (C = −1) via a double-tag technique. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 2M-event exclusive signal MC as ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar with D0 → K+K−π+π− and D0bar → K+π− (phase space). On the signal side reconstruct D0 → K+K−π+π− requiring one K+, one K−, one π+, one π− and net charge zero. On the tag side use the flavor tags D0 → K−π+, K−π+π0, K−π+π−π+, together with custom-reconstructed CP-even (K+K−, π+π−, KS0π0π0, π+π−π0, KL0π0), CP-odd (KS0π0, KS0η, KS0η′(ππη), KS0η′(ρ0γ), KS0π+π−π0), mixed-CP (KS0π+π−, KL0π+π−) and semileptonic (K−e+νe) tags, including partially reconstructed modes with missing KL0 or νe. Perform a 4C kinematic fit (no missing particle) with χ² < 200, and veto KS0 by rejecting π+π− masses in [477, 507] MeV/c². For the double-tag selection both D candidates must be reconstructed, choosing the best combination by M_BC closest to the D0 mass, and extract yields from 1D M_BC fits, with the strong-phase parameters determined from a maximum-likelihood fit to the double-tag yields in 2×4 phase-space bins.
