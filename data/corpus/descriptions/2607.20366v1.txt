On the J/ψ (√s = 3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ X(2370) with X(2370) → K*(892)0 K̄0 (+ c.c.), K*(892)0 → K_S0 π0, K_S0 → π+π− and π0 → γγ. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus 500k-event exclusive MC generated for this signal decay chain (X(2370) modelled as a ~2.36 GeV pseudoscalar decaying via phase space). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks with net charge 0 (all positives assigned π+, all negatives π−); photons with barrel 25 MeV / endcap 50 MeV energy threshold, TDC 0–14, at least 10° from any track, requiring at least three photons. Two secondary-vertex fits on the π+π− pairs reconstruct two K_S0 by minimizing the mass difference, with used tracks removed; a Kalman fit on the two photons constrains their invariant mass to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<25, requiring at least one π0. A subsequent 4C kinematic fit on γ K_S0 K_S0 π0 (nominal, four-momentum constrained) is applied with χ²<200. Downstream, at least one K_S0π0 combination is required to satisfy |M(K_S0π0) − m_K*(892)0| ≤ 50 MeV/c² to tag the K*(892)0.
