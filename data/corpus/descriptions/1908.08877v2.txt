At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), study single-tag D+ → τ+ν_τ with τ+ → π+ν̄_τ, using 2.93 fb⁻¹ of real data and inclusive MC. Tag the D− in the hadronic modes D− → K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S π−, K_S π−π0 and K_S π−π−π+, and require on the signal side exactly one positively charged π+ with a missing neutrino. Generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the signal D+ → τ+ν_τ and the normalization channel D+ → μ+ν_μ. Apply a four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ² < 200, flagged nominal, for both channels. Extract the signal with the missing-mass-squared method, fitting the mu-like (EMC energy ≤ 300 MeV) and π-like (EMC energy > 300 MeV) samples simultaneously. Additional background vetoes require EMC energy over momentum < 0.95 for π-like tracks, maximum extra-shower energy < 300 MeV, |cosθ_missing| < 0.95 (muon) / 0.75 (pion), and opening angle > 25° (muon) / 45° (pion). Peaking backgrounds from D+ → π0π+ and D+ → K_L0 π+ are estimated from data control samples with a kernel method, and the τ and μ modes share the same tag scheme and selection chain.
