At the two energy points √s = 4.918 GeV (208.1 pb⁻¹) and 4.951 GeV (160.4 pb⁻¹), search for e+e− → Λ̄_c⁻ Λc(2595)+ and e+e− → Λ̄_c⁻ Λc(2625)+, each with the charmed baryon decaying to Λc+ π⁰π⁰, using the real data and inclusive MC at both points. The Λ̄_c⁻ tag is reconstructed in three modes — p̄K⁺π⁻, p̄K_S⁰ (K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻), and Λ̄π⁻ (Λ → pπ⁻) — with the tag mass window 2.27 < M(Λ̄_c⁻) < 2.30 GeV/c², M(K_S⁰) ∈ (0.487, 0.511) GeV/c² and M(Λ) ∈ (1.111, 1.121) GeV/c². On the signal side only the two π⁰ are partially reconstructed, requiring at least four photons with Eγ < 150 MeV; the Λc+ itself is not reconstructed. A four-momentum (4C) kinematic fit is applied with χ² < 200, and the signal is extracted from the recoil-mass distribution M_recoil(Λ̄_c⁻). Both charge-conjugate channels (Λ̄_c⁻ tag with Λc*+ signal and Λc+ tag with Λc*− signal) are included, and the peaking background from Λc*+ → Λc+ π⁺π⁻ is subtracted using MC. The same selection chain and decay card (Λ̄_c⁻ → p̄K⁺π⁻ phase space) are used for the 2595 and 2625 analyses, run separately on each energy point, with tag-mode efficiencies near 48%, 50% and 38% for the p̄K⁺π⁻, p̄K_S⁰ and Λ̄π⁻ modes, respectively.
