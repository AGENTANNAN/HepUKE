On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_c2 with χ_c2 → K+K−π0, K_S K±π∓, and π+π−π0 (K_S → π+π−). Use the 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the three modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with TDC 0–14, >10° from any track, 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) threshold, requiring at least three photons for K+K−π0 and π+π−π0 and at least one for K_S K±π∓. PID uses the probability method with 0.001 cut: the K+K−π0 mode has no PID and assigns the positive track as K+ and the negative as K−, the K_S K±π∓ mode separates π/K/p and K/π/p, and the π+π−π0 mode separates π/K/p and requires at least one π+ and one π−. For K+K−π0, a two-photon π0 fit with χ²<25 and at least one π0 is followed by a 4C fit of γK+K−π0 with χ²<80 and M(K+K−) in 0–3 GeV; for K_S K±π∓, K_S is formed from two oppositely charged tracks via secondary-vertex fit with loose vertex requirements and no PID, chosen by mass closest to nominal and decay length >0.25 cm, then a 4C fit of γK_S K±π∓ with χ²<60; for π+π−π0, a two-photon π0 fit with χ²<25 is followed by a 4C fit of γπ+π−π0 with an additional π0 mass constraint, χ²<60, and M(π+π−) in 0–3 GeV. The π0 candidates satisfy |Mγγ−mπ0|<10 MeV, and the χ_c2 signal window is |M−mχc2|≤15 MeV; the π+π−π0 mode additionally vetoes π0 recoil mass <3.0 GeV and M(γπ0) in 0.7–0.85 GeV.
