Measure the Born cross section for e+e− → p pbar at 47 c.m. energies from 3.510 to 4.946 GeV, totalling 26 fb−1 of real data, with matching inclusive MC. Generate 100k-event exclusive MC at each scan point for continuum e+e− → p pbar via ConExc, injecting the correct √s per point. Event selection requires exactly one positive and one negative charged track with |cosθ|<0.80, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, net charge zero. Protons and antiprotons are identified by the probability method with a 0.001 likelihood cut, requiring the proton hypothesis to dominate over kaon and pion, and exactly one proton and one antiproton. A nominal kinematic fit with four-momentum constraint is applied to the p pbar pair with χ²<200. Further background suppression requires the positive proton to satisfy E/p<0.5, the p and pbar c.m. opening angle to exceed 3.1 rad, muon-counter hit depths below 40 cm for both tracks, and the antiproton momentum to lie within 3σ_p of its expected value. ISR corrections and detection efficiencies are obtained by iterative MC weighting per c.m. energy.
