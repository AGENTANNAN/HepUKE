On ψ(4260), study e+e− → π+π− J/ψ, with J/ψ → μ+μ− and J/ψ → e+e− as independent decay modes. Use real data at 4.226 and 4.258 GeV and the corresponding inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two J/ψ decay modes at both energy points, with ψ(4260) → π+π− J/ψ and J/ψ → l+l− in phase space. Event selection: exactly four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0. PID by the probability method, high-momentum tracks (p>1.0) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6, else muon), requiring two positive and two negative leptons. Perform a kinematic fit to the π+π− l+l− final state with a 4C constraint (e+e− → π+π− J/ψ) plus a J/ψ mass constraint on the lepton pair (5C total), χ² cut 200, and require the J/ψ mass window 0.1 < M(l+l−) < 3.2 GeV/c². Both decay modes share the same selection chain.
