On the BESIII energy scan, measure the Born cross section for e+e− → p K− K− anti-Ξ+ , reconstructing the Ξ+ via the missing mass MM(pK−K−) (partial reconstruction). Use the 39 real-data energy points spanning 3.51–4.95 GeV (about 20 fb−1) together with the matching inclusive-MC samples, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC at each point for ψ(4260) → p+ K− K− anti-Ξ+ phase space with a default cross section. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, requiring exactly one positive and two negative tracks; PID by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying the proton against kaon and pion and the kaons against pion and proton, demanding 1 p and 2 K−. Any additional identified tracks beyond the required 1p + 2K− are vetoed through the track-count constraints, and the recoil (missing) mass is required to lie in 1.28–1.38 GeV. The Ξ+ signal is then extracted from the MM(pK−K−) distribution with a Gaussian signal shape convolved with MC resolution over a Chebyshev background, after a vertex fit with χ² < 100 on the three tracks; the 39-point energy list should be validated against the paper.
