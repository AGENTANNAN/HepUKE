On ψ(3686), study χ_cJ → p pbar K_S^0 K^- π^+ + c.c. (J=0,1,2) using the 27.12×10^8 ψ(3686) real data sample and its inclusive MC; generate 1M exclusive MC for ψ(2S) → γ χ_c0, χ_c0 → p pbar K_S^0 K^- π^+, K_S^0 → π+π− (χ_c1/χ_c2 analogous). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least 3 positive and 3 negative tracks, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, photon–track angle >10°, at least one photon. PID by the probability method (prob>0.001): identify protons against kaons/pions, require ≥1 p and ≥1 antiproton, identify kaons against pions; remove the identified p/pbar/K from the charged lists and assign the remaining positive and negative tracks as π+ and π−. Reconstruct K_S^0 from an oppositely charged π+π− pair via a secondary vertex fit that minimizes the mass difference and removes the used pions. Perform two 4C kinematic fits with χ²<50: nominal γ p pbar K_S^0 K^- π+ and charge-conjugate γ p pbar K_S^0 K^+ π−.
