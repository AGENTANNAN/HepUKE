On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → Λ Λ̄ η′, with Λ → p π−, Λ̄ → p̄ π+, and η′ decaying via two modes: η′ → γ π+ π− (Mode I, final state p p̄ π+ π− π+ π− γ) and η′ → η π+ π−, η → γγ (Mode II, final state p p̄ π+ π− π+ π− γγ). Use 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus 3.773 GeV ψ(3770) real data and inclusive MC for QED continuum estimation; generate 1M-event exclusive MC for each η′ decay mode. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring at least 3 positive and 3 negative tracks and more than 5 tracks total; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC TDC 0–14, at least 10° from any track, requiring at least one photon (Mode I) or at least two photons (Mode II). PID by the probability method with 0.001 probability cut: identify at least one proton and one antiproton against π/K (then remove them), and identify pions against K/proton. Build Λ and Λ̄ via secondary-vertex fits on p π− and p̄ π+ minimizing the mass difference, taking the best Λ Λ̄ pair. Mode I performs a 4C kinematic fit on Λ Λ̄ γ π+π−π+π− with χ² cut 30 (nominal); Mode II first does a Kalman fit on γγ constraining to the η mass (χ² cut 40, ≥1 η), then a kinematic fit on Λ Λ̄ η π+π−π+π− constraining (π+π−η) to the η′ mass plus 4C with χ² cut 40, flagged nominal. Both modes also apply primary-vertex fitting and vetoes against Σ0, J/ψ, χc, Ξ and Σ(1385) backgrounds.
