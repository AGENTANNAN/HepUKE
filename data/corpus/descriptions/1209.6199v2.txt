Measure the number of ψ(2S) events collected by BESIII in 2009 from ψ′ → inclusive hadrons. Use the 3.686 GeV ψ(2S) real data and matching inclusive MC, the 3.650 GeV off-resonance data (≈44 pb⁻¹) and its continuum inclusive MC, plus a 10⁶-event exclusive ψ(2S) → anything (phase-space) sample for the detection efficiency. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<15 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring at least one good track; photons with 25 MeV (barrel, |cosθ|<0.8) / 50 MeV (endcap, 0.86<|cosθ|<0.92) energy thresholds, at least two, and separated from any track by more than 10°; there is no PID and no kinematic fit. For exactly two good tracks — dominated by Bhabha and dimuon events — require each track momentum below 1.7 GeV/c and the opening angle between them below 176°, while events with more than two tracks pass on the inclusive-hadron topology unchanged. For a single good track, demand at least two additional photons and select the photon pair whose γγ mass is closest to the π⁰, requiring |M(γγ)−M(π⁰)|<0.015 GeV/c². Require visible energy E_vis/E_cm > 0.4, take events with |V̄z| < 4.0 cm (average per-track z vertex) as signal and 6.0 < |V̄z| < 10.0 cm as a non-collision-background sideband, N_obs = N_signal − N_sideband, and finally subtract the off-resonance continuum with the scaling factor f = 3.677.
