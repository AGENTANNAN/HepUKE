On ψ(4178) at 4.178 GeV, measure Ds+ → η(′) e+ νe with a double-tag method: tag Ds− in all hadronic modes and reconstruct the semileptonic decay on the opposite side. Use the 3.19 fb−1 real data at 4.178 GeV and inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of Ds+ → η e+ νe (η → γγ) and Ds+ → η′ e+ νe (η′ → η π+π−, η → γγ). The tag-side Ds− is built from 14 hadronic modes with M_BC in (2.010, 2.073) GeV/c², choosing the candidate with M_rec closest to the Ds*+ mass, and the Ds*+ transition photon or π0 is selected by minimizing |ΔE| within (-0.04, 0.04) GeV. On the signal side, after the usual charged-track quality cuts on |cosθ|, |Vz|, and Vr, require two photons with energy >25 MeV and at least 10° from any track, at least one e+, a missing neutrino, no extra charged tracks, and E_extra < 0.3 GeV; the η′ mode additionally requires one π+ and one π−. Perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation and a γγ mass constraint to the nominal η mass (and for η′, also a π+π−η constraint to the nominal η′ mass) with χ² < 200, applying M(γγ) in [0.50, 0.57] GeV/c² for η and M(η′e+) < 1.9 GeV/c² for η′. Charged particles are identified with the standard BOSS PID (probability method, e/π/K separation) to select the required e+, π+, and π−; Bremsstrahlung energy is partly recovered by adding EMC showers within 10° of the e+, and for η′→γρ0 a helicity cut |cosθ_hel| < 0.85 is applied.
