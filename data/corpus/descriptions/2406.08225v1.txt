On ψ(3686), study the radiative transition ψ(3686) → γ 2(π+π−)η with η → γγ, covering the ηc(1S,2S) and χcJ intermediate states. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus 500k-event exclusive MC for ψ(2S) → γ ηc, ηc → 2(π+π−)η, η → γγ in phase space. Event selection: four charged tracks assigned as two π+ and two π− (net charge 0), with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; at least three photons with TDC 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, and at least 10° separation from any track. Reconstruct η → γγ via a 1C Kalman kinematic fit constraining the γγ mass to the η nominal mass, χ²<20, requiring at least one η. Then perform a 4C kinematic fit of γ π+π+π−π− η with four-momentum conservation (nominal), χ²<200, with an optimal total 5C χ²<15 applied after s/√(s+b) optimisation. Additional vetoes reject events where χ²_4C(3γ 2(π+π−)) exceeds χ²_4C(4γ 2(π+π−)) or χ²_4C(2γ 2(π+π−)), and veto ψ(3686)→P J/ψ (P recoil mass outside the J/ψ window), ψ(3686)→π0H (γγ mass outside the π0 region), and, for the γηc(2S)/χcJ modes, ψ(3686)→γχcJ with χcJ→γJ/ψ.
