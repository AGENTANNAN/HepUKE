Using the twelve BOSS 703 energy-scan points from 4.178 to 4.288 GeV (real data and matching inclusive MC), study ψ(4260) → γ X(3872) with X(3872) → π0 χc0, π+π− χc0 and π0π0 χc0, where χc0 decays through five hadronic modes (π+π−, K+K−, π+π−π+π−, π+π−K+K−, π+π−π0π0), and additionally a normalization channel X(3872) → π+π− J/ψ, J/ψ → ℓ+ℓ−; generate 100k-event exclusive MC with ψ(4260) → γX(3872), X(3872) → π0χc0, χc0 → π+π−, π0 → γγ, produced at each scan point. All charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm and Vr < 1 cm with zero net charge, in multiplicities matched to each final state (e.g. at least 1 or 2 positive and negative tracks, and at least 3+3 for the six-pion mode). Photons are accepted above 25 MeV in the barrel and 50 MeV in the endcap, with at least 10° separation from any track and EMC timing between 0 and 14, requiring at least 2, 4, 6 or 8 photons depending on the channel's π0 content. Charged pions are assigned without PID in the pion-only modes, while kaon modes use the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut to identify K+ and K− against pions, removing those tracks from the pion sample; the normalization channel identifies high-momentum tracks (p > 1.0 GeV) as leptons, treating them as electrons when EMC energy exceeds 0.85 (otherwise muons), and requires one ℓ+, one ℓ−, at least one π+ and one π−. Each mode is fitted with a 4C kinematic fit (π0 mass constrained to its nominal value where applicable, χ² cut 200), and the multi-π0 modes first pass a Kalman fit on photon pairs constraining the γγ mass to the π0 with χ² < 25, requiring the appropriate number of π0's.
