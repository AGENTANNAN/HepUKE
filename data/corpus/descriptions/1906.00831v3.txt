On BESIII data at √s = 4.226, 4.360, 4.420 and 4.600 GeV (the 4.226 GeV point being the nominal one), study e+e− → π+π−π0 ηc and Zc(3900)± → ρ± ηc, with ηc reconstructed in nine hadronic decay modes (p pbar, 2(K+K−), K+K−π+π−, K+K−π0, p pbar π0, K_S^0 K±π∓, π+π−η, K+K−η, π+π−π0π0). Use the four real-data sets and a 500k-event exclusive MC sample of ψ(4260) → π+π−π0 ηc, ηc → p pbar, π0 → γγ generated at 4.226 GeV (the representative ppbar channel). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track, total ≥4 tracks and net charge 0; photons with EMC timing in 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV / endcap >50 MeV, at least 10° from any charged track, and at least two photons. Particle ID uses the probability method (prob>0.001), identifying protons against kaons and pions and pions against kaons and protons, requiring at least one p, one pbar, one π+ and one π−. A 1C Kalman fit to γγ constrains the π0 mass with χ²<25 and requires at least one π0, followed by a 4C kinematic fit to π+π− p pbar π0 (nominal, four-momentum constraint, χ²<200). The nine ηc modes are fitted simultaneously with an ηc signal window of 2.95–3.02 GeV (sidebands 2.78–2.92 and 3.05–3.19 GeV); D-meson, K*(892), ω and η vetoes are applied, peaking background from e+e−→π+π−hc (600k MC) is subtracted, and the Zc(3900)± search uses the ρ± window M(π±π0) ∈ [0.675,0.875] GeV with pion momentum <0.8 GeV and fixed Zc mass/width; ISR and vacuum-polarization corrections are applied, with Bayesian 90% C.L. upper limits at the additional energy points.
