Study e+e− → D*+D*− and e+e− → D*+D− at 28 c.m. energies from 4.085 to 4.600 GeV, using the real data and matching inclusive MC at each point; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes, with D*+ → π+ D0, D0 → K−π+ (in the D*+D*− generator D*− → π−anti-D0, anti-D0 → K+π−), while the D*−/D− side is not reconstructed but inferred from energy-momentum conservation. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm, |Vz|<10 cm, at least two positive and at least one negative track, and no photon requirement; PID by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying kaons against pions/protons and pions against kaons/protons, requiring at least one K− and at least two π+, with pions split into low-momentum (p<0.3 GeV/c, from D*+ → π+ D0) and high-momentum (p>0.3 GeV/c, from D0 → K−π+) and at least one of each. Kaon candidates must have p>0.3 GeV/c; the K−π+_H mass must lie within ±3σ of the known D0 mass (1845.4–1885.2 MeV/c²). Perform a nominal kinematic fit on the K−π+π+ system with four-momentum constraint, χ²<200, plus a vertex fit constraining M(K−π+_H) to the D0 mass, choosing the smallest χ² combination if multiple candidates. Both signal modes share the same selection chain, and yields are extracted from a simultaneous 2D unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to RM(π+_L D0) versus M(π+_L D0).
