At six center-of-mass energies from 4611.86 to 4698.82 MeV (the 4.60–4.70 GeV scan, 4.4 fb−1), study e+e− → Λc+ K− with Λc+ → Ξ0 K+, Ξ0 → Λ π0, Λ → p π−, and π0 → γγ in a single-tag approach, using the real data and matching inclusive MC at each point plus 500,000-event exclusive MC for this signal mode. Event selection requires at least four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm, and at least two photons with EMC timing 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, and an angle to any track greater than 10°. PID uses the probability method, requiring one proton (against K/π), one K+ (against π), and at least one π− (against K), with overlaps between proton/charged-particle and kaon/charged-particle hypotheses removed. A secondary vertex fit of pπ− forms the Λ by minimizing the mass difference and removes the used daughters, after which a 1C kinematic fit of the two photons constrains their invariant mass to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<25, requiring at least one π0. The Ξ0 is then built from Λπ0 and the Λc+ from Ξ0K+, with Λ and Ξ0 masses required to be consistent with their PDG values, while the final ΔE and MBC windows are applied in ROOT rather than in this BOSS selection chain.
