On the J/ψ (3097 MeV), measure the branching fraction and search for a CP-violating asymmetry in J/ψ → γη′, η′ → π+π−e+e−. Use the J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for both the signal mode (η′ → π+π−e+e−) and the normalization mode (η′ → π+π−γ). Signal selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring two positive and two negative tracks with net charge 0; at least one photon above 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy, with >15° angle to any track and EMC timing in the 0–14 window; PID by the chi2-sum combinatorial method assigning each track as pion or electron with a combined PID chi² cut of 4. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit under the γπ+π−e+e− hypothesis with χ²<62, and also refit the same track indices under the γπ+π−μ+μ− and γπ+π−π+π− hypotheses, retaining the event only if the γπ+π−e+e− hypothesis has the smallest combined 4C+PID chi², plus a photon-conversion veto. For the normalization channel, require one positive and one negative track with the same track cuts and at least two photons with the same photon cuts, identify tracks as pions with the same chi2-sum method, and perform a 4C fit to γπ+π−γγ with χ²<140, selecting the radiative photon within 20 MeV of 1.4 GeV and requiring the other photon to have E>0.15 GeV to suppress π⁰/η backgrounds. The η′ signal window is |M(π+π−e+e−) − m_η′| < 0.02 GeV.
