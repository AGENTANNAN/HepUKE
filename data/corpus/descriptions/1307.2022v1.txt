At √s=3.773 GeV (ψ(3770)) and 3.650 GeV (continuum), study Bhabha scattering e+e− → (γ)e+e− for luminosity. Use the real data and inclusive MC at each energy point, and generate 400k-event exclusive Bhabha MC at both 3.650 and 3.773 GeV with the Babayaga generator, |cosθ|<0.83. For each energy point, select exactly one positively and one negatively charged track with net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.80, |Vz|<5 cm, and Vr<10 mm (1 cm) at closest approach. Particle ID uses the probability method with prob>0.001; tracks with momentum above 1.0 GeV are treated as leptons, identified as electrons if their EMC energy exceeds 0.6 GeV and otherwise as muons, and both the positron and electron candidates must have EMC energy >1.0 GeV to reject μ+μ−. Reject cosmic rays by requiring that the two track momenta do not both exceed E_beam+0.15 GeV (E_beam=√s/2), and suppress (γ)J/ψ, (γ)ψ(3686), and ψ(3770)→(γ)J/ψ X backgrounds by requiring |p1|+|p2|>0.9√s. Signal is extracted from Δφ=|φ1−φ2|−180° between the two EMC clusters, with sideband subtraction performed in ROOT, and the trigger efficiency for e+e−→(γ)e+e− is taken as 100%; both energy points share the same selection chain.
