At the 16 energy points from 4.47 to 4.95 GeV (6.7 fb⁻¹, BOSS 703/706/707), search for e⁺e⁻ → φ χ_c0(3415) and φ η_c2(1D) with φ → K⁺K⁻, using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at every point, while the η_c2(1D) search is restricted to the three points at and above 4.84 GeV (4.840, 4.914, 4.946 GeV); generate 50k-event exclusive MC at each available point for the χ_c0 → π⁺π⁻, π⁺π⁻π⁰π⁰, K⁺K⁻π⁺π⁻, 2(π⁺π⁻) and 3(π⁺π⁻) modes and for η_c2 → π⁺π⁻. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with the positive/negative multiplicities and net charge matching each final state (2K2π, 2K4π, 2K6π, 4K2π, 6K2π); photons must have EMC energy above 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), TDC within [0,14], be more than 10° from any track, and number fewer than 4 (fewer than 8 in the π⁰ modes). Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut and K/π separation, requiring the appropriate numbers of K± and π± per mode. Full-reconstruction modes undergo a 4C kinematic fit with χ²<40 (χ²<60 for the η_c2 mode) after a helix-parameter correction, and the χ_c0 → π⁺π⁻π⁰π⁰ mode first constrains each γγ pair to the π⁰ mass (χ²<200, at least two π⁰) before the 4C fit; two partial-reconstruction channels are also included, φ χ_c0 with one missing K± (1C fit, χ²<45) and χ_c0 → π⁺π⁻π⁰π⁰ with one missing π⁰ (1C fit, χ²<6).
