On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γη with η → e⁺e⁻e⁺e⁻ (Mode I) and J/ψ → γη′ with η′ → π⁺π⁻η, η → e⁺e⁻e⁺e⁻ (Mode II). Use the J/ψ real data and its inclusive MC; generate 2M-event exclusive MC for each mode (γη→4e and γη′→π⁺π⁻η→4e). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<10 cm and net charge 0 — two positive and two negative tracks for Mode I, three positive and three negative for Mode II; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, >15° from the nearest charged track, at least one. PID by the probability method (prob cut 0.001), electrons via L(e)>L(π), with Mode II additionally requiring ≥1 π⁺ and ≥1 π⁻ (π identified against K/p). Mode I performs a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γ e⁺e⁻e⁺e⁻, χ² cut 200 (the reported analysis applies χ²_4C+PID<60 after combining PID); Mode II performs a nominal 5C fit to γ π⁺π⁻e⁺e⁻e⁺e⁻ constraining the π⁺π⁻η(4e) system to the η′ mass, χ² cut 200 (χ²_5C<60 applied later), choosing the best combination by minimum χ². Additional vetos: photon conversions (Mode I: Φ_ee<70°, Mode II: Φ_ee<40°, both with 2 cm<R_xy<8 cm) and π→e misidentification (Mode I: 10°<θ_ee¹<30° with 10°<θ_ee²<60°; Mode II: 20°<θ_ee¹<60° with 40°<θ_ee²<80°). Track helix-parameter correction is applied before the kinematic fit in both modes.
