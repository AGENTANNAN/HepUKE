On the J/ψ(3097) resonance, search for a massless BSM particle via J/ψ → X̄Ξ⁰(→ Λ̄π⁰, Λ̄ → p̄π⁺, π⁰ → γγ) Ξ⁰(→ Λ + invisible, Λ → pπ⁻), using J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, plus two 500k-event exclusive-MC samples: one for the full signal chain (Ξ⁰ → Λ + invisible with the anti-Ξ⁰ tag) and one single-tag sample for anti-Ξ⁰ → Λ̄π⁰. Event selection requires at least two charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm and net charge 0, and at least two photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) thresholds, a TDC window of 0–14 and ≥10° (≥20°) separation from prompt (anti-proton) tracks. PID uses the probability method with proton/pion/kaon separation and demands at least one proton, one anti-proton, one π⁺ and one π⁻. Reconstruct π⁰ → γγ with a 1C mass-constrained fit (χ²<25), do secondary-vertex fits for Λ̄ → p̄π⁺ and Λ → pπ⁻ (minimum mass difference), then a nominal 4-momentum-conserving fit of J/ψ → p p̄ π⁺π⁻ π⁰ + invisible with χ²<200. Final selection applies the tag m_BC, ΔE and ΔM (|ΔM|<0.02 GeV), π⁰ (0.115–0.150 GeV), Λ (1.111–1.120 GeV) and Minv(Λ + invisible) (1.310–1.322 GeV) mass windows, Λ vertex χ²<100 and L/σ_L>2, competing 2C fits requiring χ²<8.5 with χ²_invisible-pi0 > χ²_massless, |cosθ_inv|<0.8 and |p_inv|>0.15 GeV/c, and extra-photon 5C/6C vetoes (min χ²>1000) with a data-driven E_extra shape correction.
