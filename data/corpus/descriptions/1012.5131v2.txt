Study a_0^0(980)–f_0(980) mixing in two BESIII channels: J/ψ → φ f_0(980) → φ a_0^0(980) → φ ηπ0 with φ→K+K−, η→γγ, π0→γγ, and ψ′ → γχ_c1 → γπ0 a_0^0(980) → γπ0 f_0(980) → γπ0 π+π−. Use the 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data with inclusive MC and the 3.686 GeV ψ′ real data with inclusive MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each signal mode, with the narrow mixing scalar S (m=991.3 MeV/c², Γ=8 MeV/c²) decaying to ηπ0 in the J/ψ mode and to π+π− in the ψ′ mode. For both channels, charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<2 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, and zero net charge; photons must have E>25 MeV (barrel) or E>50 MeV (endcap), lie 0–14 TDC and >10° from any track, with at least four photons in the J/ψ channel and at least three in the ψ′ channel. PID uses the probability method with probability >0.001: Channel I requires one K+ and one K− identified against pions/protons, while Channel II requires one π+ and one π− identified against kaons/protons. Channel I performs a 6C kinematic fit to J/ψ→K+K−ηπ0 (four-momentum plus π0 and η mass constraints), keeps the smallest χ² with χ²<200 at BOSS level (paper <60), and stores competing J/ψ→K+K−ηη and J/ψ→K+K−π0π0 fits for a probability veto. Channel II performs a 5C fit to ψ′→γπ0π+π− (four-momentum plus π0 mass), χ²<200 at BOSS level (paper <60), also storing competing ψ′→π0π+π− and ψ′→π0π0π+π− fits; the φ signal window is |m(K+K−)−1.02|<0.015 GeV/c² and the χ_c1 window is 3.49<M(π0π+π−)<3.54 GeV/c², with sidebands for background. Off-line ROOT-level cuts noted include |cosθ_decay|<0.95 for the η/π0 decay photon, photon-pair mass-fit χ²<25, E/p<0.85, two-track opening angle <160° in Channel II, and a γγ recoil J/ψ veto |M_recoil−3.097|>0.06 GeV/c².
