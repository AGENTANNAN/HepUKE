On the 15 BESIII scan points from 4.530 to 4.946 GeV, study e+e− → Ds+ Ds1(2536)− and e+e− → Ds+ Ds2*(2573)−, both with Ds+ → K−K+π+, where Ds1(2536)− → D*0bar K− (D*0bar → D0bar π0, D0bar → K+π−, π0 → γγ) or Ds2*(2573)− → D0bar K− (D0bar → K+π−). Use the real data at all 15 points and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes; no inclusive MC is defined in this selection code. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least two positive and two negative tracks, and no photon selection is applied. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut for K/π separation, requiring at least two K−, one K+, and one π+, with no lepton identification. Reconstruct Ds+ from K−K+π+ with a common-vertex χ²<100 and |M(K−K+π+)−m_Ds+|<8 MeV/c², and require recoil-mass windows |RM(Ds+K−)−m_D*0bar|<9 MeV/c² for the Ds1 mode or |RM(Ds+K−)−m_D0bar|<11 MeV/c² for the Ds2* mode. Additional intermediate windows are applied for Ds+ → φπ+ and K*0bar K+ modes, and the KS0K+ mode (with |M(KS0K+)−m_Ds+|<8 MeV/c² and KS0 from a secondary vertex) is combined by branching fraction with tighter recoil-mass windows of 7 MeV (Ds1) and 9 MeV (Ds2*), using M(K+K−)∈[1.004,1.034] GeV/c², M(K−π+)∈[0.832,0.928] GeV/c², and helicity-angle cuts |cosθ|>0.4 (φ) and >0.52 (K*0bar). Perform a 4C kinematic fit with a Ds+ mass constraint and a missing D*0bar (Ds1) or D0bar (Ds2*) mass constraint, iterating over the two K− assignments, and require χ²<200 flagged nominal; both decay modes share the same selection chain.
