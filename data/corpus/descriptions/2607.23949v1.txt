On the ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, measure the semileptonic decays D0 → π− e+ νe, D0 → π− μ+ νμ, D+ → π0 e+ νe and D+ → π0 μ+ νμ (π0 → γγ) using a single-tag technique, with the companion selection identical to the dynamics paper. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the four signal modes, each with the opposite D decaying via the chosen tag mode. Tag-side reconstruction uses the D0 modes K+π−, K+π−π0, K+π−π+π−, K+π−π0π0, K+π−π+π−π0 (charm −1) and the D+ modes K+π−π−, Ksπ−, K+π−π−π0, Ksπ−π0, Ksπ−π+π−, K+K−π−. The signal side requires exactly one π− together with one e+ (or one μ+) and net charge 0 for the D0 modes, or two photons plus one e+ (or one μ+) with net charge +1 for the D+ modes, with the (undetected) neutrino inferred as the missing particle. A kinematic fit imposing four-momentum conservation is applied with χ² < 200, and for the D+ channels the two-photon invariant mass is additionally constrained to the nominal π0 mass. Each of the four channels is executed over its real data, inclusive MC and matching exclusive-MC sample.
