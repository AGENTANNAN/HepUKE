Measure the absolute branching fraction of D+ → K0bar μ+νμ at ψ(3770) with the double-tag technique, using 2.93 fb⁻¹ of real data at 3.773 GeV, the matching inclusive MC, and two 500k-event exclusive signal samples: D+ → K_S0 μ+ν (K_S0 → π+π−) and D+ → K0 μ+ν (K0 → π0π0). On the tag side the D− is reconstructed in six hadronic single-tag modes — K+π−π−, K_S0π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S0π−π0, K_S0π+π−π−, K+K−π− — requiring charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93 and, unless originating from K_S0, V_xy < 1.0 cm and |V_z| < 10 cm; K_S0 daughters (|V_z| < 20 cm) are formed from π+π− without PID, must satisfy |M(π+π−) − m_K_S0| < 12 MeV and a decay length > 2σ, while K/π separation uses the combined dE/dx and TOF confidence levels (CL_K > CL_π for kaons, CL_π > CL_K for pions). Photons are accepted with shower time within 700 ns, E > 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), and > 10° opening angle to any charged track, with π0 candidates in (0.115, 0.150) GeV subject to a mass-constrained fit; for each tag mode only the minimum-|ΔE| combination is retained, with ΔE windows of ±25 MeV (K+π−π−, K_S0π−, K_S0π+π−π−, K+K−π−) or −55/+40 MeV (K+π−π−π0, K_S0π−π0), and the single-tag signal region is 1.863 < M_BC < 1.877 GeV/c². On the signal side require exactly one additional good charged track, opposite in charge to the tag, identified as a muon by CL_μ > CL_K, CL_μ > CL_e, CL_μ > 0.001, and 0.1 < E_EMC < 0.3 GeV; in the K0 → π0π0 chain the four photons must give M(π0π0) in (0.45, 0.51) GeV, keeping the combination with the smallest summed π0 mass-constrained χ². A 4C kinematic fit is then applied (tag + π+π− or 4γ + μ+ + massless ν = √s), with χ² < 200, supplemented in the π0π0 mode by the two π0 mass constraints, and background from D+ → K0bar π+(π0) is rejected via M(K0bar μ+) < 1.6 GeV and E_max^extra γ < 0.15 GeV, with the U_miss fit performed afterwards in ROOT.
