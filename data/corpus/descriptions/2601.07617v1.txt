At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → Λ anti-Σ0 η (+ c.c.), with anti-Σ0 → γ anti-Λ, Λ → p+ π−, anti-Λ → anti-p- π+, and η → γγ. Use the J/ψ real data sample 708_3097 and its inclusive MC; generate 500k exclusive MC events for this signal decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100, Vr<10, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; photons with TDC 0–14 (700 ns), opening angle >10° to the nearest charged track, energy above 50 MeV in both barrel and endcap, and at least three photons. No explicit PID is applied; tracks are assigned as pion and proton hypotheses, and Λ → p+π− and anti-Λ → anti-p-π+ are reconstructed via secondary vertex fits that minimize the mass difference and remove used particles. Perform a nominal 5C kinematic fit to Λ anti-Λ γγγ with four-momentum conservation and an η mass constraint on the two photons, χ²<200, and compare to competing 4C fits to Λ anti-Λ γγ and Λ anti-Λ γγγγ and a 5C fit with a π0 mass constraint. Apply a Λ mass window 1.111–1.121 GeV/c², veto M(γΛ) in (1.179,1.204) GeV/c², require M(γ anti-Λ) in (1.184,1.199) GeV/c², and reject backgrounds by demanding the η-hypothesis 5C χ² be smaller than the π0-hypothesis 5C χ² and the three-photon-hypothesis χ² be smaller than the two- and four-photon-hypothesis χ².
