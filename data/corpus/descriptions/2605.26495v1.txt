At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study the radiative decays J/ψ → γ K_S0 K_S0 π0 and J/ψ → γ π0 π0 η using the 708_3097 real data and inclusive MC, with 200k-event exclusive MC generated for each mode (K_S0 → π+π−, π0 → γγ, η → γγ). In the K_S0 K_S0 π0 channel, charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93 and net charge zero, no PID is applied (all tracks are taken as pions), two K_S0 → π+π− are reconstructed through secondary-vertex fits, and at least three photons are required with EMC timing within 700 ns, opening angle >10° to the nearest track, and E>25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap); a π0 → γγ mass-constrained fit (χ²<200, ≥1 π0) precedes a 4C fit to γ K_S0 K_S0 π0 with an added π0 mass constraint (effectively 5C), χ²<200 loose, tightened to 40 offline. In the π0 π0 η channel, no charged tracks are allowed (|cosθ|<0.93) and at least seven photons must pass the same criteria; π0 candidates come from a 1C γγ mass fit (χ²<10, ≥2 π0) and the η from a γγ mass fit (χ²<200, ≥1 η), followed by a 4C kinematic fit to γ π0 π0 η with χ²<200 (tight 40 applied offline). Offline selections also impose |M(π+π−)−m_K_S0|<11 MeV and K_S0 decay length >2σ, |M(γγ)−m_π0|<22 MeV with each π0 photon E>100 MeV, |M(γγ)−m_η|<27 MeV, a comparison of 4C fits assuming 7, 8 or 9 photons, and vetoes against γπ0/γη combinations at the π0 and η masses and γπ0 at the ω mass.
