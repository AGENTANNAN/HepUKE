On the 4.178 GeV data (BOSS 703/4180), study Ds+ → μ+ν_μ via the ψ(4260) → Ds+ Ds*−, Ds*− → γ/π0 Ds− chain. Use the 4180 MeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k exclusive MC events for ψ(4260) → Ds+ Ds*− with Ds+ → μ+ν_μ and Ds*− → γ Ds−. Reconstruct the single-tag Ds− in 13 hadronic modes (KKπ, KKππ0, KsK, KsKπ0, KsKsπ, KsK±ππ, Kππ, πππ, πη, ππ0η and their variants), requiring negative charm; the soft γ/π0 from Ds*− and the Ds* mass constraint are handled internally by the tag algorithm using minimum |ΔE|. On the signal side the remaining tracks must give one μ+ with net charge +1 plus a missing massless ν_μ, with muon PID based on EMC energy in (0.0, 0.3) GeV and a 2D hit-depth requirement in the muon counter. Veto events with unused shower energy above 0.4 GeV and with extra good charged tracks; the ST tag uses M_BC ∈ (2.010, 2.073) GeV/c² and the DT signal ΔE ∈ (−0.05, 0.10) GeV. Perform a 4C kinematic fit with Ds mass constraints (Ds* mass constrained internally), χ² cut 200, and extract the DT yield from an unbinned constrained fit to the MM² distribution. Apply the same selection chain to data, inclusive MC and signal MC.
