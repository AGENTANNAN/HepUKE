Search for the lepton-flavour-violating decay J/ψ → eμ in the 3.097 GeV J/ψ data sample (708_3097 real data plus its inclusive MC), with a 300k-event exclusive MC generated for the single mode J/ψ → e+μ−. Event selection requires exactly two charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm and |Vz|<10 cm, one assigned as the electron and one as the muon; particle ID uses the probability method with the electron identified against μ/π/K at probability >0.8 and the muon against e/π/K at probability >0.001, and any event containing a photon above 25 MeV (barrel cosθ<0.80, endcap 0.86<|cosθ|<0.92, opening angle >20°) is vetoed. This is a counting experiment, so no kinematic fit is performed on the BOSS side; the signal region is formed in ROOT from a back-to-back oppositely charged pair (|Δθ|<1.2°, |Δφ|<1.5°) with E/p>0.96 for the electron, MUC depth >40 cm for the muon and TOF <1.0 ns, then |Σp|/√s ≤0.02 and 0.95 ≤ E_vis/√s ≤1.04. Backgrounds are estimated from the inclusive J/ψ MC and continuum data at 3.773, 3.510 and 3.080 GeV, giving 36.8 ± 4.0 expected events in the signal region.
