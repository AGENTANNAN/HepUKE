Measure e+e− → ωπ0π0 at the 19 R-scan energy points from 2.000 to 3.080 GeV, with ω → π+π−π0 and π0 → γγ, so the observed final state is π+π− plus six photons; use the real data and inclusive MC at every energy point, and generate 100k exclusive signal-MC events per point with a ConExc DIY decay card (mode −2, requiring a user-supplied xs_user.txt). Require exactly two charged tracks with opposite charge, one positive and one negative, net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; photons must satisfy EMC TDC 0–14, barrel energy above 25 MeV, endcap energy above 50 MeV, be at least 10° from any charged track, and at least six good photons are required. Identify pions by probability PID with probability above 0.001 against kaons and protons, requiring exactly one π+ and one π−. Reconstruct π0 candidates from all γγ pairings, keeping the combination with the smallest sum of |M(γγ)−mπ0|, then apply a 1C Kalman fit constraining each γγ pair to the π0 mass with χ²<25 and require at least three π0, assigning the best π0 to the ω. Finally perform a 4C kinematic fit to the π+π−π0π0π0 hypothesis (ω→π+π−π0 plus two π0) with a nominal four-momentum constraint and χ²<200, with tighter post-fit quality cuts applied later in ROOT.
