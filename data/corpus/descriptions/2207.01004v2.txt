On the J/ψ at 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → γ η′, η′ → η π⁰π⁰ with η → γγ and π⁰ → γγ, an all-neutral 7γ final state. Use the 708_3097 J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for the same decay chain. Event selection requires zero charged tracks, hence no PID, and at least seven photons with energy above 25 MeV, polar angle |cosθ|<0.80 in the barrel or 0.86<|cosθ|<0.92 in the endcap, and a 10° minimum angle; the radiative photon is taken as the most energetic photon. Reconstruct one η via a 1C Kalman fit to γγ with χ²<25, and at least two π⁰ via additional 1C Kalman fits to γγ with χ²<25, with additional noted criteria |cosθ_{π⁰}|<0.95 in the π⁰ rest frame and photon timing within ±500 ns of the most energetic photon. Perform an 8C kinematic fit to γ η π⁰π⁰ constraining four-momentum conservation, the η mass, both π⁰ masses, and the η′ mass, with χ²<100 and flagged nominal; if multiple combinations remain, keep the one with the smallest χ². Both the η and π⁰ mass constraints use nominal masses, and the same selection is applied to data, inclusive MC, and the 500k exclusive signal MC.
