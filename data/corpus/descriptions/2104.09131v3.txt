On the ψ(3770) at √s=3.773 GeV, study e+e− → D+ D− with the signal D+ → K+ K_S0 π0, K_S0 → π+π−, and π0 → γγ. Use the 712_3773 real data sample and its corresponding inclusive MC. Tag one D− through six hadronic modes: K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S0π−, K_S0π−π0, K_S0π−π−π+, and K+K−π−, and also define a D+ tag with D+ → K−π+π+, ranked by invariant mass. The signal side requires exactly one charged K+, exactly two photons, a photon separation angle greater than 10°, and net charge +1. Perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation, the two-photon mass constrained to the nominal π0 mass, and nominal D+ mass constraints on both tag candidates, accepting χ² < 200. Generate 2,000,000 exclusive MC events using the same D+ → K+ K_S0 π0, K_S0 → π+π−, π0 → γγ decay card. No explicit tracking, PID, or photon-energy cuts are encoded; the K_S0 and π0 mass windows, flight-distance significance, and final χ² optimization are applied in the later ROOT stage, where the Dalitz-plot amplitude analysis is also performed.
