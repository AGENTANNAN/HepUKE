At √s=3.773 GeV on the ψ(3770), perform a single-tag D− recoil analysis of D+ → K_{S,L}^0 K+ (π0) using the 712_3773 real data and inclusive MC, with 100k-event exclusive MC generated for each of the four signal modes: K_S0 K+, K_S0 K+ π0, K_L0 K+, and K_L0 K+ π0. The tag side uses six D− hadronic modes — K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S0π−, K_S0π−π0, K_S0π+π−π−, and K+K−π− — with charm −1 and tag-mode-dependent ΔE requirements (Table I). On the signal side require one K+ and net charge +1; for the K_S0 modes require one π+ and one π−, reconstruct K_S0 from a secondary vertex with M(π+π−) within ±12 MeV of nominal and decay-length significance >2σ. For π0 modes require two photons with E>25 MeV and opening angle >10°, M(γγ) in 0.110–0.155 GeV, and χ² to the nominal π0 mass <20; for K_L0 modes use a missing massive K_L0 of known mass, with direction from an EMC shower above 0.1 GeV, momentum inferred from ΔE_sig=0, and choose the best candidate by minimum fit χ². Charged tracks are specified only by species counts and net charge, with no additional |cosθ|, Vz, Vr, or separate PID-method criteria encoded. Apply a kinematic fit constraining four-momentum conservation (and γγ to the nominal π0 mass in π0 modes) with χ²<200, and for K_S0 K+ π0 require signal ΔE in −0.057 to 0.040 GeV. Extract yields from an unbinned 2D fit to M_BC(tag) vs M_BC(signal), applying K_S0 (~2%) and K_L0 (~10%) efficiency corrections and fixing the peaking D+ → K_S0 K+(π0), K_S0 → π0π0 backgrounds (~3% and ~5%) in the fit.
