At √s = 3.773 GeV (ψ(3770), 2.93 fb⁻¹), measure D⁺ → K⁻π⁺e⁺ν_e with the tagged-D technique, the opposite D⁻ being single-tagged in six hadronic modes (K⁺π⁻π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰π⁻, K_S⁰π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰π⁻π⁻π⁺, K⁺K⁻π⁻). Use the 3.773 GeV real data set and the matching inclusive MC, plus 500k exclusive MC events of D⁺ → K⁻π⁺e⁺ν_e generated with PHOTOS phase space and PWA-weighted for the efficiency. Good tracks require |Vz|<10 cm, |Vr|<1 cm and |cosθ|<0.93; photons require E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) or E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92) with shower time within 700 ns; π/K separation uses combined dE/dx and TOF likelihoods (P(K)>P(π) for kaons, P(π)>P(K) for pions), π⁰ candidates need 0.115<M(γγ)<0.150 GeV/c² with a 1C mass-constrained fit χ²<200 (both photons in the endcap vetoed), and K_S⁰ need 0.487<M(π⁺π⁻)<0.511 GeV/c² with the secondary vertex within 20 cm of the IP along the beam (no good-track or PID requirement on the daughters). On the tag side select the M_BC window 1.863–1.877 GeV/c², a ΔE consistent with zero within ≈2σ (applied downstream), at most one candidate per tag mode and charge (smallest |ΔE| kept), and veto D⁰ cross-feed for the K_S⁰π⁻π⁻π⁺, K_S⁰π⁻π⁰ and K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰ modes by rejecting events with 1.860<M_BC(D⁰)<1.875 GeV/c² and |ΔE(D⁰)|<0.01 GeV. The signal side requires exactly three good tracks identified as K⁻, π⁺ and e⁺ with net charge +1, where the electron must satisfy combined dE/dx/TOF/EMC likelihoods with P²(e)/(P²(K)+P²(π)+P²(e))>0.8 and P²(e)>0.001 and have EMC energy above 80% of its MDC momentum, together with a missing massless ν_e reconstructed from energy–momentum conservation with |U_miss|=|E_miss−|p_miss||<0.04 GeV and E_miss>0.04 GeV, no unassociated shower above 0.25 GeV (clusters >15° from the closest tracks) and a minimum photon angle of 10°. Finally apply a 4C kinematic fit constraining the total four-momentum of tag + K⁻π⁺e⁺ + ν to the CMS, with a loose χ²<200 kept at this stage and a tighter cut applied downstream in ROOT.
