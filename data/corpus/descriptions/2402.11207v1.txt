In e+e− collisions at √s = 4.13–4.70 GeV, search for e+e− → p p π− d̄ plus its charge conjugate, treating the deuteron or anti-deuteron as potentially missed via partial reconstruction. Use the XYZ scan real data at 4130, 4160, 4180, 4200, 4220, 4230, 4260, 4290, 4315, 4340, 4360, 4380, 4400, 4420, 4440, 4620, 4640, 4660, 4680 and 4700 MeV, together with the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes e+e− → p+ p+ π− d̄ and e+e− → p̄ p̄ π+ d at every energy point. Select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; for the p p π− case require two positive and one negative track with net charge +1, while the charge-conjugate case requires one positive and two negative tracks with net charge −1. Identify particles with the probability method using a 0.001 threshold, separating protons from kaons/pions and pions from kaons/protons, and require two protons plus one π− (or two antiprotons plus one π+). Fit the two protons and π− (or two antiprotons and π+) to a common secondary vertex, requiring χ²_VF<70 and a vertex position within 0.5 cm of (0.12,−0.14) cm in the transverse plane, then use the partial-reconstruction recoil-mass window 1.80–2.30 GeV. For the implemented three-track cases apply pt(recoil)<0.35 GeV/c when |cosθ_r|≤0.93; the notes also record the four-track-case requirements of deuteron mass squared from TOF between 3.2 and 4.1 (GeV/c²)², cosθ'>0.9, and proton/antiproton vetoes. Both charge-conjugate selections share the same chain.
