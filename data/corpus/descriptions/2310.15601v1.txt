In the BESIII scan at 4.128–4.226 GeV (eight points: 4.128, 4.157, 4.178, 4.189, 4.199, 4.209, 4.219, 4.226 GeV, BOSS 705/703), study the doubly Cabibbo-suppressed Ds+ → K+K+π− and Ds+ → K+K+π−π0 decays produced in e+e− → Ds*+Ds−, with Ds*+ → γDs+ or π0Ds+ and a hadronic tag Ds−. Use the eight real-data samples and the corresponding inclusive MC; generate exclusive signal MC at all eight energies for both modes, Ds+ → K+K+π− and Ds+ → K+K+π−π0 with π0 → γγ, in phase space, with no explicit event count specified. Reconstruct the tag Ds− and double-tag Ds+ through K+K−π− and K+K−π+ final states, ranked by invariant-mass difference, while other hadronic tag modes listed in the paper (K+K−π−π0, π−π+π−, KS0K−, η/η′/ρ modes, etc.) are unavailable in the frozen BOSS releases. On the signal side, require the tracks not used by the tags to be exactly two K+ and one π−, with photon multiplicity 0–48 (0 for the K+K+π− mode, at least two for the K+K+π−π0 mode). Resolve the Ds* transition by looping over unused γ and π0 candidates under the Ds*+ → γDs+ and Ds*+ → π0Ds+ hypotheses and choosing the combination with minimum |ΔE|. Perform a 6C kinematic fit constraining four-momentum conservation and both tag Ds invariant masses to the nominal Ds mass, with χ² < 200. Apply M_BC windows per energy of 2.010–2.061, 2.010–2.070, 2.010–2.073, 2.010–2.076, 2.010–2.079, 2.010–2.082, 2.010–2.085, and 2.010–2.088 GeV for the eight points, respectively. The selection uses tagging defaults (local true, beam energy from the database, trimmed mode lists), and no explicit |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr, photon-energy, or PID cuts are encoded; K/π identification is implicit in the tag and signal final-state hypotheses.
