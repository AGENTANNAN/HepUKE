At √s = 4.840, 4.918, and 4.951 GeV (0.9 fb⁻¹ total), search for e+e− → η η ψ(2S) and e+e− → η ψ₀(4360), ψ₀(4360) → η ψ(2S), using partial reconstruction where only one η → γγ is reconstructed and the second η is treated as missing, with ψ(2S) → π+π− J/ψ and J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−. Use the three real-data sets and matching inclusive MC at these energies, plus 100k-event exclusive MC for four modes: ηηψ(2S) with J/ψ → e+e− and with J/ψ → μ+μ−, generated at all three points, and ηψ₀(4360) with each J/ψ lepton channel, generated only at the two higher points. Event selection requires four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge 0, and at least two photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold. PID by the probability method (prob > 0.001) with π/K separation, high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy > 0.6 GeV, muon if < 0.4 GeV) and at least one π+ and one π− required. A 1C Kalman fit constrains the γγ pair to the η mass (χ² < 200, requiring at least one η with M(γγ) in [499.5, 576.9] MeV), followed by a nominal 4C kinematic fit of the η π+π− l+l− system with the second η missing (χ² < 200). Final windows are missing-η recoil mass [516.3, 579.5] MeV, M(l+l−) in [3040.0, 3140.8] MeV, ψ(2S) in [3678.3, 3693.5] MeV, and for the μ+μ− mode M(η_h ψ(2S)) in [4310.8, 4416.4] MeV for the ψ₀(4360), with at least one muon required to have MUC hit depth > 30 cm to suppress π→μ misidentification.
