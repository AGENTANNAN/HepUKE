On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → π+π− J/ψ with J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−. Use the 709_3686 real data set and its inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two J/ψ decay modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge zero; PID by the probability method, pions identified against kaons and protons, high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >1.0 GeV, else muon), requiring 1 π+, 1 π−, and either 1 e+, 1 e− or 1 μ+, 1 μ−. Perform a vertex fit on all four tracks and a 4C kinematic fit (π+π− l+l−), with the lepton-pair mass constrained to the J/ψ window 3.087–3.107 GeV/c² and χ²<200, flagged nominal; both decay modes share the same selection chain.
