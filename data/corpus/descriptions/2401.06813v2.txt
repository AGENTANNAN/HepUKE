Study the double-tag process Λc+ → n K_S0 π+ π0 on e+e− data at seven c.m. energies from 4599.53 to 4698.82 MeV, using real data and inclusive MC at each point. The tag side is a Λc− reconstructed in eight single-tag modes (pK_S0, pK+π−, Λπ−, Λπ−π0, Λπ−π+π−, Σ−π0, Σ0π−, Σ−π+π−; the paper's other three tag modes have no available BOSS channel symbol and Λc double-tagging is not natively supported). Generate 1M exclusive MC events per energy for Λc− → p̄K_S0π+π0 phase space, with K_S0 → π+π− and π0 → γγ. On the signal side require two photons with energy above 25 MeV and opening angle greater than 10°, plus a missing neutron with mass 0.939565 GeV, and apply a kinematic fit with a four-momentum constraint and χ² < 200. Note that the BOSS implementation cannot encode the signal K_S0 reconstruction (secondary-vertex L/σ_L > 2 and π+π− mass window) or π0 reconstruction (γγ mass 0.115–0.150 GeV/c² with 1C χ² < 200), nor the direct-π+ PID requirement L(π) > L(K), the separate vertex cuts on K_S0 daughters (Vz < 20 cm) versus the direct π+ (|Vz| < 10 cm), nor the post-fit peaking-background vetoes in M(nπ±) and M(nπ0).
