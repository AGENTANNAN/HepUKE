At ψ(3770), √s = 3.773 GeV, study D⁰ → a₀(980)⁻ e⁺ν_e with a₀(980)⁻ → ηπ⁻ and η → γγ, using a tag-side D⁰ reconstructed in the Kπ, Kππ⁰, and Kπππ modes. Use the 7.93 fb⁻¹ ψ(3770) real data sample and its inclusive MC, plus 200,000 exclusive-MC events generated for D⁰ → a₀(980)⁻ e⁺ν_e, a₀(980)⁻ → ηπ⁻, η → γγ with the tag anti-D⁰ → K⁺π⁻ in phase space. The tag side requires charm = −1 and a ΔE window from −0.064 to 0.035 GeV. The signal side requires exactly one π⁻, one e⁺, two photons, net charge zero, and one missing neutrino; no explicit |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr, or photon-energy cuts are encoded beyond these requirements. A kinematic fit applies four-momentum conservation and constrains the γγ invariant mass to the nominal η mass, with χ² < 200. Electron PID uses fixed v1 thresholds; the intended E/pc > 0.8 and combined e/π/K likelihood criteria are not expressible in the tag framework, while K_L⁰, extra π⁰, and extra charged-track vetoes are applied later in ROOT. Signal extraction is a two-dimensional unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to M(ηπ⁻) versus U = E_miss − c|p_miss|, using a Flatté line shape for the a₀(980).
