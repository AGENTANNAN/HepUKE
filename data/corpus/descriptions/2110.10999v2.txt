At ψ(3770), 3.773 GeV, use the 712_3773 real data and inclusive MC to study the doubly Cabibbo-suppressed decays D+ → K+π0π0 and D+ → K+π0η, tagging the D− through three hadronic modes (K+π−π−, K_S^0π−, K+π−π−π0) with charm −1. The K+π0π0 signal side requires one K+ and four photons, while the K+π0η signal side requires one K+ and two photons. Both channels use a 4C kinematic fit constraining four-momentum with χ² < 200 and double-tag reconstruction using the database beam energy and local mode. Offline, the π0π0 invariant mass is required outside 0.388–0.588 GeV/c² to veto D+ → K+ K_S^0, and the D+D− opening angle must exceed 167° to suppress non-DD̄ background; the K*+ → K+π0 sub-resonance is extracted by a simultaneous fit to K*+ signal and sideband regions. No explicit charged-track, photon-energy, or PID cuts are encoded beyond these particle multiplicities, and no exclusive MC is generated. Both signal-mode selections are run on the same real-data and inclusive-MC datasets.
