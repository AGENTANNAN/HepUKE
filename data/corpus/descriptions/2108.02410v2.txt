On the 3.51–4.60 GeV energy scan, measure e+e− → Λ Λ̄ with Λ → pπ− and Λ̄ → p̄π+. Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC across the scan, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for e+e− → Λ Λ̄ (Λ→pπ−, Λ̄→p̄π+) at the scan points. Select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring exactly two positive and two negative tracks with net charge zero. Identify protons by probability-based PID against kaons and pions with probability >0.001, requiring at least one proton and one antiproton, then take the remaining positive and negative tracks as π+ and π−. Reconstruct Λ and Λ̄ via secondary vertex fits of pπ− and p̄π+ pairs using mass-difference minimization, with secondary-vertex χ²<500 and positive decay length, and require the Λ mass within |M(pπ)−m_Λ|<5 MeV/c² with sideband subtraction for background. Perform a 4C kinematic fit on the Λ Λ̄ system with nominal four-momentum constraints, χ²<200. Extract the signal yield as N_S − (1/4)ΣN_Bi and fit the dressed cross section.
