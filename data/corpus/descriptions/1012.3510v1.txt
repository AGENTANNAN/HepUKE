On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ X(1835), X(1835) → π+π− η' in two η' decay modes: η' → γ ρ0 with ρ0 → π+π−, and η' → π+π− η with η → γγ. Use the 3.097 GeV real data set 708_3097 and its inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<2 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero; photons with EMC TDC 0–14, angle to the nearest track greater than 5°, and 100 MeV energy threshold in both barrel and endcap, requiring at least two photons in mode 1 and at least three in mode 2. Pion identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating pions from kaons/protons, requiring at least two π+ and one π− (at least three of the four charged tracks identified as pions). Perform a 4C kinematic fit over the full final state (γγπ+π−π+π− for mode 1, γγγπ+π−π+π− for mode 2) with χ²<40, and for mode 2 an additional mass-constrained fit of the two photons to the η mass (5C in total), χ²<40, requiring at least one η. Apply background vetoes: in mode 1, |M(γγ)−mπ0|<0.04 GeV/c², |M(γγ)−mη|<0.03 GeV/c², 0.72<M(γγ)<0.82 GeV/c², |M(γπ+π−)−mη|<0.007 GeV/c², plus ρ and η' windows |M(π+π−)−mρ|<0.2 GeV/c² and |M(γπ+π−)−mη'|<0.015 GeV/c²; in mode 2, |M(γγ)−mπ0|<0.04 GeV/c² for all photon pairs, |M(γγ)−mη|<0.03 GeV/c² preselection, and |M(π+π−η)−mη'|<0.01 GeV/c².
