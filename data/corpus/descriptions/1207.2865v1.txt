Measure the number of J/ψ events collected by BESIII in 2009 by counting inclusive J/ψ events. Use the 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data (225.3 M events) and its inclusive MC, plus ψ(2S) data at 3.686 GeV (106 M events) and its inclusive MC; generate 1M-event exclusive MC for ψ(2S) → π+π− J/ψ with J/ψ → anything, and for direct J/ψ → anything. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<15 cm, Vr<1 cm and momentum p<2.0 GeV/c, requiring at least two good tracks; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold and EMC timing 0<T<15, with no PID and no kinematic fit. At event level require visible energy E_vis > 1.0 GeV, and for exactly two charged tracks demand both momenta be below 1.5 GeV/c to reject Bhabha and dimuon events, with each track's EMC deposit below 1 GeV. The detection efficiency is determined from ψ(2S) → π+π− J/ψ data using soft pions (|cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm, |Vz|<15 cm, p<0.4 GeV/c) and a recoil-mass fit to the π+π− pair, with the ψ(2S) and J/ψ inclusive-MC ratio as correction factor; luminosity is measured from e+e− → γγ.
