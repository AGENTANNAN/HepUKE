Using the ψ(3686) data set (709_3686) and its inclusive MC (1.06×10⁸ events), study ψ(3686) → ω K_S⁰ K⁺ π⁻ (ω → π⁺π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻, π⁰ → γγ) and ψ(3686) → ω K⁺ K⁻ π⁰ (ω → π⁺π⁻π⁰, π⁰ → γγ), generating 100k-event exclusive MC for each mode. For Mode I, select at least two positive and two negative charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<2 cm and zero net charge, identify at least one K⁺ by the probability method (prob>0.001, against π and p), remove identified kaons from the pion lists and treat the remaining tracks as pions, reconstruct a K_S⁰ from a secondary-vertex fit to an opposite-charge pair (closest to the nominal mass), require at least two photons with TDC 0–700 ns, E>25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap) and >10° from any track, at least one π⁰ from a photon pair constrained to the nominal π⁰ mass with χ²<25, and a 4C kinematic fit to K_S⁰ K⁺ π⁺ π⁻ π⁻ π⁰ with χ²<40. For Mode II, select exactly two positive and two negative tracks with the same tracking cuts, identify at least one K⁺ and one K⁻ by the probability method, remove kaons from the pions, require at least four photons with the same TDC/energy/angle requirements, store χ² values from competing 4C fits to K⁺ K⁻ π⁺ π⁻ 3γ and K⁺ K⁻ π⁺ π⁻ 5γ, require at least two π⁰'s from mass-constrained photon pairs with χ²<25, and perform a 6C kinematic fit to K⁺ K⁻ π⁺ π⁻ π⁰ π⁰ with χ²<100. The ROOT-level analysis then applies the K_S⁰ (0.489–0.505 GeV/c²) and ω (0.743–0.823 GeV/c²) signal windows, with sidebands and J/ψ recoil-mass vetoes as described in the notes.
