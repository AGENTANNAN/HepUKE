On the ψ(2S) (3.686 GeV), study ψ' → γ χ_cJ → γ_l γ_h V for the three vector mesons V = φ, ρ0, ω, with φ → K+K−, ρ0 → π+π−, and ω → π+π−π0 (π0 → γγ). Use the ψ(2S) real data and inclusive MC, plus the 3.65 GeV continuum real data and inclusive MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the three decay modes (ψ'→γγφ, ψ'→γγρ0, ψ'→γγω), each with its own algorithm and selection chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge zero; photons within the EMC TDC window 0–14, at least 10° from any charged track, with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds and at least two photons (at least four in the ω mode, to include the π0 daughters). PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying only the lower-momentum track: for the φ mode ≥1 K+ and ≥1 K− against π/p, for the ρ0 and ω modes ≥1 π+ and ≥1 π− against K/p. Kinematic fitting: the φ and ρ0 modes use a 4C fit to γ γ h+h− with χ²<200 (nominal, mass sidebands and the tighter χ²≤100 applied later); the ω mode first reconstructs the π0 via a mass-constrained fit to two photons (χ²<25, ≥1 π0), then performs a 5C fit to γ γ π0 π+ π− with the π0 mass constrained and χ²<200.
