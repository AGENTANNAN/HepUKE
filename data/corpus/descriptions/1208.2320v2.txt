Study ψ(2S) → K+K−π0 and ψ(2S) → K+K−η, together with ψ' → ηφ and ψ' → π0φ, all sharing the γγ K+K− final state and one common selection chain. Use the 3.686 GeV ψ(2S) real data (106 M events, 156.4 pb−1) and its inclusive MC, plus 3.773 GeV (2.9 fb−1) and 3.65 GeV (43 pb−1) real data and their inclusive MC for continuum/QED-background studies; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for ψ'→K+K−π0, ψ'→K+K−η, ψ'→π0φ, ψ'→ηφ, for the continuum γ*→K+K−π0 and γ*→ηφ at each of the two off-resonance points, and for the γχc2→K+K−π0, γχc2→K+K−η and γγ_FSR K+K− background modes. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track with net charge zero; photon candidates timed within the EMC window, at least 10° away from any charged track, with E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) and E>50 MeV in the endcaps (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), requiring at least two photons (2–10 retained later). PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying both tracks as kaons against π and p (one K+ and one K−). Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to the γγ K+K− hypothesis constrained to the initial e+e− four-momentum, with a loose χ²<200 in BOSS (χ²≤20 applied at the ROOT level). All decay modes share this identical chain.
