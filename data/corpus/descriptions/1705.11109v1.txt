At √s = 4.600 GeV (567 pb⁻¹, just above the Λc+Λ̄c− threshold), measure Λc+ → Σ−π+π+ (π0) with Σ− → nπ− via the single-/double-tag method: the anti-Λc− is reconstructed in eleven hadronic tag modes (pK_S0, pK−π+, pK−π+π0, pK_S0π0, pK_S0π+π−, Λπ+, Λπ+π0, Λπ+π−π+, Σ+π0, Σ0π+, Σ+π+π−) and the recoiling Λc+ is the signal side. Use the 4.600 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus 200k-event exclusive phase-space MC for each of the two signal modes, Λc+ → Σ−π+π+ and Λc+ → Σ−π+π+π0 (π0 → γγ), with the tag side generated generically. Tag-side tracks require |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with p/K/π separation by combined dE/dx and TOF likelihoods, forming K_S0 → π+π−, Λ → pπ−, Σ0 → γΛ, Σ+ → pπ0 and π0 → γγ candidates; per mode keep the minimum-|ΔE| candidate, apply the mode-dependent ΔE windows (≈±3σ), and obtain the ST yield from a fit to M_BC in 2.280–2.296 GeV/c². Signal-side selection requires three tracks recoiling against the tag with |cosθ|<0.93, the two π+ within |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm (the π− exempt) and all three pions satisfying L(π)>L(K); photons must be isolated EMC clusters with |cosθ|≤0.80 (barrel) or 0.86–0.92 (endcap), E>25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap), >10° from the nearest charged track and EMC time in (0,700) ns, with π0 from 0.110 < M(γγ) < 0.155 GeV/c² mass-constrained to the nominal π0. The final state is two π+, one π− and net charge +1, with no photons for Λc+ → Σ−π+π+ and two photons for Λc+ → Σ−π+π+π0, the neutron from Σ− → nπ− being undetected and inferred from four-momentum conservation. Both channels undergo a 4C kinematic fit (tag + π+π+π−(+γγ) + n to the beam energy) constraining the tag Λc+ to its nominal mass (and the γγ pair to the π0 mass in the π0 mode) with χ² < 200, and the signal yields are extracted from an unbinned fit to M_nπ− − M_n.
