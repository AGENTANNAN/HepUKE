On ψ(3686), study e+e− → γ χ_cJ → γ Λ anti-Λ, with Λ → p π− and anti-Λ → anti-p π+, using the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus a 10M-event exclusive MC sample generated for ψ(2S) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → Λ anti-Λ, Λ → p π−, anti-Λ → anti-p π+. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93 and |Vz|<20 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge 0; PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifies protons against pions, and demands at least one proton and one antiproton. The identified protons and antiprotons are removed from the generic charged-track lists, the remaining positives and negatives are treated as π+ and π−, and Λ → p π− and anti-Λ → anti-p π+ candidates are formed by secondary-vertex fits that minimize the mass difference and remove used tracks. Photons are selected with TDC 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, and at least one photon. A kinematic fit to γ Λ anti-Λ with a four-momentum constraint and nominal flag is applied with χ² < 40, rejecting γΛ and γ anti-Λ invariant masses in [1.183, 1.216] and [1.183, 1.219] GeV/c², respectively; the Λ/anti-Λ mass window [1.108, 1.123] GeV/c² and decay-length significance >2.0 are also required. A TF-PWA is performed on the selected events, with χ_cJ masses and widths fixed to PDG values except the χ_c0 width floated.
