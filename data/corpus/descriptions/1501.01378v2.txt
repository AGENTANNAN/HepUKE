On ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, measure y_CP from quantum-correlated e+e− → D0 D0bar using doubly-tagged events with one D in a CP eigenstate (K+K−, π+π−, K_S0 π0 π0, K_S0 π0, K_S0 ω) and the partner decaying semileptonically (K− e+ ν_e or K− μ+ ν_μ). Use the 2.92 fb^-1 3.773 GeV real data plus inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the ten CP-tag × semileptonic combinations. Tag tracks require |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with hadron PID from combined MDC dE/dx and TOF likelihoods (K: L(K)>L(π), π: vice versa; K_S0 daughters exempt and using a looser 20 cm longitudinal IP), mode-dependent ΔE windows (K+K−: ±0.020; π+π−: ±0.030; K_S0 π0 π0: [−0.080,0.045]; K_S0 π0: [−0.070,0.040]; K_S0 ω: [−0.050,0.030] GeV), retaining only the smallest-|ΔE| candidate, and the M_BC signal region 1.855–1.875 GeV/c². Photons are EMC showers separated by >10σ from any track with E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) and E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.84<|cosθ|<0.92); π0 and η are built from photon pairs with 1C mass constraints (0.115–0.150 and 0.505–0.570 GeV/c²), ω → π+π−π0 uses 0.7600–0.8050 GeV/c² with sideband subtraction, and the K+K−/π+π− tags receive a cosmic/Bhabha veto. The signal side requires exactly two oppositely charged unused tracks with net charge 0 and a massless missing neutrino; the electron must satisfy L'(e)>0.001 and R'(e)>0.8, the muon has EMC deposit <0.3 GeV and R_l'(e)<0.8, and the Kμ channel additionally requires M(Kμ)<1.65 GeV/c² and unmatched EMC energy <0.2 GeV. Both algorithms apply a 4C kinematic fit constraining the four-momentum and the tag D0 mass to its nominal value, with χ²<200.
