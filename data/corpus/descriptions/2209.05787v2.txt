On ψ(3770) at √s = 3.773 GeV, search for baryon- and lepton-number-violating D± → n(anti-n) e± decays via a double-tag method, covering both Δ|B−L| = 0 (D+→anti-n e+, D−→n e−) and Δ|B−L| = 2 (D+→n e+, D−→anti-n e−). Use 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC (sample 712_3773); the decay card encodes ψ(3770)→D+D− with D+→anti-n e+ and D−→n e−, but no exclusive-MC sample size is specified. The single-tag D± is reconstructed in six hadronic modes—K−π+π+, K−π+π+π0, K_S0π+, K_S0π+π0, K_S0π+π+π−, and K+K−π+—ranked by invariant mass, with charge-conjugate modes for D−. On the signal side require exactly one electron (e+ for D+, e− for D−) and zero photons, treat the (anti-)neutron as a missing particle of unknown mass, require net charge ±1 and no additional charged tracks; no explicit |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr, photon-energy threshold, or π/K PID method is encoded, while ST candidates must satisfy ΔE within (−25,+25) MeV (or (−55,+40) MeV for modes containing π0) and M_BC ∈ (1.863,1.877) GeV/c² from an ARGUS fit. Perform a 2C kinematic fit constraining four-momentum conservation, the tag mass to the nominal D+ mass, and the electron plus missing particle to the nominal D∓ mass, with χ² cut 9999 (only requiring convergence). Post-fit, require an EMC shower within 30° of the fitted neutron direction, a further 10° (15°) opening-angle cut, and a GBDT MVA on shower-shape variables (E_tot, N_hit, A20, A42 Zernike moments) in neutron momentum bins. ST yields come from binned maximum-likelihood fits to M_BC (N_ST^tot(D+) = 758.2k, N_ST^tot(D−) = 763.9k), and DT signal yields from unbinned ML fits to the fitted neutron mass, with no significant signal; 90% CL upper limits are B(D+→anti-n e+) < 1.43×10−5 and B(D+→n e+) < 2.91×10−5.
