At seven c.m. energy points from 4.59953 to 4.69882 GeV, perform a double-tag measurement of Λc+ production, using the hadronic tag side anti-Λc− (charm −1, all tag modes) against three signal modes: Λc+ → Λ K_S0 K+, Λc+ → Σ0 K_S0 K+, and Λc+ → Ξ0 K_S0 π+ (with charge conjugates). Take the seven corresponding real-data scan samples together with their matched inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC from a decay card covering all three signal modes plus their conjugate chains (Λ→pπ−, Σ0→Λγ, Ξ0→Λπ0, K_S0→π+π−, π0→γγ). On the signal side require one K_S0 reconstructed from π+π− through a secondary-vertex fit with decay-length significance L/σ_L > 2, plus charged multiplicities of 1 π− and 1 π+ with 1 K+ for the first two modes, and 1 π− and 2 π+ for the Ξ0 mode, with net charge +1 in all cases. Identify the unreconstructed Λ, Σ0 and Ξ0 by partial reconstruction through the missing mass (nominal masses 1.115683, 1.192642 and 1.31486 GeV), which the tag framework implements as a missing-particle constraint, and perform a 1C kinematic fit with the four-momentum constrained, keeping events with χ² < 200. The bachelor π+ is identified by likelihood selection requiring L(π) > L(K) and L(π) > L(p); the K_S0 mass window and the missing-mass windows for Λ/Σ0/Ξ0 are applied at the ROOT level.
