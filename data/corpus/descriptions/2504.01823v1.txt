At the J/ψ (√s=3.097 GeV), study J/ψ→γηc with ηc→ωφ, ω→π+π−π0, φ→K+K−, π0→γγ, giving a 3γπ+π−K+K− final state. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1,000,000 exclusive MC events for this signal decay chain. Select exactly four charged tracks, two positive and two negative with net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm, and require at least three photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, with the π0 decay-angle cut |cosθ_decay|<0.95. Particle ID uses the probability method (prob>0.001) with π/K separation, requiring one K+, one K−, one π+, and one π−. Reconstruct π0→γγ by a Kalman fit constraining the two-photon mass to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<25, then perform a 5C kinematic fit to K+K−π+π−γγγ with four-momentum conservation and the π0 mass constraint, χ²<20, flagged nominal. Apply the ω and φ mass windows |M_π+π−π0−M_ω|<40 MeV/c² and |M_K+K−−M_φ|<15 MeV/c², veto η′ with 943<M_π+π−3γ<969 MeV/c², require a successful primary vertex fit on K+K−π+π− before the kinematic fit, and use the Q-weight method for non-ω/φ background subtraction in the ηc fit.
