On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → Ξ0 Ξ̄0 with Ξ0 → π0 Λ, Λ → pπ−, and the charge-conjugate chain (π0 → γγ throughout). Use the 2.712×10⁹ ψ(3686) real data sample together with the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC for the full Ξ0 Ξ̄0 decay chain. Charged tracks are required to satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with at least two positive and two negative tracks; particle identification uses the probability method (prob_cut 0.001) identifying protons against kaons and pions and demanding at least one proton and one antiproton (the paper's momentum-dependent p>0.5 GeV/c proton/pion assignment is approximated by this standard probability PID). Λ and Λ̄ are formed from pπ− and p̄π+ pairs through secondary-vertex fits that minimize the mass difference, and photons are selected with TDC 0–14 and 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, requiring at least four. A 4C kinematic fit is performed on Λ Λ̄ γγγγ, constraining the two photon pairs to the π0 mass, with χ²<200, after which Ξ0/Ξ̄0 are reconstructed from π0Λ combinations using mass windows |M(π0Λ)−m_Ξ0|<15 MeV/c² and |M(pπ)−m_Λ|<5 MeV/c², positive Λ decay length, and sideband subtraction for background estimation, followed by an unbinned maximum-likelihood fit for the polarization and CP observables.
