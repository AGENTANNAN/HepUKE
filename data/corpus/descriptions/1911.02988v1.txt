On the ψ(3686) data set 709_3686 at 3.686 GeV (real data and inclusive MC), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → φ φ η, φ → K+K−, η → γγ; generate 500k exclusive MC events for ψ(3686) → γ χ_c0, χ_c0 → φ φ η with φ → K+K− and η → γγ. Select events with exactly two positive and two negative charged tracks satisfying |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge zero, and at least three photons with E>25 MeV in the barrel or >50 MeV in the endcap, each at least 10° away from any charged track. Identify kaons by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut against pions and protons, requiring two K+ and two K−, and treat all charged tracks as kaons. Reconstruct η → γγ with a constrained kinematic fit requiring M(γγ) at the nominal η mass, χ²<25 and at least one η, using the γγ pair closest to the nominal η mass within [0.52, 0.58] GeV/c². Then perform a 4C kinematic fit to γ K+K−K+K−η with nominal four-momentum constraints and χ²<40, requiring two φ candidates in M(K+K−)∈[1.005, 1.035] GeV/c² selected by minimizing ΔM²=(M1−mφ)²+(M2−mφ)², with the photon not used in the η treated as the radiative photon. Suppress backgrounds with a π⁰ veto on all γγ combinations in [0.115, 0.150] GeV/c², an M(γη) veto in [1.00, 1.04] GeV/c², and an η-recoil mass <3.05 GeV/c², and define χ_c0, χ_c1, χ_c2 signal regions as [3.38, 3.45], [3.48, 3.54], and [3.54, 3.60] GeV/c². Apply helix-parameter corrections from a K_S⁰ control sample and an E1 weighting factor (Eγ/Eγ0)³ for efficiency.
