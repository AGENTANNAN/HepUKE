On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_c0, χ_c0 → 2(π+π−) (the same γ 4π final state also covering η_c(2S) and χ_cJ). Use 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1,000,000-event exclusive MC for ψ(3686) → γ χ_c0 → γ π+π−π+π− (PHSP). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, angle to any track >10°, at least one; PID by the probability method with prob cut 0.001, pion identification against kaon and proton, requiring 2 π+ and 2 π−. Additionally veto background: all π+π− recoil masses must lie outside the J/ψ region (3.0–3.2 GeV/c²), all γπ+π− recoil masses must also lie outside that J/ψ region, and all π+π− cosθ must be within [−0.999, 0.988] to reject γ → e+e− conversions. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γ π+π−π+π− with a common vertex fit on the four pions, χ² cut 40, and also use a 3C fit (omitting photon energy) for signal extraction. Apply an FSR correction factor f_FSR = 2.00 ± 0.02 to MC and a helix-parameter correction to charged tracks in MC.
