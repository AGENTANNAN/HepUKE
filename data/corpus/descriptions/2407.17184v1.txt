On the ψ(3686), search for ψ(3686) → γ η_c(2S) and ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ, with η_c(2S)/χ_cJ → K+K−η′, using two η′ decay modes: η′→π+π−γ and η′→π+π−η with η→γγ. Use 3.686 GeV real data (2.712×10^9 ψ(3686) events) and 3.650 GeV continuum data (401 pb−1), with inclusive MC at both energies, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each mode: ψ(3686)→γη_c(2S), η′→π+π−γ and ψ(3686)→γχ_c1, η′→π+π−η, η→γγ. In both modes, charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons are accepted with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap thresholds within EMC timing 0–700 ns, requiring at least two photons for η′→π+π−γ and at least three for η′→π+π−η. PID combines dE/dx and TOF, minimizing χ²_PID together with the fit χ² to choose the best track species. Mode I uses a 4C fit to K+K−π+π−γ with χ²<25, an η′ window M(π+π−γ)∈(0.94,0.97) GeV/c² (sidebands [0.8,0.9] and [1.0,1.1]), and π0, η, J/ψ, χ_cJ, π+π−J/ψ, and φ vetoes. Mode II first performs a 1C fit on all photon pairs, constraining M(γγ) to the nominal η mass and choosing the best η candidate (at least one η), then a 4C fit to K+K−π+π−γγ with χ²<20, using M(π+π−η)∈(0.93,0.98) GeV/c² (sidebands [0.85,0.90] and [1.0,1.1]), M(γγ)∈(0.52,0.57) GeV/c², and π0, η-recoil J/ψ, and φ vetoes. After selection, a 3C fit (omitting the radiative photon energy in mode I) gives M(K+K−η′) for a simultaneous unbinned maximum-likelihood fit of both η′ modes.
