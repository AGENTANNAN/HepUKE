At the ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV, 7.9 fb⁻¹), perform a double-tag amplitude analysis of D⁰→π⁺π⁻η and D⁺→π⁺π⁰η. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each signal mode: ψ(3770)→D⁰D̄⁰, D⁰→π⁺π⁻η, D̄⁰→K⁺π⁻, η→γγ; and ψ(3770)→D⁺D⁻, D⁺→π⁺π⁰η, D⁻→K⁺π⁻π⁻, η→γγ, π⁰→γγ. For D⁰→π⁺π⁻η, tag the opposite-side D in Kπ, Kππ⁰, Kππ⁰π⁰, and Kπππ modes; on the signal side require one π⁺ and one π⁻ plus exactly two photons with a minimum photon angle of 10°; veto events with |M(π⁺π⁻)−m_K_S⁰|<0.03 GeV/c²; and apply a BDTG η classifier using M(γγ), the η fit χ², and the photon helicity angle, retaining 83% signal and rejecting 78% background. For D⁺→π⁺π⁰η, tag the opposite-side D in Kππ, Kπππ⁰, K_Sπ, K_Sππ⁰, K_Sπππ, and KKπ modes; on the signal side require one π⁺ and 4–48 photons with the same 10° minimum photon angle; and apply the η BDTG classifier retaining 77% signal and rejecting 84% background. Both channels use a 4-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ² cut 200; in D⁰ one γγ pair is constrained to the nominal η mass, while in D⁺ one γγ pair is constrained to the nominal π⁰ mass and another to the nominal η mass. The same selection chain is run on the real data, inclusive MC, and the corresponding exclusive MC samples.
