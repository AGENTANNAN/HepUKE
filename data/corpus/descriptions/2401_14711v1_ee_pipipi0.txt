Study e+e− → π+π−π0 at 19 CM energies from 2.00 to 3.08 GeV using the R-scan real data in that range and 50k-event exclusive MC per energy point generated with ConExc mode 7 for e+e− → π+π−π0 (π0 → γγ), with the beam energy set per run from the measured CM energy. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track, and net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, at least two, and photon–track angle >10°. Pion identification uses the probability method with prob >0.001, identifying pions against kaons and protons, requiring at least one π+ and one π−. A π0 mass-constrained fit on the two photons (χ²<25) selects at least one π0, followed by a 4C kinematic fit to π+π−π0 with χ²<50, flagged nominal.
