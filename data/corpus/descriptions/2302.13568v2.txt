At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → Σ+ anti-Σ− with Σ+ → p γ and anti-Σ− → anti-p π0 (π0 → γγ), together with the charge-conjugate channel Σ+ → p π0, anti-Σ− → anti-p γ. Use 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate a one-million-event exclusive MC sample for the signal mode J/ψ → Σ+ anti-Σ−, Σ+ → p γ, anti-Σ− → anti-p π0, π0 → γγ. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least one positive and one negative track; photons are selected with EMC TDC 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, angle to any track >10°, and at least three photons. Proton identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating protons from kaons and pions, providing the proton and antiproton candidates for the fit. Perform a 5C kinematic fit (p anti-p γγγ) with four-momentum and one γγ mass constraint to the nominal π0 mass, χ² < 30, nominal; a second 6C fit with two π0 mass constraints on the same photons is used to veto the competing Σ+ → p π0 background if its χ² is smaller. Require the single-tag anti-Σ− candidate to satisfy |M(anti-p π0) − M(Σ−)| < 13.5 MeV/c², choose the best candidate by minimum mass difference, impose a proton decay-length veto L/σ_L > 1.5 to suppress Δ(1232)+ → p π0, and define the double-tag signal region as 0.215 < p_p (Σ+ rest frame) < 0.235 GeV/c. If multiple photon candidates remain, keep the one with smallest 5C χ². Both charge-conjugate modes share the same selection chain.
