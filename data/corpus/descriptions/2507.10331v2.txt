On ψ(3686), search for the CLFV decay ψ(3686) → e± μ∓ (both e+μ− and charge-conjugate e−μ+). Use the 709_3686 real data set and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for ψ(3686) → e+ μ− via PHSP. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two tracks with net charge 0; no photons above 25 MeV (barrel) or 50 MeV (endcap) with TDC in [0,700] ns and angle to track >10°. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut; high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) are treated as leptons and identified as electrons if E/p>0.94, requiring one positive lepton, one negative lepton, one positive electron, and one negative electron. Apply a 4C kinematic fit to e+ μ− (nominal) with χ²<200. Additional cuts: back-to-back e-mu with |Δθ|<1.4° and |Δφ|<1.7°; Bhabha veto on muons with 0.82<|cosθ_mu|<0.86 EMC gap and MUC-MDC polar angle diff <20°, azimuthal diff <30°; signal region |Σp_i|/√s ≤0.03 and 0.97≤ΣE_i/√s ≤1.04. The e−μ+ charge-conjugate mode is handled analogously.
