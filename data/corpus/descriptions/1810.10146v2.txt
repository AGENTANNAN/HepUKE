On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686)→γχ_cJ, χ_cJ→ωφ (J=0,1,2 share the same final state), ω→π+π−π0, φ→K+K−, π0→γγ, using the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k exclusive MC events for ψ(3686)→γχ_c0 with χ_c0→ωφ, ω→π+π−π0, φ→K+K−, π0→γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge zero; photons with TDC 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, and at least three photons. Kaons are identified by the probability method with prob>0.001 against pions/protons, removed from the charged-track lists, and the surviving positive and negative tracks are assigned as π+ and π−; a π0 is formed from two photons by minimizing the γγ mass difference. A 4C kinematic fit to γγπ0K+K−π+π− is then performed with χ²<60, after which require |M(π+π−π0)−M_ω|<0.05 GeV/c² and |M(K+K−)−M_φ|<0.015 GeV/c², veto ψ(3686)→π+π−J/ψ by |M_recoil(π+π−)−M_J/ψ|>8 MeV/c², and, when more than three photons are present, require the 3γ K+K−π+π− hypothesis to have a smaller 4C χ² than the 4γ hypothesis.
