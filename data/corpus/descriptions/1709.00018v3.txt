On the J/ψ and ψ(3686), study ψ → π+π−η′, η′ → ηπ+π−, η → γγ. Use 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, and 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for J/ψ → π+π−η′ and 500k for ψ(3686) → π+π−η′, both with η′ → ηπ+π−, η → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, at least two, at least 10° from any track, and EMC TDC within 0–14. PID by the probability method with prob cut 0.001, identifying pions against kaons, requiring at least 2 π+ and 2 π−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γγπ+π−π+π−, χ² cut 40, flagged nominal. Both energy points share the same selection chain.
