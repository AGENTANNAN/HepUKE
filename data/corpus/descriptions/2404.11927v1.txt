On the 4.84, 4.91, and 4.95 GeV real datasets (707) and the matching inclusive-MC samples, search for e+e− → K+K− ψ(3770), ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar, reconstructing K+K− plus a D meson and extracting the signal from a 2D fit to RM(K+K−D) versus RM(K+K−), with each energy point run separately. Generate 200k exclusive-MC events for the signal decay chain with D0 → K−π+ and D0bar → K+π−. Event selection requires at least two positive and two negative tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm, plus at least two photons with EMC timing 0–14, angle to tracks >10°, and energy thresholds of 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap). PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001 for K/π separation, two photons are fitted to the π0 nominal mass with χ²<25, and a final 4C kinematic fit to K+K−π+π−γγ with nominal masses requires χ²<200. In the D reconstruction, nine tag modes are used (D0 → K+π−, K+π−π0, K+π+π−π−; D− → K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, KSπ−, KSππ0, KSπ−π−π+, K+K−π−), choosing the D candidate closest to the nominal D mass and applying a 1C fit constraining the Dbar mass with χ²<13. Events with |M(K+K−)−Mφ|<0.02 GeV/c² are vetoed, and the bachelor kaons are the lowest-momentum kaon pair not used in the D reconstruction.
