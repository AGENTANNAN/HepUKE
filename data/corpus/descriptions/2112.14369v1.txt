At √s = 3.097 GeV, study the electromagnetic Dalitz decay J/ψ → e+e− π+π− η′, proceeding through the intermediate states X(1835), X(2120) and X(2370), in two independent channels: η′ → γπ+π− (Mode I) and η′ → π+π−η with η → γγ (Mode II). Use the 3.097 GeV real data and the corresponding inclusive MC, together with a 500k-event exclusive signal MC generated with the decay chain J/ψ → e+e−π+π−η′, η′ → π+π−γ or π+π−η, and η → γγ. Event selection requires exactly six charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm and net charge zero, and photons above 25 MeV in the barrel / 50 MeV in the endcap, at least one photon in Mode I and at least two in Mode II. Particle ID uses the probability method, with tracks above 1.0 GeV treated as leptons (electron if the associated EMC energy exceeds 0.6 GeV) and the remaining tracks identified as pions against kaons and protons, demanding 1 e+, 1 e−, 2 π+ and 2 π−. Mode I then performs a 4C kinematic fit to e+e− 4π γ with χ² < 200, flagged nominal, while Mode II first reconstructs η → γγ via a mass-constrained Kalman fit (χ² < 25, at least one η) and then applies a 5C fit combining the four-momentum constraint with the η nominal-mass constraint, also with χ² < 200 and nominal flag. Both modes share the same track, photon and PID requirements, and the looser BOSS-level χ² = 200 is used in lieu of the paper's optimized χ² < 60, with the tight η′ mass windows (|M(γπ+π−) − m_η′| < 15 MeV/c² and |M(π+π−η) − m_η′| < 8.1 MeV/c²) and the γ-conversion, ω/φ and γe+e− vetoes applied later at ROOT level.
