On the R-scan from 2.00 to 3.08 GeV (19 CM energies), study e+e− → π+π−π0 using real data and 50k-event exclusive MC per point generated with ConExc (mode 7 for continuum π+π−π0, including Born and ISR up to second order). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, |Vr|<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with EMC TDC 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV, endcap >50 MeV, angle to nearest track >10°, at least two photons; PID by probability method (cut 0.001) identifying pions against kaons and protons, requiring at least one π+ and one π−. Perform a Kalman fit on the two photons constraining their invariant mass to the nominal π0 mass (χ²<25, at least one π0), then a 4C kinematic fit on π+π−π0 (χ²<50, nominal). Apply post-fit cuts: E/p<0.8 for electron rejection, opening angle θ(π+,π−)<160°, π0 mass window |M(γγ)−Mπ0|<15 MeV/c², and helicity |cosθ_h|<0.8 for the partial-wave analysis; extract the Born cross section using the ISR correction factor from ConExc.
