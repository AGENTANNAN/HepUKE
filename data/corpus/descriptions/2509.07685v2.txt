At ψ(3770), measure the π0 transition form factor in e+e− → e+e− π0 produced via two-photon fusion, using the single-tag method in which one scattered electron is detected and its energy/momentum used for Q² binning (bins from 0.0 to 2.5 GeV²/c²). Use the 3.773 GeV real data (712_3773) and its inclusive MC, together with a 5M-event exclusive MC of ψ(3770) → e+e− π0 (CONEXC) with π0 → γγ (PHSP). Event selection requires at least one charged track with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vxy<1 cm; PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying electrons against pions and kaons (E/p > 0.8) and requiring at least one e+ and one e−. At least two photons are required, with EMC TDC in [0,14] and energy thresholds of 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap). A nominal 4C kinematic fit to e+e−γγ with a χ² cut of 200 is applied. Additional background suppression uses a minimum 10° angle between photon candidates and charged tracks, cosθ_undetected > 0.99, |cosθ_H| < 0.8 in the φf0 centre-of-mass frame, R_γ ≤ 0.15 and ΣE_extra ≤ 0.17 GeV, with the best π0 candidate chosen by minimum p_t* in the γγ rest frame.
