On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study the radiative and direct decays ψ(3686)→γχ_cJ→γ pbar K*+ Λ and ψ(3686)→pbar K*+ Λ, with K*+→K+π0, π0→γγ, Λ→p+π−, where the χ_cJ sample includes χ_c0, χ_c1, and χ_c2 produced via E1 transitions. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC (709_3686), and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm, with at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons are accepted with TDC 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, angle to any track >5°, and at least three (radiative) or two (direct) candidates. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying protons against kaons/pions (≥1 p and ≥1 pbar) and kaons against pions/protons (≥1 K+), after which used tracks are removed and remaining positive/negative tracks are assigned as π+/π−; only dE/dx is used for the low-momentum Λ daughter pion because TOF is unreachable. An isolated-photon requirement keeps photons at least 10° from primary positive and negative tracks, while a Λ vertex fit selects p+π− pairs by minimizing the mass difference. A two-photon Kalman fit constrains the γγ mass to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<25, requiring at least one π0, followed by the nominal 5C kinematic fit using γ pbar K+ Λ π0 for the radiative channel (χ²<70) and pbar K+ Λ π0 for the direct channel (χ²<100), both with four-momentum constraints; competing 5C/4C hypotheses are also fitted to store χ² for a ROOT-level veto requiring the signal confidence level to exceed the alternative-hypothesis confidence levels.
