On the XYZ scan from 4.226–4.950 GeV (using the eight listed samples from 4.260 to 4.950 GeV, anchored on the 4260 point), study e+e− → η η J/ψ with J/ψ → e+e−/μ+μ− and η → γγ / π+π−π0. Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC, and generate a 100k-event exclusive MC sample per energy point for e+e− → η η J/ψ, J/ψ → e+e−/μ+μ−, η → γγ and η → π+π−π0. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track and net charge zero; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14 (700 ns), angle to nearest track >10°, and at least four photons. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, pions identified against K/p, tracks with p>1.0 GeV treated as leptons (electron if E>0.6 GeV, else muon) while pions have p<1.0 GeV. Reconstruct π0 → γγ and η → γγ candidates with 1C Kalman fits (χ²<25, Nπ0≥0, Nη≥1) using pre-selection windows 80–180 MeV (γγ for π0), 400–700 MeV (γγ for η), and 400–700 MeV (π+π−π0 for η). Perform a nominal 7C kinematic fit on η η ℓ+ℓ− constraining four-momentum, both η masses, and M(ℓ+ℓ−)=M(J/ψ), with χ²<200, plus a competing π0π0J/ψ hypothesis for background; in the four-photon mode veto events favoring two π0 over two η by comparing the summed χ² of the two 1C hypotheses.
