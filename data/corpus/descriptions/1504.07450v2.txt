On the ψ(3770) at √s = 3.773 GeV, study ψ(3770) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → γ J/ψ, J/ψ → e+e− / μ+μ− (J = 1, 2). Use 2916.94 pb−1 of real data and the corresponding inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the four signal modes (χ_c1 → e+e−, χ_c2 → e+e−, χ_c1 → μ+μ−, χ_c2 → μ+μ−). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 15 cm, Vr < 1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track with net charge 0; photons with >50 MeV in both barrel and endcap, >10° angle to the nearest charged track, EMC time 0–14, at least two photons. PID by the probability method (prob > 0.001), high-momentum tracks (p > 1.2 GeV/c) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy > 0.6 GeV, otherwise muon), requiring one l+ and one l−; the χ_c1 and χ_c2 hypotheses share the same selection chain, and the e+e− and μ+μ− channels use identical cuts except for flavour-specific lepton identification. Perform a 5C kinematic fit (γ γ l+ l−), constraining the four-momentum to the c.m. values and M(l+l−) to the J/ψ nominal mass, with χ² < 200, flagged nominal, vetoing γγ invariant masses in the π0 (0.124–0.146 GeV) and η (0.537–0.558 GeV) windows.
