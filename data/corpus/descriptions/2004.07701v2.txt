Two independent analyses are made on the real data sets 708_3097 (J/ψ, 3.097 GeV) and 709_3686 (ψ(2S), 3.686 GeV) together with their inclusive MC, studying e+e− → J/ψ/ψ(2S) → Σ+ Σ̄−, with Σ+ → p π0, Σ̄− → p̄ π0, π0 → γγ. Generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each resonance using a phase-space decay card for J/ψ/ψ(2S) → Σ+ Σ̄−, Σ+ → p π0, Σ̄− → p̄ π0, π0 → γγ. Selection requires exactly one positive and one negative charged track with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<2 cm; at least four photons with TDC 0–14, E>25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap and angle to nearest track >10°. Proton/antiproton PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001 and proton identification against kaon/pion, requiring exactly one p and one p̄; these proton tracks are then removed from the charged-track list. Reconstruct π0→γγ candidates with two 1C Kalman fits constraining to the nominal π0 mass (χ²<25, at least one π0 per fit), then perform a 4C kinematic fit to p p̄ π0 π0 with χ²<100 and nominal flag. The best combination is chosen by minimizing sqrt((M(pπ0)−m_Σ+)² + (M(p̄π0)−m_Σ̄−)²), and for the ψ(2S) data an additional veto requires |M(p p̄)−3.1 GeV|>0.05 GeV to suppress ψ(2S)→π0π0J/ψ.
