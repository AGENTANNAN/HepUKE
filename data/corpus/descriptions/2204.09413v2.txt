Study ψ(2S) → π0 h_c with h_c → γ η_c at √s = 3.686 GeV, using the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus 300k exclusive MC events generated for ψ(2S) → π0 h_c, h_c → γ η_c, π0 → γγ. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and at least two tracks in total, with no explicit charged-particle PID applied, and photons with EMC TDC in [0,14], angle to the nearest track >10°, barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, and at least two photons. A Kalman kinematic fit to the two photons constrains them to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<200 and requires at least one π0. In the tagged h_c channel, an additional E1 photon is required with 465 < Eγ < 535 MeV that does not form a π0 with any other photon; if multiple π0 candidates fall in the recoil-mass signal window 3.500–3.550 GeV/c², the one with the smallest 1C fit χ² is retained. Vetoes are applied for ψ(2S) → J/ψ π+π− and ψ(2S) → J/ψ π0π0 by requiring the π+π− recoil mass to lie outside M_J/ψ ± 4 MeV/c² and the π0π0 recoil mass to lie outside M_J/ψ in [−8,+38] MeV/c². The total EMC energy is required to satisfy 0.6 < E_EMC < 3.2 GeV to suppress Bhabha and continuum backgrounds, and the π0 recoil mass is computed from four-momentum conservation for the final signal-extraction fit.
