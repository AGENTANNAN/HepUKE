On the four χc1 scan points 3.5080, 3.5097, 3.5106 (paper 3.5104), and 3.5144 (paper 3.5146) GeV, study e+e− → χc1 → γ J/ψ, J/ψ → μ+μ− using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC at each point for the γ μ+μ− final state with the ψ(4260)→γJ/ψ, J/ψ→μ+μ− decay chain and PHOKHARA-modeled signal/ISR interference. Event selection: exactly two charged tracks, one positive and one negative, with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; at least one photon with EMC TDC 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, and opening angle to any track >10°. Identify the tracks as μ+ and μ− by the probability method against pions with a 0.001 probability cut, and reject muon candidates having EMC energy >0.4 GeV. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γ μ+ μ− constraining the four-momentum, require χ²<200, and flag it nominal; select the best photon by minimum χ²_4C and require |cosθ_γ|<0.80 to suppress ISR background. The same selection chain is applied to all scan-point data, inclusive MC, and exclusive signal MC.
