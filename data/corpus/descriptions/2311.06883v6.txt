Study the double-tag process e+e− → Λc+ Λc− at ten center-of-mass energies from 4.600 to 4.843 GeV (4.600, 4.610, 4.620, 4.640, 4.660, 4.680, 4.700, 4.740, 4.750 and 4.840 GeV), using the real data and inclusive MC available at each point. Generate exclusive signal MC for e+e− → Λc+ Λc− with the tag-side decay Λc− → p−K+π− and the signal side either Λc+ → p+π0 or Λc+ → p+η, separately for the two signal channels. The tag-side anti-Λc− is reconstructed through nine hadronic modes: pK+π−, pK0S, pK+π−π0, pK0Sπ0, pK0Sπ+π−, Λπ−, Λπ−π0, Λπ−π+π−, and Σπ (with Σ0 → Λγ). On the signal side require exactly one proton and two photons, with the photon–proton opening angle above 30° and photon energies above 25 MeV in the barrel / 50 MeV in the endcap, and additionally photon lateral moment between 0.05 and 0.40 and E3x3/E5x5 > 0.85. Apply a four-momentum-conservation kinematic fit with χ² < 200 and require the signal-side ΔE_{p2γ} to satisfy −0.080 < ΔE < 0.035 GeV. Suppress backgrounds by vetoing M(p_signal, π−_tag) ∈ [1.111, 1.121] GeV/c² (Λ) and M(π0_signal, π+π−_tag) ∈ [0.733, 0.833] GeV/c² (ω), and select π0 and η through M_γγ windows of [0.115, 0.150] and [0.490, 0.583] GeV/c², respectively. When several candidates remain, keep the signal candidate with the smallest |ΔE_{p2γ}| and the tag candidate with the smallest |ΔE|.
