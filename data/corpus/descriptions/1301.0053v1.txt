On the J/ψ at 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → γ η η, η → γ γ. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive J/ψ MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for the signal J/ψ → γ η η with η → γ γ in phase space, and 500k-event exclusive MC for the background J/ψ → γ η π0 π0 with η → γ γ and π0 → γ γ in phase space. Event selection: require no charged tracks, with |cosθ|<0.92, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and zero positive plus zero negative tracks. Select at least five photons with EMC time 0–14, angle to any track >10°, and energy above 25 MeV in the barrel / 50 MeV in the endcap. No PID is applied because the final state is all neutral. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to the five-photon hypothesis, constraining four-momentum, with χ²<200 (a loose default here; the paper's χ²<50 is applied at ROOT level). Additional ROOT-level selections are recorded: remove any γγ pair with |M(γγ)−m_π0|<0.015 GeV, choose the two η candidates from the photon pairing that minimizes δ = √[(M(γ1γ2)−m_η)² + (M(γ3γ4)−m_η)²], require both |M(γγ)−m_η|<0.04 GeV and only one pairing with δ<0.05 GeV, veto |M(γη)−m_φ|>30 MeV, estimate background from η mass sidebands 0.07 < |M(γγ)−m_η| < 0.15 GeV, and determine efficiency from a PWA amplitude-weighted MC sample.
