On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → γ η(1405), η(1405) → f0(980) π0, f0(980) → π+π−, π0 → γγ; use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus 5,000,000-event exclusive MC for this signal chain. Charged tracks are required with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vxy < 1 cm, and at least two charged tracks; PID uses the probability method with prob > 0.001, identifying pions against electrons, kaons, and protons, and requiring at least one π+ and one π−. Photons must satisfy TDC 0–14, E > 25 MeV in the barrel and > 50 MeV in the endcap, with at least three photons. Perform a kinematic fit to π+π−γγγ with a four-momentum constraint, nominal flag, and χ² < 20. Additional criteria: the radiative photon is the one not paired into the π0; require |M(γγ) − mπ0| < 0.025 GeV/c², veto M(γγ) in [0.52, 0.57] GeV/c², require |M(π+π−γ) − mJ/ψ| > 0.05 GeV/c², |M(π0γ) − mω| > 0.04 GeV/c², and at least 10° between photon candidates and charged tracks. A partial-wave analysis of η(1405) → f0(980)π0 → π+π−π0 is then performed with a Breit-Wigner parametrization.
