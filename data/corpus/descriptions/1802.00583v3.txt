On the J/ψ and ψ(3686), study J/ψ→φηπ0 (φ→K+K−, η→γγ or π+π−π0, π0→γγ) and ψ(3686)→γχc1 (χc1→π0π+π−, π0→γγ). Use 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, plus 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the three modes, i.e. J/ψ→φηπ0 with η→γγ, J/ψ→φηπ0 with η→π+π−π0, and ψ(3686)→γχc1, χc1→π0π+π−. For all modes, charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge zero, while photons must pass 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) thresholds and be at least 10° from tracks; PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001. Mode A1 requires at least one K+ and one K− and at least four photons, with kaons identified against pions/protons; do a nominal 4C fit to K+K−γγγγ with χ²<50, then require |M(γγ)−mπ0|<15 MeV/c², |M(γγ)−mη|<30 MeV/c² and |M(K+K−)−mφ|<10 MeV/c², and veto J/ψ→K+K−π0π0 and J/ψ→K+K−ηη via χ²(π0π0)>40 and χ²(ηη)>5. Mode A2 requires at least two positive and two negative tracks, with at least one K+, one K−, one π+ and one π− and at least four photons; kaons and pions are identified against each other and protons; do a nominal 4C fit to K+K−π+π−γγγγ with χ²<60 and apply mass windows of 20 (η), 15 (π0) and 10 (φ) MeV/c². Mode B requires at least one π+ and one π− and at least three photons, with pions identified against kaons/protons; do a nominal 4C fit to π+π−γγγ with χ²<20, plus 4C fits to π+π−γγγγ and π+π−γγ for competing-hypothesis vetoes, rejecting events where either competing χ² is smaller; use |M(π+π−π0)−mχc1|<20 MeV/c² and a π0 mass window of 15 MeV/c².
