Measure the Born cross section for e+e− → K_S0 K± π∓ (with K_S0 → π+π−) at the 15 BESIII 703 scan points from √s ≈ 3.808 to 4.600 GeV, using the real data only. Generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC at each energy point with a ConExc generator, equally populating e+e− → K_S0 K+π− and its charge-conjugate K_S0 K−π+, with the final state decayed via PHSP to K_S0 K+π−. Select events with exactly two positive and two negative charged tracks, net charge zero, satisfying |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; photons are selected with TDC 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, and a 10° angle-to-track cut, with no explicit photon multiplicity requirement. Identify particles with the dE/dx–TOF probability method at 0.001 probability, testing K and π hypotheses against π/K/proton, and require the K_S0 pion candidates to satisfy E/p < 0.8 to suppress photon conversion. Reconstruct K_S0 → π+π− with a secondary vertex fit, choosing the candidate by smallest mass difference and removing used tracks, requiring |M(π+π−) − M(K_S0)| < 0.020 GeV/c², decay length > 2σ, and pion DCA < 25 cm (z) / 20 cm (rφ), with sidebands at 0.435–0.455 and 0.545–0.565 GeV/c². Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to the K_S0 K±π∓ hypothesis with a loose χ² < 200, leaving the final tighter χ² cut to be optimized in ROOT.
