Study e+e− → Ds*+ Ds− → γ Ds+ Ds− at √s = 4.178–4.226 GeV using 6.32 fb−1 of real data at six energy points (4.178, 4.190, 4.200, 4.210, 4.220, 4.226 GeV) and the corresponding inclusive MC; no exclusive-MC sample is generated in this DSL. The Ds− tag (charm −1) is formed from ten hadronic modes (Kπ0, KKπ, Kπ0π0, KKππ0, KKπππ, KS0K, πππ, πη, πη′, KS0η), while the Ds+ tag (charm +1) uses the signal mode K+π+π− and is chosen by the smallest invariant mass; the signal side requires exactly one transition photon with a minimum photon angle of 10°. Charged-track and PID selection follows the standard tag reconstruction (no explicit |cosθ|, Vz, Vr, or PID method is overridden here). Perform a 6C/7C kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation, both tag Ds masses constrained to the nominal Ds mass, and both tag-plus-photon systems constrained to the nominal D_s* mass, with χ² < 200. Apply a KS0 veto rejecting π+π− invariant masses in [0.4676, 0.5276] GeV/c², and use per-energy-point M_rec windows (2.050–2.180 GeV/c² at 4.178 GeV, 2.048–2.190 at 4.189, 2.046–2.200 at 4.199, 2.044–2.210 at 4.209, 2.042–2.220 at 4.219, and 2.040–2.220 at 4.226 GeV) in the ROOT stage to suppress non-Ds*Ds background. The amplitude analysis uses three sample groups (4.178, 4.189–4.219, and 4.226 GeV) with signal yields 772, 444, and 140 and purities 93–96%.
