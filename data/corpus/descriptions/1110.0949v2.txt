On the ψ(2S) at 3.686 GeV, search for ψ′ → γ η_c′ with η_c′ → ρ0ρ0, K*0K̄*0 and φφ, the vector mesons decaying as 2(π+π−), π+π−K+K− and 2(K+K−). Use the 3.686 GeV real data (709_3686) and inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the three modes (phase-space ψ′ → γ η_c′, η_c′ → VV and V → PP). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks with zero net charge; photons with 25 MeV threshold in both barrel and endcap, timing within 0–14, at least 10° from any track, and at least one photon. PID uses a summed-χ² method over π/K/p hypotheses with χ²_PID cut 4, taking the track-to-final-state assignment (and channel) with minimum total χ²_PID. A 4C kinematic fit over γ plus the four charged tracks, χ²<40, picks the radiative photon, followed by a nominal 3C fit whose fitted M_VV^3C gives the final mass spectrum. At ROOT level, V-mass windows are required: 0.67 < M(π+π−) < 0.87 GeV/c² for ρ0, 0.85 < M(π±K∓) < 0.95 GeV/c² for K*0 and 1.00 < M(K+K−) < 1.03 GeV/c² for φ, together with a ψ′ → π+π−J/ψ / η J/ψ veto via M_recoil(π+π−) < 3.05 GeV/c². The three channels share the same selection chain, differing only in the final-state particle combination used in the fits.
