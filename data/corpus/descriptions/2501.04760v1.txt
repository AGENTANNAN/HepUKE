On the ψ(3770) at √s = 3.773 GeV, search for the semileptonic decay D^+ → e^+ ν_e with a double-tag method, using 20.3 fb⁻¹ of real data and inclusive MC at this energy, and generating 500k exclusive MC events for D^+ → e^+ ν_e. Tag a D^- through six hadronic modes: K^+π^-π^-, K_S^0π^-, K^+π^-π^-π^0, K_S^0π^-π^0, K_S^0π^+π^-π^-, and K^+K^-π^-, with the signal side requiring one positron of charge +1 and a missing neutrino. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, V_r<1 cm, |V_z|<10 cm (with K_S^0 daughters allowed |V_z|<20 cm); kaon and pion identification uses likelihood L(K)>L(π) and L(π)>L(K), respectively. K_S^0 → π^+π^- candidates require a secondary-vertex fit χ²<100, decay length L/σ_L>2, and M(π^+π^-) ∈ (0.487,0.511) GeV/c²; π^0 → γγ candidates require M(γγ) ∈ (0.115,0.150) GeV/c² and a 1C mass-constrained fit with χ²<50. Photons must have E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) or E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), shower-track angle >10°, and EMC time in [0,700] ns; positrons are identified by L(e)>0.001 and L(e)/(L(e)+L(K)+L(π))>0.8, with E/p>0.8 and bremsstrahlung/FSR photons within 5° of the e^+ direction added to its four-momentum. Single-tag D^- candidates must satisfy mode-dependent ΔE cuts from [-25,24] to [-62,49] MeV and M_BC ∈ (1.863,1.877) GeV/c², keeping the candidate with minimum |ΔE| when multiple exist. A kinematic fit constrains the total four-momentum and the tag D^- and signal D^+ invariant masses to the known D^± mass, determines the neutrino four-momentum, and requires χ²<50, with extra-particle vetoes of no extra π^0, no extra good charged tracks, and maximum extra photon energy <0.2 GeV.
