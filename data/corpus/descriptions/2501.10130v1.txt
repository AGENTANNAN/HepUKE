On the J/ψ peak (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γη with η → π+π−ℓ+ℓ− (ℓ=e, μ), using real J/ψ data (about 1.0087×10^10 events) and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes, J/ψ → γπ+π−e+e− and J/ψ → γπ+π−μ+μ−. Event selection: exactly four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100, Vr<10 and net charge 0; at least one photon with barrel (endcap) energy threshold 25 (50) MeV, EMC timing 0–14, and angle to any track >10°. PID by the probability method: high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) are treated as leptons, electron if EMC energy >1.0 GeV, with π/K separation, requiring one π+, one π−, one ℓ+, and one ℓ−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γπ+π−ℓ+ℓ− with χ²<200, flagged nominal. In ROOT, further require the combined χ²_4C+Σχ²_PID<50, veto photon conversions via Φ_ee vs R_xy, separate e/μ by the smallest combined χ², and select signal regions M(π+π−e+e−)∈[0.53,0.57] GeV/c² and M(π+π−μ+μ−)∈[0.531,0.567] GeV/c²; an ALP search uses additional M(ℓ+ℓ−) selection. The same selection chain is used for both decay modes.
