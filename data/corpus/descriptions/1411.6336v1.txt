Search for e+e− → γ χ_cJ (J=0,1,2), χ_cJ → γ J/ψ, J/ψ → μ+μ− at √s = 4.009, 4.230, 4.260 and 4.360 GeV, using the real data and inclusive MC at each energy and generating 200k-event exclusive MC for each χ_cJ (0,1,2) at each CME point, with ψ(4260) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → γ J/ψ, J/ψ → μ+μ− (PHOTOS/VLL). Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge 0; photons at least 20° from any charged track, with E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) and E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), EMC time 0–700 ns, and at least two photon candidates. PID uses the probability method, treating high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon) with E/p<0.35 required for muons, and demands one positive and one negative lepton after π/K separation. Perform a 5C kinematic fit to γγμ+μ− with four-momentum conservation and M(μ+μ−) constrained to the nominal J/ψ mass, χ² cut 200, flagged nominal (the published χ²<40 is applied later), retaining the candidate with smallest χ² when multiple candidates survive. Additional background suppression requires |M(γγ)−M(π0)|>0.025, |M(γγ)−M(η)|>0.03 and |M(γγ)−M(η')|>0.02 GeV/c², and at 4.009 GeV the χ_c1,2 decay photon energy must be below 0.403 GeV. J/ψ sidebands 2.917 < M(μ+μ−) < 3.057 and 3.137 < M(μ+μ−) < 3.277 GeV/c² are studied by constraining M(μ+μ−) to 3.047 or 3.147 GeV/c² in the 5C fit, and the same selection chain is used for χ_c0, χ_c1 and χ_c2.
