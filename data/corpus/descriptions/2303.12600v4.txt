At the D_s* threshold, search for D_s+ → τ+ ν_τ with τ+ → π+ ν̄_τ using the double-tag technique, the tag-side D_s- being reconstructed in 13 hadronic modes (9 implemented: K+K-π-, K+K-π-π0, π+π-π-, K_S0K-, K_S0K+π-π-, π-η, π-π0η, π-η'→π+π-η, π-η'→γρ0) and the signal D_s+ originating from D_s*+ → γ D_s+. Use real data and inclusive MC over the 8 c.m. energy points 4.128–4.226 GeV, and generate a 500k-event exclusive signal MC for D_s*+D_s- production with D_s*+ → γ D_s+, D_s+ → τ+ν_τ, τ+ → π+ν̄_τ and D_s- → K+K-π-. On the signal side require one π+ of positive charge (opposite the charm −1 tag) together with a missing massless neutrino system, and perform a 4C kinematic fit with χ² < 200. Retain the transition photon with energy 0.114–0.149 GeV in the D_s* rest frame (ΔE minimized, direct/indirect tag chosen by closest D_s* mass) and keep the candidate whose recoil mass against the tagged D_s- is closest to the nominal D_s* mass. Further double-tag selection: no extra charged tracks and no extra π0, pion PID, E/p < 0.9, maximum extra photon energy < 0.3 GeV, |cosθ_miss| < 0.9, and M_miss² in [−0.2, 0.6] GeV²/c⁴. Finally train a TMVA BDT on nine inputs including M_miss², M_tag, m_BC_tag, the missing-angle cosines, the photon energies and the π+ angle and momentum.
