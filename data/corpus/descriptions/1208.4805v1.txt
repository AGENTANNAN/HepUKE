At the ψ(3686) (√s = 3.686 GeV) search for the hadronic transition χcJ → ηc π+π− and the decay χcJ → K K̄ π π π (J = 0,1,2) produced via ψ(3686) → γ χcJ, reconstructed in two final states: (A) γ K_S0 K± π∓ π+ π−, also via χcJ → ηc π+π− with ηc → K_S0 K± π∓, and (B) γ K+ K− π+ π− π0, also via χcJ → ηc π+π− with ηc → K+ K− π0. Use the 1.06×10^8-event ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC, plus the 42 pb−1 continuum data at 3.65 GeV and its inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each χcJ state in each of the four final states (χc0/1/2 → K_S0 K+π−π+π−, K+K−π+π−π0, and the corresponding ηc π+π− modes). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm; photons with E > 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), EMC timing within 0–14, and at least 20° isolation from any charged track. Mode A requires three positive and three negative tracks (net charge 0) and at least one photon, with PID by the probability method (prob > 0.001) using π/K/p separation to give at least one K, two π+ and two π−, followed by a secondary-vertex fit forming the K_S0 from π+π− pairs (mass within 7 MeV of nominal, vertex ≥ 0.5 cm from the IP); Mode B requires two positive and two negative tracks (net charge 0) and at least three photons, with PID requiring exactly one K+, one K−, one π+ and one π−, and a π0 built from γγ with 0.120 < M(γγ) < 0.145 GeV/c² through a Kalman fit (χ² < 25). A 4C kinematic fit constrained to the initial e+e− four-momentum (γ K_S0 K±π∓π+π− in A, γ K+K−π+π−π0 in B) is then performed with a loose χ² < 200 BOSS cut, the paper's χ² < 50 applied downstream, together with a competing γγ-hypothesis 4C fit stored for extra-photon veto; all three χcJ states within a mode share the same selection chain.
