Using 22.1 fb−1 of e+e− collision data collected at c.m. energies 4.008–4.951 GeV (the XYZ scan region), together with inclusive MC at all points, study e+e− → π0π0 ψ(3686), ψ(3686) → π+π− J/ψ, J/ψ → e+e− / μ+μ−, π0 → γγ; generate 200k-event exclusive MC at each energy point with the J/ψ leptonic decays set to 50% e+e− and 50% μ+μ−. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero; photons are selected with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy thresholds, TDC in 0–700, at least 10° from any track, and at least four photons. The photons are paired into two π0 candidates by minimizing (M(γγ)−mπ0)2, each satisfying |M(γγ)−mπ0|<20 MeV/c2, and when more than four photons are present the combination with the smallest 4C χ2 is kept. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying pions against kaons/protons; tracks with p>1.1 GeV/c are treated as leptons and those with p<0.75 GeV/c as pions, electron/muon separation uses EMC E/p>0.7 for electrons and E<0.45 GeV for muons, requiring one π+, one π−, one positive lepton and one negative lepton. A nominal 4C kinematic fit to γγγγ π+π− l+l− with χ2<120 is followed by a 7C refit constraining the two π0 masses and the J/ψ mass, with χ2<200. Both J/ψ decay modes share the same selection chain.
