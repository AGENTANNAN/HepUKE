Measure the total and leptonic widths of the J/ψ with an energy-scan method, studying e+e− → e+e− (Bhabha) and e+e− → μ+μ− at 15 center-of-mass points from 3049.642 to 3119.878 MeV (total luminosity ~83 pb−1). Use the real data at all points, inclusive MC for background estimation, and 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two leptonic final states, generated with a CONEXC decay card. For the Bhabha channel, require exactly two oppositely charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, and for both tracks P > 0.7 E_beam and E > 0.6 P. For the dimuon channel, apply the same two-track acceptance (|cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm), with each track satisfying P > 0.8 E_beam, 25 MeV < E < 0.25 P, no neutral shower above 25 MeV, and |Δ t_TOF| < 1.5 ns to suppress cosmics. Cross sections are extracted from (N_sig − N_bkg)/(L ε_trg ε_sel) f at each point and then fitted simultaneously across both channels and all 15 energies to determine the J/ψ parameters.
