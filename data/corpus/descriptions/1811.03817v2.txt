On the J/ψ(3097) resonance (√s = 3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → ωη with ω → π+π−π0 in the Dalitz decay, η → γγ, and π0 → γγ. Use the 708_3097 real data and inclusive MC; generate 24 million exclusive-MC events for J/ψ → ωη with the same decay chain (ω → π+π−π0 Dalitz, η → γγ, π0 → γγ). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track, and net charge zero; photons with TDC 0–14, E > 25 MeV in the barrel / 50 MeV in the endcap, angle to any track > 10°, and at least four photons. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying pions against kaons/protons, and requires at least one π+ and one π−. Perform a 6C kinematic fit to π+π−γγγγ imposing four-momentum conservation plus nominal π0 and η mass constraints, with χ² < 200, flagged nominal. At ROOT level, also apply a Λ′ veto requiring |M(π+π−π0 γ_low) − m_η′| > 0.04 GeV/c² and select the ω signal region |M(π+π−π0) − m_ω| < 0.04 GeV/c².
