On the 4.258–4.681 GeV R-scan, study e+e− → γ η ηc and e+e− → γ η' ηc with ηc → p pbar (representative; 16 hadronic ηc modes—p pbar, 2(π+π−), 2(K+K−), K+K−π+π−, p pbar π+π−, 3(π+π−), K+K−2(π+π−), K+K−π0, p pbar π0, K_S0 K±π∓, K_S0 K±π∓π±π∓, π+π−η, K+K−η, 2(π+π−)η, π+π−π0π0, 2(π+π−)π0π0—are combined at the analysis level with best-candidate selection by smallest total χ²), η → γγ, and η' → η π+π−. Use the representative 4.681 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes (γηηc and γη'ηc). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring at least one positive and one negative track for γηηc (p and pbar) and at least two positive and two negative tracks for γη'ηc (one π+, one π−, one p, one pbar in the fit); photons with Eγ>25 MeV and angle >10°, at least three. PID uses the probability method with 0.001 cut, identifying protons against kaons/pions and pions against kaons/protons. Reconstruct η → γγ with a 1C mass-constrained fit to the nominal η mass (χ²<200); then perform a nominal 4C fit to γ η p pbar with χ²<20 for γηηc and to γ η π+π− p pbar with χ²<30 for γη'ηc, vetoing the 2γ+hadrons and 4γ+hadrons (plus π+π− for γη'ηc) competing hypotheses by their 4C χ² values and using the η' mass-difference criterion. For ηc modes with K_S0, use a secondary-vertex fit with |Mππ−m_K_S0|<20 MeV and decay length >2σ; for modes with π0, use a γγ mass constraint with |Mγγ−m_π0|<15 MeV.
