At the J/ψ (√s = 3.097 GeV), search for a massless invisible particle in Σ⁺ → p + invisible, using J/ψ → Σ⁺ anti-Σ⁻ with anti-Σ⁻ → anti-p π⁰, π⁰ → γγ, in a hyperon single-tag/double-tag technique. Use the 3.097 GeV real data (708_3097) and its inclusive MC, and generate 1M exclusive MC events for J/ψ → Σ⁺ anti-Σ⁻, Σ⁺ → p invisible, anti-Σ⁻ → anti-p π⁰ with π⁰ → γγ. Single-tag selection: at least two charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<2 cm; at least two photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds and more than 10° from any track; isolated photons more than 20° from the proton candidate and 10° from other tracks; proton identification by the probability method with a probability cut of 0.001 against π⁺ and K⁺. Reconstruct π⁰ from γγ pairs with a 1C Kalman fit constraining the γγ mass to the nominal π⁰ mass (χ²<25), then combine the π⁰ with the anti-proton to form the anti-Σ⁻, choosing the combination closest to the nominal anti-Σ⁻ mass and requiring |M(anti-p π⁰) − M_Σ| < 15 MeV/c² with a π⁰ window of 115–150 MeV/c², the ST yield being extracted from the M_BC distribution. On the double-tag side (handled at ROOT level): exactly one additional proton, 2C kinematic fits to J/ψ → p anti-p π⁰ + invisible (π⁰ mass and zero invisible mass constrained) together with a competing 2C fit with the invisible mass set to the π⁰ mass, plus 5C (extra photon) and 6C (extra π⁰) fits to veto Σ⁺ → pγ and Σ⁺ → pπ⁰, primary and secondary vertex fits with decay-length significance L/σ_L > 2, |cosθ_invisible| < 0.8, and 1.18 < M(p + invisible) < 1.20 GeV/c².
