Measure e+e− → KS KL π0 cross sections at the 19 BOSS 713 R-scan energy points from 2.000 to 3.080 GeV, using the real data and inclusive MC available at each point. For each energy point generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC with the ConExc generator for e+e− → KS KS π0 (mode 9), treating one KS as KL in the cross-section extraction. Reconstruct the KS in π+π− and the π0 in γγ, with KL taken as the missing particle. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track and net charge 0, identified as π+π−; photons with EMC TDC in [0,14], 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy threshold, >10° angle to any track, and at least two photons. Form the KS by a secondary-vertex fit of the π+π− pair minimizing the mass difference, and the π0 by a Kalman fit of the two photons constrained to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<30 and at least one π0. Apply an ISR-veto 1C fit under the γ KS KL hypothesis, then the nominal selection uses a 3C fit with KL as the missing particle, the KS mass constrained to nominal and four-momentum conservation, and χ²<200. Mass windows are |M(π+π−) − m_KS| < 12 MeV with KS decay length > 2σ and |M(γγ) − m_π0| < 0.015 GeV; selected events go to a partial-wave analysis, with ISR and vacuum-polarization corrections taken from the ConExc generator.
