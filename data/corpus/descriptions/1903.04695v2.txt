Study e+e− → γ ω J/ψ, with ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ and J/ψ → ℓ+ℓ− (ℓ = e, μ), using the 4.008–4.600 GeV energy scan (~11.6 fb⁻¹). Take real data and inclusive MC at the 19 scan points from 4009 to 4600 MeV, and generate KKMC exclusive MC per point with vpho → γ ω J/ψ, ω → π+π−π0, J/ψ → e+e− / μ+μ− (equal weights) and π0 → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm, |Vz|<10 cm, requiring four positive and zero negative tracks with net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, 3–20 photons, and more than 20° separation from any track. Lepton/pion separation is momentum-based — p>1 GeV/c tracks are lepton candidates from J/ψ and p<1 GeV/c tracks are pion candidates from ω — with e/μ separation from EMC energy (muon E_EMC<0.35 GeV, electron E_EMC>1.1 GeV), assigning 1 ℓ+, 1 ℓ−, 1 π+ and 1 π−. Reconstruct π0 from two photons with a mass-constrained Kalman fit to m(π0), then perform a 5C kinematic fit to π0π+π−ℓ+ℓ− using four-momentum conservation and the π0 mass constraint, χ²<100, flagged nominal, selecting the combination with the smallest χ² for the radiative photon among the extra photons. Apply J/ψ (3.07–3.14 GeV) and ω (0.72–0.81 GeV) mass windows, use M(ωJ/ψ) = M(π+π−π0ℓ+ℓ−) − M(ℓ+ℓ−) + m(J/ψ), and veto ψ(3686) (|RM(π+π−)−m(ψ(3686))|<8 MeV or |M(π+π−J/ψ)−m(ψ(3686))|<7 MeV) and η′J/ψ (0.93<M(γω)<0.97 GeV with M(ωJ/ψ)>3.9 GeV).
