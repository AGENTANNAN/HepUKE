At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), perform a quantum-correlated D0 D0bar double-tag analysis to measure strong-phase parameters in D0 → K_S0 π+ π−. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 1,000,000 exclusive MC events for ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar. The analysis uses a double-tag technique: tag side 1 comprises flavor, CP-even, CP-odd, and mixed-CP tags (K∓π±, K∓π±π0, K∓π±π±, K+K−, π+π−, K_S0 π0, π+π−π0, K_S0 η, K_S0 ω, K_S0 π+π−), while tag side 2 requires the signal mode D0 → K_S0 π+ π−; the signal side must have no additional photons or tracks. A kinematic fit is performed constraining four-momentum conservation and both tag1 and tag2 invariant masses to the nominal D0 mass, with a χ² cut of 200. Note that semileptonic tags, K_L0 π0, K_L0 π0 π0, and some CP-odd modes are not available, so their single-tag yields are taken from known branching fractions; the K_L0 π+ π− signal is reconstructed via missing-mass-squared, and Dalitz binning with efficiency correction and peaking background subtraction is done at ROOT level.
