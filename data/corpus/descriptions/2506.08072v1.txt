Measure the time-like Λ electromagnetic form factors via e+e− → Λ Λ̄ with Λ → p π− and Λ̄ → p̄ π+ (double-tag), using the 2.396 GeV real-data point (part of a 5-point scan at 2.396, 2.500, 2.646, 2.900 and 3.080 GeV) and 100k-event exclusive MC for continuum Λ Λ̄ production generated with ConExc including ISR and vacuum polarisation. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<30 cm and Vr<10 cm, with at least two positive and two negative tracks. PID is done by the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions and pions against kaons and protons, requiring at least one p, one p̄, one π+ and one π−. Reconstruct Λ → p π− and Λ̄ → p̄ π+ through secondary-vertex fits, building each virtual particle by minimising the mass difference and removing used tracks from the candidate list. Then perform a 4C kinematic fit to e+e− → p π− p̄ π+, nominal, with χ²<130. For the selected candidates the pπ invariant mass is required within |M(pπ)−m_Λ|<6 MeV/c² (≈±4σ), choosing the largest decay length when multiple combinations are found; the corresponding single-tag selections use a tighter ±4.7 MeV/c² window, a Λ momentum within ±3σ of expectation, and a combined production+decay vertex χ²<8 for the Λ̄.
