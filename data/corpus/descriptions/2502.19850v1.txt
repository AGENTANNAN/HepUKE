On the ψ(2S) (3.686 GeV), measure the branching fraction for ψ(2S) → τ+τ− with one τ decaying to eνν and the other to μνν, giving a final state of one electron, one muon and missing neutrinos. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 2M-event exclusive signal MC where the ψ(2S) decays to τ+τ− and each τ decays to eνν or μνν (EVTGEN TAULNUNU, ConExc lineshape). Event selection requires exactly two charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, one positive and one negative with net charge 0, and zero photons (25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap thresholds). Particle ID uses the probability method: tracks above 1.0 GeV/c are treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon), requiring one l+ and one l−. A four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit to l+l− is applied with χ²<200, flagged nominal. Additional ROOT-level cuts tighten the lepton ID (electron E/p in [0.8,1.2], χ²_dE/dx<4, |Δtof|<0.3 ns; muon E/p<0.7, χ²_dE/dx<4, |Δtof|<0.3 ns, MUC depth >81(p−0.65) cm), require track momentum <1.2 GeV/c and a successful common-vertex fit, and select missing mass M_miss<3.05 GeV/c² with |cosθ_miss|<0.8; the e+e−→τ+τ− continuum is subtracted using data at 3.650 GeV interpolated to the ψ(2S) energy.
