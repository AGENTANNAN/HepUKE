Measure the inclusive J/ψ yield at √s = 3.097 GeV, taking the J/ψ detection efficiency from ψ(3686) → π+π−J/ψ and subtracting the continuum background using data at 3.08 GeV. Use the 2017 J/ψ real data and its inclusive MC, the 3.08 GeV continuum real data, and the ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC; generate 10M exclusive ψ(3686) → π+π−J/ψ, J/ψ → γe+e− events for the efficiency study. The inclusive selection counts every event passing quality cuts: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<15 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track with at least two tracks in total, and all momenta <2.0 GeV/c (for exactly two tracks, additionally each momentum <1.5 GeV/c and each EMC energy <1.0 GeV to reject Bhabha and dimuon events); photons with barrel |cosθ|<0.83 and E>25 MeV or endcap 0.86<|cosθ|<0.93 and E>50 MeV, EMC time 0–700 ns, and more than 10° from any track; plus total visible energy >1.0 GeV. No PID is applied and no kinematic fit is performed; instead the efficiency is measured on ψ(3686) → π+π−J/ψ data requiring at least two oppositely charged soft pions with p<0.4 GeV/c, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<15 cm, Vr<1 cm, and fitting the π+π− recoil-mass spectrum. The background is scaled as N_bg = N_3.08 × (L_{J/ψ}/L_{3.08}) × (s_{3.08}/s_{J/ψ}), and the luminosity comes from e+e− → γγ with at least two EMC showers, |cosθ|<0.8, the second-most-energetic shower between 1.2 and 1.6 GeV, and |Δφ|<2.5°.
