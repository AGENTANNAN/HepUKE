On the ψ(3686) at √s = 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → π0 h_c, h_c → γ η_c, η_c → γγ, with π0 → γγ. Use the ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k exclusive MC events for this chain. Photon selection: EMC timing within 700 ns, at least 10° from any charged track, E > 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), with at least four photons. Reconstruct the tag-side π0 from two barrel photons with E > 40 MeV and 0.120 < M(γγ) < 0.145 GeV/c², constrain M(γγ) to the nominal π0 mass (1C fit, χ² < 5), require the π0 recoil mass in the h_c window [3.48, 3.57] GeV/c², and keep the smallest-χ² candidate if multiple. For the E1 photon, reject any photon that pairs with another to form a π0 candidate (M(γγ) in [0.115,0.150] GeV/c² and 1C χ² < 200), require its energy in the π0-recoiling system in [0.46,0.58] GeV, and pick one at random if several remain. Apply vetoes: π+π− recoil mass within ±7 MeV of m(J/ψ), π0π0 recoil mass in [m(J/ψ)−15, m(J/ψ)+25] MeV, π+π−π0 mass within ±12 MeV of m(η), and on the signal side E1γ paired with an η_c γ in [0.50,0.56] or [0.88,0.98] GeV. Finally require the η_c → γγ mass in [2889.1, 3066.8] MeV/c² and perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit of π0 + 3γ to the initial e+e− four-momentum with χ² < 70.
