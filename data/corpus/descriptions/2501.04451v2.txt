Using the D_s energy scan at √s = 4.128–4.226 GeV (7.33 fb⁻¹ over seven points, ECMS taken as 4.178 GeV), perform a double-tag study of D_s^+ → π+π+π-π0π0 (π0 → γγ) against three hadronic D_s^- tags: K_S^0 K^-, K^+K^-π^-, and K^+K^-π^-π^0. Use all seven real-data samples plus inclusive MC at 4.130 and 4.180 GeV, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC per energy point for the pure-phase-space signal decay. On the tag side reconstruct the D_s^- with charm −1 in any of the three modes; on the signal side require two π+, one π−, net charge +1, and exactly four photons with energy above 25 MeV. Perform a kinematic fit enforcing four-momentum conservation with the tag D_s mass constrained to its nominal value, χ² cut 200, keeping the minimum-χ² candidate per event, and extend this to 6C and 7C fits that also constrain the (tag D_s + transition γ) mass to the D_s* and the signal D_s^+ mass to the known D_s mass. Require the D_s* → γ D_s transition photon to have lab energy below 0.2 GeV and a recoil mass in [1.95, 2.00] GeV/c². Apply vetoes against η → π+π-π0 (any π+π-π0 mass in 0.49–0.58 GeV/c²), K_S^0 → π0π0 (M(π0π0) outside 0.487–0.511 GeV/c²), K_S^0 → π+π− (secondary-vertex L/σ_L > 2), misidentified open-charm D^0/D^0bar (|M(K^-π+π0) − M_D0| < 30 MeV/c² and |M(K+π+π-π-) − M_D0bar| < 30 MeV/c² simultaneously), and ρ+η′ with η′ → π+π-γ (reject when the background-hypothesis χ² is smaller than the signal-hypothesis χ²). The D_s^+ signal region is [1.93, 1.99] GeV/c².
