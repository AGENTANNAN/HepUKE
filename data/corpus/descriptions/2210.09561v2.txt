At the seven c.m. energy points from 4.600 to 4.699 GeV, measure inclusive Λc− → n̄ X using the single-tag method: reconstruct a tag Λc+ → pK−π+ with the ΔE window (−34, +20) MeV and, when several candidates pass, keep the one with minimum |ΔE|. Use the real data at these seven energies together with the inclusive MC sample. On the signal side allow no charged tracks identified as proton (highest PID probability) within Vz < 20 cm, require no photons, and identify the antineutron through its EMC shower: the most energetic shower must have E > 0.48 GeV, more than 20 hit crystals, a second moment S > 18 cm², and an opening angle greater than 20° with respect to any charged track. Apply a 4-constraint kinematic fit with χ² < 200. Tag candidates are further selected with MBC ∈ (2.275, 2.31) GeV/c², the antineutron detection efficiency being corrected data-driven as a function of n̄ momentum and cosθ using a J/ψ → p n̄ π− control sample at 3.097 GeV, with q q̄ background taken from the MBC sideband (2.20, 2.26) GeV/c² and Λc+Λc− background from inclusive Λc− → p̄ X MC.
