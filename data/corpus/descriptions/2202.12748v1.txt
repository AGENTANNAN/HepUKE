At centre-of-mass energies from 3.872 to 4.700 GeV, study e+e− → η π+ π−, η → γγ. Use the available real data and inclusive MC at these energy points, and generate exclusive MC for ψ(4260) → η π+ π−, η → γγ, mapped to the same energy points as the real data. Event selection: exactly one positive and one negative charged track (total two), with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm; exactly two photons with barrel energy > 25 MeV, endcap > 50 MeV, and photon–track opening angle > 10°; identify the two tracks as π+ and π− by the probability method against K and proton hypotheses, with probability > 0.001. Perform a Kalman kinematic fit on the two photons with a π0 mass constraint and assign the candidate as η, then a 4C kinematic fit on η π+ π− with χ² < 200, flagged nominal. Apply this selection to real data, inclusive MC, and exclusive MC.
