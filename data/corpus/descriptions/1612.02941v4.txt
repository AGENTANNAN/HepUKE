On the J/ψ at √s = 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → γ η_c with the two channels η_c → φ φ, φ → K+ K− (final state γ K+ K− K+ K−) and η_c → ω φ, ω → π+ π− π0, φ → K+ K−, π0 → γ γ (final state γ K+ K− π+ π− π0). Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive J/ψ MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal decay chains, plus 200k-event background exclusive MC for J/ψ → γ φ K+K− and J/ψ → γ K+K−K+K− in the φφ mode and J/ψ → η′ φ, η′ → γ ω in the ωφ mode. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; photons must satisfy EMC timing 0–700 ns, be at least 10° from the nearest charged track, and exceed 25 MeV (barrel) or 50 MeV (endcap), with at least one photon in the φφ mode and at least three photons in the ωφ mode. For φφ apply no PID and take the four tracks directly as K+ K− K+ K−; for ωφ use probability PID with a 0.001 cut to identify one K+ and one K− against π/p, then assign the remaining positive and negative tracks as π+ and π−. Reconstruct π0 via a 1C mass-constrained fit to a γγ pair, χ²<25, requiring at least one π0 and taking the γγ pair closest to the nominal π0 mass. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γ K+K−K+K− (φφ) or γ K+K−π+π−π0 (ωφ) with χ²<200, using the smallest-χ² combination; the ωφ fit also imposes |M(K+K−)−M_φ|<0.008 GeV/c² and |M(π+π−π0)−M_ω|<0.03 GeV/c², while the φφ mode fixes the two φ candidates by minimizing the sum of K+K− mass deviations with |M(K+K−)−M_φ|<0.02 GeV/c² applied on the fit-corrected masses at ROOT level.
