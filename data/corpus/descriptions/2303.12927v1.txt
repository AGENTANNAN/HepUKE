At the eight c.m. energies 4.128–4.226 GeV (7.33 fb⁻¹ total real data, plus inclusive MC), study e⁺e⁻ → D_s*⁺D_s⁻ with the double-tag technique, the tag D_s⁻ decaying hadronically and the signal D_s⁺ → f₀(980)e⁺ν_e, f₀(980) → π⁺π⁻, with the D_s*⁺ → γD_s⁺ transition photon and a missing massless ν_e; a 500k-event exclusive signal MC is generated from the decay card ψ(4260) → D_s*⁺D_s⁻, D_s*⁺ → γD_s⁺, D_s⁺ → f₀(980)e⁺ν_e, f₀ → π⁺π⁻, D_s⁻ → K⁺K⁻π⁻. On the tag side the D_s⁻ is reconstructed in the hadronic modes K⁺K⁻π⁻, K⁺K⁻π⁻π⁰, π⁺π⁻π⁻, K_S⁰K⁻, K_S⁰K⁺π⁻π⁻, π⁻η(γγ), ρ⁻η(ρ⁻→π⁻π⁰), π⁻η′ with η′→π⁺π⁻η and η′→γρ⁰, requiring charm −1, and the candidate with M_rec closest to the nominal D_s* mass is kept, with the M_tag shape fitted by a resolution-convolved Gaussian for signal and a Chebyshev polynomial for background. On the signal side require exactly one π⁺, one π⁻ and one electron (identified by the lepton key), plus the transition photon. Apply a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation and the f₀(980) mass constraint, χ² cut 200; select M_rec² against the transition γ plus tag D_s⁻ in 3.78–4.05 GeV²/c⁴, |M_miss²| < 0.06 GeV²/c⁴, and M(π⁺π⁻) in 0.6–1.6 GeV/c². The f₀ lineshape is treated with the Flatté formula (BESII parameters), giving a weighted signal efficiency of (35.44 ± 0.07)%, and three tag modes (K_S⁰K⁻π⁰, K⁻π⁺π⁻, K_S⁰K⁻π⁺π⁻) are unavailable in the tagging algorithm and are handled downstream in ROOT.
