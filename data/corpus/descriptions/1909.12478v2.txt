Measure Born cross sections for e+e− → D1(2420)+ D− + c.c. (with D1(2420)+ → D+ π+ π−) and e+e− → ψ(3770) π+ π− using seven energy points (4358.3, 4387.4, 4415.6, 4467.1, 4527.1, 4574.5, and 4599.5 MeV) of real data and inclusive MC, generating 100k-event exclusive MC for the D+D−π+π− final state (D+ → K−π+π+) at each energy point. Select events with at least three positive and three negative charged tracks satisfying |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100, Vr<10 cm; identify particles via the probability method (prob>0.001), separating kaons from pions and protons and requiring at least one K−, with the remaining positive and negative tracks assigned as π+ and π−. Perform a vertex fit for D+ → K−π+π+ candidates with χ²<100, followed by a kinematic fit constraining the K−π+π+ invariant mass to the nominal D+ mass and enforcing four-momentum conservation, with χ²<20. Extract the D− signal from the recoil mass against D+π+π−, using sideband subtraction for combinatorial background, and obtain D1(2420)+ and ψ(3770)π+π− yields from fits to the recoil-mass distribution.
