At √s = 4.009 GeV, study the leptonic decays Ds+ → μ+νμ and Ds+ → τ+ντ with τ+ → π+ντ, tagging the recoiling Ds− through nine hadronic single-tag modes (K_S0 K−, K+K−π−, K+K−π−π0, K_S0 K+π−π−, π+π−π−, ηπ− with η→γγ, π0ηπ−, and η′π− with η′→π+π−η or η′→π+π−γ). Use 482 pb−1 of real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes. Tag-side charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm, with pion/kaon identification by CL_π>0 and CL_π>CL_K (pions) or CL_K>0 and CL_K>CL_π (kaons); photons are required to have E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) or E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), TDC in [0,700] ns, and an angle >10° to any charged track. Intermediate mass windows are 0.115<M(γγ)<0.150 GeV/c2 for π0, 0.510<M(γγ)<0.570 GeV/c2 for η, 0.943<M(π+π−η)<0.973 GeV/c2 and 0.932<M(γρ0)<0.980 GeV/c2 with 0.570<M(π+π−)<0.970 GeV/c2 for η′, and K_S0 candidates are π+π− pairs with |Vz|<20 cm, 0.487<M(π+π−)<0.511 GeV/c2, and a secondary-vertex decay length at least twice its resolution, with π0/η mass-constrained to 1C; tag Ds− candidates must have 1.962<M_BC<1.982 GeV/c2 (yield 15127±321). On the signal side, require exactly one good charged track with charge opposite the tag, treated as a μ+ with no PID in the muonic mode or as a π+ with no PID in the tauonic mode, plus no unassociated neutral shower above 300 MeV; the squared missing mass MM2 = (E_beam−E_μ/π)2/c4 − (−p_Ds− − p_μ/π)2/c2 is stored, and the signal region −0.15<MM2<0.20 (GeV/c2)2 is applied downstream in ROOT. No kinematic fit is imposed because of the undetected neutrinos; only the four-momentum conservation constraint with χ2<200 is recorded, and the detection efficiencies are 91.4±0.5% (μν) and 41.0±0.3% (τν, τ→πν).
