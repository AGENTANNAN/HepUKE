Using 1.31×10⁹ J/ψ events at 3.097 GeV, together with the matching inclusive MC and 500k-event exclusive MC samples for each of the two isospin-violating modes, study J/ψ → γ η′ with η′ → π⁺π⁻π⁰ (Mode I) and η′ → π⁰π⁰π⁰ (Mode II). For Mode I require exactly two oppositely charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge 0, at least three photons with 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) thresholds, TDC in 0–14 and >10° from any track, and pion identification by the probability method (prob>0.001) against K/p giving one π⁺ and one π⁻; form π⁰ candidates from γγ with a 1C Kalman mass constraint to m_π⁰ (χ²<25), then perform a nominal 6C fit to γ π⁺π⁻π⁰ with 4C energy–momentum conservation plus m_π⁰ and m_η′ constraints and χ²<200, retaining the smallest-χ² combination, alongside 4C fits to the γγγπ⁺π⁻ and γγπ⁺π⁻ competing hypotheses for ROOT-level probability ordering. For Mode II require no charged tracks and at least seven photons with the same energy/TDC/angle criteria, reconstruct at least three π⁰ via the γγ Kalman mass constraint (χ²<25) and an η candidate for the veto fit, then apply a nominal 8C fit to γ π⁰π⁰π⁰ with 4C plus m_η′ and the π⁰ constraints and χ²<200, plus a competing 7C γηπ⁰π⁰ hypothesis. Backgrounds are suppressed by rejecting |M(γπ⁰)−m_ω|<0.05 GeV/c² (both modes), γγ pairs with mass in the η region 0.52–0.59 GeV/c² (Mode II), and π⁰ candidates with |cosθ_decay|>0.95 (Mode II), with published tighter χ² cuts (25 for Mode I, 70 for Mode II) deferred to the ROOT stage. The radiative photon is taken as the highest-energy photon in the event, with remaining photons used for the π⁰'s.
