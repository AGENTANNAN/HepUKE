At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), measure D0 → ω/η π+π− and D0 → ω/η π0π0, as well as D+ → ω/η π+π0, using a double-tag technique where the tag side is an anti-D0 or D− reconstructed in hadronic modes; ω/η decay via π+π−π0 and π0 → γγ. Use the 712_3773 real data set and its inclusive MC, plus a 200k-event exclusive MC sample generated for ψ(3770) → D0 anti-D0 with D0 → ω π+π−, anti-D0 → K− π+, ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ (this same exclusive sample is applied to both the D0 and D+ analyses). Tag anti-D0 modes are K+π−, K+π−π0, and K+π−π−π+, with a K_S^0 veto |M(π+π−) − M(K_S^0)| > 30 MeV/c^2 for the four-track mode; tag D− modes are K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S^0 π−, K_S^0 π−π0, K_S^0 π−π−π+, and K+K−π−, with Λ, K_S^0, and Σ− vetoes in M(pbar π+) ∈ [1.110,1.120], M(π+π−) ∈ [0.480,0.520], and M(pbar π0) ∈ [1.170,1.200] GeV/c^2. On the signal side, require at least one π+ and one π− with net charge 0 and 2–6 photons for the D0 modes, and at least two π+ and one π− with net charge +1 and at least four photons for the D+ modes; π0 candidates are γγ pairs with 0.115 < M(γγ) < 0.150 GeV/c^2 and at least one photon in the EMC barrel. Reconstruct ω/η from π+π−π0 combinations with M(π+π−π0) < 0.9 GeV/c^2, using ω signal windows 0.74–0.82 GeV/c^2 and η windows 0.52–0.57 GeV/c^2, and reject K_S^0 candidates via 0.475 < M(π+π−) < 0.520 or 0.448 < M(π0π0) < 0.548 GeV/c^2. Perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation and the γγ invariant mass constrained to the π0 nominal mass, χ² < 200, constraining each π0 separately for the D+ modes; apply mode-dependent ΔE cuts within 3.0σ (D0 → ω π+π−) or 3.5σ (D0 → ω π0π0 and D+), choosing the minimum |ΔE| combination. Signal yields come from a 2D unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to M_BC^tag vs M_BC^sig with signal plus BKGI/BKGII/BKGIII backgrounds, sideband subtraction for BKGIV, and peaking BKGV from D → K_S^0 ω/η; strong-phase corrections for the C = −1 ψ(3770) quantum correlation are applied for flavor tags. No explicit PID selection, charged-track |cosθ|/|Vz|/Vr quality cuts, or photon energy thresholds are encoded in this DSL; track and photon content is defined solely by the stated multiplicities and net charge.
