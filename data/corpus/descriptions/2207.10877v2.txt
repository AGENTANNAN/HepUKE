On the ψ(3686) (3.686 GeV), search for ψ(3686) → Λ_c^+ Σ^- + c.c., with Λ_c^+ → p K^- π^+ and Σ^- → p̄ π^0. Use the 709_3686 real data sample (448.1×10^6 events) and its inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive signal MC events with the same decay card. Charged-track selection requires 2–10 positive and 2–10 negative tracks, 4–10 total, each with |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm and |Vz|<10 cm, while the long-lived Σ^- daughters are handled with looser vertex cuts in the generated code. Particle identification uses the probability method with highest-probability assignment among p, K^-, π^+ and p̄, requiring one of each. Photons must have E>25 MeV and be more than 10° from tracks, in the barrel |cosθ|<0.80 or endcap 0.86<|cosθ|<0.92; a π^0 is reconstructed from two photons by a 1C mass-constrained fit with χ²<25, requiring at least one π^0. A 5C kinematic fit to p K^- π^+ p̄ π^0 (four-momentum conservation plus the π^0 mass constraint) is then applied with χ²<60 and nominal flagging, and the best candidate is the one with smallest 5C χ². Backgrounds are rejected by vetoing M(π^+ p̄) in [1.090,1.130] GeV/c² and M(K^- π^+) in [0.756,1.036] GeV/c², and by requiring 1.150<M(p̄ π^0)<1.230 GeV/c² for Σ^-; MC charged tracks get a helix-parameter correction before the fit.
