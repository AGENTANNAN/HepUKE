At √s = 4.23 GeV (1092 pb⁻¹) and 4.26 GeV (826 pb⁻¹), confirm the charged charmoniumlike Zc(3885) in e⁺e⁻ → π± (D D*)∓ via a double-D tag, combining the isospin processes e⁺e⁻ → π⁺ D⁰ anti-D*⁻ (anti-D*⁻ → anti-D⁰ π⁻, soft π⁻ missing) and e⁺e⁻ → π⁺ D⁻ D*⁰ (D*⁰ → D⁰ π⁰, soft π⁰ missing), with charge conjugates. Use these two real data sets and their inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for the Zc signal and non-resonant phase-space cards of each process plus the D₁(2420) D̄⁰ background. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93 with PCA within 10 cm along the beam and 1 cm transverse; K/π separation uses combined dE/dx and TOF likelihoods (K if Prob(K)>Prob(π), K_S⁰ daughters exempt); photons require E>25 MeV (barrel, |cosθ|<0.8) or E>50 MeV (endcap, 0.84<|cosθ|<0.92) and >20° from any charged track; π⁰ candidates from photon pairs with 0.115 < M(γγ) < 0.150 GeV/c² and a 1C mass constraint; K_S⁰ from vertex-fitted π⁺π⁻ with 0.487 < M(π⁺π⁻) < 0.511 GeV/c² and a secondary-vertex constraint. Tag D⁰/anti-D⁰ through K⁻π⁺, K⁻π⁺π⁰, K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻, K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻π⁰, and D⁻ through K⁺π⁻π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰ π⁻, K_S⁰ π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰ π⁺π⁻π⁻, K⁺K⁻π⁻, the latter combined with a fully reconstructed D⁰ on the signal side in one selection per D⁰ mode; keep the candidate with the smallest ΔM̂, requiring −20 < ΔM̂ < 15 MeV/c² and |ΔM| < 40 MeV/c² for the D⁰ D̄⁰ tag, or −17 < ΔM̂ < 14 MeV/c² and |ΔM| < 35 MeV/c² for the D⁻ D⁰ tag. Require at least one bachelor π⁺ (net charge +1) not from the D mesons, then perform a 4C fit constraining the D masses to their PDG values with χ² < 200 (the paper's tighter cut is applied downstream), and veto D*D* by |M_recoil(Dπ) − M(D*)| < 30 MeV/c² together with M(π⁺ D⁰) ≥ 2.03 GeV/c² or M(π⁺ D⁻) > 2.08 GeV/c².
