At √s = 3.773 GeV, perform a ψ(3770) → D0 anti-D0 double-tag analysis of the all-neutral decays D0 → π0π0π0, π0π0η, π0ηη, and ηηη (π0,η → γγ), tagging the other D as anti-D0 → K+π−, K+π−π0, or K+π−π−π+. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the four D0 signal modes (phase-space cards, with the π0ηη mode noted as dominated by a0(980)0η). Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ| < 0.93, Vr < 1 cm, and |Vz| < 10 cm, with PID from TOF and dE/dx assigning the highest confidence level, selecting the tag modes K+π−, K+π−π0, and K+π−π−π+ (two or four charged tracks as appropriate). Photons must satisfy E > 25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ| < 0.8) and E > 50 MeV in the endcap (0.86 < |cosθ| < 0.92), with EMC TDC in [0,700] ns and a minimum photon angle of 10°, while the all-neutral signal requires six photons (the code consumes only up to two signal photons, as noted). A kinematic fit applies a four-momentum constraint and constrains γγ invariant masses to the nominal π0 (or η for ηηη) with χ² < 200, noting that three 1C π0/η mass constraints are needed but only one is expressible; the tag uses mBC > 1.83 GeV and the best tag by minimum |ΔE| with windows Kπ (−0.027,0.025), Kππ0 (−0.071,0.041), and Kπππ (−0.025,0.022) GeV. The D0 signal ΔE windows are 3π0 (−0.115,0.059), 2π0η (−0.088,0.053), π0ηη (−0.061,0.045), and 3η (−0.030,0.028) GeV, choosing the candidate with smallest |ΔE|, with vetoes against D0 → 4π0 (χ²_4π > 20 when at least four independent π0), K_S0 → π0π0 (M(π0π0) in [445,535] MeV), and cross-feed χ²_3π0 > 20 and χ²_π0π0η > 20 with no other combination having χ² < 20.
