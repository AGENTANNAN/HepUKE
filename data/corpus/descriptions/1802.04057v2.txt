On the ψ(3686) (3.686 GeV), search for the FCNC decay ψ(3686) → Λc+ pbar e+e− with Λc+ → p K− π+, giving a six-charged-track final state p pbar K− π+ e+ e− and zero net charge. Use the ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for the signal mode. Event selection: exactly six charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge 0; PID by the probability method (cut 0.001) with π/K separation and high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, else muon), requiring at least two protons, one K−, one π+, one l+ and one l−. Require the vertex fit to converge and perform a 4C kinematic fit on the six particles with χ²<200, plus an anti-Λ veto rejecting M(pbar π+) > 1.13 GeV/c². The Λc+ is reconstructed in the p K− π+ mass window [2.25, 2.32] GeV/c².
