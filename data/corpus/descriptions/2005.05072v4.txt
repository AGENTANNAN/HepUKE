On the six BOSS energy points 4.178, 4.189, 4.199, 4.209, 4.219 and 4.226 GeV, study e+e− → Ds*+Ds− + c.c. → γDs+Ds− by partially reconstructing the normalization mode Ds+ → K+K−π+, reconstructing the Ds+ and the soft γ from Ds*+ while inferring the Ds− from recoil. Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at each point, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for this normalization mode (e+e− → Ds*+Ds−, Ds*+ → γDs+, Ds+ → K+K−π+, with the Ds− treated inclusively per the ConExc line-shape note); the seven signal modes Ds+ → K+η′, η′π+, K+η, ηπ+, K+K_S0, K_S0π+, K+π0 are noted as requiring separate specifications. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and at least one positive track; photons with EMC timing 0–14 (≈0–700 ns), barrel energy >25 MeV, endcap >50 MeV, >10° from the nearest charged track, and at least one photon. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating π from K/p and K from π/p, requiring at least one K+, one K− and one π+. Treat Ds− as the missing particle, require recoil mass M_rec(Ds+γ) ∈ (1.95,1.99) GeV, choose the combination with minimum |ΔE|, ΔE=(E_Ds+ + E_γ + E_rec)−E0, with the normalization-mode ΔE window (−0.030,+0.020) GeV, then constrain M_rec(Ds+γ) to the nominal Ds− mass in a kinematic fit to be implemented in C++, and apply the mass bands M_rec(Ds+) ∈ (2.100,2.130) GeV and M(Ds+γ) ∈ (2.095,2.130) GeV; notes also list per-mode ΔE windows and π0/η/η′/K_S0 selections for the other channels.
