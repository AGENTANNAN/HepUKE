On ψ(3686) data at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ (J=0,1,2), χ_cJ → η η η', with η → γγ, in two independent chains according to the η' decay mode: η' → γ π+ π− and η' → η π+ π−. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC for each of the two η' modes. Event selection: exactly two charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge 0, identified as pions by the probability method (cut 0.001) against kaons and protons; photons with at least 25 MeV energy and a minimum opening angle of 10°, requiring ≥8 photons in mode 1 (η' → γπ+π−) and ≥9 in mode 2 (η' → ηπ+π−). A π0 veto rejects any γγ pair with |M_γγ − m_π0| below 15 MeV, and the η → γγ candidates are formed by Kalman 1C fits to the nominal η mass with χ²<200, three η's in mode 1 and four η's in mode 2 (|M_γγ − m_η| window ~50 MeV), while the η' is required within 30 MeV of its nominal mass. Competing photon-hypothesis fits with one or two fewer photons are performed to reject background, with the lower-χ² hypothesis vetoed against the nominal one. The nominal kinematic fit is 6C in mode 1 (radiative γ, 3η, π+π−) and 7C in mode 2 (radiative γ, 4η, π+π−), i.e. four-momentum conservation plus two or three η mass constraints respectively, with χ²<200. Both chains are run on the same real data, inclusive MC, and their respective signal MC.
