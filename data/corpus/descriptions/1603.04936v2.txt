Search for the radiative decays h_c→γη′ and h_c→γη via ψ′→π0h_c using 4.48×10^8 ψ(2S) events (2009+2012), the matching inclusive MC, and a 44 pb^-1 continuum data set at √s=3.65 GeV; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of four chains: ψ′→π0h_c, h_c→γη′, η′→π+π−η, η→γγ; ψ′→π0h_c, h_c→γη′, η′→γπ+π−; ψ′→π0h_c, h_c→γη, η→γγ; and ψ′→π0h_c, h_c→γη, η→π+π−π0, π0→γγ. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge zero, exactly one π+ and one π− for the three charged modes, and no charged tracks for the all-neutral η→γγ mode. Photons must satisfy EMC timing 0–700 ns, lie at least 10° from the nearest charged track, and have energy >25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.8) or >50 MeV in the endcaps (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), with photon multiplicity at least 5, 4, 5, and 5 for the four final states, respectively. Pion identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating π from K and p, requiring one π+ and one π− in the charged modes. Each mode first constrains γγ to the π0 mass (χ²<25) and, where relevant, γγ to the η mass (χ²<25); the η→π+π−π0 mode requires at least two π0 candidates. Full kinematic fits are then applied: 6C (four-momentum plus m(π0) and m(η)) with χ²<120 for the π+π−ηγγ final state, 5C (four-momentum plus m(π0)) with χ²<50 for the γπ+π−η′ mode, 6C (four-momentum plus m(π0) and m(η)) with χ²<200 for the all-neutral η→γγ mode, and 6C (four-momentum plus two π0 masses) with χ²<120 for the η→π+π−π0 mode; the charged-track vertex is constrained to the IP in the three charged modes, and all photon combinations are looped over retaining the smallest χ². Signal regions are [M(η′)−12, M(η′)+12] MeV for the two η′ modes, [M(η)−25, M(η)+25] MeV for the all-neutral η→γγ mode, and [M(η)−12, M(η)+12] MeV for the η→π+π−π0 mode, with sidebands used for background checks.
