On the ψ(3686), study the isospin-violating decays ψ(3686) → Λ Λ̄ π0 and ψ(3686) → Λ Λ̄ η, with Λ → p π−, Λ̄ → p̄ π+, and π0/η → γγ, giving a common p p̄ π+π−γγ final state. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC from the 709_3686 set, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for the Λ Λ̄ π0/η signal mode. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with four tracks and net charge zero, and are identified by the probability method (prob cut 0.001), with proton/antiproton separated against π and K and pions against K and p, assigning p, p̄, π+, π−. Photons must have energy above 25 MeV, EMC time within [0,700] ns, and be at least 10° from any charged track, with at least two photons required. Λ (p π−) and Λ̄ (p̄ π+) are reconstructed with secondary-vertex fits and mass windows of 1.111–1.121 GeV/c². A 4C kinematic fit is then performed imposing energy-momentum conservation under the Λ Λ̄ γγ hypothesis with χ²<200, flagged nominal.
