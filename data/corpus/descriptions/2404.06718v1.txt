Study e+e− → η hc, hc → γ ηc, ηc → p p̄ (one of the 16 ηc hadronic modes fitted simultaneously), η → γγ, using the 4.178 GeV real data and inclusive MC and generating 500k exclusive MC events for this mode, with the analysis intended to be repeated over 22 scan points from 4.129 to 4.600 GeV. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm and Vr < 1 cm, with exactly one positive and one negative track and zero net charge. Photons are required to have 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, lie within TDC 0–14, be at least 10° from any track, and at least three photons must be present (two from η → γγ plus the hc → γ ηc transition photon). Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating protons from kaons and pions and demanding exactly one proton and one antiproton. A Kalman fit constrains the γγ invariant mass to the nominal η mass with χ² < 25 and requires exactly one η, followed by a nominal 4C fit on η γ p p̄ with χ² < 25. Signal windows are the η recoil mass in [3.510, 3.540] GeV/c² for hc and the p p̄ invariant mass in [2.944, 3.024] GeV/c² for ηc, with the ηc candidate closest to its nominal mass chosen when multiple η candidates have recoil mass in [3.480, 3.600] GeV/c².
