At the J/ψ (√s = 3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ η′, η′ → π+π−μ+μ−. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes: the signal η′ → π+π−μ+μ− and the competing η′ → π+π−π+π−. Event selection requires four charged tracks with at least two positive and two negative, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, more than 15° from the nearest charged track, and at least one photon. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying pions against kaons and protons, and treating high-momentum tracks (p>0.3) as leptons, with muons selected by EMC energy in the 0.05–0.35 range, requiring no electrons and at least two muons. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γ π+π−μ+μ− with χ²<30, plus a competing 4C fit to γ π+π−π+π− (no χ² cut) for the four-pion veto. The remaining ROOT-side selection applies an η′ mass window of 0.94–0.97 GeV/c², an η→μ+μ− veto requiring |M(μ+μ−)−M_η|>0.02 GeV/c², discards events with χ²(γ 4π) < χ²(γ π+π−μ+μ−), and chooses the best candidate by the combined χ² = χ²_4C + χ²_PID.
