Measure the transverse polarization of Σ+ in e+e− → Σ+ Σ−, with Σ+ → p π0, Σ− → p̄ π0, and π0 → γγ, at the ψ(3686) scan points. Use seven real-data sets at 3.682, 3.683, 3.684, 3.685, 3.687, 3.691, and 3.710 GeV, plus inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for ψ(4260) → Σ+ Σ−, Σ+ → p π0, Σ− → p̄ π0, π0 → γγ (phase space) at each energy point. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, net charge zero, at least one positive and one negative track, and no Vz or Vr cut; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold and EMC timing in [0,14], at least four photons. No PID is used; (anti)protons are instead identified by momentum > 0.5 GeV/c. Form two π0 from photon pairs by constraining each γγ invariant mass to the π0 mass (χ²<25, exactly two π0), then perform a 6C kinematic fit to p p̄ π0 π0 (four-momentum plus two π0 mass constraints) with χ²<100, retaining the combination with the smallest 6C χ² and pairing pπ0 / p̄π0 to minimize the Σ/Σ̄ mass deviation. Require M(pπ0) and M(p̄π0) within [mΣ−4σ, mΣ+3σ], and reject e+e− → π0π0 J/ψ by |M(π0π0)_recoil − m(J/ψ)| > 15 MeV/c².
