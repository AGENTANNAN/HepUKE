At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), search for the semileptonic decay Ξ− → Ξ0 e− ν̄e with the single-tag technique using the Ξ+ tag (Ξ+ → Λ̄ π+, Λ̄ → p̄ π+, Λ → p π−). Use the 3.097 GeV real data and its inclusive MC, and generate 1 million exclusive signal-MC events with J/ψ → Ξ− Ξ̄+, Ξ̄+ → Λ̄ π+, Ξ− → Ξ0 e− ν̄e, Ξ0 → Λ π0, Λ → p π−, π0 → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100, Vr<10, at least three positive and two negative tracks with exactly five in total; photons with EMC timing 0–14 and more than 10° from any track, barrel (endcap) energy above 25 (50) MeV, at least two. PID by the probability method (CL>0.001): protons identified against K/π, requiring at least one p and one anti-p that are then removed from the charged lists, and pions identified against K/p, while the electron is the track with the highest e/π/K confidence from dE/dx and TOF. A π0 is formed from two photons in a Kalman fit constraining the γγ mass to the π0 nominal value (χ²<25, ≥1 π0), Λ → pπ− and Λ̄ → p̄π+ are built by secondary-vertex fits minimizing the mass difference, and a nominal four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit to Λ̄ π+ Λ π0 e− is performed with a missing νe and χ²<200. The tag uses mass windows |M(p̄π+)−M(Λ)|<5 MeV and |M(Λ̄π+)−M(Ξ+)|<5 MeV with recoil mass in [1.290, 1.342] GeV, and the signal requires |M(Λπ0)−M(Ξ0)|<14.5 MeV with Ξ0 momentum 0.79–0.84 GeV.
