At √s = 4.128–4.226 GeV (seven energy-point samples totaling 7.33 fb⁻¹), measure absolute branching fractions of 15 Ds⁺ hadronic decays (19 final states) with the double-tag technique, reconstructing both Ds⁺ and Ds⁻ through hadronic tags from e⁺e⁻ → Ds*⁺Ds⁻, where the Ds*→Ds γ/π⁰ transition is not reconstructed. Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive signal MC of e⁺e⁻ → Ds*⁺Ds⁻ with Ds⁺ → K⁺K⁻π⁺ and Ds⁻ → K⁺K⁻π⁻. Tag both charm sides with the modes K⁺K⁻π⁺, K_S K⁺, K_S K⁺π⁰, K_S K_S π⁺, K⁺K⁻π⁺π⁰ and K_S K⁺π⁺π⁻, selecting the best candidate by minimum |M_rec − m(Ds*⁺)| from the Ds recoil mass (tighter window [2.10, 2.13] GeV/c² for the π⁺π⁺π⁻η, π⁺π⁰η'_{γρ} and K_S π⁺π⁰ modes). The signal side requires zero photons; photons must satisfy E>25 (50) MeV in barrel (endcap), angle to the nearest track >10°, and 0<t<700 ns. Intermediate resonances are reconstructed as K_S0 → π⁺π⁻ with |M(π⁺π⁻)−m_K_S|<12 MeV/c², vertex χ²<100 and decay length >2σ; π⁰/η from γγ use a 1C mass-constrained fit with χ²<30 and masses in [115,150] and [490,580] MeV/c² and at least one barrel photon; η→π⁺π⁻π⁰ in [530,560], η'→π⁺π⁻η in [943,973], η'→γρ in [946,970], ρ⁰→π⁺π⁻ in [570,970] MeV/c², while the K⁺π⁺π⁻ mode vetoes π⁺π⁻ pairs in [487,511] MeV/c². Perform a four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ²<200, and extract double-tag yields by counting in the signal region |m̄−m_Ds|<15 MeV/c² and |Δm|<30 MeV/c² with sideband subtraction over 80<|Δm|<140 MeV/c².
