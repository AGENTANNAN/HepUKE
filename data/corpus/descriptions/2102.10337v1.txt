Using initial-state radiation at seven c.m. energies of 3.773, 4.180, 4.230, 4.260, 4.360, 4.420, and 4.600 GeV (total ~7.5 fb⁻¹), study e⁺e⁻ → p p̄ γ_ISR for proton electromagnetic form factors. Analyse the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at each point, together with 200k-event exclusive MC per energy point generated with KKMC for ψ(4260) → p⁺ p⁻ γ (phase-space decay). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge zero; photons with E>25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), EMC TDC 0–14, >10° from the nearest charged track, at least one. PID by the probability method (prob>0.001), requiring one proton and one antiproton with the proton hypothesis favoured over kaon and pion; both proton candidates must have E/p<0.5 to suppress Bhabha. The ISR photon must be isolated by >20° from both the proton and antiproton tracks, and the highest-energy photon with E>0.4 GeV is tagged as the ISR photon after removing lower-energy photons. Perform a 4C kinematic fit (p p̄ γ) with four-momentum conservation, flagged nominal, χ²<50; no explicit invariant-mass window is applied.
