On the ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ (J=0,1,2), χ_cJ → Λ Λ̄ η', with Λ → p π−, Λ̄ → p̄ π+, and two η' decay modes: η' → γ π+ π− (Mode I) and η' → η π+ π−, η → γγ (Mode II). Use 3.686 GeV ψ(2S) real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two η' decay modes, both through χ_c0. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least three positive and three negative tracks; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, photon–track angle >10°, EMC timing 0–14, at least two (Mode I) or at least three (Mode II) photons; PID by the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying protons against kaons and pions, requiring at least one p and one p̄, with the remaining positive/negative tracks assigned as π+/π−. Reconstruct Λ → p π− and Λ̄ → p̄ π+ via secondary vertex fits minimizing the mass difference. For Mode I, perform a 4C kinematic fit to Λ Λ̄ γ π+ π− with Λ/Λ̄ mass constraints in 1.110–1.122 GeV/c² and χ²<18; for Mode II, first reconstruct η from a γγ pair constrained to the η mass (χ²<200), then perform a 5C fit to Λ Λ̄ η π+ π− with χ²<53; both modes use a Λ/Λ̄ signal window of ±5 MeV/c², Σ0 veto M(Λγ)>1.2 GeV/c², η' windows of ±12 MeV/c² (Mode I) or ±10 MeV/c² (Mode II), plus J/ψ and π0 vetoes, and signal yields are extracted with χ_cJ mass windows [3.390,3.445], [3.495,3.525], and [3.540,3.570] GeV/c².
