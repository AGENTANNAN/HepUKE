Measure the cross section for e+e− → f1(1285)π+π−, with f1(1285) → π+π−η and η → γγ, at 45 center-of-mass energies from 3.808 to 4.951 GeV. Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at the scan points shown (3.810, 4.180, 4.260, 4.420, 4.600, 4.680, and 4.946 GeV), and generate 100k-event ConExc exclusive MC per representative point for the ISR continuum signal mode e+e− → f1(1285)π+π−, f1(1285) → π+π−η, η → γγ. Event selection requires exactly four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and net charge 0; photons must have EMC timing 0–14, be at least 10° from any track, pass barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, and number at least two. Particle identification uses the probability method, separating pions from kaons, and requires two π+ and two π−. Reconstruct η → γγ with a kinematic fit constraining the γγ invariant mass to the nominal η mass, χ² < 25, requiring at least one η. Then perform a 4C kinematic fit to π+π−π+π−η, with χ² < 200 at BOSS level (tightened to χ² < 100 in the final analysis), an η mass window |M(γγ)−M(η)| < 25 MeV/c², and, when more than two photon candidates are present, select the combination with the smallest 4C χ² before fitting the M(π+π−η) spectrum at each energy point.
