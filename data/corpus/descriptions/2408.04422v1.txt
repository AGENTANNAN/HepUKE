Using 7.93 fb⁻¹ of data at √s = 3.773 GeV and the matching inclusive MC, search for the double-tag decay D⁺ → K_S⁰ π⁰ e⁺ ν_e with the tag D⁻ reconstructed in six hadronic modes (Kππ, K_S⁰π, Kπππ⁰, K_S⁰ππ⁰, K_S⁰πππ, KKπ); generate 500k exclusive MC events for D⁺ → K_S⁰ π⁰ e⁺ ν_e with K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻ and π⁰ → γγ in phase space. On the signal side require 2–48 photons with energy above 25 MeV and a minimum angle of 10°, plus exactly one π⁺, one π⁻ and one e⁺, net charge +1, and one missing massless ν_e. Perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation, constraining the γγ invariant mass to the nominal π⁰ mass and the π⁺π⁻ invariant mass to the nominal K_S⁰ mass, with χ² < 200. Tag-side candidates satisfy M_BC in [1.863, 1.877] GeV/c² with mode-dependent ΔE; double-tag events require zero extra tracks, maximum extra-photon energy below 0.25 GeV, M(K_S⁰ π⁰ e⁺) < 1.76 GeV/c², and U_miss = E_miss − |p_miss|c > 0.04 GeV, from which the signal yield is extracted.
