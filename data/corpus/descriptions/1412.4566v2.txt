On 482 pb⁻¹ of e⁺e⁻ data at √s = 4.009 GeV, study e⁺e⁻ → D*⁰ D̄⁰ with D*⁰ → D⁰π⁰ or D⁰γ, fully reconstructing the D⁰D̄⁰ tag and using the D⁰D̄⁰ recoil mass to separate the two D*⁰ decay channels. Use the 4.009 GeV real data and its inclusive MC (500 pb⁻¹ equivalent), and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of five tag modes: D⁰ → K⁻π⁺ with D̄⁰ → K⁺π⁻, K⁻π⁺ with K⁺π⁻π⁰, K⁻π⁺π⁰ with K⁺π⁻, K⁻π⁺ with K⁺π⁻π⁺π⁻, and K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻ with K⁺π⁻, with D*⁰ decaying by PDG fractions 0.647 to D⁰π⁰ and 0.353 to D⁰γ. Charged tracks must have |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, and zero net charge, with two positive and two negative tracks in modes I–III and three positive and three negative tracks in modes IV–V. Photons must have EMC energy > 25 MeV in the barrel or > 50 MeV in the endcap, be at least 20° from any charged track, and have EMC time 0–700 ns; tag modes containing a π⁰ require at least two photons. PID uses the probability method with a 0.1% probability cut and π/K separation, requiring exactly one K⁺ and one K⁻, with π⁺/π⁻ multiplicities of one each in modes I–III and two each in modes IV–V. For modes with a tag π⁰, the two-photon invariant mass must lie within ±15 MeV/c² of the nominal π⁰ mass and a mass-constrained π⁰ fit is applied with χ² < 25, then all modes use a kinematic fit with nominal particle hypotheses constraining both D⁰ and D̄⁰ candidates to the nominal D⁰ mass and χ² < 30; when multiple candidates survive, the smallest total χ² is chosen. The D*⁰ daughter π⁰ or γ is not reconstructed, and the D⁰D̄⁰ recoil-mass-squared signal windows are [0.01, 0.04] (GeV/c²)² for the π⁰ mode and [−0.01, 0.01] (GeV/c²)² for the γ mode, with both D⁰ candidates required to have momentum below 0.65 GeV/c to reject direct D⁰D̄⁰ production, applied after the kinematic fit.
