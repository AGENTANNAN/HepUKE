Study e+e− → Ds*−Ds+ (Ds*−→γDs−) at the ψ(4260), with the tag Ds− reconstructed in 12 hadronic modes (KsK, KKπ, KsKπ0, KsKKππ, KsKπππ0, π3π0, ππ0η, KKππ, KsKππ0, π3π, π3π0η, πη) and charm −1, while the signal Ds+ → π+π0η′, η′→π+π−η, η→γγ, π0→γγ. Use the six 703 energy-point real datasets and matching inclusive MC from 4.178–4.226 GeV, and generate 500k exclusive MC events for e+e− → Ds*−Ds+ with Ds−→K+K−π−, Ds+→π+π0η′, η′→π+π−η, η→γγ, π0→γγ. Signal-side selection requires at least four photons with Eγ>25 MeV and photon opening angle >10°, and exactly two π+ and one π−; particle identification is implicit in the tag-mode hypotheses and signal charged/π multiplicities. Perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation, π0 and η nominal-mass constraints on the two γγ pairs, and the tag Ds mass constrained to nominal, with χ²<200. The c.m. energy is set to 4.226 GeV.
