On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV) and ψ(3686) (3.686 GeV) resonances, study the single-baryon-tagged channels J/ψ→Σ(1385)0Σ̄(1385)0, J/ψ→Ξ0Ξ̄0, ψ(3686)→Σ(1385)0Σ̄(1385)0, and ψ(3686)→Ξ0Ξ̄0, with Σ(1385)0/Ξ0→Λπ0, Λ→pπ−, π0→γγ; the anti-baryon is inferred from the recoil mass of the reconstructed π0Λ system. Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at each energy point, and generate 1,000,000-event exclusive MC samples for each of the four modes. The common selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<20 cm, and at least one positive and one negative track; photons with TDC 0–14 and E>25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), at least two; and PID by the probability method with a 0.001 threshold, identifying protons against kaons/pions and pions against kaons/protons, with at least one proton and one π−. Reconstruct π0 through a γγ kinematic fit constrained to the nominal π0 mass (1C, χ²<20, at least one π0), then perform a secondary-vertex fit for Λ→pπ− and a partial reconstruction of the Λπ0 system with a four-momentum constraint; for the J/ψ modes additionally require |M(pπ−)−M(Λ)|<5 MeV/c². The signal windows are |M(π0Λ)−M(Σ(1385)0)|<34 MeV/c² and anti-baryon recoil mass within ±80 MeV/c² for J/ψ→Σ(1385)0Σ̄(1385)0, |M(π0Λ)−M(Ξ0)|<10 MeV/c² and recoil within ±50 MeV/c² for J/ψ→Ξ0Ξ̄0, <35 MeV/c² for ψ(3686)→Σ(1385)0Σ̄(1385)0, and <11 MeV/c² for ψ(3686)→Ξ0Ξ̄0. For the ψ(3686) modes, veto J/ψ transitions by requiring |M_recoil(π+π−)−M(J/ψ)|>5 MeV/c² and |M_recoil(π0π0)−M(J/ψ)|>15 MeV/c².
