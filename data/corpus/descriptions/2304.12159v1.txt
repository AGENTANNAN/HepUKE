On ψ(4260), search for e+e− → D_s*+ D_s− with D_s*+ → e+ν_e (first search) using a double-tag technique: a hadronic D_s− tag plus a semileptonic signal. Use real data and inclusive MC at eight c.m. energies from 4.128 to 4.226 GeV (4.128, 4.157, 4.178, 4.189, 4.199, 4.209, 4.219, 4.226 GeV; 7.33 fb−1 total) and one 500k-event exclusive signal MC sample with ψ(4260) → D_s*+D_s−, D_s*+ → e+ν_e, D_s− → K+K−π−. Tag D_s− in hadronic modes taken from the nominal 16-mode ST list, with nine modes applied (K+K−π−, K+K−π−π0, π+π−π−, K_S0K−, K_S0K+π−π−, π−η, ρ−η, π−η′(→π+π−η), π−η′(→γρ0)) and remaining modes flagged unavailable; tag charm is −1. ST selection uses charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vxy|<1 cm, |Vz|<10 cm (20 cm for K_S0 daughters); K/π PID via L(K/π)>L(π/K) and L(K/π)>L(e); K_S0 from π+π− with |M−M_K_S0|<12 MeV/c2 and decay length >2σ; π0/η from γγ with mass windows 115–150 (π0) or 500–570 (η) MeV/c2 plus mass-constrained fit; ρ mass 570–970 MeV/c2; η′ windows M(ηπ+π−)=946–970 and M(γρ0)=940–976 MeV/c2; direct pions from D_s− with p>100 MeV/c; K_S0 veto in the π+π−π− and K−π+π− tag modes; keep the candidate closest to the PDG D_s− mass with M(D_s−) within 3σ, and define the M_rec signal region by S/√(S+B). DT signal requires exactly one extra charged track identified as e+ with L_e>0.8(L_e+L_π+L_K) and L_e>0.001, bremsstrahlung recovery adding EMC showers within 10° of the e+, a massless missing ν_e, and charge +1 opposite the D_s− tag. Perform a 4C kinematic fit with χ²<200 and extract the signal from the M_miss² distribution.
