At the J/ψ(3097), study J/ψ → γ η′ with η′ → η π+π− and η′ → η π0π0, where η → γγ and π0 → γγ. Use the 1.31×10^9-event J/ψ real data sample (708_3097) plus its inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two η′ decay modes. Charged mode: require exactly two oppositely charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge 0, identified with the probability method (prob>0.001, pion against kaon and proton) as one π+ and one π−; at least three photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) thresholds, a >10° photon–track opening angle and EMC timing 0–700 ns; then a 1C Kalman fit constraining the η→γγ pair to m(η) (χ²<25, at least one η), followed by a 6C fit (4C plus m(η) and m(η′)) to γ η π+π− with χ²<200, nominal. Neutral mode: no charged tracks and at least seven photons above the same energy thresholds and EMC timing window, a 1C Kalman fit of photon pairs to m(π0) (χ²<25, exactly two π0) and a 1C Kalman fit to m(η) (χ²<25, at least one η), then an 8C fit (4C plus the three photon-pair masses and m(η′)) to γ η π0π0 with χ²<200, nominal, with π0 miscombination suppressed by |cosθ_decay|<0.95. In both modes the most energetic photon is assigned as the radiative photon from J/ψ → γ η′, with the remaining clusters forming the η and π0 candidates.
