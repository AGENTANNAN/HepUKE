At √s = 4.009–4.600 GeV (15 points, 11 fb⁻¹), measure e+e− → Ξ−Ξ̄+ with the single-baryon-tag method, using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at each point and generating 100k-event exclusive signal MC per point for e+e− → Ξ−Ξ̄+, Ξ− → π−Λ, Λ → pπ− (with charge conjugates). Event selection: charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, with at least one positive and at least two negative tracks (no Vz/Vr cut is applied in this selection); particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut and mutual p/K/π separation, requiring at least one proton and at least two π−, after which identified K± and p/p̄ are removed and the remaining positive and negative charged tracks are assigned as π+ and π−. Reconstruct Λ → pπ− with a secondary-vertex fit (χ²<500 for 3 d.o.f., M(pπ−) within 5 MeV/c² of the nominal Λ mass 1.115683 GeV/c², decay length >0), then reconstruct Ξ− from π−Λ by mass-difference minimisation, choosing the combination closest to the nominal Ξ− mass 1.32171 GeV/c² and requiring M(π−Λ) within 10 MeV/c² of it and decay length >0. The analysis uses partial reconstruction, treating the Ξ̄+ as missing, and requires the Ξ− recoil mass to lie between 1.2 and 1.5 GeV/c². The charge-conjugate Ξ̄+ tag mode is part of the full analysis, though this algorithm implements the primary Ξ− tag mode; no global kinematic fit is applied.
