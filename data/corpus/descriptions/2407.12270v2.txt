At threshold, study single-tag Λc+ in e+e− → Λc+ Λc− using 11 energy points from 4.600 to 4.840 GeV (6.1 fb−1) of real data and inclusive MC; generate 1M-event exclusive MC for each of the two Λc+ → Λ π+ η decay modes, with Λ → p π− and η → γγ (Mode I) or η → π+ π− π0, π0 → γγ (Mode II). Event selection Mode I: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least 2 positive and 1 negative; photons with EMC timing 0–14, angle to track >10°, energy >25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), at least 2; PID by the probability method (prob>0.001) for p vs K/π and π vs K, requiring ≥1 proton and ≥1 π+; remove protons from charged-pion candidates. Build Λ from p π− by minimizing mass difference, then reconstruct η from γγ constrained to the η mass (χ²<20, ≥1 η), and perform a 4C kinematic fit on Λ π+ η (the fit list includes a duplicated π+ as written), χ²<200. Mode II uses the same track and photon cuts but requires ≥2 positive and ≥2 negative tracks, adds a ≥1 π− requirement, reconstructs π0 from γγ constrained to the π0 mass (χ²<200, ≥1 π0), and performs a 4C fit on Λ π+ π+ π− π0 (i.e. Λ π+ η, η→π+π−π0), χ²<200. For both modes, keep the candidate with minimum |ΔE| per event within −0.1<ΔE<0.1 GeV, and apply the chain to all energy points, inclusive MC, and the corresponding exclusive MC.
