On ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, study the semileptonic double-tag decay D+ → K_S0 π0 l+ ν (l = e, μ), with the tag D- reconstructed in six hadronic modes: K+π-π-, K_S0 π-, K+π-π-π0, K_S0 π-π0, K_S0 π+π-π-, and K+K-π-. Use the 20.3 fb⁻¹ real data sample (712_3773) and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for D+ → K_S0 π0 μ+ ν_μ with ψ(3770) → D+D-, D- → K+π-π-, K_S0 → π+π-, and π0 → γγ. Event selection: require at least one electron, at least two π- tracks, exactly two photons, and a missing neutrino; the muon channel (separate specification) additionally requires L(μ) > L(e), L(μ) > L(K), L(μ) > L(π), EMC energy < 0.3 GeV, and maximum extra photon energy < 0.2 GeV. Perform a four-momentum constraint kinematic fit with χ² < 200. Both e+ and mu+ channels are analysed separately, with this specification covering the e+ channel.
