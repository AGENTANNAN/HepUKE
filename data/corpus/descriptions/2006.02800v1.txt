At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), reconstruct the untagged decay D0 → K_S^0 K+ K− from e+e− → D0 D0bar, using the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC and generating 200k exclusive MC events for ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar with D0 → K_S^0 K+ K−, K_S^0 → π+π−, and D0bar → K+π−. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least two positive and two negative tracks. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating kaons from pions/protons and pions from kaons/protons, and requires at least one K+, one K−, one π+, and one π−, with kaon candidates favored over pions. Form K_S^0 candidates from π+π− via a secondary-vertex fit that minimizes the mass difference and removes used tracks, with K_S^0 mass window 0.487–0.511 GeV/c^2, vertex χ²<100, and flight-distance significance >2 for the untagged sample. Perform a 1C kinematic fit to K_S^0 K+ K− constraining the invariant mass to the nominal D0 mass, with χ²<20, and select the best D0 candidate by the smallest |E_D0 − E_beam|. The implemented track selection applies uniformly to all charged tracks—including K_S^0 daughters—so the noted relaxation (Vz<20 cm, no Vr, no PID for K_S^0 pions) is not separately encoded. This single untagged selection chain is run on real data, inclusive MC, and the exclusive signal MC; no tagged or Dalitz selection is implemented here.
