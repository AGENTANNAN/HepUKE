On 20 scan energy points from 2.05 to 3.08 GeV, measure the Born cross section for e+e− → φ η′, with φ → K+K− and η′ → π+π−γ. Use the real data and inclusive MC at those points, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC per point from a ConExc φ η′ generator. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks (four total); photons with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy threshold, EMC TDC within 0–14, at least 10° from any track, and at least one photon; PID by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating π from K/p and K from π/p, requiring 1 π+, 1 π−, 1 K+, and 1 K−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to K+K−π+π−γ, nominal, with χ² < 200. Only this fully reconstructed 4-track topology is encoded; the paper's 3-track partial-reconstruction (1C with missing kaon), the 70 MeV ISR photon suppression, the M(π+π−γ) signal fit, and the Born cross-section and line-shape extraction are handled elsewhere.
