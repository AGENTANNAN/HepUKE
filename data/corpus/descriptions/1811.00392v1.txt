At √s = 4.178 GeV (3.19 fb⁻¹), study ψ(4260) → D_s*+ D_s− with D_s*+ → D_s+ γ, tagging the D_s− and fully reconstructing the D_s+ signal side. Use the 4.178 GeV real data and the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate two 2,000,000-event exclusive MC samples, one for D_s+ → ω π+ and one for D_s+ → ω K+, each with ω → π+π−π0 (Dalitz) and π0 → γγ. Tag the D_s− through the K_S K− and K+K−π− modes. For the D_s+ → ω π+ mode require two photons with a minimum photon angle of 10° and the charged configuration 2 π+ plus 1 π− (net charge +1); for the D_s+ → ω K+ mode require the same two photons and 1 K+, 1 π+, 1 π− (net charge +1). Both modes are passed through a 4-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ² < 200. In the ω K+ mode, veto K_S0 by rejecting events with |M(π+π−) − m_K_S0| < 0.03 GeV/c² and L/σ_L > 2 to suppress the D_s+ → K_S0 K+ π0 background, while the ω and π0 mass constraints are applied at the ROOT level via a 2D fit to M(π+π−π0) and M(D_s).
