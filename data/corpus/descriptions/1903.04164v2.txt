At √s = 4.178 GeV (3.19 fb⁻¹) we study D_s⁺ → K_S⁰ K⁺ and D_s⁺ → K_L⁰ K⁺, using the real data at 4.178 GeV plus inclusive MC, and generating exclusive MC with the conexc generator through D_s*±D_s∓ production for each mode (D_s⁺ → K_S⁰K⁺ with K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻, and D_s⁺ → K_L⁰K⁺). Both analyses use the same tag side: 13 single-tag D_s⁻ hadronic modes (K⁺K⁻π⁻, K⁺K⁻π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰K⁻/(K⁺)π, π⁻η/η′, K_S⁰K⁻π⁰, K_S⁰K_S⁰π⁻, etc.), with the D_s⁻ → K_S⁰K⁻ mode explicitly excluded to avoid double counting against the K_S⁰K⁺ measurement. For D_s⁺ → K_S⁰K⁺ (double-tag), the signal side requires a single K⁺ with charge +1, while the K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻ is reconstructed from the unused tracks via a secondary-vertex fit within a mass window of 0.487–0.511 GeV/c², and events with extra charged tracks satisfying |cosθ|<0.93 and |Vz|<20 cm are vetoed; no constrained kinematic fit is applied, and the yield is extracted from a 2D unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to M(K_S⁰K⁺) versus M_tag. For D_s⁺ → K_L⁰K⁺ (single-tag plus missing), the signal side requires one K⁺ of charge +1 with the K_L⁰ treated as the missing particle, and a 4C kinematic fit (constraining four-momentum conservation, plus the masses of the ST D_s⁻, the signal D_s⁺ and the D_s* intermediate state) is performed with χ²<40. The photon from D_s* → γ D_s is chosen by testing both tag- and signal-side hypotheses and keeping the smaller χ², with extra photons having E>250 MeV and an angle >15° to the missing particle rejected; the K_L⁰ signal is extracted from the missing-mass-squared MM² = (P_{e⁺e⁻} − P_{D_s⁻} − P_γ − P_{K⁺})², accounting for peaking backgrounds from D_s⁺ → K_S⁰K⁺ and D_s⁺ → ηK⁺.
