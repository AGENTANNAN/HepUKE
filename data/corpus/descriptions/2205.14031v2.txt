At ψ(3770) with √s = 3.773 GeV, measure absolute hadronic D branching fractions through double-tag D0D0bar and D+D− events, using the 2.93 fb−1 real data sample and matching inclusive MC; the configuration defines no exclusive-MC generation. In the D0 chain, tag D0bar in K+π−, K+π−π0, or K+π−π−π+, and reconstruct the signal D0 in KS0π0π0π0, K−π+π0π0π0, or KS0π+π−π0π0, with up to six photons for up to three π0, one K− and one π+ as the representative charged requirement, net charge 0, and a 10° minimum photon–track angle. In the D+ chain, tag D− in K+π−π−, KS0π−, K+π−π−π0, KS0π−π0, KS0π+π−π−, or K+K−π−, and reconstruct the signal D+ in KS0π+π0π0, KS0π+π+π−π0, KS0π+π0π0π0, or K−π+π+π0π0, with up to six photons, one K−, two π+, one π− as the representative charged requirement, net charge +1, and the same 10° photon angle. Both chains perform a kinematic fit using four-momentum conservation and constraining both tag and signal D masses to the nominal D0 or D+ mass, with χ² < 200 and a beam energy of 1.8865 GeV. KS0 candidates are formed by secondary-vertex fit with mass window [0.486, 0.510] GeV/c² and π0 candidates by γγ with [0.115, 0.150] GeV/c². Additional treatment includes KS0 veto windows [0.468, 0.528] GeV/c² for π+π− pairs, mode-dependent ΔE windows at the ROOT stage (e.g., (−85,+65) MeV for D0 π0 modes, (−35,+35) MeV otherwise; (−55,+40) MeV for D+ π0 modes, (−25,+25) MeV otherwise), best-candidate selection by minimum |ΔE|, peaking-background estimates for KS0KS0 modes, and quantum-correlation corrections for D0 → KS0π0π0π0 and D0 → KS0π+π−π0π0. The configuration specifies no further charged-track acceptance, vertex, or PID cuts; yields are extracted from 2D fits to MBC(tag) versus MBC(sig).
