Study e+e− → η_c π+π− π0, with η_c → K_S0 K+π−, K_S0 → π+π−, and π0 → γγ, at the six c.m. energies 4.178, 4.226, 4.258, 4.358, 4.416, and 4.600 GeV using the corresponding real data, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for this mode; also generate 100k-event exclusive MC for the upper-limit channel e+e− → η_c π+π− with the same η_c and K_S0 decays, noting that the π0 mode is fitted simultaneously in 16 hadronic η_c channels and includes D-meson/K*/ω/η vetoes optimized per channel plus ISR corrections. Event selection uses charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and one negative tracks, and photons with TDC 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV or endcap >50 MeV, more than 10° from any track, and at least two photons. Particle ID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, K/π/p separation, requiring exactly one K+ and at least one π−, with overlapping K+ candidates removed. Reconstruct K_S0 from π+π− by a secondary-vertex fit minimizing the mass difference and removing used tracks, then reconstruct π0 from two photons with a Kalman fit constraining the π0 nominal mass, χ²<25, requiring at least one π0. Perform a nominal four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit to K_S0 K+π−π+π−π0 with χ²<200; for η_c π+π− use the same charged, PID, and K_S0 selection and a nominal 4C fit to K_S0 K+π−π+π− with χ²<200. For the Z_c search at 4.23 GeV, additionally require 2.880 < M_ηc < 3.080 GeV/c² and scan Z_c masses 3625–3805 MeV/c² and widths 8–38 MeV.
