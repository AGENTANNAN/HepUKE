On the ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, measure the coherence factors and average strong-phase differences for quantum-correlated D0D0bar decays in two modes: D0 → K−π+π+π− and D0 → K−π+π0. Use 2.93 fb−1 of real data at 3.773 GeV and the corresponding inclusive MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each signal mode, with ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar and the appropriate D0 and anti-D0 decays (π0 → γγ for the second mode). For Mode I (K−π+π+π−), select tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 100 cm, Vr < 10 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, net charge zero; photons must satisfy TDC in [0,14], E > 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), and be >20° from any track; PID by the probability method (cut 0.001) requires ≥1 K−, ≥1 K+, ≥2 π+, ≥2 π− with K/π separation, followed by a kinematic fit to K−π+π+π− constraining the invariant mass to the nominal D0 mass with χ² < 200. For Mode II (K−π+π0), the same track and photon criteria apply but with at least one positive and one negative track and at least two photons; PID requires ≥1 K−, ≥1 K+, ≥1 π+, ≥1 π−; a Kalman fit to two photons constrains their invariant mass to the π0 mass (χ² < 25, ≥1 π0), then a kinematic fit to K−π+π0 constrains to the nominal D0 mass (χ² < 200). In Mode I, apply a Ks veto using flight significance > 2 on π+π− pairs; both modes use a ΔE cut within ±3σ of the peak and choose the best candidate closest to the nominal D0 mass, with the K3π phase space divided into 4 bins.
