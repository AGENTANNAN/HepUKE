On the five BESIII energy points 4.628, 4.641, 4.661, 4.681 and 4.698 GeV, study e+e− → K+ Ds− anti-D*0 with anti-D*0 → anti-D0 π0, anti-D0 → K+ π−, π0 → γγ, using the real data and the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC at each energy point for the two Ds− decay modes Ds− → K+K−π− (Mode I) and Ds− → K_S0 K− (Mode II). Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with Mode I requiring at least two positive and exactly two negative tracks and Mode II exactly two positive and two negative tracks. Kaons are identified by the probability method with a 0.001 cut against pion and proton hypotheses: Mode I requires at least two K+ and exactly one K−, Mode II exactly one K+ and one K−; the remaining tracks are assigned as pions, and in Mode II the two pions are combined into a K_S0 through a secondary-vertex fit with mass window 0.485–0.511 GeV/c2. Both modes use partial reconstruction, tagging the bachelor K+ and the Ds− (choosing the combination closest to the nominal 1.968 GeV/c2 Ds− mass) while the anti-D*0 is left missing, and requiring the recoil mass RM(K+Ds−) to fall in 1.990–2.027 GeV/c2. Mode I additionally keeps candidates in the Ds− mass window 1.955–1.980 GeV/c2 and restricts them to the Dalitz regions M(K+K−)<1.05 GeV/c2 (φ) or 0.850<M(K+π−)<0.930 GeV/c2 (K*(892)), while Mode II applies a Ds− mass window of 1.955–1.985 GeV/c2.
