On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → π0 h_c with h_c → γ η′/η and search h_c → γ π0, using the 27.12×10⁸ ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC, and generating 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the five modes (η′→π+π−η with η→γγ; η′→γπ+π−; η→γγ; η→π+π−π0; and π0→γγ). Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with one positive and one negative track and net charge zero. Photons must satisfy EMC timing 0–14, be separated from tracks by more than 10°, and pass 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) thresholds, requiring at least five photons (at least four in the η′→γπ+π− mode); in the fully neutral final states an additional EMC timing requirement |T−T_max|≤500 ns is applied. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying pions against kaons and protons, and requiring one π+ and one π−. Intermediate Kalman fits constrain γγ pairs to the nominal π0 or η mass with χ²<25, tagging at least one π0 (two for the η→π+π−π0 and h_c→γπ0 modes) or at least one η. The final kinematic fits are nominal and constrain four-momentum: 6C (plus π0 and η masses) with χ²<70 for η′→π+π−η(→γγ), 5C (plus π0 mass) with χ²<40 for η′→γπ+π− and η→γγ, a 6C fit with χ²<40 for η→π+π−π0 including a competing γπ+π−π0π0 hypothesis used as a veto, and a 6C fit with χ²<20 for the h_c→γπ0 search.
