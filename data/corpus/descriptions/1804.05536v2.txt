On the J/ψ (√s = 3.097 GeV, 1.31×10⁹ events), study J/ψ → η' K K̄ π in the search for the h1(1380), using two decay modes: Mode I, J/ψ → η' K⁺K⁻π⁰ with η' → π⁺π⁻η, η → γγ, π⁰ → γγ (h1 → K*(892)⁺K⁻ + c.c.), and Mode II, J/ψ → η' K_S⁰ K^±π^∓ with η' → π⁺π⁻η, η → γγ, K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻ (h1 → K*(892)K̄ + c.c.). Use the 3.097 GeV real data and its inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm; Mode I requires exactly four tracks with ≥2 positive and ≥2 negative and net charge 0, while Mode II requires exactly six tracks with ≥3 positive and ≥3 negative and net charge 0. Photons must have E>25 MeV (barrel) or >50 MeV (endcap), EMC TDC within [0,14]×50 ns, and angle to any track >10°, with at least four photons in Mode I and at least two in Mode II. PID uses the probability method (prob>0.001) with π/K separation, requiring at least one K⁺, one K⁻, one π⁺ and one π⁻. Photon pairs are kinematically fitted with χ²<25 to the π⁰ mass (Mode I) and to the η mass, and Mode II additionally reconstructs K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻ via a secondary-vertex fit (χ²<100 and |M(π⁺π⁻)−m_{K_S⁰}|<0.01 GeV); a final 4C kinematic fit to π⁺π⁻K⁺K⁻π⁰η (Mode I) or π⁺π⁻K⁺K⁻K_S⁰η (Mode II) with χ²<100 is then applied. The η' is selected by |M(π⁺π⁻η) − m_{η'}| < 0.03 GeV, with the π⁰ and η windows set at 0.02 GeV and 0.03 GeV respectively, and Vr cut 10 mm, Vz cut 100mm. Both modes share the same photon and PID criteria.
