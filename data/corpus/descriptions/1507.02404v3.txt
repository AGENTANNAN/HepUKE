On the 4.23 GeV (1092 pb⁻¹) and 4.26 GeV (826 pb⁻¹) data sets, together with the corresponding inclusive MC, study e+e− → (D*D̄*)⁰π⁰ and the neutral charmoniumlike state Z_c(4025)⁰ decaying to (D*D̄*)⁰, using a partial reconstruction in which one D (from D* → Dγ) and the primary bachelor π⁰ are fully reconstructed while the recoiling D̄* is inferred from the missing four-momentum, the signal being read from the RM(Dπ⁰) spectrum. Four D decay modes are treated, each with its own 200k-event exclusive signal MC sample generated at both energies: D⁰ → K⁻π⁺, D⁰ → K⁻π⁺π⁰, D⁰ → K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻, and D⁺ → K⁻π⁺π⁺ (charged mode, D*⁺ → D⁺γ). Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge zero, and (2+,2−), (2+,2−), (4+,4−) or (3+,3−) multiplicities for the four modes respectively; photons must satisfy E>25 MeV (barrel) or >50 MeV (endcap), lie at least 10° from any charged track, and fall inside the EMC timing window, with at least 2 photons required (≥4 in the K⁻π⁺π⁰ mode). Kaon/pion separation uses the probability method (probability cut 0.001) with dE/dx and TOF, requiring 1 K⁺, 1 K⁻ and 1π⁺1π⁻, 1π⁺1π⁻, 3π⁺3π⁻ or 2π⁺2π⁻ depending on the mode. π⁰ candidates are built from photon pairs with two-photon mass in 0.120–0.145 GeV constrained to the nominal π⁰ mass (χ²<25), requiring at least one π⁰ (at least three for K⁻π⁺π⁰), while the bachelor π⁰ is formed from the leftover photons, whose tracks must form a common D vertex (χ²<100) and whose D/D̄ pair shares no final-state particles and is chosen by minimum summed χ². The tagged D and bachelor π⁰ are selected by best mass consistency to the nominal D mass (1.86484 GeV for D⁰, 1.86962 GeV for D⁺) with mass constraints χ²(D)<15 (<20 when a π⁰ is present) and χ²(π⁰)<20, the recoil mass is required to lie in 2.10–2.22 GeV, and Dπ⁰ invariant mass above 2.02 GeV rejects D* → Dπ⁰ feed-down.
