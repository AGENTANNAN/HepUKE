Study e+e− → anti-Λc− Λc(2625)+ with Λc(2625)+ → Λc+ π+π− at √s = 4.918 and 4.950 GeV in a partial-reconstruction tag analysis, where the anti-Λc− is missing and the Λc+ is reconstructed through the pK−π+, pK_S0, and Λπ+ hadronic tag modes. Use the two real-data sets (219.78 and 148.70 pb−1) with their inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for the signal decay card and another 500k for its charge conjugate, with Λc(2625)+ → Λc+ π+π− in phase space and Λc+ → p+ K− π+ as the representative tag. The signal side requires zero photons, exactly one π+ and one π−, net charge 0, and the missing anti-Λc− constrained to the nominal mass 2.28646 GeV/c2; particle identities follow from the tag-mode definitions and the π± requirement, with no additional PID method, |cosθ|, Vz, Vr, or photon-energy cuts encoded. A 4C kinematic fit constrains the total four-momentum to the centre-of-mass energy with χ² < 200. The c.m. energy constant is set to 4.918 GeV, the two energy points are analysed separately and combined, and the analysis notes specify a ΔM = M(Λc+π+π−) − M(Λc+) peak near 0.341 GeV, an M_recoil window around the anti-Λc− mass, and a beam-constrained-mass window of 5 MeV/c2 for the pK−π+ tag mode for the offline yield extraction.
