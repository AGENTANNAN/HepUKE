Commission a measurement of e+e− → n n̄ at 18 c.m. energies from 2.0 to 3.08 GeV (total 647.9 pb−1 R-scan). Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at each point; for signal, generate 100k exclusive MC events per energy with a ConExc n n̄ (mode 79) decay card and a straight-line cross-section assumption. Select neutral-only events: zero charged tracks (no positive, no negative, total charge 0), and at least two photons with EMC TDC 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV, endcap >50 MeV. No PID or kinematic fitting is performed; detector-specific neutron/antineutron identification (TOF/EMC response, BDT, MUC cosmic veto, three statistically independent event categories A/B/C) is captured as notes for downstream systematic evaluation.
