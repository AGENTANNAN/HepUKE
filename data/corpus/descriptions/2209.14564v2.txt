On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → Σ− Σ+bar with Σ− → n π− and Σ+bar → nbar π+, reconstructing the anti-neutron from EMC showers and treating the neutron as missing, so the visible final state is π+π− plus an EMC anti-neutron. Use the 3.686 GeV real data (709_3686, 448.1M ψ(3686) events) and its inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive signal MC events for ψ(3686) → Σ− Σ+bar with the same two-body Σ decays. Select events with exactly two charged tracks, zero net charge, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<30 cm, and Vr<10 cm. Identify both tracks as pions by the probability method with prob>0.001, requiring L(π)>L(K) and L(π)>L(p), and demand one π+ and one π−. Anti-neutron candidates are EMC showers with E>600 MeV, lateral moment >20, angle to the nearest charged track >10°, and EMC time in [0,700] ns, retaining the most energetic candidate. Apply a 4C kinematic fit to π+π− with four-momentum conservation and χ²<200 in the BOSS selection, while the final fit uses the neutron three-momentum as free parameters, the anti-neutron angles, and the M(nbar π+)=M(Σ+) constraint with χ²<50. Veto ψ(3686) → π+π− J/ψ, J/ψ → n nbar by requiring the π+π− recoil mass <2.9 GeV/c², and account for peaking γχ_cJ/γη_c backgrounds and non-ψ(3686) background from 3.65 GeV off-resonance data. Signal is extracted from M(n π−) in [1.15,1.25] GeV/c², and α_{Σ−} is measured from the efficiency-corrected cosθ_{Σ−} distribution.
