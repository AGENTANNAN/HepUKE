Measure azimuthal asymmetries in inclusive charged-dipion production, e+e− → ππX, at √s = 3.65 GeV for the Collins fragmentation function, using the 62 pb−1 continuum data and the matching inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive continuum MC for e+e− → qq̄ (u,d,s) through the ConExc/ψ(4260) convention. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and at least three tracks in total; EMC photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold and cluster timing within 0–14 TDC counts (delay ≤700 ns). PID by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, high-momentum tracks (p>1.0) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6), π–K–p separation requiring at least one π+ and one π−, and an electron veto (L(e)>0.001 and L(e)/(L(e)+L(π)+L(K))>0.8). Require total visible energy (all charged tracks plus photons) above 1.5 GeV, both pion fractional energies zi = 2Eπ/√s in [0.2, 0.9], and an opening angle between the two pion candidates above 120°. If more than two pions survive, all combinations are formed, with the two pions of each pair randomly labelled h1 and h2 and each pion allowed to enter multiple pairs.
