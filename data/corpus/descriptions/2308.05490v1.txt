On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → φ η with η → γγ, using the reference mode φ → K+K− and searching for the lepton-number-violating decay φ → π+π+e−e−. Use the 708_3097 J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, and generate exclusive MC of 1,000,000 events for φ → K+K− and 500,000 for φ → π+π+e−e−. Both modes require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and photons with TDC 0–700, opening angle >20° from the nearest track, 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, at least two. The reference selection requires exactly two tracks with net charge 0, PID by the probability method (CL>0.001, kaon against pion) giving one K+ and one K−, and a 4C fit to K+K−γγ with χ²<200. The signal selection requires exactly four tracks with net charge 0, PID (electron against kaon/pion, pion against kaon; high-momentum tracks p>1.0 treated as leptons, electron if EMC energy >0.6) giving at least two π+, one e+ and one e−, and a nominal 4C fit to π+π+e−e−γγ with χ²<30. To reject mis-identification, six competing 4C hypotheses (KKππγγ, ππππγγ, KKe+e−γγ, KKeπγγ, KKπe+e−γγ, KKπeπγγ, each with χ²<200) must all give a worse χ² than the signal hypothesis, and an opening angle θ_πe>8° suppresses γ conversions; the signal window is 0.99 < M(π+π+e−e−) < 1.04 GeV and 0.52 < M(γγ) < 0.57 GeV.
