Measure the Born cross section of e+e− → p pbar in a continuum scan at c.m. energies from 2232.4 to 3671.0 MeV, using the R-scan real data and matching inclusive MC at 2232, 2400, 2800, 3050/3060, 3080, and 3500–3671 MeV. Generate per energy point 200k-event signal MC with ConExc (ISR up to second order plus the measured σ0(m), mode 0 p pbar, with the per-point sqrt(s) injected) and 50k-event background MC for e+e− → π+π−, K+K−, p pbar π0, p pbar π0π0, and Λ Λbar (Λ→pπ−, Λbar→anti-p π+). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0. PID by the probability method combining dE/dx and TOF, with probability threshold 0.001, identifying one proton against kaon and pion hypotheses and one anti-proton, and requiring E/p<0.5 for both proton candidates to suppress Bhabha. Add a |T1−T2|<4 ns TOF veto against cosmics, require cosθ_p<0.8 for sqrt(s)>2400 MeV, and require the p pbar opening angle >178° for sqrt(s)≤2400 MeV or >179° for sqrt(s)>2400 MeV. The nominal endpoint is a 4C kinematic fit on the p pbar pair with χ²<200, but the published yield is obtained by event counting in the |p_meas−p_exp|<5σ_p window without a kinematic fit.
