Using the Λc+Λc− threshold scan real data at seven points from 4.600 to 4.700 GeV, with matching inclusive MC and 200k-event exclusive MC for each mode, we study the single-tag channels Λc+ → p η' (η' → π+π−γ) as signal and Λc+ → p ω (ω → π+π−π0) for normalization. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and one negative track, and at least three tracks in total; photons are required to have TDC 0–14, energy above 25 MeV in the barrel or 50 MeV in the endcap, and an angle >10° from any charged track, with at least one photon in the signal and at least two in the normalization. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying protons against kaons and pions and pions against kaons. In the signal channel, η' is formed from π+π−γ by a mass-constrained fit with χ²<200, requiring at least one η' and M(π+π−γ) in [0.90,1.00] GeV/c², followed by a nominal Λc+ mass constraint on p η' with χ²<200; we veto Λ (M(pπ−) outside [1.10,1.15] GeV/c²) and K_S0 (M(π+π−) outside [0.48,0.51] GeV/c²), select the Λc+ candidate via M_BC and the smallest |ΔE| with −65 < ΔE < 60 MeV, and apply a Particle-Transformer DNN ensemble (10 models, three-class output) with S = score_sig·(1−score_hadronic) > 0.98. In the normalization channel, π0 is reconstructed from γγ with a mass-constrained fit (χ²<200), requiring at least one π0 and M(γγ) in [0.115,0.150] GeV/c², then a nominal fit constrains M(π+π−π0) to the ω mass and M(pπ+π−π0) to the Λc+ mass with χ²<200; we apply the same Λ and K_S0 vetoes plus a Σ+ veto (M(pπ0) outside [1.17,1.20] GeV/c²), require M(π+π−π0) in [0.73,0.83] GeV/c², retain the smallest-|ΔE| candidate with −40 < ΔE < 30 MeV, and use the same DNN discriminator S > 0.98.
