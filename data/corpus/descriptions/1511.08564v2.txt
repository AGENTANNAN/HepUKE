At five centre-of-mass energies (4.416, 4.467, 4.527, 4.574 and 4.599 GeV), study e+e− → ωχ_c1,2 with χ_c1,2 → γJ/ψ, J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−, ω → π+π−π0, and e+e− → ωχ_c0 with χ_c0 → π+π− or K+K−, ω → π+π−π0. Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at each energy point, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the five signal modes (χ_c1 with J/ψ → e+e−, χ_c1 with J/ψ → μ+μ−, χ_c2 with J/ψ → e+e−, χ_c0 → π+π−, χ_c0 → K+K−) at each energy point. For the γπ+π−π0 l+l− final state require exactly four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, two positive and two negative tracks with zero net charge; photons must be at least 10° from any track, exceed 25 MeV (barrel) or 50 MeV (endcap), and number at least three (two for the χ_c0 modes). Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut and π/K separation: tracks above 1.0 GeV/c are treated as leptons, electrons if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muons, and the χ_c1,2 mode requires 1 l+, 1 l−, 1 π+ and 1 π−; the χ_c0 → π+π− mode requires 2π+2π−, and χ_c0 → K+K− requires 1 K+, 1 K−, 1 π+ and 1 π−. Perform a 5C kinematic fit constraining the total four-momentum to the initial beams and the photon-pair mass to the nominal π0 mass, with the photon pair chosen by smallest χ²; the BOSS χ² cut is 200, while the published analysis applies χ²_5C <60, and the signal windows (0.75–0.81 GeV/c² for ω, 3.08–3.12 GeV/c² for J/ψ, 3.49–3.53 GeV/c² for χ_c1, 3.54–3.58 GeV/c² for χ_c2, and the nominal χ_c0 window) are applied later.
