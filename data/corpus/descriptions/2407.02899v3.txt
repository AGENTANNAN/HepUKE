At the J/ψ(3097), study J/ψ → p p̄ η with two η decay modes: η → γγ and η → π+π−π0 (π0 → γγ). Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 1,000,000-event exclusive MC for each of the two η decay modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; for η→γγ require at least one positive and one negative track and, after PID, exactly one proton and one anti-proton; for η→π+π−π0 require at least two positive and two negative tracks and, after PID, one proton, one anti-proton, at least one π+, and at least one π−. Photons must have 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, TDC 0–700 ns, be more than 20° from the nearest charged track, and number at least two. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut: in the γγ channel protons are identified against kaons; in the π+π−π0 channel protons are identified against pions and pions against protons, with no kaon requirement. Reconstruct η → γγ from a γγ pair with invariant mass 0.200–0.900 GeV/c² and χ²<200, or π0 → γγ constrained to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<25. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to p p̄ η (η mass unconstrained) in the γγ channel and to p p̄ π+π−π0 (π0 mass constrained to nominal, η mass unconstrained) in the π+π−π0 channel, both with χ²<200 and flagged nominal, choosing the candidate with the smallest χ² when multiple candidates survive.
