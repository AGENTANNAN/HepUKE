At √s = 4.260 GeV, study e+e− → D*+ D*0bar π− (charge conjugate implied) with D*+ → D+π0, D+ → K−π+π+, D*0bar → D0barπ0, D0bar → K+π−, using a partial-reconstruction technique in which the bachelor π− and D+ are tagged and the D*0bar is inferred from the D+π− recoil. Use the 827 pb−1 real data at 4.260 GeV and the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive PHSP MC for D*+ D*0bar π−, with notes also treating a resonant Zc+(4025) cascade and D** backgrounds separately. Require at least four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and at least two positive and two negative tracks; identify particles by the probability method with prob>0.001 and K/π separation, requiring ≥1 K−, ≥2 π+, and ≥1 π−. Photons are selected with TDC 0–14, E>25 MeV in the barrel or >50 MeV in the endcap, at least 10° from any track, and at least two photons. Reconstruct D+ → K−π+π+ via a secondary vertex fit minimizing the vertex χ² and accepting 1.854 < M(K−π+π+) < 1.884 GeV/c², and reconstruct a soft π0 → γγ with a mass-constrained fit to the nominal π0 mass, χ²<25 (mass window 0.120–0.145 GeV/c²), requiring at least one π0 that satisfies either 2.008 < M(D+π0)−M(D+)+m(D+)−M(π0)+m(π0) < 2.013 GeV/c² or P*(π0) in (0.03,0.05) GeV in the D+π− recoil system. Finally, for the partial reconstruction take the D+ candidate closest to 1.86962 GeV/c² and require the D+π− recoil mass in 2.135–2.175 GeV/c², with additional suppression of two-body backgrounds by RM(D+)+M(D+)−m(D+)>2.3 GeV/c² and exclusion of RM(π−)>4.1 GeV/c²; wrong-sign events scaled by 1.9 model the combinatorial background.
