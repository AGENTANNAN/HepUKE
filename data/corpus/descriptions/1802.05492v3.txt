On ψ(3770) data at 3.773 GeV, study the semileptonic decays D0 → π⁻ μ⁺ ν_μ and D+ → π⁰ μ⁺ ν_μ using tag-side hadronic reconstruction of the accompanying D meson. Use the real ψ(3770) dataset and inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each channel: ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar with D0 → π⁻ μ⁺ ν_μ and D0bar → K+ π-, and ψ(3770) → D+ D- with D+ → π⁰ μ⁺ ν_μ, D- → K+ π- π-, and π⁰ → γγ. For the D0 mode, tag the anti-D0 via Kπ, Kππ⁰, or Kπππ modes, requiring ΔE ∈ (−0.055, 0.040) GeV and mBC ∈ (1.859, 1.873) GeV/c²; the signal side requires exactly one π⁻ and one μ⁺, net charge 0, and a missing neutrino. For the D+ mode, tag the D- via Kππ, K_Sπ, Kπππ⁰, K_Sππ⁰, K_Sπππ, or KKπ modes, with ΔE ∈ (−0.055, 0.040) GeV and mBC ∈ (1.863, 1.877) GeV/c²; the signal side requires two photons forming a π⁰ (γγ), one μ⁺, net charge +1, and a missing neutrino. Apply background vetoes on the signal side: M(π⁻μ⁺) < 1.7 GeV/c² and extra photon energy < 0.07 GeV for D0, with a K_S⁰ veto by requiring M(π⁻μ⁺) outside (0.46, 0.50) GeV/c²; for D+, M(π⁰μ⁺) < 1.7 GeV/c² and extra photon energy < 0.07 GeV, with a K_S⁰ veto via the recoil mass M(recoil D⁻ μ⁺) outside (0.45, 0.55) GeV/c². Muon identification uses fixed v1 thresholds (detailed MUC hit-depth/polar-angle criteria are not expressed). Perform a kinematic fit with a 4-momentum constraint and χ² < 200 for both modes, and for D+ additionally constrain the γγ invariant mass to the π⁰ nominal mass.
