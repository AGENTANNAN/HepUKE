At the Ds threshold (√s = 4.178 GeV), perform a double-tag measurement of Ds+ → π+ π0 π0 η (with π0 → γγ and η → γγ) using 7.33 fb−1 of real data and inclusive MC, plus 500k exclusive MC events generated for Ds*+ → γ Ds+, Ds+ → π+ π0 π0 η in phase space. Tag the recoiling Ds− through nine hadronic decay modes: KsK, KKπ, KsKπ0, KKππ0, πππ, πη, ππ0η, πη′, and Kππ. On the signal side, require exactly one charged π+ track (net charge +1) and seven photons (four from the two π0's, two from the η, and one transition photon), with a minimum photon angle of 10°; the transition photon must have laboratory energy below 0.18 GeV. Further require the recoil mass against the Ds*+ (signal Ds+ plus transition photon) to be within 1.93–1.98 GeV/c2 and veto photon pairs whose invariant mass lies in 0.10–0.14 GeV/c2 to suppress fake π0's. Perform a 9C kinematic fit imposing four-momentum conservation, the nominal π0 and η masses on the photon pairs, the tag Ds− mass, and the Ds*+ mass from either the Ds+γ or Ds−γ combination, keeping the candidate with the smallest χ² and accepting only χ² < 200.
