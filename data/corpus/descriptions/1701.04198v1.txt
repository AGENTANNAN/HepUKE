Measure e+e− → p pbar π0 Born cross sections at 13 center-of-mass energies from 4.008 to 4.600 GeV using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC for nine of the scan points. Generate 200k-event exclusive signal MC at every energy point with a ConExc generator (mode 50, p pbar π0) and phase-space decays vhdr→p+ pbar− π0, π0→γγ, plus 500k-event inclusive Y(4260) background MC at 4.26 GeV from generic ψ(4260) decays. Select events with exactly one positive and one negative charged track, net charge zero, each with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm; require at least two photons with EMC timing 0–14, energy >25 MeV in the barrel and >50 MeV in the endcap. Identify one track as proton and the other as antiproton by the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating p from K/π, and require one p+ and one p−. Isolated photons must be >10° from the proton and >30° from the antiproton, still with at least two photons. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to p pbar γγ with the total four-momentum constrained to the e+e− CMS and a loose BOSS χ²<200; the tighter χ²<30 and the π0 window |M(γγ)−mπ0|<15 MeV/c² are applied downstream in ROOT.
