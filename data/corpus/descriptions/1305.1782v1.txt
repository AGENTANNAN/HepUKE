On ψ(3770) and ψ(4040), search for baryonic decays ψ → ΛΛ̄π+π−, ΛΛ̄π0, ΛΛ̄η, Σ+Σ̄−, Σ0Σ̄0, Ξ−Ξ̄+, and Ξ0Ξ̄0, with Λ→pπ−, Λ̄→p̄π+, Σ0→Λγ, Ξ−→Λπ−, Ξ0→Λπ0, and π0/η→γγ. Use 2.9 fb−1 of 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, 482 pb−1 of 4.009 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus continuum data at 3.554 and 3.650 GeV; generate 50k-event exclusive MC for each of the seven modes (also at 4.009 GeV and the continuum points). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm and net charge 0, with no primary-vertex requirement on Λ/Σ/Ξ daughter tracks, requiring ≥3 positive and ≥3 negative tracks for the ΛΛ̄π+π− and Ξ−Ξ̄+ modes and ≥1 of each otherwise; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, >10° from any track, at least two (four for the two-π0 modes). PID uses the probability method with CL>0.001, protons identified against K/π and pions against K/proton, requiring ≥1 p, ≥1 p̄ and the relevant π+/π−; protons and p̄ with transverse momentum below 300 MeV/c are removed. Reconstruct Λ→pπ− and Λ̄→p̄π+ with secondary vertex fits minimizing the mass difference, and π0/η→γγ with a Kalman fit to the nominal mass (χ²<25, ≥1 π0, or ≥2 for two-π0 modes). Perform a 4C kinematic fit on ΛΛ̄π+π−, ΛΛ̄π0, ΛΛ̄η, pp̄π0π0, ΛΛ̄γγ, Λπ−Λ̄π+ or ΛΛ̄π0π0, χ² cut 200, flagged nominal (the paper's tight χ²<60 is applied in ROOT), with final mass windows Λ 1.107–1.124, π0 115–150, η 515–569, Σ+ 1.164–1.206, Σ0 1.178–1.205, Ξ− 1.305–1.337 and Ξ0 1.281–1.330 GeV/c², plus |M(pπ−)−M(Λ)|<40 MeV/c².
