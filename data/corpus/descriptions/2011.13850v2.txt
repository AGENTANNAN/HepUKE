Search for e+e− → ηc η π+π− at the five BESIII energy points 4.230, 4.260, 4.360, 4.420 and 4.600 GeV, using their real data and inclusive MC and 200,000-event exclusive MC for each of the sixteen ηc decay modes (π+π−K+K−, 2(K+K−), 2(π+π−), K+K−π0, K+K−η, ppbar, plus ten more such as 3(π+π−), K_S0 K±π∓, π+π−η, π+π−π0π0, ppbarπ0 and ppbarπ+π−). Charged tracks are selected with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and per-mode track/charge counts: four positive and four negative tracks for the all-charged modes, three and three for K+K−π0 and K+K−η, two and two for ppbar, always with net charge zero. Photons are required with EMC TDC 0–14, thresholds of 25 MeV (barrel) and 50 MeV (endcap), an angle of at least 10° from any track, and multiplicity exactly two for the all-charged/ppbar modes or at least four when a π0 or η is present. Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut: kaons are identified against pions and protons (2K+2K− for π+π−K+K−, 4K+4K− for 2(K+K−), 1K+1K− for K+K−π0 and K+K−η) and protons against kaons and pions in ppbar (1p, 1pbar), with all remaining tracks treated as pions. A nominal kinematic fit is performed: for the all-charged and ppbar final states it constrains four-momentum conservation and the γγ invariant mass to the η nominal mass with χ²<200; for K+K−π0 and K+K−η intermediate Kalman fits first mass-constrain γγ pairs to π0 (χ²<30, ≥1 π0) or η (χ²<30, ≥1 or ≥2 η) before the final four-momentum fit with χ²<200. The selection also encodes a K_S0 vertex requirement L/σ_L>2 with a ±15 MeV/c² mass window, π0 and η mass windows of [110,150] and [500,570] MeV/c², per-final-state χ² cuts chosen to retain 90% of signal, and retention of multiple candidates with identical χ².
