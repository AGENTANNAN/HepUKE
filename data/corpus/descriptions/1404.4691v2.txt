At √s = 3.773 GeV (ψ(3770)) measure the D → K−π+ strong-phase difference by CP tagging: one D0 (or D0bar) is reconstructed in a CP eigenstate as a single tag, and the other D is reconstructed in the flavour mode D0 → K−π+ to form a double tag. Use the 2.92 fb−1 real data set and inclusive MC at 3.773 GeV, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC samples for each of the eight CP-tag modes (K+K−, π+π−, K_S0π0π0, π0π0, ρ0π0, K_S0π0, K_S0η, K_S0ω, each with the opposite side decaying to K+π−) plus a 200k-event Kπ–Kπ sample for right/wrong-sign studies. Both D tags are reconstructed, with mode-dependent ΔE windows (e.g. ±0.025 GeV for K+K−, ±0.030 GeV for π+π− and K−π+, up to [−0.080, +0.045] GeV for K_S0π0π0), retaining only the combination with least |ΔE| per mode; π0 (η) candidates are built from photon pairs requiring at least one barrel photon and mass windows 0.115–0.150 (0.505–0.570) GeV/c² followed by a 1C mass constraint. K_S0 candidates come from π+π− with a vertex fit (χ² < 100) using looser IP tracks (< 20 cm along the beam, no transverse requirement), then a second fit constraining the momentum to the IP with L/σ_L > 2 and 0.487 < m(π+π−) < 0.511 GeV/c²; in the K+K− and π+π− modes cosmic-ray and Bhabha backgrounds are rejected by a TOF time difference < 5 ns, inconsistency with μ+μ−/e+e−, and an extra EMC shower > 50 MeV or an additional MDC track. The ρ0π0 and K_S0ω modes additionally require 0.60 < m(π+π−) < 0.95 GeV/c² and 0.72 < m(π+π−π0) < 0.84 GeV/c². A kinematic fit over the two tag sides constrains the total four-momentum and both tag masses to the D0 nominal mass, with χ² < 200.
