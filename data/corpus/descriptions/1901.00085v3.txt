On the J/ψ(3097), study J/ψ → φ η η′ with φ → K+K−, η → γγ, and η′ → γ π+π− (Mode I) or η′ → η π+π− with η → γγ (Mode II). Use the 1.3×10^9-event J/ψ real data set and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes. Selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge 0; photons must satisfy EMC timing 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV or endcap energy >50 MeV, photon–track angle >10°, and at least three photons (Mode I) or at least four (Mode II). Particle identification uses the probability method with probability >0.001, separating kaons from pions/protons and pions from kaons/protons, and requires exactly one K+, one K−, one π+, and one π−. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to K+K−π+π−γγγ (Mode I) with χ²<40 or to K+K−π+π−γγγγ (Mode II) with χ²<80. Reject any γγ pair with invariant mass in 0.12–0.15 GeV/c², and apply mass windows: Mode I η [0.522,0.573], φ [1.010,1.030], η′ [0.936,0.979] GeV/c²; Mode II η [0.509,0.586], φ [1.010,1.030], η′ [0.920,0.995] GeV/c²; Mode I also requires M(π+π−)<0.87 GeV/c².
