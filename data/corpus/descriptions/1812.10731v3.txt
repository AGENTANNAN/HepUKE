Using 567 pb−1 of 4.600 GeV real data and matching inclusive MC, measure Λc+ → Ληπ+ and the Σ(1385)+η intermediate through the Λπ+ mass spectrum with a single-tag method that reconstructs one Λc+ and leaves the other Λc− in recoil; generate 200,000 exclusive MC events for ψ(4260) → Λc+Λc−, Λc+ → Ληπ+, Λ → pπ−, η → γγ, and Λc− → p̄K+π−. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge 0, while the Λ daughter p and π− are allowed a looser |Vz|<20 cm and no Vr cut; photons must have 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap thresholds, TDC 0–14, angle to any track >10°, and at least two candidates. Particle ID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, proton identification against kaon and pion and pion identification against kaon, requiring at least one proton (antiproton count unconstrained). Two photons are then Kalman-fitted to the η mass with χ²<25 and at least one η, and Λ candidates are built from pπ− with mass in [1.111,1.121] GeV/c², vertex χ²<100, and flight distance >2σ. A final 4C kinematic fit to π+Λη with nominal four-momentum constraints requires χ²<200; offline, the best candidate is the one with minimal |ΔE| in the window [−0.03,0.03] GeV, and the M_BC fit range is [2.25,2.30] GeV/c².
