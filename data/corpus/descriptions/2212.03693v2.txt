Using the eight energy points 3.4900, 3.5080, 3.5097, 3.5104, 3.5146, 3.5815, 3.6500, and 3.6702 GeV, search for e+e− → Ω− Ω+ with a single-hyperon tag: reconstruct Ω− → Λ K−, Λ → p π− and infer the Ω+ from the recoil mass, while a separate charge-conjugate selection reconstructs Ω+ → anti-Λ K+, anti-Λ → pbar π+ and infers the Ω−. Use the real data and inclusive MC at all eight points, plus a 200k-event exclusive MC sample for e+e− → Ω− Ω+ with Ω− → Λ K−, Λ → p π− in phase space, applied to both tag selections. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; the Ω− tag requires at least one positive and two negative tracks, and the c.c. tag at least two positive and one negative track. Particle identification uses the probability method from dE/dx and TOF with a 0.001 probability cut, requiring at least one π− (π+), one K− (K+), and one proton (antiproton) after π/K/p separation. The Λ (anti-Λ) is built in a secondary vertex fit with |M_pπ − m_Λ|<4 MeV and positive decay-length significance, then combined with K− (K+) into Ω− (Ω+), choosing the best candidate closest to the Ω mass 1.67245 GeV. A partial reconstruction with a recoil-mass window 1.59–1.75 GeV is applied, with the signal region quoted as M_ΛK = 1.6665–1.6785 GeV and recoil mass 1.65–1.69 GeV; no 4C kinematic fit is used, and the nominal c.m. energy is fixed to 3.510 GeV in both tag selections.
