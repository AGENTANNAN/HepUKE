Study e+e− → K_S0 anti-Ξ+ Σ− (charge conjugate implied) across 56 c.m. energy points from 3.510 to 4.951 GeV (44 fb^-1 total), spanning ψ(3770), ψ(4040), ψ(4160), Y(4230), Y(4360), ψ(4415), Y(4500), Y(4660) and Y(4710), with the ψ(3770) sample (712_3773) as the reference/placeholder point; use real data and inclusive MC, and generate 400k PHSP exclusive-MC events per energy point for the signal channel. Reconstruct K_S0 → π+π−, anti-Ξ+ → anti-Λ π+ and anti-Λ → anti-p π+, with the Σ− inferred by partial reconstruction from the recoil against the K_S0 anti-Ξ+ system. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100, Vr<10, requiring at least 3 positive and 2 negative tracks; PID by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions and pions against kaons and protons, with at least 1 anti-proton, 3 π+ and 1 π−. Sequential secondary-vertex fits (each built by minimizing the mass difference and removing used particles) form K_S0 from π+π−, anti-Λ from anti-p π+, and anti-Ξ+ from anti-Λ π+, with windows |M(π+π−) − m_K_S0| < 12 MeV/c² and decay-length significance L/ΔL > 2 for the K_S0, |M(pbar π+) − m_Λbar| < 5 MeV/c² and positive decay length for the anti-Λ, and |M(Λbar π+) − m_Ξbar| < 8 MeV/c² and positive decay length for the anti-Ξ+, the best anti-Λ/anti-Ξ+ combination being chosen by minimizing δ_min = sqrt(|M(pbar π+) − m_Λ|² + |M(Λbar π+) − m_Ξ|²). The Σ− is then required to have a recoil mass M_recoil = sqrt((sqrt(s) − E_KsXibar)² − |p_KsXibar|²) within [1.1, 1.3] GeV/c². The same selection chain is executed on every scan point, for real data, inclusive MC and signal MC alike.
