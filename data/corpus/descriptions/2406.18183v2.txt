On 31 scan energy points from 3.650 to 4.914 GeV, measure the cross sections for e+e− → K−Ξ̄−Λ and K−Ξ̄−Σ0 (Σ0 → Λγ), with Ξ̄− → Λ̄π+ and Λ̄ → p̄π+. Use the real data at each point plus the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for the signal channel at every energy point. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, at least two positive and two negative tracks; particle identification by the probability method with a 0.001 cut, requiring at least one anti-proton, one K−, and two π+ after proton-versus-kaon/pion and kaon-versus-pion separation. Reconstruct Λ̄ → p̄π+ through a secondary vertex fit, requiring positive decay length and a ±8 MeV/c² mass window around the nominal Λ mass, and form Ξ̄− → Λ̄π+ with a ±6 MeV/c² mass window, choosing the best candidates by minimizing the combined mass difference. Then use partial reconstruction, treating the Λ as missing and requiring the recoil mass against K−Ξ̄− to lie in 1.0–1.3 GeV/c², which covers both the Λ and Σ0 peaks; the charge-conjugate channel and the Σ0 mode are implicitly included, and the ISR correction is obtained by iterative QED reweighting of the signal MC.
