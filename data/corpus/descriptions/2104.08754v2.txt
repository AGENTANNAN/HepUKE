Study e+e− → φ Λ anti-Λ at 28 energy points from 3.51 to 4.60 GeV (BOSS 703/705 real data, total 19.5 fb−1) with inclusive MC at 4.178 GeV, and generate 500k exclusive MC events for e+e− → φ Λ anti-Λ, φ → K+K−, Λ → p+π−, anti-Λ → anti-p−π+, at the 4.178 GeV point. Select events with exactly four charged tracks, two positive and two negative, |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<10 cm, |Vz|<20 cm, and no photon requirement; use probability-based PID with a 0.001 probability cut to identify p/anti-p against π/K and K± against p/π, effectively requiring one p+, one π−, one K+, and one K− for the visible Λ K+K− system. Reconstruct Λ → p+π− with a secondary vertex fit (χ²<100) and require flight-distance significance >2σ between the IP and the Λ decay vertex. Treat the anti-Λ as missing and perform a partial reconstruction of the Λ K+K− system with the missing mass constrained to the nominal Λ mass, χ²<30; apply a Λ mass window [1.112,1.120] GeV/c² and a φ mass window [1.01,1.03] GeV/c². For multiple candidates, choose the combination with the smallest combined vertex plus kinematic χ².
