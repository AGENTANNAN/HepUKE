At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), study double-tagged hadronic D0/D+ decays using the 712_3773 real data sample and its inclusive MC, with 100k-event exclusive MC generated for each of three signal modes: D0→K−3π+2π− (tagged by anti-D0→K+π−), D0→K−2π+π−2π0 with π0→γγ (tagged by anti-D0→K+π−), and D+→K−3π+π−π0 with π0→γγ (tagged by D−→K+π−π−). The tagging D is reconstructed from standard hadronic ST modes: for D0, K π, K π π0, and K π π π; for D+, K π π, K_S0 π, K π π π0, K_S0 π π0, K_S0 π π π, and K K π (charge conjugates implied). The signal D is built from the remaining tracks with required charged/neutral multiplicities: mode 1 has 1 K−, 3 π+, 2 π− and 0 photons; mode 2 has 1 K−, 2 π+, 1 π− and 4 photons; mode 3 has 1 K−, 3 π+, 1 π− and 2 photons. Each signal undergoes a four-momentum-constraint kinematic fit with χ²<200; no explicit track |cosθ|/Vz/Vr cuts, photon-energy thresholds, or PID criteria are encoded in this BOSS configuration beyond those multiplicities. A signal-side K_S0 veto (π+π− invariant mass within 12 MeV of m_K_S0) and ΔE/M_BC window cuts are applied later in ROOT. All three selections run over the real data, inclusive MC, and their corresponding exclusive MC.
