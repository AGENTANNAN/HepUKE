On the J/ψ and ψ(3686) resonances, study J/ψ → K_S0 K_S0 and ψ(3686) → K_S0 K_S0 with K_S0 → π+π−. Use the 3.097 GeV and 3.686 GeV real data sets and the corresponding inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of J/ψ → K_S0 K_S0 and ψ(3686) → K_S0 K_S0, both with K_S0 → π+π−. Event selection requires four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge zero, and at least one photon with energy >25 MeV and angle >10°. Pion identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying pions against kaons and protons, yielding four pion candidates. Two K_S0 are reconstructed from π+π− pairs by secondary-vertex fits minimizing the mass difference with χ²<200, with |Mππ−m_K_S0|<18 MeV for the J/ψ mode or <30 MeV for the ψ(3686) mode and decay length >2σ. A nominal 4C kinematic fit to K_S0 K_S0 is required with χ²<15 for J/ψ or <30 for ψ(3686); a competing γK_S0 K_S0 four-momentum-constrained fit is also performed and rejected when its χ² is smaller. Both selections share the same chain, differing only in the K_S0 mass window and nominal χ² cut.
