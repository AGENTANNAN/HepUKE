On the J/ψ at √s = 3.097 GeV, search for J/ψ → D⁻ e⁺ νₑ with D⁻ → K⁺ π⁻ π⁻. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k exclusive signal MC for J/ψ → D⁻ e⁺ νₑ, D⁻ → K⁺ π⁻ π⁻, with the semileptonic decay treated as VLL and the D⁻ decay as phase space. Event selection: exactly four charged tracks with zero net charge, each satisfying |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; PID by the probability method, requiring one K⁺, two π⁻, and one e⁺, with kaon/pion separation by likelihood and positron identification using P_e > 0.001 and P_e/(P_π+P_K) > 1/4 (E/p 0.85–1.05 applied later). Photon candidates must have energy above 25 MeV in the barrel or 50 MeV in the endcap, EMC time within 0–700 ns, and be at least 10° from the K⁺, π⁻, and e⁺ tracks; the total energy of good photons is required to be <0.2 GeV to suppress extra-photon backgrounds. Reconstruct the D⁻ from K⁺ π⁻ π⁻ and perform a 1C kinematic fit constraining the K⁺π⁻π⁻ invariant mass to the nominal D⁻ mass with χ²<10, using a D⁻ mass window of 1.85–1.89 GeV/c²; no 4C fit is used because the neutrino is missing. Require |p_miss| > 50 MeV/c in the partial reconstruction of D⁻ e⁺ plus missing νₑ, and extract the signal yield from U_miss = E_miss − c|p_miss| in ROOT.
