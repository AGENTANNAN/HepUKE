At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), study the semileptonic decays D0 → a0(980)− e+ νe and D+ → a0(980)0 e+ νe with a double-tag technique, using a0(980)− → ηπ−, a0(980)0 → ηπ0, η → γγ, and π0 → γγ. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 200k exclusive-MC events for each signal mode, with the D0 sample containing ψ(3770) → D0 anti-D0, D0 → a0(980)− e+ νe, a0(980)− → ηπ−, η → γγ, anti-D0 → K+π−, and the D+ sample containing ψ(3770) → D+D−, D+ → a0(980)0 e+ νe, a0(980)0 → ηπ0, η → γγ, π0 → γγ, D− → K+π−π−. Tag the opposite-charm D in D0→Kπ, Kππ0, Kπππ (ΔE between −64 and +35 MeV) and in D+→Kππ, Kπππ0, KSπ, KSππ0, KSπππ, KKπ (ΔE between −60 and +34 MeV). On the signal side require one π− plus one e+ and two photons with net charge 0 and a missing νe for the D0 mode, or one e+ and four photons with net charge 1 and a missing νe for the D+ mode; explicit |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr and photon-energy thresholds are not encoded in the code and follow the standard tag-framework selections. Electron PID uses fixed v1 thresholds, with the intended criteria E/pc>0.8 and combined e-PID ratio >0.8, while π/K separation is implicit in the tag modes. Perform a kinematic fit with a four-momentum constraint, constraining the two photons to the nominal η mass (and, in the D+ mode, an additional photon pair to the nominal π0 mass), and require χ²<200. Apply KL0 veto via a lateral-moment cut (0,0.35) on the higher-energy photon from η, plus extra-π0, extra-charged-track, and extra-unused-track vetoes in ROOT; extract the signal with a 2-D unbinned ML fit to M(ηπ) (or M(ηπ0)) versus U = E_miss − c|p_miss| in ROOT, using a Flatté a0(980) line shape and, for D+, the best a0 combination by the smallest sum of π0 and η 1C χ².
