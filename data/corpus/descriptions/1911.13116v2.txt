On ψ(3770), study Δ(B−L)=0 and Δ(B−L)=2 baryon-number-violating D+ decays with a D−→K+π−π− tag: D+→Λe+, D+→Σ0e+ (Σ0→γΛ), D+→Λ̄e+, and D+→Σ̄0e+ (Σ̄0→γΛ̄), with Λ→pπ− and Λ̄→p̄π+. Use the 2.93 fb−1 e+e− data at √s=3.773 GeV ("712_3773") plus its inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the four modes. Charged-track selection requires |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm (loose, for the Λ daughters), Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and one negative track. The two Σ0 modes additionally require at least one photon with EMC TDC in [0,14], energy above 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), and at least 10° from any track. PID uses the probability method with CL>0.001: a proton identified against K and π with exactly one p (or, for the Λ̄ modes, exactly one p̄), and an electron identified against π, K, p with at least one e+; remaining positive and negative tracks are assigned as π+ and π−. A secondary-vertex fit builds the Λ (Λ̄) from pπ− (p̄π+) using the mass-difference minimization. A kinematic fit then constrains four-momentum, with the Σ0 (Σ̄0) modes adding a 1C constraint fixing M(Λγ) (M(Λ̄γ)) to the nominal Σ0 (Σ̄0) mass, all with χ²<200 and flagged nominal. Note that the paper's tighter positron CL_e/(ΣCL)>0.8 and 0.8<E/p<1.2, Λ/Σ0 mass windows, photon-conversion veto, and ΔE/MBC selections are applied downstream in ROOT, not in this selection chain.
