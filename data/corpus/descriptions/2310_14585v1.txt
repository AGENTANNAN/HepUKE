On the ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → Σ+ Σ̄− ω and ψ(3686) → Σ+ Σ̄− φ, with Σ+ → p π0 and Σ̄− → p̄ π0 in both channels (ω → π+π−π0, φ → K+K−). Use the 3.686 GeV real data set and its inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two decay modes. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<2 cm, with at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons are required with EMC timing 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV and a 10° minimum angle to any track. PID uses the probability method (prob>0.001), separating protons from pions and kaons, and requiring p/p̄ together with either π± (ω mode) or K± (φ mode). In the ω mode, demand at least 6 photons, a 1C γγ mass-constrained π0 fit (χ²<20) with at least three π0, then a 7C fit on p p̄ π+π− π0π0π0 (four-momentum plus three π0 mass constraints) with χ²<45, keeping the best combination, and apply the Σ mass window [1176,1197] MeV/c² and J/ψ, η, Λ vetoes. In the φ mode, require at least 2 photons, one γγ→π0 1C fit (χ²<20), treat the second π0 as missing via best-combination-by-mass, then perform a 2C fit on p p̄ K+K− π0 (four-momentum plus one π0 mass constraint) with χ²<20, using the Σ window [1176,1200] MeV/c².
