On ψ(4260) → Ds*+ Ds−, with Ds*+ → γ Ds+ and Ds+ → K_S0 K− π+ π+ (K_S0 → π+ π−), reconstruct a single Ds− tag in eight hadronic modes: K_S0 K−, K+ K− π−, K_S0 K− π+ π−, K+ π− π−, π− η, K_S0 K− π0, K+ K− π+ π− π−, and π+ π− π−. Use the six BESIII c.m. energy points from 4.178 to 4.226 GeV (4180–4230 MeV) real data and inclusive MC; generate 1M-event exclusive MC for ψ(4260) → γ Ds+ Ds− with Ds+ → K_S0 K− π+ π+, Ds− → K+ K− π−, and K_S0 → π+ π−. On the signal side require one charged K− and two charged π+ (with the K_S0 reconstructed from its π+π− daughters), net charge +1, and at least one photon with energy above 25 MeV. Perform a 4C kinematic fit with χ² < 200, applying a helix-parameter correction beforehand and vetoing D0/D0bar mass backgrounds. Apply the same selection chain to real data, inclusive MC, and the exclusive signal MC; only eight of nine tag modes are used since the Ds− → π− η′ mode is unavailable.
