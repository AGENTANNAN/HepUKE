At the six c.m. energies 4178, 4189, 4199, 4209, 4219, and 4226 MeV, study D_s+ → τ+ ν_τ with τ+ → e+ ν_e ν_τ using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive signal MC for e+e− → D_s+ D_s− followed by D_s+ → τ+ν_τ, τ+ → e+ν_eν_τ. Reconstruct the tag D_s− in 11 hadronic modes: K_S K−, K+K−π−, K+K−π−π0, K_S K−π+π−, K_S K+π−π−, π+π−π−, π−η, π−π0η, π−η′(π+π−η), π−η′(γρ0), and K−π+π−. Select single-tag candidates via M_ST fits with signal region 1.89–2.04 GeV/c², applying energy-point-dependent recoil-mass windows for D_s*D_s/D_sD_s, e.g. 2.050–2.195 GeV at 4178 MeV and 2.040–2.220 GeV at 4226 MeV. On the signal side require exactly one extra positron, charge opposite to the tag, and a massless missing ν_τ; identify the positron with CL_e>0.1%, CL_e/(CL_e+CL_π+CL_K)>0.8, p>0.2 GeV/c, and E/p>0.8, adding photons within 5° to recover FSR. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining tag + e+ + ν_τ to the lab four-momentum, with χ²<200. Extract the double-tag yield from E_extra^tot<0.4 GeV, suppress Bhabha events with a relative-probability sum cut (<2.0 for n>1 or <0.9 for n=1), and account for backgrounds from non-D_s−, D_s+ → K_L^0 e+ν_e, and D_s+ → X e+ν_e.
