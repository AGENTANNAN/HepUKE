Study the double-tag decays Λc+ → n K_S^0 π+ and Λc+ → n K_S^0 K+ using the seven BESIII energy points from 4599.53 to 4698.82 MeV (4.5 fb−1), with the corresponding real data and inclusive MC samples, and generate phase-space exclusive signal MC for both modes. Reconstruct the tag-side Λc (charm −1) in ten hadronic modes: pK_S^0, pK+π−, pK_S^0π0, pK_S^0π+π−, pK+π−π0, Λπ−, Λπ−π0, Λπ−π+π−, Σπ with Σ0→Λγ, and Σ−π+π+, omitting the eleventh mode because it is not in the frozen tag-mode list. On the signal side, reconstruct K_S^0 → π+π− from a secondary vertex with L/σ_L > 2, loose daughter PID requiring χ²_π or χ²_K < 4, and keep the candidate with the largest L/σ_L; add a bachelor π+ or K+ with |Vz| < 10 cm and Vxy < 1 cm, loose PID, and likelihood separation L(π) > L(K), L(π) > 0 for the pion or L(K) > L(π), L(K) > 0 for the kaon. Identify the missing neutron via Mmiss² = E_miss² − p_miss², expected near the neutron mass squared, and perform a four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ² < 200. Apply the mode-dependent vetoes: for nK_S^0π+, exclude 0.235 < M_nπ+ − Mmiss < 0.265 GeV/c² and 0.240 < M_nπ− − Mmiss < 0.270 GeV/c²; for nK_S^0K+, exclude 0.240 < M_nπ+ − Mmiss < 0.260 GeV/c² and 0.248 < M_nπ− − Mmiss < 0.268 GeV/c². Extract the yields from a two-dimensional unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to Mmiss² versus M(π+π−), using PHSP MC signal shapes and ST-sideband plus peaking Λc+ background descriptions.
