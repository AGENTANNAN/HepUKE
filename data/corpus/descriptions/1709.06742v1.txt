On the ψ(3686) resonance at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ with χ_cJ → γγ for J=0 and 2, plus the Landau-Yang-forbidden J=1 generated hypothetically to extract an upper limit. Use the 448.1×10^6-event ψ(3686) real data sample and its inclusive MC, and generate 1.2 million exclusive-MC events for each of the χ_c0, χ_c1, and χ_c2 decay chains. The common event selection requires no charged track, hence no charged-particle PID, and exactly three photon candidates, each with Eγ > 70 MeV in both barrel and endcap and |cosθ| < 0.75 with respect to the positron beam direction. A 4C kinematic fit to the three photons enforces four-momentum conservation with nominal constraints and a loose χ² < 200 at BOSS level, the published χ²_4C ≤ 80 being applied downstream in ROOT; the lowest-energy photon is taken as the radiative photon and its spectrum is used for yield extraction. The same selection chain is applied to all three χ_cJ hypotheses, with the χ_c2 signal MC generated in a pure helicity-two configuration plus a 2% helicity-zero admixture for systematic studies.
