On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study the isospin-violating decay J/ψ → Λ Σ̄⁰ + c.c. (Σ̄⁰ → γ Λ̄, Λ → p π⁻, and charge conjugate), together with J/ψ → γ η_c with η_c → Λ Λ̄ and a search for J/ψ → Λ Λ̄(1520) + c.c. with Λ(1520) → γ Λ; all share the final state γ Λ Λ̄ → γ p π⁻ p̄ π⁺. Use the 225.2 M J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of eight modes: J/ψ → Λ Σ̄⁰, J/ψ → Λ̄ Σ⁰, J/ψ → γ η_c, J/ψ → Λ Λ̄(1520), J/ψ → Λ(1520) Λ̄, and the backgrounds J/ψ → Λ Λ̄, J/ψ → Σ⁰ Σ̄⁰, J/ψ → Λ Λ̄ π⁰. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<10 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks with net charge zero; photons with EMC timing 0–14, at least 5° from any charged track, barrel energy >25 MeV and endcap energy >50 MeV, requiring at least one photon, and at least one isolated photon at least 5° from the nearest proton and 30° from the nearest antiproton. Reconstruct Λ → p π⁻ and Λ̄ → p̄ π⁺ with secondary vertex fits using proton/pion mass hypotheses that minimize the mass difference and remove used tracks. Finally, perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to the γ Λ Λ̄ hypothesis with four-momentum conservation, χ²<200 in BOSS (the paper’s χ²<45 is applied later at ROOT level), selecting the photon combination with smallest χ²; the Λ/Λ̄ mass windows of ±5 MeV/c² and a veto on protons/antiprotons with p<0.3 GeV/c are ROOT-level additions.
