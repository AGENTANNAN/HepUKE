At √s = 3.686–4.600 GeV, study e+e− → η Y(2175), Y(2175) → φ f0(980) → φ π+π−, φ → K+K−, η → γγ, using real data at 3.686 (ψ(3686)), 3.773, 4.008, 4.226, 4.258, 4.358, 4.416, and 4.600 GeV and the corresponding inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for this signal mode at each energy point. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly four tracks and net charge 0; photons with barrel 25 MeV / endcap 50 MeV energy threshold, EMC timing 0–14, angle to tracks >10°, at least two photons; PID by the probability method, with π/K separation, requiring 1 K+, 1 K−, 1 π+, and 1 π−. Reconstruct η → γγ with a Kalman fit constraining the γγ mass to the nominal η mass, χ²<25, at least one η; then perform a 4C kinematic fit to η K+K− π+π−, χ²<60, flagged nominal. Apply mass windows η [0.513,0.578] GeV/c², φ [1.009,1.031] GeV/c², f0(980) [0.868,1.089] GeV/c², with sidebands [μ−5W, μ−2W] and [μ+2W, μ+5W].
