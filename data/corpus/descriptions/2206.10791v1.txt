On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → Λ Λ̄ using the double-tag technique: reconstruct the single-tag Λ̄ → p̄ π+ first and then the signal Λ → n γ with the neutron missing; both charge-conjugate modes are considered. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC (708_3097), and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for J/ψ → Λ Λ̄ with Λ̄ → p̄ π+ and Λ → n γ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93 and at least two tracks; photons with 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, EMC timing 0–700 ns, angle to tracks >10°, and at least one photon; PID by the probability method (prob > 0.001), identifying antiprotons against kaons and pions, requiring at least one antiproton, and treating the remaining tracks as pions; isolated photons required to be >20° from the antiproton track, with at least one such photon. Reconstruct Λ̄ from p̄π+ with a secondary-vertex fit (χ² < 20), requiring M(p̄π+) within 8 MeV/c² of the Λ mass, recoil mass in 1.03–1.18 GeV/c², and decay length >2σ. Perform a 1C kinematic fit with the neutron missing (χ² < 10), veto π⁰ via a 3C fit under J/ψ → Λ̄ n γγ rejecting M(γγ) within 20 MeV/c² of the π⁰ mass, require a photon BDT > 0.3, photon E > 150 MeV, opening angle to the neutron candidate >20°, and signal photon E < 400 MeV.
