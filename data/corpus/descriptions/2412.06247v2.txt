At BESIII on the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study the two channels ψ(3686) → p p̄ π0 and ψ(3686) → p p̄ η, both with π0/η → γγ, using the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, the 3.773 GeV data (2.93 fb⁻¹) and its inclusive MC for continuum subtraction, plus scan points around 3.670–3.710 GeV; generate 500k exclusive-MC events for p p̄ π0 and 200k for p p̄ η, both via γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, and net charge zero; photons with TDC 0–700 ns, E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80), E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), more than 20° from the nearest charged track, and at least two photons. PID is not applied in the main selection — the positive and negative tracks are simply assigned as proton and antiproton (a proton-over-π/K confidence-level requirement is used only for the scan data). First reconstruct the π0 through a Kalman fit constraining the two-photon invariant mass to the nominal π0 mass (χ²<100), then perform a 5C kinematic fit of p p̄ π0 (4C energy–momentum plus the π0 mass constraint, nominal) with χ²<30, the η channel being separated at the ROOT level by the γγ invariant-mass window. Finally, reject J/ψ → p p̄ background by requiring |M(p p̄) − 3.097| > 0.050 GeV after the kinematic fit.
