On the J/ψ(3097), study J/ψ → φπ0η with φ → K+K−, π0 → γγ, η → γγ, using the 708_3097 real data and inclusive MC (10B J/ψ events) and generating an exclusive signal MC sample of the same decay chain (φ→K+K− VSS, π0/η→γγ phase space). Require exactly two charged tracks, one K+ and one K−, with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vxy<1 cm, plus at least four photons with E>25 MeV (barrel) and E>50 MeV (endcap). Identify kaons by the probability method against pions with a 0.001 probability cut. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to K+K−γγγγ with χ²<45 and nominal constraints; for events with more than four photons, choose the γγ combination minimizing the π0η χ², and apply π0 and η mass windows of 15 MeV and 25 MeV around their nominal masses, respectively. Then perform 1C mass-constrained fits to π0 and η, followed by a 6C fit imposing both π0 and η masses, and keep candidates with 1.01 < M(K+K−) < 1.03 GeV. Further suppress J/ψ→K+K−π0π0 and J/ψ→K+K−ηη backgrounds by requiring χ²_{π0π0}>90 and χ²_{ηη}>8, and assign Q-factor weights from a 2D fit to M_{γγ} vs M_{γγ} for non-π0/non-η subtraction. The selected events are used for a GPUPWA partial-wave analysis including φ(1680), h1(1900), X(2000), a0(980)-f0(980) mixing, and sideband-modeled non-φ background.
