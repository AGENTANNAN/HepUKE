On ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, study the semileptonic decays D0 → K_S0 π− ℓ+ νℓ (ℓ = e, μ) using 20.3 fb−1 of real data and inclusive MC. Generate 500k-event exclusive signal MC for each lepton mode: D0 → anti-K0 π− e+ νe and D0 → anti-K0 π− μ+ νμ, with anti-K0 → K_S0, K_S0 → π+π−, and the anti-D0 tag decaying through the hadronic modes K+π−, K+π−π+π−, K+π−π0 (π0 → γγ). Reconstruct the tag anti-D0 with charm +1 using the three hadronic modes K+π−, K+π−π+π−, K+π−π0, and the signal side requiring one π+, two π−, and one e+ (or μ+), net charge 0, plus a missing massless neutrino. Apply a four-momentum constraint with χ² < 200. Use tag-side mode-dependent ΔE windows: K+π− [−0.027, 0.027] GeV, K+π−π+π− [−0.026, 0.024] GeV, K+π−π0 [−0.062, 0.049] GeV, and tag M_BC in 1.859–1.873 GeV/c². K_S0 candidates are formed from π+π− with a secondary-vertex fit and a mass window around M(K_S0). Signal regions are −0.05 < U_miss < 0.05 GeV for the electron mode and −0.02 < U_miss < 0.02 GeV for the muon mode; extract yields from an unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to U_miss, and apply an extra-photon E_gamma_max veto and M(K_S0 π− ℓ+) or M(K_S0 π− μ+ (π0)) cuts to suppress D0 → K_S0 π− π+ π0 peaking background. No further explicit charged-track geometric or PID selections are specified.
