Study e+e− → π+π−J/ψ with J/ψ → μ+μ− or e+e−, measuring the cross section across the √s = 3.77–4.60 GeV scan. Use the high-luminosity XYZ real-data points at 3.773, 3.810, 3.900, 4.009, 4.090, 4.190, 4.210, 4.220, 4.230, 4.260, 4.360, 4.420, 4.470 and 4.600 GeV together with inclusive MC, and generate 60k-event exclusive signal MC at every energy point with the ConExc generator (mode 90) for e+e− → π+π−J/ψ. Event selection requires exactly four charged tracks with net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm; photons are selected with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, TDC 0–14 and a 10° track-veto, though they are not used in the fit. PID uses the probability method (prob cut 0.001), treating tracks with p>1.06 GeV/c as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 MeV), with π/K separation, requiring 1 π+, 1 π−, 1 l+ and 1 l−. A 4C kinematic fit to π+π−ℓ+ℓ− is applied with χ² cut 60 and flagged nominal. The μ/e separation is finalized downstream in ROOT via EMC energy (μ: EMC<0.35 GeV, e: EMC>1.1 GeV), where the cos(θ_π+π−)<0.98 opening-angle cut and J/ψ window 3.08–3.12 GeV/c² are also imposed.
