At six BESIII energy points spanning 4.178–4.226 GeV, study e+e− → D_s*± D_s∓ with D_s*+ → γ D_s+, D_s+ → π+ π0 π0, using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC samples at each point. Tag the D_s− in seven hadronic modes — K_S0 K−, K+ K− π−, K_S0 K+ π0, K_S0 K− π− π+, K_S0 K+ π− π−, π− η′ (η′ → π+ π− η), and K− π+ π− — ranking candidates by invariant mass and applying per-mode M_tag windows of roughly 1.94–1.996 GeV/c² plus a recoil-mass window against the tag. On the signal side require five photons (four from the two π0 plus the D_s* transition photon) and one π+, with photon energy >25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), |cosθ|<0.80 (barrel) / 0.86–0.92 (endcap), and photon angle >10°. Intermediate resonances are constrained by π0, η and η′ mass windows of [0.115,0.150], [0.490,0.580] and [0.946,0.970] GeV/c². Perform a 4C kinematic fit with the two π0 mass constraints, χ²<200, picking the best double-tag candidate by the minimum χ² of the 8C fit. The amplitude analysis then further tightens with an 8C fit (4C plus tag D_s and D_s* mass constraints), transition-photon energy <0.18 GeV, M_recoil(γ_trans, D_s+) in [1.952,1.995] GeV/c², vetoes against η, D0 → K π π0 and K_S0 → π0 π0, and a signal D_s+ mass window of [1.925,1.985] GeV/c².
