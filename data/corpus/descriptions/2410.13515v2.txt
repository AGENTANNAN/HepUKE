At the 7 energy points from 4.600 to 4.699 GeV (4.5 fb⁻¹ total), study e⁺e⁻ → Λc⁺ anti-Λc⁻ with a double-tag technique, where the anti-Λc⁻ is reconstructed in 10 hadronic decay modes and the signal Λc⁺ → n e⁺ νe proceeds through the Cabibbo-suppressed beta decay. Use the real data and inclusive MC at all seven points, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for Λc⁺ → n e⁺ νe. On the tag side require the hadronic anti-Λc⁻ (charm −1); on the signal side require exactly one positron with net charge +1, at least one photon (the neutron is identified only through its EMC shower pattern), and a missing massless νe. Positron identification uses likelihoods with L(e) > 0.001 and L(e)/(L(e)+L(π)+L(K)) > 0.8 together with EMC energy over MDC momentum > 0.5, and the neutron is handled by a ParticleNet graph-neural-network classifier trained and calibrated on J/ψ and Λ control samples. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the tag plus e⁺ plus neutrino to the lab four-momentum, with χ² cut 200, and apply the same chain across all datasets.
