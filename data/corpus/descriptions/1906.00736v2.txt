On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → φ π+π− η with φ → K+K− and η → γγ. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive signal MC events for this decay chain. Event selection: exactly two positive and two negative charged tracks, net charge 0, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100.0, Vr<10.0; photons with TDC 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, angle to any track >10°, and at least two photons. Identify particles by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating K+ and K− against π/p and π+ and π− against K/p, requiring exactly one K+, one K−, one π+, and one π−, and remove those from the generic charged lists. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to K+K−π+π−γγ with a four-momentum constraint, χ²<80, flagged nominal. Apply φ and η mass windows |M(K+K−)-m_φ|<0.015 GeV and |M(γγ)-m_η|<0.040 GeV, and veto J/ψ backgrounds with |M(K+K−γγ)-M_J/ψ|>0.05 GeV and |M(K+K−π+π−)-M_J/ψ|>0.05 GeV when M(π+π−η)>1.1 GeV.
