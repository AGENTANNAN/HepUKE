On the six BESIII energy points 4.008, 4.178, 4.226, 4.258, 4.416 and 4.682 GeV, study e+e− → p K⁻ Λ (charge-conjugate implied) with the aim of determining the spin-parity of the X(2085). Use the real data and inclusive MC at all six points, and generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC per point with the KKMC generator via ψ(4260) → p⁺ K⁻ Λ phase space, Λ → p π⁻. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93 and |Vz|<20 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks (prompt p and Λ→p, and K⁻ plus Λ→π⁻) with net charge zero; PID by the probability method (cut 0.001) with proton identification against K/π and kaon against π/p, requiring at least two protons and one K⁻. Reconstruct Λ → p π⁻ through a secondary-vertex fit (vertex χ²<100) with a mass window |M(pπ⁻) − M(Λ)| < 6 MeV/c² and decay length > 2σ of the vertex resolution, using the two remaining tracks as prompt p and K⁻ with tighter |dz|<10 cm and |dr|<1 cm, and reject events with |cosθ_K|>0.83 to suppress Bhabha background. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to p K⁻ Λ, χ² cut 100, flagged nominal, before an amplitude analysis of the X(2085) with the K*(1980), K*(2045), K₂(2250), Λ(1520), Λ(1890), Λ(2350), N(1720) and N(2570) contributions.
