On the ψ(3686) (2712M events, E_cm = 3.686 GeV), study ψ(3686) → π0 h_c, h_c → π+π− J/ψ, J/ψ → ℓ+ℓ− (e+e− in the generated signal). Use the ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for ψ(3686) → π0 h_c, h_c → π+π− J/ψ, J/ψ → e+e−. Event selection requires exactly four charged tracks with net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100, Vr<10, and at least two photons with TDC 0–14, angle to track >10°, and energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap). PID uses the probability method: tracks with p>1.0 GeV/c are treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >1.0 GeV, otherwise muon) while pions (p<1.0 GeV/c) are identified against kaons, yielding two π and two leptons. Reconstruct π0 → γγ with a 1C Kalman fit (γγ mass constrained to the nominal π0 mass, χ²<25, at least one π0), then perform a 5C kinematic fit to π+π− ℓ+ℓ− π0 with nominal four-momentum conservation plus the π0 mass constraint and χ²<15; an additional fit to the π+π−π0 hypothesis is stored for a veto. Apply ROOT-level cuts: J/ψ mass window [3.085,3.108] GeV/c², e/μ separation (E>1.0 GeV → e, E<0.4 GeV → μ), η veto |M(π+π−π0)−m_η|>24.5 MeV/c², and M(π+π−)>0.3 GeV/c². Signal yields are extracted from a 2D fit to M(π0h_c) versus M(π+π−J/ψ), with Bayesian upper limits.
