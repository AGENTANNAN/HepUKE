On the ψ(2S) resonance at 3.686 GeV, measure the branching fraction of J/ψ → K+K− relative to J/ψ → μ+μ− via the chain ψ(2S) → π+π− J/ψ, using the 709_3686 real data set and its inclusive MC, and generating 750k-event exclusive MC for each of the two J/ψ decay modes (K+K− signal and μ+μ− reference). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.80, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0; no photon selection is applied (all-charged final state), though a total EMC energy window of 0.3–2.5 GeV and an electron veto E_dep/p<0.8 are imposed later. No PID is used at selection level: all positive tracks are assigned π+ and negatives π−, with the K/π separation performed by momentum (tracks below 1.0 GeV/c treated as pions, above 1.2 GeV/c as kaons). Perform a common vertex fit on all four charged tracks (no explicit nC constraint) with χ²<200, flagged nominal; the J/ψ is tagged by the π+π− recoil mass in [3.087,3.107] GeV/c², and the signal is selected with X_vis=(E_K+ + E_K−)/E_J/ψ in [0.98,1.01] for K+K− (or [1.02,1.07] for μ+μ−), requiring X_vis>0.90, cosθ(ππ)<0.5, cosθ(KK)<−0.95, and |Σp_i|/E_J/ψ<0.025.
