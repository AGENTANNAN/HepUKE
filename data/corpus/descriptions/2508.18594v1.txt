Search for the Λc–Σc bound state Hc in e+e− → π+ Hc−, Hc− → π− Λc+ Λ̄c−, using a partial reconstruction of the Λc+ and treating the Λ̄c− as missing. The analysis uses real data at √s = 4918.02 and 4950.93 MeV, the corresponding inclusive MC, and 100k-event exclusive MC at each energy point for ψ(4260) → π+π−Λc+Λ̄c− (PHSP) with Λc+ → p K−π+ and the charge-conjugate decay, with the c.m. energy constant set to 4.91802 GeV. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons are selected with EMC timing 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, and at least 10° separation from tracks. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against kaons/pions, kaons against pions/protons, and pions against kaons/protons, requiring at least one p, one K−, and one π+. A Λc+ candidate is formed by a vertex fit to p K−π+ with invariant mass 2.270–2.300 GeV and χ²<200, nominal. The missing mass is accessed via RM(Λc+) = sqrt((P_cms − P_Λc+)²), with two tag strategies: a Λc+ tag using RM(Λc+) + M(Λc+) − m(Λc+), and a Λc+π tag using a 2D fit of RM(Λc+π)+M(Λc+)−m(Λc+) versus RM(π); the remaining π+ and π− are also vertex-fit with the Λc+ (χ²<200, best minimum χ²). Signal extraction is an unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to RM distributions, using signal MC for 15 Hc mass–width combinations (4715–4735 MeV, widths 5/10/20 MeV) and backgrounds from qq̄ in the Λc+ sidebands [2.190,2.250] and [2.320,2.380] plus e+e− → ΣcΣc, ΛcΣcπ, ΛcΛc(2595), and ΛcΛc(2625). The result is a 90% C.L. Bayesian upper limit on σ(e+e− → π+ Hc− + c.c.) × B(Hc− → π−Λc+Λ̄c−), with multiplicative systematics via likelihood convolution and additive systematics taken as the most conservative limit across fit variations.
