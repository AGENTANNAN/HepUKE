At √s = 3.686 GeV (ψ(3686)), study ψ(3686) → Λ Λ̄ with Λ → pπ⁻ and Λ̄ → p̄π⁺, measuring the Λ transverse polarization. Use the 709_3686 real data set and its matching inclusive MC; generate 5,000,000 exclusive signal MC events for ψ(3686) → ΛΛ̄ (phase space) with hyperon weak decays for both Λ and Λ̄. Event selection requires at least four charged tracks with |cos θ| < 0.93 and PID by the probability method (cut 0.001), identifying protons against pions with at least one p and one p̄. Momentum-dependent PID assigns tracks with p < 0.6 GeV/c as pions and p > 0.8 GeV/c as protons, excluding the 0.6–0.8 GeV/c region. Protons and antiprotons are removed from the charged list and the remaining positive/negative tracks are assigned to π⁺/π⁻. Secondary vertex fits form Λ from pπ⁻ and Λ̄ from p̄π⁺ by minimizing the mass difference, requiring decay-length significance L/σ_L > 2, and the best ΛΛ̄ pair is selected by minimizing (M_pπ⁻ − m_Λ)² + (M_p̄π⁺ − m_Λ)² within Λ mass windows of [1.108, 1.123] GeV/c². A nominal 4C kinematic fit to pπ⁻p̄π⁺ is applied with χ² < 100. The Λ transverse polarization is extracted from the proton/antiproton angular distributions in the Λ helicity frame, with Λ mass sidebands used for background subtraction.
