Measure the integrated luminosity of the 21 e+e− data samples collected between 3.810 and 4.600 GeV (the two acquisitions at 4.230, 4.260 and 4.420 GeV are merged, giving 18 distinct sample names) using large-angle Bhabha scattering e+e− → (γ) e+e−, with a cross-check from e+e− → γγ. Use the real data, the inclusive MC corresponding to 500 pb−1 at 4.260 GeV for background studies and optimisation, and generate 1M exclusive Bhabha (e+e−γ with FSR) and 1M exclusive γγ events at each energy point. For the Bhabha selection, require exactly two oppositely charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; apply no PID, assigning electrons/positrons by charge, and require EMC energy E_dep > sqrt(s)/4.26 × 1.55 GeV and momentum p > sqrt(s)/4.26 × 2.0 GeV/c (BOSS uses the 4.260 GeV reference values, rescaled per energy point at analysis level). Then perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit of the e+e− pair to the c.m. four-momentum with χ²<200. For the γγ cross-check, veto all charged tracks with the same |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and zero net charge, select at least two photons with barrel/endcap EMC energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, TDC 0–14 and angle to the nearest track >10°, require each of the two most energetic photons to have energy > sqrt(s)/4.26 × 1.8 GeV (4.260 GeV reference), and perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit of the two photons to the c.m. four-momentum with χ²<200.
