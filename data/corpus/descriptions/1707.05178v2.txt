On ψ(3686) (3.686 GeV, 448.1×10⁶ events), study the radiative decays ψ(3686)→γ ηc with ηc→π+π−π0 and ψ(3686)→γ η(1405) with η(1405)→f0(980)π0, f0(980)→π+π−. Use the ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two decay modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with TDC 0–14 (50 ns/count), at least 10° from any charged track, E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) and E>50 MeV in the endcaps (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), at least three photons; PID by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying both tracks as pions against kaons and protons using dE/dx and TOF, requiring one π+ and one π−. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γγγπ+π− with χ²<20, and also store competing 2γπ+π− and 4γπ+π− fits. For both channels, select the π0 as the γγ pair with |Mγγ−mπ0|<0.015 GeV closest to the nominal π0 mass, veto events where either other γγ pair has |Mγγ−mη|<0.02 GeV, veto |Mγπ0−mω|<0.05 GeV, veto |Mγπ+π−−mJ/ψ|<0.02 GeV, and require χ²(3γ)<χ²(2γ) and χ²(3γ)<χ²(4γ). Apply a helix correction to pion tracks from a ψ(3686)→π+π−π0 control sample before the kinematic fit. Both decay modes share the same selection chain.
