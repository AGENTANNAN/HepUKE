On the 44-point BESIII energy scan from 3.808 to 4.951 GeV (22.42 fb⁻¹), study e⁺e⁻ → η J/ψ with J/ψ → l⁺l⁻ (l = e or μ), using the real data and inclusive MC at each point (represented here by the ψ(4260) dataset). Generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two η decay modes: η → γγ and η → π⁰π⁺π⁻ with π⁰ → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; Mode I requires exactly two charged tracks with net charge 0, while Mode II requires exactly four with net charge 0. Photons are required with EMC timing 0–14, >20° from any track, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, and at least two photons. PID uses the probability method (prob > 0.001): tracks with p > 1.0 GeV/c are leptons (electron if E/p > 0.8 or EMC energy > 0.8, muon if E ≤ 0.4 GeV), with Mode I requiring one l⁺ and one l⁻, and Mode II additionally identifying pions against kaons/protons and requiring one π⁺, one π⁻, one l⁺, one l⁻. Mode I performs a 4C fit to γγl⁺l⁻ with a nominal four-momentum constraint, χ² < 200 in the selection (final noted 4C χ² < 40), and photons above 0.08 GeV after the fit; Mode II first fits γγ to the π⁰ mass (χ² < 25, ≥1 π⁰), then performs a 5C fit to π⁰π⁺π⁻l⁺l⁻ constraining M(π⁰π⁺π⁻) to the η mass plus four-momentum, χ² < 200 (final noted 5C χ² < 80). Both modes require M(l⁺l⁻) in [3.067, 3.127] GeV/c², with sidebands [3.027, 3.057] and [3.137, 3.167] GeV/c², and the cross sections are extracted with ISR and vacuum-polarization corrections applied iteratively.
