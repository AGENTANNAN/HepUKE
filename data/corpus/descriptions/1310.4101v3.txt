At √s = 4.009, 4.229, 4.260 and 4.360 GeV, study e+e− → γ X(3872) with X(3872) → ρ0 J/ψ, ρ0 → π+π−, and J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−. Use the four real-data sets and matching inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC per energy point for each J/ψ lepton mode and for the dominant peaking background e+e− → π+π− J/ψ (ISR) with J/ψ → e+e−. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero. Photon candidates require EMC timing 0–14, angle to any charged track >10°, barrel energy >25 MeV or endcap >50 MeV, and at least one photon. Particle ID uses the probability method (prob >0.001), treats tracks with p>1.0 as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon), separates pions from kaons/protons, and demands 1 π+, 1 π−, 1 ℓ+, 1 ℓ−. A 4C kinematic fit to γπ+π−ℓ+ℓ− with nominal beam four-momentum is applied with χ²<60, together with a loose γπ+π− mass requirement of 0.6–20.0 GeV/c² and a J/ψ window 3.08–3.12 GeV/c²; the highest-energy photon is taken as the radiative photon, the fit iterates over photon candidates keeping the smallest χ², and events with |cos(angle between the two pions)|≥0.98 are rejected to suppress radiative Bhabha/dimuon backgrounds.
