On the 15 c.m. energy points from 4.085 to 4.600 GeV (the 703 scan samples), study e+e− → η h_c, h_c → γ η_c, η → γγ, reconstructing the η_c in 16 exclusive hadronic modes X_i: p pbar, 2(π+π−), 2(K+K−), K+K−π+π−, p pbar π+π−, 3(π+π−), K+K−2(π+π−), K+K−π0, p pbar π0, K_S0 K+π−, K_S0 K+π−π+π−, π+π−η, K+K−η, 2(π+π−)η, π+π−π0π0, and 2(π+π−)π0π0. Use the real data at these 15 points, inclusive MC at 4.23, 4.26, and 4.36 GeV, and generate 40k-event exclusive MC for each of the 16 modes at every energy point. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and mode-specific minimum multiplicities of positive and negative tracks (e.g., ≥1 p and ≥1 pbar, ≥2 π+ and ≥2 π−, etc.), with particle identification by the probability method (prob_cut 0.001) identifying protons, kaons, and pions against the other hypotheses and requiring the corresponding minimum counts. Photons must have energy >25 MeV (barrel) or >50 MeV (endcap), TDC in 0–14, angle to any charged track >10°, and at least three photons (five for modes with one π0 or two η, seven for modes with two π0). Then perform a 1C mass-constrained fit on γγ pairs to the η mass (and to the π0 mass where present) with χ²<25, requiring at least one η (and the appropriate number of π0), followed by a 4C kinematic fit to γ η + X_i with a common production-vertex fit on the charged tracks, four-momentum constraint, nominal flag, and χ²<200; for modes containing a K_S0, a secondary-vertex fit on π+π− builds the K_S0 by minimizing the mass difference, removing the daughters from the candidate list, and the 4C vertex constraint applies only to the prompt tracks. The published analysis further requires the E1 photon energy in (400,600) MeV, enlarged to (350,650) MeV for √s>4.416 GeV, the η recoil mass in (3480,3600) MeV/c², the hadronic system mass in (2940,3020) MeV/c², and a 4C χ²<25, all applied at the ROOT level while BOSS uses the loose χ²<200 cut.
