On ψ(4260) at √s = 4.178 GeV, study ψ(4260) → D_s*+ D_s− with D_s*+ → D_s+ γ and D_s+ → p n̄, using a tag-based analysis. Use the 4.178 GeV real data (3.19 fb⁻¹) and inclusive MC; generate 4 million exclusive MC events for the signal mode. Reconstruct the D_s− via 11 single-tag modes (KsK, KKπ, KsKπ0, KKππ0, KsKππ, πππ, πη, ππ0η, ππππ, ππργ, Kππ). On the signal side, require exactly one photon with angle >10°, exactly one charged track with charge +1 (proton), and one missing anti-neutron. Perform a kinematic fit constraining the D_s−, D_s+, and D_s* masses and the initial four-momentum, with the anti-neutron treated as missing, and select the best photon hypothesis with χ² < 200.
