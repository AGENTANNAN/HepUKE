On the J/ψ at √s = 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → γη′ with two modes: η′ → ωγ, ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ (final state γγγπ+π−), and η′ → ω e+e−, ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ (final state γγγπ+π−e+e−). Use the 1.31×10^9-event J/ψ real data sample and inclusive J/ψ MC; generate exclusive MC with 200k events for the ωγ mode and 600k for the ωe+e− mode, with J/ψ → γη′, a Dalitz ω → π+π−π0 decay and π0 → γγ, and phase space for η′ → ωe+e−. For the ωγ mode, require charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 20 cm, Vr < 2 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0, and at least four photons with EMC time 0–14 (700 ns), at least 10° from the nearest track, and energy above 25 MeV in the barrel or 50 MeV in the endcap; apply no PID and take the two tracks as π+π−. Then constrain a photon pair to the nominal π0 mass with a Kalman fit (χ² < 25, at least one π0) and perform a 4C fit to γγγπ+π− with a nominal four-momentum constraint and χ² < 200, retaining the best combination, with the radiative photon required to be the highest-energy photon above 1.0 GeV, |M(γγ) − mπ0| < 0.015 GeV, and the final χ²_4C < 80. For the ωe+e− mode, use the same track and photon quality cuts but require two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0, and at least three photons, with particle identification by minimizing the combined TOF and dE/dx χ² (χ²_PID cut 4) for π/e hypotheses, requiring π+π−e+e−, and selecting the event by the smallest χ²_4C+PID. Apply the same π0 Kalman constraint (χ² < 25) and a 4C fit to γγγπ+π−e+e− with nominal four-momentum constraint and χ² < 200, again requiring the highest-energy photon above 1.0 GeV, |M(γγ) − mπ0| < 0.015 GeV, final χ²_4C < 80, and a gamma-conversion veto R_xy < 2 cm on the e+e− vertex.
