On the 17 high-luminosity BESIII c.m. energy points from 3.8962 to 4.5995 GeV (XYZ data, L > 40 pb⁻¹), with inclusive MC at a nine-point subset, measure e⁺e⁻ → π⁺π⁻ h_c with h_c → γ η_c, reconstructing the η_c in 16 exclusive hadronic modes (p pbar, 2(π⁺π⁻), 2(K⁺K⁻), π⁺π⁻K⁺K⁻, p pbar π⁺π⁻, 3(π⁺π⁻), 2(π⁺π⁻)K⁺K⁻, K_S⁰K±π∓, K_S⁰K±π∓π⁺π⁻, K⁺K⁻π⁰, p pbar π⁰, K⁺K⁻η, π⁺π⁻η, 2(π⁺π⁻)η, π⁺π⁻π⁰π⁰, 2(π⁺π⁻π⁰)); generate 40k-event exclusive MC for every mode at each energy point. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm and Vr < 1 cm, with mode-dependent positive/negative track multiplicities (e.g. ≥1 p and ≥1 pbar for p pbar, ≥2π⁺ and ≥2π⁻ for 2(π⁺π⁻), etc.), while photons must pass 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap thresholds with at least one photon, and one photon pair extra for each π⁰ or η (≥3 photons when one π⁰/η is present, ≥5 for two π⁰). Charged-particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, requiring the relevant proton, kaon or pion hypotheses. K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻ candidates are built with a secondary-vertex fit, π⁰/η → γγ candidates with a 1C mass-constrained fit (χ² < 25), and the event is then subjected to a 4C kinematic fit under the e⁺e⁻ → π⁺π⁻ γ X_i hypothesis with χ² < 200, keeping the smallest-χ² candidate. The η_c is selected within about ±50 MeV/c² (charged-only and K_S⁰ modes) or ±45 MeV/c² (π⁰/η modes) of its nominal mass, and the h_c signal window is M(γ η_c) ∈ [3.515, 3.535] GeV/c².
