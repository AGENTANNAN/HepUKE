Study e+e− → π0π0 J/ψ at the BOSS 703 scan energy points from 3.808–4.600 GeV, with J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ− and π0 → γγ. Use the corresponding real data and matched inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for the π0π0 J/ψ final state with both J/ψ leptonic modes, using ConExc for the ISR treatment. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, more than 10° from any track, at least four photons. PID by the probability method with 0.001 cut, separating pions from kaons and protons, while lepton ID uses E/p (electron E/p>0.7, muon E/p<0.3) to assign the positive/negative tracks as l+ and l−. Reconstruct π0 candidates from γγ with mass-constrained Kalman fits (χ²<25, each requiring at least one π0), then perform a 4C kinematic fit to l+l−π0π0 with four-momentum conservation, χ²<75, choosing the best combination among fewer than three π0π0 pairings (the paper's 6C fit also reapplies two π0 mass constraints). Apply the J/ψ mass window 3.06–3.13 GeV/c² and subtract peaking ηJ/ψ and γψ(2S) backgrounds for the Born cross-section measurement with ISR and vacuum-polarization corrections.
