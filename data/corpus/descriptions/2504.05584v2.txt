At \(\sqrt{s}=3.773\) GeV (\(\psi(3770)\)), study \(e^+e^-\to\Lambda\bar\Lambda\to p\bar p\pi^+\pi^-\), using the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generating 500k exclusive MC events for the signal decay chain \(\Lambda\to p\pi^-\), \(\bar\Lambda\to\bar p\pi^+\). Event selection requires charged tracks with \(|\cos\theta|<0.93\), \(|V_z|<10\) cm, \(V_r<1\) cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero. PID is by the probability method, identifying protons against kaons and pions with probability \(>0.001\), requiring at least one proton and one antiproton, and assigning the remaining positive/negative tracks as \(\pi^+/\pi^-\). Reconstruct \(\Lambda\to p\pi^-\) and \(\bar\Lambda\to\bar p\pi^+\) through secondary-vertex fits that minimize the mass difference, removing used tracks, then perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to \(\Lambda\bar\Lambda\) with \(\chi^2<200\) (a tighter \(\chi^2<100\) is applied later). Additional downstream cuts are a charged-track momentum requirement \(p>0.6\) GeV/\(c\) and a \(\Lambda\) mass window \(|M-m_\Lambda|<5\) MeV/\(c^2\).
