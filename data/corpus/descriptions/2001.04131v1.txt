On the BESIII R-scan data at ten points from 2.000 to 2.644 GeV (2.000, 2.100, 2.125, 2.175, 2.200, 2.232, 2.309, 2.386, 2.396, and 2.644 GeV, total 300 pb−1), study e+e− → K+K−π0π0 for a partial-wave analysis. Use the real data and generate 200,000-event exclusive MC for each energy point for e+e− → K+K−π0π0 with the ConExc generator including ISR. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with EMC TDC 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, angle to nearest track >10°, and at least four photons. Kaon identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying kaons against pions and requiring exactly one K+ and one K−. Reconstruct two π0 candidates from photon pairs by constraining each γγ invariant mass to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<25, requiring at least two π0; then perform a 6C kinematic fit to K+K−π0π0 (four photons) constraining four-momentum, χ²<80, and pass the survivors to the GPUPWA partial-wave analysis.
