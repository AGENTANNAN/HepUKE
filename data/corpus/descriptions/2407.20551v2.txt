At √s=3.773 GeV, using the 7.9 fb⁻¹ real data set and the corresponding inclusive MC, reconstruct the semileptonic double-tag decays D0 → b1(1235)− e+νe with b1− → ωπ− and D+ → b1(1235)0 e+νe with b10 → ωπ0, both followed by ω → π+π−π0 and π0 → γγ. The tag side uses six hadronic modes for D0bar (K+π−, K+π−π0, K+π−π−π+, KS0π+π−, K+π−π0π0, K+π+π−π−π0) and six for D− (K+π−π−, KS0π−, K+π−π−π0, KS0π−π0, KS0π+π−π−, K+K−π−), applying per-mode ΔE windows (Table 1) and keeping the candidate with minimum |ΔE| per mode per charge per event, with MBC windows 1.859–1.873 GeV/c² for D0bar and 1.863–1.877 GeV/c² for D−. Generate 500k-event exclusive MC samples for each signal mode, with ψ(3770)→D0D0bar (D0→b1−e+νe, b1−→ωπ−, ω→π+π−π0, π0→γγ, D0bar→K+π−) and ψ(3770)→D+D− (D+→b10e+νe, b10→ωπ0, ω→π+π−π0, π0→γγ, D−→K+π−π−). On the signal side require 2 photons and 1 e+, 1 π+, 2 π− with net charge 0 for D0, or 4 photons and 1 e+, 1 π+, 1 π− with net charge +1 for D+, one missing νe, and a minimum photon angle of 10°; charged-track selection is otherwise only multiplicity and total charge, with no explicit |cosθ|, |Vz|, or Vr cuts encoded. Positron identification uses CL_e>0.001, CL_e/(CL_e+CL_π+CL_K)>0.8, and E/p>0.8, while KS0 is vetoed by |M(π+π−)−m(KS0)|>0.008 GeV/c², the ω signal region is M(π+π−π0)=0.757–0.807 GeV/c² with sidebands 0.697–0.742 and 0.822–0.867 GeV/c², a0(980) is vetoed by M(π+π−π0)>0.6 GeV/c², and M(b1 e+) is required below 1.82 GeV/c². Extra shower vetoes demand maximum extra photon energy below 0.30 GeV, no extra π0, and cosθ(γ,miss)<0.3 for D0 or <0.4 for D+. A kinematic fit imposes four-momentum conservation and constrains the γγ invariant mass to the π0 nominal mass with χ²<200, and signal extraction is by a 2D unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to M(ωπ) versus U_miss, with U_miss computed from the beam energy and the known tag-D mass.
