Using J/ψ → Ξ⁰Ξ̄⁰ events at √s = 3.097 GeV, measure Ξ⁰ → Λγ via single-tagged Ξ⁰ → Λπ⁰ and double-tagged Ξ⁰ → Λγ, with Λ → pπ⁻, Λ̄ → p̄π⁺ (both via hyperon weak decay) and π⁰ → γγ. Use the J/ψ real data and inclusive MC and a 100k-event exclusive MC generated for J/ψ → Ξ⁰Ξ̄⁰, Ξ⁰ → Λπ⁰, Ξ̄⁰ → Λ̄γ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 mm, Vr<10 mm, at least two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero; photons with EMC TDC in [0,14], angle to any charged track >10°, energy above 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), at least two photons. PID uses the probability method (prob > 0.001), identifying protons against kaons and pions, requiring at least one p and one p̄, with the remaining positive and negative tracks assigned as π⁺ and π⁻. Reconstruct Λ → pπ⁻ and Λ̄ → p̄π⁺ from secondary vertices minimising the mass difference, and π⁰ → γγ in a 1C Kalman fit to the nominal π⁰ mass with χ² < 25 (at least one π⁰). Then perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to Λ π⁰ Λ̄ γ with the γγ mass constrained to m(π⁰) and χ² < 40, together with a competing Σ⁰ hypothesis fit (same constraint, no nominal flag); the L/σ_L > 2 vertex separation, the |M(γΛ̄) − M(Σ⁰)| > 12 MeV/c² veto, and the ST/DT yields from the M_recoil and p_Λ fits are applied at the ROOT level.
