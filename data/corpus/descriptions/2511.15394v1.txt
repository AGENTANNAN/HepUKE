Search for the lepton-number-violating decay Ξ− → Σ+ e− e− (|ΔS|=|ΔL|=2) produced in J/ψ → Ξ− anti-Ξ+, using a single tag on anti-Ξ+ → anti-Λ π+ with anti-Λ → anti-p π+ and reconstructing Σ+ → p π0, π0 → γγ; the Ξ− yield is extracted from the recoil mass against the tag, 10% of the data is used to validate the procedure before unblinding, and the signal is modelled with the LNV width of Barbero et al. (B=0). Use J/ψ data at ECM = 3.097 GeV (~1.0087×10^10 events) together with the matching inclusive MC, and generate 1,000,000 exclusive MC events with the full signal decay card. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm, and at least three positive and two negative tracks; photons with EMC TDC 0–14, more than 10° from any track, more than 20° from the primary track, energy above 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), at least two. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against kaons/pions and electrons against pions/kaons/protons, requiring at least one proton, one anti-proton and one electron, and removing the identified protons, anti-protons and electrons from the charged-track lists. Reconstruct anti-Λ → anti-p π+ and anti-Ξ+ → anti-Λ π+ with secondary-vertex fits that minimize the mass difference, and π0 → γγ with a Kalman fit constraining the γγ invariant mass to the nominal π0 (χ²<30, at least one π0). Finish with a nominal kinematic fit on the anti-Ξ+, p, π0 and e− system imposing four-momentum conservation, treating the second electron as a missing track, with χ²<20.
