At √s = 3.773 GeV on the ψ(3770), perform a double-tag analysis of e+e− → D+D−, reconstructing the tag D− in the Cabibbo-favored modes D− → K+π−π−, D− → K_S0π−, and D− → K+π−π−π0 (relative fractions 0.340, 0.270, 0.390, with K_S0 → π+π− and π0 → γγ) and the signal D+ in D+ → K+π+π−π0, including the D+ → K+ω, ω → π+π−π0 channel. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC (dataset 712_3773); generate 1,000,000-event exclusive MC for D+ → K+π+π−π0 and 500,000-event exclusive MC for D+ → K+ω with ω → π+π−π0 Dalitz decay, both using the same three D− tag modes. Select signal events with exactly two photons, one K+, two π+, one π−, and net charge +1. Apply a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation and the γγ invariant mass constrained to the nominal π0 mass, with χ² < 200. Additional requirements applied after reconstruction are: signal-side K_S0 veto |M(π+π−) − M(K_S0)| > 20 MeV/c²; D+D− opening angle > 160°; tag ΔE windows of (−25, 25) MeV for tag modes without π0 and (−55, 40) MeV for modes with π0, used in the mBC_tag vs mBC_sig 2D fit; and for the K+ω subsample |M(π+π−π0) − M_ω| < 40 MeV/c², |cosθ_ω| > 0.57, and λ/λ_max > 0.21.
