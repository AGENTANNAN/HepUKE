On the ψ(3686) (E_cm = 3.686 GeV), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ with χ_cJ → ρ+ρ−, ρ± → π±π0, π0 → γγ, i.e. the π+π−π0π0 final state, for J = 0, 1, 2. Use the ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the three χ_cJ modes with the decay chain ψ(2S) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → ρ+ρ− (VSS), ρ → ππ0, π0 → γγ, sharing one selection chain across all three. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, with exactly one positive and one negative track and net charge zero. Photons are taken with EMC timing 0–700 ns and at least 10° opening angle to the nearest charged track, using 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds and requiring at least five photons (the radiative γ plus four from the two π0). Pion identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating pions from kaons and protons, requiring exactly one π+ and one π−. Two photon pairs are fitted to the nominal π0 mass (χ² < 25) requiring at least two π0 candidates, followed by a 6C kinematic fit of γ π+π− π0π0 (four-momentum constraint plus nominal) with χ² < 200 at BOSS level, a tighter χ² < 60 applied later in ROOT; two competing-hypothesis fits (6γ π+π− and 4γ π+π−, four-momentum constrained, no χ² cut) provide χ²_more and χ²_less. Additional vetoes remove π0π0 and π+π− J/ψ recoil regions (M_recoil > 3.07 and 3.09–3.10 GeV/c²), η/J/ψ (3.09 < M(π+π−π0_high) < 3.18 GeV/c²), and a 2D K_S K_S window, with a charged-track helix correction applied before the fit.
