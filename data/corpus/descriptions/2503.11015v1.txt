On 12 energy points from 4.612 to 4.951 GeV (5.8 fb⁻¹), search for X(1⁻⁺) in e⁺e⁻ → γ Ds⁺ Ds1⁻(2536), with Ds1⁻ → D̄*⁰ K⁻, D̄*⁰ → D̄⁰ π⁰. Use real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two Ds⁺ decay modes: Ds⁺ → K⁺K⁻π⁺ (with φ/K* intermediate cuts) and Ds⁺ → K_S⁰ K⁺ (K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; at least two photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, angle to track >10°. PID by the probability method (prob_cut 0.001): for Mode 1 require exactly one K⁺ and one K⁻, then identify remaining tracks as pions requiring exactly one π⁺ and one π⁻; for Mode 2 require exactly one K⁺, reconstruct K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻ via secondary vertex fit with mass in (487,511) MeV/c² and decay length >2× vertex resolution, and assign the remaining negative track as K⁻. Perform a partial reconstruction: constrain Ds⁺ mass to 1.968 GeV and require the recoil mass of γ Ds⁺ K⁻ in 2.000–2.020 GeV (D*⁰ window). For Mode 1, apply φ mass window ±10 MeV, K* mass window ±50 MeV, and helicity angle |cosθ_hel|>0.5.
