Using 3.19 fb⁻¹ of real data and inclusive MC at √s = 4.178 GeV, search for e+e− → D_s+ D_s*−, with the tag side D_s*− → γ D_s− reconstructed in three hadronic modes (D_s− → K+K−π−, K_S K−, and K_S K−π+π−) and the signal side D_s+ → p p̄ e+ ν_e. Generate a 100k-event exclusive MC sample for the signal mode. On the tag side, require pion momenta > 0.1 GeV/c, K_S candidates with invariant mass in (0.487, 0.511) GeV/c² and decay length greater than twice its uncertainty, and a D_s− recoil mass in [2.06, 2.18] GeV/c², choosing the best candidate by proximity to the D_s*+ nominal mass and allowing fewer than four unused charged tracks. On the signal side require one p, one p̄ and one e+ with net charge +1 and a massless missing ν_e; since the electron is not reconstructed in ~95% of events, events are classified as signal by the p p̄ pair alone, but when a third track is found its momentum must be below 0.09 GeV/c. Require missing-mass squared > 0 to suppress continuum q q̄ background. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the tag, signal, and neutrino four-momenta to the centre-of-mass energy, with χ² < 200.
