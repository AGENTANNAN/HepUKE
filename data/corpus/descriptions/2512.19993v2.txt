On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ with χ_cJ → p p̄ K_S^0 K_S^0, each K_S^0 → π+π−. Use the 3.686 GeV real data sample (709_3686, 2.712×10^9 events) and its inclusive MC; generate 1,000,000 exclusive MC events for ψ(3686)→γ χ_c1 → p p̄ K_S^0 K_S^0, with both K_S^0 decaying to π+π−. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least 3 positive and 3 negative tracks, and net charge 0; photons with barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, EMC timing 0–14, more than 10° from any track, and at least one photon. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying protons against kaons and pions, requiring at least one proton and one antiproton. Reconstruct two K_S^0 → π+π− secondary vertices by minimizing the π+π− mass difference, each with |M(π+π−)−m_K0|<0.012 GeV/c^2 and decay length >2σ from the IP, plus a Λ/Λ̄ veto |M(pπ−)−m_Λ|>0.012 GeV/c^2 and |M(p̄π+)−m_Λ̄|>0.012 GeV/c^2. Then perform a 4C kinematic fit to γ p p̄ K_S^0 K_S^0 with nominal four-momentum constraints and χ²<200 (loose BOSS value; the paper optimizes <50).
