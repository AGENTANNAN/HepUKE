At 22 center-of-mass energies from 2.00 to 3.08 GeV, study e+e− → φπ+π−, φ → K+K−, using real data and inclusive MC at each point. Generate 1M exclusive-MC events per energy for the signal channel with ConExc ISR mode 35 and γ* → π+π−K+K− phase space. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and at least three tracks, with no photon selection. Identify particles by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating π from K/p and requiring at least one π+, one π−, and one K+, with the K− allowed missing; the charge-conjugate π+π−K− hypothesis is also tested. Perform a vertex fit on π+π−K+ followed by a 1C kinematic fit with a missing K− and four-momentum constraint, χ²<10, nominal, keeping the missing-kaon hypothesis with smallest χ². The φ window is |M(K+K−) − mφ| < 0.01 GeV/c², with sidebands [0.995, 1.005] and [1.035, 1.045] GeV/c² for non-φ background.
