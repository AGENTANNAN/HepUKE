Study e+e− → KS0 KS0 J/ψ with J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ− and KS0 → π+π−, using 36 energy points from 4.130 to 4.946 GeV (real data plus inclusive MC), and a 100k-event exclusive signal MC of e+e− → KS0 KS0 J/ψ generated from a ψ(4260) mother with J/ψ → e+e−. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with photons accepted for EMC timing 0–700 ns, ≥10° from any track, and 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, treating tracks with p>0.95 GeV as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.95 GeV, otherwise muon). Two reconstruction chains are commissioned: a two-KS0 sample requiring ≥6 tracks, two secondary-vertex KS0 candidates from opposite-charge pion pairs (mass-difference minimization with used particles removed), and a 4C kinematic fit of KS0 KS0 l+l− with χ²<200; and a one-KS0 sample requiring ≥4 tracks, ≥2 photons, one KS0 vertex, and a 1C fit where the missing KS0 is constrained to the KS0 mass, with χ²<20. The J/ψ and KS0 mass windows (±3σ) and the KS0 decay-length >2σ requirement are applied subsequently in ROOT, and both χ² cuts were optimized using FOM = S/√(S+B).
