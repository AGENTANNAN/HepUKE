On the seven energy points from 4.600 to 4.700 GeV (4.5 fb⁻¹), study Λ_c⁺ → Λ K_S⁰ K⁺, Λ_c⁺ → Λ K_S⁰ π⁺, and Λ_c⁺ → Λ K*⁺ with K*⁺ → K_S⁰ π⁺, where Λ → p π⁻ and K_S⁰ → π⁺ π⁻. Use the real data and inclusive MC at all seven points, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the three decay modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, at least three positive and two negative tracks, net charge +1; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC timing in 0–14, angle to the nearest track > 10°, and fewer than four photons. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions (and, for the K⁺ mode, kaons against pions; for the π⁺ modes, pions against kaons), requiring at least one proton (plus at least one kaon in the K⁺ mode); tracks identified as proton or kaon are removed from the positive-track list, and the remaining positive tracks are taken as π⁺ while negative tracks are taken as π⁻. Form Λ from p π⁻ and K_S⁰ from π⁺ π⁻ via secondary-vertex fits constrained to the nominal masses, both with χ² < 100 and at least one candidate, using mass windows 1.090–1.140 GeV/c² for Λ and 0.450–0.540 GeV/c² for K_S⁰ and requiring flight significance > 2σ. Perform a four-momentum-constrained fit to Λ K_S⁰ K⁺ or Λ K_S⁰ π⁺ with χ² < 200; if multiple candidates remain, keep the one with the smallest |ΔE| within −0.02 to 0.02 GeV. The same selection chain is used for the K*⁺ mode, with the K*⁺ resonance described in the decay card.
