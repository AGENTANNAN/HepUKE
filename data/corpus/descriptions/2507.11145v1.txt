On the J/ψ, study J/ψ → γ Λ Λ̄ via Λ(1520) → γ Λ (Mode I) and J/ψ → γ γ Λ Λ̄ via Λ(1520) → γ Σ0, Σ0 → γ Λ (Mode II). Use 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each mode. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<10 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, at least one (Mode I) or two (Mode II). PID by the probability method (prob>0.001), identifying at least one proton and one anti-proton (against K/π) and at least one π+ and one π− (against K/p). Reconstruct Λ from pπ− and Λ̄ from p̄π+ with |M(pπ)−M_Λ|<5 MeV/c², choosing the candidate closest to the nominal Λ mass. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γ Λ Λ̄ (Mode I) or γγ Λ Λ̄ (Mode II) with χ² cut 200; Mode I additionally vetoes photons forming a Σ0 mass with Λ or Λ̄ within 20 MeV/c², and Mode II requires the lower-energy photon to satisfy |M(γ_low Λ)−M_Σ0|<10 MeV/c² and vetoes |M(γγ)−M_π0|>30 MeV/c².
