At √s = 4.009 GeV (ψ(4040)), measure the semileptonic decays Ds+ → η e+ ν_e and Ds+ → η' e+ ν_e using a double-tag technique: the tag Ds− is fully reconstructed in hadronic modes and the signal Ds+ is identified by the electron plus missing neutrino. Use the 482 pb⁻¹ real data set at 4.009 GeV and the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of three signal modes: Ds+ → η e+ ν_e with η → γγ, Ds+ → η' e+ ν_e with η' → η π+π− (η → γγ), and Ds+ → η' e+ ν_e with η' → γ ρ0 (ρ0 → π+π−). The Ds− tag is built from ten hadronic modes (K+K−π−, φρ−, K_S0 K+π−π−, K_S0 K−π+π−, K_S0 K−, π+π−π−, ηπ−, η'π− via ηππ and via γρ, and ηρ−), with charged tracks required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, |Vxy|<1 cm and pion/kaon PID by likelihood (pion if CL_π>0 and CL_π>CL_K; kaon if CL_K>0 and CL_K>CL_π), and photons with E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) or E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), TDC in [0,700] ns and >10° from any charged track. Intermediate resonances are selected with windows 0.115<M(γγ)<0.150 (π0), 0.510<M(γγ)<0.570 (η), 1.005<M(K+K−)<1.040 (φ), 0.570<M(π0π−)<0.970 (ρ−), 0.943<M(ηπ+π−)<0.973 or 0.932<M(γρ0)<0.980 (η' with 0.570<M(π+π−)<0.970), and K_S0 as π+π− with Vz within ±20 cm, 0.487<M(π+π−)<0.511 and a positive-length secondary vertex; π0 and η are mass-constrained (1C). On the signal side require an electron with CL_e>0.001 and CL_e/(CL_e+CL_π+CL_K)>0.8 opposite in charge to the tag, the appropriate photon and charged-pion multiplicities (2 photons for the η/ηγγ and η'→ηππ modes, 1 photon plus π+π− for the η'→γρ0 mode), net charge +1, and a missing neutrino. A kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation plus the η→γγ mass constraint is applied with χ²<200, the tag is chosen as minimum |ΔE| with |ΔE| within 3σ, and the mBC signal region is (−4σ,+5σ) about the fitted peak. Extra-photon energy is vetoed above 300 MeV with no additional charged tracks (and no extra π0 for the two η' modes), and the final U_miss signal window is (−0.10,+0.12) GeV for the 2-photon modes and (−0.08,+0.10) GeV for the η'→γρ0 mode.
