On ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, measure the branching fractions of D+ → K+K−π+π+π−, D+ → φπ+π+π− (φ→K+K−), and the modes D+ → K_S0K+π+π−π0, K_S0K+η, K_S0K+ω via the double-tag method. Use the 20.3 fb−1 real data sample at 3.773 GeV and the corresponding inclusive MC; generate 1,000,000-event exclusive MC for the two implemented signal modes: D+ → K+K−π+π+π− (phase space) and D+ → φπ+π+π− with φ→K+K−. Event selection: tag one D− through six hadronic modes (K+π−π−, K_S0π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S0π−π0, K_S0π−π+π−, K+K−π−) with mode-dependent ΔE windows from Table 2 (nominally ±60 MeV), and tag the D+ signal via K+K−π+π+π− (the φ mode collapses to the same final state), with no additional signal-side particles (pure double tag). Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the total four-momentum, require χ² < 200, and define the signal region as M_BC ∈ (1.863, 1.877) GeV/c². Single-tag yields are extracted from ML fits to M_BC with an ARGUS background, and the branching fraction is obtained from BF = N_DT / Σ(N_ST^i × ε_DT^i / ε_ST^i). The three remaining signal modes (K_S0K+π+π−π0, K_S0K+η, K_S0K+ω) are not implemented because their required tag modes are absent from the available tag-mode vocabulary, so only the two modes above are commissioned.
