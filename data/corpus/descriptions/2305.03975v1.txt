On the ψ(3770), study quantum-correlated D0 D0bar pairs to measure the CP-even fraction of D0 → K_S0 π+ π- π0, using 3.773 GeV real data (2.93 fb−1, sample 712_3773) together with the corresponding inclusive MC, and generating 500k-event exclusive MC with the decay card ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar, D0 → K_S0 π+ π- π0, K_S0 → π+ π-, π0 → γγ (single-tag D0bar → K+ π- flavor mode). Event selection requires on the signal side two photons (from π0 → γγ), three π+ and one π- from K_S0 → π+π- plus the D0 daughters, with net charge 0. The reconstruction uses a K_S0 → π+π- vertex fit with decay length significance L/σ_L > 2 and a π+π- mass window, and a π0 → γγ mass-constrained fit, with M_BC and ΔE used for single- and double-tag selection. A kinematic fit applies a four-momentum constraint and constrains the two photons to the nominal π0 mass, with χ² cut 200. Note that only the D0 → K+π- flavor tag is available in the current tag-side implementation, the CP-eigenstate and quasi-CP tag modes of Table I being applied in ROOT rather than this code.
