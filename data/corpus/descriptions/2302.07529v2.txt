Study of the semileptonic decays Λc+ → Λπ+π−e+νe (Λ → pπ−) and Λc+ → pKS0π−e+νe (KS0 → π+π−) with a single-tag/double-tag technique on the 4.600–4.699 GeV data. Use the seven real-data sets at 4.600, 4.612, 4.628, 4.641, 4.661, 4.682 and 4.699 GeV plus inclusive MC at six of these points (4.612 GeV is missing from the standard table, so the closest sample stands in), and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes. The tag side is an anti-Λc− reconstructed from nine ST modes (p̄KS0, K+π−, KS0π0, KS0π+π−, K+π−π0, Λ̄π−, Λ̄π−π0, Λ̄π−π+π− and π+π+π−-type), with charm flavour −1. On the signal side require five charged tracks — 1 p, 1 π+, 2 π−, 1 e+, net charge +1 — no photons, and one missing massless νe; the five tracks are tight (|Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, |cosθ|<0.93), while Λ/KS0 daughter tracks are looser (|Vz|<20 cm, no Vr cut). Intermediate resonances come from secondary-vertex fits (χ²<100) with positive decay length and mass windows M(pπ−) ∈ [1.09,1.14] GeV (Λ) and M(π+π−) ∈ [0.490,0.504] GeV (KS0). PID uses likelihood ratios: p with L(p)>L(K), L(p)>L(π), L(p)>0; π with L(π)>L(K), L(π)>0; e+ with L(e)>0.001 and L(e)/(L(e)+L(π)+L(K))>0.99 in mode 1 (loosened to 0.98 in mode 2), with FSR showers inside a 5° cone around the positron added back. Backgrounds are rejected by cosθ(e,π)<0.88 (0.92 in mode 2), M(Λπ+π−e)<2.20 GeV (M(pKS0π−e)<2.28 GeV), cosθ(P_miss, γ_most-energetic)<0.82 (0.90), and U_miss = E_miss − |p_miss| ∈ [−0.08,0.08] GeV. Both modes share the same tag-side chain and a 4C kinematic fit constraining the tag to the nominal Λc mass, with χ² cut 200.
