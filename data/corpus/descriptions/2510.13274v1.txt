On a multi-energy scan of 19 c.m. energies from 4395.38 to 4950.93 MeV, use the representative 4681.92 MeV real data and inclusive MC; study e+e− → K_S0 K− π+ J/ψ (+ c.c.), with J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ− and K_S0 → π+π−, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two J/ψ decay modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, three positive and three negative tracks (net charge 0). PID by the probability method, with high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >1.0 GeV for the e+e− mode, >0.4 GeV for the μ+μ− mode) and π/K separation, requiring one K+, zero K−, one l+, one l−. Remove the identified K+ and leptons, assign the remaining positive and negative tracks as π+π−, reconstruct K_S0 via a secondary-vertex fit minimizing the mass difference, then perform a 4C kinematic fit on K_S0 K+ π+ l+l− with χ²<200, flagged nominal. For the μ+μ− channel, additionally require a MUC hit-depth sum >75 cm in the later ROOT stage.
