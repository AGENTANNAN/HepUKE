On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → Ξ− Ξ̄+, with Ξ− → Λ π−, Λ → p π−, and Ξ̄+ → Λ̄ π+, Λ̄ → n̄ π0, π0 → γγ. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC (708_3097), and generate a 2M-event exclusive MC sample with the full decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and at least three negative tracks; photons with TDC 0–14, 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, opening angle >20° to any track, at least two photons. PID by the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying protons against π/K; a positive track with p>0.32 GeV/c is taken as a proton, and positive/negative tracks with p<0.30 GeV/c as π+/π−. Reconstruct Λ → p π− by a secondary vertex fit choosing the combination that minimizes the mass difference, with |M(pπ−)−m_Λ|<11 MeV, |M(pπ−π−)−m_Ξ|<11 MeV, positive Λ and Ξ− decay lengths, and |cosθ_Ξ|<0.84 in the CM. Then do a Kalman fit for π0 → γγ constraining the γγ mass to m_π0 (χ²<25, at least one π0), followed by a full kinematic fit on Λ π− π+ π0 with the anti-neutron treated as a missing particle of unknown mass, a four-momentum constraint, χ²<200, nominal, and |M(n̄γγπ+)−m_Ξ̄|<11 MeV after the fit. Additional requirements include a BDT photon classifier (90% signal efficiency, 55% background rejection), a >15° opening angle between the photon and the Ξ̄ direction to veto anti-neutron EMC showers, and a blinded angular analysis.
