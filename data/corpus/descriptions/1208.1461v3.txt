On the ψ(3686) at √s=3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686)→π+π−J/ψ with J/ψ→3γ and J/ψ→γηc, ηc→γγ. Use the ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two J/ψ decay modes (3γ and γηc→γγ) with default cross sections. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, and net charge 0. Photons must have E>25 MeV in the barrel, E>50 MeV in the endcap, be more than 5° from any charged track, have shower time within 0–14 (≈700 ns), and number at least three, with only events having 3 or 4 photon candidates retained. The two tracks are assigned as π+ and π− without an explicit PID method. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to π+π−γγγ with nominal flag, a vertex constraint on the π+π− pair, and a four-momentum constraint, using χ²<200, with a tighter χ²<50 applied later in ROOT. Additional ROOT/user-code requirements are: the π+π− recoil mass must lie in [3.091,3.103] GeV/c² to select the J/ψ; events with any γγ mass in [0.10,0.16] (π0), [0.50,0.60] (η), or [0.90,1.00] GeV/c² (η′) are vetoed; and J/ψ→γe+e− background is rejected by requiring the total number of MDC hits within five EMC crystals around each photon center to be <40.
