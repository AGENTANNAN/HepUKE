On the 4.130–4.230 GeV energy-scan points (eight points, total 7.33 fb⁻¹) real data and matching inclusive MC, study e+e− → Ds*+ Ds− with the Ds*+ decaying electromagnetically as Ds*+ → e+e− Ds+, generating 200k exclusive-MC events for this signal mode. The analysis is tag-based, with one Ds reconstructed in eleven tag modes (K_S K, K K π, K_S K π0, K K π π0, K_S K π π, π π π, π η, π π0 η, π η'(π+π−η), π η'(γρ0), K π π); tag tracks require |Vz| < 10 cm, Vxy < 1 cm, |cosθ| < 0.93 and K/π separation from MDC dE/dx plus TOF, with the tag Ds mass in [1.85, 2.06] GeV/c². Intermediate states are selected via K_S → π+π− ([0.487, 0.511] GeV/c², decay length > 2σ, |Vz| < 20 cm), π0 → γγ ([0.115, 0.150] GeV/c², 1C mass-constrained fit), η → γγ ([0.490, 0.580] GeV/c², 1C fit), ρ0 → π+π− ([0.570, 0.970] GeV/c²), η' → π+π−η ([0.943, 0.973] GeV/c²) or η' → γρ0 ([0.946, 0.970] GeV/c²), with extra tag π/π0 required above 100 MeV/c and a π+π− K_S veto in [0.468, 0.528] GeV/c² for the K π π and π π π modes. On the signal side require one e+ and one e− with net charge 0 and momentum < 200 MeV/c because of the small Ds*+ → e+e− Ds+ phase space, identified by dE/dx-only likelihoods (L(e)>0, L(e)>L(K), L(e)>L(π)), with a γ-conversion veto from the e+e− vertex (Rxy < 2.0 cm) and a minimum photon angle of 10°. The tag Ds recoil mass must lie in the energy-dependent window (~[2.04, 2.22] GeV/c²) and the e+e−Ds recoil mass in (1.93, 2.03) GeV/c², and when several candidates survive per mode the one whose e+e−Ds recoil mass is closest to the Ds nominal mass is retained. Finally a 4C kinematic fit constraining the tag, the e+e− pair and the missing Ds to the measured centre-of-mass four-momentum is applied with χ² < 200 (loose, tighter cuts downstream), storing the fitted momenta.
