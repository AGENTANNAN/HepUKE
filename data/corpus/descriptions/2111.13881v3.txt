At ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → π+π− J/ψ with J/ψ → e+e−e+e−, e+e−μ+μ−, and μ+μ−μ+μ−. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the three J/ψ decay modes. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least six tracks, and net charge zero. Two oppositely charged soft pions with p<0.45 GeV/c form the π+π− J/ψ tag, retaining the combination whose recoil mass against π+π− is closest to the J/ψ mass within a 5σ window (σ≈4.0 MeV for 4e and 2.8 MeV for 2e2μ). PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, treating high-momentum tracks above 1.0 GeV/c as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV) and applying π/K separation; muon candidates require prob(μ)>prob(e), prob(K), EMC energy 0.1–0.3 GeV, and muon-counter depth requirements, while electron candidates require prob(e)>prob(μ), prob(K). A photon-conversion veto uses R_xy and Δp=(p_e_high−p_e_low)>1.0 GeV/c, and simulated tracks are helix-corrected before fitting. Perform nominal 4C kinematic fits with χ²<200 for π+π−e+e−e+e−, π+π−e+e−μ+μ−, and π+π−μ+μ−μ+μ−; for the latter two also fit the competing e+e−π+π− or π+π−π+π− hypothesis and keep events where the signal χ² is smaller, with two peaking π+π−π+π− background events in the 4μ mode accounted for at ROOT level.
