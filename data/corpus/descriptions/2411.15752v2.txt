Measure e+e− → K_S^0 K_S^0 ψ(3686) with ψ(3686) → J/ψ + X (X unobserved) and J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−, K_S^0 → π+π−, identifying the ψ(3686) via the K_S^0 K_S^0 recoil-mass spectrum. Use the eight real-data c.m. energy points 4.682, 4.699, 4.740, 4.750, 4.781, 4.843, 4.918, and 4.951 GeV, together with inclusive MC at each point, and generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC for each of the two J/ψ decay modes, e+e− and μ+μ−, with the ψ(3686) decaying as J/ψ π+π−. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm, with at least one positive and one negative track and at least six tracks in total. Lepton identification treats high-momentum tracks (p>1.2 GeV/c) as leptons, an electron if its EMC energy exceeds 0.6 GeV and otherwise a muon, and exactly one positive and one negative lepton are required; the remaining tracks are assigned as a π+π− pair. A secondary-vertex fit forms a K_S^0 from that π+π− pair by minimizing the mass difference, and all K_S^0 K_S^0 combinations are kept. A kinematic fit to K_S^0 K_S^0 l+l− constrains the l+l− invariant mass to the J/ψ nominal mass and imposes four-momentum conservation, with χ²<200 in a nominal fit, while the missing X system is not constrained. The e+e− and μ+μ− channels share the same selection chain, differing only in the lepton flavour.
