At c.m. energies 4.130–4.230 GeV (8 points, BOSS 705 for 4.130/4.160 and 703 for 4.178–4.230), study the relative branching fraction of D_s^{*+} → D_s^+ π^0 and D_s^{*+} → D_s^+ γ from e+e− → D_s^{*+} D_s^{*−}, using all real data and the matching inclusive MC samples, and generating 500k-event exclusive signal MC per mode from the decay cards. Three D_s decay modes are commissioned: Mode I D_s^+ → K^+K^−π^+ with D_s^− → K^+K^−π^−; Mode II D_s^+ → K^+K^−π^+ with D_s^− → K_S^0 K^−; Mode III D_s^+ → K_S^0 K^+ with D_s^− → K_S^0 K^−, the D_s^* always decaying to D_s γ or D_s π^0 (π^0 → γγ). Event selection requires exactly the charged-track multiplicities of each mode — 2 positive/2 negative/4 total for Modes I and III, 2 positive/3 negative/5 total for Mode II — with particle identification by the probability method at the 0.001 level, K^± separated against π/e and π^± against K/e, plus photons with at least 25 MeV. K_S^0 candidates (one in Mode II, two in Mode III) are formed by secondary-vertex fit with mass window 0.485–0.510 GeV/c² and flight significance above 2, the two-candidate ambiguity being resolved by choosing the pairing with the closest vertices. For each mode a 2C kinematic fit is performed on the D_s^+ and D_s^− daughters with both invariant masses constrained to the nominal D_s mass, χ² cut 200, flagged nominal, the D_s^*→D_sγ versus D_sπ^0 separation coming from M_miss² and the ratio extracted by a simultaneous fit over the eight energies with MC-derived cross-feed transfer factors.
