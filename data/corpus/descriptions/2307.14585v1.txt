Measure Ds+ → μ+νμ with a tag-based analysis at the eight continuum energy points from 4.128 to 4.226 GeV (7.33 fb⁻¹), using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at each point plus a 200k-event exclusive signal sample of e+e− → Ds*± Ds∓ with Ds+ → μ+νμ, Ds− → K+K−π−, and Ds* → Ds γ. Reconstruct the Ds− tag in 14 hadronic modes (K+K−π−, K+K−π−π0, π+π−π−, KS K−, KS K−π0, KS KS π−, KS K−π+π−, ηγγπ−, ηπ+π−π0π−, η'γγπ+π−π−, η'γπ+π−π−, ηγγρ, ηπ+π−π0ρ), two additional modes being unavailable in the tag-mode vocabulary. On the signal side require at least one μ+ candidate with EMC energy between 0 and 0.3 GeV and muon-chamber hit-depth conditions, treat the νμ as missing, and require at least one photon above 25 MeV for the Ds* → Ds γ transition, selected by the minimum |ΔE| method, with the maximum extra-photon energy below 0.3 GeV. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining four-momentum conservation as well as the Ds and Ds* masses, and keep events with χ² < 200. The identical selection chain is applied to the data, inclusive MC, and exclusive signal MC.
