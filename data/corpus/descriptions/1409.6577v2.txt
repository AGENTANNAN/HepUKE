On the 4.23, 4.26 and 4.36 GeV data, search for the neutral Zc(4020)^0 in e+e− → π0π0 h_c, h_c → γ η_c, with η_c decaying through sixteen hadronic modes (p pbar, π+π−K+K−, π+π−p pbar, K+K−K+K−, 2π+2π−, 3π+3π−, 2π+2π−K+K−, K_S K π, K_S K πππ, K+K−π0, K+K−η, p pbar π0, π+π−η, π+π−π0π0, 2π+2π−η, and 2π+2π−π0). Use the real data and inclusive MC at all three energies, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the sixteen modes at each energy point. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, mode-dependent positive/negative multiplicities (1+1, 2+2 or 3+3) and net charge zero, while photons must have E>25 MeV in the barrel or >50 MeV in the endcap (TDC 0–14) and number at least 5, 7 or 9 depending on the η_c mode. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating π, K and p, with particle counts matched to the final state. At least two π0 are reconstructed from γγ with a mass-constrained fit (χ²<200); modes containing η or K_S0 also reconstruct η→γγ or K_S0→π+π− through a secondary vertex. A 4C kinematic fit to the nominal four-momentum is then applied, choosing the best photon/π0/η combination by minimizing the composite χ², with χ²<30 for modes containing only charged tracks or K_S0 and χ²<25 for modes with π0/η. The η_c signal window is |M−m_ηc|<35 MeV/c² via the γπ0π0 recoil mass in [2.945,3.015] GeV/c², the h_c window is the π0π0 recoil mass in [3.51,3.55] GeV/c², with sidebands; preselection keeps γπ0π0 combinations in [3.3,3.7] GeV/c² (h_c) and [2.8,3.2] GeV/c² (η_c), and the larger of the two π0 recoil-mass values is retained for the Zc fit.
