At √s = 4.600 GeV (567 pb⁻¹), search for Λc+ → Σ+η, Σ+η′, Σ+π0, and Σ+ω, with Σ+ → pπ0, η → γγ, η′ → π+π−η, ω → π+π−π0 (Dalitz), and π0 → γγ. Use the 4.600 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate four 500k-event exclusive MC samples, one per decay mode. All charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; photons must have energy above 25 MeV (barrel) or 50 MeV (endcap), an EMC TDC time in 0–14, an opening angle >10° with any charged track, and at least four photons are required. Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 threshold, identifying protons against kaons and pions and pions against kaons and protons; modes (a) Λc+ → Σ+η and (c) Λc+ → Σ+π0 require at least one p and one p̄ with net charge zero, while (b) Λc+ → Σ+η′ and (d) Λc+ → Σ+ω require at least two positive and one negative track with net charge +1, including ≥1 p, ≥1 π+, and ≥1 π−, with proton/pion candidate overlap removed. Photon pairs are kinematically constrained to the nominal π0 mass and, in (a) and (b), also to the η mass, each with χ²<200, requiring the appropriate numbers of π0 (≥1 in (a), ≥2 in (c) and (d)) and η (≥1 in (a),(b)); the full final state (p π0 plus η, π+π−η, π0, or π+π−π0) then undergoes a 4C kinematic fit with χ²<200. In (a) and (c) an antiproton recoiling against the Λc+ is required to suppress combinatorial background, (c) additionally vetoes K_S0 via |M(π0π0) − m_K_S0| > 0.02 GeV/c², and ΔQ windows of about [−0.032, 0.022] (a), [−0.030, 0.020] (b), [−0.050, 0.030] (c), and [−0.030, 0.020] (d) GeV are applied.
