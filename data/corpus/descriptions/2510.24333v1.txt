At the J/ψ peak (√s = 3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → Λ anti-Λ, with Λ → n π0 and anti-Λ → anti-p π+, π0 → γγ, using the (10087±44)×10⁶ J/ψ real data sample and its inclusive MC, together with a 100k-event exclusive signal MC generated with exactly this decay chain. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and at least one positive and one negative track; PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions and requiring at least one proton, with candidates having proton multiplicity ≤ negative-track multiplicity removed and remaining positive tracks assigned as π+ and negative as π−. A secondary-vertex fit on the proton and pion builds the anti-Λ by minimizing the mass difference, removing the used particles, while photons are selected with EMC TDC 0–14, energy thresholds of 25 MeV (barrel) and 50 MeV (endcap) and at least two photons, followed by a Kalman fit on the γγ pair constraining the invariant mass to the π0 nominal value (χ²<100) and requiring at least one π0. Because the neutron is undetected, a partial reconstruction treats it as missing (the decay-card entry for Λ → n π0), choosing the best anti-Λ combination by mass near 1.115683 GeV and requiring the recoil mass in 0.926–0.957 GeV. After the BOSS chain, a BDT classifier (BDT > 0.15) suppresses neutron backgrounds and the neutron momentum is inferred as P_n = P_Λ − P_π0, with the Λ mass region 0.926–0.957 GeV/c² used for the CP asymmetry measurement.
