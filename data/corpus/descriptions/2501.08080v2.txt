Search for the FCNC decay J/ψ → D0 μ+μ−, tagging the D0 in three modes: D0 → K−π+, D0 → K−π+π0, and D0 → K−π+π+π−. Use the 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for J/ψ → D0 μ+μ− (D0 → K−π+) as the signal. On the tag side, reconstruct a D0 from pre-stored candidates in any of the three modes; on the signal side require one μ+ and one μ− with net charge zero. Perform a 5C kinematic fit (four-momentum conservation plus the D0 nominal-mass constraint), with χ² < 200. Additional cuts: D0 mass windows [1.84, 1.89] GeV/c² for modes I and III and [1.80, 1.91] GeV/c² for mode II, χ²_D0 < 4.5, 3.1, and 3.7 respectively, muon identification via EMC energy in [0.11, 0.25] GeV, missing momentum |p_miss| < 0.05 GeV/c, and vetoes against K_S0, four-pion, and recoil-mass backgrounds. The signal region is M(D0 μ+μ−) ∈ [3.05, 3.15] GeV/c², with yields extracted from a fit to that distribution and Bayesian upper limits.
