Measure the absolute branching fraction of inclusive Λc+ → K_S^0 X using the double-tag technique at seven c.m. energies (4599.53–4698.82 MeV). Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at each of the seven points, and generate 1M-event exclusive signal MC per point with e+e− → Λc+ anti-Λc- (phase space), Λc+ → K_S^0 X, and the anti-Λc- decaying into eight hadronic modes (anti-p K_S^0, anti-p K+π−, anti-p K_S^0 π0, anti-p K_S^0 π−π+, anti-p K+π−π0, anti-Λ π−, anti-Λ π−π0, anti-Λ π−π+π−), with K_S^0 → π+π− and anti-Λ → anti-p π+; the three Σ0/Σ− tag modes from the paper are dropped as they have no matching tag symbol. Reconstruct the tag side as anti-Λc- via eight hadronic tag modes (charm −1). On the signal side, form K_S^0 → π+π− from tracks not used by the tag, requiring M(π+π−) ∈ [487, 511] MeV, vertex-fit χ² < 100 and decay length L > 2σ_L, choosing the candidate with largest L/σ_L. Apply mode-dependent vetos: anti-Λ via M(anti-p π+) ∉ (1110, 1120) MeV/c² for anti-p K_S^0 π0, anti-p K_S^0 π−π+ and anti-Σ− π−π+, anti-Σ− via M(anti-p π0) ∉ (1170, 1200) MeV/c² for anti-p K_S^0 π0, and K_S^0 via M(π+π−), M(π0π0) ∉ (480, 520) MeV/c² for anti-Λ π−π+π−. Perform a four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ² < 200. ST yields come from the M_BC distribution with an ARGUS background and mode-dependent ΔE windows (best candidate by min |ΔE|), while DT yields come from a simultaneous 2D unbinned fit to M_BC and M(π+π−) across all seven energies, including a 1.8 ± 0.3% K_S^0 efficiency correction and truth-matching R_dp < 0.5.
