On the J/ψ at 3.097 GeV, measure the double-tag process J/ψ → Ξ0 Ξ̄0, where the tag is Ξ̄0 → Λ̄ π0, Λ̄ → p̄ π+, π0 → γγ, and the signal is Ξ0 → γ Σ0, Σ0 → γ Λ, Λ → p π−; α_γ is extracted from the decay angular distribution. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and matching inclusive MC, plus 1M exclusive MC events for the full signal chain (one mode: J/ψ → Ξ0 Ξ̄0 with all subsequent decays). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, loose |Vz|<100 and Vr<10, at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons with TDC 0–14, >10° from any track, barrel/endcap energy >25/50 MeV, and at least four photons. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, p-versus-K/π and π-versus-K/p separation, requiring at least one p, one p̄, one π+, and one π−. Reconstruct Λ→pπ− and Λ̄→p̄π+ via secondary vertex fits minimizing the mass difference; form π0→γγ with a Kalman fit to the nominal π0 mass (χ²<30, at least one π0), then reconstruct the tag Ξ̄0→Λ̄π0 by a secondary vertex fit minimizing the mass difference. Perform a final kinematic fit on Ξ̄0 Λ γ γ with nominal four-momentum constraints, constraining the γΛ invariant mass to the Σ0 nominal mass and applying χ²<200. The dominant Ξ0→Λπ0 background with misidentified photons is evaluated via MC and sidebands.
