Study e+e− → p pbar p pbar (two proton–antiproton pairs) over the 23 c.m. energy scan points spanning 4.009–4.600 GeV. Use the real data at these points together with the matching inclusive MC samples, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for the signal mode e+e− → 2(p pbar) at each of the 23 points, with a ψ(4260) decaying to p+ p− p+ p− in phase space. Event selection: exactly two positive and two negative charged tracks with net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with no photon requirement. Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating protons from kaons and pions, and requires exactly two protons and two antiprotons. A 3C kinematic fit is applied to the four (anti)proton tracks constraining the three-momentum, with χ²<60, flagged as the nominal fit. The same selection chain is applied to the real data, inclusive MC, and exclusive signal MC samples.
