At the J/ψ(3097), search for the lepton-flavor-violating decay J/ψ → e±μ∓. Use 225.3 million J/ψ real data events and inclusive J/ψ MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for the e+μ− final state. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<5 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; veto photons with EMC timing between 0 and 14, energy thresholds of 15 MeV in both barrel and endcap, and requiring more than 20° from any extrapolated track, with zero good photon candidates. PID by the probability method, high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, else muon), requiring one l+ and one l−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit of the two leptons to the J/ψ four-momentum, with χ²<200. Reject cosmic rays by requiring the TOF difference between the two tracks to be <1.0 ns, and require acollinearity <0.9° and acoplanarity <1.4°; the signal region is defined by 0.93 ≤ E_vis/√s ≤ 1.10 and |Σp|/√s ≤ 0.10.
