On ψ(2S), study ψ(2S) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → p pbar η π0, η → γγ, π0 → γγ. Use 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 200k exclusive MC events for the signal mode ψ(2S) → γ χ_cJ → γ p pbar η π0. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; PID by the probability method with 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions, requiring at least one proton and one antiproton. Photons with EMC TDC 0–14, angle to any track >10°, barrel energy >25 MeV, endcap >50 MeV, and at least five photons. Perform two mass-constrained Kalman fits on photon pairs to the π0 and η nominal masses, each with χ²<25 and requiring at least one π0 and one η, followed by a nominal 4C fit to p pbar π0 η with χ²<200. At ROOT level the final selection applies 6C χ²<20 (the 4C plus π0/η mass constraints), a photon-multiplicity veto favoring the 5γ p pbar hypothesis over 4γ/6γ, J/ψ vetoes |RM(η)-m_J/ψ|>7.2 MeV and |M(p pbar η)-m_J/ψ|>21.3 MeV, and mass-window vetoes against γ p pbar η η, p pbar η η, γ p pbar π0 π0, p pbar π0 π0, and γ γ p pbar π0 π0 backgrounds.
