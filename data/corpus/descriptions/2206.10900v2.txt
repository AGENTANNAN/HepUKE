On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686)→Ξ−Ξ̄+ with Ξ−→Λπ−, Λ→pπ− and the charge-conjugate chain Ξ̄+→Λ̄π+, Λ̄→p̄π+, giving the six-charged-track final state p p̄ π− π− π+ π+. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive signal MC events for the full ψ(3686)→Ξ−Ξ̄+ hyperon decay chain. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93 and at least six tracks are selected; no explicit Vz, Vr, or photon selection is encoded. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifies protons against kaons and pions, and requires at least one proton and one antiproton, with the remaining charged tracks assigned as pions. Form Λ→pπ− and Λ̄→p̄π+ candidates, then Ξ−→Λπ− and Ξ̄+→Λ̄π+ candidates using vertex constraints and mass-difference minimization, retaining the best combination by minimal mass differences. Apply Λ and Ξ mass windows of 5 MeV/c² and 8 MeV/c² around their nominal masses, with decay length >0. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to the Ξ−Ξ̄+ system constraining four-momentum to the CMS, with χ²<200.
