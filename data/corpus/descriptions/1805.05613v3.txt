On the J/ψ peak (√s = 3.097 GeV, 1.31×10⁹ events), search for invisible decays of the ω and φ via J/ψ → V η, η → π⁺π⁻π⁰, where the signal appears as a peak in the recoil mass against the η; use the 3.097 GeV real data with inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for the invisible mode, for the visible reference J/ψ → ω η (ω → π⁺π⁻π⁰), and for the visible reference J/ψ → φ η (φ → K⁺K⁻). Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 mm and Vr<10 mm; photons must have E>25 MeV in the barrel and E>50 MeV in the endcap, EMC timing within 700 ns, and an angle to any track greater than 10°. PID uses the probability method (cut 0.001) identifying pions against kaons. In the invisible mode, select exactly two charged tracks (one π⁺, one π⁻, net charge 0) and at least two photons, build a π⁰ from a γγ pair constrained to the nominal π⁰ mass (χ²<25, ≥1 π⁰), and perform a 4C kinematic fit to π⁺π⁻π⁰ with χ²<200. The ω reference mode requires four charged tracks (two π⁺, two π⁻) and at least four photons forming ≥2 π⁰ with the same 4C fit (χ²<200) and mass windows M(ω) ∈ [0.65, 0.98] GeV and M(η) ∈ [0.41, 0.65] GeV; the φ reference mode instead assigns the two positive and two negative tracks as K⁺ and π⁻ (φ→K⁺K⁻ without PID, M(K⁺K⁻) ∈ [0.987, 1.10] GeV) with at least two photons forming ≥1 π⁰. All three modes share the same track, photon, and PID selection chain.
