Search for the isospin-violating decay Y(4260) → J/ψ η π0 at BESIII, with J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−, η → γγ and π0 → γγ. Use real data and inclusive MC at six centre-of-mass energies of 4.009, 4.226, 4.257, 4.358, 4.416 and 4.599 GeV, and generate 100k-event exclusive phase-space MC for each J/ψ lepton mode at every energy point, with ψ(4260) → J/ψ η π0, J/ψ → e+e−/μ+μ− with PHOTOS, and η/π0 → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, and net charge 0; at least four photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC timing 0–14, and at least 5° away from any charged track. Particle ID uses the probability method with probability >0.001, treating high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon), and requires one l+ and one l−. Reconstruct η and π0 from photon pairs with 1C Kalman fits constraining the γγ mass to the nominal η/π0 mass, χ²<25, requiring exactly one η and one π0; then perform a 4C kinematic fit of η π0 l+l− with four-momentum conservation, flagged nominal, χ²<200 as the loose BOSS pass (the published χ²<40 is applied later in ROOT), choosing the photon-pair combination with smallest χ². Both J/ψ decay channels share the same selection chain.
