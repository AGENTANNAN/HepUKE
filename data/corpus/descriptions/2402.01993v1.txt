On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ η′, η′ → π+π− l+l− with l = e or μ, treating the e+e− and μ+μ− final states as two independent modes. Use the J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each mode, η′ → π+π−e+e− and η′ → π+π−μ+μ−, with a VMD model. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero; photons with EMC timing 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, at least one photon, and photon–track opening angle > 15°. PID uses the probability method, high-momentum tracks (p > 1.0 GeV) are treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy > 1.0 GeV, otherwise muon), with π/K separation, requiring 1π+, 1π−, 1l+, 1l−; for the e mode apply a photon-conversion veto on Rxy vs M_BP(e+e−) and Rxy vs Φ_ee. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γπ+π−l+l− with χ² < 200 at BOSS level, using tighter combined 4C+PID χ² < 60 for the e mode and χ²_4C < 25 for the μ mode, and for the μ mode veto the γπ+π−π+π− hypothesis by refitting with the muon tracks assigned as pions and requiring the μμ hypothesis to have the smaller combined χ². Apply η′ mass windows of 0.91–1.0 GeV/c² (e mode) and 0.90–1.0 GeV/c² (μ mode) for the branching-fraction measurement, with narrower 0.945–0.97 and 0.945–0.975 GeV/c² windows for TFF/CPV/ALP, and in the μ mode require |M(μ+μ−) − 0.548| > 0.02 GeV/c² to suppress η′ → π+π−η, η → μ+μ−.
