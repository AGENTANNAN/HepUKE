At √s = 3.097 GeV, search for the baryon-number-violating decay J/ψ → Λ_c+ e− (charge conjugate implied), with Λ_c+ → p K− π+. Use the 1.31×10⁹-event J/ψ real data sample (708_3097) together with the matching inclusive MC, and generate 100k exclusive-MC events for J/ψ → Λ_c+ e−, Λ_c+ → p K− π+ using the same decay card. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero. PID by per-track probability (highest confidence level), with high-momentum tracks above 0.1 GeV treated as leptons and identified as electrons when the associated EMC energy exceeds 0.6 GeV (electron: CL_e>0.001 and CL_e/(CL_e+CL_K+CL_π)>0.8), while other tracks are assigned the highest CL among π, K, p; require exactly one e−, one proton, one K− and one π+. Perform a 4C kinematic fit with the nominal p K− π+ e− mass assignment and χ²<200. At ROOT level the same four tracks are refit under six competing mass hypotheses — pK−π+e−, π⁺π⁻π⁺π⁻, K⁺K⁻K⁺K⁻, π⁺π⁻K⁺K⁻, π⁺π⁻pp̄ and K⁺K⁻pp̄ — and the event is kept only if the p K− π+ e− hypothesis gives the smallest χ²; the Λ_c+ is then required to lie in the p K− π+ mass window (2.27, 2.30) GeV/c².
