Measure the Born cross section for e+e− → p p̄ π0 with π0 → γγ at 20 R-scan energy points from 2.1000 to 3.0800 GeV, using the real data and a 200k-event exclusive ConExc continuum sample (including ISR and vacuum-polarization corrections) generated at every point. For the low-energy points (2.1000–2.2324 GeV) use partial reconstruction: Mode I requires one proton and one antiproton with no photons, charged tracks having |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, the p–p̄ opening angle <173° and |cosθ_recoil|≤0.96, followed by a four-momentum-constrained fit to (p, p̄) with χ²<200; Mode II requires one antiproton plus at least two photons with the missing proton, the same track cuts, photon energy >25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), TDC 0–700, and photon–track angle >30°, followed by a 1C fit to (p̄, γ, γ) with χ²<60. For the higher-energy points (2.3094–3.0800 GeV) use full reconstruction of p p̄ γγ with the same track and photon cuts but photon–track angle >10°, and a 4C fit with χ²<100, selecting the best combination by minimum χ². Charged-particle identification uses the best (probability) method, separating protons and antiprotons from π and K. In Mode I the signal is extracted from a recoil-mass M(p p̄) fit (MC shape convolved with a Gaussian plus a Landau background), while in Mode II and the full reconstruction it is extracted from the M(γγ) π0 mass peak (MC shape convolved with a Gaussian plus a linear background).
