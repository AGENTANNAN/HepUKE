At six c.m. energies (4.01, 4.23, 4.26, 4.36, 4.42 and 4.60 GeV), search for e+e− → γ η_c(1S) with twelve η_c decay channels: π+π−π0π+π−π0, π+π−π0π0, π+π+π−π−η, K+K−π+π−π0, π+π−π+π−, π+π−π+π−π+π−, π+π−η, K±K_S π∓π+π−, K±K_S π∓, K+K−π0, K+K−π+π−, and K+K−π+π+π−π−, with π0/η → γγ and K_S → π+π−. Use the six corresponding real-data sets and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC for every mode at each of the six energy points. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and net charge zero, with charged multiplicities matched to each mode (2/2, 1/1, 3/3, etc.); photons with E>25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap, TDC 0–14, separated from tracks by >10°, and at least 1, 3, or 5 photons depending on the mode. Particle ID uses the probability method, requiring Pπ>1e-5 and PK>1e-5 with PK>Pπ from combined dE/dx and TOF, identifying the required π/K multiplicities; K_S daughters are exempt from the vertex requirements and are built from a secondary-vertex fit to π+π− by minimizing mass difference. Intermediate π0 and η are formed from γγ with mass constraints and χ²<25, requiring at least two π0, one π0, or one η as appropriate, with mass windows 0.107<M(γγ)<0.163 GeV/c² for π0, 0.400<M(γγ)<0.700 GeV/c² for η, and 0.471<M(π+π−)<0.524 GeV/c² for K_S. A final kinematic fit to γ plus the exclusive final state uses four-momentum conservation (4C, plus one 1C per π0 candidate), with χ² cut 200 and χ²/dof optimized in the range 3.0–5.2 separately per channel; the transition photon must not form a π0 with any other cluster, and in π+π−η it must be >17.5° from tracks. The η_c is reconstructed in the recoil mass of the transition photon, with ISR γ_ISR J/ψ and continuum qq̄ backgrounds parameterised.
