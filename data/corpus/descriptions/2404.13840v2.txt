Study e+e− → ω X(3872) and e+e− → γ X(3872) at 4.66, 4.68, 4.70, 4.74, 4.75, 4.78, and 4.84 GeV using real data and inclusive MC at each point; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for four modes: ω X(3872) → ω γ J/ψ, ω χ_c1 → ω γ J/ψ, ω X(3872) → ω π+π− J/ψ, and γ X(3872) → γ π+π− J/ψ, with ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ, J/ψ → ℓ+ℓ− (ℓ=e or μ), and for the χ_c1 mode χ_c1 → γ J/ψ. In all final states require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and photons with E>25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), EMC timing 0–700 ns, at least 10° from any charged track, at least two photons for ωγJ/ψ and at least one for the other modes. Use probability-based PID (prob>0.001) with π/K, e/π, and μ/π separation; tracks with p<1 GeV are assigned as pions and p>1 GeV as leptons, with EMC energy <0.4 GeV for muons and >1.0 GeV for electrons. For the ωγJ/ψ channel, perform a γγ mass-constrained π0 fit (χ²<25), then a nominal 4C fit to π+π−γγγℓ+ℓ− (χ²<200), and apply the ω window [0.74,0.82] GeV, J/ψ window [3.06,3.14] GeV, a ψ(3686) veto rejecting |M(π+π−)−m_ψ(3686)|<0.03 GeV and |M(π+π−J/ψ)−m_ψ(3686)|<0.01 GeV, and require at least one muon candidate with MUC hit depth >30 cm. For ωππJ/ψ use a π0 mass-constrained fit (χ²<25) and a 4C fit to π+π−π+π−ℓ+ℓ−γγ (χ²<200), with ω window [0.75,0.81] GeV and J/ψ window [3.07,3.13] GeV, covering both the five-track (π+π−π±ℓ+ℓ−γγ) and six-track (π+π−π+π−ℓ+ℓ−γ) topologies. For γππJ/ψ, use a 4C fit to π+π−ℓ+ℓ−γ (χ²<200) with J/ψ window [3.08,3.12] GeV.
