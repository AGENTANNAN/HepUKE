Study e+e− → φη, φ → K+K−, η → γγ, across 22 R-scan energy points from 2.00 to 3.08 GeV. Use the 713 real data sets and the matching inclusive MC at all 22 points, and generate a 2.5M-event ConExc exclusive signal MC (mode 23, φη → K+K−γγ) for every energy point. Event selection: exactly two charged tracks with opposite charge (net charge 0), |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; at least two photons with E>70 MeV in both barrel and endcap and EMC time within [0,700] ns of the event start. Identify both tracks as kaons by the probability method against π+/π−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to K+K−γγ with χ² cut 100, nominal, keeping the photon pairing with the smallest χ² when more than two good photons are found. Veto the ISR φ background by rejecting events with χ²(K+K−γ) < χ²(K+K−γγ). Apply the signal region |M(γγ) − m_η| < 30 MeV and 0.98 < M(K+K−) < 1.08 GeV in ROOT, and extract the cross section from the Born formula with efficiencies and radiative corrections iterated using the ConExc MC.
