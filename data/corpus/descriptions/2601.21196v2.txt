At sqrt(s)=3.773 GeV, measure the four semileptonic decays D0 → K− e+ νe, D0 → K− μ+ νμ, D+ → Kbar0 e+ νe and D+ → Kbar0 μ+ νμ (Kbar0 → K_S0 → π+π−) with 20.3 fb−1 of ψ(3770) real data and matching inclusive MC, generating 2 million-event exclusive MC for each signal mode. Using the double-tag technique, the opposite D is fully reconstructed in six hadronic tag modes for each charm: Kπ, Kππ0, K3π, K_S ππ, Kππ0π0, K3ππ0 for the Dbar0, and K+π−π−, K_S π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S π−π0, K_S 3π, KKπ for the D−, with per-tag-mode ΔE windows of roughly ±25–60 MeV and tag M_BC in (1.859, 1.873) GeV (Dbar0) or (1.863, 1.877) GeV (D−). On the signal side require exactly one K− and one e+ (or μ+) with net charge 0 for D0, or one π+, one π− and one e+ (or μ+) with net charge +1 for D+, plus one missing neutrino, photons above 25 MeV, no extra charged tracks, and extra-photon energy below 0.25 GeV. Electron identification uses L_e > 0.001 and L_e > 0.8(L_e+L_π+L_K); muon identification requires L_μ > L_e, L_μ > 0.001, and 0.1 < E_EMC < 0.3 GeV. The Kbar0 → K_S0 → π+π− decay is rebuilt from two opposite-charge tracks without PID, with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 20 cm, vertex χ2 < 100, decay length > 2σ, and M(π+π−) in (0.487, 0.511) GeV. A 4C kinematic fit (including a K_S0 mass constraint on the π+π− pair for the D+ modes) is applied with χ2 < 200, after which M(K− l+) < 1.83 GeV (e) or 1.56 GeV (μ) and M(Kbar0 l+) < 1.84 GeV (e) or 1.59 GeV (μ) vetoes remove misidentified backgrounds, and the yields are obtained from a fit to Umiss = E_miss − |p_miss|c.
