At √s = 3.773 GeV (ψ(3770)) measure the branching fractions of D0 → K_L0 φ, K_L0 η, K_L0 ω and K_L0 η' in a double-tag analysis, where one D is reconstructed as the tag in the K+π−, K+π−π0 or K+π−π+π− final state (both charm assignments, ranked by invariant mass) and the other D0 decays to K_L0 plus the resonance, with the K_L0 treated as the missing particle. Use the 3.773 GeV real data together with the matching inclusive MC, and generate a dedicated signal-side exclusive MC sample for each sub-decay chain: φ→K+K−, η→γγ, η→π+π−π0, ω→π+π−π0, η'→π+π−η (η→γγ), and η'→γρ0 (ρ0→π+π−). The signal side requires 1 K+ with 1 K− for the φ mode, 2 photons for the η→γγ mode, 1 π+ plus 1 π− plus 2 photons for the η→π+π−π0, ω→π+π−π0 and η'→π+π−η modes, and 1 π+ plus 1 π− plus 1 photon for the η'→γρ0 mode, always with the K_L0 missing. Each channel is subjected to a four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ² < 200, adding a nominal-mass constraint on the two-photon invariant mass to the π0, η or η mass where two photons are present; the φ→K+K− and η'→γρ0 channels use the four-momentum constraint only.
