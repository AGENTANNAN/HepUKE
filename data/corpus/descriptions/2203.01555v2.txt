On the ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, measure the doubly Cabibbo-suppressed decay D0 → K+π−π0 and search for D0 → K+π−π0π0, tagging one D0 through its hadronic modes D0 → K+π−, K+π−π0 and K+π−π−π+. Use the 712_3773 real data and inclusive MC, and generate exclusive MC for each signal mode, with ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar, the signal D0 decaying to K+π−π0 or K+π−π0π0, the recoiling D0bar → K+π−, and π0 → γγ, all phase space. On the signal side require one K+, one π−, and 2 photons (π0 mode) or 4 photons (π0π0 mode). Perform a kinematic fit, applying a four-momentum constraint and, for the K+π−π0 channel only, constraining the γγ invariant mass to the nominal π0 mass, with a χ² cut of 200; both signal modes share the same hadronic tag selection chain.
