On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → φφ, φ → K+K−, using the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1,000,000 exclusive MC events for ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → φφ, φ → K+K− with phase-space χ_cJ → φφ decay. Event selection requires exactly four charged tracks with net charge zero, i.e. two K+ and two K−, each with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, and Vr < 1 cm. Kaon identification uses the probability method with a probability cut of 0.001, separating K± from pions. At least one photon is required with E > 25 MeV in the barrel or E > 50 MeV in the endcap, opening angle > 10° from any charged track, and EMC timing between 0 and 700 ns. A 4C kinematic fit to γ K+K−K+K− is performed with χ² < 60, flagged nominal. At ROOT level, the four kaons are paired into two φ candidates by minimizing sqrt[(M1 − mφ)² + (M2 − mφ)²], and if multiple photons survive the fit the one with the smallest χ² is retained. The φ signal region is |M(K+K−) − mφ| < 0.015 GeV/c², with sidebands for background estimation, and the χ_cJ yields are extracted from fits to M(K+K−K+K−) using photon-energy windows for the radiative transitions.
