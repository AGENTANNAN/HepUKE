At √s = 3.773 GeV, using the 712_3773 real data and inclusive MC, study the semileptonic decays D0 → K−π+π−e+νe (tagging D0bar) and D+ → K−π+π0e+νe (tagging D−) with a double-tag method. Generate 1,000,000-event exclusive MC for each channel: ψ(3770)→D0D0bar with D0→K−π+π−e+νe and D0bar tagged in K+π−, K+π−π0, or K+π−π+π−; and ψ(3770)→D+D− with D+→K−π+π0e+νe and D− tagged in K+π−π−, K_S0π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S0π−π0, K_S0π−π+π−, or K+K−π− (K_S0→π+π−). On the signal side require one K−, one π+, one π−, one e+, missing νe, and net charge 0 for D0; and one K−, one π+, one e+, a π0 from photons, missing νe, and net charge +1 for D+; no additional charged tracks are allowed, and no explicit |cosθ|, |Vz|, or Vr cuts appear in this selection. Electron PID uses CL_e>0.001 and CL_e/(CL_e+CL_π+CL_K)>0.8, with hadron suppression E/p−0.05×χ²(e−dE/dx)>0.60 for D0 or >0.53 for D+, and FSR photons within 5° of the e+ are merged. Apply a four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ²<200, select |U_miss|<0.03 GeV (U_miss≡E_miss−|p_miss|), and veto backgrounds with D0 cuts M(K−π+π−π+)<1.80 GeV/c², cosθ(e+,π−)<0.93, cos(νe,γ_extra)<0.78, M(π+π−)<0.31 GeV/c², and |ΔE(tag K+π−, signal π−)|>7 MeV, as well as D+ cuts p(π0)>0.2 GeV/c, M(K−π+π0e+)<1.78 GeV/c², and U'_miss<0.03 GeV; for the D+ channel also constrain p_D = −p̂_Dbar√(E_beam²−m_Dbar²). Downstream amplitude fits, angular asymmetry, and form-factor extraction are noted as ROOT-side only.
