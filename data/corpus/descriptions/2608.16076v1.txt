On the J/ψ(3097), measure the absolute branching fraction of J/ψ → Σ0 Σ̄0 using a double-tag method, with Σ̄0 → Λ̄γ (tag side) and Σ0 → Λ e+e- (Dalitz), and Λ(Λ̄) → pπ-(π+). Use the 708_3097 J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for both the signal (Λ e+e-) and the peaking Σ0 → Λγ double-radiative background. Event selection requires at least three positive and three negative charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93 and |Vz|<30 cm (no Vr cut, given the long Λ flight), and at least one photon with >25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap) energy, shower time within 0–700 ns and more than 10° from any track. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, π/K separation, and high-momentum tracks (p>0.5 GeV/c) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV), requiring exactly 1 p, 1 p̄, 1 l+ and 1 l-, with the remaining tracks assigned as soft π+π-. Reconstruct Λ → pπ- and Λ̄ → p̄π+ via secondary vertex fits that minimize the mass difference, then perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit on γ p p̄ π+ π- l+l- with χ²<200. The single-tag Σ0 mass window |M(Λ̄γ)-M(Σ0)|<15 MeV/c², the Λ mass/vertex cuts (|M(pπ)-M(Λ)|<8 MeV/c², decay length >0, χ²<200) and the electron PID (|Vz|<10 cm, |Vr|<1 cm, P(e)/(P(e)+P(π)+P(K))>0.8) are applied in the downstream ROOT analysis.
