At ψ(3770), search for the CP-forbidden decay D0 anti-D0 → (K_S0 π0)(K_S0 π0), where both D0 and anti-D0 decay to the same CP eigenstate K_S0 π0, using the double-tag method with 20.28 fb^-1 of 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC (712_3773). Generate 500k exclusive MC events for ψ(3770) → D0 anti-D0, D0 → K_S0 π0, anti-D0 → K_S0 π0. Event reconstruction requires one D0 (charm +1) tag and one anti-D0 (charm −1) tag, both in K_S0 π0, with the second tag ranked by invariant mass, and no additional photons on the signal side. A kinematic fit is performed with four-momentum conservation and both tag masses constrained to the nominal D0 mass, with χ² < 200. Signal extraction uses three regions in the 2D M_BC plane: region I with both D0 in the signal window [1.859, 1.873] GeV, region II with one signal and one sideband [1.830, 1.850] or [1.878, 1.900] GeV, and region III with both sideband, giving N_sig = N_I − N_II/2 + N_III/4. No significant signal is observed (N_obs = −19 ± 10), and a 90% C.L. upper limit is set.
