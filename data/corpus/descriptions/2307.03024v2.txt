On the eight energy points from 4.128 to 4.226 GeV, study e+e− → Ds*+ Ds− with Ds*+ → γ/π0 Ds+ and the semileptonic signal Ds+ → K+K−μ+νμ, using the opposite-side Ds− as a tag. Use all eight real-data samples plus inclusive MC at 4.178 GeV (about 40× luminosity), and generate 200k phase-space exclusive MC events per energy point for the K+K−μ+νμ signal mode. Reconstruct the tag-side Ds− in the 14 hadronic modes encoded in the analysis (KKπ, Kππ, πππ, KKππ0, πEρ, ππ0η, KSK−ππ, KSK+ππ, πη, KSK Sπ, πππη, πEππη, KSKπ0, KSK); on the signal side require one K+, one K− and one μ+ with net charge +1 and a missing νμ. Muon candidates must satisfy the combined MDC dE/dx, TOF and EMC likelihood criteria Lμ > LK, Lμ > Le and Lμ > 0.001. Require zero unused charged tracks and zero unused π0 candidates, veto extra photons with Eγ > 0.2 GeV, and reject Ds+ → K+K−π+ background with M(K+K−νμ) > 1.30 GeV/c² and M(K+K−μ+) < 1.75 GeV/c². Apply a 4C kinematic fit imposing energy-momentum conservation with χ² < 200, with the Ds* mass constraint applied at the ROOT level; the Ds* transition γ or π0 is chosen among unused photons by minimum |ΔE| when multiple candidates exist, and the signal is extracted in the missing-mass Umiss variable.
