At √s=3.773 GeV (ψ(3770)), study ψ(3770)→D0 D0bar with a single-tag anti-D0 in three hadronic modes D0bar→K+π−, K+π−π−π+, and K+π−π0, and a semileptonic signal D0→Kbar0 π− e+ νe, Kbar0→K_S0→π+π−. Use the 7.9 fb−1 real data and inclusive MC at 3.773 GeV, plus 500k exclusive MC events for the signal D0→Kbar0 π− e+ νe with Kbar0→K_S0→π+π−. On the signal side require no photons (with E_γ_max<0.25 GeV to suppress π0), exactly one π+, two π−, one e+, and net charge zero, with one missing ν_e. Reconstruct K_S0 from the π+π− pair in the 0.485–0.510 GeV/c² mass window with decay-length significance >2σ, identify the electron by L'_e>0.001 and L'_e/(L'_e+L'_π+L'_K)>0.8 together with E/p>0.7 and bremsstrahlung recovery within 5°, and veto M(Kbar0 π− e+)<1.80 GeV/c² against D0→Kbar0 π+π−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the tag, signal, and neutrino to the c.m. energy, with χ²<200 (loose; tighter at ROOT level). Charged tracks are selected only by multiplicity and net charge; no explicit |cosθ|, |Vz|, or Vr cuts are encoded.
