Study single-tag e+e− → Λc+ Λc− at the seven threshold points 4.600–4.699 GeV, using the matching real data and inclusive-MC samples, and reconstruct the tag side through the hadronic Λc decay mode ΛcP→Kπp with charm −1 (only this one of the paper's ten tag modes is declared). On the signal side reconstruct Λc+ → Λπ+π0 with Λ → pπ− and π0 → γγ, requiring two photons, one p, one π− and one π+ with net charge +1. Generate 1M-event exclusive signal MC for e+e− → Λc+Λc−, Λc+ → Λπ+π0 (Λ → pπ−, π0 → γγ) in phase space, sharing the same selection chain. Charged tracks use the standard quality cuts (|cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm — 20 cm for Λ daughters — and Vr<1 cm), photons require E>25 MeV in the barrel / >50 MeV in the endcap with EMC time within 700 ns, and the π0(γγ) and Λ(pπ−) candidates must fall in [0.115,0.150] GeV and [1.111,1.121] GeV with Λ vertex fit χ²<100 and decay length >2σ. A kinematic fit constrains four-momentum conservation plus the Λ, π0 and tag-Λc+ masses with χ²<200 (the paper's 3C fit is emitted here as an equivalent 7C fit). The signal-side Λc+ is chosen with minimal |ΔE| within [−0.03,0.02] GeV, tag-side mBC and ΔE are stored unconditionally, a Σ0 veto removes events also satisfying the Λc+ → Σ0π+ selection, and the partial-wave analysis of Λc+ → Λπ+π0 (ρ(770)+, Σ(1385)+, Σ(1385)0) is left to the subsequent ROOT stage.
