Study e+e− → η π+ π−, with η → γγ, at the 19 R-scan energy points from 2.000 to 3.080 GeV, using real data and inclusive MC at each point. Generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC with the ConExc generator (mode 37 for e+e− → η π+ π−, η → γγ, ISR included). Select events with exactly two charged tracks and net charge 0, each satisfying |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying both tracks as pions and rejecting kaon/proton hypotheses. Require at least two photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC-EMC in 0–14, and photon–track opening angle >10°. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to π+π−γγ with χ²<100, flagged nominal, and require cosθ_γ<0.95 for each photon in the η helicity frame; the η→γγ signal window is [0.523,0.573] GeV/c² with sidebands [0.478,0.503] and [0.593,0.618] GeV/c², and Bhabha events are suppressed by E/p<0.8.
