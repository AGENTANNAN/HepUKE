On the ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV (7.93 fb⁻¹), study the semileptonic decays D⁺ → K₁(1270)⁰ μ⁺ν_μ with K₁(1270)⁰ → K⁻π⁺π⁰ and D⁰ → K₁(1270)⁻ μ⁺ν_μ with K₁(1270)⁻ → K⁻π⁺π⁻, using single-tag events where the opposite D is reconstructed in six D⁺ tag modes (Kππ, K_Sπ, Kπππ⁰, K_Sππ⁰, K_Sπππ, KKπ) with m_BC = 1.863–1.877 GeV and three D⁰ tag modes (Kπ, Kππ⁰, Kπππ) with m_BC = 1.859–1.873 GeV, each with per-mode ΔE windows. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 1,000,000-event exclusive signal MC for each mode, with ψ(3770) → D⁺D⁻ (D⁺ → K⁻π⁺π⁰ μ⁺ν_μ, D⁻ → K⁺π⁻π⁻, π⁰ → γγ) and ψ(3770) → D⁰D̄⁰ (D⁰ → K⁻π⁺π⁻ μ⁺ν_μ, D̄⁰ → K⁺π⁻), using a dedicated generator based on the corresponding D → Kππ e⁺ν amplitude analysis. Charged tracks are selected with |cosθ|<0.93, |V_z|<10 cm, V_r<1 cm; the D⁺ signal requires one K⁻, one π⁺, one μ⁺, net charge +1, two photons, and a missing ν_μ, while the D⁰ signal requires one K⁻, one π⁺, one π⁻, one μ⁺, net charge 0, and a missing ν_μ. Photons must satisfy the standard barrel (25 MeV) / endcap (50 MeV) energy thresholds; muon identification uses the probability method with CL_μ>0.001, CL_μ>CL_K, CL_μ>CL_π, and EMC deposit <0.28 GeV. Extra-shower vetos require no additional π⁰, E_extra γ <0.20 GeV, and cosθ between the missing momentum and photon <0.69 (D⁺) or <0.54 (D⁰); a four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit is applied with χ²<200. Signal mass windows are M(Kππ⁰μ)<1.72 GeV/c² (D⁺) and M(Kππμ)<1.65 GeV/c² (D⁰), with the K₁(1270) mass window 1.163–1.343 GeV/c²; peaking backgrounds are suppressed by mass and PID cuts.
