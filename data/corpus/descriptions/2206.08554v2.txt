Measure the e+e− → π+π− J/ψ cross section using XYZ and R-scan real data at 40+ energy points from 3.7730 to 4.7008 GeV together with inclusive MC. Two independent J/ψ decay modes are analysed, J/ψ → e+e− and J/ψ → μ+μ−, modelled by π+π−J/ψ phase-space decay cards with PHOTOS and VLL for the leptonic decays. Both modes share the same selection: exactly four charged tracks with net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm. Particle ID uses the probability method, with high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) treated as leptons — electrons if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muons — and pions identified against kaons and protons, requiring one π+, one π−, one l+ and one l−. A 4C kinematic fit to π+π− l+l− is performed with χ²<60 and the nominal flag. Further ROOT-stage requirements include cos(π+,π−)<0.98 (plus cos(π±,e∓)<0.98 in the electron mode), pion dE/dx discriminators (χ²_π+<2.8 and χ²_π−<3.0 for e+e−, χ²_π+<3.2 and χ²_π−<4.10 for μ+μ−), a BDT against two-photon background in the electron mode, and EMC electron-muon separation (>1.1 GeV for electrons, <0.4 GeV for muons). An iterative KKMC-based ISR correction is applied, and the final cross section is the inverse-variance weighted average of the two modes.
