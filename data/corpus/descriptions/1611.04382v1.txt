At √s = 4.600 GeV with 567 pb⁻¹ of real data and the matching inclusive MC, measure the absolute branching fraction of Λ_c⁺ → Λ μ⁺ ν_μ with the single-/double-tag technique: the anti-Λ_c⁻ is fully reconstructed in eleven hadronic modes (p K_S⁰, p K⁻π⁺, p K_S⁰π⁰, p K_S⁰π⁺π⁻, p K⁻π⁺π⁰, p π⁺π⁻, Λπ⁺, Λπ⁺π⁰, Λπ⁺π⁺π⁻, Λπ⁺ω, and Σ⁰π⁺ with Σ⁰ → γΛ), and the signal Λ_c⁺ is sought in the recoil. Generate 500k exclusive signal MC of Λ_c⁺ → Λ μ⁺ ν_μ (phase space) with the anti-Λ_c⁻ decaying inclusively and Λ → pπ⁻. Tag-side selection uses charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, V_xy < 1 cm and |V_z| < 10 cm, π/K separation from dE/dx and TOF confidence levels, EMC photon energy thresholds of 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), a π⁰ mass window 0.110–0.155 GeV/c² with a 1C mass constraint, K_S⁰ mass and positive decay-length requirements, and a Λ mass window, retaining tag candidates in M_BC and mode-dependent ΔE windows (~3σ, spanning −0.025 to 0.062 GeV) with the M_BC signal region 2.280–2.296 GeV/c². On the signal side the Λ is formed from pπ⁻ with a common vertex fit requiring positive decay length and keeping the largest L/σ_L, and the event must contain no photons, exactly one p, one π⁻ and one μ⁺, net charge +1, a minimum photon angle of 10°, and an undetected ν_μ. Muon identification uses MDC dE/dx, TOF and EMC probabilities, requiring L′_μ > 0.001 with L′_μ > L′_e and L′_μ > L′_K. Backgrounds from Λ_c⁺ → Λπ⁺ and Σ⁰π⁺ are removed by M(Λμ⁺) < 2.12 GeV/c², while Λ_c⁺ → Λπ⁺π⁰ is suppressed by the largest unused-photon energy below 0.25 GeV and the muon EMC energy below 0.30 GeV. The kinematic fit constrains four-momentum conservation including the missing neutrino plus the Λ mass over the pπ⁻ pair, with χ² < 200, and the neutrino kinematics is inferred from U_miss = E_miss − |p_miss|c.
