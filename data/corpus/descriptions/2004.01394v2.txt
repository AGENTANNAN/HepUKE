Study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ (J = 0, 1, 2), χ_cJ → Σ− Σ̄+, with Σ− → n π− and Σ̄+ → n̄ π+, using the 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data and the corresponding inclusive MC, plus a 100k-event exclusive MC generated for χ_cJ → Σ− Σ̄+ (J = 0, 1, 2) with the same chain. Event selection requires exactly one positive and one negative charged track with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 30 cm and Vr < 10 cm, and at least one photon (EMC TDC 0–14, energy > 25 MeV barrel / > 50 MeV endcap, more than 10° from any track). Pion identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating π from K and p, and requires one π+ and one π−. The n̄ is taken as the most energetic EMC shower with deposited energy 0.2–2.0 GeV, second moment > 20 cm² and > 20 EMC hits in a 40° cone, while the n is left missing. Before the fit, vetoes reject γγ pairs within 12 MeV of the π0 mass, π+π− within 10 MeV of K_S0, recoil π+π− within 10 MeV of J/ψ, and any competing Σ−Σ̄+ hypothesis with smaller χ² than the γΣ−Σ̄+ hypothesis; the radiative photon must have energy > 80 MeV and be more than 40° from the n̄ and 10° from tracks. A kinematic fit to ψ(3686) → γ n̄ π+ π− (n missing), constraining the n̄π+ mass to the Σ̄+ nominal value and requiring χ² < 20, selects the best combination by minimum χ².
