On the ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, study D+ → K_S^0 π+ π0 π0 with K_S^0 → π+π- and π0 → γγ, using the double-tag technique. Use the 3.773 GeV real data (2.93 fb⁻¹) and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for the D+ → K_S^0 π+ π0 π0 signal mode. The tag side selects six hadronic D- modes — K+π-π-, K+π-π-π0, K_S^0 π-, K_S^0 π-π0, K+K-π-, K+π-π-π- — while the signal side requires one π+, four photons (from two π0) and net charge +1. A kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation and M(γγ) constrained to the nominal π0 mass is applied with χ² < 200; for the amplitude analysis (restricted to the D- → K+π-π- tag) a 4C fit constraining the D+, K_S^0 and π0 masses is used, together with tag/signal ΔE windows of [-25,20] MeV and [-40,20] MeV, a K_S^0 veto rejecting M(π0π0) in [0.46,0.52] GeV/c², and a D0D0bar wrong-combination veto on M_BC outside [1.862,1.870] GeV, yielding 1458 events at 96.86% purity. The branching fraction is obtained from a two-dimensional M_BC^sig versus M_BC^tag fit across the six tag modes with K_S^0 sideband subtraction.
