Study e+e− → K+K−J/ψ with J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ− at 28 center-of-mass energies from 4.127 to 4.600 GeV, using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC samples (available at 4.127, 4.226, 4.436 and 4.600 GeV); generate 300k-event exclusive MC for each lepton mode at 4.226, 4.436 and 4.600 GeV, with J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−, weighting the PHSP, f0(980)J/ψ and f2(1270)J/ψ intermediate components. Reconstruct partially: identify the e+e− or μ+μ− pair plus at least one K+, inferring the missing K− through a 1C kinematic fit constrained to the kaon mass. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and at least three tracks; lepton identification uses the probability method (C.L. > 0.001), with high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) treated as leptons (E/p>0.8 for electrons, and muon-counter penetration >40 cm for at least one muon), while kaons are selected against pions with C.L.(K)>0.001. Apply a kinematic fit to the e+e− K+ (or μ+μ− K+) system with a four-momentum constraint, χ² cut 200 and, for the partial reconstruction, combined vertex-plus-1C χ²<20, choosing the K+ candidate with minimum χ². Remove radiative Bhabha background by requiring all opposite-charge track pairs to have opening-angle cosθ<0.98, and define the J/ψ signal as M(l+l−) within 2.5 times the resolution, with sidebands shifted by 0.01 GeV.
