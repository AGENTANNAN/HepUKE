At a center-of-mass energy of 3.773 GeV, measure the integrated luminosity from large-angle Bhabha scattering e+e− → e+e−, using the three ψ(3770) data periods (total ~20.3 fb−1) with run-by-run calibrated E_cm. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive Bhabha MC for e+e− → e+e− (phase space) with the Babayaga@NLO cross section. Event selection: exactly two charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge 0 (at least one positive and one negative); photon candidates defined by 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) thresholds and a >10° separation from tracks, but with no explicit photon requirement, to veto radiative Bhabha events with hard photons. PID is by the probability method (cut 0.001), treating high-momentum tracks (p>0.5) as leptons and identifying electrons via E/p>0.8, requiring at least one e+ and one e−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to the e+e− hypothesis constraining four-momentum, with χ² cut 200 (loose, tightened later in the ROOT analysis).
