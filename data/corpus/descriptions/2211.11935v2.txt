On ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV (448M events), search for η_c(2S) → π+π−η via ψ(3686) → γ η_c(2S), with η → γγ and the M1 photon recoiling against the η_c(2S). Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus 100k-event exclusive MC for the signal mode η_c(2S) → π+π−η. Event selection: exactly two charged tracks with net charge 0, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; at least three photons with energy >25 MeV (barrel) / >40 MeV (endcap), angle to any track >10°, and EMC time in [0,700] ns; PID by the probability method (prob>0.001) identifying pions against kaons and protons, requiring one π+ and one π−. Form η candidates from photon pairs via a 1C mass-constrained fit to the nominal η mass (χ²<200, at least one η), then perform a 5C kinematic fit to η γ π+π− with four-momentum conservation (χ²<200), where the M1 photon is the one not used in the η. A modified 4C fit excluding the M1 photon energy is used for the final M(π+π−η) spectrum, with a competing-hypothesis veto requiring χ²_5C(3γ) < χ²_5C(2γ) and < χ²_5C(4γ), and a J/ψ veto M(γ_M1 π+π−) ≤ 3.00 GeV/c², both applied in the ROOT stage.
