On the ψ(2S) (1.06×10⁸ events), study ψ(2S) → p p̄ η with η → γγ. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive ψ(2S) MC, plus 42.6 pb⁻¹ of continuum data at 3.65 GeV for the QED background estimate, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for ψ(2S) → p p̄ η, η → γγ in phase space. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<2 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track with net charge zero; PID by the probability method (probability cut 0.001) separating protons from pions/kaons, requiring one proton and one antiproton. Photons require EMC timing 0–14, energy >25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) and >50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), at least two photons, and isolation of at least 10° from the proton and 30° from the antiproton track. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to the p p̄ γγ hypothesis with χ²<20, flagged nominal, repeated over all photon permutations with the minimum χ² chosen. Additional cuts applied after the fit: proton and antiproton momenta >300 MeV/c (data/MC disagreement at low momentum), M(p p̄)<3.067 GeV/c² and M(p p̄)<3.1 − 0.75·M(γγ) GeV/c² to reject ψ(2S)→η J/ψ and γχ_cJ, and an η mass window |M(γγ)−M_η|<21 MeV/c², with sidebands at 0.43 and 0.65 GeV and inclusive ψ(2S) MC used to estimate the non-η and residual backgrounds.
