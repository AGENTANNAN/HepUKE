On the ψ(2S) resonance at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(2S) → π+π− J/ψ with J/ψ → γ η_c and η_c → γγ. Use the 709_3686 real data and inclusive MC; generate 200k exclusive MC events for ψ(2S) → π+π− J/ψ, J/ψ → γ η_c, η_c → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; PID by the probability method (cut 0.001) identifying pions against electrons, requiring at least one π+ and one π−; photons with TDC 0–14, angle to any track >10°, energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), at least three photons. Perform a 4C kinematic fit (π+π−γγγ) with four-momentum constraint, χ² cut 200, flagged nominal. At ROOT level, apply a lepton veto rejecting events where any pion track has L(e)/(L(e)+L(π)) > 0.8; require the π+π− recoil mass within 0.018 GeV/c² of m_J/ψ; select the γγ pair whose invariant mass is closest to m_ηc as the η_c, the remaining photon being the radiative photon; veto two-photon invariant masses in [0.10,0.16] (π0), [0.48,0.59] (η), and [0.88,1.10] (η′) GeV/c²; apply a final χ²_4C < 19; and restrict photon multiplicity to ≤4.
