On the BESIII scan from 4.236 to 4.600 GeV, study e+e− → η ψ(2S) with ψ(2S) → π+π− J/ψ and J/ψ → l+l− (e or μ), η → γγ. Use the 14 real-data energy points and inclusive MC at 4.258 GeV, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC at each energy point following the decay chain (η ψ(2S), ψ(2S) → π+π− J/ψ, J/ψ → e+e− with PHOTOS). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly four tracks with two positive and two negative; photons with 25 MeV energy threshold, more than 10° from any track, at least two. PID uses the probability method, high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) are treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >1.0 GeV, else muon) with π/K separation, requiring 1 π+, 1 π−, 1 l+, 1 l−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γγπ+π−l+l− with χ²<40, nominal. Mass windows applied downstream in ROOT: J/ψ 3064.6–3140.8, η 507.1–579.1, ψ(2S) 3680.3–3692.5 MeV/c², with an η′ → π+π−η veto (M(π+π−γγ)>1.0 GeV/c²) and, for multiple photons, retention of the combination with smallest χ²_4C.
