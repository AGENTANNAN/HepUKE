At seven energy points from 4.5995 to 4.6988 GeV, study e+e− → Λc+ anti-Λc− with Λc+ → p π0, π0 → γγ (signal) and Λc+ → p η, η → γγ (reference), the anti-Λc− left inclusive; use the real data and inclusive MC at all seven points, and generate 50k-event exclusive MC for each of the two decay modes. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least one positive track; photons require EMC time 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV or endcap >50 MeV, at least two photons, and angle to any track >10°. PID uses the probability method with probability >0.001, identifying protons against kaons and pions and requiring at least one proton; after a proton-versus-charged-pion momentum-ordering removal, the remaining positive and negative tracks are assigned as π+ and π−, and isolated photons must have angle to the primary track >20° with at least two such photons. Helix corrections are applied to all charged tracks before fitting, and a Particle Transformer DNN (20-model ensemble, iterative weighting, trained jointly for pη with pπ0) must give score >0.95, using all charged tracks and isolated showers not associated with the Λc+ candidate. The two photons are first fit with a 1C constraint to the nominal π0 (or η) mass, χ²<200, requiring at least one π0 (or η); then the proton plus π0 (or η) is fit with a 4C four-momentum constraint, χ²<200. Both modes share the same selection chain except for the π0/η mass constraint.
