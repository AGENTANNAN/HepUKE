On the J/ψ at √s = 3.097 GeV, measure the branching fraction of J/ψ → γ η in two independent decay modes treated as separate selection chains: η → π+π−π0 (Mode I) and η → 3π0 (Mode II), with every π0 → γγ. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1M-event exclusive MC for each mode (J/ψ → γη via VSP_PWAVE, η → π+π−π0 or 3π0 phase space, π0 → γγ). For Mode I, require exactly two charged tracks of net charge zero with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, at least one π+ and one π−, identified against kaons by the probability method (prob cut 0.001); require at least three photons with E > 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), EMC timing in 0–14 (×50 ns) and photon–track opening angle > 10°. Reconstruct one π0 from a γγ pair by a 1C Kalman fit to the π0 mass with χ² < 25, take the highest-energy remaining photon as the radiative γ, and perform a 6C kinematic fit on γ π+π−π0 (4C plus π0 and η mass constraints, χ² < 80, flagged nominal), with ROOT-level vetoes against J/ψ → 3γ and 4γ. For Mode II, require no charged tracks and at least seven photons with the same energy/timing/opening-angle criteria, build at least three π0 from γγ pairs (1C Kalman fit, χ² < 25, each with |cosθ_decay| < 0.95), take the highest-energy remaining photon as the radiative γ, and perform an 8C kinematic fit on γ π0π0π0 (4C plus three π0 and η mass constraints, χ² < 70, flagged nominal). Signal yields are obtained from fits to the η mass region [0.50, 0.60] GeV/c².
