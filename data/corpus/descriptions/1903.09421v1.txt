At √s = 2.396 GeV, study e+e− → Λ anti-Λ with Λ → p π− and anti-Λ → anti-p π+. Use the 2.396 GeV real data (66.9 pb−1) and matching inclusive MC, plus 500k exclusive MC events of e+e− → Λ anti-Λ with the same Λ/anti-Λ decays. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; no photon is required. PID uses the probability method (cut 0.001) identifying protons against pions, requiring at least one proton and one antiproton, with the remaining positive/negative tracks assigned as π+/π−, and p<0.2 GeV/c tracks treated as pions while p>0.2 GeV/c tracks are treated as protons/antiprotons. The Λ (p π−) and anti-Λ (anti-p π+) are formed by secondary-vertex fits that minimize the mass difference to the nominal Λ mass, with no explicit Λ mass window applied beyond this. A 4C kinematic fit to the Λ anti-Λ system constrains four-momentum with χ² < 50 and is flagged nominal.
