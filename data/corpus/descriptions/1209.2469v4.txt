On the J/ψ(3097) resonance, study J/ψ → φ η and J/ψ → φ η′, with φ → K+K−, for both the invisible decays η/η′ → invisible (the η/η′ escapes undetected and is inferred from the mass recoiling against the φ) and the normalisation channels η/η′ → γγ. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of seven modes: J/ψ→φη with η undecayed, J/ψ→φη′ with η′ undecayed, J/ψ→φη, η→γγ, J/ψ→φη′, η′→γγ, and backgrounds J/ψ→γη_c, η_c→K+π−K_L0, J/ψ→φK_L0K_L0, and J/ψ→φf_0(980), f_0(980)→K_L0K_L0. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold (100 MeV in the barrel/endcap transition region), TDC 0–14, isolated by >10° from all tracks, and at least two photons for the γγ modes. PID by the probability method with probability >0.001, separating kaons from pions/protons, requiring one K+ and one K−. For the invisible modes, reconstruct only the φ→K+K− tag and require the recoil mass against the φ to be 0.495–0.601 GeV (η, ±3σ with σ=17.8 MeV) or 0.80–1.20 GeV (η′); additional ROOT-level cuts are N_shower=0 within a 1 rad cone around the recoil direction, |cosθ_recoil|<0.7, and 1.01<m(K+K−)<1.03 GeV. For the γγ modes, perform a 4C kinematic fit to J/ψ→K+K−γγ with χ²<200 at selection level (optimal χ²<90 for η and <40 for η′ applied later), using mass windows 0.99<m(K+K−)<1.10 GeV and 0.35<m(γγ)<0.75 GeV for η or 0.75<m(γγ)<1.15 GeV for η′. The charged-track, photon, and PID criteria are common to all four chains.
