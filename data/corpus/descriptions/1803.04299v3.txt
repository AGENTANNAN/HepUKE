At E_CMS = 4.5995 GeV (√s ≈ 4.6 GeV), measure the absolute branching fractions of Λc+ → Ξ0 K+ and Λc+ → Ξ(1530)0 K+ with the double-tag technique, using 567 pb⁻¹ of real data and inclusive MC at 4.6 GeV, plus 200k-event exclusive MC for each signal mode (ψ(4260) → Λc+ anti-Λc−, Λc+ → Ξ0 K+ or Ξ(1530)0 K+, Ξ0 → Λ0 π0, Λ0 → p π−, π0 → γγ, anti-Λc− → anti-p K_S0, K_S0 → π+ π−). Reconstruct the ST anti-Λc− tag in 10 hadronic modes (pbar K_S0, pbar K+ π−, pbar K_S0 π0, pbar K_S0 π− π+, pbar K+ π− π0, pbar π− π+, Λbar π−, Λbar π− π0, Λbar π− π+ π−, Σbar0 π−; the Σbar− π0 and Σbar− π− π+ modes have no available channel symbols). Charged tracks require |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm; PID is by likelihood ratios (protons L(p)>L(K) and L(p)>L(π), kaons L(K)>L(π), pions L(π)>L(K)); photons require E > 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) with EMC time 0–700 ns; π0 candidates use a 115–150 MeV/c² window with a 1C mass-constraint fit; K_S0/Λ vertices require fit χ² < 100 and decay vertex > 2σ from the IP, with K_S0 mass 487–511 MeV/c² and Λbar mass 1111–1121 MeV/c². Use mode-dependent ΔE windows (~3σ, spanning roughly −50 to +30 MeV) and extract the ST yield from mBC in 2.282–2.291 GeV/c². On the signal side require exactly one K+ opposite the tag, total signal-side charge +1, and no multiple DT candidates, inferring a missing Ξ0 (1.31486 GeV/c²) or Ξ(1530)0 (1.5318 GeV/c²) from the missing mass; a kinematic fit constraining four-momentum is applied with χ² < 200, and both signal modes share the same selection chain.
