On the J/ψ(3097), search for the ΔS=ΔQ violating decay Ξ⁰ → Σ⁻ e⁺ ν_e with a double-tag technique: the single-tag side is anti-Ξ⁰ → anti-Λ π⁰, anti-Λ → anti-p π⁺, π⁰ → γγ, while the double-tag Ξ⁰ → Σ⁻ e⁺ ν_e, Σ⁻ → n π⁻ leaves the neutron and neutrino undetected. Use the 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, plus an exclusive signal MC generated with the full J/ψ → Ξ⁰ anti-Ξ⁰ chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, a minimum 10° separation from any track, and EMC shower times within [0, 700] ns. Reconstruct Λ → p π⁻ and anti-Λ → anti-p π⁺ by secondary vertex fits, keeping candidates with L/σ_L > 2 and |M_pπ − m_Λ| < 5 MeV/c². Form π⁰ → γγ with a Kalman kinematic fit (χ² < 25), requiring M_γγ ∈ (115, 150) MeV/c² and rejecting events with both photons in the endcap; combine into Ξ⁰/anti-Ξ⁰ → Λ π⁰ with |M_Λπ⁰ − m_Ξ⁰| < 20 MeV/c², choosing the candidate of smallest |ΔM|. On the double-tag side require exactly two remaining tracks of opposite charge, identified by the probability method as one π⁻ (likelihood L(π) > 0.001 and greater than L(K) and L(p)) and one e⁺ (L'(e) > 0.001 with L'(e)/(L'(e)+L'(π)+L'(K)) > 0.8), with χ_dE/dx(e as π) < −4.5, χ_dE/dx(π as e) < −2.5, p(π⁻) ∈ (0.20, 0.38) GeV/c and p(e⁺) < 0.20 GeV/c. Both n and ν_e are treated as missing, and the double-tag yield is extracted from an M_BC fit on the single-tag side.
