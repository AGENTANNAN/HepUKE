At √s = 3.773 GeV on the ψ(3770), test lepton universality in D0 → K−π0μ+νμ using an opposite-side anti-D0 tag (charm −1). Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, together with a 500k-event exclusive MC sample generated from ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar phase space. Tag the anti-D0 in the hadronic modes K+π−, K+π−π0, K+π+π−π−, KS0π+π−, K+π−π−π0 and K+π+π−π−π0. On the signal side require two photons from π0 → γγ, one K− and one μ+, net charge zero, and an unconstrained missing νμ. A kinematic fit imposes 4-momentum conservation on the tag plus signal (including the neutrino) at the centre-of-mass energy and constrains the two photons to the nominal π0 mass, with χ² < 200. Apply the same selection to real data, inclusive MC and the exclusive signal MC.
