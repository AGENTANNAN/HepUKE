Using 1.31×10⁹ J/ψ events at √s = 3.097 GeV (real data plus inclusive MC), measure the Dalitz-plot matrix elements of η→π⁺π⁻π⁰ and η/η′→3π⁰ through J/ψ→γη and J/ψ→γη′; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the three modes — η→π⁺π⁻π⁰ (η Dalitz decay), η→3π⁰, and η′→3π⁰ — all as J/ψ→γη(′) with π⁰→γγ. For the η→π⁺π⁻π⁰ channel, select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, requiring exactly one positive and one negative track with net charge zero, and photons with EMC timing 0–14, an angle of at least 10° from any track, energy above 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), at least three photons; the most energetic photon is taken as the radiative photon and the remaining two are combined into the π⁰. Perform a nominal 6C kinematic fit to γγγπ⁺π⁻ with four-momentum conservation, m(γγ)=m(π⁰) and m(3γπ⁺π⁻)=m(η), χ²<80, and discard events whose signal χ² exceeds that of the competing 4C hypotheses π⁺π⁻4γ or π⁺π⁻2γ. For the two 3π⁰ channels, require no charged tracks and at least seven photons with the same energy and timing cuts, first pairing photons into π⁰ candidates with a 1C fit to m(γγ)=m(π⁰) (χ²<25, at least three π⁰, and each π⁰ satisfying |cosθ_decay|<0.95 in its γγ rest frame), then applying a nominal 7C fit to γπ⁰π⁰π⁰ with four-momentum conservation plus m(3π⁰)=m(η) or m(η′) and χ²<70 to improve the Z-variable resolution. In the η′→3π⁰ channel, additionally veto the J/ψ→γηπ⁰π⁰ background by discarding events whose alternative 7C fit under that hypothesis gives a smaller χ², reject any event with |m(γπ⁰)−m(ω)|<0.05 GeV/c² for the radiative photon or with any γγ pair within 0.03 GeV/c² of m(η).
