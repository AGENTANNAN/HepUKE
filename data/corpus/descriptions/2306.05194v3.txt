We measure Ds+ → η e+νe and Ds+ → η′ e+νe with the double-tag method at eight c.m. energies from 4.128 to 4.226 GeV (total 7.33 fb−1), using real data plus inclusive MC and a 500k-event exclusive MC sample for Ds+ → η(′)e+νe, where η→γγ or π0π+π− and η′→ηπ+π− or γρ0. The Ds− tag side uses 14 hadronic modes (e.g. K+K−π−, K+π−π−, π+π−π−, K+K−π−π0, K_S0π+π−π−) with charged tracks required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vxy<1 cm, kaon/pion separation by likelihood ratios L_K>L_π (kaons) and L_π>L_K (pions), K_S0→π+π− within |M−M(K_S0)|<12 MeV/c2, and π0/η→γγ mass windows, with ST yields from M_BC cuts per energy point and M_tag fits. On the signal side we require one positron, three photons (η→γγ plus a transition γ), and a missing neutrino, with positron identification CL_e>0.001 and CL_e/(CL_e+CL_π+CL_K)>0.8; the transition γ/π0 is chosen with the smallest |ΔE|, and extra energy and track vetoes are applied. A 3C kinematic fit enforces energy-momentum conservation plus Ds/Ds* mass constraints with χ²<200 (for the η′→γρ0 mode), after which we require M(η(′)e+)<1.9 GeV/c2 and extract the signal via a simultaneous M_miss^2 fit for the η and η′ channels.
