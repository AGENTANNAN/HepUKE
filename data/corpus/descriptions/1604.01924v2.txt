Measure the J/ψ leptonic width via initial-state radiation at √s = 3.773 GeV (ψ(3770)), studying e+e− → J/ψ γ → μ+μ− γ with an untagged ISR photon. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and matching inclusive MC, plus a 100k-event exclusive MC sample of e+e− → μ+μ− γ generated with PHOKHARA over the full photon-angle range, with no intermediate charmonium imposed at generator level. Require at least two charged tracks with net charge zero (at least one positive and one negative); in three-track events use the pair closest to the interaction point, with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, and Vr < 1 cm. Identify leptons with the probability method (probability cut 0.001, P(μ) > P(e), >96% electron suppression), treating high-momentum tracks (p > 1.0 GeV/c) as leptons (electron if EMC energy > 0.6 GeV, otherwise muon) and demanding exactly one μ+ and one μ−; hadronic background is further rejected by a TMVA ANN with y_ANN < 0.3 for both tracks, and each selected track must have transverse momentum p_t > 300 MeV/c. Perform a 1C kinematic fit to e+e− → μ+μ− γ with a missing massless photon (four-momentum balance), χ² < 10, and retain the untagged ISR topology by requiring the fit-corrected photon direction to satisfy θ_γ < 0.3 rad or θ_γ > π − 0.3 rad; a competing fit without the ISR photon is also stored for a consistency check. For the efficiency, use the full-range true-MC J/ψ sample with per-track corrections, giving (32.04 ± 0.09)%, and subtract the remaining non-muon backgrounds bin by bin in the 150 di-muon invariant-mass bins between 2.8 and 3.4 GeV/c².
