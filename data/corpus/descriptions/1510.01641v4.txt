At the J/ψ(3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ A0, A0 → μ+ μ−. Use the 2009 J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, and generate a 200k-event exclusive MC sample for J/ψ → γ A0, A0 → μ+ μ−. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, and net charge 0; photons must have E>25 MeV (barrel) or >50 MeV (endcap), EMC TDC in 0–14, angle to the nearest extrapolated track >10° (mass-dependent: >20° for m(A0)≤0.3 GeV), with at least one photon. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001; high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) are treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy>0.6 GeV, otherwise muon), requiring at least one positive and one negative lepton, with an electron veto on both tracks via E_cal/p<0.9. At least one track must be muon-identified using the muon mass hypothesis: 0.1<E_cal<0.3 GeV, |Δt_TOF|<0.26 ns, and MUC penetration depth >(−40+70 p/GeV) cm for 0.5≤p≤1.1 GeV/c or >40 cm for p>1.1 GeV/c. The two muon candidates are fitted to a common vertex to form the A0, then a nominal 4C kinematic fit is performed on γ μ+ μ− with χ²<200; for multiple candidates the minimum-χ² candidate is kept and required to satisfy χ²<40.
