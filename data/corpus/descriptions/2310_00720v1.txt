On the J/ψ(3097) resonance, study Λ + ⁹Be → Σ⁺ + X using the J/ψ → Λ Λ̄, Λ̄ → p̄ π⁺, Λ → p π⁻, Σ⁺ → p π⁰, π⁰ → γγ chain, with a single-tag (Λ̄) plus double-tag (Σ⁺) method. Use the 3.097 GeV real data (708_3097) and its inclusive MC, and generate 1M exclusive signal MC events for the full J/ψ → ΛΛ̄ decay card with all sub-decays. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring at least one positive and one negative track; photons with EMC TDC in [0,14], 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, at least 10° separation from any track, and at least two photons. PID is by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against pions and kaons. Reconstruct Λ̄ via a secondary vertex fit on p̄π⁺ (chosen by minimizing the mass difference, removing used particles), and π⁰ via a 1C Kalman fit on γγ constrained to the nominal π⁰ mass with χ²<25, requiring at least one π⁰. Then perform a 4C kinematic fit on Λ̄ π⁰ p with χ²<200, flagged nominal.
