Measure e+e− → Σ− Σ̄+ Born cross sections from √s = 3.51–4.95 GeV, reconstructing Σ− → n π− with the neutron left missing and Σ̄+ → n̄ π+ with only the antineutron detected in the EMC, using a partial-reconstruction/recoil technique, and search for ψ(3770) → Σ− Σ̄+. Use real data and inclusive MC at the representative scan points 3.773, 4.600, 4.610, 4.620, 4.640, 4.660, 4.680, 4.700, 4.740, 4.750, 4.780, 4.840, 4.914, and 4.946 GeV, with the nominal energy set to 3.773 GeV, and generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC at each point for e+e− → Σ− Σ̄+, Σ− → n π−, Σ̄+ → n̄ π+. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<30 cm, |Vr|<10 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, and net charge 0; photons with EMC time in [0,700] ns, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, angle to any charged track >15°, and at least one photon. PID uses the probability method (prob>0.001), identifying pions against kaons/protons and requiring exactly one π+ and one π−; the antineutron is the highest-energy EMC shower with deposited energy >0.62 GeV at 3.773 GeV (energy-dependent), second moment S>18 cm², more than 16 hits, and angle to charged tracks >15°. Veto cosmic rays by requiring the π+ and π− MDC time-of-flight difference to be greater than −5 ns. Perform a 1-C kinematic fit constraining the (π+ n̄) invariant mass to the Σ̄+ nominal mass and the antineutron direction to the EMC shower position, with χ²<60, and require the recoil mass M(π+ n̄) in [1.10,1.35] GeV/c²; detection efficiencies and ISR corrections are obtained iteratively.
