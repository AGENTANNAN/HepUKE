At the ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → Σ+Σ̄− ω and ψ(3686) → Σ+Σ̄− φ, with Σ+ → pπ0, Σ̄− → p̄π0, ω → π+π−π0 (π0 → γγ) and φ → K+K−. Use 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each mode (ω and φ decay cards). Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<2 cm, two positive and two negative tracks and net charge 0, and photons with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy threshold, TDC 0–14, at least 10° away from any track, with ≥6 photons in the ω mode and ≥2 in the φ mode. PID uses the probability method (prob>0.001): in the ω mode identify p and p̄ against K/π requiring ≥1 each, remove them, then require exactly one π+ and one π− against K/p; in the φ mode identify p and p̄ (≥1 each) plus exactly one K+ and one K− against the other hypotheses and remove them. The ω mode performs a 7C kinematic fit (4C plus three π0 mass constraints on γγ pairs) with χ²<45, along with 4C fits of the 5γ and 7γ hypotheses for a wrong-photon-count veto; the φ mode uses partial reconstruction, one π0 formed by a 1C Kalman γγ mass fit (χ²<20) and a 2C fit with the second π0 missing, χ²<20. Signal regions require both M(pπ0) in [1176,1197] MeV/c² (ω) or [1176,1200] MeV/c² (φ), with sideband background subtraction and J/ψ and η vetoes applied at the ROOT level.
