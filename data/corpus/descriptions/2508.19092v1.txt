On ψ(3686), study ψ(2S)→ωηη with ω→π+π−π0 (Dalitz) and each η→γγ, π0→γγ, giving a π+π−6γ final state. Use the 709_3686 ψ(2S) real data (2.712×10^9 events) and its inclusive MC; generate 200k exclusive signal MC for ψ(2S)→ωηη with ω→π+π−π0, π0→γγ, and both η→γγ. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one π+ and one π− with net charge zero, and at least six photons with EMC timing 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, and angle to any track >10°. PID uses the probability method (prob>0.001) to identify pions against kaons/protons, requiring exactly one π+ and one π−. Reconstruct π0 and η candidates from γγ pairs via 1C Kalman fits to the nominal masses (χ²<200), requiring ≥1 π0 with |M_γγ−m_π0|<20 MeV and ≥2 η with |M_γγ−m_η|<25 MeV, choosing the combination that minimizes the combined π0–ηη mass-deviation χ². Then perform a 4C kinematic fit to π+π−π0ηη with nominal four-momentum constraints, χ²<40, discarding events for which alternative 4C fits to π+π−7γ or π+π−8γ yield a smaller χ² (compared also against π0π0η, π0π0π0, and ηηη hypotheses). Apply background vetoes: two-π0 veto |M_γγ−m_π0|>0.03 GeV for non-π0 pairs, ψ(2S)→ππJ/ψ veto |M(ππ)_recoil−m_J/ψ|>0.02 GeV, and ψ(2S)→XJ/ψ, J/ψ→ωη veto M(ωη)<3.0 GeV. Signal is extracted from the M(π+π−π0) distribution using an MC signal template plus Gaussian, second-order Chebyshev combinatorial background, η sidebands, with continuum from ψ(3770) data (709_3773) scaled by 1/s and interference phase ±90°.
