On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study the decays ψ(3686) → Ξ(1530)⁰ Ξ̄(1530)⁰ and ψ(3686) → Ξ(1530)⁰ Ξ̄⁰ using single-baryon tagging, where the tag side reconstructs Ξ(1530)⁰ → Ξ⁻π⁺ (or charge conjugate) and the signal side is inferred from the recoil mass M_recoil(Ξ⁻π⁺). Use real data and inclusive MC from the 709_3686 sample; generate 1,000,000 exclusive MC events for each mode with the decay chains: Ξ(1530)⁰ → Ξ⁻π⁺, Ξ⁻ → Λπ⁻, Λ → pπ⁻ (and charge conjugates), and for mode II additionally Ξ̄⁰ → Λ̄π⁰, π⁰ → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm, and at least three positive and three negative tracks; PID by probability with prob>0.001 for both protons and pions, identify protons against kaons and pions, requiring at least one proton and one anti-proton; remove proton tracks, then identify pions against kaons and protons. Reconstruct Λ → pπ⁻ and Λ̄ → p̄π⁺ via secondary vertex fits minimizing mass difference, with mass windows |M(pπ⁻)−M(Λ)|<5 MeV and |M(Λπ⁻)−M(Ξ⁻)|<8 MeV for the Ξ⁻ cascade (decay length >0) and Ξ(1530)⁰ from Ξ⁻π⁺ minimizing |M(Ξπ)−M(Ξ(1530)⁰)|. For mode II, additionally require at least two photons with energy >25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), TDC 0–14, angle to track >10°, and perform a 1C kinematic fit on the two photons constraining their invariant mass to the π⁰ mass with χ²<25, requiring at least one π⁰; also veto ψ(3686) → π⁺π⁻J/ψ background by |M_recoil(π⁺π⁻) − M(J/ψ)| > 4 MeV. Signal yields are extracted from simultaneous unbinned ML fits to the recoil mass M_recoil(Ξ⁻π⁺), and the angular distribution parameter α and branching fractions are determined.
