At √s = 4.260 GeV, study e+e− → π+π− J/ψ with J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−. Use the 4.260 GeV real data and the corresponding inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two J/ψ decay modes, with ψ(4260) → π+π− J/ψ followed by J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge zero. PID by the probability method: tracks with p>1.0 GeV/c are treated as leptons and the rest as pions; an EMC energy deposit above 0.6 GeV identifies an electron, otherwise the lepton is taken as a muon, with π/K separation, requiring one π+, one π−, one l+, and one l−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit of the π+π−l+l− system to the initial four-momentum, flagged nominal, with χ²<60. Both J/ψ decay modes share the same selection chain, and the e+e− decay card is attached to the algorithm.
