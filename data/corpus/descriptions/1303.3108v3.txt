On the J/ψ peak at 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → ω p pbar, ω → γ π0, π0 → γ γ, giving a γ γ γ p pbar final state. Use the J/ψ real data and inclusive J/ψ MC; generate 500k-event phase-space exclusive MC for the signal chain and 500k-event phase-space exclusive MC for the irreducible background J/ψ → γ π0 p pbar, whose p pbar shape serves as the non-resonant template. Event selection: exactly one positive and one negative charged track with |cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and net charge zero; PID by the probability method with confidence-level cut 0.001, identifying protons against π/K and requiring exactly one proton and one antiproton. Photons must satisfy the EMC timing window 0–14, E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.8) and E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), be at least 10° from the nearest track, and at least three photons are required, with each photon more than 30° from the proton and antiproton; proton and antiproton candidates with momentum below 0.3 GeV/c are removed. Perform a nominal 4C energy-momentum-conserving fit to γ γ γ p pbar, iterating photon combinations and keeping the minimum χ², with χ²<200 as encoded, and then in the later ROOT analysis form the π0 from the two photons whose invariant mass is closest to Mπ0 requiring |M(γγ)−Mπ0|<15 MeV/c² and select the ω signal as 0.753–0.813 GeV/c² with sidebands 0.663–0.693 and 0.873–0.903 GeV/c². The signal efficiency is taken as (16.1 ± 1.7)% from the phase-space MC, with the X(ppbar) acceptance entering the fit through an acceptance-weighted S-wave Breit-Wigner.
