On the J/ψ(3097), measure J/ψ → γη and the absolute η decay branching fractions using four exclusive η decay modes: η → γγ, η → π0π0π0, η → π+π−π0, and η → π+π−γ, plus an inclusive J/ψ → γη channel with γ → e+e− conversion reconstructed by the Photon Conversion Finder (selection not expressible in this framework). Use 1.0087×10^10 J/ψ(3097) real data events and inclusive MC at 3.097 GeV, and generate 1M-event exclusive signal MC for each of the four η decay modes. For η → γγ, require no charged tracks and at least three barrel photons (|cosθ|<0.80) with energy above 0.07 GeV, then a 4C kinematic fit to γγγ with χ²<80, keeping all γγ combinations. For η → π0π0π0, require no charged tracks and at least six barrel photons with energy above 0.025 GeV (barrel) or 0.050 GeV (endcap), and perform a 7C fit (4C plus three π0 mass constraints) with χ²<100, choosing the best combination by minimum χ². For η → π+π−π0, require exactly one π+ and one π− with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least three barrel photons with the same energy thresholds, π/K separation by the probability method with a 0.001 cut, and a nominal 5C fit (4C plus π0 mass constraint) to π+π−γγ with χ²<100. For η → π+π−γ, require one π+ and one π− (same track cuts), at least two barrel photons with the same thresholds, the same PID, a nominal 4C fit to π+π−γγ with χ²<60, and if more than two good photons are found, a competing non-nominal 5C fit under the π+π−π0 hypothesis (using the same tracks) requiring the probability of the γπ+π−γ hypothesis to exceed that of γπ+π−π0.
