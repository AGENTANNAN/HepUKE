On the J/ψ(3097), study J/ψ → K+ K− π0, π0 → γγ, for a partial-wave analysis of the K+ K− π0 final state. Use the 2009 J/ψ real data at 3.097 GeV (223.7×10^6 events) and the corresponding inclusive MC; generate 1,000,000 exclusive phase-space MC events for J/ψ → K+ K− π0 with π0 → γγ. Require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track, and net charge zero; photons must have E>25 MeV (barrel) or E>50 MeV (endcap), be more than 10° from any charged track, have EMC timing 0–700 ns, and number at least two. Identify kaons with the probability method using a 0.001 probability cut, assigning each track among π, K, and p hypotheses and requiring one K+ and one K−. Perform a 5C kinematic fit to K+ K− γγ with four-momentum conservation plus a π0 mass constraint on the photon pair, χ²<60, nominal. The code additionally notes ROOT-level requirements: any charged track with pT<120 MeV/c is rejected, full PID uses the highest combined dE/dx+TOF confidence level, diphoton pairs are preselected with M(γγ)<300 MeV/c², π0 candidates must satisfy 110<M(γγ)<150 MeV/c² after the 4C preselection, and events are rejected if a background hypothesis (γγπ+π−, γK+K−, or γγγK+K−) gives a lower χ².
