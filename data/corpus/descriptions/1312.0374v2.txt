At √s = 3.773 GeV (ψ(3770), 2.92 fb⁻¹), measure B(D⁺→μ⁺ν_μ) with a tag analysis: reconstruct a single charmed D⁻ in nine hadronic modes (K⁺π⁻π⁻, K_S⁰π⁻, K_S⁰K⁻, K⁺K⁻π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰, π⁺π⁻π⁻, K_S⁰π⁻π⁰, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁻π⁺, K_S⁰π⁻π⁻π⁺), with the recoiling D⁺ decaying to μ⁺ν_μ (K_S⁰→π⁺π⁻, π⁰→γγ). Use the ψ(3770) real data and its inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the nine tag modes. On the tag side, store m_BC and ΔE and later require the D⁻ mass window and |E_beam − E_{mKnπ}| < 2.5σ_E. On the signal side, require exactly one leftover positively charged track identified as a muon by MUC penetration depth, no other charged particles, and one undetected massless ν_μ. Apply a 4C kinematic fit (tag + μ⁺ + ν_μ to the measured CMS four-momentum) with χ² < 200. Additional selection: any extra good EMC photon must have E_γ_max < 300 MeV; all charged tracks except K_S⁰ daughters have DCA < 1.0 cm transverse and < 15.0 cm along the beam and are constrained to a common vertex; π/K separation uses CL_π > CL_K (CL_K > CL_π) below 0.75 GeV/c and CL > 0.1% above, with the π⁰ mass window from a 1C fit χ² < 100. The signal is extracted in |M_miss²| < 0.12 GeV²/c⁴, with peaking backgrounds (D⁺→K_L⁰π⁺, D⁺→π⁺π⁰, D⁺→τ⁺ν_τ, ISR ψ(3686)/J/ψ, qq̄, τ⁺τ⁻, non-DD̄) estimated from 10× data MC and subtracted, and the M_miss² resolution corrected by 1.194.
