Search for e+e− → K_S0 K_S0 h_c, with h_c → γ η_c and η_c → γγ, using partial reconstruction in which the η_c is not reconstructed but identified as the recoil against K_S0 K_S0 γ. Use real data and inclusive MC at 13 energy points from 4.600 to 4.951 GeV, and generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC per energy point (ψ(4260) → K_S0 K_S0 h_c, h_c → γ η_c, η_c → γγ, K_S0 → π+π−, phase space). Event selection requires exactly two positive and two negative charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm; K_S0 candidates are formed from π+π− pairs via a secondary-vertex fit, keeping pairs within ±6 MeV/c² of the K_S0 mass and with decay length greater than twice the vertex resolution. At least one photon is required with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold and EMC time within [0, 700] ns of the event start. Reconstruct K_S0 K_S0 γ (η_c missing) and require the recoil mass against the K_S0 K_S0 system to lie between 2.94 and 3.06 GeV, with the photon giving M(γ K_S0 K_S0) closest to the η_c mass chosen as the E1 photon. Signal yields are extracted at each energy point from missing-mass/recoil-mass fits, using an MC-derived signal shape convolved with a Gaussian resolution calibrated on the e+e− → K_S0 K_S0 J/ψ control sample and a second-order Chebyshev background.
