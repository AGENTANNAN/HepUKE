On ψ(4260), study e+e− → γ X(3872) with the normalization channel X(3872) → π+π− J/ψ and the search channel X(3872) → π0 χ_cJ (exclusive MC generated for χ_c1) → π0 γ J/ψ, where J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ− and π0 → γγ. Use the 4.258 GeV real data and inclusive MC (combined 4.15–4.30 GeV, 9.0 fb−1); generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the four modes: π+π−J/ψ with J/ψ→e+e− and J/ψ→μ+μ−, and π0χ_c1 with J/ψ→e+e− and J/ψ→μ+μ−. For the normalization selection, require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks with net charge 0, at least one photon, and probability-based PID treating high-momentum tracks (p>1.0) as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6, else muon) with exactly one l+ and one l−; the 4C kinematic fit uses one γ, one π+, one π−, one l+, one l−, flagged nominal, with χ² cut 200 (optimized χ²/ndf<10), a J/ψ mass window of ±20 MeV/c², and additional Bhabha and η/η′ vetoes (cosθ(ππ)<0.98, cosθ(γ-track)<0.98, M(γπ+π−)>0.6 GeV/c², |M(γπ+π−)−m_η′|>0.02 GeV/c²). For the search selection, require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track with net charge 0, at least four photons, the same lepton identification requiring 1 l+ and 1 l−, and a 5C kinematic fit (4C plus π0 mass constraint on two photons), flagged nominal, with χ² cut 200 (optimized χ²/ndf<5) and J/ψ mass window ±20 MeV/c²; apply π0π0, ω(782), ISR-γ and χ_cJ vetoes/windows (other photon pairs >20 MeV/c² from m_π0, M(γπ0)<0.732 GeV/c², recoil mass >3.7 GeV/c², ΔM0<25 MeV/c², ΔM1,2<20 MeV/c², loose χ_cJ in [3.35,3.60] GeV/c²). Use the J/ψ-substituted masses M(π+π−J/ψ)=M(π+π−l+l−)−M(l+l−)+m_J/ψ and M(π0χ_cJ)=M(π0l+l−)−M(l+l−)+m_J/ψ to improve resolution.
