On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → pbar K+ Σ0 + c.c. with Σ0 → Λ γ, Λ → p π−, giving the final state pbar K+ p π− γ (and its charge conjugate) for a partial-wave analysis of the new Σ(2330). Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive MC events for the pbar K+ Σ0 mode with a phase-space ψ(2S) → pbar K+ Σ0 decay followed by Σ0 → γ Λ and Λ → p π−. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least two positive and two negative tracks, exactly four tracks in total and zero net charge; photons must have at least 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy, fall in the TDC window 0–14 and lie more than 10° from any track, with at least one photon required. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying protons and kaons against pions and each other. A secondary-vertex fit on the p π− pair builds a virtual Λ by minimizing the mass difference (removing the used particles), and Λ candidates are kept if |M(pπ−) − m_Λ| < 7.5 MeV/c². A nominal 4C kinematic fit to e+e− → pbar K+ Λ γ is performed with the pπ− mass constrained to the nominal Λ mass, requiring χ²<45, and its χ² must be smaller than those of the competing 4C fits to e+e− → pbar K+ Λ and e+e− → pbar K+ Λ γ γ; the photon recoil mass must also exceed the nominal χ_c2 mass by at least 15 MeV/c². Helix-parameter corrections are applied to charged tracks before the kinematic fit, and both charge-conjugate modes share the same selection chain.
