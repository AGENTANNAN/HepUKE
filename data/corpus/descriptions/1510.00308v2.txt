On the ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, study e+e− → D+D− with a hadronic single-tag D recoiling against the semileptonic decay D → K_L0 e+ν_e. Use the 2.92 fb−1 real data at this energy and the matching inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for the signal mode, with the tag D decaying through the six hadronic modes K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S0π−π0, K_S0π−π−π+, K_S0π− and K+K−π−. For the tag D (both charges scanned), select one of these six modes using mode-dependent ΔE windows of about ±3σ (Kππ ±30 MeV, Kπππ0 −52/+39 MeV, Ksππ0 −57/+40 MeV, Ksπππ ±34 MeV, Ksπ ±32 MeV, KKπ ±30 MeV) together with 1.86 < M_BC < 1.88 GeV, keeping the single candidate with the smallest |ΔE| per mode. Tag π0's are built from photon pairs with 0.110 < M(γγ) < 0.155 GeV and a 1C mass-constrained fit to the nominal π0 mass with χ² < 20, while tag K_S0's come from oppositely charged track pairs with a vertex fit, vertex within 20 cm along the beam axis, |M(π+π−) − m(K_S0)| < 12 MeV and decay length greater than 2σ from the IP. On the signal side require exactly one positron, of charge opposite the tag D, identified by the combined EMC/dE/dx/TOF likelihood ratio L′(e)/[L′(e)+L′(π)+L′(K)] > 0.8 with bremsstrahlung recovery adding unmatched showers within 5° of the electron and no other unused charged track. Require exactly one photon with energy above 0.1 GeV and angle above 10°, used to define the K_L0 shower direction with the K_L0 left missing, its momentum magnitude solved from four-momentum conservation (U_miss = 0) and the most energetic shower chosen if several qualify, vetoing photons that pair into 0.110 < M(γγ) < 0.155 GeV to suppress π0 feed-through. Perform a kinematic fit with a four-momentum constraint and require χ² < 200.
