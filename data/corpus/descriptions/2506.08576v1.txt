On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γη′, η′ → π+π−η, with η → γμ+μ− or η → γe+e−. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 1,000,000-event exclusive MC for each of the two η decay modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold and at least 10° separation from any track, requiring at least two photons. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating π/μ/e: the μ mode requires one π+, one π−, one μ+, one μ−, with pions identified against muons and electrons and muons against pions and electrons; the e mode requires one π+, one π−, one e+, one e−, with pions identified against electrons and electrons against pions. Perform a 5C kinematic fit to γπ+π−l+l− (l=μ or e) imposing four-momentum conservation and the η mass constraint on M(γl+l−), with χ²<40 for the μ mode and χ²<200 for the e mode, flagged nominal. The full 6C fit adding the η′ mass constraint and, for the e mode, a photon-conversion veto (2.0 cm < Rxy < 8.0 cm and cosθeγ > 0.5) are applied in the ROOT analysis stage. Both decay modes share the same selection chain.
