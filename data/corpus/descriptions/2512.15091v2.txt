On the XYZ scan, study ψ(4260) → γ X(3872), X(3872) → K_S0 K+π− or K*(892) K, with K_S0 → π+π−. Use the listed real-data c.m. energy points from 4.180 to 4.340 GeV (no inclusive MC) and generate 100k-event exclusive MC at each point for the two modes: K_S0 K+π− and K*+K−/K*0 anti-K0, with K*→Kπ and K0→K_S0→π+π−; the fit uses a c.m. energy constant of 4.226 GeV. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero. Photons are accepted with barrel (endcap) energy above 25 (50) MeV, at least one, EMC TDC in 0–14, and an angle to any track greater than 10°. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying kaons against pions and protons and requiring at least one K±; a secondary vertex fit on π+π− forms K_S0 by minimizing the mass difference, keeping the smallest-χ² combination with |M(π+π−)−m_K_S0|<22 MeV/c² and decay length >2σ from the IP, while sidebands 0.432–0.454 and 0.542–0.564 GeV/c² are used to estimate non-K_S0 background. The K_S0, kaon, and pion are vertex-fit to a common IP, helix-parameter corrections are applied, and a nominal 4C kinematic fit of γ K_S0 K+π− is performed with χ²<200, with additional 3C (no photon) and 5C (extra photon) fits saved for photon-veto/photon-add studies. π0 background is suppressed by M_recoil²(K_S0 K±π∓)<0.012 (GeV/c²)², and K*(892) candidates must satisfy 0.748<M(K±π∓)<1.036 GeV/c² for neutral K* or 0.743<M(K_S0π±)<1.05 GeV/c² for charged K*.
