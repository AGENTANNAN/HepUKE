On the ψ(4260) energy region, study e+e− → Λc+ anti-Λc− with the Λc+ decaying through twelve hadronic modes. Use 4.5 fb−1 of real data collected at seven c.m. energy points between 4599.53 and 4698.82 MeV, together with the matching inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC per energy point from a decay card in which Λc+ → p K_S0, p K−π+, p K_S0 π0, p K_S0 π+π−, p K−π+π0, Λ0π+, Λ0π+π0, Λ0π+π−π+, Σ0π+, Σ+π0, Σ+π+π−, pπ+π− and anti-Λc− → anti-p K+π− (with Σ0 → Λ0γ, Σ+ → pπ0/nπ+, Λ0 → pπ−, K_S0 → π+π−, π0 → γγ). Since double-tagging is not supported, reconstruct a single tag of the anti-Λc− in these twelve hadronic channels, leaving the recoiling Λc+ signal side to the downstream ROOT analysis, and on that signal side allow 0–48 photons. Apply intermediate-resonance vetoes: reject M(anti-p π+) in [1.17, 1.20] GeV/c² in anti-p K_S0 π0, anti-p K_S0 π−π+, anti-Σ−π−π+ and anti-p π−π+; reject M(π+π−) or M(π0π0) in [0.48, 0.52] GeV/c² in anti-Λ0π−π+π−, anti-Σ−π0, anti-Σ−π−π+ and anti-p π−π+; and require M(anti-p π0) outside [1.17, 1.20] GeV/c² in anti-p K_S0 π0. For events with multiple candidates keep the one with the smallest |ΔE|. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the four-momentum and the tag mass to the nominal Λc+ mass, with χ² < 200.
