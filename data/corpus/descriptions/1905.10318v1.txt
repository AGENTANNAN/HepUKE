On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ η_c, η_c → ω ω, with ω → π+π−π0 and π0 → γγ. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 2M-event exclusive MC for the signal decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two π+ and two π−, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC in [0,14], angle to track >10°, at least five; PID by the probability method (prob>0.001) identifying pions against kaons and protons, requiring 2 π+ and 2 π−, and removing identified pions from the charged track lists. Reconstruct π0 from photon pairs with a 1C Kalman mass constraint (χ²<25) requiring at least two π0. Perform a 6C kinematic fit to γ π+π−π0 π+π−π0 (nominal, four-momentum constraint, χ²<200), with a final χ²_6C<60 selection; ω mass window ±26 MeV/c², signal region m(ωω) ≥ 2.65 GeV/c². Use Q-factor background subtraction and a partial wave analysis.
