Study e+e− → Λc+Λc− at the Λc+ scan energies √s = 4.600, 4.612, 4.628, 4.641, 4.661, 4.682, and 4.699 GeV (4.5 fb−1 total), looking for the semileptonic decays Λc+ → Σ+/-π∓e+νe with charge conjugate; use the real data and matching inclusive MC at every energy point, and produce 200k-event exclusive signal MC at all seven points for two generated samples: mode 1, Λc+ → Σ+π−e+νe with Σ+ → pπ0, and modes 2+3, Λc+ → Σ+/-π∓e+νe with Σ+/- → nπ+/-, both with the generated tag side Λ̄c− → p̄KS0. Reconstruct the single-tag Λ̄c− through the 12 hadronic tag modes (p̄KS0, p̄K+π−, p̄KS0π0, p̄KS0π−π+, p̄K+π−π0, Λ̄π−, Λ̄π−π0, Λ̄π−π+π−, Σ̄0π−, Σ̄π0, Σ̄π−π+, p̄π−π+), scanning both charges, and if multiple tag candidates occur retain the one with minimum |ΔE|. On the signal side require zero photons at the analysis level, at least one π− and one e+ (extra charged tracks allowed), net charge 0, a missing νe, photon angle >10°, and photon energy >0.025 GeV, then apply a 4C kinematic fit with χ2 < 200. The three signal submodes are fitted simultaneously, using Mmiss² for mode 1 and Umiss for modes 2+3; mode 1 additionally requires three charged tracks (p, π−, e+), π0 → γγ, and a kinematic mass constraint on M(pπ0) in [1.176, 1.200] GeV/c², while the neutron modes use the EMC neutron shower with the Umiss = 0 constraint. Background suppression uses M(pπ−) >1.13 GeV/c², M(Σ+π−π(e)+) <2.27 GeV/c², cosθ(π,e) <0.95, a neutron-mode π0 veto (E3×3/E5×5 >0.9, second moment <20 cm², M(γγ) in [0.115, 0.150] GeV/c², χ2_1C <20), and neutron mass windows M(nπ+) in [1.15, 1.23] and M(nπ−) in [1.16, 1.24] GeV/c². No explicit PID method or standard |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr charged-track cuts are specified in the analysis.
