On the J/ψ(3097), study J/ψ → γπ+π−η' with two independent η' decay modes: η' → γπ+π− (Mode I) and η' → π+π−η, η → γγ (Mode II). Use the 10.09×10^9-event J/ψ real data sample and inclusive MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each mode, with J/ψ → γπ+π−η' followed by the corresponding η' decay, all phase-space. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least four tracks with ≥2 positive and ≥2 negative and net charge 0, and photons with E>100 MeV in both barrel and endcap, TDC 0–14, separated from charged tracks by more than 10°, requiring at least two photons in Mode I and at least three in Mode II. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut and π/K/p separation, requiring at least one π+ and one π−. For Mode I, select the γπ+π− combination closest to the η' mass with |M(γπ+π−)−m_η'|<15 MeV/c², veto γγ pairs near π0 (|M−m_π0|<40 MeV/c²), near η (|M−m_η|<30 MeV/c²), or in the ω region 720<M(γγ)<820 MeV/c², veto 400<M(γπ+π−)<563 MeV/c², veto |M(γγ)−m_π0|<15 MeV/c², then perform a 4C kinematic fit to γγπ+π−π+π− with χ²<40. For Mode II, reconstruct η from γγ with a 1C fit to the nominal η mass with χ²<25 and at least one η, veto all γγ pairs within 40 MeV/c² of the π0 mass and within 15 MeV/c² of the π0 mass at the η' stage, select the π+π−η combination with |M(π+π−η)−m_η'|<10 MeV/c², then perform a nominal 5C fit (4C plus η mass) to γγγπ+π−π+π− with χ²<40, plus an additional 4C fit without the η mass constraint for background suppression. Both decay modes share the same charged-track, photon, and PID requirements.
