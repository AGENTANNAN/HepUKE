At the ψ(3770) energy point (3.773 GeV), perform a double-tag analysis of D0→π+π−π+π− and D0→π+π−π0π0 using the real data and inclusive MC samples at 3.773 GeV; no exclusive signal MC is specified. On the tag side reconstruct a D0bar through the three hadronic modes D0→K+π−, D0→K+π−π0, and D0→K+π−π+π−, with charm −1. For the charged four-pion signal require exactly two π+ and two π− and no photons, while for the neutral four-pion signal require one π+ and one π− plus four photons (two π0). Perform a 1C kinematic fit constraining the four-pion invariant mass to the nominal D0 mass, with χ²<200. Both modes apply the same background vetoes: reject K_S0 candidates if any π+π− pair has |M(ππ)−m_K_S0|<12 MeV/c2, reject D−→K+π−π−/K−π+π+ if |M−m_D−|<10 MeV/c2, reject η if any γγ pair has |M(γγ)−m_η|<20 MeV/c2, and reject events with extra π0 candidates beyond those forming the signal; the charged mode additionally vetoes D0→π+π−π0 when M(π+π−π0) is near the D0 mass. In the neutral mode the two π0 are reconstructed through a 1C Kalman fit constraining the γγ mass to the π0 mass, requiring χ²_1C<20. No explicit charged-track or PID cuts are encoded.
