On the J/ψ(3097) resonance, study the radiative decays J/ψ → γ P with P = π⁰, η, η′, each P → γγ, giving an all-photon three-gamma final state. Use the 3.097 GeV real J/ψ data set and its inclusive MC, plus a signal exclusive MC of 2.3 million events generated from a decay card covering all three modes (J/ψ → γπ⁰, γη, γη′ with P → γγ, HELAMP for the radiative decay and PHSP for the P → γγ). Event selection requires exactly zero charged tracks (|cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm applied to any track, i.e. a full charged-track veto), and photons with EMC time difference within ±500 ns and energy threshold 80 MeV in both barrel and endcap, requiring at least three photons. Since the final state is purely neutral, no charged-particle PID is applied. A 4C kinematic fit to γγγ with nominal constraints is performed, kept at χ² < 50, alongside competing 2γ and 4γ 4C fits used as vetoes (requiring χ²(3γ) < χ²(2γ) against e⁺e⁻ → γγ and χ²(3γ) < χ²(4γ) against π⁰π⁰ backgrounds). Signal yields are finally extracted from unbinned maximum-likelihood fits to the M_γγ spectra in the π⁰/η/η′ mass windows, with the QED background estimated from 3.080 GeV data normalized to the J/ψ luminosity, and the branching fraction obtained as B(J/ψ → γP) = N_obs / (N_J/ψ × B(P → γγ) × ε).
