On ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, using the 2.93 fb⁻¹ real data set and inclusive MC, measure absolute D→Kπω branching fractions with a double-tag method. Three independent signal modes are considered: D⁰→K⁻π⁺ω, D⁰→K_S⁰π⁰ω, and D⁺→K_S⁰π⁺ω, all with ω→π⁺π⁻π⁰; the first two tag the recoiling anti-D⁰ through D⁰→K⁻π⁺, D⁰→K⁻π⁺π⁰, or D⁰→K⁻3π, while the third tags the D⁻ through D⁻→K⁺π⁻π⁻, D⁻→K_S⁰π⁻, D⁻→K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰, D⁻→K_S⁰π⁻π⁰, D⁻→K_S⁰3π⁻, or D⁻→K⁺K⁻π⁻. Signal-side requirements are the charged multiplicities (K⁻π⁺ plus π⁺π⁻, K_S⁰ pions plus π⁰, or K_S⁰ plus π⁺ plus ω pions) and 2 or 4 photons, with the D⁺ mode requiring net charge +1; tag ∆E is restricted to |∆E|<25 MeV for non-π⁰ tag modes and to (−55,+40) MeV for tag modes containing a π⁰, while signal ∆E windows are (−42,+57) MeV for D⁰→K_S⁰π⁰ω and (−37,+44) MeV for D⁺→K_S⁰π⁺ω, and the D⁰→K⁻π⁺ω candidate is chosen by smallest |∆E_sig|. K_S⁰→π⁺π⁻ is selected with |M(π⁺π⁻)−M(K_S⁰)|<12 MeV/c², flight distance >2σ, and (in the K_S⁰π⁰ω mode) K_S⁰-daughter |Vz|<20 cm, with no PID or Vr requirement on those pions; ω→π⁺π⁻π⁰ uses |M(π⁺π⁻π⁰)−M(ω)|<25 MeV/c², allows both π⁺π⁻π⁰ combinations, and keeps events in the 2D ω signal region. A four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit is applied with χ²<200, double-tag yields come from a 2D unbinned ML fit to M_BC^tag vs M_BC^sig, peaking backgrounds are subtracted using K_S⁰/ω sidebands, and the D⁰→K⁻3π tag mode has a K_S⁰ veto excluding M(π⁺π⁻) in (0.478,0.518) GeV/c². No exclusive-MC samples, photon-energy thresholds, or generic track-quality/PID cuts are specified in the encoded selection.
