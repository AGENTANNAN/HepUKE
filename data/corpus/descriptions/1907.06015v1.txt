On the BESIII R-scan data from 2.100 to 3.080 GeV (20 c.m. energies, representative point 2.125 GeV), measure e+e− → K+K−K+K− and e+e− → φK+K− with φ → K+K−. Use the real data and inclusive MC, and generate a 100k-event exclusive MC for K+K−K+K− via ConExc mode 16 with PHSP. For the 4K mode, select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100, Vr<10, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; identify kaons by the probability method (cut 0.001) against pions and protons, requiring exactly 2 K+ and 2 K−; then perform a 4C kinematic fit to K+K−K+K−, nominal, with χ²<200. For the φK+K− mode, select charged tracks with the same |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100, Vr<10, at least one positive and one negative track; identify kaons as above but require at least 1 K+ and 1 K−; then perform a 1C kinematic fit to the three identified kaons plus one missing kaon assigned the kaon mass, nominal, with χ²<20. In both modes, identified kaon momenta are required to be below 0.8 p_beam to suppress QED backgrounds, and both are run on the same real data, inclusive MC, and 4K exclusive MC.
