Using the seven c.m. energy points from 4.600 to 4.699 GeV (4.4 fb⁻¹), study e⁺e⁻ → Λ_c⁺ anti-Λ_c⁻ via the single-tag channel Λ_c⁺ → Λ π⁺ π⁰, with Λ → p π⁻ and π⁰ → γγ. Use the real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive signal-MC events with anti-Λ_c⁻ decaying inclusively through anti-p K⁺ π⁻. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm, with PID by the probability method (prob >0.001), identifying protons against kaons and pions and pions against kaons. Photons must have energy >25 MeV, EMC time within 0–700 ns, and be at least 10° from any charged track, with at least two photons used to form π⁰. Reconstruct π⁰ from γγ with a 1C kinematic fit to the π⁰ mass in the 0.115–0.150 GeV window, and Λ from p π⁻ with a secondary-vertex fit (χ²<100, decay length >2σ) in the 1.111–1.121 GeV window, requiring L(p)>L(K) and L(p)>L(π) for the proton and no PID for the pion; reject Σ⁰ by vetoing M(Λγ) in 1.179–1.203 GeV. Form Λ_c⁺ from Λ π⁺ π⁰, apply ΔE in (−0.03, 0.02) GeV with the best candidate chosen by minimum |ΔE|, and obtain the M_BC signal region per energy point; for the PWA, apply a 3C kinematic fit constraining the Λ mass, Λ_c⁺ mass, and recoil mass to the Λ_c⁺ mass.
