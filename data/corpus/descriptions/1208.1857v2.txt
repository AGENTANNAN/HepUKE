On 4.009 GeV, study e+e− → η J/ψ and e+e− → π0 J/ψ with ψ(4040) as generator parent, J/ψ → ℓ+ℓ− (ℓ = e, μ), η/π0 → γγ. Use 478 pb−1 of real data at 4.009 GeV, the corresponding inclusive MC, and a ψ(3686) data control sample; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for ψ(4040) → η J/ψ (J/ψ → μ+μ− or e+e− 50/50, η → γγ) and 200k events for ψ(4040) → π0 J/ψ (J/ψ → μ+μ−, π0 → γγ). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with barrel E>25 MeV, endcap E>50 MeV, at least 20° from any charged track, TDC 0–14, exactly two good photons. Lepton identification uses the probability method with a probability cut of 0.001, treating tracks with p>1.0 GeV as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon) and requiring both tracks to be the same species; EMC deposit <0.4 GeV defines muons, E/p>0.8 defines electrons, and photons within a 5° cone of the lepton are added back to its four-momentum for FSR/bremsstrahlung recovery. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to ℓ+ℓ−γγ (nominal, χ²<200) and store a second 3C fit of the same final state (no nominal, no χ² cut). Require the two-photon recoil mass M_recoil(γγ) in [2.9, 3.4] GeV/c² for J/ψ candidates and, from the 3C fit, M(γ_H ℓ+ℓ−)<3.93 GeV/c² to reject radiative Bhabha and radiative dimuon; for the π0 J/ψ search additionally require at least one charged track with muon-counter depth >30 cm to suppress e+e− → π+π−π0. Both final states share the same selection chain except for that extra muon-depth cut.
