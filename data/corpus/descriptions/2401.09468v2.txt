Measure the Born cross section of e+e− → Σ+Σ̄− at √s from 3.510 to 4.951 GeV using the 41 energy points (24.1 fb⁻¹) of BESIII real data together with inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC per energy point with KKMC+PHSP for the chain Σ+ → p π0, Σ̄− → p̄ π0, π0 → γγ. Require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track, and net charge zero; photons with EMC timing 0–700 ns, E>25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), more than 10° from any charged track, and at least four photons. Identify protons and antiprotons against kaons and pions by the probability method (prob>0.001), requiring at least one proton and one antiproton; in practice this is a momentum threshold p>0.5 GeV/c, with E/p<0.8 for Bhabha suppression. Reconstruct π0 via a 1C Kalman fit constraining each γγ pair to the π0 mass with χ²<25, demanding at least two π0 candidates, then apply a 6C kinematic fit (4C energy-momentum plus the two π0 mass constraints) on p p̄ π0π0 with χ²<200, flagged nominal. Signal and sideband regions are defined by |M(pπ0) − m_Σ|<2.5σ and 5σ–7.5σ, respectively.
