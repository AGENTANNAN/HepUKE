At BESIII, perform a double-tag study of inclusive Λ_c^+ → Λ X with the Λ_c^-bar tagged in eleven hadronic single-tag modes (pbar K_S0, pbar K+ π−, pbar K_S0 π0, pbar K_S0 π−π+, pbar K+ π−π0, Λbar π−, Λbar π−π0, Λbar π−π+π−, Σbar0 π−, Σbar− π0, Σbar− π−π+), using the 4.5 fb−1 e+e− data at the seven c.m. energies 4600, 4610, 4620, 4640, 4660, 4680 and 4700 MeV together with the corresponding inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each dataset with a representative tag card containing Λ_c^-bar → pbar K+ π− and Λ → p π−, intended to cover the signal-side inclusive Λ_c^+ → Λ X and any tag mode. On the signal side reconstruct the inclusive Λ_c^+ → Λ X through Λ → p π− from the remaining tracks, requiring at least one proton and one π− and treating X as a massless missing placeholder. The tag-side single-tag selection follows the BESIII procedure of Phys. Rev. D 106, 072002, and no further explicit PID, photon, or generic track-quality cuts are specified in the encoded selection. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the total four-momentum to √s, with ECMS = 4.682 GeV and χ² < 200. In MC, truth-match with θ(p) < 15° and θ(π−) < 25° on both tag and signal sides and θ < 10° for other tag-side particles, otherwise classify the event as unmatched; reweight the signal MC with a BDT for the recoil mass against Λ and the Λ momentum, and correct the Λ reconstruction efficiency using J/ψ → pbar K+ Λ and J/ψ → Λ Λbar control samples.
