Study D_s+ → γ ρ(770)+, with ρ+ → π+ π0 and π0 → γγ, in D_s*+ D_s− events using a double-tag method in which the tag D_s− is reconstructed in five hadronic modes: K_S K, K K π, K K π π0, K_S K π π, and K π π. Use D_s data at 4.128, 4.157, 4.178, 4.189, 4.199, 4.209, 4.219, and 4.226 GeV plus the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for D_s+ → γ ρ+ with ρ+ → π+ π0, π0 → γγ. On the signal side require 3–48 photons with minimum energy 25 MeV and minimum angle 10°, exactly one π+, and net charge +1; the tag side supplies the ST D_s−, with an energy-dependent recoil-mass window against it used to select D_s*+ D_s− events. Perform a kinematic fit with a four-momentum constraint and m(γγ) constrained to the nominal π0 mass, χ² < 200. Additional ROOT-level selection keeps π+π0 in [0.62, 0.91] GeV/c², vetoes D_s+ → π+η by requiring M(γγ_h) > 0.68 GeV/c², applies extra γγ mass vetoes in the π0 [0.115, 0.150] and η [0.50, 0.57] GeV/c² windows, and chooses the best candidate by (M_sig + M_tag)/2 closest to the known D_s mass; signal extraction uses a 2D unbinned ML fit in M_sig versus cosθ_H. No explicit PID or charged-track geometry cuts are encoded here.
