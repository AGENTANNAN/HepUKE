At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV, 2.93 fb⁻¹ real data with inclusive MC), study D⁺ → K_S⁰ π⁺ η, K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻, η → γγ, using a double-tag technique in which the recoiling D⁻ is reconstructed in six hadronic modes (K⁺π⁻π⁻, K_S⁰ π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰ π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰ π⁻π⁻π⁺, K⁺K⁻π⁻), with charm tagging the D⁻. Generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC for D⁺ → K_S⁰ π⁺ η. On the signal side require two photons with opening angle above 10° and energy above 25 MeV, together with 2 π⁺ and 1 π⁻ (net charge +1). Choose the best tag and signal candidates by minimum |ΔE|, imposing M_BC ∈ [1.865, 1.875] GeV, ΔE ∈ [−0.025, 0.025] GeV for most tag modes and the signal side ([−0.020, 0.020] GeV), but [−0.055, 0.040] GeV for tag modes containing π⁰, and vetoing extra showers with energy above 0.23 GeV. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the γγ, π⁺π⁻ and tag-D invariant masses to the nominal η, K_S⁰ and D masses, with χ² < 200.
