Study e+e− → K+K−J/ψ, J/ψ → ℓ+ℓ− (e or μ), at twelve center-of-mass energies from 4.612 to 4.951 GeV. Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC at each energy point for both the full- and partial-reconstruction channels, using J/ψ → μ+μ−. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; identify leptons with the probability method, treating high-momentum tracks (p>0.95 GeV/c) as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, else muon), requiring 1 l+ and 1 l−, with e/μ separation via EMC energy (muon E_EMC<0.4 GeV, electron E_EMC>1.0 GeV). For the full reconstruction require 1 K+ and 1 K− identified against pions, and apply a 4C kinematic fit to K+K−ℓ+ℓ− with χ²<200, flagged nominal; in μμ mode require at least one muon with MUC penetration depth >30 cm, and in ee mode require cosθ(K+K−)<0.98 to reject radiative Bhabha. For the partial reconstruction require one charged kaon and 1 l+ and 1 l−, perform a 1C fit to K+ℓ+ℓ− with the K− missing and χ²<200, flagged nominal; in ee mode require cosθ(e+)<0.8, cosθ(e−)>−0.8, |cosθ(K±)|<0.8, and |cos(α_K±K_miss)|<0.95, |cos(α_K±e±)|<0.95, |cos(α_K±e∓)|<0.95, in μμ mode require both muons to have MUC penetration depth >30 cm, and use lepton momentum thresholds p>0.95 GeV/c for √s<4.84 GeV and p>1.05 GeV/c for √s≥4.84 GeV.
