On the four BESIII energy points 4.009, 4.230, 4.260 and 4.360 GeV, study the untagged ISR process e+e− → γ_ISR X(3872) → γ_ISR π+π− J/ψ with J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−, the same final state also serving as the ψ(3686) reference for the Γ_ee measurement. Use real data and inclusive MC at all four energies, together with 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the four signal/reference modes (ISR X(3872) and ISR ψ(3686), each with J/ψ → e+e− and μ+μ−) and for the two background samples e+e− → η J/ψ (η → π+π−π0) and e+e− → γ_ISR π+π−π+π−. The ISR photon is emitted nearly collinear with the beam (|cosθ_ISR| > 0.95), undetected, and reconstructed from the missing momentum. Event selection requires exactly four charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, two positive and two negative, net charge zero. PID uses the probability method (prob cut 0.001): high-momentum tracks (p > 1 GeV/c) are treated as leptons, an electron if the EMC energy deposit exceeds 0.4 GeV and otherwise a muon, while pions are separated from kaons and protons, requiring one l+, one l−, one π+ and one π−, with pion candidates above 600 MeV/c removed. The surviving γ_ISR π+π− l+l− candidates undergo a 2C kinematic fit constraining the missing ISR photon to zero mass and the lepton pair to the nominal J/ψ mass, with χ² cut 200 at the BOSS stage (the published χ² < 15, J/ψ mass window 3.05–3.14 GeV/c² and cos α_ππ ≤ 0.6 are applied later in ROOT). Both lepton channels share the identical selection chain.
