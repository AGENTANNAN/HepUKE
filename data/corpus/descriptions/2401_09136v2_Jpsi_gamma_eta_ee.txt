At the J/ψ peak (√s = 3.097 GeV, ~10 billion J/ψ events), study J/ψ → γη and J/ψ → γη′ with η/η′ → γe+e−, using the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC (708_3097) and generating 100k-event exclusive MC for each mode (J/ψ → γη, η → γe+e− and J/ψ → γη′, η′ → γe+e−) with a custom generator based on theoretical η/η′ → γe+e− amplitudes and PHSP as the decay-card placeholder. Events must pass a primary-vertex fit, then have exactly one positive and one negative charged track with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and net charge 0. Photon selection requires at least two photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, EMC TDC 0–14, and angle to any track >10°, rejecting conversions with R_xy>2 cm when cosθ_eg>0 and |Δ_xy|<0.8 cm. Lepton identification treats p>1.0 GeV tracks as leptons, calling them electrons if EMC energy >0.6 GeV (otherwise muons), requires one positive and one negative lepton, and applies an electron likelihood-ratio cut L(e)/(L(e)+L(π))>0.5 for η or >0.95 for η′. QED background is suppressed by requiring the low-energy photon E>0.15 GeV, its angle to e± >10°, and the radiative photon angle to e± >20°; the highest-energy photon is identified as the J/ψ radiative photon and the other as the η/η′ Dalitz photon. To suppress J/ψ → e+e−η/η′ with η/η′ → γγ, veto γγ masses within 0.03 GeV/c² of 0.547 or 0.958 GeV/c². Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γγe+e− with χ²<100, flagged nominal. The η and η′ modes share this chain except for the electron PID likelihood cut; signal regions are M(γe+e−)=0.52–0.56 GeV/c² for η (sidebands 0.50–0.51 and 0.59–0.60) and 0.94–0.98 GeV/c² for η′ (sidebands 0.88–0.90 and 1.00–1.02).
