On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ (J=0,1,2) → γ Σ+ anti-p- K_S0, with Σ+ → p+ π0, π0 → γγ, and K_S0 → π+π−, all three χ_cJ states sharing the final state γ p pbar π+ π− γγ. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for the combined χ_c0, χ_c1, χ_c2 decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, angle to track >10°, and at least three photons. PID by the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying protons against kaons and pions, requiring at least one proton and one anti-proton; remove identified protons and assign remaining charged tracks as π+ and π−, then form K_S0 from π+π− via a secondary vertex fit minimizing the mass difference. Perform a Kalman fit of two photons to the nominal π0 mass (χ²<25, at least one π0; the γγ pair closest to the nominal π0 mass after the 4C fit is chosen, with the third photon being radiative), followed by a nominal 4C kinematic fit of the p pbar K_S0 π0 γ final state (i.e., γ p pbar π+ π− γγ γ) with χ²<50; veto backgrounds using |M(pbar π+) − m_Λ| > 6 MeV and by comparing χ² of competing 4C fits with different photon multiplicities (no radiative photon and extra photon).
