At √s = 4.178 GeV, using 3.19 fb⁻¹ of real data and inclusive MC, commission a precision measurement of the Ds*+−Ds+ mass difference with signal Ds*+ → Ds+ π0, Ds+ → K+ K− π+ (charge conjugate implied) and a calibration channel D*+ → D+ π0, D+ → K− π+ π+. Generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two decay modes. For both chains, select charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, at least two positive and one negative track, and apply probability-based PID with prob > 0.001 and K/π separation: the signal requires at least one K+, one K−, and one π+, while the calibration requires at least one K− and two π+; the corresponding positive/negative track charges are required for the kaon and pion candidates. Photons must have EMC timing 0–14 and energy above 25 MeV in the barrel or 50 MeV in the endcap, with at least two photons; a Kalman fit then constrains two photons to the nominal π0 mass with χ² < 25, requiring at least one π0. The signal is fitted with a 4-momentum constraint on K+K−π+π0 and χ² < 200, followed by the Ds+ mass window |M(K+K−π+) − m_Ds+| < 12 MeV/c², soft-π0 momentum p(π0) < 100 MeV/c, recoil-mass cuts against the tag Ds−, and a ΔM = M(K+K−π+π0) − M(K+K−π+) fit; the calibration uses K−π+π+π0 with the same 4C fit and χ² < 200, a D+ mass window of 12 MeV/c², p(π0) < 100 MeV/c, recoil-mass cuts, and a ΔM fit to calibrate the Ds*+ mass difference.
