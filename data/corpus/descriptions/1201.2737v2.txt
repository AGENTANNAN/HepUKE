At the J/ψ (√s = 3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ η(1405) with η(1405) → f0(980) π0, where f0(980) → π+π− for Mode I and → π0π0 for Mode II; also use J/ψ → γ η′ control/measurement modes with η′ → π+π−π0 or 3π0, and include the peaking backgrounds η′ → γ ρ0(→π+π−) and η′ → γ ω(→π+π−π0). The input is the 225.2M-event J/ψ real data sample with its inclusive MC (Lund-Charm, 2.25×10^8), plus exclusive MC: 200k events each for the η(1405) charged/neutral and η′ charged/neutral modes, and 100k events each for the two η′ peaking backgrounds. Mode I selection (γ π+π−π0) requires exactly one π+ and one π−, net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<2 cm, at least three photons with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap thresholds, more than 10° from any charged track, and TDC 0–14, with pion identification by probability (>0.001) against kaons and protons. It then requires at least one π0 from a γγ Kalman fit to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<25 and |M(γγ)−m_π0|<15 MeV, performs a nominal 4C fit to γγγ π+π− with χ²<30, compares against 2γ and 4γ hypotheses, and vetoes |M(γπ0)−m_ω|<50 MeV. Mode II selection (γ 3π0) requires no charged tracks and net charge zero, 7 or 8 photons with the same energy/angle/TDC requirements, at least three π0 candidates from γγ fits with χ²<25 and |cosθ_decay|<0.95 for the daughter photon in the π0 rest frame, followed by a nominal 4C fit to γ π0π0π0 with χ²<60, and the same ω veto |M(γπ0)−m_ω|<50 MeV.
