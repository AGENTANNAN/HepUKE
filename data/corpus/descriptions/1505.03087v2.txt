At √s = 3.773 GeV on the ψ(3770), search for the FCNC decay D0 → γγ with a double-tag technique and, from the same sample, measure B(D0 → π0π0). Use the 2.92 fb⁻¹ ψ(3770) real data and the inclusive MC, plus 500k-event exclusive MC for each signal mode, generated as ψ(3770) → D0 D̄0 with one D decaying to γγ or π0π0 (π0 → γγ) and the other to the generic K+π− tag. Tag the recoiling D̄0/D0 hadronically in five modes (K+π−, K+π−π0, K+π−π+π−, K+π−π+π−π0, K+π−π0π0), choosing one candidate per mode by reconstructed energy closest to the beam energy and among multiple double-tag combinations the one whose average of tag and signal mBC is closest to the known D0 mass, with 1.847 < mBC(tag) < 1.883 GeV/c² and |ΔE(tag)| < 0.1 GeV. On the signal side take the two (four) most energetic showers not used by the tags, requiring each photon to be more than 10° from any charged track (doubly-radiative Bhabha veto) and above a 25 MeV barrel energy floor. Perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation plus the D0 mass constraint on the signal γγ and on both tags (giving 6C), and for π0π0 additionally the two π0 mass constraints, with χ² < 200. For γγ apply a π0 veto against combining a signal photon with any other photon, and tighten at the ROOT stage to |ΔE(γγ)| < 0.25 GeV (mBC(γγ) > 1.85 GeV/c²); for π0π0 select the photon pair with smallest |ΔE(π0π0)| and require −0.070 < ΔE(π0π0) < +0.075 GeV before fitting mBC. Both signal modes run over the real data, inclusive MC and their respective exclusive MC.
