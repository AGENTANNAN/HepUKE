On the ψ(3770), measure the D0 → π+π−π+π− strong-phase difference with a double-tag tag-versus-signal analysis at 3.773 GeV, using the corresponding real data set (2.93 fb−1) and inclusive MC. Generate 200,000 exclusive MC events for ψ(3770) → D0 anti-D0 phase space. Reconstruct the tag D0 in the hadronic flavor modes D0 → K−π+, K−π+π0, and K−π+π−π+, and build the signal D0 from the remaining tracks requiring two π+ and two π− with net charge zero. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the tag-plus-signal four-momentum and the tag D0 to its nominal mass, with χ² < 200. The selection applies no explicit charged-track |cosθ|, |Vz|, or Vr cuts, no photon energy threshold or photon multiplicity requirement, and no dedicated PID or π/K separation; particle content is fixed only by the tag modes and the two-π+ two-π− signal. Tag mBC and ΔE are stored without an encoded mass window and are windowed later in ROOT. Semileptonic K−e+νe and KL0 tag modes use partial reconstruction with missing-mass variables in ROOT and are not part of this selection; doubly-Cabibbo-suppressed corrections for the K−π+π0 and K−π+π−π+ tags and the two phase-space binnings (equal-Δδ_D and optimal, 2×5 bins with KS0 veto [0.481, 0.514] GeV, MC efficiency and migration matrix) are also applied in ROOT.
