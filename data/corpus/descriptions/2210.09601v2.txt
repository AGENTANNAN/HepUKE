On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV) and ψ(3686) (3.686 GeV), study e+e− → ηΣ+Σ−, with η → γγ, Σ+ → pπ0, Σ− → p̄π0, and π0 → γγ. Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at both energies, and generate 500k-event exclusive phase-space MC for the ηΣ+Σ− channel at each resonance. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93 and |Vz|<20 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track; photons with EMC timing 0–14, energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), and at least six photons. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, separating protons from π/K and requiring one p and one p̄. Perform a 7C kinematic fit to p p̄ and the six photons, imposing four-momentum conservation plus one η and two π0 nominal-mass constraints, with χ²<30 for J/ψ and χ²<25 for ψ(3686), flagged nominal. Apply post-fit windows: η ∈ (0.450, 0.650) GeV/c², π0 ∈ (0.110, 0.160) GeV/c², Σ± signal M(pπ0) ∈ [1.177, 1.201] GeV/c² (sidebands [1.141, 1.165] ∪ [1.213, 1.237]); for J/ψ minimize the Σ+Σ− pairing Δ and veto η′ → ηπ0π0 in M(ηπ0π0) ∈ [0.95, 0.97], while for ψ(3686) require Mrec(η)<3.050 GeV/c² and veto ψ(3686) → π0π0J/ψ by Mrec(π0π0) ∈ [3.080, 3.120]. Peaking backgrounds, J/ψ → π0Σ+Σ− and ψ(3686) → γχc0,1,2 with χc0,1,2 → π0Σ+Σ−, are subtracted using control samples and exclusive MC shapes.
