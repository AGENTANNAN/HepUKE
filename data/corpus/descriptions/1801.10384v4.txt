At √s = 2.125 GeV, search for Z_s in e+e− → φππ and measure its cross section using two independent channels: e+e− → φπ+π− with φ → K+K− (Mode I) and e+e− → φπ0π0 with φ → K+K−, π0 → γγ (Mode II). Use the 2.125 GeV real data and inclusive MC (713_Rscan_2125) and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each channel via ψ(4260) → φππ decay cards. For Mode I, select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and at least three tracks; apply probability-based PID (prob>0.001) with π/K separation requiring at least one K, one π+, and one π−, then perform a nominal 1C kinematic fit to K+π+π− with the K− missing and a four-momentum constraint, χ²<10, plus a complementary 1C fit with missing K+. For Mode II, select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and at least one track; require at least four photons with E>25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), angle to nearest track >10°, and EMC timing 0–14 (0–700 ns); apply the same probability PID with at least one K. Reconstruct π0 candidates with a 2C Kalman fit on four photons constraining two γγ pairs to the nominal π0 mass, χ²<25, requiring at least two π0, then perform a nominal 1C fit to K+π0π0 with missing K− and four-momentum constraint, χ²<20, plus a complementary fit with missing K+; finally apply a π0 mass window of ±20 MeV/c². Both modes use the same data, inclusive MC, and their respective 100k-event exclusive MC samples.
