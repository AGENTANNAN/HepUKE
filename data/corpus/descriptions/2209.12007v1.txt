Study e+e− → π+π−J/ψ produced via two-photon fusion at four c.m. energies (3807.7, 3867.4, 3871.3, 3896.2 MeV), using the corresponding real data together with inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of J/ψ → e+e− and J/ψ → μ+μ− with the chain e+e− → π+π−J/ψ followed by the chosen leptonic decay. Event selection requires four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, assigned as one π+, one π−, and one lepton pair (e+e− or μ+μ−), giving net charge zero. Particle ID uses the probability method with probability >0.001: electrons are identified against pions and pions against electrons and kaons in the electron mode; muons are identified against pions and pions against kaons in the muon mode. Additional requirements are pion momentum <0.6 GeV/c, lepton momentum >1.0 GeV/c, electron E/p>1.1, and muon E/p<0.35. A 4C kinematic fit with four-momentum constraint and nominal hypothesis is performed with χ²<200 in the selection (the final ROOT analysis applies χ²<60), and photon-conversion vetoes require cosθ(π+π−)<0.95 and cosθ(π±e∓)<0.98. The signal is extracted from the π+π− recoil mass, i.e. the lepton-pair invariant mass, in [3.08, 3.12] GeV/c² around the J/ψ, and cross sections are measured at the four energies to set an upper limit on Γ_ee × B(X(3872)→π+π−J/ψ).
