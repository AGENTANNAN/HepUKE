At the 14 c.m. energy points from 4.189 to 4.600 GeV, study continuum e+e− → η′ J/ψ with η′ → γπ+π− or η′ → ηπ+π− (η → γγ), and J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−. Use all 14 real-data points and inclusive MC at 4.258, 4.416, and 4.600 GeV, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC at every energy point for each of the four final states: γπ+π−e+e−, γπ+π−μ+μ−, γγπ+π−e+e−, and γγπ+π−μ+μ−. Event selection requires four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge 0. Photons must satisfy EMC timing 0–14, be at least 20° from any charged track, exceed 25 MeV (barrel) or 50 MeV (endcap), and be at least one for the γπ+π− channels or at least two for the ηπ+π− (η→γγ) channels. Particle ID uses the probability method with prob>0.001; tracks with p<0.8 GeV are taken as pions and p>1.0 GeV as leptons, e/μ separation uses E/p>0.8 for electrons and E/p<0.4 for muons, and the event must contain exactly one π+, one π−, one l+, and one l−, with the l+ and l− of the same species. A nominal kinematic fit is applied: 4C to γπ+π−l+l− for the γπ+π− modes, and 5C to γγπ+π−l+l− for the ηπ+π− modes with M(γγ) constrained to the η mass, using χ²<200 in BOSS (the paper’s tighter χ²<40 is applied offline). The η′ is selected in 0.94<M(γ(η)π+π−)<0.98 GeV/c² and the J/ψ in 3.07<M(l+l−)<3.13 GeV/c², with sidebands 0.90–0.94 and 0.98–1.02 GeV/c² and offline vetoes against ISR ψ(3686) and FSR using the M(π+π−J/ψ) and recoil-mass regions.
