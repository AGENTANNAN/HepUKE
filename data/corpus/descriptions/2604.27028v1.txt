On the J/ψ(3097), study J/ψ → K− Σ0 Ξ̄+ (charge conjugate included), with Ξ̄+ → Λ̄ π+, Λ̄ → p̄ π+, Σ0 → Λ γ and Λ → p π−. Use the 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data together with the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 200k exclusive MC events for this single decay mode. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm and Vxy<10 cm (loose because of the long-lived Λ/Ξ), at least two positive and at least one negative track; PID by the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying proton, kaon and pion against each other, requiring at least one p̄, one K− and two π+. The K− must originate from the IP, so it is additionally required to have |Vxy|<1 cm and |Vz|<5 cm. Reconstruct Λ̄ from p̄π+ and then Ξ̄+ from Λ̄π+ through secondary-vertex fits minimizing the mass difference, and treat the Σ0 as missing, requiring the recoil mass of the K−Ξ̄+ system to lie in 1.174–1.204 GeV (m_Σ0 ± 15 MeV). Perform a 1C kinematic fit constraining the missing mass to the nominal Σ0 mass, applied only to improve the mass resolution, with no χ² cut. Intended windows: Ξ̄+ mass within 10 MeV of nominal with positive decay length, a Λ/Ξ peaking-suppression cut on the mass-difference combination below 4 MeV, and a recoil-K− mass below 2.58 GeV to reject poorly measured low-momentum kaons.
