At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), study the semileptonic decays D0 → K− η e+ νe, D+ → KS0 η e+ νe and D+ → η η e+ νe using a double-tag method, with η → γγ and KS0 → π+π−. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the three signal modes. Reconstruct the tag side from D0 → Kπ, Kππ0, Kπππ or D+ → Kππ, KSπ, Kπππ0, KSππ0, KSπππ, KKπ decays with opposite charm; on the signal side require two photons and one K− plus one e+ (mode I), two photons plus π+π−e+ (mode II), or four photons plus e+ (mode III), with missing νe, photon energy >25 MeV and photon–track angle >10°, and zero net charge for modes I–II. Photon candidates must satisfy barrel E>25 MeV (|cosθ|<0.80) or endcap E>50 MeV (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92) with TDC in [0,700] ns. Particle ID uses kaon/pion likelihoods and electron selection L_e>0.001, L_e/(L_e+L_π+L_K)>0.8, E/p in [0.8,1.1]; KS0 is built from two oppositely charged tracks with |Vz|<20, vertex fit χ²<100, mass [0.487,0.511] and decay length >2σ. Perform a kinematic fit constraining four-momentum and the γγ invariant mass to the η nominal mass, with χ²<200, and for modes I–II require the γγ mass in [0.505,0.575]; veto M_P_eta_e<1.80 GeV, extra photon energy <0.5 GeV, extra π0, and for mode I require M_K_eta>1.30 GeV, while π0 candidates are selected from γγ in [0.115,0.150] with 1C fit χ²<50.
