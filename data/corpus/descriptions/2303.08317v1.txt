On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV (448.1M events of real data plus inclusive MC), study ψ(3686) → φ K_S^0 K_S^0 with φ → K+K− and K_S^0 → π+π−, generating 1M exclusive MC events using the ψ(3686) → φ K_S^0 K_S^0 phase-space decay, φ → K+K− (VSS) and K_S^0 → π+π− phase space. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least three positive and three negative tracks and net charge zero. Kaons are identified by the probability method at a 0.001 cut against π and p, requiring at least one K+ and one K−; pions are then identified the same way against K and p, requiring at least two π+ and two π−, with used particles removed from the candidate list. Two K_S^0 → π+π− vertices are reconstructed by secondary vertex fits (mass-difference minimization) with a K_S^0 mass window of 0.486–0.510 GeV/c² (sidebands 0.454–0.478 and 0.518–0.542 GeV/c²), requiring decay length >2σ and secondary-track |Vz|<20 cm. A 4C kinematic fit to φ K_S^0 K_S^0 (φ → K+K−) is applied with χ²<50, helix corrections are used, a φ → K+K− mass window is applied, charge conjugation is implied throughout, and continuum QED background is estimated from off-resonance data with 1/s scaling.
