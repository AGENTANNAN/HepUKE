Search for e+e− → η′ψ(2S) at the nine BESIII scan points from 4.660 to 4.950 GeV (4.67 fb−1 total), with ψ(2S)→π+π−J/ψ, J/ψ→l+l−, and two η′ decay modes: Mode I η′→γπ+π− and Mode II η′→ηπ+π−, η→γγ. Use the scan real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each mode, with J/ψ→e+e− in the generated decay card. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons must satisfy 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy thresholds, TDC 0–14, be at least 10° from any track, and number at least one in Mode I or at least two in Mode II. Particle identification uses the probability method with prob>0.001; tracks with p>1.0 GeV/c are treated as leptons, an electron if E/p>0.7 otherwise a muon with E<0.45 GeV, tracks below 0.8 GeV/c are pions, and one l+ and one l− are required with the remaining positive and negative tracks assigned as π+ and π−. Mode I performs a 4C fit to γπ+π−π+π−l+l− with χ²<200; Mode II first reconstructs η→γγ via a 1C fit to the η nominal mass with χ²<200, then performs a 4C fit to ηπ+π−π+π−l+l− with χ²<200, selecting the best candidate by minimizing the η′ and ψ(2S) mass pulls. Mass windows are J/ψ [3.083,3.111] GeV/c² for Mode I or [3.073,3.121] GeV/c² for Mode II, ψ(2S) [3.680,3.693] GeV/c², and η [0.482,0.604] GeV/c² for Mode II, with 5-track missing-pion events handled by a 1C pion-mass constraint and Mode I applying a competing-hypothesis veto on photon multiplicity.
