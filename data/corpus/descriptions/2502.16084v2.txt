Measure single inclusive π± and K± production in e+e− annihilation across the continuum from √s = 2.000 to 3.671 GeV as a cross-section measurement, with no specific decay channel reconstructed. Use real data at the five 713 Rscan points 2.000, 2.200, 2.396, 2.644 and 2.900 GeV together with the matching inclusive MC, and generate 1M-event exclusive continuum MC per point with the ConExc R-value mode 74110 for light hadrons. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm; photons with EMC timing in 0–14, energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) and at least 10° from any track. PID uses the combined dE/dx and TOF probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying π against K/p and K against π/p. Hadronic event selection (Bhabha and γγ rejection, prong counting, QED background suppression) is done in ROOT, and no kinematic fit is applied since π± and K± are extracted inclusively from the selected hadronic events.
