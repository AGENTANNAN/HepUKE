Measure exclusive cross sections for e+e− → K+K−π+π−(π0), K+K−K+K−(π0), π+π−π+π−(π0), and p pbar π+π−(π0) over sqrt(s)=3.773–4.600 GeV using 40 BOSS energy points of real data and matching inclusive MC, with eight independent final states and representative points spanning ψ(3770), 4.009, 4.178–4.600, 4.130/4.160, 4.612–4.700, and 4.740–4.914 GeV. Generate 100k-event exclusive ConExc continuum MC for each of the eight modes at these points, using generator modes 14 (K+K−π+π−), 16 (K+K−K+K−), 12 (π+π−π+π−), 19 (K+K−π+π−π0), 17 (π+π−π+π−π0), and form-D user cross sections for p pbar π+π−, p pbar π+π−π0, and K+K−K+K−π0. All channels require exactly four charged tracks, two positive and two negative, with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; the four-charged modes have no photon requirement (nγ≥0), while the π0 modes require at least two photons with Eγ>25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), photon |cosθ|<0.80 (barrel) / <0.92 (endcap), and EMC time within 0–700 ns. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut: K/π separation for K-containing modes, π/K separation for the 4π modes, and p/pbar against π and K with pions against protons for the p pbar modes, always selecting 2 positive and 2 negative tracks of the required species plus two photons for the π0 modes. Perform 4C kinematic fits for the four-charged modes and 5C fits (4C plus a π0 mass constraint) for the π0 modes, with χ²<50, nominal, and channel-dependent vetoes: |M(ππ)−M_J/ψ|>15 MeV, |M(ppbar)−M_J/ψ|>15 MeV, |M(ppbar)−M_ψ(2S)|>20 MeV, |M(4π)−M_ψ(2S)|>20 MeV, |M(Kπ)−M_D|>20 MeV, |M(KK)−M_χc0|>50 MeV, |M(ππ)−M_χc0|>50 MeV, |M(ππ)−M_Ks|>30 MeV, E_EMC/p<0.8, and cosθ(ππ)<0.9 where applicable, keeping the best combination.
