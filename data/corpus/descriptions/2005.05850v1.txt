Measure the Born cross sections for e+e− → Ds+ Ds1(2460)− + c.c. and e+e− → Ds*+ Ds1(2460)− + c.c., with Ds1(2460)− → Ds*+ π0, Ds*+ → γ Ds+, π0 → γγ and Ds+ → K+K−π+ (including the φ→K+K− and K*(892)0→K−π+ sub-modes). Use real data and inclusive MC at 4.467, 4.527, 4.575 and 4.600 GeV; generate 200k-event exclusive signal MC for the Ds+Ds1(2460)− mode at each of these four points, and 100k events for the Ds*+Ds1(2460)− mode only at 4.600 GeV (the small-scan statistics points 4.550–4.590 GeV are excluded). For the Ds+Ds1 mode, require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and one negative track, PID by the probability method with a 0.001 cut and K/π/p separation, demanding at least one K+, one K− and one π+; the Ds+ is reconstructed at 1.96835 GeV/c2 and Ds1(2460)− is inferred from the recoil mass against the Ds+, with intermediate-resonance windows |M(K+K−)−m_φ|<15 MeV/c2 or |M(K−π+)−m_K*0|<84 MeV/c2. The Ds*+Ds1 mode uses the same tracking and PID selection plus photons with EMC TDC 0–14, energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap), an angle to the nearest track larger than 20° and at least one photon, reconstructs the Ds*+ at 2.1122 GeV/c2, and applies a 2C mass-constrained fit to the nominal Ds+ and Ds*+ masses with χ²<10 before the recoil-mass study; no kinematic fit is used in the Ds+Ds1 mode. Ds1(2460)− yields are extracted from unbinned maximum-likelihood fits to the recoil-mass spectra with MC-derived signal shapes and polynomial (or ARGUS) backgrounds, and 90% C.L. upper limits are quoted whenever the significance is below 3σ.
