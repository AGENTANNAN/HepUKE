On the J/ψ peak at √s = 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → γ η′, η′ → γ π+π−. Use 1.31×10^9 real data events and inclusive MC; generate a 1M-event exclusive MC sample for the signal decay J/ψ → γ η′, η′ → γ π+π−. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with 40 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC TDC 0–14, and a minimum angle of 10° to any charged track, requiring at least two photons. Veto π0 by |M(γγ) − m(π0)| > 20 MeV/c², and select the γπ+π− combination closest to the nominal η′ mass with |M(γπ+π−) − m(η′)| < 20 MeV/c². Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γ γ π+ π−, χ² < 100, flagged nominal. Apply helix parameter corrections to MC tracks. Particle hypotheses are defined by the kinematic fit; no additional PID is applied.
