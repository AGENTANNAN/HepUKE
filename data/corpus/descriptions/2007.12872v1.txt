Measure e+e− → μ+μ− cross sections at c.m. energies from 3.80 to 4.60 GeV across 133 scan points (the DSL enumerates 45 representative BOSS 703/705/706/707 samples, including 3.810, 3.872, 4.180–4.280, 4.310, 4.390, 4.400–4.440, and the 4.610–4.946 GeV points). Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC for every scan point, and generate 200k-event exclusive e+e− → μ+μ− MC per energy point via KKMC+VLL (an approximation to the paper's BABAYAGA generator). Event selection: exactly two oppositely charged tracks with net charge 0, |cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and one μ+ plus one μ−. Muon identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, discriminating μ± against electron, pion, kaon, and proton hypotheses. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to the μ+μ− hypothesis, flagged nominal, with χ² cut 60. The paper's tighter E/p muon-ID window (0.05<E/p<0.40) and the energy-dependent Σ|p|>0.9√s cut are approximated or deferred to ROOT-level steps.
