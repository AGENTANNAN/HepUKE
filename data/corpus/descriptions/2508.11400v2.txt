Study Lambda_c+ transverse polarization and decay asymmetry via single-tag e+e- → Lambda_c+ anti-Lambda_c- at 13 centre-of-mass energies from 4600 to 4951 MeV (6.4 fb^-1), using real data and inclusive MC at each point, plus a 200k-event exclusive signal MC per point in which Lambda_c+ decays with equal 20% probability to p K_S0, Lambda pi+, Sigma0 pi+, Sigma+ pi0, and p K- pi+, while anti-Lambda_c- decays inclusively. Tag-side reconstruction uses only three of these five modes—p K- pi+, p K_S0, and Lambda pi+—because Sigma0 pi+ and Sigma+ pi0 have no match in the tagger's decay vocabulary and are dropped. The signal side is required to have zero photons. A kinematic fit is performed constraining four-momentum conservation and the tag Lambda_c+ invariant mass to its nominal value, with χ² < 200. The resulting tagged Lambda_c+ samples are then used in a multidimensional angular analysis in ROOT to extract the transverse polarization and the decay-asymmetry parameters alpha, beta, and gamma.
