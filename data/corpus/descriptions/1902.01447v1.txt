At the ψ(3686) (3.686 GeV), search for J/ψ → φ e+ e−, with ψ(3686) → π+π− J/ψ, J/ψ → φ e+ e−, φ → K+ K−, using 448.1×10^6 ψ(3686) events of real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k exclusive MC events for the full decay chain. Select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring exactly three positive and three negative tracks and net charge zero. Particle identification uses the probability method with a probability cut of 0.001: identify at least one K+ and one K− against π/p, and at least one e+ and one e− against π/K, with remaining positive and negative tracks assigned as π+ and π−, respectively; soft pions are required to have p<0.45 GeV/c with no pion PID, and electron candidates must have E/p>0.8. Require the recoil mass M(π+π−)_rec in [3.05, 3.15] GeV/c^2 to select the J/ψ, then perform a 4C kinematic fit to π+π−K+K−e+e− with χ²<40 (nominal), retaining the assignment with the smallest χ² if multiple survive. The signal region is |M(π+π−)_rec − M_{J/ψ}|<0.007 GeV/c^2 and |M(K+K−) − M_φ|<0.010 GeV/c^2, applied in the ROOT analysis with sidebands used for background estimation.
