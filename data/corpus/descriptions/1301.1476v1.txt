On the ψ(3686) (3.686 GeV), search for the M1 radiative transition ψ(3686) → γ η_c(2S), η_c(2S) → K_S^0 K^± π^∓ π^+ π^−, K_S^0 → π^+ π^−. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus the 3.65 GeV continuum data for non-resonant background; generate 1M-event exclusive MC for the signal, for the phase-space ψ(3686) → γ K_S^0 K^± π^∓ π^+ π^− background, and for ψ(3686) → π^0 K_S^0 K^± π^∓ π^+ π^−, all with K_S^0 → π^+ π^−. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, exactly three positive and three negative tracks, net charge 0; photons with EMC timing 0–14 and E > 25 MeV in both barrel and endcap, at least one. Reconstruct K_S^0 from all opposite-charge pion pairs via secondary-vertex fit, keeping the best mass-difference combination and requiring |M(π^+π^−) − M_K_S^0| < 10 MeV/c²; the remaining four tracks are identified with a χ²-sum PID using dE/dx and TOF, with χ²_min cut 4 and only K/π hypotheses. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γ K_S^0 K^+ π^− π^+ π^− with a four-momentum constraint and χ² < 200, flagged nominal, while the final selection applies χ²_total = χ²_4C + χ²_K + χ²_π < 60 (with a 3C variant leaving the photon energy free) downstream in ROOT. Also apply ROOT-level vetoes rejecting any π^+π^− recoil mass within 15 MeV/c² of J/ψ, any K_S^0 K^±π^∓ mass above 3.05 GeV/c², and any γπ^+π^− mass within 20 MeV/c² of η′; fit the K_S^0 K 3π mass spectrum over [3.30, 3.70] GeV/c² with η_c(2S), χ_cJ, and background shapes, with the continuum scaled by 3.6.
