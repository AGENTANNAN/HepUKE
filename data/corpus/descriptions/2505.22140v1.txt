On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), search for a dark baryon χ in Ξ− → π− χ(invisible), produced via J/ψ → Ξ− Ξbar+ with Ξbar+ → π+ Λbar, Λbar → pbar π+. Use the 1.0087×10¹⁰ J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, and generate a 500k-event exclusive MC of the full J/ψ → Ξ− Ξbar+ chain with χ invisible, for the five m_χ hypotheses 1.07, 1.10, m_Λ, 1.13, and 1.16 GeV/c². Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and three negative tracks with net charge ≥0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC timing 0–700 ns, shower |cosθ|<0.80 (barrel) or 0.86<|cosθ|<0.92 (endcap), isolated by >10° from charged tracks and >20° from antiprotons. PID uses the probability method, identifying pbar against π−/K− and π− against p/K. Reconstruct the single tag Ξbar+ with two secondary-vertex fits (Λbar → pbar π+ and Ξbar+ → Λbar π+, each χ²<200), selecting the best candidate by minimum |M(pbar π+)−M_Λ| + |M(Λbar π+)−M_Ξbar|, with |M(pbar π+)−M_Λ|<0.004 GeV/c² and the Ξbar+ recoil mass in (1.290,1.345) GeV/c². For the double tag, require exactly one additional π− and a missing χ, and perform a nominal kinematic fit constraining four-momentum conservation and the Λ and Ξbar+ masses (χ²<20), plus a 1C constraint of the missing mass to m_χ (χ²_π−χ<20), vetoing the competing Ξ− → π− Λ hypothesis by χ²_π−Λ > χ²_π−χ except when m_χ = m_Λ; the π− momentum window (0.070–0.192 GeV/c, per mass hypothesis) is optimized by Punzi significance.
