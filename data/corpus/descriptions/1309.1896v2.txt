At 13 center-of-mass energies from 3.900 to 4.420 GeV, study e+e− → π+π−h_c with h_c → γη_c and η_c reconstructed in 16 exclusive hadronic final states, in order to observe the charged charmoniumlike Zc(4020) and search for Zc(3900); use the corresponding real-data scan samples plus inclusive MC at 4.009, 4.180, 4.260 and 4.360 GeV, and generate 50k-event phase-space exclusive MC for every η_c decay mode. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with mode-dependent minimum charged multiplicities (typically ≥2, ≥3 or ≥4 positive and negative tracks), while photons must have E>25 MeV in the barrel / 50 MeV in the endcap, TDC in 0–14, more than 10° from any track, and at least one photon (three or five in modes with π0/η). Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying pions, kaons and/or protons as appropriate (e.g. ≥1 p+/p−, ≥1 π+/π−, ≥1 K+/K− for the relevant modes); K_S0 → π+π− candidates are built by a secondary-vertex fit minimizing the mass difference. Neutral π0 and η are formed in a Kalman fit constraining M(γγ) to the nominal mass with χ²<25, corresponding to a 15 MeV/c² mass window. A 4C kinematic fit of the full final state is then applied with χ²<200 in BOSS (the published tighter cuts of 35/20 are applied later), selecting the combination of minimum χ². Recoil-mass requirements M_recoil(π+π−) ∈ [3.45, 3.65] GeV/c² and M_recoil(γπ+π−) ∈ [2.8, 3.2] GeV/c², and η_c windows of ±50 MeV/c² (charged/K_S0 modes) or ±45 MeV/c² (modes with π0/η), are imposed.
