On the ψ(2S) resonance at √s = 3.686 GeV, study the hindered electromagnetic Dalitz decay ψ(3686) → e⁺e⁻ η_c with inclusive η_c decays, using the recoil-mass technique. Use the 3.686 GeV real data set and its inclusive MC, together with a dedicated exclusive signal MC generated from the ψ(2S) → e⁺e⁻ η_c, η_c → inclusive decay card. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two tracks and net charge zero; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, and EMC shower times required within [0, 700] ns. Identify electrons/positrons by the probability method, requiring L(e)>0.001 and L(e)/(L(e)+L(π)+L(K))>0.8, keeping all e⁺e⁻ candidates with at least one pair and e± momentum below 0.8 GeV/c. Veto ψ(3686)→π⁺π⁻J/ψ by rejecting any oppositely charged pair whose recoil mass, treating each track as a pion, lies in [3.090, 3.104] GeV/c²; veto π⁰ and η by removing γe⁺e⁻ combinations with invariant mass in [0.115, 0.150] GeV/c² and [0.505, 0.570] GeV/c² respectively; and reject γ conversions by requiring the e⁺e⁻ vertex R_xy < 2 cm and opening angle below 40°. Extract the η_c yield from an unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to the e⁺e⁻ recoil-mass distribution, modelling the signal with the η_c signal-MC shape convolved with a Gaussian and the background with a second-order Chebyshev polynomial plus peaking components.
