On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), search for invisible K_S0 decays via J/ψ → φ K_S0 K_S0, with φ → K+K−, one K_S0 tagged through its π+π− decay and the other K_S0 invisible. Use the 708_3097 J/ψ real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for J/ψ → φ K_S0 K_S0, φ → K+K−, K_S0 → π+π−. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 100 cm, Vr < 10 cm, exactly four charged tracks with net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC TDC in [0,14], angle to nearest track > 10°, no minimum photon multiplicity. PID by the probability method with K/π separation, requiring exactly 1 K+, 1 K−, 1 π+, 1 π−. Reconstruct the tagged K_S0 from a secondary vertex fit on π+π− (minimizing mass difference) and remove the used particles. Perform a kinematic fit including K+ K− and the tagged K_S0, treating the invisible K_S0 as a missing particle, with a four-momentum constraint and χ² < 200, flagged nominal. Additional ROOT-level cuts: φ mass in [1.00, 1.04] GeV/c², K_S0 mass in [0.486, 0.510] GeV/c², recoil mass of φ > 1.08 GeV/c², cosθ(φK_S0) in [-0.80, 0.80], |M_recoil(φK_S0) − M(K_S0)| < 40 MeV/c², and K_S0 decay length significance > 2; the signal is extracted from the E_EMC distribution with Bayesian upper limits.
