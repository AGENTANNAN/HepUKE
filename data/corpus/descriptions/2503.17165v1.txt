At the J/ψ and ψ(3686), study e+e− → Σ+ Σ̄− with Σ+ → p π0, Σ̄− → p̄ π0, and π0 → γγ. Use the 3.097 GeV J/ψ and 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1,000,000-event exclusive MC for each energy point with the same decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<2 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, and net charge zero; photons with EMC TDC 0–14, 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy thresholds, at least 10° from any track, and at least two photons, plus at least two isolated photons each at least 20° from prompt positive and negative tracks. Identify particles by probability (prob>0.001), separating protons from kaons and pions, and require exactly one proton and one anti-proton; the identified protons are removed from the generic charged lists and any remaining positive/negative tracks are assigned as π+/π−. Reconstruct π0 through a fit to the γγ invariant mass constrained to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<200, requiring at least one π0, then perform a 2C kinematic fit to p π0 p̄ π0 with four-momentum conservation and π0 mass constraints, χ²<30, using nominal masses. The signal regions are M(pπ0) ∈ [1.172, 1.200] GeV/c² and M(p̄π0) ∈ [1.167, 1.212] GeV/c², while a proton/anti-proton DCA>0.34 cm from the IP is noted but not applied in this selection.
