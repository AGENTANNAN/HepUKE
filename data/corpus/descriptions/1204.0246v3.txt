On the ψ(3686), study the two-photon transition ψ(3686) → γγ J/ψ with J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−. Use the 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each signal mode (J/ψ → e+e− and J/ψ → μ+μ−) and a 500k-event ψ(3686) → γχ_cJ, χ_cJ → γJ/ψ cascade sample, with 3.65 GeV continuum data used for background estimation. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, and net charge 0; photons must be EMC showers with E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.8) and E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), in a 700 ns timing window, at least 10° from any charged track, and exactly two photon candidates; each charged track must have p>0.8 GeV/c. Particle identification uses the probability method (probability cut 0.001), treating high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) as leptons—electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV (E/p>0.7), otherwise muon (E/p<0.6)—and requiring one positive and one negative lepton. Perform a nominal kinematic fit with a common vertex for the two leptons, a 1C constraint of the dilepton mass to the nominal J/ψ mass and a 4C constraint of the J/ψ plus two photons to the initial ψ(3686), accepting χ²/ndof < 12 (χ² cut 200) and vertex-fit χ²/ndof < 20. Identify J/ψ candidates via the two-photon recoil mass in (3.08, 3.14) GeV/c², and suppress backgrounds by requiring M_{γγ} > 0.15 GeV/c², diphoton recoil momentum > 0.25 GeV, and discarding events with M_{γ_sm recoil} > 3.6 GeV/c². Both J/ψ decay modes share the same selection chain.
