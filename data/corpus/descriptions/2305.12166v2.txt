On six low-energy points (2.3094, 2.3864, 2.3960, 2.5000, 2.6444 and 2.6464 GeV, total 179 pb⁻¹), study e⁺e⁻ → Δ⁺⁺Δ⁻⁻ and the semi-Δ channel Δ⁺⁺pbarπ⁻ + c.c., with Δ⁺⁺ → pπ⁺ and Δ⁻⁻ → pbarπ⁻, reconstructing the p pbar π⁺π⁻ final state; use the real data and inclusive MC at these energies, and generate 500k exclusive ConExc events for e⁺e⁻ → Δ⁺⁺Δ⁻⁻ with the same two-body Δ decays. Event selection requires exactly four charged tracks (net charge zero) with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm. Particle identification uses the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying protons against kaons and pions and pions against kaons, and requiring two proton candidates and two pion candidates. The tracks are assigned to p, pbar, π⁺, π⁻ and subjected to a nominal 4C kinematic fit to the p pbar π⁺π⁻ hypothesis with four-momentum conservation and χ²<50.
