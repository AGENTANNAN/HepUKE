On the J/ψ at √s = 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → γ η′, η′ → π+π− η, η → e+μ− / e−μ+. Use the 10.087×10^9-event J/ψ real data set and its inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive MC events for the full signal chain including both charge-conjugate η decays. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge zero; photons with barrel energy >25 MeV, endcap >50 MeV, at least one, and >10° from the nearest charged track. PID uses a combined dE/dx+TOF+EMC χ², identifies pions, and treats high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) as leptons—electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon—requiring 1 π+, 1 π−, 1 l+, 1 l−. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γπ+π−l+l− with χ²_{4C+PID}<36, and also fit the competing γπ+π−e+e− and γπ+π−π+π− hypotheses, requiring the signal χ² to be smaller than both to veto those backgrounds. Additional electron ID requires E/p>0.8 for the e± candidate, the μ∓ candidate must have EMC energy deposit in (0.1, 0.3) GeV, and the final M(π+π−l+l−) and M(l+l−) are restricted to the η′ (0.946, 0.970) GeV/c² and η (0.538, 0.558) GeV/c² signal windows. The same chain is used for both charge-conjugate η decays.
