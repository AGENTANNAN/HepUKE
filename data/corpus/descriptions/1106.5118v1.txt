At the J/ψ peak (3.097 GeV, 225.2×10^6 events) search for the CP/P-violating decays J/ψ → γ η, γ η', γ η_c, with η/η'/η_c → π+π− or π0π0. Use the real J/ψ data set and inclusive J/ψ MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for the charged γ π+π− topology (via η → π+π−) and the neutral γ π0π0 topology (via η → π0π0 → 4γ). Charged mode: require exactly one positive and one negative track with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, |Vr|<1 cm, plus at least one photon with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC timing 0–14 and ≥20° separation from any track; PID by the probability method (cut 0.001) requires one π+ and one π−, with pions selected as Prob(π)>Prob(K); then perform a 4C kinematic fit to γ π+π− (nominal, χ²<200) and a second 4C fit storing the χ² for the competing γ K+K− hypothesis for the ROOT-level comparison. Neutral mode: require no charged tracks and at least five photons with the same energy thresholds but no EMC timing cut, reconstruct two π0 from photon pairs with a Kalman fit constraining each pair to the nominal π0 mass (χ²<25), then perform a 4C fit to γ π0 π0 (nominal, χ²<200); the most energetic photon is taken as radiative, and ROOT-level steps include π0/ω mass windows, signal regions in m(π+π−) and m(π0π0), and the vetoes noted for the three pseudoscalar states.
