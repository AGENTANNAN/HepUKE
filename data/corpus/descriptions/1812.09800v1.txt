At √s = 4.600 GeV (567 pb−1), study e+e− → Ds+ D̄*0 K− and e+e− → Ds+ D̄0 K−, with Ds+ → K+K−π+; in the first channel D̄*0 → D̄0π0, π0 → γγ, and the channels are dominated by Ds1(2536)− → D̄*0 K− and Ds2*(2573)− → D̄0 K−, respectively. Use the 4.600 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes (ψ(4260) → Ds+ D̄*0 K− with Ds+ → K+K−π+, D̄*0 → D̄0π0, π0 → γγ; and ψ(4260) → Ds+ D̄0 K− with Ds+ → K+K−π+); real-data samples at 4.575, 4.527, 4.467, and 4.416 GeV are also prepared for the cross-section scan, though the selection is executed on the 4.600 GeV data. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge 0; PID by the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating π from K; no explicit photon selection is applied. No kinematic fit is performed; a partial reconstruction chooses the best Ds+ candidate by mass (nominal 1.96834 GeV/c2), and Ds+ → K+K−π+ candidates must lie in 1.955–1.980 GeV/c2. For the D̄*0 channel, use Ds+ sub-resonance regions A (M(K+K−)<1.05 GeV/c2, φπ+) and B (0.863<M(K−π+)<0.930 GeV/c2, K+K*0) combined for the cross section, veto Λc+ with RQ(Ds+)<2.59 GeV/c2, and require the D̄*0 recoil-mass window 1.993<RQ(K−Ds+)<2.024 GeV/c2; for the D̄0 channel, use only region A to suppress Λc+ and require 1.850<RQ(K−Ds+)<1.880 GeV/c2. The analysis notes record unbinned-ML fits to RQ(Ds+) for the Ds1(2536)− and Ds2*(2573)− parameters (S/D-wave BW convolved with resolution, ISR and vacuum-polarization corrections for the cross section), with Ds2*(2573)− J^P=2+ favored.
