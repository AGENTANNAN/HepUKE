On the ψ(3686) (3.686 GeV), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → γ X, X → e+e−, with the accompanying J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−, giving the final states γ e+e− e+e− and γ e+e− μ+μ−; the three χ_cJ (J=0,1,2) share identical topology and selection, so a single decay card (written for χ_c0) and one selection chain per lepton channel are used. Use the 3.686 GeV real data set and its inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two J/ψ decay modes (J/ψ → e+e− and J/ψ → μ+μ−), with the X decaying to e+e−. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks; photons with EMC timing within 0–14, an angle of more than 10° to any track, and energy thresholds of 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), requiring at least one photon. Particle ID uses the probability method with high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) identified as leptons, an electron if the EMC energy exceeds 0.6 GeV (0.4 GeV in the μ+μ− channel), otherwise a muon, requiring 2 l+ and 2 l−. A 4C kinematic fit (γ l+l−l+l−) is performed with four-momentum constraint, nominal flag, and χ²<200. Photon conversions are vetoed by requiring R_xy < 2 cm, and the χ_cJ channels are separated in the ROOT stage by E_γ and M(e+e−l+l−) windows.
