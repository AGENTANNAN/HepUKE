Study e+e− → Σ0 anti-Σ0 at the BESIII scan energies, with Σ0 → Λγ, anti-Σ0 → anti-Λγ, Λ → pπ−, anti-Λ → anti-pπ+. Use the 42 real-data points from 3.650 to 4.946 GeV, plus the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive ConExc mode-3 signal MC at each energy point. Select events with at least two positive and two negative charged tracks satisfying |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and at least two photons with barrel energy >25 MeV or endcap energy >50 MeV, EMC TDC 0–14 and angle to any track >10°. Particle ID uses the probability method (prob>0.001): first identify at least one proton and one antiproton against K/π, remove them, then identify at least one π+ and one π− against K/p from the remaining tracks. Build Λ and anti-Λ candidates from pπ− and anti-pπ+ via secondary vertex fits that minimize the mass difference, removing used tracks. Then perform a 4C kinematic fit to Λ anti-Λ γγ, requiring χ²<200. At ROOT level require |M(pπ−)−m_Λ|<5 MeV and vertex χ²<500 for the Λ/anti-Λ, choose the Λγ/anti-Λγ pairing that minimizes ΔM, and require |M(Λγ)−m_Σ0|<15 MeV; ISR and vacuum-polarization corrections and sideband background estimates are also applied.
