On the ψ(4260), study e+e− → π+π− X(3823), with X(3823) → γ χ_c1 or γ χ_c2, χ_c1/c2 → γ J/ψ, and J/ψ → e+e− / μ+μ−, all generated with proper ISR/FSR. Use the real data at 4.230, 4.260, 4.360, 4.420 and 4.600 GeV together with the matching inclusive MC, and produce 40k-event exclusive signal MC for each of the four modes (γχ_c1 or γχ_c2, each with J/ψ→e+e− or μ+μ−) at every energy point. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, at least two candidates, and at least 10° from the nearest charged track. PID by the probability method, high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, else muon), with π/K separation, requiring 1 π+, 1 π−, 1 l+, 1 l−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to the hypothesis e+e− → π+π− γγ l+l−, χ² cut 200, flagged nominal. The γχ_c1 and γχ_c2 modes share the identical selection chain and are separated only later by the M(γ_H J/ψ) window.
