At the ψ(2S) (3.686 GeV) study ψ(2S) → π+π− J/ψ with J/ψ → γ p p̄. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for ψ(2S) → π+π−J/ψ, J/ψ → γ p p̄ (phase space). Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 and Vr<10, with at least two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero. Photons must satisfy an EMC TDC window 0–14, energies above 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), be isolated from all charged tracks by more than 10°, and at least one such photon is required.
