On the ψ(3686), study e+e− → γ η_c(2S), with η_c(2S) → K_S0 K± π∓ (K_S0 → π+π−) and η_c(2S) → K+ K− π0 (π0 → γγ). Use 3.686 GeV ψ(2S) real data and inclusive MC, plus 3.65 GeV continuum real data and inclusive MC; generate 1,000,000-event exclusive MC for each of the two decay modes. For K_S0 K± π∓, require two positive and two negative charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge 0, photons with 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, angle to track >10°, and at least one photon, and PID by the probability method with prob>0.001, separating K from π/p and requiring at least one K+ and one K−; after removing kaons, treat the remaining charged tracks as π+π− and fit them to a K_S0 secondary vertex with |M(π+π−)−m_K_S|<7 MeV and decay length >2×vertex resolution, then perform a modified 3C kinematic fit to γ K_S0 K+ π− with a four-momentum constraint and χ²<200, nominal (photon energy floating, χ²_m3C<20). For K+ K− π0, require one positive and one negative charged track with the same |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge 0, photons with 25 MeV/50 MeV thresholds, TDC 0–14, angle to track >10°, and at least three photons, PID by probability method with prob>0.001, separating K from π/p and requiring exactly one K+ and one K−, and form π0 from two photons with a Kalman fit to the nominal π0 mass and χ²<15, requiring at least one π0; then perform a modified 4C kinematic fit to γ K+ K− π0 with a four-momentum constraint and χ²<200, nominal (photon energy floating, χ²_m4C<15). Both modes use nominal fits, with continuum subtraction and FSR correction applied.
