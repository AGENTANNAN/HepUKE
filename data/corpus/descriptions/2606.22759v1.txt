Measure the Born cross section for e+e− → p pbar via ConExc at 47 c.m. energies from 3.510 to 4.946 GeV (26 fb−1 total), using real data and matching inclusive MC at each scan point; the current configuration lists two representative points, 4.260 GeV (Y(4260)) and 3.773 GeV (ψ(3770)), and should be expanded to the full 47-point scan for production. Generate 100k-event exclusive ConExc signal MC per scan point using a vpho → p+p− PHSP decay card. Event selection: exactly one positive and one negative charged track, with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and net charge 0; no photon cuts are applied. Identify particles by the probability method with a 0.001 threshold, separating protons from kaons and pions, and require exactly one proton and one antiproton. The positive proton must also satisfy E/p<0.5, the p and pbar opening angle must exceed 3.1 rad, and both tracks must have muon-counter hit depth <40 cm. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to p+p− using the per-run measured c.m. energy, with χ² cut 200, and obtain efficiency and ISR/vacuum-polarization corrections iteratively from ConExc MC per energy.
