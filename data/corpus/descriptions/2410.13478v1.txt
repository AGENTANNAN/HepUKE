Using the 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) data set (BOSS 709) together with its inclusive MC, study the radiative decays ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ with χ_cJ → Σ+ Σ− η (J = 0, 1, 2), followed by Σ+ → p π0, Σ− → p̄ π0, η → γγ and π0 → γγ; generate 3M exclusive-MC events for the χ_cJ → Σ+Σ−η chain with the same decay card. Select charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 15 cm and Vr < 2 cm, requiring at least one positive and one negative track, and photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, at least 10° away from any charged track, with at least seven photons in the event. Identify protons against kaons and pions with the probability method (probability > 0.001), requiring at least one p and one p̄, and treat the remaining charged tracks as pions. Reconstruct at least two π0 candidates from photon pairs through a mass-constrained fit to the nominal π0 mass with χ² < 200. Perform a nominal 6C kinematic fit (four-momentum conservation plus the two π0 and the η mass constraints) on γ p p̄ π0 π0 γγ with χ² < 45, and use a competing 4C hypothesis including an extra photon as a background veto, requiring the signal χ² to be smaller. The radiative photon is chosen among the remaining photons as the one giving the largest ΔM, and the signal is selected in the Σ mass window M(pπ0) ∈ [1.174, 1.204] GeV/c² and the η window M(γγ) ∈ [0.517, 0.577] GeV/c², after vetoing χ_cJ → γ J/ψ and χ_cJ → Σ+Σ−π0 backgrounds.
