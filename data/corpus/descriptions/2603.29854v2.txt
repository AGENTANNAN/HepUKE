On the ψ(2S) energy scan, measure the e+e− → K+K− cross-section line shape using 495 pb−1 of BESIII real data at nine c.m. energies from 3.5815 to 3.7098 GeV. Generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC at each of the nine energy points for e+e− → K+K− (ConExc mode 45, K+K− PHSP). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; PID by the probability method with 0.001 probability cut, kaon identification against pion, requiring one K+ and one K−. Suppress Bhabha events with E/p<0.8, cosmic rays with |T(K+)−T(K−)|<3 ns, and extra tracks with |p_CMS−p(K+)−p(K−)|<0.07 GeV/c. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to K+K−, nominal, χ² cut 200. The signal region is M(K+K−) within 0.12 GeV of √s, used in a ROOT unbinned maximum-likelihood fit, with the line-shape fit including ISR and beam-energy-spread convolution.
