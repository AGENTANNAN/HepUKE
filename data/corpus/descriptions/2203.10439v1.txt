On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, search for ψ(3686) → π0 hc with hc → pp̅π+π−π0, hc → pp̅η (η → γγ and η → π+π−π0), and hc → pp̅π0. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the four final states (pp̅π+π−π0, pp̅η with η→γγ, pp̅η with η→π+π−π0, pp̅π0), all sharing the ψ(3686)→π0hc production. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least two positive and two negative tracks (total ≥4) for pp̅π+π−π0 and pp̅η(π+π−π0), and at least one positive and one negative track for pp̅η(γγ) and pp̅π0. Photons are required to have energy >25 MeV (barrel) or >50 MeV (endcap), TDC 0–14, angle to any track >10°, with at least six photons for pp̅π+π−π0 and at least four for the other modes. Particle identification uses the probability method, identifying p/p̅ against pions and, where present, π± against kaons, at a 0.001 probability cut. The selection then reconstructs π0→γγ with a mass-constrained fit (χ²<20), requiring at least three π0 for pp̅π+π−π0, at least one π0 and one η→γγ (χ²<200) for pp̅η(γγ), at least two π0 for pp̅η(π+π−π0) and pp̅π0, and for η→π+π−π0 builds the η from π+π−π0 in a 532–562 MeV window. Finally a 6C kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation plus the relevant π0/η mass constraints is applied to pp̅π+π−π0π0π0, pp̅π0η, or pp̅π0π0, with χ²<200 in the selection (final χ²_6C cuts of 45 for modes I and IIa and 64 for mode III are noted), flagged nominal.
