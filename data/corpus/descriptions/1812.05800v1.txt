On the ψ(3686) and J/ψ resonances, study ψ(3686)/J/ψ → p p̄ η′ with two η′ decay modes: η′ → γ π⁺π⁻ and η′ → η π⁺π⁻ with η → γγ. Use the 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) and 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data samples with their matching inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC for each of the four mode/resonance combinations (two η′ decay modes at each energy). Event selection: exactly four charged tracks with net charge zero (two positive, two negative), each with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm; photons with E>25 MeV and at least 10° away from any charged track, requiring at least one photon in the γπ⁺π⁻ mode and at least two in the ηπ⁺π⁻ mode. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying two pions and two protons (π⁺/π⁻ separated from K and p, p/p̄ separated from π and K). For the ηπ⁺π⁻ mode the two photons are first constrained to the nominal η mass with a Kalman fit, then a 4C kinematic fit is applied to p p̄ π⁺π⁻ γ(γ) with χ²<200, taking the combination with the smallest χ² when extra photons are present, and the η′ is selected in 920 < M < 1000 MeV/c² with a signal window 948.2 < M < 967.4 MeV/c² plus sidebands, with the yield extracted from a simultaneous unbinned ML fit to both η′ mass spectra. At ψ(3686) energy, veto ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ, ψ(3686) → π⁺π⁻ J/ψ and ψ(3686) → η J/ψ backgrounds via recoil-mass windows.
