At six centre-of-mass energies 4.178, 4.190, 4.200, 4.210, 4.220 and 4.226 GeV (6.32 fb−1 total), study the purely leptonic decays Ds+ → μ+νμ and Ds+ → τ+ντ with τ+ → π+ anti-ν, using real data and inclusive MC plus 500k-event exclusive signal MC for each leptonic mode (e+e− → Ds+ Ds− at ψ(4260), with the tag Ds− decaying inclusively). The analysis is a double-tag measurement: the Ds− is reconstructed in 13 hadronic tag modes, and the signal Ds+ is sought in the recoil system through the missing-mass-squared distribution. Tag-side charged tracks require |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, |Vxy|<1 cm (K_S0 and Λ daughters exempt); photons are EMC clusters with E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80) or E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), EMC time in (0,700) ns and more than 10° from any charged track; π0 candidates are γγ pairs with 0.115<M<0.150 GeV and a 1C mass-constrained fit χ²<20, while K_S0 candidates are π+π− pairs with a secondary-vertex fit, positive decay length and 0.485<M<0.510 GeV. Tag candidates use M_BC > 2.05 GeV/c² and |ΔE| within about ±3σ (mode-dependent), with K/π separation from combined dE/dx and TOF confidence levels (kaon if CL_K>CL_π, pion if CL_π>CL_K). On the signal side require exactly one positively charged track opposite the tag and no extra photon beyond the Ds* radiative photon (E_extra<0.3 GeV; the Ds*+ → γDs+ photon is selected in the Ds* rest frame within 119–149 MeV), with muon PID L'_μ>0.001, L'_μ>L'_e and L'_μ>L'_K for the muonic channel, whereas the tauonic channel requires a pion with CL_π>CL_K, CL_π>CL_p and muon veto L'_μ<L'_π. A four-momentum-conservation kinematic fit including the missing neutrino is applied with χ²<200; the muonic MM² peaks near 0 and the tauonic MM² near +1.4 GeV²/c⁴, and the absolute branching fractions follow from N_DT/(N_ST_tot ε_DT).
