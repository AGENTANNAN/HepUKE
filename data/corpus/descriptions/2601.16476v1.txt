Study e+e− → D_s^{*+} D_s−, with the signal D_s^{*+} → γ D_s+, D_s+ → γ K*+, and K*+ → K+π0 or K_S0π+, tagging the recoiling D_s− (charm −1) in the K_S0K, KKπ, or K_S0Kππ hadronic modes. Use real data and inclusive MC at 4.128, 4.157, 4.178, 4.189, 4.199, 4.209, 4.219, and 4.226 GeV (7.33 fb−1 total), and generate 150k exclusive-MC events for each K*+ sub-mode, K+π0 and K_S0π+, with the tag decay D_s− → anti-K0 K−; the decay chain includes π0 → γγ and K_S0 → π+π−. For the K+π0 channel, require at least three photons with a minimum photon angle of 10° and energy above 25 MeV, exactly one K+, and perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining M(γγ) to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<200. The most energetic photon is the radiative γ and must have Eγ>0.55 GeV; require M(K+π0) in 0.83–0.94 GeV/c², veto extra photons in the π0 0.115–0.150 and η 0.50–0.57 GeV/c² windows, require M(γγ_h)>0.62 GeV/c², and keep events with the single-tag recoil mass in the per-energy window (2.040–2.220 GeV/c² depending on E_cm). For the K_S0π+ channel, require at least one photon with a minimum photon angle of 10° and energy above 25 MeV, exactly two π+ and one π−, and perform the same 4C fit with M(π+π−) constrained to the K_S0 mass and χ²<200. Reconstruct K_S0 from two oppositely charged tracks assigned as π+π− without PID, requiring |Vz|<20 cm, vertex-fit χ²<100, M(π+π−) in 0.487–0.511 GeV/c², and decay length >2σ from the IP; also require the radiative γ to be the most energetic photon with Eγ>0.55 GeV, M(K_S0π+) in 0.83–0.94 GeV/c², the same π0/η extra-photon vetoes, and the same per-energy tag-recoil window.
