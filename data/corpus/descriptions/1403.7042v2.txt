Study the electromagnetic Dalitz decays J/ψ → P e+e− with P = η′, η, π0 at √s = 3.097 GeV in five independent final states: η′ → γπ+π−, η′ → π+π−η with η → γγ, η → π+π−π0 with π0 → γγ, η → γγ, and π0 → γγ. Use the 225.3 × 10^6-event J/ψ real data set and its inclusive MC, together with the 3.773 GeV continuum data (~2.9 fb−1) and its inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the five signal modes and 500k events for the peaking J/ψ → η′γ, η′ → γπ+π− conversion background. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, and Vr < 1 cm; the η′ → γπ+π−, η′ → π+π−η, and η → π+π−π0 modes require two positive and two negative tracks with net charge zero, while the η → γγ and π0 → γγ modes require one positive and one negative track with net charge zero. Photons must have EMC timing 0–14, be at least 10° from any charged track, and exceed 25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ| < 0.80) or 50 MeV in the endcap (0.86 < |cosθ| < 0.92), with at least one photon in the η′ → γπ+π− mode and at least two photons in the other modes. Electron identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying electrons against pions and kaons and requiring one e+ and one e−; in the three modes containing charged pions, the remaining tracks are taken as π+ and π− without further PID. Mode I performs a vertex fit on the charged tracks and a 4C fit to e+e−γπ+π− with χ2 < 200; modes II–V first apply a Kalman mass-constrained fit of a photon pair to the nominal η or π0 mass with χ2 < 25 and require at least one η/π0, then a vertex fit and a 4C fit to the corresponding e+e−π+π−(η/π0) or e+e−η/π0 final state with χ2 < 200. Additional suppression includes a photon-conversion veto with reconstructed conversion radius sqrt(Rx^2 + Ry^2) < 2 cm, |cosθ_decay| < 0.9 for the η/π0 → γγ modes, M(e+e−) ≤ 0.4 GeV/c^2 for J/ψ → π0e+e−, and a veto of 0.10 < M(γe+e−) < 0.16 GeV/c^2 in the η′ → γπ+π− mode; the published analysis ultimately applies χ2_4C < 100, while this first pass keeps the looser χ2 < 200, and the continuum QED background e+e− → e+e−γ(γ) and 3γ is modelled with Babayaga on the 3.773 GeV data sample.
