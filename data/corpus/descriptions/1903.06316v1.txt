On ψ(3770) at √s=3.773 GeV (2.93 fb⁻¹), study D⁰ → K⁻π⁺π⁰π⁰ using a double-tag technique with the tag anti-D⁰ → K⁺π⁻. Use the 712_3773 real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1M exclusive MC events for the full ψ(3770)→D⁰D̄⁰, D⁰→K⁻π⁺π⁰π⁰, D̄⁰→K⁺π⁻, π⁰→γγ process. Tag side selects anti-D⁰ → K⁺π⁻ with charm −1, ΔE ∈ [−0.03,0.02] GeV and m_BC ∈ [1.8575,1.8775] GeV/c². Signal side requires 4 photons for two π⁰, one K⁻ and one π⁺, photon angle >10° from charged tracks, photon energy >0.025 GeV, and total charge −1. The kinematic fit constrains four-momentum and both diphoton masses to the π⁰ nominal mass, with χ²<80; the code also records the paper’s signal-side K⁻π⁺ vertex fit χ²<100, m_BC [1.8600,1.8730] and ΔE [−0.04,0.02], diphoton mass window [0.115,0.150] with at least one barrel photon, K_S⁰ veto [0.458,0.520], photon timing <700 ns, and barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV. The implemented fit combines tag and signal (4C plus two π⁰ mass constraints, i.e. 6C) rather than the paper’s signal-side 3C fit.
