On the ψ(2S) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ′ → π0 J/ψ and ψ′ → η J/ψ, with π0/η → γγ and J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−. Use the 3.686 GeV real data (106.4M ψ′ events) and inclusive MC, plus 3.650 GeV continuum real data for QED background; generate 1,000,000-event exclusive MC for each of the four modes (π0 e+e−, π0 μ+μ−, η e+e−, η μ+μ−). Event selection: exactly two charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, one positive and one negative, net charge zero; at least two photons with EMC timing 0–14, angle to the nearest charged track >10°, and energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap). Lepton identification uses the probability method, with high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, else muon), requiring 1 l+ and 1 l−; the note specifies E/p-based identification (muons 0.08<E/p<0.22, electrons E/p>0.8). Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γγ l+l− with beam four-momentum constraint, χ²<200, flagged nominal. A J/ψ mass window 3.05–3.15 GeV/c² is applied after the fit; the π0 mode vetoes χ_cJ by excluding M(γ_h J/ψ) in 3.50–3.57 GeV/c², while the η mode requires M(γ_h J/ψ)<3.5 GeV/c², where γ_h is the more energetic photon. The π0 and η channels share the same selection chain.
