On the J/ψ(3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → e+e− η(1405), η(1405) → π0 f0(980), f0(980) → π+π−, π0 → γγ. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC (708_3097), and generate 500k exclusive signal MC events for this full chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks with net charge 0; photons with EMC timing 0–700 ns, at least 10° from any charged track, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, and at least two photons. PID by the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying electrons against kaons and pions, requiring one e+ and one e−, and the remaining positive/negative tracks are taken as π+/π−; additional ROOT-level cuts are noted: E/p>0.8 for electrons with p>0.8 GeV/c, a γ-conversion veto Rxy<2 cm, an f0(980) window on M(π+π−), and a π0 window of ±3σ. First perform a Kalman fit of the two photons to the π0 nominal mass with χ²<200 and at least one π0, then a 4C kinematic fit to e+ e− π+ π− π0 with χ²<20, flagged nominal.
