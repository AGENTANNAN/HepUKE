Study e+e− → Λc+ Λc− cross sections and electromagnetic form factors using a tag-based analysis. Use 12 real-data energy points from 4.6119 to 4.9509 GeV plus a 4.5995 GeV sample for double-tag studies, together with inclusive MC at 4.682 GeV; generate 200k-event exclusive MC per energy point for Λc+ → p K− π+ (PHSP). Event selection: tag a single Λc+ → p K− π+ candidate (single-tag method), leaving the other Λc− unreconstructed, and require zero photons on the signal side. Perform a 4C kinematic fit with χ² < 200. The single-tag yield is extracted from the M_BC distribution with an ARGUS background, while a double-tag 2D simultaneous fit to (M_BC+, M_BC−) across 9 energy points cancels the branching-fraction systematic.
