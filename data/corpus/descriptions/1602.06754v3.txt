On J/ψ and ψ(3686), study the single-baryon-tag reactions ψ → Ξ−Ξ+ and ψ → Σ(1385)−Σ(1385)+ (charge conjugates included), reconstructing the tag baryon as Ξ−/Σ(1385)− → π−Λ with Λ → pπ− and inferring the antibaryon from the recoil mass of the πΛ system. Use the 223.7M J/ψ (3.097 GeV) and 106.4M ψ(3686) (3.686 GeV) real data samples with their inclusive MC, and generate 1M-event exclusive MC for each of the four modes (J/ψ→Ξ−Ξ+, J/ψ→Σ(1385)−Σ(1385)+, ψ(3686)→Ξ−Ξ+, ψ(3686)→Σ(1385)−Σ(1385)+), including the measured α angular distribution. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least one positive and two negative tracks (or the charge-conjugate ≥2 positive, ≥1 negative); PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, requiring ≥2 π and ≥1 p identified against K and p. The pπ− combination is vertex-fitted to form Λ with χ²<500 and |M(pπ−)−M_Λ|<6 MeV/c², and the πΛ system is reconstructed, taking the candidate closest to the nominal mass. No kinematic fit is performed; instead a partial reconstruction requires the πΛ recoil mass in [1.312,1.332] GeV/c² (J/ψ Ξ), [1.308,1.338] GeV/c² (ψ(3686) Ξ), or [1.350,1.420] GeV/c² (Σ(1385)), and for ψ(3686) events an offline veto |M_recoil(π+π−)−M_J/ψ|>0.005 GeV/c² suppresses ψ(3686)→π+π−J/ψ.
