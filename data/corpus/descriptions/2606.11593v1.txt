On the 4.130–4.230 GeV energy scan (4.130, 4.160, 4.180, 4.190, 4.200, 4.210, 4.220, 4.230 GeV, total 7.33 fb⁻¹), study e⁺e⁻ → Ds*⁺Ds⁻ with Ds*⁺ → γDs⁺ (93.5%) or π⁰Ds⁺ (6.5%), Ds⁺ → τ⁺ν_τ, and τ⁺ → e⁺ν_eν̄_τ, μ⁺ν_μν̄_τ, π⁺ν̄_τ or π⁺π⁰ν̄_τ (25% each). Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive signal MC at each energy point with Ecm fixed to 4.178 GeV. Reconstruct the Ds⁻ tag in eleven hadronic modes (K_S⁰K⁻, K⁺K⁻π⁻, K⁺K⁻π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰K⁻π⁺π⁻, K_S⁰K⁺π⁻π⁻, π⁺π⁻π⁻, π⁻η, π⁻π⁰η, π⁻η′(→π⁺π⁻η), π⁻η′(→γρ⁰), K⁻π⁺π⁻), requiring charged tracks with |Vz|<10 cm, |Vxy|<1 cm and |cosθ|<0.93, K_S⁰→π⁺π⁻ with vertex-fit χ²<100, M(π⁺π⁻)∈[0.487,0.511] GeV/c² and decay length >2σ, and photons with E>25 (50) MeV in the barrel (endcap), |cosθ|<0.80 (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), >10° from any track and EMC time in [0,700] ns; π⁰/η from γγ pairs with 1C mass fits and windows, ρ⁰∈[0.500,0.970] GeV/c², η′→π⁺π⁻η∈[0.946,0.970] GeV/c² and η′→γρ⁰∈[0.940,0.976] GeV/c², with p>0.1 GeV/c for pions and photons from η′→γρ⁰, and the K⁻π⁺π⁻ mode vetoing M(π⁺π⁻)∈[0.480,0.515] GeV/c². The tag Ds⁻ must have recoil mass in [2.050,2.195] GeV/c² (keeping the candidate closest to the Ds*⁺ mass if multiple) and invariant mass within a mode-dependent ±3σ window. On the signal side, require the Ds*⁺ transition photon or π⁰, exactly one charged track identified as e⁺, μ⁺ or π⁺ (plus the π⁺ in the π⁺π⁰ mode), one to 48 photons with energy >25 MeV and >10° from tracks, and a massless missing system, with ΔE = Ecm − E_ST − E′_miss − E_γ(π⁰) constrained to [−0.2,0.2] GeV. A 4-momentum kinematic fit with χ²<200 is applied (adding a γγ→π⁰ mass constraint in the π⁺π⁰ mode), and particle identification uses MDC+TOF likelihoods: e⁺ with L(e)>0.001, L(e)/(L(e)+L(π)+L(K))>0.8, p>0.2 GeV/c and EMC/p>0.8; μ⁺ with L(μ)>0.001 and >L(K),L(e), EMC energy 0.1–0.3 GeV, p>0.5 GeV/c and MUC hit-depth requirements; π⁺ with L(π)>L(K), EMC/p<0.9, no π⁰, residual photon energy <0.3 GeV and |cosθ_miss|<0.9; and for τ⁺→π⁺π⁰, |M(π⁺π⁰)−m_ρ⁺|<0.2 GeV/c², residual photon energy <0.1 GeV and missing-mass squared ∈[3.82,3.98] GeV²/c⁴.
