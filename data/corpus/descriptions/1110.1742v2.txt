Study ψ(2S) → γ χ_c2 with χ_c2 → π+π− and χ_c2 → K+K− at 3.686 GeV. Use the 3.686 GeV ψ(2S) real data (106 M events, 156.4 pb−1) and inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event phase-space exclusive MC for each of the two modes, γ π+π− and γ K+K−, for normalisation in the PWA. Both modes require exactly one positive and one negative charged track with net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, and at least one photon with E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.8) or E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), at least 20° from any track and with EMC TDC 0–14, excluding showers in the barrel–endcap gap 0.8<|cosθ|<0.86; the highest-energy photon is taken as the radiative γ. PID uses the probability method with Prob>0.001: the ππ mode requires one π+ and one π− (against K and p), while the KK mode requires one K+ and one K− (Prob(K)>Prob(π) and Prob(K)>0.001). Both modes perform a 4C kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation under their nominal γ π+π− or γ K+K− hypothesis, χ²<200 (loose) and nominal flagged, and also store the competing hypothesis for later arbitration; the final tight selection uses χ²<60 and the smaller χ² of the two hypotheses. Electron backgrounds are suppressed by requiring each track EMC energy <1.4 GeV and dE/dx within 3σ of the expected value (tightened to 2σ in the EMC-insensitive region 0.81<|cosθ|<0.86 for the ππ mode), and in the ππ mode at least one track must have EMC energy >0.34 GeV to reject muon-pair backgrounds.
