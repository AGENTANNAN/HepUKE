At six energy points from 4.178 to 4.226 GeV, study e+e− → Ds+ Ds− with a tag-based single-tag plus missing approach, where the tag side is a hadronic Ds− reconstructed in 14 modes (e.g. KS0 K−, KS0 K− π+π−, K+K−π−, K+K−π−π0, KS0 K+π−π−, π+π+π−π−π−, etc.) and the signal is Ds+ → τ+ ντ, τ+ → π+ π0 ν̄τ, π0 → γγ. Use real data at 4.178, 4.189, 4.199, 4.209, 4.219 and 4.226 GeV, inclusive MC at 4.178 GeV, and 2,000,000 exclusive MC events for the full anti-Ds− hadronic-tag plus Ds+ → τ+ντ, τ+ → π+π0ν̄τ, π0 → γγ decay chain. On the signal side require exactly one positive charged pion, exactly two photons, a minimum photon angle of 10°, net charge +1, and one missing massless τ neutrino; no explicit track |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr or PID criteria are encoded at this level. Perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation and a π0 mass constraint on the two photons, accepting fits with χ² < 200. Further MM² signal/background discrimination, extra-photon vetoes, π0 mass window, and final χ² optimisation are deferred to the ROOT analysis, where branching fractions are extracted from simultaneous fits to the MM² distributions across the six energy points.
