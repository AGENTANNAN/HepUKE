Study ψ(3686) → Ξ0 Ξ̄0, with Ξ0 → π0 Λ, Ξ̄0 → π0 Λ̄, Λ → p π−, Λ̄ → p̄ π+, and π0 → γγ, using the 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC, and generating 5M exclusive-MC events for the full Ξ0Ξ̄0 decay chain. Event selection requires at least two positive and two negative charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; photons are selected with TDC 0–14, Eγ>25 MeV in the barrel and >50 MeV in the endcap, angle to the nearest charged track >10°, and at least four photons. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut: protons are identified against kaons and pions and required to have p>0.5 GeV/c, while pions are identified against kaons and protons and required to have p<0.5 GeV/c. Λ and Λ̄ candidates are formed by secondary vertex fits to pπ− and p̄π+ with minimization of the mass difference, removing used tracks, followed by a 6C kinematic fit to 4γ Λ Λ̄ with four-momentum conservation and two π0 mass constraints, χ²<200, flagged nominal. Additional suppression rejects events whose π0π0 recoil mass lies within 20 MeV/c² of the J/ψ mass; the best Ξ0Ξ̄0 pair is chosen by minimizing the summed mass-difference χ², with |M(π0Λ)−m_Ξ0|<15 MeV/c² and Λ decay length >0, including helix and efficiency corrections.
