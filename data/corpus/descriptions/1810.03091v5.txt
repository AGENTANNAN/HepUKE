At 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → e+ e− η with the η decaying to γγ (Mode I) or to π+π−π0 with π0→γγ (Mode II), using the J/ψ real data and inclusive MC together with 100k-event exclusive MC for each mode. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm; Mode I has exactly one positive and one negative track with net charge 0, while Mode II has exactly two positive and two negative tracks with net charge 0. Photons must have E>25 MeV in the barrel or >50 MeV in the endcap, be at least 10° from any track, and at least two are required; PID uses the probability method, treats tracks with p>1.0 as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon) and demands one l+ and one l−, with the non-lepton tracks in Mode II assigned as π+ and π−. A nominal 4C kinematic fit is then performed: Mode I fits l+l−γγ with a vertex fit on the two lepton tracks and χ²<100, while Mode II fits l+l−π+π−γγ with a vertex fit on the four charged tracks and χ²<100. Mass windows on the 4C-corrected momenta are η→γγ in [0.45,0.65] GeV for Mode I, and π0→γγ in [0.08,0.16] GeV plus η→π+π−π0 in [0.45,0.65] GeV for Mode II. Additional cuts include a photon-conversion veto (Δxy<2 cm) in both modes, resonance vetoes of 0.65<m(e+e−)<0.90 GeV and 0.96<m(e+e−)<1.08 GeV in both modes, and for Mode I only |cosθ_hel|<0.9 and p(e±)<1.45 GeV when m(e+e−)>0.5 GeV.
