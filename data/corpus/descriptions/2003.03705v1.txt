Measure the cross section for e+e− → η J/ψ using real data and inclusive MC at 23 energy points from 3.810 to 4.600 GeV. Reconstruct two modes: Mode I, η → γγ with J/ψ → e+e−, and Mode II, η → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ, with J/ψ → e+e−. A single 100k-event exclusive MC sample per scan point is generated for e+e− → η J/ψ (ConExc mode 80, J/ψ → e+e−) and used for both modes. Charged tracks must have |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm; Mode I requires exactly one positive and one negative track with net charge zero, while Mode II requires exactly two positive and two negative tracks with net charge zero. Photons must have EMC TDC 0–14, energy above 25 MeV (barrel) or 50 MeV (endcap), be at least 10° from any charged track, and number at least two. In both modes probability PID treats high-momentum tracks (p > 1.0 GeV) as leptons (electron if EMC energy > 0.6 GeV, otherwise muon), requiring one positive and one negative lepton; Mode II then removes these leptons and applies pion PID against kaons and protons with probability > 0.001, requiring at least one π+ and one π−. Mode II additionally forms a π0 from two photons via a Kalman fit constraining the γγ mass to the nominal π0 mass with χ² < 25, requiring at least one π0. Finally, Mode I is fitted to e+e−γγ and Mode II to e+e−π+π−π0, both with four-momentum conservation and the e+e− invariant mass constrained to the nominal J/ψ mass, χ² < 200, and the nominal fit flag.
