Study e+e− → Σ+Σ− at √s = 2.3960, 2.6454, and 2.9000 GeV with the corresponding real data (for 2.6454 GeV, combining the 2.6444 and 2.6464 datasets) and inclusive MC, and generate 6M-event exclusive ConExc samples at each energy point with vpho → Σ− anti-Σ+, anti-Σ− → anti-p π0, Σ+ → p π0, and π0 → γγ. The single-tag selection at 2.3960 GeV reconstructs only anti-Σ− → anti-p π0: charged tracks have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least one negative track; photons must be >20° from any track, pass 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) thresholds, and number at least two; PID uses the probability method with cut 0.001, identifies protons against kaons/pions (dE/dx only at this energy), and requires at least one antiproton. It then asks for at least two isolated photons (>20° from the antiproton track), a γγ fit to the π0 mass with χ²<25 and at least one π0, and combines anti-p π0, with π0 mass 0.126–0.139 GeV, ΔE −0.013–0.005 GeV, and signal Mbc 1.185–1.191 GeV/c². The double-tag selections at 2.6454 and 2.9000 GeV require at least one positive and one negative track, the same track, photon, and PID criteria (at least one proton and one antiproton), at least two isolated photons (>20° from either proton or antiproton track), and a γγ fit to the π0 mass with χ²<25 and at least one π0. They then perform a 2C fit to p anti-p π0 with one π0 missing, χ²<15, and choose the best Σ candidate as the minimum |M(p/anti-p π0) − M_Σ|, with M_Σ tag in 1.175–1.200 GeV/c² and the recoil mass used as the observable.
