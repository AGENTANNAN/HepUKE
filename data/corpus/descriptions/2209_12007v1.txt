Study e+e− → π+π− J/ψ with J/ψ → e+e− / μ+μ− using real data and inclusive MC at the three energy points 703_3810, 703_3872 and 703_3900 (≈3807.7, 3867.4 and 3896.2 MeV), the paper's 3871.3 MeV point being absorbed into the 3872 sample. Generate 200k exclusive MC events for the π+π−J/ψ final state using a ψ(4260) → π+π− J/ψ, J/ψ → e+e− PHSP generation with default cross section; the μ+μ− channel is noted as needing a separate sample. Select events with exactly two positive and two negative charged tracks, net charge zero, each with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm. Identify high-momentum tracks above 1.0 GeV as leptons, treating those with EMC energy above 1.1 GeV as electrons (otherwise muons), require one positive and one negative lepton, and remove these leptons from the charged-track list. The remaining tracks are identified as pions by the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut against kaons and protons, requiring one π+ and one π−. Perform a kinematic fit to π+π− l+l− with the l+l− invariant mass constrained to the nominal J/ψ mass and a four-momentum constraint, χ²<60, nominal fit. Run this selection on all three real-data samples, their inclusive MC, and the signal MC.
