At sqrt(s)=3.773 GeV, using 20.3 fb^-1 of psi(3770) data and the corresponding inclusive MC, we study the semileptonic decays D0 → K− e+ ν_e, D0 → K− μ+ ν_μ, D+ → Kbar0 e+ ν_e, and D+ → Kbar0 μ+ ν_μ (Kbar0 → K_S0 → π+ π−), with the opposite D meson reconstructed hadronically as a tag in six modes each for D0 and D+. Generate 1,000,000 exclusive MC events for each of the four signal modes. Tag selection requires mBC in [1.859,1.873] GeV/c^2 (D0) or [1.863,1.877] GeV/c^2 (D+), mode-dependent DeltaE windows of about ±3.5 sigma, and the smallest |DeltaE| combination. On the signal side, require one K− with one e+ or μ+ and net charge 0 for D0 modes, or π+π− (from K_S0) with one e+ or μ+ and net charge +1 for D+ modes, plus a missing neutrino; the π+π− pair is constrained to the K_S0 nominal mass. A 4C kinematic fit with χ² < 200 is applied (including the K_S0 mass constraint for D+ modes), and additional suppression cuts M(K− μ+) < 1.56 GeV/c^2 and M(K_S0 μ+) < 1.59 GeV/c^2 are used for the muon modes. Signal yields are extracted from fits to Umiss = Emiss − |pmiss|, with the D momentum constrained using the tag direction.
