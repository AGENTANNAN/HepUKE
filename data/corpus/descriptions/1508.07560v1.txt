On the ψ(3770) at √s = 3.773 GeV, measure absolute branching fractions and hadronic form factors in the double-tag channels ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar, with the semileptonic D0 → K− e+ νe or D0 → π− e+ νe and the D0bar reconstructed in five hadronic tag modes. Use the 2.92 fb−1 real data sample and the corresponding inclusive MC; generate 1M-event exclusive MC for each signal mode, with the tag D0bar decaying as a PDG-weighted cocktail of K+π−, K+π−π0, K+π−π−π+, K+π−π+π−π0 and K+π−π0π0. Tag selection uses charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vr|<1.0 cm, |Vz|<15.0 cm, kaon/pion PID from combined dE/dx and TOF confidence levels (CL_K>CL_π for kaons, CL_π>CL_K for pions when p<0.75 GeV/c; CL>0.1% above 0.75 GeV/c), and π0→γγ with photon energies above 25 MeV (barrel) or 50 MeV (endcap), in-time photons, photon-to-nearest-track angle >10° and a 1C mass-constrained fit with χ²<50; for K+π− additionally |t1−t2|<5 ns, opening angle <176° and sum E/p<1.4. Keep one tag per mode with the smallest |ΔE| in the per-mode windows (Kπ −0.049/+0.044, Kππ0 −0.071/+0.052, Kπππ −0.043/+0.043, Kππππ0 −0.067/+0.066, Kππ0π0 −0.082/+0.050 GeV) and M_BC in [1.858,1.875] GeV. On the signal side require exactly two oppositely charged recoil tracks with net charge 0, identified as one K− and one e+ (or one π− and one e+) plus a massless missing νe; positrons must satisfy CL_e>0.1% and CL_e/(CL_e+CL_π+CL_K)>0.8 using dE/dx, TOF and EMC shower information, while the hadron satisfies CL_K>CL_π or CL_π>CL_K. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the total four-momentum of the tag, K−/π−, e+ and νe system, with χ²<200, and reconstruct U_miss = E_miss − |p_miss| for the signal extraction, adding FSR photons within the positron direction and requiring any unused recoil photon to have E_max<300 MeV.
