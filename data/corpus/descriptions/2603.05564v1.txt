At √s = 4.395 and 4.416 GeV, study three T_ccbar(4020)^- channels: e+e− → D*0 D*− π+ with partial reconstruction (the π− from D*− → D̄0 π− is missed, D*0 → D0 π0 or D0 γ, and D0 → K−π+, K−π+π0, or K−π+π+π−), e+e− → π+π− J/ψ with J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−, and e+e− → π+π− h_c with h_c → γ η_c reconstructed through 16 hadronic η_c modes. Use the real data and inclusive MC at both energy points, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the three channels. Charged tracks require |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm, |Vz|<10 cm, and zero net charge; photons require E>25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), TDC within 0–14, and >10° from any track; PID uses the probability method with π/K (and p) separation. Channel 1 (D*0D*−π+) requires at least four tracks with ≥1 K+, ≥1 K−, ≥1 π+, ≥1 π−, a 1C fit of γγ to the π0 mass (χ²<200, ≥1 π0), then a kinematic fit with the missed D*− π− and the D0 mass constrained (χ²<200), together with windows on D0 (1.850–1.880 GeV/c²), π0 (0.120–0.145 GeV/c²), D*0 masses, and recoil masses, plus MQ(D0π+)>2.03 GeV/c² to veto D*+. Channel 2 (π+π−J/ψ) requires exactly four tracks with one π+, one π−, one l+ and one l−, where tracks with p>1.06 GeV/c are leptons (muon if EMC energy <0.35 GeV, electron if >1.1 GeV), a 4C fit constraining M(l+l−) to the J/ψ (χ²<40), a J/ψ window of (3.090–3.105) GeV/c², and vetoes cosθ(π+π−)<0.98, cosθ(πe)<0.98, and at least five MUC hit layers for muon candidates. Channel 3 (π+π−h_c) requires at least four tracks (4, 6, or 8 for the η_c modes), ≥1 photon, ≥1 π+ and ≥1 π−, a 4C fit of π+π−π+π−γ (χ²<35), K_S0 and π0/η mass windows, and h_c and η_c mass selections.
