On the ψ(2S) scan, study e+e− → p p̄ through the ConExc generator with the proton-antiproton pair produced via phase space, using nine real-data CM energy points from 3.581 to 3.710 GeV (about 495 pb⁻¹ total) together with matching exclusive MC generated at each point (100k events per mode, with the correct √s injected per scan point). Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.80 (barrel MDC only), |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track with net charge zero; PID uses the probability method (cut 0.001) separating protons from kaons and pions and demands one p and one p̄. Further suppress backgrounds with E/P < 0.5 on the raw EMC/MDC quantities for both candidates (rejecting Bhabha and dimuon events), a cosmic-ray veto of |T_p − T_p̄| < 3 ns from TOF, and a back-to-back requirement that the p p̄ opening angle in the e+e− CM frame lie within [178, 180]°. The signal yield is extracted by counting events in a symmetric 6σ window around p_mean = sqrt(s²/4 − m_p²), with σ ≈ 15 MeV taken from a double-Gaussian fit to signal MC.
