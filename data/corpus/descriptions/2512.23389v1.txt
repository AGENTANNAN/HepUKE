At √s = 3.773 GeV (ψ(3770)), use the 20.3 fb⁻¹ real data and corresponding inclusive MC to study double-tag D⁰D̄⁰ events, with the signal D⁰ → K_S⁰ π⁰ η and the tag D̄⁰ reconstructed in three hadronic modes (K⁺π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁰, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁺). Generate 500k exclusive signal MC events for ψ(3770) → D⁰D̄⁰, D⁰ → K_S⁰π⁰η, D̄⁰ → K⁺π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻, π⁰ → γγ, η → γγ; the three tag modes are otherwise used only for data/inclusive MC. Select tag candidates with 1.859 < M_BC < 1.873 GeV/c², and signal candidates with 1.858 < M_BC < 1.871 GeV/c² and −50 < ΔE < 45 MeV. Veto events where any pair of the four photons from π⁰ and η has an invariant mass in 0.115–0.150 GeV/c² to suppress π⁰–η photon mis-pairing. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the total four-momentum and the nominal D̄⁰, D⁰, K_S⁰, π⁰, and η masses, with χ² < 200, storing the fitted momenta; for multiple candidates choose the one with minimum |ΔE|. No separate charged-track |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr, photon-energy, or PID cuts are encoded beyond the tag-mode reconstruction, and the signal side uses the four photons from π⁰ and η with no additional photons.
