Measure e+e− → D_s^{*+} D_s^{*−} Born cross sections at 76 c.m. energies from 4.226 to 4.95 GeV, with 15.67 fb^{-1} total luminosity, using real data (including a representative 4.226 GeV sample) and inclusive MC; generate 500k exclusive MC events for the signal mode, with D_s^{*±} → γ D_s^± and D_s^± → K^+K^-π^±, covering both D_s^+ and D_s^- decays. The analysis is semi-inclusive: reconstruct one D_s^{*±} per event. Select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm, and identify kaons and pions using probability PID (prob >0.001, K against π/p, π against K/p), requiring K^+K^-π^± candidates with at least two kaons and one pion. Select photons with energy above 25 MeV for the D_s^* radiative transition. Build D_s candidates in |M(K^+K^-π^±) − m_{D_s}| < 15 MeV and then D_s^* candidates from γD_s. Use the modified missing mass M_miss = m_miss + m(γK^+K^-π^±) − m_{D_s^*} and require |M_miss − m_{D_s^*}| < 5σ_{M_miss}(E_CM), extracting the signal yield from a fit to the M(γK^+K^-π^±) distribution, with Born cross sections corrected for ISR/VP effects using an iterative line-shape treatment.
