On the ψ(2S) at 3.686 GeV, measure absolute branching fractions of Ω⁻ decays through ψ(2S) → Ω⁻ Ω̄⁺, tagging the Ω̄⁺ (Ω⁺ → ΛK⁺) as a single tag and studying three Ω⁻ double-tag modes: Ω⁻ → Ξ⁰π⁻, Ξ⁰ → Λπ⁰ (A); Ω⁻ → Ξ⁻π⁰, Ξ⁻ → Λπ⁻ (B); and Ω⁻ → ΛK⁻ (C). Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, together with 1M-event exclusive MC generated for each of the three signal decay chains (all including Ω⁺ → ΛK⁺ and Λ → pπ⁻). Charged tracks are required with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least 2 positive and 3 negative tracks; PID uses the probability method (0.001 cut) identifying protons against π/K and kaons against π/p, requiring ≥1 K⁺ in modes A and B or ≥1 K⁺ and ≥1 K⁻ in mode C, with remaining positives/negatives assigned as π⁺/π⁻. Photons (modes A and B) are selected with EMC timing 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV / endcap >50 MeV, angle to any track >10°, and at least two photons. Λ candidates are built from pπ⁻ via a secondary-vertex fit minimizing the mass difference with |M(pπ⁻) − m_Λ| < 11 MeV, and π⁰ (modes A, B) is reconstructed from two photons via a 1C fit to the nominal π⁰ mass with χ²<200 and M(γγ) in [0.115, 0.150] GeV. A final 4C kinematic fit with χ²<200 is applied to ΛK⁺π⁻Λπ⁰π⁻ (A), ΛK⁺π⁻Λπ⁰ (B), or ΛK⁺ΛK⁻ (C); the Ω⁺ tag is selected in the ΛK⁺ mass window [1.664, 1.680] GeV, the signal π⁻ in modes A and B is the highest-energy π⁻, and yields are extracted from two-dimensional fits of M(ΛK⁺) versus the signal-side recoil mass.
