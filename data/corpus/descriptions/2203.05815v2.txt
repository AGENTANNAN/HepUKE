On the 4.23–4.70 GeV energy scan, study e+e− → π+π− ψ2(3823), ψ2(3823) → γ χc1, χc1 → γ J/ψ, J/ψ → ℓ+ℓ− (e+e− or μ+μ−). Use the 20 real-data points (4.2263–4.6984 GeV) with their matching inclusive MC, and generate 50k-event exclusive MC at each point with the full decay chain. Event selection: exactly two positive and two negative charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; at least one photon with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC TDC in [0,14] and opening angle to the nearest track >10°; PID by the probability method, identifying e± and μ± against pions with probability >0.001, both lepton hypotheses combined for lepton universality. Perform a kinematic fit to π+π−ℓ+ℓ−γγ with a four-momentum constraint, χ² cut 200, flagged nominal (with track helix-parameter corrections applied to MC). J/ψ candidates are taken in 3.06 < M(ℓ+ℓ−) < 3.135 GeV/c², with γ-conversion (cos of π+π− opening angle <0.98), ηJ/ψ (M(γγ_miss π+π−) > 0.65 GeV/c²) and ψ(2S) (|M(π+π−J/ψ) − m[ψ(2S)]| > 7 MeV/c²) vetoes.
