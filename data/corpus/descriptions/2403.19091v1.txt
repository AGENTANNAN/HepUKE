At ψ(3770), 2.93 fb⁻¹, study the semileptonic decays D0→K_S0π−π0e+νe and D+→K_S0π+π−e+νe with a double-tag method, where one D (anti-D0 or D−) is reconstructed from hadronic tag modes—K+π−, K+π−π0, K+π−π+π−, K+π−π+π−π0 for anti-D0, and K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S0π−, K_S0π−π0, K_S0π+π−π−, K+K−π− for D−—and the signal D is built from the remaining tracks. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive signal MC for each mode, ψ(3770)→D0D0bar with D0→K_S0π−π0e+νe (ISGW2) and ψ(3770)→D+D− with D+→K_S0π+π−e+νe (ISGW2), with K_S0→π+π− and π0→γγ. On the signal side require net charge 0; for D0 select 2 photons plus one π+, two π−, and one e+ with missing νe, while for D+ select no photons plus two π+, two π−, and one e+ with missing νe. Reconstruct K_S0 from π+π− with a secondary-vertex fit, flight distance >2σ, and mass in (0.486,0.510) GeV/c²; in the D0 channel reconstruct π0 from γγ with photon energies >25 (50) MeV in barrel (endcap), mass in (0.115,0.150) GeV/c², and a π0 mass-constrained fit. Positron PID uses L'(e)>0.001, L'(e)/(L'(e)+L'(π)+L'(K))>0.8, and E/p>0.18×χ²(dE/dx)+0.32; photons within 5° of the e+ with E>50 MeV are recovered into the e+ four-momentum. Apply signal-side suppression cuts: for D0, π0 energy >0.22 GeV, |cosθ(π0)|<0.83, and M(K_S0π−π0π+) with the positron assigned the pion mass <1.78 GeV/c²; for D+, M(K_S0π+π−π+) with the positron assigned the pion mass <1.83 GeV/c², cosθ(e+,π−)<0.95, M(K_S0π+π−π+π0) with the positron assigned the pion mass <1.4 GeV/c², and cosθ(missing momentum, most energetic unused shower)<0.88. Finally perform a 4C kinematic fit with D mass constraints, χ²<200, and extract yields from a 2D unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to M²_miss versus M(K_S0ππ).
