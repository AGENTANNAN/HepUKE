On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → Ξ(1530)− anti-Ξ(1530)+ (and Ξ(1530)− anti-Ξ+) via single-baryon tagging with partial reconstruction, reconstructing the tag chain Ξ(1530)− → π− Ξ0, Ξ0 → π0 Λ, Λ → p π−, π0 → γγ, and inferring the recoil anti-baryon. Use 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for ψ(3686) → Ξ(1530)− anti-Ξ(1530)+ with the full decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm, at least one positive and at least two negative tracks; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds and TDC 0–700, at least two. PID by the probability method with prob>0.001, proton against kaon/pion and pion against kaon/proton, requiring at least one proton and at least two π−, with remaining positive tracks assigned as π+. Perform a 1C kinematic fit of the two photons to the π0 nominal mass, χ²<20, requiring at least one π0; build Λ from p π− by a secondary vertex fit minimizing mass difference; then select Ξ0 and Ξ(1530)− candidates by closest nominal masses 1.31486 and 1.535 GeV, and require recoil mass in 1.20–1.70 GeV. The notes also specify Ξ0/Ξ− windows of 10 MeV/c², a Λ window of 5 MeV/c², and an Ξ(1530)− window of 15 MeV/c²; the π0Ξ− tag mode requires a separate decay card and is combined in the full analysis.
