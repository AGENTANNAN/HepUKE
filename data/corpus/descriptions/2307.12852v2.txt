At 4.128–4.226 GeV (eight energy points, 7.33 fb⁻¹) study Ds+ → η(') μ+νμ with a tag-based missing-mass analysis. Use the real data at all eight points with the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive signal MC for each mode: Ds+ → η μ+νμ, η → γγ, and Ds+ → η' μ+νμ, η' → γπ+π-, with ISGW2 form factors and the η/η' decays treated as phase space. Reconstruct the Ds- tag in 14 hadronic modes (KKπ, KKππ0, KsK, KsKπ0, KsKsπ, KsKππ, πππ, η(γγ)π, η(πππ0)π, η'(γγ)πππ, η'γπππ, η(γγ)ρ, η(πππ0)ρ). On the signal side require two photons (η→γγ) and at least one μ+ with a missing νμ, or one photon plus π+π- and at least one μ+ with a missing νμ (η'→γπ+π-), with photon energy above 25 MeV. Perform a 3C kinematic fit imposing four-momentum conservation and, in the η mode, constraining the γγ invariant mass to the nominal η mass, with χ²<200 (the η' mode additionally requires χ²<30, with the γπ+π- mass constrained to the η', to suppress non-DsDs* background). Apply the ROOT-level requirements: muon EMC energy 0.10–0.28 GeV, no extra charged tracks, no extra π0, extra-photon energy below 0.2 GeV, and peaking-background vetoes M(ημ+)<1.8 GeV/c² and M(ηνμ)>0.97 GeV/c² (for η': M(η'μ+)<1.8 and M(η'νμ)>1.27 GeV/c²); the η→π0π+π- and η'→ηγγπ+π- sub-modes are treated in separate analyses and combined in the ROOT fit, while the soft transition photon from the Ds* is chosen by minimum |ΔE| inside the tagging.
