On ψ(3770) → D0 anti-D0, study D0 → ω φ, with ω → π+π−π0, φ → K+K−, π0 → γγ, at √s = 3.773 GeV using a single-tag technique in which one D0 is fully reconstructed and the anti-D0 decays generically. Use 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for D0 → ω φ with the full ω/φ/π0 decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, at least two, and at least 10° from any charged track. PID by the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating pions from kaons (π against K, K against π), requiring at least 1 π+, 1 π−, 1 K+, and 1 K−. Reconstruct π0 → γγ with a Kalman kinematic fit constraining M(γγ) to the nominal π0 mass, using a pre-fit window of 0.115–0.150 GeV/c², then perform a 4C kinematic fit to π+π−π0K+K− with nominal four-momentum constraint and χ² < 200. Select D0 candidates with M_BC > 1.84 GeV/c² and −0.03 < ΔE < 0.02 GeV, choosing the best by minimum |ΔE| and vetoing K_S0 for M(π+π−) in 0.490–0.503 GeV/c²; signal yields are extracted from a 2D unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to M(π+π−π0) versus M(K+K−) in M_BC signal and sideband regions, with the longitudinal polarization fraction f_L measured from cosθω and cosθK in five bins.
