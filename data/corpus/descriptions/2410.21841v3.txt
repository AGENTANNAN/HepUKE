At the J/ψ (√s=3.097 GeV), search for Λ–Λbar oscillation via J/ψ→ΛΛbar (right-sign) and J/ψ→ΛΛ (wrong-sign), with Λ→pπ− and Λbar→pbarπ+. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate exclusive MC samples: 10M J/ψ→ΛΛbar, 1M J/ψ→ΛΛ for the oscillation mode, and 0.5M J/ψ→ΛΣbar0 + c.c. peaking background (Λ→pπ−, anti-Σ0→γ anti-Λ0, anti-Λ0→pbarπ+). Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and at least four tracks; PID is probability-based with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against kaons/pions and requiring at least one proton and one antiproton, after which the remaining positive and negative tracks are taken as π+ and π−. Reconstruct Λ and Λbar via secondary-vertex fits to pπ− and pbarπ+ by minimizing the mass difference, removing used particles, and retain candidates in a ±3σ mass window (σ=1.8 MeV). Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to ΛΛbar with χ²<50, keeping the pair with minimum χ² if multiple combinations survive. Signal extraction is a simultaneous fit to M(pπ−) and M(pbarπ+) in ROOT.
