On the J/ψ, study e+e− → φη with φ → K+K− and η → γγ at 26 scan points, currently using the five identifiable points (2950, 2981, 3000, 3020, 3080 MeV). Use real data and inclusive MC at these points; generate 50k-event exclusive ConExc signal MC per point for the φη mode (mode 23) with φ → K+K−, η → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with at least one positive and one negative, net charge 0, |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 100 cm, Vr < 10 cm; photons with EMC time 0–700 ns and angle to the nearest track > 10°, energy threshold 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), and at least two photons. PID by the probability method (prob > 0.001), identifying kaons against pions and protons, requiring at least one K+ and one K−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit on K+K−γγ with four-momentum constraint and χ² < 200; the η mass window |M(γγ)−M_η| < 30 MeV and φ mass window are applied as post-fit cuts in the ROOT analysis, along with the paper's tighter χ² rejection of 85.
