Study e+e− → Xi(1530)^0 anti-Xi^0 across a cross-section scan, with Xi(1530)^0 → Xi− π+, Xi− → Λ π−, Λ → p π−, anti-Xi^0 → anti-Λ π0, anti-Λ → anti-p π+, and π0 → γγ. Use the real-data scan points between 3.51 and 4.95 GeV (44.2 fb^-1 total); only exclusive MC is generated, 100k events at each scan point for this full decay chain. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, with at least two positive and two negative tracks, and particle identification by the probability method (cut 0.001) separates protons and pions, requiring at least 1 p, 1 anti-p, 1 π+, and 2 π−. Reconstruct Λ from p π− and Xi− from Λ π− through secondary-vertex fits, then form Xi(1530)^0 from Xi− π+ by minimizing the mass difference; the Λ window is |M(pπ−)−m_Λ|<7 MeV with vertex fit χ²<500 and decay length >0, and the Xi− window is |M(Λπ−)−m_Xi−|<6.5 MeV, also with decay length >0 and best candidate closest to the nominal Xi− mass. Apply a 4C kinematic fit to Xi− π+ with χ²<200, and select Xi(1530)^0 by minimizing |M(Xi−π+)−m_Xi(1530)| with a ±13 MeV window; tag anti-Xi^0 by recoil mass against Xi(1530)^0 using |M_recoil−m_anti-Xi0|<25 MeV, with the signal region [1.277,1.352] GeV/c^2 and sidebands [1.440,1.490] and [1.165,1.190] GeV/c^2. A Xi(1530)^0 sideband [1.602,1.658] GeV/c^2 with scale factor 0.52 is used for background subtraction in the single-baryon tagging approach, where only the Xi(1530)^0 side is reconstructed and the anti-Xi^0 is extracted from the recoil mass spectrum.
