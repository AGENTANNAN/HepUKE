On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → Ω− K+ anti-Ξ0 (+c.c.) through partial reconstruction of Ω− K+ and the recoil mass against anti-Ξ0, with Ω− → Λ K− and Λ → p π−. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1,000,000 exclusive signal MC events for this mode with the decay chain ψ(3686) → Ω− K+ anti-Ξ0, Ω− → Λ K−, Λ → p π− (anti-Ξ0 → anti-Λ π0 is in the decay card but anti-Ξ0 is not reconstructed). Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least two positive and two negative tracks. Particle identification uses the probability method: protons against kaons and pions with at least one proton, kaons against pions, and pions against kaons and protons; the K+ from the interaction point must have |Vz|<10 cm and |Vxy|<1 cm, with the highest kaon confidence if multiple candidates, proton candidates satisfy CL_p > CL_K, CL_p > CL_π, and CL_p > 0.001, and remaining tracks are taken as pions. A secondary vertex fit forms Λ from p π− by minimizing the mass difference and removes used particles, with the Λ mass window 1.111–1.121 GeV. The Ω− is reconstructed from Λ K− with a common vertex, Ω− vertex χ² < 200, and reduced mass M(Λ K−) − M(p π−) + M_Λ_PDG in 1.663–1.681 GeV, choosing the candidate closest to the nominal Ω− mass. The anti-Ξ0 is not reconstructed; instead require the recoil mass against Ω− K+ in 1.282–1.352 GeV. No global nC kinematic fit or photon selection is applied; continuum background is subtracted using 3.773 GeV data with luminosity and cross-section scaling (n=1, 1/s dependence).
