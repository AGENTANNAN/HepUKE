On the ψ(3770), study quantum-correlated D0–anti-D0 decays for a CKM angle γ measurement, with signal D0→Ks π+π− and D0→Ks K+K−, each paired against a tag anti-D0 decaying to flavor tags (Kπ, Kππ0, Kπππ), CP-even tags (KK, ππ, Ksπ0π0, πππ0), CP-odd tags (Ksπ0, Ksη, Ksη′, Ksω), or other self-conjugate Ks h+h− modes (Ksππ, KsKK). Use the ψ(3770) real data and inclusive MC at 3.773 GeV (dataset 712_3773), and generate 500,000-event exclusive MC for each signal mode, D0→Ks π+π− and D0→Ks K+K−, with the tag anti-D0→K+π− and K_S→π+π−. The selection is a double-tag analysis: for each signal mode, the signal D0 (charm +1) is combined with a tag anti-D0 (charm −1) chosen from the above modes, with the tag candidate ranked by invariant mass. A four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit is performed with χ²<200, and D, K_S, and K_L masses fixed to their PDG values. The notes specify that missing K_L is treated via M_miss² or U_miss, signal yields are extracted from M_BC fits, Dalitz efficiency is taken from phase-space MC, per-event optimal Fourier weights are applied, and peaking backgrounds for the KsKK channel are handled at the fit stage; no explicit charged-track, photon, or PID cuts are encoded in this DSL.
