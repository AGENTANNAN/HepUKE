On the ψ(2S) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ′ → γ χ_cJ → γ p p̄ K+ K− for J=0,1,2, using 106×10^6 real ψ(2S) events and 100×10^6 inclusive ψ(2S) MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the direct χ_c0, χ_c1 and χ_c2 → p p̄ K+ K− modes, plus 200k events each for χ_c1 → p̄ K+ Λ(1520) (Λ(1520) → p K−) and χ_c1 → p p̄ φ (φ → K+ K−). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero; photons with TDC 0–14, more than 10° from the nearest charged track, barrel/endcap energy above 80 MeV and at least one photon candidate. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying one proton against kaon/pion, one antiproton against kaon/pion, one K+ against pion/proton and one K− against pion/proton, requiring 1 p, 1 p̄, 1 K+ and 1 K−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γ p p̄ K+ K− (energy-momentum conservation, nominal), retaining the photon combination with smallest χ²_4C and applying a loose χ² cut of 200, with the analysis-level χ² and χ_cJ mass windows (χ_c0: 3.365–3.455, χ_c1: 3.490–3.530, χ_c2: 3.530–3.580 GeV/c²) applied later; for the direct branching fraction, veto intermediate Λ(1520), Λ̄(1520) and φ with |M(pK−)−1.52|>0.07, |M(p̄K+)−1.52|>0.07 and |M(K+K−)−1.02|>0.03 GeV/c². The same selection chain is used for all three χ_cJ states.
