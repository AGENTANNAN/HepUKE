At the J/ψ peak (√s = 3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → Λ Σ0 + c.c., with Σ0 → γΛ, Λ → pπ− and Λbar → anti-pπ+. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k exclusive MC events for this decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, net charge zero; photons with TDC in 0–14, energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), at least one photon, and angle to nearest track >10°. No particle identification is applied: one positive track is assigned as proton, removed from the positive list, and the remaining tracks are treated as π+ and π−. Reconstruct Λ and Λbar via secondary vertex fits of pπ− and anti-pπ+, minimizing the mass difference, then perform a 4C kinematic fit to γΛΛbar with χ²<200, using mass windows |M(pπ−)−M_Λ|<5 MeV/c² and M(γΛ)>1.135 GeV/c², and if multiple candidates pass, select the one with smallest sqrt((M_pπ− −M_Λ)^2 + (M_anti-pπ+ −M_Λ)^2); the final selection applies χ²_4C<30.
