On the ψ(3686)/ψ(3770) energy range, measure inclusive e+e− → J/ψ X cross sections at 69 c.m. energies from 3.645 to 3.891 GeV, reconstructing the J/ψ via J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−. Use real data at the major points 3.650, 3.682, 3.686, 3.773 and 3.800 GeV together with the corresponding inclusive MC, the full scan sample being assembled from the 3.645–3.891 GeV scan data. Generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two J/ψ leptonic modes at 3.686 GeV; signal generation uses KKMC for e+e− → ψ(3686)/ψ(3770) with ISR followed by EvtGen for the ψ decays. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, |Vr|<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC in [0,14], more than 10° from the nearest track, at least one required. PID identifies high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.7, else muon), requiring at least one l+ and one l−. No conventional kinematic fit is applied — the J/ψ yield comes from a fit to the l+l− invariant-mass spectrum, with additional quality cuts imposed in the ROOT stage: e E/p>0.7, μ 0.05<E/p<0.35, lepton |cosθ|<0.81, opening angle <179°, lepton momentum between 1 GeV and 0.47·E_cm, π/K separation by dE/dx+TOF (CL(π)>CL(K)), and an event topology of exactly two tracks plus ≥1 photon or 3–4 charged tracks; selection efficiency is 58.8–60.8%.
