At √s=3.773 GeV, search for dark photons in untagged ISR e+e− annihilation by studying e+e−→γ_ISR μ+μ− and e+e−→γ_ISR e+e−, where the ISR photon is not required to be reconstructed in the EMC and is instead inferred from energy-momentum conservation. Use the 2.93 fb−1 ψ(3770) real data and the corresponding inclusive MC sample at 3.773 GeV, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two ISR modes from a ψ(4260)→ℓ+ℓ−γ phase-space decay card. For both channels require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.921, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge zero, and reject tracks with transverse momentum below 0.3 GeV/c to suppress spiraling. Particle ID uses a probability-based method with high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) treated as leptons, an electron if the EMC energy exceeds 0.6 GeV and otherwise a muon (with muons selected by P(μ)>P(e)), together with π/K separation, requiring exactly one positive and one negative lepton; the e+e−γ channel identifies the leptons as electrons with a large EMC deposit (E/p>0.8). Perform a nominal 1C kinematic fit to the ℓ+ℓ− system with the photon as a missing track and a four-momentum constraint, applying χ²<20 for μ+μ−γ and χ²<5 for e+e−γ, with no explicit mass window. Downstream, the fit-predicted missing photon must have polar angle θγ<0.1 or θγ>π−0.1 rad for μ+μ−γ and θγ<0.05 or θγ>π−0.05 rad for e+e−γ, and the e+e−γ yield is corrected for Bhabha scattering in m(e+e−) bins by 2%–8% using PHOKHARA and BABAYAGA@NLO.
