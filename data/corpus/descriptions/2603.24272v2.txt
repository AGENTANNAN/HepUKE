At √s = 3.097 GeV (the J/ψ resonance), measure antineutron-proton scattering n̄p → K⁺K⁻π⁺ and n̄p → K⁺K⁻π⁺π⁰, using antineutrons tagged through J/ψ → p π⁻ n̄. Use the 708_3097 J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, plus a 2M-event exclusive MC for J/ψ → p⁺π⁻n̄ generated in phase space. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring at least two positive and two negative tracks; PID by the probability method (prob>0.001) separating proton, kaon and pion, demanding ≥1 p, ≥1 π⁻, ≥1 K⁺, ≥1 K⁻ and ≥1 π⁺; photons with EMC TDC in 0–14, angle to the nearest track >10°, and energy above 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap). For the K⁺K⁻π⁺π⁰ mode additionally require ≥2 photons and form the π⁰ with a Kalman fit constraining the γγ invariant mass to the nominal π⁰ mass (χ²<50, ≥1 π⁰). Tag the antineutron with a secondary vertex fit on p π⁻ (χ²<5) building a virtual pπ⁻ tag by minimizing the mass difference, then a 1C kinematic fit constraining the recoil mass against p π⁻ to the nominal n̄ mass (χ²<11). Fit the K⁺K⁻π⁺ vertex (χ²<6) as the scattering point, requiring it to match the expected n̄ impact point on the beam pipe within |Δr|<0.3 cm and |Δz|<2.0 cm, and select scattering on ¹H in the oil layer by P(p)=|P(K⁺K⁻π⁺[π⁰])−P(n̄)|<0.04 GeV/c; the final kinematic fit on p π⁻ K⁺K⁻π⁺(π⁰) uses a χ² cut of 200. Both modes share the same tagging and scattering-selection chain.
