Measure inclusive prompt J/ψ and ψ(3686) cross sections in e+e− annihilation at 49 energy points from √s=3.808 to 4.951 GeV (BOSS 703, 705, 706, 707; ≈22 fb−1), using the real data and inclusive MC at each point. Generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each mode: e+e−→J/ψX with J/ψ→μ+μ−, ISR e+e−→γ_ISR J/ψ with J/ψ→μ+μ−, e+e−→ψ(3686)X with ψ(2S)→π+π−J/ψ in both the J/ψ→μ+μ− and J/ψ→e+e− channels and their ISR analogues, and e+e−→χ_c1X with χ_c1→γJ/ψ, J/ψ→μ+μ−. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge zero, and at least one positive and one negative track (at least two of each for the ψ(3686) channels); photons need TDC 0–700 ns, E>25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap and >20° from the nearest track, with no photon required except in the χ_c1 channel, which demands ≥1 photon isolated by >20° from both muon tracks. PID uses the probability method (cut 0.001), treating p>1.0 GeV tracks as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon) and requiring ≥1 positive and ≥1 negative lepton; in the ψ(3686) channels the identified leptons are removed and the remaining charged tracks are assigned as π+π−. A Kalman fit constrains the di-lepton mass to the nominal J/ψ (χ²<50), followed by a 4C fit with χ²<200 on μ+μ−, π+π−μ+μ−, π+π−e+e− or γμ+μ−, with the χ_c1 channel also storing an alternative 4C fit constraining the di-muon to the J/ψ. Yields are extracted by fitting M(μμ) in (2.8,3.4) GeV excluding 3.0–3.2, M(ππμμ) in (3.50,3.90) GeV excluding 3.65–3.72, and M(γJ/ψ) in (3.45,3.70) GeV excluding 3.47–3.53 for χ_c1 and 3.53–3.60 for χ_c2, after subtracting the observed ISR γ_ISR J/ψ / γ_ISR ψ(3686) backgrounds and the ψ(3686)→J/ψX and χ_cJ→γJ/ψ feed-down, and the Born cross section is obtained with an iterative ISR correction including the vacuum-polarization factor.
