On ψ(2S), study ψ(2S) → γ χ_{c0,2} with χ_{c0,2} → γγ, a three-photon final state with no charged tracks. Use 3.686 GeV ψ(2S) data (106 M ψ′ events, 156.4 pb⁻¹) and inclusive MC, together with off-resonance data at 3.65 GeV (44.1 pb⁻¹) and ψ(3770) data at 3.773 GeV (921.8 pb⁻¹), each with matching inclusive MC, to model the continuum. Generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of ψ′→γχ_{c0}→γγγ and ψ′→γχ_{c2}→γγγ, for the ψ′→γχ_{c0,2}, χ_{c0,2}→K⁺K⁻ control samples, for the peaking backgrounds χ_{c0}→π⁰π⁰ and χ_{c2}→ηη, and a shared 100k-event Babayaga e⁺e⁻→γγ(γ) sample for the two off-resonance points. Event selection requires zero charged tracks and exactly three photon candidates with EMC timing 0–14, an angle of at least 10° to the nearest charged track, and energy above 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), together with a fiducial |cosθ|<0.75 with respect to the e⁺ beam direction. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γγγ with a loose χ² cut of 200 (tightened to χ²≤80 in the final analysis), flagged nominal. Both χ_{c0} and χ_{c2} share the identical three-photon selection chain, with the lowest-energy photon assigned to the radiative γ₁ and the two higher-energy photons to the χ_{c0,2} decay.
