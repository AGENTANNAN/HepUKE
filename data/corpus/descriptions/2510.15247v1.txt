On the J/ψ peak (√s = 3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ η_c with η_c → p pbar, using the real J/ψ data sample (about 1.0×10^10 events) and the corresponding inclusive MC. Generate two 200k-event exclusive MC samples: the signal J/ψ → γ η_c, η_c → p pbar, and the dominant background J/ψ → p pbar π0. Event selection requires exactly one positive and one negative charged track with net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm. Photons are accepted with E>25 MeV in the barrel and E>50 MeV in the endcap, with EMC timing 0–700 ns, and at least one photon is required after rejecting clusters within 20° of the proton and 30° of the antiproton. Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, requiring the proton hypothesis to exceed both the pion and kaon hypotheses. A 4C kinematic fit to γ p pbar is then performed with four-momentum conservation, flagged nominal, retaining events with χ²<200 and automatically choosing the combination with the smallest χ². The same selection chain is applied to data, inclusive MC, and both exclusive MC samples.
