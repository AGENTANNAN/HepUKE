At e+e− centre-of-mass energies 4.128–4.226 GeV (7.33 fb−1, primary dataset 703_4180 at √s = 4178 MeV), study Ds+ → K0 μ+ νμ with K0 → KS0 → π+π−, using real data and inclusive MC. The Ds− tag side is reconstructed through 14 hadronic modes — K−K+π−, K−K+π−π0, KS0K−, KS0K−π0, KS0π−, KS0π−π0, K−π+π−, K−π+π−π0, π−π+π−, π−π+π−π0, π−π+π−π+π−, π−η, π−π0 and π−ηπ+π−π0. On the signal side require one π+, one π− and one μ+ with a missing νμ, and apply a kinematic fit constraining the Ds four-momentum with χ² cut 200 at BOSS level (the paper's χ² < 40 being applied later at ROOT level). Muons are identified by the likelihood method, L_μ > 0.001 with L_μ > L_e and L_μ > L_K and an EMC energy deposit 0.1 < E < 0.3 GeV. Backgrounds are suppressed by M(K0 μ+) < 1.70 GeV/c² against Ds+ → K0 π+, by requiring the maximum unused photon energy below 0.15 GeV against π0 modes, and by the Ds*±Ds∓ pair constraint (χ² < 40). The signal double-tag yield is finally obtained from an unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to the missing-mass-squared distribution.
