At the J/ψ(3.097 GeV) energy point, search for baryon- and lepton-number-violating Ξ0 decays in J/ψ → Ξ0 Ξ̄0 via a double-tag technique: tag Ξ̄0 → Λ̄ π0 with Λ̄ → p̄ π+ and π0 → γγ, and signal Ξ0 → K− e+ (Δ(B−L)=0) or Ξ0 → K+ e− (|Δ(B−L)|=2). Use the 708_3097 real data sample and matching inclusive MC at 3.097 GeV; generate 500k exclusive MC events for the J/ψ → Ξ0 Ξ̄0 chain with Ξ̄0 → Λ̄ π0, Λ̄ → p̄ π+, π0 → γγ, and Ξ0 → K− e+. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; photons are selected with E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.80). Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying proton against pion/kaon and pion against kaon/proton for the Λ̄, while the signal electron must have CL_e>0.1% and CL_e/(CL_e+CL_pi+CL_K)>0.8 and the kaon is taken as the hypothesis with the highest confidence level. The tag side is reconstructed by forming Λ̄ from p̄π+ with a secondary-vertex fit and |M(p̄π+)−M_Λ|<5 MeV, π0 from γγ with 115<M_γγ<150 MeV, and Ξ̄0 from Λ̄π0 with |M(Λ̄π0)−M_Ξ0|<20 MeV, followed by a 4C kinematic fit to p̄π+γγ with a nominal four-momentum constraint, a γγ mass constraint to the π0 nominal mass, and χ²<200. For the double-tag selection, the Ξ0 → K±e∓ candidate must recoil against the tagged Ξ̄0, satisfy an opening-angle cut θ(Ξ0,Ξ̄0)>178.5°, and lie in the signal region |M(Ke)−M_Ξ0|<20 MeV and |M(Λ̄π0)−M_Ξ0|<20 MeV.
