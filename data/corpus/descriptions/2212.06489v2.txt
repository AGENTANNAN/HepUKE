At ψ(3770), measure the CP-even fraction F+ in D0 → K+K−π+π− using D0 D̄0 pairs in which the D̄0 is tagged in a CP eigenstate. Use the 3.773 GeV real data sample (712_3773) together with its inclusive MC, and generate 1M-event exclusive MC for ψ(3770) → D0 D̄0 with D0 → K+K−π+π− and D̄0 → K+π− (charge conjugation implied throughout). Tag the anti-D0 with the standard D-tag modes Kπππ and KS0 π+π−, noting that most of the paper's CP-eigenstate tags (K+K−, π+π−, KS0π0π0, KS0π0, KS0η, KS0ω, ...) are not present in the frozen BOSS D-tag list. Signal side: require exactly one K+, one K−, one π+ and one π− with net charge 0, kaons and pions identified by likelihood (L(K)>L(π) for kaons, L(π)>L(K) for pions), with the signal π+π− pair required to originate from a vertex within 2× the vertex resolution of the IP and to have an invariant mass outside [477, 507] MeV/c² (KS0 veto). Tag-side reconstruction: KS0 → π+π− with |Vz|<20 cm, secondary-vertex fit, mass within 12 MeV/c² and decay length >2× vertex resolution; π0/η from γγ with M(γγ) in [115,150] and [480,580] MeV/c²; η′ via π+π−η ([940,976] MeV/c²) or ρ0γ ([940,970] MeV/c², M(ππ) in [626,924] MeV/c²); fully reconstructed tags kept within 3σ of ΔE, KL0 modes handled by partial reconstruction, and Kalman fits constraining the KS,L0 and D masses for KS,L0π+π− tags. Apply a kinematic fit imposing four-momentum conservation and the tag D0 mass at its nominal value, with χ² < 200, and extract F+ from a simultaneous fit to the single- and double-tag yields.
