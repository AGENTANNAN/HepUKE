On the 15 c.m. energy scan points from √s = 4.4156 to 4.9509 GeV (8.5 fb⁻¹ total), study e⁺e⁻ → π⁺π⁻ Ds⁺ Ds⁻ using real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the four Ds-pair sub-modes: (K⁺K⁻π⁺)(K⁺K⁻π⁻), (K⁺K⁻π⁺)(K_S⁰K⁻), (K_S⁰K⁺)(K⁺K⁻π⁻) and (K_S⁰K⁺)(K_S⁰K⁻), with Ds → K⁺K⁻π⁺ treated as a Dalitz decay and K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm — loosened to |Vz|<20 cm and Vr<10 cm in the K_S⁰ modes — with at least three positive and three negative tracks and net charge zero. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut separating π from K, requiring at least 3 π⁺, 3 π⁻, 2 K⁺, 2 K⁻ (mode I), ≥1 K⁺ and ≥2 K⁻ (mode II), ≥2 K⁺ and ≥1 K⁻ (mode III), ≥1 K⁺ and ≥1 K⁻ (mode IV). In the K_S⁰ modes the K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻ is built by a secondary vertex fit, requiring an invariant mass in (0.487, 0.511) GeV/c², a decay length greater than twice its resolution, and removal of the daughters from the candidate list, with the double-K_S⁰ mode requiring two independent such candidates. A nominal 4C kinematic fit is performed on the full final state with χ² < 200, the (Ds⁺, Ds⁻) pairing being chosen by minimizing |½(M(Ds⁺)+M(Ds⁻)) − M_PDG(Ds)|; for Ds → K⁺K⁻π⁺ a φ veto M(K⁺K⁻)<1.2 GeV/c² and a K*(892) veto M(K⁻π⁺)<1.0 GeV/c² are applied, together with a recoil-mass window |M(Ds) − M_PDG(Ds)| < 15 MeV/c² on the recoiling Ds in the RM(π⁺π⁻ Ds) fit.
