On 22 R-scan energy points from 2.00 to 3.08 GeV, study e+e− → ppbar for the proton electromagnetic form factors. Use the real data at these 22 points together with inclusive MC, and generate signal exclusive MC (500k events per point) with a ConExc ppbar decay card, the per-point c.m. energy being injected for each scan point. Event selection requires exactly two charged tracks of opposite charge (one positive, one negative, net charge 0) with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm and Vr<10 cm, the two tracks vertexed to a common vertex with χ²<100, cosmic rays rejected by |T₁−T₂|<4 ns in TOF, and momenta inside an asymmetric window (p̄−4σ) < p < (p̄+3σ) fitted at each energy; the opening angle between p and pbar in the c.m. frame must exceed 170° at 2.00/2.05 GeV, 175° at 2.100–2.309 GeV and 178° at 2.386–3.080 GeV. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions and requiring one proton and one antiproton, after which the identified p/pbar are removed from the charged-track lists, with an E/p cut applied above 2.150 GeV to suppress Bhabha events. A 4C kinematic fit constraining the ppbar system to the c.m. energy is performed with χ²<200. Both real data and the ConExc signal MC are processed through this same chain.
