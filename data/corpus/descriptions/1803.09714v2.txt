On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV (448.1M events), study ψ(3686)→η′ e+e− with η′→γπ+π− (Mode I) and η′→π+π−η, η→γγ (Mode II). Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two η′ decay modes. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 mm, Vr<10 mm, exactly four tracks with two positive and two negative and net charge zero; photons with E>25 MeV (barrel) or >50 MeV (endcap), EMC TDC 0–700 ns, angle to nearest track >10°, with at least one photon in Mode I and at least two in Mode II. PID uses the probability method with C.L.>0.001; tracks with p>1.0 GeV are treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, else muon), requiring at least one ℓ+ and one ℓ−, and the remaining charged tracks are pions. Backgrounds are suppressed by requiring the e+e− pair vertex distance from IP δxy<2 cm, cosθ(e+)<0.8 and cosθ(e−)>−0.8, recoil mass of π+π− <2.9 GeV (Mode I) or <3.2 GeV (Mode II), and in Mode I E/p>0.8 on the higher-momentum e± track. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γπ+π−ℓ+ℓ− with χ²<80 for Mode I; for Mode II first require a γγ invariant mass consistent with the η (Kalman fit, χ²<25, 0.520–0.575 GeV), then do the 4C fit to π+π−ℓ+ℓ−η with χ²<80, and extract yields from M(γπ+π−) or M(γγπ+π−) in 0.85–1.05 GeV.
