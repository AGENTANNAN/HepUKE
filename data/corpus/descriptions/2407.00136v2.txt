Study hc → e+e− ηc (EM Dalitz) in two production modes with ηc undetected, to measure R = B(hc → e+e− ηc)/B(hc → γ ηc). Mode I uses ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC, with 500k-event exclusive MC each for ψ(2S) → π0 hc, hc → e+e− ηc and hc → γ ηc; Mode II uses 17 XYZ scan points (4.130–4.780 GeV) real data with 100k-event exclusive MC per point for e+e− → π+π− hc, hc → e+e− ηc and hc → γ ηc. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring ≥1 positive and ≥1 negative track for Mode I signal, ≥2 each for Mode II signal, and ≥1 each for Mode II reference; photons with 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) threshold, at least 2 for Mode I signal, at least 3 for Mode I reference, and at least 1 for Mode II reference; PID by the probability method (cut 0.001), with high-momentum tracks (p>1.0) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6) in the electron modes and π/K separation, requiring 1 e+ and 1 e− for Mode I signal, 1 e+, 1 e−, 1 π+, 1 π− for Mode II signal, and 1 π+ and 1 π− for Mode II reference. Mode I reconstructs π0 from γγ with a Kalman fit constraining the π0 mass (χ²<25). The hc is reconstructed by best mass combination at 3.525 GeV with the ηc missing, requiring recoil mass 2.92–3.08 GeV; additional requirements include E/p 0.5–1.2, π0 mass window 120–145 MeV, π0 γ energy >40 MeV, J/ψ and π0→e+e−γ vetoes, photon-conversion veto (Δxy<2 cm), and E(e+e−) in 470–540 MeV.
