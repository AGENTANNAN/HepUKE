On the J/ψ(3097) peak, study J/ψ → p pbar and J/ψ → n nbar. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC (225.2M J/ψ events from dataset 708_3097); generate 1,000,000-event exclusive MC for J/ψ → p pbar and J/ψ → n nbar, plus 200,000-event exclusive backgrounds for each: J/ψ → γ η_c with η_c → p pbar or n nbar, and J/ψ → π0 p pbar or π0 n nbar (π0 → γγ). For J/ψ → p pbar, require exactly two good charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.80, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, one positive and one negative track, net charge zero; apply probability-based PID only to the positive track, requiring the proton hypothesis over π and K with probability cut 0.001, and treat the negative track as an antiproton without PID; a vertex fit to the two tracks is performed with χ²<200 and flagged nominal, followed at ROOT level by an opening-angle cut >178° and a momentum requirement within 30 MeV/c of 1.232 GeV/c. For J/ψ → n nbar, require zero good charged tracks and at least two photons with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy thresholds, photon–track angle >10°, and TDC 0–14; the most energetic EMC shower (0.6–2.0 GeV, |cosθ|<0.8, second moment >20 cm², >40 hits in a 50° cone) is the nbar candidate, an opposite-side shower of 0.06–0.6 GeV is the neutron, E_extra=0, and no kinematic fit or recoil-mass constraint is applied. Both selections share the same data, inclusive-MC and exclusive-MC sets, with the n nbar signal extracted from the nbar–n opening-angle distribution near 180°.
