On the ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ with χ_cJ → Λ Λ̄ ω (J = 0,1,2), followed by Λ → pπ⁻, Λ̄ → p̄π⁺, ω → π⁺π⁻π⁰, and π⁰ → γγ. Use the 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data (≈2.712×10⁹ events) and its inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of χ_c0, χ_c1, and χ_c2 with the same decay chain. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least three positive and three negative tracks, and net charge zero; photons with EMC TDC 0–14, angle to any track >10°, energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap), and at least three photons. PID uses the probability method with prob_cut 0.001, identifies protons against kaons and pions, requires at least one p and one p̄, then removes the identified protons and treats all remaining charged tracks as pions. Reconstruct Λ → pπ⁻ and Λ̄ → p̄π⁺ through secondary vertex fits that minimize the mass difference, and reconstruct at least one π⁰ → γγ with a kinematic fit constraining the two photons to the nominal π⁰ mass, χ² < 25. Perform a nominal 5C fit to γ Λ Λ̄ π⁺π⁻ π⁰, constraining four-momentum and the π⁰ mass, with χ² < 30; also store a competing ψ(3686) → ΛΛ̄ω fit without the radiative photon for a χ²-based veto. The three χ_cJ states share the same selection chain; additional mass windows and sidebands (Λ/Λ̄ ±8 MeV/c², ω 0.756–0.810 GeV/c², ω sidebands [0.693,0.747] and [0.819,0.873] GeV/c²) and the competing-hypothesis veto are applied later at ROOT level.
