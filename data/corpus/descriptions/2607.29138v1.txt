At √s = 4.682 GeV, using the 1667 pb⁻¹ BESIII data set (706_4680) and its inclusive MC, reconstruct e⁺e⁻ → D⁰D⁻π⁺ (charge conjugate implied) in single-tag (ST) and double-tag (DT) analyses. Generate 500k exclusive MC events for the same final state with a decay table where D⁰ → K⁻π⁺, K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻, K⁻π⁺π⁰ and D⁻ → K⁺π⁻π⁻, K_S⁰π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰π⁻π⁻π⁺, with K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻ and π⁰ → γγ. In the DT selection, fully reconstruct D⁰ and D⁻ using D⁰ tags Kπ, K3π and Kππ⁰ (charm +1) and D⁻ tags the five modes above (charm −1, ranked by invariant mass), require exactly one signal-side charged π⁺ and net charge +1 with no additional charged tracks, then perform a 4C kinematic fit with χ² < 200 (loose BOSS-level; the paper applies χ² < 40), constrain the D⁰ and D⁻ masses to PDG values, and veto D* background by RM(D⁻) > 2.04 GeV/c² or M(D⁰π_d⁺) > 2.04 GeV/c². The ST samples are ST⁰_Kπ (D⁰→K⁻π⁺), ST⁰_K3π (D⁰→K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻), and ST⁻_K2π (D⁻→K⁺π⁻π⁻), each requiring one reconstructed direct π⁺, net charge 0 with the recoiling D missing, and the same 4C fit with χ² < 200; the K3π ST additionally applies |M(K⁻π_d⁺π⁺π⁻)−m_D⁰| > 10 MeV/c² and |M(K⁻π_d⁺π⁺)−m_D⁺| > 16 MeV/c². No explicit charged-track, photon, or PID cut values are encoded in this DSL, so the tag algorithms' standard object reconstruction is used.
