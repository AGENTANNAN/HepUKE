Study e+e− → Σ+Σ− using the 41-point R scan from 3.510 to 4.951 GeV, with Σ+ → pπ0, Σ− → p̄π0 and π0 → γγ, so the final state is pp̄4γ. Use the real scan data together with inclusive MC at each energy point, and generate 50k-event exclusive signal MC from a single ConExc decay card (mode 44 for charged Σ-pair production, Σ± and π0 decays through PHSP) applied over all scan points; the CM energy is taken per run from the measured beam energy rather than a fixed value. Charged-track selection: |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0. Photon selection: EMC timing window 0–14, barrel energy threshold 25 MeV and endcap 50 MeV, at least 10° away from any track, and at least four photons. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions, requiring at least one p and one p̄ (protons additionally required to have p>0.5 GeV/c). The selection then applies a Kalman kinematic fit on each γγ pair constraining it to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<25 and requiring at least two π0, followed by a 6C kinematic fit on pp̄π0π0 (both π0 mass constraints plus overall four-momentum conservation) with χ²<200, flagged nominal. Signal yields are extracted with a signal/sideband method including iterative ISR weighting for the Born cross-section measurement.
