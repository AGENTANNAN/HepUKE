On the J/ψ at 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → γ X(1840), X(1840) → 3(π+π−). Use the 708/3097 real data and inclusive MC, and generate phase-space exclusive MC for J/ψ → γ X(1840), X(1840) → 3(π+π−); the selection is run on the real data. Event selection requires exactly six charged tracks with zero net charge (three π+ and three π−), applies no charged-particle identification so all tracks are treated as pions, and has no additional track |cosθ|, Vz, or Vr cuts; require at least one photon, with no explicit photon energy threshold. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to the γ 3(π+π−) hypothesis with the total four-momentum constrained to 3.097 GeV, χ² < 30, flagged nominal. The code additionally notes ROOT-stage vetoes: reject events with two or more K_S^0 candidates from secondary-vertex fits on π+π− pairs satisfying |M(π+π−) − m_{K_S^0}| < 5 MeV; for events with at least two photons require |M(γγ) − m_{π^0}| > 10 MeV; and require χ²_4C(γ 3(π+π−)) < χ²_4C(γγ 3(π+π−)) to suppress γγ 3(π+π−) background.
