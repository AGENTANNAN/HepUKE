On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), search for charged lepton flavor violating decay J/ψ → e τ with τ → π+ π0 ν̄τ, π0 → γγ. Use 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k exclusive MC events for J/ψ → e− τ+ (VLL) with τ+ → π+ π0 ν̄τ (TAUHADNU) and π0 → γγ (PHSP). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, angle to nearest track >10°, at least two photons. PID by the probability method with prob>0.001, pions identified against kaons and protons, high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV), requiring at least one e+, one e−, one π+, one π−. Reconstruct π0 via Kalman mass-constrained fit of two photons with χ²<25, requiring at least one π0. Perform a kinematic fit with e−, π+, π0 and missing ν_τ, constraining four-momentum, χ²<200, flagged nominal. Additional ROOT-level selection: electron PID with CL_e/(CL_e+CL_π)>0.95 and E/p>0.8, electron momentum 1.009–1.068 GeV/c, recoil mass 1.742–1.811 GeV/c², missing energy >0.43 GeV, and signal region U_miss = E_miss − c|P_miss| in [−0.081, 0.112] GeV.
