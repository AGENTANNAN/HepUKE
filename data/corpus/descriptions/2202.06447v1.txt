On 19 R-scan energy points from 2.000 to 3.080 GeV, study e+e− → K+K−π0 using the ConExc generator (mode 8) with the π0 decaying via phase space. Use all 19 real-data points and their matching inclusive-MC sets, plus a single 100k-event exclusive signal sample generated once over all scan points. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track with net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy thresholds and at least two candidates; PID by the probability method (cut 0.001) separating kaons from pions, requiring at least one K+ and one K−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to K+K−γγ with χ²<65, flagged nominal, and also a second 4C fit under the K+K−γ hypothesis (no nominal, no χ² cut) for the ISR φ veto. The π0 mass window [0.120, 0.150] GeV and the χ²(K+K−π0) < χ²(K+K−γ) comparison are applied later at the ROOT analysis stage.
