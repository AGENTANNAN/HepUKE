At √s = 3.773 GeV, using the 2.93 fb−1 real data and inclusive MC, study the semileptonic decay D+ → ω μ+ ν_μ reconstructed with a single-tag D−. Generate 200,000 exclusive signal events for ψ(3770) → D+D−, D+ → ω μ+ν_μ (ISGW), D− → K+π−π−, ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ. The tag-side D− (charm −1) is selected in six hadronic modes (Kππ, K_Sπ, Kπππ0, K_Sππ0, K_Sπππ, KKπ) using M_BC ∈ [1.863, 1.877] GeV and |ΔE| < 0.055 GeV, with a broader ΔE window for π0-containing modes. On the signal side require one μ+, one π+, one π−, net charge +1, exactly two photons with energy > 25 MeV and minimum angle 10°, and a missing massless ν_μ. The ω candidate must satisfy |M(π+π−π0) − m_ω| < 0.025 GeV, with K_S0 veto |M(π+π−) − m_K_S0| > 0.015 GeV, extra photon energy < 0.15 GeV, M(ω μ+) < 1.5 GeV, and a recoil-mass veto outside 0.45–0.55 GeV. Muon identification requires CL_μ > 0.001, CL_μ > CL_e, CL_μ > CL_K, and EMC energy 0.15–0.25 GeV. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the tag, signal, and ν_μ to the lab four-momentum, with χ² < 200.
