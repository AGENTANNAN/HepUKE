Study ψ(2S) → γ χ_c1 with χ_c1 → π+ π− η′, using the 3.686 GeV ψ(2S) real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two η′ decay modes: η′ → γ π+ π− (Dalitz) and η′ → π+ π− η with η → γ γ. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<10 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; photons must have EMC timing within [0,14] (700 ns), opening angle to any charged track >10°, energy above 100 MeV in barrel and endcap, and at least two photons (channel 1) or at least three photons (channel 2). All charged tracks are treated as pions (no PID applied). For channel 1, perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γ γ π+ π− π+ π− with χ²<200 (loose; tight χ²_4C<40 applied later), requiring exactly one combination with |M(γ π+ π−)−m_η′|<45 MeV/c² and M(γ π+ π− π+ π−)>3.4 GeV/c²; final η′ window |M(γ π+ π−)−m_η′|<15 MeV/c², χ_c1 window |M(γ π+ π− π+ π−)−m_χ_c1|<15 MeV/c², M(π+π−) from η′ >0.60 GeV/c², with π0/η vetoes |M(γ γ)−m_π0|<17 MeV/c² and |M(γ π+ π−)−m_η|<22 MeV/c² rejected, and J/ψ vetoes |M(2(π+π−))−m_J/ψ|>28 MeV/c² and |M_recoil(π+π−)−m_J/ψ|>6 MeV/c². For channel 2, perform a nominal 4C fit to γ γ γ π+ π− π+ π− and a nominal 5C fit with an additional η mass constraint on a γγ pair, both with χ²<200 (tight χ²_5C<40 later); require exactly one combination with |M(π+ π− γ γ)−m_η′|<21 MeV/c² and M(γ γ π+ π− π+ π−)>3.4 GeV/c², final η′ window |M(π+ π− η)−m_η′|<7 MeV/c², and χ_c1 window |M(η π+ π− π+ π−)−m_χ_c1|<15 MeV/c².
