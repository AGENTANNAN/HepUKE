On the XYZ/R-scan energy points from √s ≈ 3.81 to 4.95 GeV, study e+e− → D0 D0bar and e+e− → D+ D− using the single-tag technique, reconstructing one charmed meson and inferring the other from the recoil mass. Use the full set of real XYZ data points together with the matching inclusive MC, and generate 50k-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes: D0 → K−π+π+π− (with anti-D0 → anything) and D+ → K−π+π+ (with D− → anything). Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; for the D0 tag at least two positive and two negative tracks, for the D+ tag at least two positive and one negative track. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating kaons and pions against each other and against protons, requiring at least one K− and two π+ in both modes plus at least one π− in Mode I. Each tag is partially reconstructed by choosing the best combination around the nominal mass (1.86484 GeV/c2 for D0 with a ~14 MeV window; 1.86966 GeV/c2 for D+ with a ~16 MeV window) and requiring the recoil mass to lie in [1.80, 1.95] GeV/c2, with charge-conjugate decays included. The yields are then extracted from the M_D_recoil spectrum in the same window.
