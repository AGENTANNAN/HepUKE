At the ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV, 20.3 fb⁻¹ real data plus inclusive MC), study ψ(3770) → D⁺D⁻ with a tag D⁻ reconstructed in six hadronic modes (K⁺π⁻π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰π⁻, K_S⁰π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰π⁻π⁻π⁺, K⁺K⁻π⁻) and the signal D⁺ → π⁺π⁰π⁰; generate a 2M-event exclusive signal MC including these tag decays. Signal-side selection requires exactly one π⁺ (net charge +1) and 4–48 photons with energy above 25 MeV and lab angle relative to the beam greater than 10°, then a 4-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ² < 200. Each π⁰ is formed from a γγ pair with 0.115 < M(γγ) < 0.150 GeV/c², at least one photon in the barrel EMC, and a 1C mass-constrained fit with χ² < 50; the amplitude analysis then uses a 7C fit (initial e⁺e⁻ four-momentum, D⁺ mass, and both π⁰ masses). Veto D⁺ → K_S⁰π⁺ by rejecting 0.428 < M(π⁰π⁰) < 0.548 GeV/c², and reject D⁺→π⁺π⁰π⁰ / D⁻→K⁺K⁻π⁻ mispartition when both |M(K⁻π⁺π⁰) − 1.865| and |M(K⁺π⁻π⁰) − 1.865| are below 0.05 GeV/c². Keep the best candidate minimizing the sum of squared ΔE on tag and signal sides, and require M_BC in 1.865–1.875 GeV/c² on both sides, with per-mode ΔE windows: D⁺→π⁺π⁰π⁰ (−0.100, 0.045); D⁻→K⁺π⁻π⁻ (−0.025, 0.024); D⁻→K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰ (−0.057, 0.046); D⁻→K_S⁰π⁻ (−0.025, 0.026); D⁻→K_S⁰π⁻π⁰ (−0.062, 0.049); D⁻→K_S⁰π⁻π⁻π⁺ (−0.028, 0.027); D⁻→K⁺K⁻π⁻ (−0.024, 0.023) GeV.
