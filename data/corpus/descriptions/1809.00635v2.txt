At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), measure J/ψ → η' e⁺e⁻ with η' → γπ⁺π⁻ (Mode I) and η' → π⁺π⁻η, η → γγ (Mode II). Use the 3.097 GeV real data and its inclusive MC, plus 200k exclusive MC events for Mode I and 100k for Mode II. Require exactly four charged tracks with two positive and two negative, net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm and Vr<10 cm; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold and EMC TDC in 0–14, at least one (Mode I) or at least two (Mode II), and >10° from any track. PID uses the probability method (prob cut 0.001), with high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, else muon) and π/K separation applied; remaining tracks are assigned as π⁺π⁻. Mode I performs a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γπ⁺π⁻e⁺e⁻ with a vertex constraint on the four charged tracks and χ²<100; Mode II first does a Kalman fit to γγ with the η mass constraint, M(γγ)∈[0.48,0.60] GeV/c² and χ²<25 (≥1 η), then the same 4C fit on ηπ⁺π⁻e⁺e⁻ with χ²<100. Additional requirements: a γ→e⁺e⁻ conversion veto (δxy<2 cm), and for Mode I a π⁰ Dalitz veto M(γe⁺e⁻)∈[0.10,0.16] GeV/c²; the dark-photon search scans M(e⁺e⁻) within the η' window [0.93,0.98] GeV/c², excluding the ω [0.74,0.84] and φ [1.00,1.04] GeV/c² regions.
