At the J/ψ(3.097 GeV), measure B(Σ+→Λ e+ν_e) with a double-tag method using J/ψ→Σ+ anti-Σ−, where the tag is anti-Σ−→anti-p π0 (π0→γγ) and the signal is Σ+→Λ e+ν_e with Λ→pπ−. Use the 3.097 GeV real data sample 708_3097 and its inclusive MC, and generate 1,000,000 exclusive MC events for this full decay chain. Require exactly four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93; at least two photons with barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, TDC in [0,14], and photon-track angle >10°, then reconstruct π0 through a 1C kinematic fit to γγ with the π0 mass constraint and χ²<25. PID uses the probability method: proton candidates satisfy L(p)>L(K), L(p)>L(π), and L(p)>0.001; π− candidates satisfy L(π)>L(K) and L(π)>L(e) with a 0.001 probability cut; the high-momentum e+ is identified by the combined EMC+TOF likelihood L′(e)/(L′(e)+L′(π)+L′(K))>0.8. Reconstruct Λ→pπ− from a secondary vertex with |M(pπ−)−m_Λ|<10 MeV/c² and decay length >2 times the vertex resolution from the IP, treating the neutrino as missed and using all other final-state particles (anti-p, γ, γ, p, π−, e+) to form M_miss² from E_miss=E_beam−E_Λ−E_e+ and p_miss=|p_Σ+−p_Λ−p_e+|, with the Σ+ momentum constrained from the tag as −p̂_Σ−√(E_beam²−m_Σ+²). Apply the double-tag selection windows: ST ΔE_tag∈[−48,52] MeV (retaining the minimum |ΔE_tag| if multiple combinations) and DT |M_BC^tag−m_Σ−|<0.049 GeV/c², with additional background suppression from angle(anti-p,π0)>170° and p_anti-p∈[0.16,0.21] GeV/c in the tag anti-Σ− rest frame, M_recoil(Σ,Λ)+M(pπ−)−m_Λ>−60 MeV/c², and M_Λ(π+→e+)<1.32 GeV/c²; extract the ST yield from a binned extended fit to M_BC^tag and the DT yield from an unbinned extended fit to M_miss².
