In the ψ(4260) region, study e+e− → π+π−hc, hc → γηc, with ηc decaying to sixteen hadronic final states, using the 59-point energy scan from 4.009–4.950 GeV over the XYZ-I, XYZ-II and R-scan samples with real data and inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for ηc → K+K−π+π−, ηc → π+π−η (η→γγ), and ηc → 2(π+π−)π0; the implemented selection runs on a representative 4.260 GeV point, with the full scan and remaining 13 modes deferred. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge 0, at least two positive and two negative tracks for the first two modes and at least three positive and three negative for 2(π+π−)π0, plus photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds and TDC 0–14, requiring at least one photon for K+K−π+π− and at least three photons for the modes containing η→γγ or π0→γγ. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating K from π/p or π from K/p, requiring 1 K+, 1 K−, 1 π+, 1 π− for K+K−π+π−, 2 π+ and 2 π− for π+π−η(→γγ), and 3 π+ and 3 π− for 2(π+π−)π0. The K+K−π+π− mode undergoes a nominal four-momentum-constrained 4C fit on π+π−K+K−γ with χ²<200, while the π+π−η(→γγ) and 2(π+π−)π0 modes first constrain γγ to the η or π0 nominal mass with χ²<25 and then perform a nominal 4C fit on π+π−π+π−γη or 3π+3π−γπ0 with χ²<200.
