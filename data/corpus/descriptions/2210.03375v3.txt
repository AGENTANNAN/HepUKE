Study Cabibbo-suppressed Λc+ → nπ+π0, nπ+π-π+, nK-π+π+ using a double-tag method, with the Λc+ reconstructed in the system recoiling against an ST Λc- (the neutron from the signal side is identified via missing mass). Use the 7 c.m. energy points from 4599.53 to 4698.82 MeV real data plus inclusive MC. For each signal mode build ST Λc- tags: 10 modes for nπ+π0 (excluding pK+π-π0 and pπ-π+), 11 for nπ+π-π+ (excluding pπ-π+), and all 12 for nK-π+π+ (the full list pK-π0, pπ+π-, pK-π0π0, pK-π0π+π-, pK-π0π+π-π0, Λπ+, Λπ+π0, Λπ+π-π+, Σ+π0, Σ+π0π0, Σ+π+π- and pπ-π+), all with charm -1. On the signal side require the corresponding charged multiplicity and total charge +1 while leaving the neutron as missing: π+π0 for mode 1 (with 2 photons, a minimum photon separation angle of 10° and requiring a π0), π+π+π- for mode 2, and K-π+π+ for mode 3. Perform a 4-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ²<200. Apply peaking-background vetos: for mode 1, Λc+→Λπ+/Σ+π0/Σ0π+ feed-down removed by missing-mass cuts Mmiss(π+)>1300 MeV/c² and Mmiss(π0)>1370 MeV/c²; for mode 2, KS0→π+π- (Mπ+π- outside [487,511]), Σ+→nπ+ (Mmiss(π+π-) outside [1150,1250]) and Σ-→nπ- (Mmiss(π+π+) outside [1150,1250]) vetos. Signal is extracted in an MBC window of 2.275-2.300 GeV/c² at 4.600 GeV, 2.275-2.306 GeV/c² at 4.612-4.641 GeV and 2.275-2.310 GeV/c² at 4.661-4.699 GeV, with mode-dependent (~3σ) ΔE tag windows.
