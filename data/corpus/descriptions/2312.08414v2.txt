On 4.918 and 4.9509 GeV, study e+e− → Λc+ Λc(2595)− + c.c. and Λc+ Λc(2625)− + c.c., with Λc+ → p K− π+ and the excited Λc decaying via Λc+ π+π− (and Λc+ π0π0 with an isospin 2:1 ratio, varied 1.5:1–5:1 for systematics). Use the 4.918 GeV real-data sample with matching inclusive MC, the 4.9509 GeV inclusive-MC sample, and generate exclusive MC at both energies for e+e− → Λc+ Λc(2595)− + c.c. with Λc+ → p K− π+ and Λc(2595)− → anti-Λc− π+π−, anti-Λc− → anti-p K+ π−. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, |Vxy|<1 cm, with at least three tracks and net charge zero. Identify p, K−, and π+ by the probability method with prob>0.001, vetoing K+, π−, e±, and μ±, and combine them into a Λc+ candidate. No kinematic fit is performed; instead select M(pK−π+) ∈ (2.27, 2.30) GeV/c2 and recoil mass M_rec+ > 2.55 GeV/c2, then fit the M_rec+ spectrum unbinned for Λc(2595)− and Λc(2625)− yields, with the Λc(2625) cosθ distribution binned in 6 (8) bins at 4.918 (4.9509) GeV and iterative ISR corrections using a pQCD-motivated power-law line shape. Backgrounds include qq continuum, ΣcΣc, and Λc+Σcπ.
