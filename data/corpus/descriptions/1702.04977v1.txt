At c.m. energies from 2.232 to 4.590 GeV (131 points), measure the QED luminosities via large-angle Bhabha e+e−→(γ)e+e− and diphoton e+e−→γγ, using real data and inclusive MC in that energy window, plus per-point exclusive MC for the Bhabha signal (200k events, ψ(4260)→e+e−γ with PHOTOS), the diphoton signal (200k events, ψ(4260)→γγ), τ+τ− background (100k events, ψ(4260)→τ+τ−) and continuum-hadron background (100k events, generic). For the Bhabha chain require two charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge zero, and for each such track EMC energy over momentum >0.65; apply no particle identification but assign the positive track as e+ and the negative as e−, then perform a nominal 4C fit to the e+e− four-momentum with χ²<200. For the diphoton chain veto charged tracks with the same |cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and require zero positive, zero negative, net charge zero, and select at least two photons with EMC timing in the 0–700 ns window, >25 MeV in the barrel and >50 MeV in the endcap, at least 10° from any track, then perform a nominal 4C fit to the two-photon four-momentum with χ²<200. Both selection chains are energy independent, are applied to real data, inclusive MC and their respective exclusive samples, and impose no explicit mass window at this level.
