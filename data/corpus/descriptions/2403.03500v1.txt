On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, search for ψ(3686) → π0 h_c with h_c decaying in five hadronic modes: 3(π+π−)π0, 2(π+π−)π0η, 2(π+π−)η, p pbar, and 2(π+π−)ω (ω→π+π−π0). Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the five modes. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with photon candidates having E>25 MeV (barrel) or >50 MeV (endcap), EMC timing 0–700 ns, and >10° separation from tracks; photon multiplicity is ≥4 for 3(π+π−)π0 and 2(π+π−)η, ≥6 for 2(π+π−)π0η, and ≥2 for p pbar. Track and charge counts are exactly 3π+3π− (net 0) for 3(π+π−)π0 and 2(π+π−)ω, 2π+2π− (net 0) for 2(π+π−)π0η and 2(π+π−)η, and 1p+1pbar (net 0) for p pbar. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, separating pions from kaons/protons (or protons from kaons/pions), requiring the same π+/π− or p/pbar multiplicities. Intermediate γγ mass-constrained fits use χ²<20 for π0 and χ²<200 for η, with ≥2 π0 for 3(π+π−)π0 and 2(π+π−)π0η, ≥1 π0 for 2(π+π−)η and p pbar, and ≥1 η for the η modes; then a nominal 4C fit (plus a γγ π0 mass constraint in the 3(π+π−)π0 mode) is performed over the full final state with χ²<200, with the p pbar mode also using a common charged-track vertex fit and a |cosθ_p|<0.8 proton cut, and the 2(π+π−)ω mode sharing the 3(π+π−)π0 selection and being extracted by a simultaneous fit to the π+π−π0 mass in ω signal and sideband regions. J/ψ-related backgrounds are vetoed by recoil-mass windows and photon-count hypothesis tests where noted.
