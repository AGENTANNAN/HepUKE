On the J/ψ(3.097 GeV) data and inclusive MC, study anti-n p reactions to multi-pion final states using the antineutron from J/ψ → p π− anti-n and a proton target from the beam-pipe cooling oil; extract cross sections in five anti-n momentum intervals from 200 to 1174 MeV/c for anti-n p → 2π+2π−, 2π+2π−π0, and 2π+2π−2π0. Generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the three final states, with J/ψ → p π− anti-n as the anti-n source and π0 → γγ for the modes containing π0. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm, and at least three positive and three negative tracks; photons with EMC TDC 0–14, at least 10° from any track, energy >25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), requiring at least 2 photons for the 5π mode and at least 4 for the 6π mode. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions and pions against kaons and protons, requiring ≥1 proton, ≥2 π+, and ≥2 π−. For the 4π mode, perform a 4C fit on p π− with a missing anti-n, χ²<200. For the 5π and 6π modes, additionally reconstruct π0 → γγ with a Kalman fit constraining the γγ mass to the nominal π0, χ²<30, requiring ≥1 π0 (5π) or ≥2 π0 (6π), and also apply the same 4C tag-side fit on p π− with missing anti-n, χ²<200.
