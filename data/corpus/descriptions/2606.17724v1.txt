On the ψ(3770) at √s = 3.773 GeV, study ψ(3770) → D+D− with the signal side D+ → a0(980)+η, a0(980)+ → π+η, both η → γγ (i.e. D+ → π+ η η), and the recoil D− reconstructed in six hadronic tag modes: K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S0π−, K_S0π−π0, K_S0π−π−π+, and K+K−π−. Use the 20.3 fb−1 real data at 3.773 GeV and its inclusive MC, plus 200k-event exclusive MC for the full D+ → π+ η η chain. Tag-side tracking, PID, K_S0, π0 and η reconstruction follow the standard BESIII D-tag prescription, with the η mass window widened to 0.45 < M(γγ) < 0.65 GeV/c²; both tag and signal candidates with M_BC < 1.83 GeV/c² or |ΔE| > 0.1 GeV are rejected before fitting, using per-mode ±3.5σ ΔE windows. The signal side requires exactly one positively charged pion and four photons with a minimum photon opening angle of 10°, and before the fit the signal must satisfy 1.860 < M_BC < 1.880 GeV/c² and |ΔE| < 0.040 GeV. One best tag candidate per mode is chosen by |ΔE| closest to zero, and the signal candidate with M_BC closest to the nominal D+ mass is kept. A kinematic fit applies four-momentum conservation together with nominal-mass constraints on both η → γγ candidates, with χ² < 200. A BDTG trained on inclusive MC suppresses no-η backgrounds using ln(χ²) of the double-η mass-constraint fit and the invariant mass and helicity-angle cosine of the higher-energy photon in each η candidate, retaining 78.3% of signal and rejecting 89.7% of background with negligible Dalitz-plot distortion.
