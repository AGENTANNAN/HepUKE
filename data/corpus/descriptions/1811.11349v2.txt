At √s = 3.773 GeV on the ψ(3770), study D0 → K_S0 π− e+ ν_e with a tag-based analysis: the anti-D0 is reconstructed in three hadronic single-tag modes (K+π−, K+π−π0, K+π−π+π−), while the signal D0 decays to K_S0 π− e+ ν_e with K_S0 → π+π−. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k exclusive MC events for ψ(3770) → D0 anti-D0 with D0 → anti-K0 π− e+ ν_e and tag anti-D0 → K+π−. On the signal side require exactly one π+, two π−, one e+, net charge 0, and a massless missing ν; reconstruct K_S0 from π+π− with a secondary-vertex fit, |M(π+π−) − m_K_S0| < 12 MeV and decay-length significance > 2σ. Pion PID uses CL_π > CL_K, and the electron candidate requires E/p > 0.8 plus an EMC shower shape consistent with an electron; veto D0 → K_S0 π+π− background. Perform a 4C kinematic fit with a D mass constraint and χ² < 200, apply tag-mode-dependent ΔE windows in the single-tag selection, and extract the signal yield from U_miss = E_miss − |p_miss|.
