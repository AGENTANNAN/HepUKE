Study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → K_S^0 K_S^0 K_S^0 K_S^0, K_S^0 → π+π− at 3.686 GeV, using the 448.1M-event ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC; generate a 500k-event exclusive MC sample for ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → 4K_S^0, K_S^0 → π+π−. Event selection requires four positive and four negative charged tracks with net charge 0, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm and Vr<1 cm, plus at least one photon with EMC TDC 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV or endcap >50 MeV, and angle to any track >10°; no PID is applied, all charged tracks are assumed pions. Four K_S^0 candidates are formed by secondary-vertex fits to π+π− pairs, minimizing the mass difference and requiring M(π+π−) within 12 MeV/c² of the nominal K_S^0 mass and decay length L > 2σ_L, with used tracks removed. The selected γ K_S^0 K_S^0 K_S^0 K_S^0 combination is then subjected to a nominal 4C kinematic fit with χ²<200, including MC helix-parameter corrections. The same selection is run on data, inclusive MC and exclusive MC.
