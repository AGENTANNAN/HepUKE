At the ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), study e+e− → Λ Λbar, with Λ → p π− and Λbar → anti-p π+. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and its inclusive MC, plus a 200M-event exclusive MC sample generated for this channel (ΛΛbar phase space, Λ and Λbar hyperon weak decays). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm, Vz<10 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge zero. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying protons against kaons and pions, requiring at least one proton and at least one antiproton. Reconstruct Λ from p π− and Λbar from anti-p π+ via secondary vertex fits that minimize the invariant-mass difference, requiring the Λ decay length to be positive to suppress non-Λ background. Require M(p π−) and M(anti-p π+) within 5 MeV/c² of the known Λ mass, with sidebands used for background estimation. Then perform a nominal 4-momentum-constrained kinematic fit to Λ Λbar with χ²<200. The same selection chain is applied to data and MC.
