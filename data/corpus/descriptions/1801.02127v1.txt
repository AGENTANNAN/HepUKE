On the J/ψ resonance at √s = 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → γγφ with φ → K+K−, using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC and 500k exclusive MC events for the signal mode. Charged tracks are required to satisfy |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, with exactly one positive and one negative track and net charge 0. Photons are accepted with E > 25 MeV in the barrel or E > 50 MeV in the endcap, EMC TDC within 0–14, and at least two photons are required. Kaons are identified by the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating K from π/p, requiring at least one K+ and one K−. A nominal 4C kinematic fit to γγK+K− is performed with χ² < 40; competing 4C fits to 3γK+K− and 4γK+K− are also done, and events are vetoed if the signal χ² exceeds the smaller of the two competing χ² values. Further background suppression rejects π0 by requiring |M(γγ) − m(π0)| > 0.03 GeV/c², rejects η by requiring M(γγ) < 0.50 or > 0.58 GeV/c², and rejects η′ by requiring |M(γγ) − m(η′)| > 0.03 GeV/c². The same selection chain is applied to the real data, inclusive MC, and exclusive signal MC.
