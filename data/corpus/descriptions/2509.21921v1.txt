On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV) dataset, search for the lepton-number-violating decay η → π+π−e+e− via J/ψ → φη, using real data and inclusive MC. Two independent selections are implemented: the signal channel J/ψ → φ(→K+K−) η(→π+π−e+e−) and the reference channel J/ψ → φ(→K+K−) η(→γγ). For the signal, require six charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; identify high-momentum leptons and apply probability-based PID for kaons and pions. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the six tracks (K+, K−, π+, π−, e+, e−) to four-momentum conservation, with a nominal χ² cut of 200 (the paper's tight cuts of 20 and 40 are applied later in ROOT). For the reference channel, require two charged tracks with the same track-quality cuts, kaon PID by probability, and two photons with energy between 25 MeV and 1.4 GeV, angle to any track greater than 20°, and EMC TDC in [0,14]. Then perform a 4C kinematic fit on K+, K−, γ, γ with χ²<200. Both channels use the same J/ψ dataset and decay cards describing the full decay chains.
