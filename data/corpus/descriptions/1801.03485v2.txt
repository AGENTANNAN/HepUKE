On the ψ(2S) (3.686 GeV), study ψ(2S) → π⁰ h_c with h_c → π⁺π⁻ J/ψ and J/ψ → ℓ⁺ℓ⁻, using the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC plus two exclusive-MC samples: 200k events for the signal mode and 500k events for the reference mode ψ(2S) → η J/ψ, η → π⁰ π⁺ π⁻. Charged tracks are required to satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero; photons must have 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy, TDC within 0–14, and at least two are required. Particle ID uses the probability method (cut 0.001), with high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon); at least one ℓ⁺ and one ℓ⁻ are demanded, the remaining good positive/negative tracks being assigned as π⁺/π⁻. Two photons are then fitted with a Kalman fit constraining them to the nominal π⁰ mass (χ²<25, at least one π⁰), followed by a 5C kinematic fit (4C plus the π⁰ mass constraint) on π⁰ π⁺ π⁻ ℓ⁺ℓ⁻ with χ²<60, taking the nominal fit. The selection chain is applied identically to real data, inclusive MC, and both exclusive-MC samples.
