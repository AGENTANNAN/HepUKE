On the ψ(3770) at √s=3.773 GeV (2.93 fb⁻¹), study the double-tag D⁰D̄⁰ pair with the tag D̄⁰ → K⁺π⁻ and the signal D⁰ → K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻, using the real data and inclusive MC; generate 1,000,000 phase-space signal D⁰ → K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻ exclusive MC events and 500,000 D⁰ → K_S⁰ K⁻π⁺ peaking-background exclusive MC events, both with the same tag. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; the tag K⁺π⁻ pair must fall in 1.8575 < mBC < 1.8775 GeV/c² and -0.03 < ΔE < 0.03 GeV, while the signal side uses the remaining one K⁻, two π⁺, and one π⁻ with net charge zero, opposite charm, and no shared tracks. No photon cuts are applied since the final state is all charged. PID combines MDC dE/dx and TOF information: kaons require P(K)>P(π), pions require P(π)>P(K), and tracks lacking PID are rejected. A 5C kinematic fit constrains the total four-momentum to the initial e⁺e⁻ system and m(K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻) to the nominal D⁰ mass, with χ²<200 applied in BOSS (the paper's χ²<40 is imposed later in ROOT). A vertex fit on the D⁰D̄⁰ pair requires χ²<200, and any signal-side π⁺π⁻ combination with |m(π⁺π⁻)-m_K_S⁰|<0.03 GeV/c² is rejected if its decay-length significance exceeds 2σ; signal-side ΔE (-0.033, 0.033) GeV and the shared mBC window are applied in ROOT.
