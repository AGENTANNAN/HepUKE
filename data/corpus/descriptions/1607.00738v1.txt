Measure the inclusive J/ψ event yield at BESIII via N_J/ψ = (N_sel − N_bg)/(ε_trig·ε_data^{ψ(3686)}·f_cor), an inclusive counting analysis with no exclusive final state and no kinematic fit. Use J/ψ real data and inclusive MC at 3.097 GeV (2009 and 2012 samples) and ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC at 3.686 GeV, plus two 1M-event exclusive MC samples: ψ(3686) → π+π− J/ψ with J/ψ → anything (PHSP), and J/ψ → anything (PHSP) produced directly at rest. Event selection is run at the J/ψ energy (ECMS = 3.097 GeV): charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm, |Vz|<15 cm and at least two tracks; photons with E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.83) and E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.86<|cosθ|<0.93), with EMC cluster timing 0<T≤700 ns. Event-level cuts recorded as notes, since they have no dedicated selection keyword, require each good track to have p<2.0 GeV/c, visible energy E_vis>1.0 GeV, both track momenta <1.5 GeV/c when exactly two tracks are present (Bhabha/dimuon veto), and per-track EMC deposit <1 GeV. The ψ(3686) tag side selects at least two oppositely charged pions with |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm, |Vz|<15 cm and p<0.4 GeV/c, with no further requirement on the remaining tracks or showers; the π+π− recoil-mass fit yields ε_data^{ψ(3686)} and the two large inclusive MC samples give f_cor = ε_MC^{J/ψ}/ε_MC^{ψ(3686)}. No PID is required, and the same inclusive selection chain is applied to the J/ψ and ψ(3686) data, the inclusive MC, and both exclusive MC samples.
