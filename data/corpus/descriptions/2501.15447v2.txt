On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686)→π0 hc with hc radiative decays to four light-hadron final states: γπ+π−, γπ+π−η (η→γγ), γ2(π+π−), and γpp̄. Use the 709_3686 real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the four modes, with ψ(2S)→π0 hc, π0→γγ, and hc→γ+final state according to phase space (η→γγ also included for the η mode). Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100, Vr<10, net charge 0, and comprise two tracks for the π+π−, π+π−η, and pp̄ modes or four tracks for the 2(π+π−) mode. Photons must have EMC TDC in 0–14, be more than 10° from any track, and exceed 25 MeV in the barrel or 50 MeV in the endcap, with at least three photons (at least five for the π+π−η mode). PID uses the probability method: pions are identified against kaons, requiring 1π+ and 1π− for modes I and II and 2π+ and 2π− for mode III, while mode IV requires 1p and 1p̄ identified against kaons and pions. A 1C Kalman fit constrains γγ to the nominal π0 mass with χ²<25 and at least one π0 (mode II also adds a 1C γγ fit to the nominal η mass with χ²<25 and at least one η), followed by a 5C fit (4C plus π0 mass constraint) on the selected charged tracks, radiative photon, and π0 (plus η in mode II) with χ²<200. At ROOT level the analysis applies π0 and η mass windows (0.10–0.16 and 0.513–0.583 GeV/c², respectively), selects the minimum 5C χ² over photon combinations, and vetoes specific ω/φ, η′, ηc, and J/ψ regions as noted per mode.
