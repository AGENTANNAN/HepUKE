At BESIII, study J/ψ → Λ Λbar π0 / Λ Λbar η and ψ(2S) → Λ Λbar π0 / Λ Λbar η, with Λ → p π−, Λbar → anti-p π+, and π0/η → γγ. Use the 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, plus the 3.686 GeV ψ(2S) real data and inclusive MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the four modes (J/ψ and ψ(2S) to Λ Λbar π0 and Λ Λbar η). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, at least two, and EMC TDC in 0–14. PID by the probability method with probability >0.001, identifying protons against kaons and pions, requiring at least one proton and one antiproton; remaining positive/negative tracks are assigned as π+/π−. Reconstruct Λ and Λbar via secondary-vertex fits (pπ− and anti-pπ+) by minimizing the mass difference. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to Λ Λbar γγ, χ²<200 (loose; tighter cut downstream). Additional per-mode requirements: proton/antiproton pT>0.2 GeV/c; for J/ψ→ΛΛbarπ0 veto |M(pπ0)−1189.0|<10 MeV/c² and require M(ΛΛbar)<2.8 GeV/c²; for J/ψ→ΛΛbarη require M(ΛΛbar)<2.6 GeV/c²; for ψ(2S)→ΛΛbarπ0/η require |M_recoil(π+π−)−3097|>8 MeV/c² and M(ΛΛbar)<3.08 GeV/c². All four modes share the same common selection chain, with the listed mode-specific vetoes applied downstream.
