At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), search for J/ψ → φ η with η → π0 + invisible (S → χχ̄), φ → K+K− and π0 → γγ, simulating the invisible η decay as η → π0 νν̄. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for the signal chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two tracks with at least one K+ and one K−; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds within TDC EMC 0–14, at least two photons, with photons within 10° of a track rejected. Kaon PID by the probability method (L(K)>0, separated against π and p) requiring ≥1 K+ and ≥1 K−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit of K+K−γγ, nominal, with four-momentum constraint, K+K− mass window [1.00,1.04] GeV, γγ mass window [0.115,0.150] GeV constrained to the nominal π0 mass, χ²<200. Further require the K+K− recoil mass in [0.45,0.65] GeV/c² to tag the η, |cosθ_recoil(K+K−π0)|<0.7, total energy of photons outside the π0 below 0.1 GeV, and M(K+K−π0) < 2.7 / 2.15 / 2.0 GeV/c² for m_S in [0,165) / [165,305) / [305,400] MeV/c².
