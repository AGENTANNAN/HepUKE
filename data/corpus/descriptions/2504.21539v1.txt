At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), search for the lepton-number-violating decay ω → π+π−e+e− in J/ψ → ωη, η → γγ, using the J/ψ-peak real data and inclusive MC together with two 100k-event exclusive-MC samples, one generated as the reference mode ω → π+π−π0 and one as the signal mode ω → π+π+e−e− (both with η → γγ). For the reference channel (final state π+π−4γ) require exactly two charged tracks with net charge 0, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, at least four photons above 25 MeV and 10° minimum angle, and pion identification (CL>0.001 against kaons and protons); reconstruct π0 → γγ and η → γγ with 1C Kalman fits (χ²<200), form competing 4C hypotheses for π+π−π+π−γγ and K+K−K+K−γγ, and apply a nominal 5C fit (four-momentum plus π0 mass constraint) with χ²<20. For the signal channel (final state e+e−π+π−2γ) require four charged tracks with net charge 0 under the same tracking cuts, at least two photons above 25 MeV, electron identification (CL_e>0.001 and CL_e/(CL_e+CL_K+CL_π)>0.8) alongside pion identification, and an η → γγ 1C Kalman fit (χ²<200); reject the competing 4C hypotheses π+π−π+π−γγ, K+K−K+K−γγ, π+π−K+K−γγ and π+π−pp̄γγ, and apply a nominal 4C fit (no η mass constraint in the fit) with χ²<10. Final-state mass windows — |M_γγ−m_π0|<15 MeV and |M_γγ−m_η|<40 MeV for the reference, |M_γγ−m_η|<40 MeV and M_π+π−e+e−>1.0 GeV/c² for the signal, the latter defining the LNV signal region — are applied downstream from stored invariant masses, and competing-hypothesis discrimination is done by comparing stored χ² values.
