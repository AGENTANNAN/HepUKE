On the J/ψ at 3.097 GeV (dataset 708_3097, ~1.31×10⁹ events), study J/ψ → ω η′ π⁺π⁻ with ω → π⁺π⁻π⁰ (ω Dalitz, π⁰→γγ) and η′ → η π⁺π⁻ (η→γγ), giving a 3π⁺3π⁻4γ final state, and search for X(1835) → η′π⁺π⁻ recoiling against the ω. Use the real J/ψ data plus inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes — J/ψ → ω η′π⁺π⁻ phase space, and J/ψ → ω X(1835), X(1835) → η′π⁺π⁻ — both including the ω → π⁺π⁻π⁰ and η′ → ηπ⁺π⁻ decays. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 and Vr<10 (as coded), exactly three positive and three negative tracks with net charge zero; photons with EMC TDC 0–14, at least 5° from any charged track, 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, requiring at least four. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying pions against kaons, requiring 3 π⁺ and 3 π⁻, with the pion candidates removed from the charged-track list. Two Kalman fits constrain γγ pairs to the nominal π⁰ and η masses (χ²<25 each, at least one π⁰ and one η), followed by a nominal 4C kinematic fit over the six pions + π⁰ + η with χ²<60. The best ω/η′ combination is chosen by minimising √((M(π⁺π⁻π⁰)−m_ω)² + (M(ηπ⁺π⁻)−m_η′)²), with mass windows |M(π⁺π⁻π⁰)−m_ω|<22 MeV and |M(ηπ⁺π⁻)−m_η′|<12 MeV applied; the X(1835) yield is then extracted from a 2D fit to the π⁺π⁻π⁰ and ηπ⁺π⁻ mass distributions.
