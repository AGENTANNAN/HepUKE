At √s = 3.773 GeV (ψ(3770), 2.93 fb⁻¹) we study the semileptonic decays D⁺ → π⁺π⁻ ℓ⁺ν_ℓ with ℓ = μ or e, using a single-tag technique: the tag-side D⁻ is fully reconstructed in six hadronic modes (K⁺π⁻π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰, K_Sπ⁻, K_Sπ⁻π⁰, K_Sπ⁻π⁺π⁻, K⁺K⁻π⁻), and the recoil D⁺ is searched for in π⁺π⁻ℓ⁺ν. We use the 3.773 GeV real data and the inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each channel (D⁺ → π⁺π⁻μ⁺ν_μ and D⁺ → π⁺π⁻e⁺ν_e, the tag being D⁻ → K⁺π⁻π⁻) with PHOTOS/ISGW2 for the signal decay. Signal side requires exactly one π⁺, one π⁻ and one same-flavour lepton, net charge 0, with the neutrino treated as missing. A four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with a massless neutrino is imposed, retaining events with χ² < 200. Additional selection is applied later at the ROOT level: |U_miss| < 0.025 GeV, maximum energy of unused photons < 0.25 GeV, and no extra charged tracks beyond the three signal tracks; the muon is identified by CL_μ > 0.001 together with CL_μ exceeding the e, π and K likelihoods and an EMC energy deposit in the 0.09–0.31 GeV MIP window, while for the electron channel no equivalent PID note is set. The f0(500) and ρ⁰ content is then extracted from fits to the M(π⁺π⁻) spectrum in q² bins, combining the electron and muon channels, which is done outside the BOSS selection.
