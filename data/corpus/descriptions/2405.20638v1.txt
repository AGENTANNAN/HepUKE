On the ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → Λ Λ̄ φ (J=0,1,2), with Λ→pπ−, Λ̄→p̄π+, φ→K+K−, using the 3.686 GeV BOSS 709 real data and inclusive MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the χ_c0, χ_c1, and χ_c2 modes. Event selection uses charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least three positive and three negative tracks, and net charge zero; photons are required with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy threshold, TDC 0–14, angle to any track >10°, and at least one photon. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying protons against kaons/pions and kaons against pions/protons, requiring at least one p+, p̄, K+, K−; remaining charged tracks are assigned as pions. Reconstruct Λ→pπ− and Λ̄→p̄π+ through displaced secondary vertices, choosing the combination that minimizes the Λ/Λ̄ mass difference, then perform a 4C kinematic fit to γ Λ Λ̄ K+K− with four-momentum conservation and χ²<60, nominal. The same selection chain is used for all three χ_cJ states, with ROOT-level simultaneous fit applying Λ/Λ̄ windows of ±6 MeV/c², a φ window of |M(K+K−)−mφ|<18 MeV/c², and Ω−/Ω̄+ vetoes of |M(ΛK−)−mΩ|>12 MeV/c².
