On the ψ(4260) energy scan at 4.180, 4.230, and 4.260 GeV, study e+e− → γ X(3872) in three modes: X(3872) → γ J/ψ with J/ψ → e+e− (selected as l+l−), X(3872) → D*0 D0bar with D*0 → γ D0, D0 → K−π+, D0bar → K+π−, and the normalization mode X(3872) → π+π− J/ψ with J/ψ → e+e−; use the real data at these points and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each mode at the scan points. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge 0, with at least one positive and one negative track for γJ/ψ and at least two of each for D*0D0 and π+π−J/ψ; photons must have 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy, EMC TDC 0–14, angle to nearest track >10°, and at least two photons for γJ/ψ and D*0D0 or at least one for π+π−J/ψ. PID uses the probability method (prob>0.001); high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) are treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, else muon), with π/K separation for the D*0D0 and π+π−J/ψ selections; requirements are ≥1 l+ and ≥1 l− for γJ/ψ, ≥1 K+, ≥1 K−, ≥1 π+, ≥1 π− for D*0D0, and ≥1 l+, ≥1 l−, ≥1 π+, ≥1 π− for π+π−J/ψ. For γJ/ψ, after lepton removal the remaining charged tracks are pions, the two photons are ordered by energy (higher as γ_H, lower as γ_L), and a nominal 4C fit to l+l−γγ is applied with χ²<40, using |M(l+l−)−m_J/ψ|<0.02 GeV, a π0/η veto on M(γ_L γ_H), a χ_c veto |M(γ_L J/ψ)−m_χ_c|>0.02 GeV, and Bhabha suppression |cosθ_γ|∈[−0.7,0.7] for the e+e− mode. For D*0D0, an intermediate γγ fit to the π0 mass (χ²<25, no π0-count requirement) precedes a nominal 4C fit to K+K−π+π−γγ with χ²<60, with D*0 mass window ±0.006 GeV for the γD0 mode (and ±0.004 GeV noted for a π0D0 mode), D-meson mass windows, π0/η veto, and the smallest-χ² combination chosen among multi-photon ambiguities. For the normalization mode, a nominal 4C fit to π+π−l+l−γ is performed with χ²<200, applying |M(l+l−)−m_J/ψ|<0.02 GeV and a π0/η veto.
