On 44 energy points from 3.808 to 4.951 GeV, study e+e− → η J/ψ with η → γγ or π0π+π− and J/ψ → e+e− / μ+μ−. Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC at all points, and generate a 100k-event exclusive MC sample for each of Mode I and Mode II using the same e+e− → η J/ψ decay card. Both modes require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and zero net charge; Mode I has exactly two tracks, Mode II exactly four. Photons must satisfy EMC timing 0–14, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, opening angle >20° to any track, and at least two photons. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001; high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) are treated as leptons (electron-like if E/pc>0.8, muon-like if E≤0.4 GeV), accepting only same-flavor opposite-charge lepton pairs, and in Mode II pions are separated from kaons/protons. Mode I performs a 4C fit to γγℓ+ℓ− with χ²<40, nominal, and requires each fitted photon energy >0.08 GeV. Mode II first constrains two photons to the π0 mass (χ²<25, at least one π0), then performs a 5C fit to π0π+π−ℓ+ℓ− with four-momentum and π0-mass constraints (χ²<80, nominal). A J/ψ mass window [3.067, 3.127] GeV/c2 is applied downstream, with sidebands [3.027, 3.057] and [3.137, 3.167] GeV/c2 for background.
