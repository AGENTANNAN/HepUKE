On the ψ(3686) (3.686 GeV), study the radiative decays ψ(3686) → γη′, γη and γπ0, using the 3.686 GeV real data set together with its inclusive MC, and generating 200k-event exclusive MC samples for each of five modes: γη′ with η′→π+π−η, η→γγ; γη′ with η′→π0π0η, η→γγ; γη with η→π+π−π0, π0→γγ; γη with η→π0π0π0; and γπ0. For the charged final states (γπ+π−γγ) require exactly one positive and one negative track with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm and net charge 0, at least three photons of E>25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) with TDC in 0–14 and, for the η′→π+π−η channel only, an angle of more than 10° from any charged track; the neutral modes (7γ or 3γ) veto charged tracks entirely, and the γπ0 channel accepts exactly three photons restricted to the barrel (|cosθ|<0.8). Particle ID for the charged-pion samples uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying pions against kaons and protons. All channels are subjected to a 4C kinematic fit with χ²<80 (χ²<40 for γπ0). Additional suppression for γπ0 comes from |cosθ_hel|<0.7 of the π0 and a veto of fewer than 8 MDC hits between the interaction point and the two EMC shower positions to remove converted photons from e+e−→γγ(γ_ISR); in the η′→π0π0η channel the photon combination minimising χ²_M = Σ(M_γγ−M_π0)²/σ_π0² + (M_γγ−M_η)²/σ_η² (σ_π0=4.8 MeV, σ_η=8.7 MeV) is retained.
