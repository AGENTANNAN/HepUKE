At the nine c.m. energies from 4.210 to 4.420 GeV, study e+e− → ω χ_cJ (J=0,1,2), with ω → π+π−π0, χ_c0 → π+π− or K+K−, and χ_c1,2 → γ J/ψ, J/ψ → e+e−/μ+μ−. Use the real data and inclusive MC at all nine points, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the three modes (ωχ_c0, ωχ_c1, ωχ_c2) at every energy. For χ_c0, select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks with net charge zero, and at least two photons with 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) thresholds; identify pions and kaons by probability (prob>0.001, π vs K/p and K vs π/p), first fit the two photons to the π0 mass (χ²<200, at least one π0), then perform 5C fits to π0 π+π−π+π− and π0 π+π−K+K− constraining the total four-momentum and M(γγ) to m(π0), with χ²<100 for the ππ hypothesis and the smaller-χ² hypothesis defining the mode; tracks with p>1 GeV/c are taken as χ_c0 daughters and lower-momentum pions as ω daughters. For χ_c1 and χ_c2, use the same track and photon requirements but require at least three photons and probability PID at 0.001 with p>1.0 GeV/c tracks treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon), then perform the same π0 fit followed by a 5C fit to π0 γ π+π− l+l− (total four-momentum plus M(γγ) constrained to m(π0)) with χ²<60, and apply a ψ(2S) veto rejecting M(π+π−l+l−) or recoil M(π+π−) in 3.68–3.70 GeV/c²; the χ_c1 and χ_c2 selections are identical.
