Using the 3.686 GeV ψ(2S) data (106 M events) and matching inclusive MC, study ψ(2S)→γχ_cJ with χ_cJ→ΛΛ̄π+π−, Λ→pπ−, Λ̄→p̄π+, for χ_c0, χ_c1 and χ_c2; generate 500k exclusive-MC events for the χ_c1 mode (phase-space χ_c1→ΛΛ̄π+π−) and clone the card with the appropriate χ_cJ mother for the other two. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, |Vr|<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; photons with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap thresholds, at least one. No charged PID is applied—positive tracks are assigned as either p or π+ and negatives as either p̄ or π−—then two secondary-vertex fits reconstruct Λ from pπ− and Λ̄ from p̄π+ by minimizing the invariant-mass difference, removing used tracks. A 4C kinematic fit to γ Λ Λ̄ π+π− with a four-momentum constraint and nominal flag is required with χ²<200 (the paper applies the tighter χ²<80 in ROOT). Additional ROOT-side vetoes are noted for J/ψ (π+π− recoil mass outside 3.088–3.108 GeV/c²), Σ0 (M(γΛ) or M(γΛ̄) outside 1.183–1.202 GeV/c²), and Ξ (M(Λπ−) or M(Λ̄π+) outside 1.312–1.331 GeV/c²).
