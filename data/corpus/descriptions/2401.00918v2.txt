Study J/ψ → γ γ φ, φ → K+ K− at √s = 3.097 GeV. Use the J/ψ real data (10087×10^6 events) and inclusive MC at 3.097 GeV; generate 1M-event exclusive MC for J/ψ → γ γ φ, φ → K+ K− using a phase-space generator for partial-wave analysis. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track; PID by the probability method, identifying kaons against pions and protons, requiring one K+ and one K−; photons with barrel energy >25 MeV, endcap >50 MeV, EMC time within 0–14, and angle to any track >10°, at least two photons. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γ γ K+ K−, χ² cut 40, plus competing 4C fits for 1γ K+ K−, 3γ K+ K−, and 4γ K+ K−, retaining events only if the nominal χ² is smaller than all competing hypotheses. Post-fit, require |M(K+K−)−M_φ|<0.005 GeV/c², veto γγ masses in the π0/η/η′ windows (0.11–0.16, 0.46–0.59, 0.92–0.99 GeV/c²), and apply a Dalitz cut M²(γ_high φ)<9 GeV²/c⁴. Background subtraction (Q-factor, reweighting) and the partial-wave analysis are performed at the ROOT stage.
