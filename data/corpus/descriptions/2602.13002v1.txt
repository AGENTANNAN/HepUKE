On ψ(3770), measure the coherence factors and strong-phase differences in D0 → K−π+π+π− and D0 → K−π+π0 using quantum-correlated D0D̄0 pairs. Use the 7.93 fb−1 real data sample at √s = 3.773 GeV and its matching inclusive MC, and generate 1.5M-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes (ψ(3770) → D0D̄0, signal D0 decaying according to a phase-space model). Reconstruct double tags, with one D0 in the signal mode and the other in a tag mode taken from flavour tags (K−π+, K−π+π0, K−π+π+π−), CP-even tags (K+K−, π+π−, π+π−π0, KS0π0π0, KL0π0, KL0ω), CP-odd tags (KS0π0, KS0η, KS0ω, KS0η′, KS0φ, KL0π0π0) and self-conjugate tags (KS0π+π−, KL0π+π−), dropping the like-sign K−π+π+π− tag in the K−π+π0 signal analysis. Tag modes containing a KL0 are reconstructed with a missing-mass technique, vetoing events with extra π0's or charged tracks, any η→γγ candidate, or π0–π0 shower sharing. A KS0 flight-distance veto is applied to D → π+π−π0 and D → K−π+π+π− to suppress KS0 backgrounds, and like-sign tags use tightened PID (track called K if P_K > 100 P_π, π if P_π > 100 P_K). When several double-tag candidates are found, retain the pair whose average reconstructed invariant mass is closest to the nominal D0 mass. Perform a 6C kinematic fit constraining the total four-momentum and both D0 candidate masses to nominal m_D0, with χ² < 200, and for the binned K3π analysis additionally apply a mass constraint to nominal m_D0 on the K−π+π+π− side.
