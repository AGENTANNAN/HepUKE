At √s = 3.097 GeV, using the J/ψ resonance, measure the absolute branching fractions of the Σ+ nonleptonic decays Σ+ → p π0 and Σ+ → n π+ via a double-tag technique, with the Σ− → p̄ π0 decay reconstructed as the tag; use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive J/ψ MC, plus two 200k-event exclusive MC samples, one for J/ψ → Σ+Σ̄− with Σ+ → p π0 and one with Σ+ → n π+, both with Σ− → p̄ π0. Charged tracks are required to satisfy |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, and Vr < 2 cm; photons must have TDC 0–14, lie more than 10° from any charged track and more than 20° from the antiproton candidate, deposit at least 25 MeV in the barrel or 50 MeV in the endcap, and be at least two in number. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating protons against kaons and pions and pions against kaons; Mode 1 (Σ+ → p π0) requires at least two positive and one negative track with net charge ≥ 0, plus at least one proton and one antiproton, while Mode 2 (Σ+ → n π+) requires at least one positive and one negative track with net charge 0, plus at least one antiproton and one π+. A kinematic fit constrains the two photons to the π0 mass with χ² < 25, requiring at least one π0. The Σ− tag is formed from p̄π0, keeping the combination closest to the Σ− mass and requiring M(p̄π0) in [1.169, 1.205] GeV; for the double-tag signal, the extra proton (Mode 1) or π+ (Mode 2) must give a recoil-mass variable in [0.034, 0.231] GeV or [0.881, 0.997] GeV, respectively, corresponding to three-sigma windows around the π0 and neutron masses.
