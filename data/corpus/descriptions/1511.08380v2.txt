At the Λc+Λc− threshold (√s = 4.599 GeV), measure absolute hadronic Λc+ branching fractions from 567 pb−1 of real data and inclusive MC using the double-tag technique in twelve Cabibbo-favored modes: p K_S0, p K−π+, p K_S0π0, p K_S0π+π−, p K−π+π0, Λπ+, Λπ+π0, Λπ+π−π+, Σ0π+, Σ+π0, Σ+π+π−, and Σ+ω. Generate 200k-event exclusive signal MC per mode, with Λc+ decaying to that final state and the recoil Λc− decaying generically, all phase space. Reconstruct one Λc as the tag from the pre-stored tag collection over all twelve modes with charm fixed to Λc−, and the recoil Λc+ on the signal side with the mode-specific charged multiplicity and net charge +1, allowing zero, one, two, or (for the two-π0 modes) two-to-four photons. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm (K_S0 and Λ daughters exempt); proton PID uses L(p)>L(K) and L(p)>L(π), and K/π separation uses L(K)>L(π) or L(π)>L(K). Photons require E>25 MeV in the barrel (|cosθ|<0.8) or E>50 MeV in the endcap (0.84<|cosθ|<0.92) with EMC time in (0,700) ns; π0 candidates have 0.115<M(γγ)<0.150 GeV and are mass-constrained to the nominal π0. K_S0 and Λ come from oppositely charged pairs with DCA along the beam within ±20 cm, vertex χ²<100, a decay vertex at least twice its resolution from the IP, and mass windows 0.487–0.511 GeV (K_S0) and 1.111–1.121 GeV (Λ); Σ0, Σ+, and ω use 1.179–1.203, 1.176–1.200, and 0.760–0.800 GeV, with vetoes against Λ, Σ+, and K_S0 backgrounds. Each mode is kinematically fitted with four-momentum conservation, the tag-side Λc mass, and the relevant intermediate resonance(s) (π0, K_S0, Λ, Σ0, Σ+, Λc+), χ²<200; mode-dependent ΔE windows of (−0.020,0.020), (−0.030,0.020), or (−0.050,0.030) GeV and an M_BC signal region 2.276–2.300 GeV are applied, while the two modes with two π0 use only the four-momentum and tag-side Λc mass constraints. Charge-conjugate modes are implicit with all charges reversed; single- and double-tag yields are extracted from M_BC fits for the global branching-fraction fit.
