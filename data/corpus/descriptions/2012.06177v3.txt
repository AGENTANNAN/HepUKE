On the J/ψ at √s = 3.097 GeV, study J/ψ → γ η η η′ with η′ reconstructed in two modes: η′ → γ π+ π− (with η → γ γ) and η′ → π+ π− η (with η → γ γ). Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 500,000-event exclusive MC for each of the two η′ decay modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track, with no particle identification applied. Photons must satisfy TDC 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV and endcap energy >50 MeV, be at least 10° from any charged track, and number at least 6 (Mode I) or at least 7 (Mode II). Reconstruct three η mesons from γγ pairs constrained to the nominal η mass with χ²<25, then perform a 6C kinematic fit for Mode I (four-momentum plus two η mass constraints on γ η η η γ π+π−) with χ²<30 and a 7C fit for Mode II (four-momentum plus three η mass constraints on γ η η η π+π−) with χ²<50, both flagged nominal; additional π0 veto (|M(γγ)−Mπ0|<0.015 GeV/c²), extra-η veto, and, for Mode I, M(π+π−)>0.5 GeV/c² are applied in the final ROOT analysis.
