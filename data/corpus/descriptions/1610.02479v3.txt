On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → γ χ_c1 with χ_c1 → η π+π−, using three η decay modes: η→γγ, η→π+π−π0, and η→π0π0π0. Use the real 3.686 GeV ψ(2S) data and inclusive MC (106M generic ψ(2S) events); generate exclusive MC samples of 800k, 460k, and 660k events for the three modes, respectively. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<2 cm, zero net charge, and either 1π+1π− (modes I/III) or 2π+2π− (mode II), with all charged tracks treated as pions. Photons are required to have TDC 0–14, be at least 10° from any track, and have energy >25 MeV (barrel) or >50 MeV (endcap), requiring at least 3 photons in modes I/II and at least 7 photons in mode III. Mode I uses a two-photon mass constraint to the η (χ²<15, ≥1 η) followed by a nominal 5C fit to γ η π+π− (4C plus mγγ=mη), χ²<200; mode II uses a two-photon mass constraint to the π0 (χ²<25, ≥1 π0) and a nominal 5C fit to γ π0 π+π−π+π− (4C plus mγγ=mπ0), χ²<200; mode III uses three photon-pair mass constraints to π0 (χ²<25, ≥3 π0) and a nominal 7C fit to γ π0π0π0 π+π− (4C plus three mγγ=mπ0 constraints), χ²<200. The published tighter χ² cuts (40 for modes I/II, 56 for mode III), the η mass window 0.535<m(3π)<0.560 GeV/c² in modes II/III, the radiative-photon energy window 0.155<Eγ<0.185 GeV, and sideband/background vetoes are applied later in ROOT.
