Measure e+e− → Σc Σ̄c and Λc+ Σ̄c− via a single-tag technique, reconstructing the tagging Λc+ in pK−π+, pK_S0 and Λπ+ (with K_S0 → π+π−, Λ → pπ−), and aiming at the four Λc+ modes pK_S0, pK−π+, Λπ+ and Σ0π+ (Σ0 → Λγ) for the signal. Use real data and inclusive MC at seven scan points: two for Σc Σ̄c (4.918, 4.951 GeV) and five for Λc+ Σ̄c− (4.750, 4.781, 4.843, 4.918, 4.951 GeV); generate 200k-event exclusive MC of ψ(4520) → Λc+ Σ̄c− phase space with the four Λc+ modes at 25% each (plus the K_S0, Λ and Σ0 cascade decays). Event selection: a tag-side Λc+ from the three listed modes, and no photons required on the signal side at BOSS level. Perform a 4-momentum kinematic fit constraining the tag Λc+ invariant mass to its nominal value, with χ² < 200. The Λc+ → Σ0π+ tag mode is dropped for lack of a decay-mode vocabulary match, and the Σc(2455/2520) states are reconstructed outside BOSS from M_BC(Λc+π) with RM(Λc+) > 2.54 GeV/c² and |ΔM| < 0.02 GeV/c²; the ST, ST+π and ST+ππ tag samples are all foreseen, but only the single-tag sample is encoded here.
