At √s = 4.6 GeV (567 pb⁻¹) and near Λ_c⁺Λ_c⁻ threshold, use the double-tag method to measure the absolute branching fraction for inclusive Λ_c⁺ → Λ + X and to search for direct CP violation. Take the 4.6 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus 200k-event exclusive MC generated from ψ(4260) → Λ_c⁺ anti-Λ_c⁻ with Λ_c⁺ → Λ π⁺π⁻, Λ → p⁺π⁻, anti-Λ_c⁻ → anti-p K_S⁰, K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻. Tag the anti-Λ_c⁻ in two hadronic modes, anti-p K_S⁰ and anti-p K⁺π⁻, and reconstruct the signal Λ inclusively from the remaining tracks as p⁺π⁻ with net charge 0. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; PID by likelihood, requiring L(p)>L(K) and L(p)>L(π) for protons and L(K)>L(π) for kaons, with no PID applied to pions from Λ or K_S⁰ decays; ΔE windows mode-dependent at ±2.5σ. Reconstruct K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻ and Λ → pπ⁻ with a vertex-fit χ²<100 and a secondary-vertex constraint pointing back to the IP with decay length more than twice its resolution, keeping only one track pair per event, with K_S⁰ mass in [487,511] MeV/c² and Λ mass in [1111,1121] MeV/c². Perform a kinematic fit constraining four-momentum with χ² cut 200, extracting the signal via a 2D M_BC versus M(pπ⁻) fit with ARGUS background and five sideband regions.
