On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ η′ in two independent decay modes: η′ → π+π−π+π− and η′ → π+π−π0π0 with π0 → γγ. Use the 1.3×10^9-event 2009+2012 J/ψ real data set and its inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each signal and background mode, namely η′→π+π−π+π− and η′→π+π−η (η→γπ+π−) plus J/ψ→γ f2(1270) (f2→π+π−π+π−) for the charged mode, and η′→π+π−π0π0 (π0→γγ), η′→π+π−η (η→π0π0π0), and J/ψ→γ f2(1270) (f2→π+π−π+π−) for the neutral mode. Both modes require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; mode A takes exactly two π+ and two π− with zero net charge and at least one photon, while mode B takes exactly one π+ and one π− with zero net charge and at least five photons. Photons must have E>25 MeV in the barrel or E>50 MeV in the endcap and be at least 10° from the nearest charged track. Pion identification uses the probability method with prob>0.001 against K and p; mode A requires at least one identified π+ and π− (remaining tracks are assumed pions), while mode B requires exactly one of each. Mode A performs a vertex fit on the four charged tracks then a 4C fit to γπ+π−π+π− with χ²<200 (nominal), and also stores the competing 4C γγπ+π−π+π− hypothesis for a χ² ordering veto; mode B first performs 1C mass-constrained π0 fits on photon pairs with χ²<50 and at least two π0, then a vertex fit on the two charged tracks followed by a 6C fit to γπ+π−π0π0 with χ²<200 (nominal), plus the competing 6C γγπ+π−π0π0 hypothesis for a χ² ordering veto. The tighter published χ²<35 cut and the η/ω mass vetoes (|M(π+π−π0)−m|<0.02 GeV/c²) are applied in the later analysis.
