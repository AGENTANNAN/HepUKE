At c.m. energies from 3.810 to 4.600 GeV, measure E_cms from e+e− → γ_ISR/FSR μ+μ− using 18 nominal real-data samples (25 data sets, with separately acquired sub-samples at 4.009, 4.230, 4.260 and 4.420 GeV merged by sample name) and inclusive MC for the 4009, 4190scan, 4210scan, 4220scan, 4230, 4260, 4360, 4420 and 4600 points. Generate 50k-event exclusive MC per energy point for each mode: di-muon signal with ISR/FSR on and off, J/ψ momentum check with FSR on and off (e+e− → γ_ISR J/ψ, J/ψ → μ+μ−), D0 → K−π+ / D0bar → K+π−, e+e− → π+π−K+K−, and e+e− → π+π−p pbar. For the di-muon and J/ψ channels require exactly one positive and one negative track with |cosθ|<0.80, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge zero; the di-muon case additionally requires EMC energy deposition <0.4 GeV per track and identifies one high-momentum lepton pair by the probability method (p>1.0 treated as lepton, electron if EMC energy >0.6, otherwise muon), with one l+ and one l−. The D0, π+π−K+K− and π+π−p pbar validations use |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge zero, one positive plus one negative track for D0 modes and two positive plus two negative tracks for the four-body modes, with probability-method PID (probability cut 0.001) separating K, π and p against each other and requiring exactly one K−π+ or K+π−, one K+K−π+π−, or one p pbar π+π−. No photon requirement is applied, and no invariant-mass window is encoded in the selection; every algorithm ends with a nominal 4C kinematic fit constraining the final-state four-momenta to the c.m. four-momentum and χ²<200, although the paper’s E_cms extraction actually uses the measured M(μ+μ−) plus the ISR/FSR shift, with the μ+μ− peak fitted run-by-run in a −1σ to +2σ window at ROOT level.
