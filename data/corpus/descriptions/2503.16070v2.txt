On ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV (20.3 fb⁻¹), search for the semileptonic decay D⁺ → γ e⁺ ν_e using a double-tag method in which the D⁻ tag is reconstructed in six hadronic modes (K⁺π⁻π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰, K_S π⁻, K_S π⁻π⁰, K_S π⁻π⁻π⁺, K⁺K⁻π⁻) with charm −1 and a loose common |ΔE| < 60 MeV window, precise windows being applied later. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, together with 1M-event exclusive MC for the signal D⁺ → γ e⁺ ν_e (phase space) and 1M events for the validation mode D⁺ → π⁰ e⁺ ν_e with π⁰ → γγ (phase space). On the signal side require exactly one photon and one positron (net charge +1) with one massless missing ν_e; if several photons pass, the most energetic one is retained, and FSR photons within 5° of the positron track are added back into the positron momentum. A 4C kinematic fit constrains the tag, γ, e⁺ and ν_e to the c.m. energy with χ² < 200, the double-tag yield is extracted from a U_miss fit with U_miss ∈ [−0.2, 0.2] GeV and an upper limit set by a Bayesian method, and a three-class DNN (signal / π⁰ e ν / other) provides additional signal–background separation through score cuts applied in the ROOT analysis. The validation channel requires two photons and an e⁺, with M(γγ) ∈ [115, 150] MeV/c² before the fit and constrained to the nominal π⁰ mass in the 4C fit (χ² < 200), and uses partially reversed DNN requirements (π⁰ e ν score > 0.15, other-background score < 0.05) with the U′_miss observable.
