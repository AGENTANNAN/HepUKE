Using real data and inclusive MC for the 4.237–4.699 GeV scan (10.64 fb−1), measure semileptonic Ds+ branching fractions via e+e− → Ds*+Ds*− with a double-tag method. Generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC for each scan point for e+e− → Ds*+Ds*− (decay card ψ(4260) → Ds*+Ds*−, Ds*± → γDs±, Ds± → K+K−π±). Fully reconstruct the tag-side Ds*− through 14 hadronic modes Ds*− → γ(π0)Ds−, using ΔE and M_BC requirements with the M_BC signal region 2.104–2.123 GeV/c² (energy-dependent), choosing the best candidate per mode by minimum |ΔE| and extracting ST yields from M_BC fits. On the signal side, use tracks/showers not used in the tag and reconstruct Ds+ → ηe+νe, η′e+νe, φe+νe, f0(980)e+νe, K0e+νe, or K*0e+νe, requiring one electron with CL_e > 0.001 and CL_e/(CL_e+CL_π+CL_K) > 0.8 plus a missing neutrino, and recovering bremsstrahlung by adding EMC showers within 10°. No explicit charged-track |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr, or photon-energy thresholds are encoded beyond these requirements. Apply mass windows for φ → K+K− [1.004,1.034], f0(980) → π+π− [0.880,1.080], K*0 → K+π− [0.882,0.992] GeV/c², require the hadron–lepton invariant mass <1.90 (1.75) GeV/c² for Cabibbo-favored (suppressed) decays, veto extra energy with maximum unused shower energy <0.3 GeV and no extra charged tracks on the double-tag side, and extract the signal from M²_miss fits with the Ds*+ momentum constrained to the tag Ds*− direction. A four-momentum (4C) constrained kinematic fit is applied with χ² < 200, and simultaneous fits are performed for the η and η′ modes; hadronic form factors are obtained from a two-parameter series expansion.
