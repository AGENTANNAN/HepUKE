On the J/ψ resonance at 3.097 GeV, using (10087±44)×10⁶ real J/ψ events and inclusive J/ψ MC, study J/ψ → γ η′, η′ → π⁺π⁻η, with η → μ⁺μ⁻ (mode I) and η → e⁺e⁻ (mode II); generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the two η decay modes. Event selection requires at least two positive and two negative charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge zero, and at least one photon with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC TDC 0–14, and angle to nearest track >10°. Particle identification uses the probability method with probability >0.001, identifying muons against pions/kaons and pions against kaons (mode I) or electrons against pions/kaons (mode II), requiring at least one μ⁺ and one μ⁻ (or one e⁺ and one e⁻) with π/K separation; identified leptons are then removed from the track list, and the remaining positive (negative) tracks are assigned as π⁺ (π⁻). Perform a 4C kinematic fit on the γ π⁺π⁻π⁺π⁻ hypothesis (all charged tracks treated as pions) with χ²<40, and use the sum of the 4C and PID χ² for best-candidate selection, requiring for the muon mode that the all-pion hypothesis χ² exceed that of the γ π⁺π⁻μ⁺μ⁻ hypothesis to reject J/ψ → γ 2(π⁺π⁻) background. The η′ mass window is [0.945, 0.970] GeV/c², and for the e⁺e⁻ mode the η signal region is [0.534, 0.560] GeV/c² with sidebands [0.508, 0.534] and [0.560, 0.586] GeV/c².
