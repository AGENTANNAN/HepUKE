On ψ(4260), study e+e− → K_S0 K+ π− π0 and e+e− → K_S0 K+ π− η, with K_S0 → π+π− and π0/η → γγ. Use the 4.260 GeV point of the 3.90–4.60 GeV multi-energy scan (5.2 fb−1) real data and inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0; photons with TDC 0–14, 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy threshold, at least 20° from any track, and at least two photons. PID by the probability method with probability >0.001, identifying kaons against pions/protons, requiring one K+ and one K−; remove identified kaons from the charged list, assign remaining positives as π+ and negatives as π−. A secondary vertex fit on π+π− builds K_S0 by minimizing the mass difference, removing used particles, with |M(π+π−)−M_K_S0|<12 MeV/c², decay length >2σ, and best K_S0 by smallest vertex χ². Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to K_S0 K+ π− γγ with four-momentum constraint and χ²<60; after the fit use the K_S0 window 0.488–0.508 GeV/c², the Mode I π0 window 0.12–0.15 GeV/c², and the Mode II η window 0.52–0.58 GeV/c². Both decay modes share the same selection chain.
