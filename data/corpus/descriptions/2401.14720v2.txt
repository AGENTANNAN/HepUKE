Measure e+e− → ωχ_c1,2 cross sections at √s = 4.310–4.946 GeV, with ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ, χ_cJ → γJ/ψ, and J/ψ → e+e−/μ+μ−. Use the 19 real-data energy points and matched inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for the representative ωχ_c1 mode via ψ(4260) → ωχ_c1, ω → π+π−π0, χ_c1 → γJ/ψ, J/ψ → e+e−, noting that the identical selection is applied to both χ_c1 and χ_c2 and to both J/ψ lepton channels. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge zero; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, >10° from any track, and at least three photons. PID uses the probability method, treating tracks with p>1.0 GeV as leptons (electron if EMC energy >1.0 GeV) and requiring at least one positive and one negative lepton, with the remaining tracks assigned to π+/π−. Reconstruct π0 from two photons with an invariant-mass constraint to the nominal π0 mass, χ²<25, requiring at least one π0, then perform a 5C kinematic fit to π+π− l+l− γ π0 with four-momentum and π0-mass constraints, χ²<200, flagged nominal; the tighter published χ²<60 and the ω [0.75,0.81] GeV/c² and J/ψ [3.08,3.12] GeV/c² windows are applied later at ROOT level, and a helix correction is applied to charged tracks before the 5C fit.
