At 4.178–4.278 GeV, search for e+e− → γ χ_c1(3872) with the radiative cascade χ_c1(3872) → γ ψ_2(3823), ψ_2(3823) → γ χ_c1, χ_c1 → γ J/ψ, J/ψ → l+l−, giving a final state of two leptons plus four photons. Use the eleven real data points from 4.178 to 4.280 GeV (including 4.237, 4.246, 4.260) with the matching inclusive MC, plus a 200k-event exclusive MC for e+e− → γ χ_c1(3872) → four photons + J/ψ → e+e− (cascade generated with phase space, E1 transition for the initial radiative production). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and at least one positive and one negative track (exactly two in total); photons with 25 MeV energy threshold in both barrel and endcap, EMC timing within 0–700 ns, more than 10° from any track, at least four required. Particle ID uses the probability method (cut 0.001), identifying electrons and muons against pions, with EMC-based separation (electron deposit ≥0.8 GeV, muon <0.4 GeV) and the dilepton mass required within 30 MeV/c² of the J/ψ, while any photon-pair mass within 15 MeV/c² of the nominal π0 is vetoed to reject π0π0J/ψ. A 4C fit constraining the lepton pair plus four photons to the beam four-momentum is performed with χ²<200 (nominal, best combination by minimum χ²), followed by a 7C fit adding the J/ψ, χ_c1 and ψ_2(3823) mass constraints with χ²_7C<100 to resolve the photon assignment in the cascade. No signal is observed, and an upper limit R<0.075 at 90% CL is set on B(χ_c1(3872) → γ ψ_2(3823))/B(χ_c1(3872) → π+π−J/ψ).
