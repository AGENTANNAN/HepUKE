At √s = 4.178–4.230 GeV (three data-set groups: 4.178, 4.189–4.219, and 4.225–4.230 GeV), study e+e− → D_s*± D_s∓ with the tag D_s− → K+K−π− and the signal D_s+ → X e+ ν_e (inclusive semielectronic). Use real data and inclusive MC at the six energy points; generate 1M-event exclusive signal MC for D_s+ → X e+ ν_e with φ → K+K−. Event selection: reconstruct a single tag D_s− in K+K−π− (charm −1), and on the signal side require zero photons, exactly one positron with charge +1 and p > 200 MeV/c, plus a missing neutrino; perform a 4C kinematic fit with χ² < 200. Recoil-mass windows are [2057, 2177] MeV/c² at 4.178 GeV, [2145, 2190] MeV/c² at 4.189–4.219 GeV, and [2150, 2200] MeV/c² at 4.225–4.230 GeV. Determine the single-tag yield from an unbinned fit to the K+K−π− invariant mass (±40 MeV/c² around the D_s for 4.178 GeV, ±35 MeV/c² for other sets) with an MC-convolved Gaussian signal and second-order Chebyshev background, allowing multiple tag candidates. Bin the positron momentum in 18 bins of 50 MeV/c from 200 to 1100 MeV/c, apply 3×3 PID unfolding, right-sign minus wrong-sign subtraction, τ+ν_τ subtraction, and extrapolate below 200 MeV/c using six exclusive semielectronic modes; extract B(D_s+ → X e+ ν_e) = N_DT/(n_ST·b_tag) with tag bias ≈1.004–1.007.
