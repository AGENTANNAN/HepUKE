On the ψ(3770) at √s=3.773 GeV, measure absolute D-meson branching fractions with the single-tag method using the 2.93 fb^-1 real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for D0→K−π+, D0→π+π−, D+→K+π0, D+→K_S^0 π+, D0→K_S^0 π0, and D+→π+η, including the charge-conjugate and intermediate decays π0→γγ, η→γγ, and K_S^0→π+π−. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with topology-dependent multiplicities: two opposite-charge tracks and net charge zero for D0→K−π+; at least one track for D+→K+π0 and D+→π+η; at least three tracks for D+→K_S^0 π+; and at least two tracks for D0→K_S^0 π0. Photons are required to have EMC TDC 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV, endcap energy >50 MeV, and be at least 10° from any charged track, with at least two photons for π0 or η reconstruction from γγ. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating kaons from pions/protons and pions from kaons/protons; K_S^0 candidates are formed from π+π− by a secondary-vertex fit with flight significance L/σ>2 and |M(π+π−)−m_{K_S^0}|<12 MeV/c², while π0 and η are obtained from γγ constrained to the nominal π0 or η mass, requiring at least one such candidate, with χ²<25 and mass windows 0.115–0.150 GeV/c² for π0 and 0.515–0.575 GeV/c² for η. The tagged D is then fitted with a four-momentum constraint and χ²<200 for the final states K−π+, K+π0, K_S^0 plus a charged track, K_S^0 π0, and π+η, and mBC and ΔE (±3σ) cuts are applied at the ROOT level; cosmic/Bhabha background is rejected by a TOF time difference <5 ns, exclusion of e+e− and μ+μ− pairs, and requiring an extra EMC cluster >50 MeV or an extra MDC track.
