On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ η_c(2S)/γ χ_cJ with η_c(2S) or χ_cJ (J=0,1,2) → p pbar, and ψ(3686) → π0 h_c → γγ p pbar with h_c → p pbar. Use the 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data and matching inclusive MC, plus a 44 pb⁻¹ continuum sample at 3.65 GeV; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of γ η_c(2S), γ χ_c0, γ χ_c1, γ χ_c2, π0 h_c (all with p pbar in phase space) and for the irreducible ψ(3686) → π0 p pbar background. For the γ p pbar final state, require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with >25 MeV in both barrel and endcap, EMC timing in the 0–14 window, at least 10° from any charged track and at least 15° (25°) from the proton (antiproton), at least one photon; PID by the probability method with probability >0.001, identifying protons against kaons and pions, requiring exactly one p and one pbar; then perform a 4C kinematic fit to γ p pbar with χ²<40. For the π0 h_c final state, use the same track and photon criteria but at least two photons, require at least one proton by the same PID, and make a 4C fit to γγ p pbar with χ²<40 and 0.11 < M(γγ) < 0.15 GeV/c². Both selections are run on data, inclusive MC, continuum data, and the corresponding exclusive-MC samples; in the π0 h_c channel an additional M(p pbar γ_high) < 3.66 GeV/c² veto removes the γχ_cJ (J=1,2) background, while a second 3C fit with floating photon momentum is used for the γ p pbar signal-yield extraction.
