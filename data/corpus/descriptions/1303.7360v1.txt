On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γη′, with η′ → π+π−e+e−, π+π−μ+μ−, and γπ+π−, using the 2.253×10^8-event real J/ψ data sample and inclusive J/ψ MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the three η′ decay modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0; photons with EMC timing within 0–14, energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), at least 15° from any good track, and at least one photon for the e+e− and μ+μ− modes or at least two photons for the γπ+π− mode. For the lepton modes, PID uses a χ²-sum method with χ²_min<4 to identify each track as π or e (mode I) or π or μ (mode II), requiring the four tracks to form π+π−l+l−; the γπ+π− mode applies no PID. Perform a 4C kinematic fit with χ² cut 75: under the γπ+π−e+e− or γπ+π−μ+μ− hypothesis for modes I/II, keeping the best photon and track combination, plus a second 4C fit on the same final state to store the competing χ² used to reject J/ψ→γ2(π+π−); for mode III fit π+π−γγ with the same χ² cut and require M(γγ)>0.16 GeV/c² to veto π0. An η′ mass window |M(π+π−l+l−)−m(η′)|<0.02 GeV/c², with sidebands 0.88–0.90 and 1.02–1.04 GeV/c² for background estimates, is applied after the fit; in the γπ+π− mode the γπ+π− combination closest to the nominal η′ mass is chosen.
