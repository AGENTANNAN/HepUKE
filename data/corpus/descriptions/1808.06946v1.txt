On the J/ψ (√s = 3.097 GeV), study radiative J/ψ → γ K_S K_S with K_S → π+π−, using real data at 3.097 GeV and inclusive MC, together with a 1,000,000-event exclusive signal MC sample of the same decay chain. Event selection requires exactly four charged tracks, two positive and two negative, with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, and at least one photon with energy above 25 MeV in the barrel or 50 MeV in the endcap; no particle identification is applied, and all charged tracks are assumed to be pions. The two K_S candidates are formed by fitting each π+π− pair to a common secondary vertex, minimizing the mass difference and removing used tracks, with each K_S required to have L/σ_L > 0 and the combined √((L1/σ1)^2 + (L2/σ2)^2) > 2.2. A 6C kinematic fit to γ K_S K_S is then performed, imposing four-momentum conservation plus one mass constraint for each K_S, with χ² < 60 and flagged nominal. Backgrounds from inclusive MC (~0.5%) and continuum (~0.7%) are negligible and are ignored in the amplitude analysis.
