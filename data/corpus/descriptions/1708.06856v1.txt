At BESIII, using 2.93 fb−1 of ψ(3770) data at √s=3.773 GeV (the 712_3773 real data and inclusive MC), search for D+ → D0 e+νe with a double-tag technique: reconstruct the D− tag in the six standard modes K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S^0π−, K_S^0π−π0, K_S^0π+π−π−, and K+K−π−, and reconstruct the D0 on the signal side, while the soft positron (p<5 MeV/c) is not reconstructed and is merged with the neutrino into one massless missing four-vector. Generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of D0 → K−π+, D0 → K−π+π+π−, and D0 → K−π+π0. Require signal-side net charge zero and a 10° minimum photon angle; for D0 → K−π+π0 require exactly two photons with energy above 25 MeV, diphoton mass in 0.110–0.155 GeV/c², and reject events where both photons are in the endcap. Kinematic fits constrain four-momentum and the D0 mass for K−π+ and K−π+π+π−, and additionally the π0 and D0 masses for K−π+π0, all with χ²<200; π0 candidates also pass a separate 1C fit with χ²<20. Tag candidates require MBC>1.83 GeV/c² and mode-dependent ~3σ ΔE windows, choosing the smallest |ΔE| per mode, and signal optimization probability cuts of >0.37, >0.34, and >0.54 for the three D0 modes respectively, implemented in the downstream ROOT stage. PID is not separately specified—particle identities are fixed by the tag-mode definitions and signal charged multiplicities—and no explicit track |cosθ|, |Vz|, or Vr cuts are encoded.
