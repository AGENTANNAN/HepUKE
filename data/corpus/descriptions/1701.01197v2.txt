On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ (J=0,1,2) → γγ J/ψ and ψ(3686) → γ η_c(2S) → γγ J/ψ, with J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate exclusive MC samples: 1,000,000 events for γχ_cJ → γγ J/ψ and 500,000 events each for γη_c(2S) → γγ J/ψ, ψ(3686) → π0 J/ψ, ψ(3686) → π0π0 J/ψ, and nonresonant ψ(3686) → γγ J/ψ, all with J/ψ → e+e−/μ+μ−. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, p>1.0 GeV/c, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge zero; photons with EMC TDC 0–14, at least 10° from any charged track, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25 MeV, and 2–4 photons. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut; tracks above 1.0 GeV/c are treated as leptons, identified as electrons if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise as muons, requiring at least one positive and one negative lepton. A 4C kinematic fit to the ψ(3686) four-momentum is performed on γγ l+l−, taking the two-photon combination with smallest χ², with χ²<60 and flagged nominal; the J/ψ window is 3.08–3.12 GeV/c² for M(l+l−), and γγ masses in 0.11–0.15 GeV/c² (π0) and 0.51–0.57 GeV/c² (η) are vetoed. For the e+e− mode, radiative Bhabha suppression requires cosθ(e+)<0.3 and cosθ(e−)>−0.3.
