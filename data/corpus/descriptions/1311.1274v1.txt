Search for baryonic decays of ψ(3770) and ψ(4040) in seven final states: ΛΛ̄π+π−, ΛΛ̄π0, ΛΛ̄η, Σ+Σ̄−, Σ0Σ̄0, Ξ−Ξ̄+, and Ξ0Ξ̄0. Use 2.9 fb−1 of ψ(3770) data at 3.773 GeV, 482 pb−1 at 4.009 GeV, and 67 pb−1 of continuum data at 3.542–3.650 GeV; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the seven modes at ψ(3770), plus extra 100k-event ψ(4040) samples for ΛΛ̄π+π−, Σ+Σ̄−, and Ξ−Ξ̄+ (decay chains: Λ→pπ−, Λ̄→p̄π+, π0/η→γγ, Σ+→pπ0, Σ̄−→p̄π0, Σ0→Λγ, Ξ−→Λπ−, Ξ̄+→Λ̄π+, Ξ0→Λπ0, Ξ̄0→Λ̄π0). All charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge 0, and multiplicity (2+,2−) for ΛΛ̄π+π−, ΛΛ̄π0, ΛΛ̄η, Σ0Σ̄0, Ξ0Ξ̄0, (1+,1−) for Σ+Σ̄−, or (3+,3−) for Ξ−Ξ̄+. Where photons are used, require EMC TDC 0–14, angle to charged tracks >10°, barrel/endcap energy thresholds 25/50 MeV, and nγ≥2 for ΛΛ̄π0, ΛΛ̄η, Σ0Σ̄0 or nγ≥4 for Σ+Σ̄− and Ξ0Ξ̄0. Proton identification uses the probability method with prob>0.001 against kaons/pions, requiring at least one proton and one antiproton (exactly one each for Σ+Σ̄−). Λ→pπ− and Λ̄→p̄π+ are reconstructed by secondary-vertex fits minimizing the mass difference in all modes except Σ+Σ̄−; π0/η→γγ candidates from 1C Kalman mass-constrained fits have χ²<25, with ≥1 π0/η for ΛΛ̄π0/ΛΛ̄η and ≥2 π0 for Σ+Σ̄− and Ξ0Ξ̄0. Final nominal 4C fits with χ²<200 are performed for [ΛΛ̄π+π−], [ΛΛ̄π0], [ΛΛ̄η], [pp̄π0π0], [ΛΛ̄γγ], [ΛΛ̄π+π−π−π+], and [ΛΛ̄π0π0]; Λ/Λ̄ mass windows 1.111–1.121 GeV are imposed in the Σ0Σ̄0, Ξ−Ξ̄+, and Ξ0Ξ̄0 fits, and for Ξ−Ξ̄+ the best Ξ assignment is the smallest-χ² combination. Run each selection on the ψ(3770), ψ(4040), and continuum data sets, using the continuum for subtraction.
