Measure the ISR cross section for e+e− → pp̄ with an undetected photon at √s = 3.773, 4.008, 4.226, 4.258, 4.358, 4.416, and 4.600 GeV. Use the real-data samples at these seven energies and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each, with ψ(4260) → pp̄γ phase-space decays representing e+e− → pp̄γ_ISR. Event selection requires exactly one positive and one negative charged track with net charge zero, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; no explicit photon is required. Protons are identified by the probability method (prob>0.001) against kaons and pions, requiring one proton and one antiproton, and e+ background is suppressed by E_EMC/p_rec<0.5 for the positive track. The missing momentum polar angle must be <0.125 rad or >π−0.125 rad, and the missing mass squared must lie in [−0.1,0.2] GeV²/c⁴ for √s>4 GeV or [−0.02,0.10] GeV²/c⁴ at 3.773 GeV; the proton and antiproton must also satisfy |cosθ_{p,pp}^CM|<0.75. No kinematic fit is used. Backgrounds from pp̄π⁰ and two-photon processes are subtracted via sidebands, J/ψ→pp̄ and ψ(3686)→pp̄ resonances are fitted and subtracted, and NLO Phokhara radiative corrections are applied.
