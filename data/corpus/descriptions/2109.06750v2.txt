On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → ω K_S^0 K_S^0 with ω → π+π−π0, K_S^0 → π+π−, and π0 → γγ. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500,000 exclusive MC events for ψ(3686) → ω K_S^0 K_S^0 with ω → π+π−π0 (Dalitz), K_S^0 → π+π−, and π0 → γγ. Event selection requires at least three positive and three negative charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and net charge zero; at least two photons with EMC timing 0–14, angle to the nearest track >10°, and energy >25 MeV (barrel) or >50 MeV (endcap). Pion identification uses the probability method with prob>0.001, separating pions from kaons and protons, and requires at least three π+ and three π−. A π0 is reconstructed from a photon pair via a kinematic fit constraining the γγ invariant mass to the π0 nominal mass, χ²<25, within the π0 mass window (0.115,0.150) GeV/c², requiring at least one π0; two distinct K_S^0 → π+π− candidates are formed by secondary vertex fits minimizing the mass difference, with K_S^0 mass window (0.486,0.510) GeV/c² (3σ) and decay length >2σ from the IP. A final 4C kinematic fit is applied to the π+π− K_S^0 K_S^0 π0 system with four-momentum conservation (nominal), χ²<200, and the ω mass window (0.777,0.807) GeV/c² is used; combinatorial background is subtracted with a 2D K_S^0 K_S^0 sideband method, and continuum contributions from 3.65 GeV data (43.88 pb⁻¹) are subtracted with a 1/s scaling. Signal MC is modeled with a BODY3 data-driven generator using the efficiency-corrected Dalitz plot of M(ω K_S^0) vs M(ω K_S^0).
