On 37 c.m. energy points from 4.190 to 4.946 GeV (17.4 fb⁻¹ total), measure e⁺e⁻ → π⁺π⁻ D⁺D⁻ cross sections using the partial-reconstruction method: D⁺ → K⁻π⁺π⁺ is the tag, and D⁻ is inferred from the recoil mass of the D⁺π⁺π⁻ system. Use all real data at these energies plus inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive phase-space MC events for e⁺e⁻ → π⁺π⁻D⁺D⁻ with D⁺ → K⁻π⁺π⁺ and D⁻ → K⁺π⁻π⁻. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; PID by the probability method (prob>0.001) with kaon identified against pion and pion against kaon, assigning one K⁻, two π⁺ and two recoil pions. Reconstruct D⁺ from K⁻π⁺π⁺ with a secondary vertex fit and |M−M(D⁺)|<11 MeV/c², then identify D⁻ through the recoil mass of D⁺π⁺π⁻ with a ~9 MeV/c² window; tighter vertex cuts (Vr<0.55 cm, |Vz|<3 cm) and proton/antiproton vetoes suppress Λ_c background. Apply a D⁺-mass-constrained kinematic fit, and veto D⁰ and K_S⁰ by mass and secondary-vertex criteria. Cross sections and the Y(4390) and 4.706 GeV resonance structures are extracted from recoil-mass fits in signal and sideband regions.
