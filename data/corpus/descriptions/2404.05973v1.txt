On the ψ(4260) at 4.178 GeV, study e+e− → D_s*+ D_s− with D_s*+ → γ D_s+, D_s+ → π+ φ, φ → e+e− and the tag side D_s− → K+K−π−. Use the 4.18 GeV energy-point real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC of the full D_s*+ D_s− decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and one negative track; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, at least 10° from any track, at least one required. PID by the probability method, treating high-momentum tracks (p>1.0) as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6), with π/K separation against each other and protons; electrons must satisfy L'(e)>0.001, L'(e)/(L'(e)+L'(π)+L'(K))>0.8 and E/p>0.8 (0.7) for p above (below) 0.4 GeV/c. Veto γ→e+e− conversions by discarding e+e− pairs whose vertex lies 2.0–8.0 cm from the IP, with photon recovery in a 5° cone around the electron tracks. Require the D_s+ candidate mass in [1.88, 2.02] GeV/c² and the e+e− mass in [0.98, 1.04] GeV/c² (φ window), then select signal in the M_rec vs ΔM plane with windows optimized per energy point by S/√(S+B); when multiple photons are present, pick the D_s*+ → γ D_s+ transition photon whose D_s+γ recoil mass is closest to m_D_s*+.
