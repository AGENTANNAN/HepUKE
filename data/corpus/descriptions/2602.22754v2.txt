Using 6.4 fb⁻¹ of e⁺e⁻ data taken at 13 XYZ energy points from 4.600 to 4.950 GeV, measure the branching fractions of Λ_c⁺ → Σ⁰ K_S⁰ π⁺ and Λ_c⁺ → Σ⁰ K_S⁰ K⁺, with Σ⁰ → γΛ, Λ → pπ⁻, K_S⁰ → π⁺π⁻, reconstructing the Λ_c⁺ as a single tag while constraining the recoil Λ̄_c⁻. Use the matching inclusive MC at each energy point and generate 200k-event exclusive signal MC per mode per point with a phase-space decay card and the default production cross section. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; photons with E>25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), TDC within [0,700] ns, angle to the nearest track >10°, and at least one photon. PID uses the probability method with probability >0.001, separating p, K, and π against the other hypotheses, requiring ≥1 p, ≥1 π⁺, ≥1 π⁻, and for the K⁺ mode ≥1 K⁺. Reconstruct Λ and K_S⁰ through secondary-vertex fits minimizing the mass difference, with mass windows M(pπ⁻) ∈ [1.111,1.121] GeV/c² and M(π⁺π⁻) ∈ [0.487,0.511] GeV/c², allowing their tracks |Vz|<20 cm and requiring vertex χ²<100. Form Σ⁰ from γΛ with a Kalman fit constraining M(γΛ) to the Σ⁰ nominal mass (χ²<200), then perform a 4C kinematic fit of the Σ⁰ K_S⁰ π⁺ (or Σ⁰ K_S⁰ K⁺) final state, constraining the total four-momentum to √s, the intermediate masses to their nominal values, and the missing Λ̄_c⁻ recoil mass to the Λ̄_c⁻ nominal mass, with χ²<200 at reconstruction and tighter optimized cuts χ²_4C<29 (π⁺ mode) and χ²_4C<171 (K⁺ mode) applied in the M_BC signal window [2.282,2.291] GeV/c².
