Study the singly Cabibbo-suppressed decay Λc+ → p K+ K− (charge conjugate included) in e+e− annihilation at CM energies 4600, 4628, 4641, 4661, 4682 and 4698 MeV, reconstructing a single Λc+ tag directly in the pK+K− final state. Use the real data and inclusive MC at all six energy points; generate 1,000,000-event phase-space exclusive MC per point, with ψ(4260) → Λc+ anti-Λc−, Λc+ → p K+ K− and the charge-conjugate decay. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm and Vr < 1 cm, with at least two positive and one negative track. Particle identification uses the probability method with zero probability cut, separating proton against kaon/pion, kaon against pion/proton and pion against kaon/proton, and requires at least one proton, one K+ and one K−. A tighter proton vertex cut |Vr| < 0.2 cm is also applied. The pK+K− system is subjected to a kinematic fit with a four-momentum constraint to the e+e− CM, χ² < 200; loose preselection is M_BC > 2.25 GeV/c² and |ΔE| < 0.1 GeV, keeping the candidate with the smallest |ΔE| among multiple combinations, followed by final windows of |ΔE| < 0.005 GeV for the amplitude analysis or |ΔE| < 0.02 GeV for the branching-fraction fit. The same selection is applied to data, inclusive MC and exclusive signal MC.
