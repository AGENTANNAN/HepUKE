Using 2712×10⁶ ψ(3686) events at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → π⁰ h_c with h_c → π⁺π⁻π⁰, K⁺K⁻π⁰, K⁺K⁻η, and π⁺π⁻η, where the bachelor π⁰ and the signal π⁰ or η decay to γγ. Analyse the real ψ(3686) data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the four h_c decay modes. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two tracks with one positive and one negative charge; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–14, at least 10° from any track, and at least four photons. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut: require at least one π⁺ and one π⁻ (against K/proton) for the π⁺π⁻π⁰ and π⁺π⁻η modes, and at least one K⁺ and one K⁻ (against π/proton) for the K⁺K⁻π⁰ and K⁺K⁻η modes. Reconstruct π⁰ → γγ and η → γγ with mass-constrained Kalman fits (χ²<200), requiring at least two π⁰ candidates in the two π⁰ modes and at least one π⁰ plus one η in the η modes. Perform a nominal 6C kinematic fit imposing four-momentum conservation plus the bachelor π⁰ and signal π⁰/η masses, with χ²<200 (paper cuts <30, <50, <35, <24 for modes I–IV), and also fit the competing one-π⁰ (fewer-photon) hypotheses for photon-count vetoes. Additional ROOT-level background vetoes use recoil-mass windows against ψ(3686)→π⁰π⁰J/ψ or ηJ/ψ, resonance vetoes (ω, f₀(980), K*(892)), and a muon penetration-depth cut for J/ψ→μ⁺μ⁻.
