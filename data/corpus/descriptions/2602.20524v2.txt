At the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study e+e− → J/ψ → Ξ− Ξ̄+, with Ξ− → Λ π−, Λ → p π− and the charge-conjugate chain. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 2M phase-space exclusive MC for the signal decay chain to determine the normalisation factor in the maximum-likelihood fit. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100, Vr<10, at least three positive and three negative tracks; PID by the probability method (cut 0.001), proton identified against kaon/pion and pion against kaon/proton, requiring ≥1 p, ≥1 p̄, ≥2 π+, ≥2 π−, with protons above and pions below 0.3 GeV/c momentum. Reconstruct Λ → p π− and Λ̄ → p̄ π+ and then Ξ− → Λ π− and Ξ̄+ → Λ̄ π+ through secondary-vertex fits minimising the mass difference, removing used tracks and applying a per-Ξ cut δ<0.016 GeV/c², where δ = sqrt((m_{Λπ−}−m_Ξ−)² + R²(m_{pπ−}−m_Λ)²) with R = σ_Ξ−/σ_Λ, and rejecting candidates with negative decay length. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to the Ξ− Ξ̄+ system with χ² cut 200, nominal. A two-dimensional sideband in m_{Λπ−} versus m_{Λ̄π+} (1.274–1.306 and 1.338–1.370 GeV/c²) is used for background subtraction.
