On the J/ψ peak (3.097 GeV), search for J/ψ weak decays to a D meson plus a light hadron using the 10,087 million J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of five modes: anti-D0 π0, anti-D0 η, anti-D0 ρ0, D− π+, and D− ρ+, with anti-D0→K+ e− ν̄_e, D−→K_S0 e− ν̄_e, π0→γγ, η→γγ, ρ0→π+π−, ρ+→π+π0 (π0→γγ), and K_S0→π+π−, including charge conjugates. Common selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, and Vr<1 cm; photons with TDC 0–14, angle to nearest track >10°, energy >25 MeV in the barrel or >50 MeV in the endcap; and probability-based PID with CL>0.001, where tracks with p>1.0 are treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.8 GeV and CL_e>CL_π, CL_e>CL_K, else muon). Modes anti-D0 π0 and anti-D0 η require two tracks with net charge 0, at least two photons, one K+ and one e−, then a γγ mass-constrained fit to π0/η (χ²<20, ≥1 π0/η) and a nominal kinematic fit to K+ e− π0/η with a missing neutrino and χ²<200; U_miss = E_miss − c|p_miss| windows are (−0.083,0.119) GeV for π0 and (−0.050,0.060) GeV for η, the anti-D0 recoil mass is 1.80–1.95 GeV/c², and the η mass window is 0.50–0.57 GeV/c². Mode anti-D0 ρ0 requires four tracks with net charge 0, one K+, one e−, one π+, and one π−, and a nominal kinematic fit to K+ e− π+π− with a missing neutrino and χ²<200; U_miss is (−0.040,0.050) GeV, the ρ0 mass window is 0.62–0.95 GeV/c², and the anti-D0 recoil mass is 1.80–1.95 GeV/c². Mode D− π+ requires four tracks with net charge 0, one e−, at least three π+, and at least one π−, reconstructs K_S0 from a π+π− secondary vertex with |M(π+π−)−m_K_S0|<12 MeV/c² and decay length >2σ, then performs a nominal kinematic fit to K_S0 e− π+ with a missing neutrino and χ²<200; U_miss is (−0.037,0.040) GeV and the D− recoil mass is 1.80–1.95 GeV/c². Mode D− ρ+ uses the same four-track, one e−, ≥3 π+, ≥1 π−, and K_S0 requirements, requires at least two photons and ≥1 π0 from a γγ mass-constrained fit (χ²<20), then performs a nominal kinematic fit to K_S0 e− π+ π0 with a missing neutrino and χ²<200; U_miss is (−0.058,0.074) GeV, the ρ+ mass window is 0.62–0.95 GeV/c², and the D− recoil mass is 1.80–1.95 GeV/c².
