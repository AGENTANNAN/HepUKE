At the ψ(3770) (√s = 3.773 GeV), use the 20.3 fb⁻¹ BESIII data and inclusive MC to study four semileptonic D decays via the double-tag technique: D⁰ → π⁻ e⁺ ν_e, D⁰ → π⁻ μ⁺ ν_μ, D⁺ → π⁰ e⁺ ν_e, and D⁺ → π⁰ μ⁺ ν_μ. For each mode generate 500k exclusive MC events, with the tag side decaying hadronically (D̄⁰ → K⁺π⁻ for the neutral channels, D⁻ → K⁺π⁻π⁻ for the charged channels) and the signal side using PHOTOS/ISGW2, including π⁰ → γγ where relevant. On the tag side reconstruct the charm −1 D meson through the modes D⁰→Kπ, Kππ⁰, Kπππ, Kππ⁰π⁰, Kππππ⁰, or D⁻→Kππ, K_Sπ, Kπππ⁰, K_Sππ⁰, K_Sπππ, KKπ. On the signal side require one π⁻ plus one e⁺ or μ⁺ with net charge 0 for the D⁰ modes, or one e⁺ or μ⁺ plus two photons with net charge +1 for the D⁺ modes, always leaving a missing neutrino. Perform a kinematic fit constraining four-momentum—and for the D⁺ modes additionally constraining the γγ invariant mass to the nominal π⁰ mass—with χ² < 200.
