At √s ≈ 2.125 GeV (the 713_Rscan_2125 point, 2.12655 GeV), measure the integrated luminosity and the c.m. energy from e+e− → (γ)e+e− (large-angle Bhabha), e+e− → γγ (cross-check) and e+e− → (γ)μ+μ−. Use that real data point with its inclusive MC, and generate 1M-event exclusive MC for each of the Bhabha, γγ, μ+μ− and generic u+u− modes (the ISR/FSR photon via PHOTOS). Bhabha selection: |cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track with net charge 0, each track's EMC energy above 0.69 GeV (0.65 E_beam), then a 4C fit of the e+e− pair with χ²<200, nominal. γγ selection: no charged tracks, exactly two photons with TDC 0–14, 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy thresholds, each cluster |cosθ|<0.8 (and, ROOT-level, 0.7–1.15 E_beam and back-to-back |Δφ|<2.5°), followed by a 4C fit of the two photons with χ²<200. μ+μ− selection: the same |cosθ|<0.8, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and one-positive/one-negative net-zero track requirement, Bhabha-like tracks with E/p>0.4 vetoed, PID by the probability method with high-momentum (p>1.0) tracks treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV), requiring one μ+ and one μ− and a 4C fit of the μ+μ− pair with χ²<200. For all channels the collinearity/cosmic vetoes (|Δθ|, |Δφ|, and |Δt|<1.5 ns for the dimuon) and, for the dimuon, the per-run M(μ+μ−) fit in [2.0, 2.2] GeV/c² that extracts the c.m. energy are applied at ROOT level after the kinematic fit.
