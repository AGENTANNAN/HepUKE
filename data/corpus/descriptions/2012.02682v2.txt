Using the 14 BOSS 703 data sets collected between 4.178 and 4.600 GeV (15 energy points, including the combined 4.236+4.244 GeV sample) and the corresponding inclusive MC, search for e+e− → χcJ π+π− (J=0,1,2) with χcJ → γ J/ψ and J/ψ → ℓ+ℓ− (ℓ=e,μ). Generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each χcJ mode at every energy point, using χcJ → γ J/ψ followed by J/ψ → e+e−, with the μ+μ− final state treated as a competing hypothesis and the two lepton channels combined. Require four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; require at least one photon with barrel E>25 MeV (|cosθ|<0.80) or endcap E>50 MeV (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), EMC timing within 700 ns, and >20° from the nearest charged track. Particle ID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, identifying exactly one electron and one positron against pion/kaon/proton, while pion and lepton candidates are separated by momentum (pion p<1.0 GeV/c, lepton p>1.0 GeV/c) and e/μ separation uses E_EMC/P_MDC >0.7c for electron and <0.3c for muon. Perform a 5C kinematic fit (four-momentum plus J/ψ mass constraint) on π+π−e+e−γ with χ²<50, nominal, then refit the competing π+π−μ+μ−γ hypothesis with the same J/ψ mass constraint and choose the best candidate by lowest χ²_5C. The three χcJ states are separated at the ROOT level by the photon energy in the π+π− recoil frame, with additional background vetoes including cos(α_π+π−)<0.98, η/η' J/ψ, ω, and ψ(2S)/X(3872) recoil-mass windows.
