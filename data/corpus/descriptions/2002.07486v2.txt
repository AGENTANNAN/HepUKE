At √s = 3.097 GeV (J/ψ), study J/ψ → γ π0 η′ with η′ → π+π− η, η → γγ, π0 → γγ, sharing one selection chain with two companion sub-analyses: J/ψ → ω η′ (ω → γ π0) for the ω η′ branching-fraction measurement and J/ψ → γ η_c (η_c → π0 η′) for an η_c CP-violation search. Use the 1310.6-million-event J/ψ real data sample and inclusive MC (BOSS 708), and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the three signal modes (γ π0 η′, ω η′, γ η_c → π0 η′). Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track, and net charge zero; photons with EMC TDC 0–14, 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, more than 10° from any track, and at least five photons; and PID by the probability method (prob cut 0.001) with π/K separation, requiring at least one π+ and one π−. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to π+π−5γ (four-momentum constrained, χ²<200), accepting only events whose χ² is smaller than that of the competing π+π−6γ hypothesis; then reconstruct π0 and η from γγ pairs via mass-constrained Kalman fits (χ²<200 each, at least one π0 and one η), followed by a 5C fit to π+π− γγγ η with the η mass constrained (χ²<30). Apply mass windows |M(γγ)−m_π0|<15 MeV and |M(π+π−η)−m_η′|<15 MeV, veto M(radiative γ + η photon) in [0.115, 0.155] GeV, and for the dark-photon U′ → γπ0 search scan M(γπ0) from 0.2 to 2.1 GeV in 10 MeV steps while excluding the ω region 0.75–0.82 GeV.
