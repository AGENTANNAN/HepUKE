Study e+e− → K_S0 D_s+ D*− and e+e− → K_S0 D_s*+ D− at five energy points (4.628, 4.641, 4.661, 4.682 and 4.699 GeV, 3.8 fb−1 total), searching for a neutral near-threshold structure in the K_S0 recoil-mass spectrum; use the real data and inclusive MC at all five points, plus 500k-event exclusive MC generated at 4.682 GeV for each channel, with D*− → D̄0π−, D_s*+ → D_s+γ, D_s+ → K+K−π+, D− → K+π−π− and K_S0 → π+π−. Charged tracks are required to satisfy |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, with at least four tracks in total; photons must have EMC timing between 0 and 14, lie at least 10° from any track, and pass a 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, with no minimum photon multiplicity. Particle identification uses the χ²_sum method, identifying K+, K−, π+ and π− with χ²_min > 0.001. Two partial-reconstruction tag methods are applied: a D_s+ tag reconstructs the bachelor K_S0 plus a D_s+ (from K+K−π+, K_S0K+, K+K−π+π0, K_S0K+π+π− or η'π+, with η' → π+π−η, η → γγ) while leaving the D*− undetected, and a D− tag reconstructs the bachelor K_S0 plus a D− (from K+π−π−, K_S0π− or K_S0π+π−π−) while leaving the D_s*+ undetected. K_S0 → π+π− candidates require a vertex-fit χ² < 100, a second IP-pointing vertex fit with χ² < 40, decay length greater than 2σ and |M(π+π−) − m_K_S0| < 11 MeV, and π0/η → γγ candidates must pass a 1C kinematic fit constraining the two-photon mass with χ² < 10. D_s+/D− candidates are kept within 15 MeV of the known mass (best candidate chosen by closest mass) with the resonance vetoes M(K+K−) < 1.05 GeV (φ), |M(K+π−) − m_K*(892)| < 70 MeV and |M(π−π0) − m_ρ| < 150 MeV, plus a K_S0 veto for D− → K_S0π+π−π−; the signal is selected in the recoil-mass windows |RQ(K_S0 D_s+) − m_D*−| < 20 MeV and |RQ(K_S0 D−) − m_D_s*+| < 10 MeV, where RQ = RM + M(D) − m(D), with D_s+ sidebands (1.895, 1.935) and (1.995, 2.035) GeV and D− sidebands (1.800, 1.840) and (1.900, 1.940) GeV. The Z_cs(3985)^0 signal is finally extracted by a simultaneous unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to the K_S0 recoil mass across all five energy points using S-wave Breit-Wigner shapes.
