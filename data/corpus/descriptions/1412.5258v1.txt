On the J/ψ at √s = 3.097 GeV, study the final state ηφπ+π− with η→γγ and φ→K+K−, targeting J/ψ→ηY(2175), Y(2175)→φf0(980), f0(980)→π+π−, and also the same-final-state channels J/ψ→φf1(1285)→φηπ+π− and J/ψ→φη(1405)→φηπ+π−. Use the J/ψ real data sample 708_3097 and its inclusive MC for background, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC samples for each of the three modes, all with η→γγ and φ→K+K−; the nominal Y(2175) signal is generated with P-wave η–Y(2175), S-wave φ–f0(980) and ππ, and a Flatté f0(980) lineshape using BESII parameters. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<2 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, and zero net charge. Photons are required to be at least two, with barrel energy >25 MeV (|cosθ|<0.80), endcap energy >50 MeV (0.86<|cosθ|<0.92), more than 10° from the nearest charged track, and EMC timing within 0–700 ns. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 confidence cut, assigns each track to the π/K/p hypothesis with the highest confidence, and requires 1 K+, 1 K−, 1 π+, and 1 π−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to the K+K−π+π−γγ hypothesis, trying all photon-pair combinations and retaining the smallest χ², with χ²<200, and after the fit the η and φ signal windows are 0.528<M(γγ)<0.566 GeV/c² and 1.006<M(K+K−)<1.032 GeV/c², corresponding to |M(γγ)−M_η|<0.019 and |M(K+K−)−M_φ|<0.013, while the f0(980) region 0.90<M(π+π−)<1.05 GeV/c² is used in the fit.
