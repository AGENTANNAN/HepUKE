At √s = 3.097 GeV (J/ψ), search for Ξ⁰n → ΛΛX from Ξ⁰ interacting with the beam-pipe neutron, using J/ψ → Ξ⁰ Ξ̄⁰ with the tag side Ξ̄⁰ → Λ̄ π⁰ (Λ̄ → p̄ π⁺, π⁰ → γγ) and the signal side Ξ⁰ → Λ Λ (each Λ → p π⁻). Use the full J/ψ real data set (10087×10⁶ events) and inclusive MC at 3.097 GeV, plus 2.25M exclusive MC events of J/ψ → Ξ⁰ Ξ̄⁰ → (Λ̄ π⁰)(Λ Λ) followed by Λ̄ → p̄ π⁺, Λ → p π⁻ and π⁰ → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, exactly three positive and three negative tracks; photons with E > 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), EMC time 0–700 ns, angle to nearest track > 10°, at least two photons. PID by the probability method with prob cut 0.001, identifying protons (largest likelihood versus K, π) and pions (largest likelihood versus K, p), requiring 2 p, 1 p̄, 1 π⁺ and 2 π⁻. Reconstruct the tag Λ̄ from p̄π⁺ by a secondary-vertex fit minimizing the mass difference (used tracks removed), then a Kalman 1C fit of γγ to the π⁰ mass with χ² < 25 and at least one π⁰; form Λ candidates from all remaining pπ⁻ pairs by secondary-vertex fit. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to J/ψ → Λ̄ π⁰ Λ Λ with χ² < 200, then select the Ξ̄⁰ closest in mass to nominal (window [−15, +10] MeV), require recoil mass against Ξ̄⁰ in [1.295, 1.325] GeV, |M(pπ⁻) − m_Λ| < 3 MeV for the Λ pair, and a ΛΛ vertex Rxy in [2.9, 3.6] cm.
