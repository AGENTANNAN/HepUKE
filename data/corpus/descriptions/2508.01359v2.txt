Study e+e− → Ω− Ω+ Born cross sections with single-baryon tagging, reconstructing either Ω− → K−Λ, Λ → p+π− or the charge-conjugate Ω+ → K+ anti-Λ, anti-Λ → anti-p π+. Use the 39 listed real-data scan points from 3.81 to 4.78 GeV with matching inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each tag mode from ψ(4260) → Ω− Ω+ with the corresponding Ω decay. For the Ω− tag require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, one positive and two negative tracks; use probability-based particle identification with a 0.001 cut, requiring one proton, one K−, and one π−. Reconstruct a Λ from the p+ and π− through a secondary-vertex fit that minimizes the mass difference and removes the used tracks, then select the best Ω− candidate by mass at 1.67245 GeV and require its recoil mass to be 1.64–1.70 GeV. The Ω+ tag is charge conjugate: two positive and one negative track, one anti-proton, one K+, one π+, an anti-Λ secondary vertex, and the same best-mass and recoil-mass criteria. The final signal extraction uses a two-dimensional sideband method in corrected masses (M_cor, RM_cor), with signal region M_cor(Ω) ∈ [1.6635,1.6815] GeV and RM_cor ∈ [1.64,1.70] GeV and four equal-area sidebands; N_sig = N_obs − N_bkg/4 is applied later in ROOT. No photon selection is applied, and no global kinematic fit is used; the algorithm constant ECMS is 4.260 GeV.
