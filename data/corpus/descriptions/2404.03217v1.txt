On ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → π0 h_c with h_c → K_S0 K+ π−, K_S0 → π+π− and π0 → γγ (charge conjugate implied), using the 2.712×10^9-event real data and inclusive MC at this energy; generate one million exclusive MC events for this decay chain. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, with at least two positive and at least two negative tracks, while photons must have EMC time in [0,700] ns, energy above 25 MeV (barrel) or 50 MeV (endcap), lie more than 10° from any charged track, and number at least two. Particle identification uses the probability method (cut 0.001), separating kaons from pions/protons and pions from kaons/protons, and requires at least one K+ and at least two π−. A K_S0 is reconstructed from a π+π− secondary vertex fit that minimizes the mass difference (with the pair removed from the track list), keeping candidates in the (0.487, 0.511) GeV/c² mass window with L/ΔL>2. The two photons are then subjected to a Kalman fit constraining their invariant mass to the nominal π0 mass (χ²<25, at least one π0), followed by a 5C kinematic fit (4C energy-momentum plus 1C π0 mass) on K_S0 K+ π− π0 with χ²<200 and nominal flag, selecting the candidate with the smallest χ²; the π0 mass window (0.12, 0.15) GeV/c² is applied afterwards in ROOT, and the signal yield is extracted from an unbinned ML fit to M(K_S0 K+ π−) with peaking background from ψ(3686) → γχ_c2 → K_S0 K+ π− plus a fake photon and a smooth ARGUS background.
