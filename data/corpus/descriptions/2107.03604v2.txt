Study e+e− → γ χ_c1,2 with χ_c1 → γ J/ψ and J/ψ → e+e− (Mode I) or μ+μ− (Mode II), using the full scan of real data and inclusive MC from 3.773 to 4.600 GeV, plus 100k-event exclusive signal MC generated for each of the two J/ψ decay modes with the appropriate decay cards. Both modes share the same charged-track selection: |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track with net charge zero, dropping all tracks below 1.0 GeV; photons must satisfy 0–14 TDC, barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, at least 10° separation from any track, and at least two photons are required. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, treating tracks above 1.0 GeV momentum as leptons (electron if EMC energy exceeds 1.0 GeV in Mode I or 0.4 GeV in Mode II), selecting at least one e+ and one e− (Mode I) or one μ+ and one μ− (Mode II). A 4C kinematic fit on γ γ l+l− with χ²<40 is applied and the J/ψ is required in the [3.08, 3.12] GeV/c² mass window. Mode I additionally vetoes radiative Bhabha events (cos θ_{eγ}<0.86 and |cos θ_γ|<0.8 for both photons) and η contamination (|M(γγ)−m_η|>0.03 GeV/c²), while Mode II suppresses π+π−π0 background with |M(γγ)−m_π0|>0.015 GeV/c² and |cos θ_γ|<0.8. The χ_c1,2 signal is finally extracted from the M(γ J/ψ) distribution.
