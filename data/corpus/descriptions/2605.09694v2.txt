On the 4.178 GeV Ds*Ds data point (7.33 fb⁻¹ over 4.128–4.226 GeV) and its inclusive MC, study the double-tag decays Ds+ → K_S0 K_S0 π+ π0 and Ds+ → K_S0 K+ π0 π0 (K_S0 → π+π−, π0 → γγ), with the tag Ds− reconstructed in 16 hadronic modes (charge conjugate, charm −1) applying per-mode M_BC and M_tag windows and, for the Ds− → π−π+π− tag, vetoing π+π− pairs with mass in 0.468–0.528 GeV. Generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes. The signal side requires net charge +1: mode I takes three π+ and two π− with at least three photons, mode II takes one K+, one π+, and one π− with at least five photons, with photons required to have opening angle >10° to the nearest track, barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, and TDC in [0,700] ns. K_S0 candidates are formed from π+π− with |M(π+π−) − m_Ks| in 0.486–0.510 GeV and flight distance >2σ (retaining the longest-lived candidate(s)), while π0 candidates from γγ must have mass in 0.115–0.150 GeV and pass a 1C fit (for mode II keeping the two smallest χ²_1C); pions not originating from K_S0/η/η′ are vetoed if p < 0.1 GeV/c, and the transition photon or π0 from Ds*+ is the unused candidate minimizing |ΔE|. A 4C kinematic fit is then performed with χ² < 200, and the same chain is applied to the data, inclusive MC, and both exclusive-MC samples.
