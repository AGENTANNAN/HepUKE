On ψ(3686) at √s=3.686 GeV, study ψ(2S)→Σ⁰Σ̄⁰ω with Σ⁰→γΛ, Λ→pπ⁻, Σ̄⁰→γΛ̄, Λ̄→p̄π⁺, ω→π⁺π⁻π⁰, π⁰→γγ. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 3M exclusive MC events for the signal mode with the full decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least 3 positive and 3 negative; photons with at least 4, barrel (endcap) energy >25 (50) MeV, TDC 0–14, angle to track >10°, and isolated photons (at least 4) with angle to proton/anti-proton >20°. PID by the probability method with prob>0.001, identify protons against kaons and pions, require ≥1 p and ≥1 p̄, remove them from generic tracks, then identify remaining tracks as pions against kaons and protons. Reconstruct Λ→pπ⁻ and Λ̄→p̄π⁺ via secondary vertex fits minimizing mass difference, with |M_pπ−m_Λ|<0.01 GeV/c² and positive decay length, with vertex fit on each π⁺π⁻ pair from ω; reconstruct at least one π⁰→γγ via Kalman fit with π⁰ mass constraint and χ²<25. Perform a 5C kinematic fit to ΛΛ̄π⁺π⁻γγ with π⁰ mass constraint and four-momentum conservation, χ²<60, nominal. Apply a J/ψ veto via recoil mass of π⁺π⁻(π⁰γⁱγʲ) outside [3.089,3.105] GeV/c² (π⁺π⁻J/ψ) and [3.090,3.145] GeV/c² (π⁰π⁰J/ψ), a 4C fit veto to reject wrong photon multiplicities, and select the 2-D Σ⁰Σ̄⁰ signal region M_γΛ∈[1.176,1.204], M_γΛ̄∈[1.175,1.204] GeV/c², with the Σ⁰ photon chosen by minimizing Δ=(M_γΛ−M_γΛ̄)² among photons not used in π⁰ reconstruction.
