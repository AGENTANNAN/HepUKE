Above 4.08 GeV, measure the e+e− → π+π− D0 D̄0 cross section with D0 → K−π+ (charge conjugate implied), using real data and inclusive MC at 15 scan points from ~4.085 to ~4.600 GeV, and generating 200k-event exclusive MC of the π+π−D0D̄0 (D0→K−π+) phase-space mode at each point. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC within 0–14, and angle to the nearest charged track >20°. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying at least one K+ and one K− against pions, with the remaining charged tracks taken as π+ and π−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining four-momentum to the π+π−K+K− system, χ² < 56, flagged nominal. All energy points share the same selection chain.
