This is a study of e+e− → π0π0 ψ(3686) with ψ(3686) → π+π− J/ψ and J/ψ → e+e− or μ+μ−, using the 4.416 GeV real data point (from the XYZ scan) together with the corresponding inclusive MC; 100k-event exclusive MC samples are generated for both signal modes (J/ψ → e+e− and J/ψ → μ+μ−) and for the dominant background e+e− → π+π− ψ(3686), ψ(3686) → π0π0 J/ψ (J/ψ → e+e−). Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero, while photons must satisfy the EMC timing window, be at least 10° away from any track, exceed 25 MeV (barrel) or 50 MeV (endcap) in energy, and number at least four. Particle ID uses the probability method: tracks with momentum above 1.0 GeV are treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy exceeds 0.6 GeV, otherwise muon), π/K separation is applied, and the event must contain one π+, one π−, one l+ and one l−. Two kinematic fits are performed on γγγγ π+π− l+l−: a 4C fit with χ² < 120 and a nominal 7C fit that additionally constrains both γγ pairs to the π0 mass and the l+l− pair to the J/ψ mass with χ² < 200. Additional requirements are a muon-counter hit condition on the l+l− pair, pairing of the four photons into the two π0 minimizing the summed mass difference and each within 20 MeV/c² of the π0 mass, and a J/ψ mass window of 3.05–3.15 GeV/c² for the dilepton.
