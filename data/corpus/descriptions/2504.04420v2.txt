On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → Ξ− K_S^0 Ω+ (charge conjugate included). Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 2,000,000 exclusive MC events for the signal decay chain ψ(3686) → Ξ− K_S^0 Ω+, Ξ− → Λ π−, Λ → p π−, K_S^0 → π+ π−. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 20 cm, Vr < 1 cm, at least two positive and three negative tracks; identify protons via the probability method with a 0.001 cut against kaons and pions, requiring at least one proton, then remove proton candidates from the positive track list and treat the remaining as pions. Reconstruct Λ → p π− and K_S^0 → π+ π− via secondary vertex fits minimizing the mass difference, removing used particles; reconstruct Ξ− via a vertex fit on Λ and π−, requiring decay length > 0 and selecting the best candidate by minimizing sqrt((M(Λπ)−M_Ξ)/σ)^2 + ((M(pπ)−M_Λ)/σ)^2. Perform a partial reconstruction missing the Ω+ (recoil mass technique) from the Ξ− and K_S^0. Apply mass windows: Ξ− [1.313, 1.330] GeV, Λ [1.111, 1.120] GeV, K_S^0 [0.489, 0.506] GeV; veto ψ(3686) → π+π− J/ψ by requiring the π+π− recoil mass outside [3.09, 3.105] GeV. No kinematic fit is applied.
