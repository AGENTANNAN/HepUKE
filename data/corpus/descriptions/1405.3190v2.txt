On ψ(3770), study the radiative transitions ψ(3770) → γ η_c, γ η_c(2S), and γ χ_c1 (cross-check), with η_c/η_c(2S)/χ_c1 → K_S0 K+π− (charge conjugate implied) and K_S0 → π+π−. Use 2.92 fb−1 of real data and inclusive MC at 3.773 GeV; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the three signal modes. All modes share one selection chain: at least four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring ≥2 positive and ≥2 negative tracks and net charge 0; at least one photon with EMC timing within 700 ns of the collision, >20° from any charged track, and E>25 MeV (barrel) or >50 MeV (endcap). Assign K_S0 daughters as pions, perform a secondary vertex fit to π+π− minimizing the mass difference, and retain K_S0 candidates with |M(π+π−)−M(K_S0)|<10 MeV/c² and decay length >2× the vertex resolution. Identify kaons and pions with a χ²-sum PID method (χ²_PID cut 4); then perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to the γ K_S0 K+π− (and c.c.) hypothesis with χ² cut 200 (loose; the paper applies χ²_4C<20), plus a second 4C fit to γγ K_S0 K+π− to store χ² for the π0 veto. Apply a D0 veto |M(K±π∓)−M(D0)|>3σ and the π0 veto χ²_4C(γ K_S0 K+π−) < χ²_4C(γγ K_S0 K+π−), and correct track helix parameters for data/MC differences using the J/ψ → φ f0(980) control sample.
