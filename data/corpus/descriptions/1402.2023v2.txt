On ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686)→γχ_cJ (J=1,2), χ_cJ→η'K+K−, with two η' decay modes: η'→γρ0, ρ0→π+π− (mode I) and η'→ηπ+π−, η→γγ (mode II). Use the ψ(3686) real data and inclusive MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each mode (ψ(2S)→γχ_c1, χ_c1→η'K+K− followed by the corresponding η' decay). Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; photons must be more than 5° from any charged track and pass barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, with at least two photons in mode I and at least three in mode II. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying kaons against pions/protons and requiring at least one K+ and one K−, while remaining positive and negative tracks are assigned as π+ and π− with overlapping K/π assignments removed. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit: mode I fits γγK+K−π+π− with χ²<40, and mode II fits γγγK+K−π+π− with χ²<50. Mode I then assigns the photon giving the smallest |M(γπ+π−)−M(η')| as the η' daughter and the other as the radiative photon, applies π0 veto |M(γγ)−M(π0)|>15 MeV, J/ψ vetoes |M(π+π−)−M(J/ψ)|>8 MeV and |M(γγ)−M(J/ψ)|>22 MeV, an η' window |M(γπ+π−)−M(η')|<15 MeV, and χ_cJ windows of 30/15/16 MeV for J=0/1/2 on M(γπ+π−K+K−). Mode II vetoes any γγ pair within 20 MeV of M(π0), selects the photon pair closest to M(η) with |M(γγ)−M(η)|<25 MeV, uses |M(ηπ+π−)−M(η')|<25 MeV, and applies χ_cJ windows of 36/18/18 MeV for J=0/1/2 on M(γγπ+π−K+K−).
