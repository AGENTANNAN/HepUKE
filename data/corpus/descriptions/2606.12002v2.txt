On the J/ψ scan (full analysis 26 energy points, total 440.7 pb−1; this BOSS spec uses the resolved 713 Rscan real-data points at 3080, 3020, 3000, 2981, and 2950 MeV, with their matched inclusive MC), study e+e− → K_S0 K+ π− with K_S0 → π+π−. Generate 200k-event exclusive MC at each scan point for e+e− → K_S0 K+ π−, K_S0 → π+π− using the ConExc continuum/J/ψ lineshape. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; photon candidates must satisfy EMC time 0–14, be at least 10° from any track, and have energy >25 MeV in the barrel or >50 MeV in the endcap, with no photon-multiplicity requirement. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating kaons from pions/protons and pions from kaons/protons. Reconstruct K_S0 → π+π− via a secondary-vertex fit of two oppositely charged pions, choosing the combination that minimizes the mass difference and removing used pions from the candidate list. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to e+e− → K_S0 K+ π− with χ²<200, followed by a 5C fit that adds the constraint M(π+π−)=m_K_S0, yielding pre-PWA candidate NTuples.
