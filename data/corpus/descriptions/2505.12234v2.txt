On ψ(3686), study ψ(2S) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → p \bar{p} η η, with each η → γγ. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate a 100k-event exclusive MC for ψ(2S) → γ χ_cJ, χ_cJ → p \bar{p} η η, η → γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC time 0–700 ns, at least 5 photons, and photon–track angle >10°. PID by the probability method (prob >0.001), identifying protons against kaons and pions, requiring at least one proton and one antiproton. Reconstruct two η from photon pairs via a Kalman fit constraining each γγ mass to m_η (χ²<25, at least two η), then perform a 4C kinematic fit to p \bar{p} η η with χ²<200 (loose; paper uses 35), plus a competing 5γ p \bar{p} hypothesis fit without χ² cut for ROOT-level comparison; ROOT-level cuts include a π0 veto |M(γγ)−m_π0|>15 MeV, an η window |M(γγ)−m_η|<20 MeV, and a χ² comparison among 5γ, 4γ, and 6γ hypotheses.
