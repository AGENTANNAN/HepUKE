On the ψ(3770) at √s = 3.773 GeV, measure the absolute branching fractions of D0 and D+ into KKKπ final states using the double-tag method. Use the 20.3 fb⁻¹ ψ(3770) real data together with inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC samples for ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar and ψ(3770) → D+ D− (each decaying via the corresponding Kπ/KS0 modes). For the D0 analysis, tag one side with anti-D0 → K+π−, K+π−π0, K+π−π−π+ and the other side with the same modes as D0 (charm +1, ranked by invariant mass), reconstructing the signal D0 from the remaining tracks in the modes KS0 K+ K− π0, KS0 KS0 K− π+, KS0 KS0 K+ π−, K+ K− K− π+, plus the φ sub-modes φ KS0 π0 and φ K− π+. For the D+ analysis, tag with the six modes D− → K+π−π−, KS0 π−, K+π−π−π0, KS0 π−π0, KS0 π−π−π+, K+K−π− (and the charge-conjugate D+ tag ranked by invariant mass), reconstructing the signal D+ in KS0 K+ K− π+ and φ KS0 π+ (φ → K+K−). Both analyses fit the event with a four-momentum constraint and constrain the invariant masses of both tags to the nominal D0 (D+) mass, requiring χ² < 200, with no extra particles beyond the D Dbar pair at the ψ(3770).
