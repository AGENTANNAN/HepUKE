Study e+e− → π+π− h_c with h_c → γ η_c at √s = 4.23, 4.26, 4.36, and 4.42 GeV, tagging the h_c via the recoil mass against π+π− in the [3.515, 3.535] GeV/c² window, and reconstructing the η_c in four exclusive decay modes: K+K−π0, K_S0 K± π∓, 2(π+π−π0), and p pbar. Use the real data and inclusive MC at all four energy points, and generate exclusive MC for each of the four modes. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vr|<1 cm, |Vz|<10 cm, with exactly 2 charged tracks (net charge 0) for the K+K−π0 and p pbar modes, and exactly 4 charged tracks (net charge 0) for the K_S0 K± π∓ and 2(π+π−π0) modes. Photons are required to have energy >25 MeV in the barrel or >50 MeV in the endcap, with minimum multiplicities of 3, 1, 5, and 1 for the four modes, respectively. Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 threshold: K± against π for K+K−π0, K+ against π for K_S0 K± π∓, π+ against K for 2(π+π−π0), and p/pbar against π for p pbar. A π0 is formed from two photons via a mass-constrained fit, a K_S0 is reconstructed from a π+π− secondary vertex, and a 4C kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation is applied to the η_c decay products, with χ² cuts of 25 (K+K−π0), 45 (K_S0 K± π∓), 35 (2(π+π−π0)), and 40 (p pbar); the combination with minimum total χ² (including π0 and PID contributions) is selected. An inclusive mode requires at least 2 charged tracks (assumed pions) and 1 photon, with recoil mass against π+π− in [3.515, 3.535] GeV/c² and recoil mass against π+π−γ in [2.52, 3.4] GeV/c², and a simultaneous fit across all four energies is used to extract the η_c yields.
