At the 18 BESIII energy points from 4.189 to 4.437 GeV, study e+e− → π+π− h_c with h_c → π0 J/ψ, J/ψ → ℓ+ℓ− (ℓ=e or μ), together with the normalization channel h_c → γ η_c, η_c → K+ K− π0; use real data and inclusive MC at each point, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two decay modes at every energy point. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge 0; photons are selected with 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, TDC 0–14, angle to tracks >10°, and at least two photons in the signal (at least three in the normalization). For the signal, tracks with p>1.0 GeV/c are taken as leptons from J/ψ (electron if EMC energy >1.0 GeV, muon if <0.4 GeV, the rest as pions), M(π+π−π0) is vetoed in [0.51,0.58] and [0.75,0.81] GeV/c², and M(ℓ+ℓ−) is required to be in [3.085,3.115] GeV/c²; a Kalman fit constrains γγ to the π0 mass with χ²<25 and at least one π0, followed by a kinematic fit to π+π− ℓ+ℓ− π0 with four-momentum and π0 mass constraints and χ²<30. For the normalization, use the probability PID method (prob>0.001) with K/π separation against other hypotheses, requiring 1 K+, 1 K−, 1 π+, 1 π−, then the same π0 Kalman fit (χ²<25) and a kinematic fit to π+π− K+K− γ π0 with four-momentum and π0 constraints, χ²<40, extracting η_c in M(K+K−π0)∈[2.92,3.04] GeV/c². The h_c signal is extracted by a simultaneous unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to the π+π− recoil mass in the signal and normalization channels (MC signal shape convolved with a Gaussian, first-order polynomial background), and the branching fraction combines luminosity, cross section, radiative correction, and efficiency over the energy points.
