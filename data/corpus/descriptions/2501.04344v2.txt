On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study the electromagnetic Dalitz decay J/ψ → e⁺e⁻π⁰ with π⁰ → γγ, final state e⁺e⁻γγ. Use the 708_3097 J/ψ real data and inclusive MC, plus 500k-event exclusive signal MC generated with J/ψ → e⁺e⁻π⁰ and π⁰ → γγ phase space. Event selection: exactly one positive and one negative charged track with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge 0; at least two photons with EMC TDC in 0–14 and energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap); PID by the probability method treating high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV/c) as leptons, electrons identified by EMC energy >0.6 GeV, requiring exactly one e⁺ and one e⁻. Reconstruct π⁰ → γγ via a 1-C Kalman kinematic fit constraining the diphoton invariant mass to the nominal π⁰ mass with χ²<25 and at least one π⁰, then perform a 4C kinematic fit (nominal hypothesis) with vertex fit on the e⁺e⁻ pair, χ²<200. Additional cuts suppress backgrounds: E/p>0.8 for electrons with p>0.25 GeV/c against pions, m_γγ ∈ [0.09, 0.18] GeV/c² for the π⁰ signal region, photon-conversion veto δ_xy<2 cm, two-photon-process veto cosθ(e⁺)<0.8 and cosθ(e⁻)>−0.8, p_{e±}<1.45 GeV/c against radiative Bhabha, lower-energy photon E_γ>0.14 GeV, and a tightened 4C χ²<100. Signal yields are extracted in 30 m_{e⁺e⁻} bins via an extended ML fit to m_γγ with a data-calibrated signal shape and peaking backgrounds subtracted.
