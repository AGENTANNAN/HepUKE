On the ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, search for ψ(3770) → D0 D̄0 with one D̄0 (tag) reconstructed in K+π−, K+π−π0, or K+π−π+π−, and the other D0 decaying via two signal modes: D0 → ω γ′ (ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ) and D0 → γ γ′, where γ′ is a missing, invisible particle of unconstrained mass. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal decay chains. For the ωγ′ mode, require one π+ and one π− and exactly two photons with energy above 25 MeV and minimum photon angle 10°, with π0 from γγ in 0.115–0.150 GeV and ω from π+π−γγ in 0.700–0.850 GeV, then perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation plus π0 and ω nominal-mass constraints and χ² < 200. For the γγ′ mode, require exactly one photon with energy above 0.5 GeV and angle above 10° plus the missing γ′, and perform a four-momentum-constrained fit with χ² < 200. Both modes veto extra photons by requiring the total energy of all other photons below 0.1 GeV and suppress endcap background with |cosθ_recoil| < 0.7.
