Measure e+e− → ω π+π− (ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ) at c.m. energies 2.000–3.080 GeV over 19 points (647 pb−1 total), using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive MC events for e+e− → ω π+π− phase-space with ω → π+π−π0, π0 → γγ. Select events with four charged tracks and net charge zero, each track satisfying |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, and require at least two photons with E>25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), EMC time in 0–700 ns, and at least 10° from any charged track. Use probability-based PID (probability >0.001) to identify pions against kaons, and assign two π+ and two π− (π+π−π+π−). Build π0 candidates from γγ pairs with invariant mass 0.120–0.150 GeV/c², then perform a 4C kinematic fit to e+e− → 2(π+π−)γγ imposing four-momentum conservation with the nominal flag, keeping events with χ²<200.
