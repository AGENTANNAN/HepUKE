On ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, measure D-meson decays to φ plus a pseudoscalar with φ→K+K−: D+→φπ+ and D+→φK+ (both tagged against D−→anti-K0π−), and D0→φπ0 and D0→φη (π0/η→γγ, tagged against anti-D0→K+π−). Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the four modes. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm and Vr<10 cm; the charged-D+ modes require at least two positive and one negative track with net charge +1, while the neutral-D0 modes require at least one positive, one negative and net charge 0. For the neutral modes, photons must have 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, EMC TDC in 0–700, at least two photons, and be isolated by more than 10° from any track. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut: π/K/proton separation for mode I (1 K+, 1 K−, 1 π+), and K-vs-π/proton identification for the others (mode II: 2 K+, 1 K−; modes III and IV: 1 K+, 1 K−). A 4C kinematic fit with χ²<200 is applied to K+K−π+ (mode I), K+K+K− (mode II), K+K−π0 (mode III) and K+K−η (mode IV), preceded in the two neutral modes by a Kalman fit of the photon pair to the π0 or η nominal mass with χ²<25. The ΔE windows, applied afterwards in ROOT, are [−0.020,0.019], [−0.019,0.018], [−0.077,0.035] and [−0.040,0.038] GeV for modes I–IV; if more than one D candidate survives, the one with smallest |ΔE| is kept, followed by an unbinned 2D fit to M_BC vs M_KK (with a quantum-coherence correction for D0→φη).
