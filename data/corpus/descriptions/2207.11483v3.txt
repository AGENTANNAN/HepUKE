Study semileptonic Λc+ decays using a double-tag method at BESIII: ST Λc− reconstructed in 14 hadronic tag modes, DT signal Λc+ → pK− e+ νe, also searching for Λ(1520) and Λ(1405) resonances in the pK− spectrum. Use real data at 7 c.m. energies from 4.600 to 4.699 GeV (total 4.5 fb−1) and inclusive MC; generate 500k exclusive MC events for Λc+ → pK− e+ νe. ST selection requires M_BC in [2.28, 2.30] GeV/c2 and mode-dependent ΔE. DT selection requires exactly 3 charged tracks recoiling against the ST Λc−, proton and kaon PID as in ST, e+ PID with CL_e > 0.001, CL_e > CL_π, CL_e > CL_K, M(pKe+) < 2.15 GeV, and π0 veto. Perform a 4-momentum conservation fit with missing neutrino, χ2 cut 200, using U_miss = E_miss − |p_miss| for neutrino identification. Search for Λ(1520) and Λ(1405) via 2D unbinned fit to M(pK−) vs U_miss, with Breit-Wigner and Flatté parameterizations, respectively. Results: ST yield 122,268 ± 474, DT yield 33.5 ± 6.3 (8.9σ), BF(Λc+ → pK− e+ νe) = (0.88 ± 0.17 ± 0.07)×10−3, and |Vcs| = 1.3 ± 0.3 ± 0.1.
