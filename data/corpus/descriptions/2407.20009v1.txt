Study e+e− → K+K− ψ(2S) from 4.700 to 4.946 GeV, using the eight real-data points (4.700, 4.720, 4.740, 4.750, 4.780, 4.840, 4.914, 4.946 GeV) with matching inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive signal MC (e+e− → K+K−ψ(2S), ψ(2S) → J/ψπ+π−, J/ψ → e+e−) at every energy point. Four orthogonal partial-reconstruction approaches are commissioned: (i) tag K+K− plus a J/ψ from ψ(2S) → J/ψ X with signal extracted from the recoil mass against K+K− (signal region 3.67–3.71 GeV/c², sidebands twice as wide), (ii) tag a single K and fully reconstruct ψ(2S) → π+π−J/ψ (J/ψ → μ+μ−) with a 1C fit to the missing kaon mass (χ²_1C<50), (iii) tag K+K− with ψ(2S) → l+l−, and (iv) tag a single K with ψ(2S) → l+l− (J/ψ → μ+μ−) and a 1C missing-kaon fit (χ²_1C<15). Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with at least one positive and one negative track; photons must satisfy 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds with EMC timing 0–700 ns and >10° opening from any track. Particle ID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, separating K and π, electrons identified by E/p>0.8 and muons by E/p<0.4 (J/ψ → μ+μ− requiring at least one muon through three or more MUC layers), with a lepton pair defined as two opposite-charge tracks of p>1.0 GeV/c. J/ψ candidates must lie in M(l+l−) ∈ (3.05, 3.15) GeV/c², ψ(2S) candidates in approach (iii) in (3.631, 3.726) GeV/c², and approach (iv) additionally vetoes Bhabha-like events with cosθ(e+)>0.85 or cosθ(e−)<−0.85. A nominal kinematic fit imposing four-momentum conservation on K+K− l+l− with χ²<200 is applied, and the same selection chain is run on all real data, inclusive MC and the exclusive signal samples for the cross-section and Z_cs± measurements.
