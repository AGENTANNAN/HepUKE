Using 7.33 fb−1 of e+e− data at eight center-of-mass energies from 4.128 to 4.226 GeV (4.128, 4.157, 4.178, 4.189, 4.199, 4.209, 4.219, 4.226 GeV) together with the matching inclusive MC, study the semileptonic decays Ds+ → f1(1285) e+ νe with f1(1285) → π+π−η, η → γγ, and Ds+ → f1(1420) e+ νe with f1(1420) → K+K−π0, π0 → γγ, using a double-tag topology where a hadronic Ds− tag is reconstructed together with a transition photon from Ds*± → γ Ds±. Generate 500k exclusive MC events for each of the two signal modes. Reconstruct the Ds− tag through twelve hadronic tag modes (including KKπ, KsK, πη, πη′, KKππ0, KsKππ, ρη, πη′ρ(γ), KsKπ0, πππ, Kππ) for the f1(1285) mode and nine of these modes for the f1(1420) mode. On the signal side require one e+ identified by combined MDC+TOF+EMC probabilities with L′e>0, L′e/(L′e+L′π+L′K)>0.8 and EEMC/|p|>0.8c, plus one π+ and one π− for f1(1285) or one K+ and one K− for f1(1420), net charge +1, at least three photons with Eγ>25 MeV and angle >10°, a missing νe, non-Ks0 pions with p>100 MeV/c, per-energy mrec windows against the tagged Ds− (e.g. [2.048,2.190] GeV/c2 at 4.178 GeV), and an mrec2 window against the photon plus tagged Ds− of (3.82,4.05) or (3.78,4.05) GeV2/c4 for the two modes. Perform a 3C kinematic fit enforcing four-momentum conservation and the η→γγ or π0→γγ invariant mass to the nominal η or π0 mass, with χ2<200 and storing fitted momenta, while selecting the transition photon from two Ds* parent-assignment hypotheses by the smallest 3C χ2 that also constrains the Ds−, Ds+, and Ds* masses. For the f1(1420) mode also veto φe+νe and K+K−π+π0 backgrounds by requiring M(K+K−)>1.03 GeV/c2 and M(K+K−π0e+)>1.92 GeV/c2. All eight data, inclusive-MC, and exclusive-MC samples are processed through the same two chains.
