At e+e− collision energies from 4.178 to 4.226 GeV, using the six BOSS 703 data sets 4180–4230 and the matching inclusive MC samples, search for the two missing-energy decays D*+ → e+νe and D*+ → μ+νμ with a single-tag plus missing-neutrino technique. Generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each signal mode, with ψ(4260) → D*+D*−, the tag decay D*− → D0bar π− followed by D0bar → K+π−, and the signal decay D*+ → e+νe or D*+ → μ+νμ. Reconstruct the tag D*− through the charm −1 D0 hadronic modes Kπ, Kππ0, and Kπππ; only these D0 tag modes are included, while D+ tag modes contributing through D−π0 are not covered. On the signal side require exactly one charged positron or muon with charge +1 and a missing massless neutrino, and veto extra photons by requiring the maximum unused photon energy to be below 0.3 GeV. Identify the positron with L′(e)>0.001, L′(e)/(L′(e)+L′(π)+L′(K))>0.8, and E/p>0.8, and identify the muon with EMC energy in (0, 0.3) GeV together with MUC hit-depth requirements. Perform a 4C kinematic fit constraining the tag D*−, the signal lepton, and the neutrino to the centre-of-mass four-momentum, and accept events with χ²<200; the electron and muon channels use the same tag-side selection chain.
