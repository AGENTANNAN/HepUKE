On ψ(4260) at 4.600 GeV, study e+e− → Λc+ Λc− with Λc+ → p K_S0 η, K_S0 → π+π−, η → γγ, and the charge-conjugate anti-Λc− decaying to anti-p π+ as specified in the decay card. Use the 4.600 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1,000,000 exclusive MC events for the signal decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, requiring at least one positive and at least three negative tracks with net charge zero; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold and TDC 0–700, at least two. Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying at least one proton against kaons and pions; then remove one proton from the positive tracks and treat the remaining positives as π+ and negatives as π−. Reconstruct K_S0 from a π+π− pair via secondary vertex fit with invariant mass 0.487–0.511 GeV, and η from two photons via a Kalman fit constraining the γγ mass to the nominal η mass (χ²<25), requiring at least one η. Finally, perform a kinematic fit on the p K_S0 η system with a four-momentum constraint, χ²<200, flagged nominal.
