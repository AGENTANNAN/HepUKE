At √s = 4.599 GeV (567 pb⁻¹), measure B(Λc⁺ → n K_S0 π⁺) with a single-tag/double-tag analysis: the anti-Λc⁻ is reconstructed in eleven hadronic modes, recoiling against the Λc⁺ decaying to the signal with an undetected neutron and K_S0 → π⁺π⁻. Use the 4.599 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive signal MC with Λc⁺ → n K_S0 π⁺ in phase space, the anti-Λc⁻ decaying generically, and K_S0 → π⁺π⁻. Tag-side selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, |Vr| < 1 cm (V0 daughters exempt); PID from dE/dx, TOF and EMC requiring π (L_π > L_K), K (L_K > L_π) and proton (L'_p > L'_π and L'_p > L'_K); photons with E > 25 MeV (barrel, |cosθ| ≤ 0.80) or E > 50 MeV (endcap, 0.86 ≤ |cosθ| ≤ 0.92), >10° from the nearest charged track, EMC time in (0,700) ns; π⁰ from γγ in (0.110,0.155) GeV/c² with a 1C mass constraint and χ² < 20; K_S0 → π⁺π⁻ and Λ → p̄π⁺ from vertex fits with L > 0 and masses M(π⁺π⁻) ∈ (0.485,0.510) GeV/c², M(p̄π⁺) ∈ (1.110,1.121) GeV/c², vetoing Σ⁰ via M(γΛ̄) ∈ (1.179,1.205) GeV/c² and Σ⁻ via M(p̄π⁰) ∈ (1.173,1.200) GeV/c², with additional Λ/Σ⁻ and K_S0/Λ vetoes for specific tag modes. Tags are kept in the M_BC window (2.280,2.296) GeV/c² with a ~3σ ΔE window. The signal side requires one K_S0, one pion of charge opposite to the tag, no photons, net charge +1, and a missing neutron; a kinematic fit enforces four-momentum conservation including the undetected neutron and the K_S0 nominal mass, with χ² < 200.
