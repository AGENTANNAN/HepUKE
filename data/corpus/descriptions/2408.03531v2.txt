Study the radiative decay ψ(2S) → γ π0, π0 → γγ, giving a three-photon final state, to measure the e+e− → γ π0 form factor. Use the ψ(2S) (3.686 GeV) real data and inclusive MC, the ψ(3773) real data and inclusive MC, and off-resonance data at 3.650 and 3.682 GeV with their inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for e+e− → γ π0 with a ψ(4260) → γ π0, π0 → γγ decay card. Event selection: veto all charged tracks (nTot = 0) using |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm; require exactly three photons, all in the barrel EMC with |cosθγ| < 0.8 and energy above 25 MeV, with standard timing and shower-quality cuts and no photon isolation. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to the three photons (no π0 mass constraint), flagged nominal, with χ² < 40. The highest-energy photon is taken as the radiative photon and the other two as π0 daughters; the helicity angle (cosθhel < 0.7) suppresses the e+e− → γγγ QED background, and the number of MDC hits along the line between the IP and the EMC shower must be below 8 to reject photon conversions. The π0 mass window and signal extraction are left to downstream ROOT analysis.
