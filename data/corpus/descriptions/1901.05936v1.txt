On the ψ(3770) peak, using 2.93 fb⁻¹ of real data and inclusive MC, study D⁺ → K_S0 π⁺ π⁺ π⁻ with the D⁻ tag reconstructed as K⁺ π⁻ π⁻, where K_S0 → π⁺ π⁻. Generate 100k-event exclusive MC for the full ψ(3770) → D⁺D⁻ chain, with D⁺ → K_S0 π⁺ π⁺ π⁻, D⁻ → K⁺ π⁻ π⁻, and K_S0 → π⁺ π⁻. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 20 cm, Vr < 1 cm, at least 3 positive and 3 negative tracks, net charge zero. Particle identification by the probability method with prob_cut 0.001: identify at least one kaon against pions, remove identified kaons, then identify pions against kaons. Form K_S0 candidates from π⁺π⁻ pairs via a secondary vertex fit using mass-difference minimization, removing used particles. Perform a 6C kinematic fit on K_S0 π⁺ π⁺ π⁻ K⁺ π⁻ π⁻ with four-momentum conservation, D⁺ mass constraint on K_S0 π⁺ π⁺ π⁻, K_S0 mass constraint, and χ² < 100. Before the fit, apply K_S0 mass window [0.4676, 0.5276] GeV/c², ΔE cuts (tag: [-0.027, 0.025] GeV; signal: [-0.033, 0.030] GeV) and M_BC in [1.8628, 1.8788] GeV/c² for both D candidates, a prefit vertex fit on all charged tracks with χ² < 100, and veto events with additional K_S0 candidates (π⁺π⁻ mass within 30 MeV/c² of K_S0 and decay length > 2σ).
