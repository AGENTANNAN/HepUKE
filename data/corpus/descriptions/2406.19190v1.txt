At the BESIII scan energies √s = 4.128–4.226 GeV (eight points: 4.128, 4.157, 4.178, 4.189, 4.199, 4.209, 4.219, 4.226 GeV), measure the branching fraction of Ds+ → K0S e+ νe using a double-tag method, with the per-run measured E_cms (central value 4.178 GeV). Generate 500k-event exclusive MC for the signal, ψ(4260) → Ds*− Ds+, Ds*− → γ Ds−, Ds+ → K0S e+ νe (ISGW2), K0S → π+π−, with the tag Ds− → K+K−π−. Tag side: reconstruct a single tag Ds− (charm = −1) in 14 hadronic modes (K+K−π−, K+K−π−π0, π+π−π−, K0S K−, K0S K−π0, K−π+π−, K0S K0S π−, K0S K−π+π−, K0S K−π+π−π0, ηγγπ−, η′π+π−ηρ, η′ηγγπ+π−π−, η′γρπ−, ηγγρ). Signal side: require exactly one π+, one π−, one e+, net charge +1, zero photons, and a missing νe, with K0S reconstructed from π+π−. Perform a 4C kinematic fit with an additional K0S mass constraint and χ² < 200, testing two transition-γ hypotheses (γ + ST Ds− → Ds*−; γ + semileptonic → Ds*+) and retaining the smaller χ² (<100). Positron identification combines dE/dx, TOF, and EMC: CLe' > 0.001 and CLe'/(CLe'+CLπ'+CLK') > 0.8. Additional requirements: K0S mass window |M(π+π−) − m_K0S| < 12 MeV/c² with decay length > 2× vertex resolution; largest unused shower energy < 0.2 GeV; M(K0e+) < 1.78 GeV/c² veto against Ds+ → K0 K+; and pion momentum > 0.1 GeV/c for pions not from K0S/η/η′.
