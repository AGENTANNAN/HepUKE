On the e+e− continuum from 3.508 to 4.951 GeV, study e+e− → φη′ cross sections, with φ→K+K− and two η′ decay modes: η′→γπ+π− (Mode I) and η′→ηπ+π− with η→γγ (Mode II), including a search for ψ(3770)→φη′. Use real data at 41 energy points (26.1 fb⁻¹ total) and inclusive MC for BOSS versions 703, 705, 706, 709, 712; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for the continuum φη′ signal with η′→γπ+π−. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly four tracks with net charge 0; PID by the probability method with K/π separation, requiring one K+ and one K−, then assigning the remaining tracks as π+ and π−; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, TDC 0–700, and angle to any track >10°, requiring at least one photon for Mode I and at least two for Mode II. For Mode I, perform a 4C kinematic fit on K+ K− π+ π− γ, constraining total four-momentum, χ² cut 100, nominal; for Mode II, first constrain two gammas to the η mass (χ² cut 200, at least one η), then perform a 5C kinematic fit on K+ K− π+ π− η (4C plus η mass constraint), χ² cut 200, nominal. Apply the φ mass window |M(K+K−) − m_φ| < 0.04 GeV/c² and an η′ mass window in the subsequent ROOT analysis. Both decay modes share the same base selection.
