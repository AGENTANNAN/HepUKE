On the J/ψ, study the radiative decay J/ψ → γ D0 with three D0 decay modes: K−π+ (Mode I), K−π+π0 (Mode II), and K−π+π+π− (Mode III). Use 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the three modes. All modes share a common selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold and TDC within 0–14. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, K versus π separation, requiring for Mode I one K+ and zero K−, one π+ and zero π−; for Mode II the same charged multiplicity plus a π0 reconstructed from two photons; for Mode III one K+, zero K−, two π+, and one π−. Kinematic fits: Mode I performs a nominal 4C fit to K−π+γ with χ²<200, plus alternative fits to π+π−γ, K+K−γ, and π+π−γγ for background veto; Mode II first does a 1C fit to γγ with a π0 mass constraint (χ²<200), then a nominal 5C fit to K−π+γγγ with π0 mass constraint (χ²<200), plus alternative fits to π+π−π0γ, K+K−π0γ, and π+π−π0γγ; Mode III performs a nominal 4C fit to K−π+π+π−γ with χ²<200, plus an alternative fit to π+π−π+π−γ.
