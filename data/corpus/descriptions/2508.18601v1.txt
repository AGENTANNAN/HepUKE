At 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → γ χ_c1 with χ_c1 → π+π− η_c, the η_c being reconstructed in 16 exclusive decay modes (p pbar, K+K−π+π−, p pbar π+π−, 4π, 4K, 6π, K+K−4π, K_S K π, K_S K 3π, K+K−π0, p pbar π0, K+K−η, π+π−η, 2π 2π0, 4π η, 6π π0), grouped into charged, K_S and neutral categories; use the 709_3686 ψ(2S) real data (2712.4×10⁶ events) and its inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for ψ(3686)→γχ_c1, χ_c1→π+π−η_c. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) threshold, EMC TDC 0–14, opening angle to any track >10°, at least one photon; PID by the probability method (prob cut 0.001), protons identified against kaons and pions, at least one p+ and one p−, with the remaining positive and negative tracks assigned to π+ and π−, and soft pions with p<0.4 GeV/c removed. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γ π+π− p pbar with the γπ+π− invariant mass constrained to [2.80, 3.20] GeV/c² and χ²<42 (charged modes; χ²<36 for K_S modes and χ²<23 for neutral modes), K_S reconstructed from a π+π− secondary-vertex fit within |M−m_K_S|<12 MeV/c², π0/η from a 1C γγ fit with χ²<200, and the candidate with the smallest combined χ² (4C + 1C + PID + vertex) retained. Apply ψ(3686)→π+π−J/ψ and ψ(3686)→η J/ψ vetoes at 3σ, select χ_c1 in the recoil-mass window [3.48, 3.54] GeV/c², and extract the η_c yield from a combined maximum-likelihood fit over all 16 modes (Johnson function for the π+π− dip plus a 5th-order polynomial) to set a 90% C.L. Bayesian upper limit; a non-nominal 4C fit to the competing 4π hypothesis is kept for the ROOT-level veto.
