On e+e− data at 4.180, 4.190, 4.200, 4.210, 4.220 and 4.230 GeV, study e+e− → Ds*+ Ds− with Ds*+ → γ Ds+ and Ds+ → π+π+π− η, η → γγ, using a double-tag technique where the tag Ds− is reconstructed in eight hadronic modes (K_S K, K K π, K_S K π0, K K π π0, K_S K− π+ π−, K_S K+ π− π−, π+π+π− η, K π π; for the K− π+ π− mode, M(π+π−) is excluded in [0.487, 0.511] GeV/c² to remove Ds− → K_S0 K− overlap) and the signal Ds+ is built from the remaining tracks. Use all six real-data points with the matching inclusive MC, and generate a 500k-event exclusive signal MC for Ds*+ → γ Ds+, Ds+ → π+π+π− η, η → γγ. Signal-side selection requires exactly two photons with a minimum photon angle of 10°, plus two π+ and one π− charged track, and the Ds− tag is required to carry opposite charm. Backgrounds are suppressed with a K_S0 veto (secondary-vertex M(π+π−) in [0.487, 0.511] GeV/c² and L/σ_L > 2), an η′ veto (M(π+π−η) < 1 GeV/c²) and a π0 cross-feed veto on M(γ_eta γ) in [0.115, 0.150] GeV/c², plus a BDT for purity above 85%. A 7C kinematic fit is applied constraining four-momentum conservation, the η mass and the tag Ds− mass (the Ds*+ mass constraint involving the transition photon being applied later in ROOT), with χ² cut 200, keeping the minimum-χ² candidate when multiple combinations (≈15%) arise.
