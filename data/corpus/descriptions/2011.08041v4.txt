At √s = 4.178 GeV (3.19 fb⁻¹) perform a double-tag analysis of e+e− → Ds*+ Ds− → γ Ds+ Ds−, reconstructing the signal Ds+ in K+K−π+ and the tag Ds− through eight hadronic modes: K+K−π+, K_S K, K_S K+π−π−, K−π+π−, K_S K−π+π−, π+π−π−, π+π−π−η and K+K−π+π0. Use the 4.178 GeV real data and matching inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive MC events following ψ(4260) → Ds+ Ds*−, Ds*− → γ Ds−, Ds+ → K+K−π+. Require exactly one photon from Ds* → Dsγ with a minimum photon opening angle of 10°, rank the K+K−π+ Ds+ tag by invariant mass, and apply a 6C kinematic fit imposing four-momentum conservation plus both Ds invariant masses at the nominal Ds mass, with χ² < 200. Tag Ds mass windows are mode-dependent, 1.940–1.996 GeV/c², the signal Ds window is 1.950–1.986 GeV/c², and the recoil mass is required within 2.051–2.180 GeV/c². Background suppression vetoes soft π± and π0 with momentum below 0.1 GeV/c and applies K_S (0.487–0.511 GeV/c²), π0 (0.115–0.150 GeV/c², χ²<30) and η (0.490–0.580 GeV/c², χ²<30) mass windows together with a secondary-vertex significance cut L/σ_L > 2.
