On the ψ(3686), search for ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ with χ_cJ → μ+μ− J/ψ, in two J/ψ decay modes, J/ψ → e+e− and J/ψ → μ+μ−. Use the 4.48×10⁸-event ψ(3686) real data sample and its inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two J/ψ decay modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks with net charge 0; photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC timing within 0–14, angle to any track >20°, at least one photon. PID uses the probability method: tracks with p>1.0 GeV/c are treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy above 1.0 GeV for the e+e− mode, or above 0.3 GeV for the μ+μ− mode, otherwise muon), with muons identified against π/K/p, requiring 1 l+ and 1 l− plus at least 1 μ+ and 1 μ− in the electron mode, and at least 2 μ+ and 2 μ− in the muon mode. A vertex fit on the four charged tracks precedes a 4C kinematic fit (γ μ+μ− l+l−) with χ²<40, flagged nominal. Additional requirements: the J/ψ lepton-pair mass in [3.085, 3.110] GeV/c², R_xy>8.5 cm to veto photon conversions, M(γμ+μ−) outside [0.535, 0.560] GeV/c² to suppress ψ(3686)→η J/ψ, a helix-parameter correction from the ψ(3686)→π+π−J/ψ control sample, and, when several photons are found, retention of the one giving the smallest fit χ².
