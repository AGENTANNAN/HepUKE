At the 13 energy points from 4.600 to 4.951 GeV, study e+e− → γ φ J/ψ via φ χ_c1,c2, with φ → K+K− or K_S^0 K_L^0 and J/ψ → l+l−, using all real data and inclusive MC at each point, plus 100k-event exclusive MC for φ→K+K− and φ→K_S^0K_L^0 (J/ψ→e+e−, generated via ψ(4260)→φ γ J/ψ). Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm; photons are selected with EMC TDC 0–700 ns, angle to the nearest track >10°, and energy thresholds of 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap). Particle identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, treats high-momentum tracks (p>0.95 GeV/c) as leptons (electron if EMC energy >1.0 GeV, otherwise muon), and separates K from π; the three reconstruction modes require exactly 3 tracks with at least one K+ (missing K− constrained), exactly 4 tracks with one K+ and one K−, or at least 4 tracks with a K_S^0→π+π− secondary vertex. The K+K− 3-track mode performs a 1C fit to K+ l+ l− with the missing K− mass constrained to nominal, the K+K− 4-track mode a 4C fit to K+ K− l+ l−, and the K_S^0K_L^0 mode a 1C fit to K_S^0 l+ l− with the missing K_L^0 mass constrained, all with χ²<200. The J/ψ mass window is |M(l+l−)−m_J/ψ|<3σ, the K_S^0 window is |M(π+π−)−m_K_S^0|<3σ with decay length >2σ, and for the 3-track mode the smallest 1C χ² combination is chosen with an additional muon hit depth >40 cm for muon ID; χ_c1 and χ_c2 are separated by fitting M(φ J/ψ) after merging the modes.
