Using seven energy points from 4599.53 to 4698.82 MeV (4.5 fb−1), search for Λc+ → Σ0 K+ π0 and Λc+ → Σ0 K+ π+ π−, with Σ0 → Λγ, Λ → pπ−, and π0 → γγ in the first mode, via a single-tag method that reconstructs one Λc+ without a recoil. Use real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1M-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes at each energy point. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm: for the π0 mode at least one positive and one negative track (≥2 total), identified as p, K+, π−; for the π+π− mode at least two positive and two negative tracks (≥4 total), identified as p, K+, π+, π−. Photons are required with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, at least three in the π0 mode and at least one in the π+π− mode, with EMC timing in [0,700] ns and >10° from charged tracks. Reconstruct Λ → pπ− with a secondary vertex fit (χ²<100, decay length >2), requiring |M(pπ−)−1.1157|<0.005 GeV/c². For the π0 mode, select M(γγ) in [0.115,0.150] GeV/c², perform a 1C kinematic fit constraining M(γγ) to the π0 nominal mass with χ²<200, flagged nominal, require M(Λγ) in [1.179,1.203] GeV/c², cos(angle(p̄,γ))<cos20°, and −0.160<ΔE(pπ−K+γγ)<−0.030 GeV; for the π+π− mode, require M(Λγ) in [1.179,1.203] GeV/c² and ΔE(pπ−K+π+π−)<−0.040 GeV. In both modes choose the best Λc+ candidate by minimum |ΔE|, with final ΔE windows [−27,6] MeV (π0) and [−21,7] MeV (π+π−) and M_BC in [2.282,2.291] GeV/c².
