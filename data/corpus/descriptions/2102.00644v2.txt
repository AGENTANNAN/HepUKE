On the ψ(4260) energy scan, study e+e− → π0 X(3872) γ, X(3872) → π+π− J/ψ, J/ψ → e+e−, π0 → γγ, using 23 real-data points from 4.178 to 4.600 GeV and the corresponding inclusive MC. Generate 100k-event exclusive MC for this signal mode. Apply a helix-parameter correction to charged tracks before the kinematic fit. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0; photons with TDC 0–700, barrel energy threshold 25 MeV / endcap 50 MeV, angle to any track >10°, at least three photons. PID by the probability method with probability >0.001, treating high-momentum tracks (p>1.0) as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6), requiring one l+ and one l− and assigning the remaining positive/negative tracks as π+ and π−. Perform a Kalman fit on the two photons constraining their invariant mass to the π0 nominal mass, χ²<25, requiring at least one π0; then a 4C kinematic fit on l+ l− π+ π− π0 γ with χ²<60, flagged nominal. The same selection chain is applied to real data, inclusive MC, and the exclusive MC signal sample.
