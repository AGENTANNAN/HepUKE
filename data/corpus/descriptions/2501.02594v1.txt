On the ψ(3686), study ψ(3686)→K⁻Λ(1520)Ξ̄⁺ + c.c., with Λ(1520)→pK⁻, Ξ̄⁺→Λ̄π⁺, and Λ̄→p̄π⁺, giving a pp̄K⁻K⁻π⁺π⁺ final state. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive signal MC events with the full decay chain. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly three positive and three negative tracks, and net charge zero. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut: identify one p and one p̄ against kaons/pions, two K⁻ against pions, and two π⁺ against kaons, choosing the best combination by the sum of PID likelihoods. Reconstruct the Λ̄→p̄π⁺ secondary vertex by minimizing the mass difference and remove its daughters from the candidate list. Then perform a 4C kinematic fit to the six charged particles pp̄K⁻K⁻π⁺π⁺ with a nominal four-momentum constraint and χ²<200. Tag the Ξ̄⁺ via the recoil mass RM(pK₁⁻K₂⁻), using signal region 1.31–1.34 GeV/c² and sidebands 1.242–1.272 and 1.372–1.402 GeV/c²; assign K₁⁻ as the kaon giving M(pK⁻) closer to the nominal Λ(1520), and use the Λ(1520) signal region 1.50–1.54 GeV/c² with sidebands 1.43–1.47 and 1.57–1.61 GeV/c². The continuum background from 3.773 GeV data is scaled by f_c=1.386, and at least three tracks are required to originate from the IP.
