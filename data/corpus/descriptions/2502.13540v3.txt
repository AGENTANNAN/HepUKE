On ψ(3686), study ψ(3686) → γ K_S^0 K_S^0 (both K_S^0 → π+π−) for an amplitude analysis. Use the 3.686 GeV real data sample together with its inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive MC events for ψ(2S) → γ K_S^0 anti-K_S^0 with both kaons decaying to π+π−. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<20 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0; photons with EMC TDC in [0,14], energy thresholds 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), at least 10° away from any track, and at least one photon. Two secondary-vertex fits on π+π− pairs build K_S^0 candidates by minimizing the mass difference and remove the used tracks, requiring exactly two K_S^0 per event with |M(π+π−) − M(K_S^0)| < 12 MeV/c² (2.5σ) and decay length exceeding 2σ of the vertex-fit resolution; a cut M(K_S^0 K_S^0) < 2.8 GeV/c² suppresses χc0,2 backgrounds. A 4C kinematic fit to γ π+π− π+π− is applied with χ²<50, nominal. The two K_S^0 candidates are randomly mixed because they are indistinguishable, and the continuum background, estimated from a 3650 MeV data sample (401 pb⁻¹), is found negligible.
