At sqrt(s)=4.599 GeV, use 567 pb^-1 of real data and the matching inclusive MC (sample 703_4600) plus 500k exclusive MC events of e+e− → Λc+ anti-Λc- with Λc+ → Λ e+ ν_e, Λ → p π- and anti-Λc- → anything (PHOTOS, phase space) to measure the absolute branching fraction of Λc+ → Λ e+ ν_e via the single-tag/double-tag technique, charge-conjugate modes included. The anti-Λc- tag is reconstructed in eleven hadronic modes: pbar K_S0, pbar K+π-, pbar K+π-π0, pbar K_S0π0, pbar K_S0π+π-, Lambdabar π-, Lambdabar π-π0, Λ π-π+π-, Sigmabar0 π-, Σ-π0 and Σ-π+π-. Charged tracks require |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm (tracks from K_S0/Λ excepted); photons are barrel (|cosθ|≤0.80) EMC clusters with E>25 MeV or endcap (0.86≤|cosθ|≤0.92) clusters with E>50 MeV, isolated by >10° from the nearest charged track and with EMC time in (0,700) ns, while π0 candidates are γγ pairs with 0.110<M(γγ)<0.155 GeV/c² mass-constrained with χ²<20. Particle ID uses MDC dE/dx and TOF likelihoods for π/K separation (L_π>L_K for pions, L_K>L_π for kaons), dE/dx+TOF+EMC for protons (L'_p>L'_π and L'_p>L'_K), and for electrons L'_e>0.001 with L'_e/(L'_e+L'_π+L'_K)>0.8, including bremsstrahlung recovery in a 5° cone. K_S0, Λ, Sigmabar0 and Σ- are formed with secondary-vertex fits, positive decay length and mass windows 0.485–0.510 (π+π-), 1.110–1.121 (pbar π+), 1.179–1.205 (γ Λ) and 1.173–1.200 (pbar π0) GeV/c²; pbar K_S0π0 tags veto M(pbar π+)∈(1.105,1.125) and M(pbar π0)∈(1.173,1.200), while Λπ+π-π- and Σ-π+π- tags veto M(π+π-)∈(0.480,0.520) and M(pbar π+)∈(1.105,1.125) GeV/c². Tags are selected with beam-constrained mass and per-mode ΔE windows spanning −0.049 to +0.062 GeV (M_BC signal region 2.280–2.296 GeV/c²), giving N_tot=14415±159; the signal side requires exactly one p, one π- and one e+ with net charge +1, no photons and a missing ν_e, followed by a kinematic fit imposing 4-momentum conservation (with the neutrino) and the nominal Λ mass on pπ-, with χ²<200. The neutrino kinematics is inferred from U_miss = E_miss − c|p_miss|, which peaks at zero for signal.
