At √s = 3.773 GeV, on the ψ(3770), search for the dineutrino decay D0 → π0 ν νbar using a double-tag method. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 300k exclusive MC events for ψ(3770) → D0 D0bar. Tag the anti-D0 (charm = −1) in three hadronic modes: D0 → K−π+, D0 → K−π+π0, and D0 → K−π+π+π−; on the signal side require the all-neutral D0 → π0 ν νbar, i.e. exactly two photons from π0 → γγ that are not used by the tag, zero additional charged tracks, and one massless missing neutrino reconstructed by the recoil method. Perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation plus a γγ invariant-mass constraint to the nominal π0 mass, χ² < 200, and at ROOT level require the π0 mass in (0.115, 0.150) GeV/c² with a π0 kinematic-fit χ² < 20. Reject D0 → K_L^0 π0 and D0 → K̄*(892)0 π0 backgrounds by requiring the missing-mass squared M²_miss = (p_e+e− − p_tag − p_π0)² in (1.1, 1.9) GeV²/c⁴. Model the dominant D0 → π0 K_L^0 X background data-driven from J/ψ → φK±π∓K_L^0 and J/ψ → K±π∓K_L^0 control samples, the X component from D0 → π0 K_S^0 X with K_S^0 → π+π−, and extract the yield via an extended maximum-likelihood fit to the summed EMC energy E_EMC, with wrong-tag (non-D0D0bar) events taken from the M_BC sideband (1.830, 1.855) GeV/c² and scaled by 0.611 into the signal region (1.858, 1.874) GeV/c². This gives a 90% CL upper limit B(D0 → π0 ν νbar) < 2.1×10⁻⁴.
