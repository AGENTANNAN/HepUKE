Study e+e− → ηψ(2S) with η→γγ, ψ(2S)→π+π−J/ψ, J/ψ→l+l− (l=e,μ) at 18 energy points from 4.288 to 4.951 GeV, using real data and inclusive MC in the same range, and search for e+e− → ηX̃(3872), X̃(3872)→π+π−J/ψ. Generate 200k-event exclusive signal MC for each of ηψ(2S) (HELAMP) and ηX̃(3872) (PHSP), both with ψ(2S)/X̃(3872)→π+π−J/ψ, J/ψ→e+e−, η→γγ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 mm, Vr<10 mm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge 0; photons with EMC timing 0–14, >10° from tracks, 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy thresholds, at least two. PID uses the probability method with probability >0.001; tracks with p>1.0 GeV/c are treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon), with π/K separation, requiring exactly 1 π+, 1 π−, 1 l+, 1 l−. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to γγπ+π−e+e− with χ²<40 and a competing 4C fit to γγπ+π−μ+μ− without χ² cut, storing the latter χ² for ROOT-level choice of the lepton-pair hypothesis. Apply post-fit mass windows: J/ψ 3064.6–3140.8 MeV/c², η 507.1–579.1 MeV/c², ψ(2S) 3680.3–3692.5 MeV/c², with background veto M(π+π−γγ)>1 GeV/c² and, for √s>4.600 GeV, M(γγJ/ψ)>3.74 GeV/c².
