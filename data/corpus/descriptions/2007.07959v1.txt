On ψ(3770) at √s = 3.773 GeV, study e+e− → D0 D̄0 with the double-tag method to extract the strong-phase difference between D0 and D̄0 → K_S,L^0 K+ K−. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 1M-event exclusive MC for each of the two signal modes, D0 → K_S^0 K+ K− and D0 → K_L^0 K+ K−. Tag the D̄0 (charm −1) through the modes K−π+, K−π+π0, K+K−, π+π−, K_S^0π0, π+π−π0 and K_S^0π+π−. On the signal side reconstruct a K_S^0 K+ K− final state from one K+, one K− and one π+π− pair (K_S^0 → π+π−) with net charge 0; for the K_L^0 mode require one K+ and one K− with charge 0 and treat the K_L^0 as a missing massive particle (mass fixed to the nominal K_L^0, momentum from the EMC shower). Apply a four-momentum-constrained kinematic fit with χ² cut 200. The TagAnalysis chain is applied to data, inclusive MC, and the corresponding exclusive MC for each signal mode. Note that the full tag-mode list, the K_L^0 EMC-shower reconstruction, the Dalitz-plot binning (N = 2, 3, 4 equal Δδ_D bins), DCS corrections and the unbinned maximum-likelihood extraction of c_i and s_i are performed at ROOT level rather than in this selection code.
