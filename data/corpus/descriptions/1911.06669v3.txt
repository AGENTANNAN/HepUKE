On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), measure J/ψ → Ξ(1530)⁻ Ξ̄⁺ using single-tag (ST) and double-tag (DT) methods, with Ξ̄⁺ → Λ̄ π⁺, Λ̄ → p̄ π⁺, and for DT the radiative decay Ξ(1530)⁻ → γ Ξ⁻, Ξ⁻ → Λ π⁻, Λ → p π⁻. Use the 1.31×10⁹ J/ψ real data and inclusive MC; generate 300k-event exclusive MC for the ST mode (Ξ̄⁺ tag, missing Ξ(1530)⁻) and 300k-event exclusive MC for the DT radiative mode. For ST, select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two π⁺ and one p̄; identify by probability method (prob>0.001) requiring ≥1 p̄ and ≥2 π⁺, then remove kaons and protons and assign remaining tracks as pions; reconstruct Λ̄ → p̄ π⁺ via secondary vertex fit (±5 MeV of Λ), form Ξ̄⁺ by combining with the remaining π⁺ (±8 MeV of Ξ⁺), and require the recoil mass against Ξ̄⁺ in the Ξ(1530) region 1.44–1.65 GeV, with decay lengths >0 cm. For DT, require charged tracks with the same |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr cuts, ≥3 positive and ≥3 negative tracks; photons with EMC timing 0–700 ns, barrel energy >25 MeV, endcap >50 MeV, angle to nearest track >10°, and ≥1 photon; PID as above but requiring ≥1 p, ≥1 p̄, ≥2 π⁺, ≥2 π⁻, remove kaons, and assign remaining tracks as pions; reconstruct Λ → p π⁻ and Λ̄ → p̄ π⁺ with ±5 MeV mass windows, then perform a 4C fit to γ + Λπ⁻ + Λ̄π⁺ with χ²<5 (optimized by S/√(S+B)), selecting the lowest χ², with Ξ mass windows ±8 MeV and decay lengths >0 cm, and excluding photons in the gap 0.80<|cosθ|<0.86. A 3.08 GeV continuum sample is used to verify negligible QED background.
