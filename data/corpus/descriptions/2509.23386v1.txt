On ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, search for χ_cJ → e+e− φ via ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ (J=0,1,2), with φ → K+K−. Use the 709_3686 real data and inclusive MC; the signal decay card includes all three χ_cJ states with equal weight. Event selection: exactly four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; PID by the probability method, with high-momentum leptons identified as electrons and kaons identified, requiring one e+, one e−, one K+, one K−. At least one photon with energy between 25 MeV and 1.4 GeV, angle to any track >20°, and EMC TDC in [0,14]; electron candidates must satisfy 0.8 < E/p < 1.1. Apply a gamma-conversion veto (reject e+e− pairs with Rxy < 2 cm) and a vertex fit χ² < 100 in ROOT. Perform a 4C kinematic fit (e+e−K+K−γ) with four-momentum constraint, χ² cut 200 (nominal); the paper's tight cut of 40 is applied later in ROOT.
