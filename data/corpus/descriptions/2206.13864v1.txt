At the ψ(3770) (√s = 3.773 GeV, 2.93 fb⁻¹, dataset 712_3773), perform a double-tag measurement of Cabibbo-suppressed hadronic D⁰ and D⁺ decays into multiple pions using the real data and the corresponding inclusive MC. The tag side uses hadronic tags: for a D⁰bar tag, K⁻π⁺, K⁻π⁺π⁰, K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻, K_S⁰π⁺π⁻, K_S⁰π⁺π⁻π⁰, K_S⁰π⁺π⁻π⁺π⁻, and K⁺K⁻π⁺; for a D⁻ tag, K⁻π⁺π⁻, K_S⁰π⁻, K_S⁰π⁺π⁻π⁻, and K⁺K⁻π⁻, with the tag ΔE window (−55,40) MeV when a π⁰ is present and (−25,25) MeV otherwise, keeping the best |ΔE_tag| per tag mode. On the recoil side reconstruct 9 D⁰ signal modes (π⁺π⁻π⁰, π⁺π⁻2π⁰, π⁺π⁻2η, 4π⁰, 3π⁰η, 2π⁺2π⁻π⁰, 2π⁺2π⁻η, π⁺π⁻3π⁰, 2π⁺2π⁻2π⁰) and 11 D⁺ signal modes (2π⁺π⁻, π⁺2π⁰, 2π⁺π⁻π⁰, π⁺3π⁰, 3π⁺2π⁻, 2π⁺π⁻2π⁰, 2π⁺π⁻π⁰η, π⁺4π⁰, π⁺3π⁰η, 3π⁺2π⁻π⁰, 2π⁺π⁻3π⁰); the representative D⁰→π⁺π⁻π⁰ selection requires one π⁺ and one π⁻ plus at least two photons with minimum angle 10° and minimum energy 25 MeV, while the representative D⁺→2π⁺π⁻ selection requires two π⁺ and one π⁻, net charge +1, and no required photons (the same 10°/25 MeV photon criteria are set). K_S⁰ backgrounds are vetoed by excluding M(π⁺π⁻) in [0.468,0.528] GeV/c² and M(π⁰π⁰) in [0.428,0.548] GeV/c², with the D⁺ modes using the 4σ K_S⁰ mass windows. No kinematic fit is applied on the signal side; signal D mesons are identified using ΔE_sig and M_BC_sig, with mode-dependent ΔE_sig windows, and yields are extracted from 2D unbinned maximum-likelihood fits to M_BC^tag versus M_BC^sig in ROOT, including quantum-correlation corrections for neutral D decays. Exclusive signal MC uses phase-space models for multi-body modes and a three-body BODY3 generator for three-body modes, with no per-mode event counts specified; the real data and inclusive MC are both from dataset 712_3773. The encoded selection imposes only the listed charged multiplicities, net charge, photon requirements and vetoes; no explicit |cosθ|, |Vz|, Vr or PID algorithm is specified in this selection.
