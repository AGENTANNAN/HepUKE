On the J/ψ peak (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → φ π0 f0(980) and J/ψ → φ f1(1285), with φ → K+K−, f0 → π+π− or π0π0, f1 → π0 f0(980), and J/ψ → φ η′, η′ → π+π−π0 or 3π0, reconstructed in the charged final state K+K−π+π−π0 and the neutral final state K+K−π0π0π0. Use the 2009+2012 J/ψ real data (1.311×10^9 events) and inclusive J/ψ MC (1.2×10^9 events), and generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of the six modes: φπ0f0 with f0→π+π−, φπ0f0 with f0→π0π0, φf1 with f0→π+π−, φf1 with f0→π0π0, φη′ with η′→π+π−π0, and φη′ with η′→3π0. Charged tracks are required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and net charge 0, with two positive and two negative tracks in the charged channel or one positive and one negative track in the neutral channel; photons must satisfy 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds, TDC 0–14, be at least 10° from any track, and number at least two (charged) or at least six (neutral). PID uses the probability method with probability cut 0.0 and K/π separation, requiring at least one K+ and one K−; the kaon candidates are removed from the charged-pion sample, and the remaining positive/negative tracks are assigned as π+/π−. The charged channel performs a 5C kinematic fit to K+K−π+π−γγ with four-momentum and a di-photon π0 mass constraint, χ²<100, while the neutral channel performs a 7C fit to K+K− with six photons and three π0 mass constraints, χ²<90, both flagged nominal. A K*0 veto rejects events with |M(K±π∓)−M(K*0)|<0.050 GeV/c², kaons are chosen as the oppositely charged pair with invariant mass closest to the φ, and the six photons are paired into three π0's by minimizing the π0 mass χ²; the charged channel uses the three charged-mode exclusive samples and the neutral channel uses the three neutral-mode samples.
