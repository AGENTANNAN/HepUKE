Measure R = σ(e+e−→hadrons)/σ(e+e−→μ+μ−) at 14 centre-of-mass energies from 2.2324 to 3.6710 GeV, using real data and inclusive MC at each point; no exclusive MC is generated. For inclusive hadronic events, select charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<5 cm, Vr<0.5 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks (total ≥2); photons with EMC TDC in 0–700 and energy >100 MeV in both barrel and endcap, at least one. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, identifying pions against kaons and protons. Additional topology cuts: reject e+e−→e+e−/γγ by requiring ≥2 EMC showers with |Δθ|<10° and second-most-energetic shower E>0.65 E_beam; remove deuterons with χ_p<10; charged track p<0.94 p_beam, remove if E/(pc)>0.8 and p>0.65 p_beam; remove gamma conversions (opposite charge, both E/(pc)>0.8, M_inv<0.1 GeV/c², opening angle<15°). For 2-prong events, tracks must not be back-to-back (|Δθ|<10° and ||Δφ|−180°|<15°) and N_iso_2prg>1 (isolated photon E>100 MeV, angle to track >20°); for 3-prong events, the two highest-p tracks must not be back-to-back, and fewer than 2 tracks with E/(pc)>0.8 or r_PID>0.25; events with >3 prongs are counted directly. A placeholder nominal 4C kinematic fit on π+π− with χ²<200 is included but not used in the actual counting analysis. Beam background is estimated via V_z sideband (5,10) cm versus (0,5) cm, and R is extracted from N_obs_had − N_bkg divided by σ⁰(μμ)·L_int·ε_trig·ε_had·(1+δ).
