On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → pbar Σ+ K_S0 and its charge-conjugate J/ψ → p Σ− K_S0, with Σ+ → p π0, π0 → γγ, and K_S0 → π+π−. Use the BOSS 708 J/ψ real data sample at 3.097 GeV and the corresponding inclusive MC; generate exclusive MC for the same decay chain, with no explicit event count specified. Event selection requires exactly four charged tracks with two positive and two negative charges, plus at least two photons. Identify one proton and one antiproton using the probability-based PID with a loose probability cut (value 0); no pion PID is applied to the K_S0 daughters. Reconstruct K_S0 → π+π− via a secondary-vertex fit with χ² < 100 and decay-length significance > 2.0, applying Vz < 20 cm for the daughters, and reconstruct π0 → γγ with a Kalman fit constrained to the π0 mass. Combine the proton with the π0 to form Σ+, then perform a kinematic fit to pbar Σ+ K_S0 with four-momentum conservation plus Σ+ and π0 mass constraints, χ² < 200, nominal. The K_S0 mass window and the Λ and η vetoes are not applied in this BOSS selection but are left to the ROOT stage; both charge-conjugate modes share this same selection chain, with the yield separation done later from the proton/antiproton charge.
