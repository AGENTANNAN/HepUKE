Study e+e− → Σ+Σ− produced through untagged initial-state radiation at 12 ISR scan energy points (BOSS 703) plus the 3.773 GeV point (BOSS 712), using the real data and the inclusive MC for every scan point. For the exclusive signal MC, generate 100k events per scan point with the chain ψ(4260) → Σ+Σ−, Σ+ → p π0, Σ− → p̄ π0, π0 → γγ, all in phase space. Charged-track selection requires exactly two good tracks with net charge zero and at least one positive and one negative, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm. Photon selection requires at least four photons with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy thresholds and at least 10° separation from any charged track, and proton/anti-proton PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying protons against π+ and K+. Reconstruct π0 mesons from γγ pairs with a 1C Kalman kinematic fit constraining M(γγ) to the nominal π0 mass and χ² < 25, then perform a 4C kinematic fit on the p p̄ π0 π0 system with χ² < 200, flagged nominal. At ROOT level, choose the Σ+ combination minimizing the deviation from the nominal Σ+ mass and apply the untagged-ISR requirements: missing energy U_miss ∈ [−0.14, 0.06] GeV, ISR photon polar angle < 0.25 or > 2.90 rad, and M(γγ) within [−60, 40] MeV of the π0 mass.
