On the J/ψ and ψ(3686), study J/ψ, ψ(3686) → Λ Λ̄ and Σ0 Σ̄0, with Λ → p π−, Λ̄ → p̄ π+, Σ0 → γ Λ, Σ̄0 → γ Λ̄. Use 3.097 GeV J/ψ real data (1310.6M events) and inclusive MC, and 3.686 GeV ψ(3686) real data (447.9M events) and inclusive MC; include continuum QED e+e−→B B̄ samples of 30 pb−1 at 3.08 GeV and 44 pb−1 at 3.65 GeV. Generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the four channels: J/ψ→ΛΛ̄, ψ(3686)→ΛΛ̄, J/ψ→Σ0Σ̄0, ψ(3686)→Σ0Σ̄0, each with the corresponding decay chain. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, net charge 0; photons with energy >25 MeV in the barrel and >50 MeV in the endcap, at least 10° from any charged track, and for the Σ0Σ̄0 modes at least two photons, with isolated photons at least 30° from the anti-proton and 10° from other tracks. PID by probability method with prob>0.001, identifying protons against kaons and pions, requiring at least one p and one p̄; remove the p/p̄ and treat remaining positive/negative tracks as π+/π−, then form Λ and Λ̄ by secondary-vertex fits minimizing the mass difference. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit with χ²<200 over ΛΛ̄ for the ΛΛ̄ modes and over ΛΛ̄γγ for the Σ0Σ̄0 modes. Apply mass windows M(ΛΛ̄) in [3.05,3.15] GeV (J/ψ) or [3.63,3.75] GeV (ψ(3686)) for ΛΛ̄, and [2.82,3.02] GeV (J/ψ) or [3.34,3.61] GeV (ψ(3686)) for Σ0Σ̄0; for the ΛΛ̄ modes require the Λ(Λ̄) mass within 3σ (σ=2.3 MeV for J/ψ, 4.0 MeV for ψ(3686)), for the Σ0Σ̄0 modes require the Σ0(Σ̄0) mass within 3σ (σ=4.3 MeV for J/ψ, 6.0 MeV for ψ(3686)), require the Σ0Σ̄0 opening angle >178° (J/ψ) or >178.5° (ψ(3686)), require Λ decay length >0.2 cm keeping the largest, and choose the Σ0 photon pair by minimizing Δm.
