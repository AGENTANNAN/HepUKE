On the J/ψ scan, study e+e− → K_S K+ π− with K_S → π+π−. Use the 26-point scan from 3000.00 to 3119.88 MeV (440.7 pb−1); the current implementation enumerates the five available points 3080, 3020, 3000, 2981, and 2950 MeV with matching inclusive MC, noting that the remaining points will be added. Generate 200k-event exclusive MC per scan point for e+e− → K_S K+ π− via ConExc mode 9, with K_S → π+π− and phase-space decay. Charged tracks must have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge zero; photons are selected with 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap thresholds, TDC 0–14, and an angle to the nearest track greater than 10°. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating kaons from pions/protons and pions from kaons/protons. The K_S0 → π+π− is reconstructed with a common vertex fit (χ²<100, mass-difference minimization), |M(π+π−)−m_K_S0|<15 MeV, decay length >2σ, and the two pion tracks have |Vz|<20 cm without PID; if multiple K_S0 candidates remain, the longest decay length is kept, and a γ-conversion veto requires the opening angle between the two pions to exceed 30°. A nominal 4C kinematic fit to K_S0 K+ π− is applied with χ²<200, followed by a 5C fit adding the K_S0 mass constraint, and the PWA is performed downstream in ROOT.
