At the psi(3770) energy point (sqrt(s)=3.773 GeV, 20.3 fb^-1), study the double-tag process e+e- -> D+ D-, with the single-tag D- reconstructed in the three hadronic modes K+ pi- pi-, K_S0 pi-, and K_S0 pi+ pi- pi-, and the signal D+ -> K_S0 K_L0 pi+ followed by K_S0 -> pi+ pi-. Use the real psi(3770) data and inclusive MC, together with 500k exclusive-MC events for D+ -> K_S0 K_L0 pi+ with K_S0 -> pi+ pi-. For the tag D-, require the beam-constrained mass in (1.863, 1.877) GeV/c^2 and pick the best candidate per event by minimum |Delta E|. On the signal side require exactly two pi+ and one pi- (net charge +1), treat K_L0 as missing, reconstruct K_S0 from an oppositely charged pion pair with a secondary-vertex fit following the standard BESIII selection, and veto reconstructed pi0 or eta -> gamma gamma to suppress D+ -> K_S0 pi+ eta and D+ -> K_S0 K_S0 pi+ backgrounds. Perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation and a K_S0 mass constraint on the pi+pi- pair, requiring chi^2 < 100 and interpreting the missing four-momentum as the K_L0. The K_L0 is selected by requiring missing mass squared in (0.220, 0.265) GeV^2/c^4; a minimum photon angle of 10.0 degrees is also imposed, while no explicit |cos theta|, |Vz|, Vr, photon-energy, photon-count, or PID cuts are encoded.
