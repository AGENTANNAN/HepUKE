Study J/ψ → γ η π⁰ with η → γγ and π⁰ → γγ for an amplitude analysis and branching-fraction measurement, using the 10087M J/ψ real data sample at 3.097 GeV and its inclusive MC, and generating 1,000,000 exclusive phase-space MC events for this decay chain. The event selection requires zero charged tracks (no positive, negative, or net charge) and at least five photons, with photon TDC between 0 and 14, angle to any track greater than 10°, and energy thresholds of 25 MeV in the barrel and 50 MeV in the endcap. A 6C kinematic fit is performed on the five photons, imposing four-momentum conservation plus nominal π⁰ and η mass constraints on the diphoton pairs, choosing the combination with the smallest χ², with a loose BOSS χ² cut of 200 (the paper’s tighter χ² < 25 is applied later in ROOT). Further suppression requires min(Δπ⁰²) = min[(m(γγ)−mπ⁰)² + (m(γ′γ″)−mπ⁰)²] > 0.05 GeV²/c⁴ over all four-photon combinations, vetoes |m(π⁰γ_η)−m_ω| < 65 MeV/c² and |m(γγ_η)−m_ω| < 65 MeV/c² against ω → γπ⁰, vetoes |m(γγ_π⁰)−m_η| < 39 MeV/c² against J/ψ → γηη, and selects the five-photon assignment whose two-photon masses best match the nominal π⁰ (5 MeV/c²) and η (9 MeV/c²) resolutions. The center-of-mass energy is fixed to 3.097 GeV.
