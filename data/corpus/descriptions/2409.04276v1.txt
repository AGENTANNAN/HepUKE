At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), study the semileptonic decay D0 → π− π0 e+ ν_e with the double-tag method, tagging the other D through its hadronic modes Kπ, Kππ0, Kπππ, K_S0ππ, Kππ0π0 and Kππππ0. Use the 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k exclusive MC events of ψ(3770)→DDbar with D0→π−π0e+ν_e, Dbar→K+π−, π0→γγ. On the signal side require one π−, one e+, two photons, net charge 0 and a missing neutrino; photons must have E>25 MeV (barrel, |cosθ|<0.80) or E>50 MeV (endcap, 0.86<|cosθ|<0.92) and be at least 10° from any charged track, with EMC time in [0,700] ns. Charged tracks not originating from K_S0 must satisfy |Vz|<10 cm and |Vxy|<1 cm; K_S0 candidates require a vertex-fit χ²<100, decay length >2σ and |M_{π+π−}−m_K_S0|<12 MeV. Positrons are identified by L_e/(L_e+L_π+L_K)>0.8, L_e>0.001 and E/(cp)>0.8, while dE/dx and TOF provide π/K separation (L_π>L_K for pions, L_K>L_π for kaons). Perform a kinematic fit constraining the four-momentum and the γγ mass to the nominal π0 mass, with the γγ invariant mass in [0.115, 0.150] GeV and χ²<200; a preliminary 1C fit to the nominal π0 mass must give χ²<50 before the main fit. Background vetoes include E_max_extra γ<0.25 GeV, no extra charged tracks or π0 from unused photons, M_{π−π0e+}<1.70 GeV, a K veto |M_{π−π0}−m_K|<70 MeV for the BF measurement, |U_miss|<0.03 GeV for the amplitude analysis, and rejection of π0 candidates with both photons in the EMC endcap, the best π0 being the γγ pair closest to the PDG mass.
