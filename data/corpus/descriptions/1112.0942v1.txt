Study J/ψ → γ p pbar and ψ(2S) → γ p pbar using J/ψ real data (225.2 million events) and Lund-model inclusive MC (2×10^8 events) at 3.097 GeV, plus ψ(2S) real data (1.06×10^8 events) and inclusive MC at 3.686 GeV; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of four modes: J/ψ → γ p pbar, J/ψ → π0 p pbar, ψ(2S) → γ p pbar, and ψ(2S) → π0 p pbar, the π0 p pbar modes being the dominant backgrounds. Require exactly one proton and one antiproton (net charge 0) with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, and at least one photon with >25 MeV barrel / >50 MeV endcap energy and EMC TDC between 0 and 14. Use probability-based PID with a 0.001 cut, identifying the proton and antiproton against kaon and pion; the photon must be isolated from both the proton and antiproton tracks by more than 30°. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γ p pbar, flagged nominal, with χ²<20, and additionally require |U_miss|<0.05 GeV from the two charged tracks and P_tγ²<0.0005 (GeV/c)², while vetoing any event containing a track with momentum below 0.3 GeV/c. The J/ψ and ψ(2S) channels use the same selection chain.
