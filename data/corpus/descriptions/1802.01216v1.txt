Measure e+e− → K+K−J/ψ and e+e− → KS0KS0J/ψ cross sections (and e+e− → π+π−J/ψ for the ratio) at √s = 4.189–4.600 GeV, with J/ψ → ℓ+ℓ−; use real data and inclusive MC at the 14 scan points (representative set 703_4260), plus 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the three modes. All modes require charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge 0; Mode I and III need ≥2 positive and ≥2 negative tracks, Mode II needs ≥3 of each. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, treating high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6, else muon), separating K/π against proton; Mode I requires ≥1 K+, ≥1 K−, ≥1 ℓ+, ≥1 ℓ−, Mode III requires ≥1 π+, ≥1 π−, ≥1 ℓ+, ≥1 ℓ−, and Mode II requires ≥1 ℓ+, ≥1 ℓ− after building two KS0. Two KS0 are reconstructed via secondary-vertex fits (vertex χ²<100, flight significance L/σ>4, M(π+π−) in [471,524] MeV/c²) with the mass-difference minimization and used-pion removal. Mode I and III apply a 4C kinematic fit (K+K−ℓ+ℓ− and π+π−ℓ+ℓ−, respectively), while Mode II applies a 6C fit adding the J/ψ and two KS0 mass constraints, all with χ² cut 200. A radiative-Bhabha veto rejects events with any oppositely charged pair having cos(opening angle)>0.98, and the same selection chain is applied identically at every energy point.
