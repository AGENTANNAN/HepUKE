At ψ(3770), measure D+ → η μ+ ν_μ with η → γγ via the double-tag method, using 2.93 fb−1 of real ψ(3770) data, inclusive MC, and a 1M-event exclusive ψ(3770) → D+D− phase-space sample. Reconstruct the tag D− in six hadronic modes—Kππ, K_Sπ, Kπππ0, K_Sππ0, K_Sπππ, and KKπ—selecting single-tag candidates with ΔE in (−0.025, 0.025) GeV for non-π0 tags and (−0.055, 0.045) GeV for tags containing π0, and M_BC in (1.863, 1.877) GeV/c², keeping the best candidate per mode and charge by smallest |ΔE|. On the signal side require two photons for η → γγ, one μ+, and missing ν_μ, with muon identification using fixed v1 thresholds CL_μ > 0.001, CL_μ > CL_e, and CL_μ > CL_K, and with the muon EMC energy in (0.105, 0.275) GeV. Suppress backgrounds by requiring maximum extra photon energy below 0.30 GeV, zero extra π0, and M(η μ+) < 1.74 GeV/c² to reject D+ → η π+ with pion–muon misidentification. Perform a 4C kinematic fit with the two photons constrained to the nominal η mass, with χ² < 200. Extract the signal from an unbinned fit to U_miss = E_miss − |p_miss|, using the tag-side direction to constrain the D+ momentum, and measure partial decay rates in five q² intervals with an efficiency-matrix correction.
