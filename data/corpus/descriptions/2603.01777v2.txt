Search for ψ(2S) → D_s^- π^+ and ψ(2S) → D_s^- ρ^+, with D_s^- → φ e^- \barν_e and φ → K^+K^-, and for the second mode ρ^+ → π^+π^0, π^0 → γγ. Use the 3.686 GeV ψ(2S) real-data sample and inclusive ψ(2S) MC, plus 100k-event exclusive MC generated for each of the two decay modes with the corresponding decay cards. Charged tracks must satisfy |cosθ|<0.93, Vr<1 cm, Vz<10 cm, exactly four tracks with net charge zero and at least one positive and one negative track; photons are accepted with EMC TDC 0–14 (700 ns), angle to charged tracks >10°, barrel/endcap energy thresholds of 25/50 MeV, and at least two photons are required in the ρ mode. Particle ID uses the probability method with zero probability cut, high-momentum tracks above 1.0 GeV/c treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, otherwise muon), K/π and π/(K,e) separation, requiring at least one K^+, one K^−, one π^+, and one negative lepton; electron candidates must satisfy 0.86 < E/p < 1.03 and L(e)>0.001 with L(e)/(L(e)+L(π)+L(K))>0.8. In the D_s^- π^+ mode, φ candidates are selected with M(K^+K^−) in (1.005,1.035) GeV, the antineutrino is treated as missing, and D_s^- is reconstructed by recoil against the π^+, requiring M_recoil in (1.91,2.02) GeV, |p_miss|>0.02 GeV/c, and |U_miss|<0.064 GeV. In the D_s^- ρ^+ mode, ρ^+ candidates are selected with M(π^+π^0) in (0.61,0.93) GeV, a kinematic fit constrains the two photons to the nominal π^0 mass with χ²<200 and at least one π^0, and after treating the antineutrino as missing the D_s^- recoil mass is required in (1.89,2.08) GeV with |p_miss|>0.02 GeV/c, |U_miss|<0.10 GeV, and extra photon energy in the rest frame <0.12 GeV. Both modes share the same charged-track, photon, and PID baseline selection.
