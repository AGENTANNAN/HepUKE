At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), perform a tag-based double-tag analysis of rare D → h(h') e⁺e⁻ decays using the 712_3773 real data and inclusive MC, with 200k-event exclusive MC generated for each of eleven signal modes: D⁺ → π⁺π⁰e⁺e⁻, K⁺π⁰e⁺e⁻, K_S⁰π⁺e⁺e⁻, K_S⁰K⁺e⁺e⁻; and D⁰ → K⁻K⁺e⁺e⁻, π⁺π⁻e⁺e⁻, K⁻π⁺e⁺e⁻, π⁰e⁺e⁻, ηe⁺e⁻, ωe⁺e⁻, K_S⁰e⁺e⁻. Tag one D in hadronic modes: for the D⁺ tags, D → K⁻π⁺π⁺, K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁰, K_S⁰π⁺, K_S⁰π⁺π⁰, K_S⁰π⁺π⁺π⁻, K⁻K⁺π⁺ with ΔE ∈ (−0.060, 0.034) GeV; for the D⁰ tags, D⁰ → K⁻π⁺, K⁻π⁺π⁰, K⁻π⁺π⁺π⁻ with ΔE ∈ (−0.064, 0.035) GeV, requiring opposite charm. On the signal side require the charged final-state particles: D⁺ modes π⁺e⁺e⁻ plus 2γ (π⁰), K⁺e⁺e⁻ plus 2γ (π⁰), or (K_S⁰)π⁺e⁺e⁻ (K_S⁰π⁺, K_S⁰K⁺) with K_S⁰ reconstructed via L/σ_L > 2; D⁰ modes K⁻K⁺e⁺e⁻, π⁺π⁻e⁺e⁻, K⁻π⁺e⁺e⁻, e⁺e⁻ plus 2γ (π⁰, η), π⁺π⁻e⁺e⁻ plus 2γ (ω), or (K_S⁰)e⁺e⁻. Apply a φ veto rejecting M(e⁺e⁻) in (0.935, 1.053) GeV/c² and an e⁺e⁻ vertex R_xy outside (2.0, 8.0) cm to suppress γ-conversion; for ω require M(π⁺π⁻π⁰) ∈ (0.720, 0.840) GeV/c²; electron PID uses fixed v1 thresholds, with E/pc > 0.8 and combined e-PID criteria not implemented. Perform a kinematic fit with four-momentum conservation, additionally constraining γγ to the nominal π⁰ (π⁰ and ω modes) or η (η mode) mass, and require χ² < 200.
