Using ψ(3686) data at √s = 3.686 GeV, comprising 2.7124×10⁹ events collected in 2009, 2012 and 2021, together with inclusive MC, study the radiative decay ψ(3686) → γ χcJ (χc0, χc1, χc2) in two chains: χcJ → 2K+2K−ω (mode A) and χcJ → φK+K−ω (mode B), with φ → K+K−, ω → π+π−π0, and π0 → γγ. Generate 500k-event exclusive MC samples for each mode, both related to the ψ(3686) data. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm, Vr < 1 cm, exactly three positive and three negative tracks, and net charge zero; photons must have EMC timing 0–700 ns, be at least 10° from any charged track, exceed 25 MeV in the barrel and 50 MeV in the endcap, and number at least three. Kaon/pion identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, requiring at least two K+, two K−, one π+, and one π−. A 1C mass-constrained fit to γγ selects at least one π0 with χ² < 25, followed by a 5C kinematic fit to γK+K+K−K−π+π−π0 (four-momentum plus π0 mass constraint) with χ² < 40, flagged nominal. Both decay modes share this same selection chain.
