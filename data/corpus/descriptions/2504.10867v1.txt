At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV, 7.9 fb⁻¹) we measure the D0 → K*(892)− μ+ νμ form factors via the D-tag single-tag method, with the signal reconstructed as D0 → K0 π− μ+ νμ, K0 → K_S0 → π+ π−, and the tag-side anti-D0 built from five hadronic ST modes: K+π−, K+π−π−π+, K+π−π0, K_S0 π+π−, and K+π−π−π+π0 (the paper's sixth ST entry shares the same final state as the fifth and cannot be separated). Use the 3.773 GeV real data and matching inclusive MC, and generate 2M exclusive signal events of ψ(3770) → D0 anti-D0 with D0 → K0 π− μ+ νμ and anti-D0 → K+π−. On the signal side require exactly one π+, two π−, and one μ+, net charge zero, with one massless νμ missing, while the tag side takes charm −1. Apply a 4C kinematic fit constraining the tag, signal, and neutrino four-momenta to the lab-frame c.m. energy, with χ² < 200. After BOSS, veto D0 → K0 π+π−(π0) background with M(K0 π− μ+(π0)) < 1.60 GeV/c², suppress extra π0 with E_γ^max < 0.15 GeV, and cut on the U_miss variable.
