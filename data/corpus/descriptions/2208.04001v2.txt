Measure the branching fraction of the singly Cabibbo-suppressed decay Λc+ → Λ K+ via the single-tag technique, with Λc+ → Λ π+ as the reference mode and Λ → p π− in both, produced through e+e− → Λc+ anti-Λc− (anti-Λc− inclusive). Use the 13 c.m. energy points from 4.599 to 4.950 GeV (6.44 fb−1) of real data plus inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC samples for each of the two modes (Λc+ → Λ K+ and Λc+ → Λ π+). Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm; PID by the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying protons against kaons and pions, pions against kaons, and kaons against pions. Reconstruct Λ from p π− with a secondary-vertex fit and a mass window of 1.111–1.121 GeV, then identify the Λc+ via the beam-constrained mass M_BC and ΔE, requiring −0.009 < ΔE < 0.012 GeV, keeping the minimum-|ΔE| candidate among multiples, and applying E/p < 0.9 to the kaon to suppress Λc+ → Λ e+ νe background (the reference mode uses the same selection, with the bachelor pion instead). Extract the signal through a simultaneous unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to the M_BC distributions of both modes across all 13 energies, using an MC signal shape convolved with a Gaussian and an ARGUS background.
