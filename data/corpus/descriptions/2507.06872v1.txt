On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), search for the lepton-number-violating decay J/ψ → K+ K+ e− e− and its charge conjugate. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k exclusive phase-space MC events for J/ψ → K+ K+ e− e−. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly four tracks, and net charge 0; no explicit photon energy threshold or photon-count requirement is imposed, but e+e− pairs from γ conversions are vetoed. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, high-momentum tracks (p>1.0) treated as leptons (electron if EMC energy >0.8, with electron CL_e>0.001, CL_e/(CL_e+CL_K+CL_π)>0.8, and 0.8<E/p<1.2; kaons require CL_K>0, CL_K>CL_π, and E/p<0.8 as an electron veto), and π/K separation, requiring 2 K+, 0 K−, 0 l+, and 2 l−. Perform a nominal 4C kinematic fit to K+ K+ e− e− with χ²<20, together with competing 4C fits to K+ K− π+ π−, K+ K− K+ K−, and π+ π− π+ π−, keeping events where the signal χ² is the smallest among the four hypotheses. The signal region is M(K+ K+ e− e−) in [3.07, 3.12] GeV/c² (3σ J/ψ window), and the charge-conjugate mode is treated analogously with counters swapped.
