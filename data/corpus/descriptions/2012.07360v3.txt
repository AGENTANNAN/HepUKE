On 19 R-scan energy points from 2.000 to 3.080 GeV, measure the Born cross section for e+e− → η′π+π− via the ConExc generator, with η′ reconstructed in two decay modes. Use the real data at each point together with the matching inclusive MC, and generate 200k-event exclusive signal MC across all 19 points with the decay chain vpho → η′π+π−, η′ → ηπ+π− (Mode I) and η′ → γπ+π− (Mode II), η → γγ. Both modes require at least two positive and two negative tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, and photons with E>25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap), TDC 0–14, and a 10° angle to the nearest track. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying pions against kaons and protons: Mode I needs ≥4 photons and first reconstructs η from γγ with a mass-constrained fit (χ²<25, ≥1 η), then applies the nominal 4C fit to π+π−π+π−η with χ²<100; Mode II needs ≥3 photons and applies the nominal 4C fit to π+π−π+π−γγγ with χ²<50. In each mode, two competing non-nominal 4C hypotheses, 2(π+π−) and 2(γπ+π−), are fitted and used as vetoes by comparing their χ² to the nominal one. Additional η′ and η mass windows, the M(π+π−)>0.5 GeV (Mode I) and photon-energy >0.1 GeV (Mode II) cuts, plus ISR and vacuum-polarization corrections, are applied downstream in ROOT.
