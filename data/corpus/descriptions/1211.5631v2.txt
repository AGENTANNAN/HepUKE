At the ψ(3686), study ψ′ → pbar K+ Σ0 with Σ0 → γ Λ and Λ → p π−, and ψ′ → γ χ_cJ with χ_cJ → pbar K+ Λ (Λ → p π−, J = 0, 1, 2). Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC; generate 200k-event exclusive MC for each of ψ′ → pbar K+ Σ0, ψ′ → γ χ_c0 pbar K+ Λ, ψ′ → γ χ_c1 pbar K+ Λ, and ψ′ → γ χ_c2 pbar K+ Λ. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 30 cm, |Vr| < 15 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks; at least one photon with 25 MeV (barrel) / 50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold and EMC TDC in [0, 14]; PID by the probability method with probability cut 0.001, identifying proton against K/π, K against π/proton, and π against K/proton, requiring exactly one p, one pbar, one K+, and one π−. Reconstruct Λ from p π− via a secondary-vertex fit that minimizes the mass difference and removes used tracks, then perform a 4C kinematic fit on γ pbar K+ Λ, constraining pbar and K+ to a common vertex, with χ² < 200, flagged nominal. For the Σ0 mode require |M(p π−) − M_Λ| < 7 MeV/c² and |M(γ Λ) − M_Σ0| < 15 MeV/c², and tighten the pbar/K+ vertex-fit tracks to |Vz| < 10 cm and Vr < 1 cm; for the χ_cJ mode veto |M(γ Λ) − M_Σ0| < 15 MeV/c², apply the same Λ mass window and the same tightened pbar/K+ vertex requirement, with all three χ_cJ decays sharing this selection.
