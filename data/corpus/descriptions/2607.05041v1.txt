On the ψ(3686) (3.686 GeV), study ψ(3686) → γ χ_cJ with χ_cJ → p K⁻ anti-Λ η, anti-Λ → anti-p π⁺, and η → γγ for J=0,1,2. Use the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each of the χ_c0, χ_c1, and χ_c2 modes. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and two negative tracks, and net charge 0; photons with EMC time 0–14 (≤700 ns), at least 10° from any charged track, >25 MeV in the barrel / >50 MeV in the endcap, and at least three candidates. PID by the probability method (prob>0.001), identifying protons against kaons/pions and kaons against protons/pions, requiring ≥1 proton and ≥1 K⁻, then removing the proton and kaon candidates from the charged list used for anti-Λ reconstruction. Require an isolated photon at least 20° from the primary anti-proton track with ≥3 photons, assign the remaining positive track as π⁺ and negative as anti-p, and perform a secondary-vertex fit for anti-Λ by minimizing the mass difference, removing used tracks. Then apply a nominal 4C kinematic fit to p K⁻ anti-Λ γγγ with a loose χ²<200, with the tighter χ²_4C<35 applied in the final ROOT analysis; downstream requirements include anti-Λ vertex χ²<200, decay-length significance L/σ_L>2, |M(anti-p π⁺)−m_Λ|<0.015 GeV, η mass window 0.525–0.570 GeV, π⁰ and J/ψ recoil-mass and Σ⁰ vetoes, and restricting the η to photon pairs γ₁γ₂ or γ₁γ₃ after energy ordering. All three χ_cJ modes share the same selection chain.
