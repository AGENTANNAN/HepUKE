On the ψ(3770) at 3.773 GeV, study the semileptonic decays D+ → η e+ν_e with η → γγ or η → π+π−π0, and D+ → η' e+ν_e with η' → π+π−η, η → γγ, using a double-tag technique where a hadronic D− tag is fully reconstructed against the D+ signal (η/η' + e+ + missing ν_e). Use 2.93 fb−1 of real data and inclusive MC at 3.773 GeV, and generate 200k-event exclusive MC samples for each of the three decay chains. The D− tag uses six modes: K+π−π−, K+π−π−π0, K_S0π−, K_S0π−π0, K_S0π+π−π−, K+K−π−, with charged tracks required to have |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and pion/kaon likelihood PID (L(π)>L(K) for π, L(K)>L(π) for K); photons must have E>25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap) and EMC time<700 ns, π0→γγ mass in (0.115,0.150) GeV/c² with a 1C mass constraint, K_S0→π+π− vertex fit χ²<100 and mass in (0.487,0.511) GeV/c², and tag ΔE within ±3σ (mode-dependent) with M_BC in [1.86,1.88] GeV/c². On the signal side, η→γγ requires two photons and one e+ (charge +1), η→π+π−π0 requires two photons, one π+, one π−, and one e+ (charge +1), and η'→π+π−η(→γγ) requires two photons, two π+, one π−, and one e+ (charge +1), all with missing ν_e and photon energy >25 MeV. The positron must satisfy L(e)>0 and L(e)/(L(e)+L(π)+L(K))>0.8, with E_EMC/p>0.8 for the η channels and >0.6 for the η' channel, p>0.2 GeV/c for the η' channel, and no extra unused EMC shower above 250 MeV. Signal η/η' mass windows are applied: η→γγ (0.50,0.58) GeV/c² with a 1C fit to the nominal η mass and χ²<20 (best χ² candidate), η→π+π−π0 (0.52,0.58) GeV/c² (candidate closest to nominal η), η'→π+π−η_2γ (0.935,0.980) GeV/c², η'→π+π−η_3π (0.930,0.980) GeV/c², and η'→γρ0 with ρ0→π+π− (0.55,0.90) GeV/c² including radiative photon E>0.1 GeV, |cosθ_{π,ρ}|<0.85, angle to e+ >0.20 rad, and angle to tag tracks >0.52 rad. A kinematic fit constrains the γγ invariant mass to the nominal η or π0 mass, constrains the four-momentum, and requires χ²<200.
