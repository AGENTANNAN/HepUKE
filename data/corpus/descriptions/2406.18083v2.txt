At BOSS 706, study threshold e+e− → Λc+ anti-Λc− in a double-tag analysis for three Λc+ signal modes: Λc+ → p K_L0, p K_L0 π+π−, and p K_L0 π0, with K_L0 missing. Use the six 4.600–4.699 GeV real-data points 4.61186, 4.62800, 4.64091, 4.66124, 4.68192, and 4.69882 GeV, the corresponding inclusive MC, and a 200k-event exclusive MC of e+e− → Λc+ anti-Λc− with Λc+ → p K_L0 and anti-Λc− → anti-p K_S0, K_S0 → π+π−, shared by the three modes. Tag the anti-Λc− through twelve Cabibbo-favored modes: pK_S0, pK−π+, pK_S0π0, pK_S0π+π−, pK−π+π0, pπ+π−, Λπ+, Λπ+π0, Λπ+π−π+, Σ0π+, Σ+π0, and Σ+π+π−; on the signal side require a proton and missing K_L0 with net charge +1, adding either π+π− or two photons with opening angle >10° for the other modes. The declared fit constrains four-momentum and the tag Λc mass (plus the γγ mass to the π0 mass in the π0 mode) with χ²<200, while optimized χ² cuts are <60, <25, and <20 for pK_L0, pK_L0π+π−, and pK_L0π0; the signal Λc mass constraint (and the π0 1C fit) is applied in the later ROOT stage. Veto Λ→pπ− (1.11–1.12 GeV/c²), K_S0→π+π− (0.48–0.52 GeV/c²), Σ+→pπ0 (1.17–1.20 GeV/c²), and use recoil-mass cuts M_recoil(p)>1.0 GeV/c² for pK_L0π+π− and >0.65 GeV/c² for pK_L0π0; for π0 use 0.115–0.150 GeV/c² and photon isolation >20° from the proton. Charged-track quality and PID are delegated to the specified analysis headers, while the selection above fixes the particle content, missing K_L0, photon count/angle, and fit constraints.
