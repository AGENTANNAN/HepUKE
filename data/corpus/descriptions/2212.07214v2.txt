At √s = 4.60–4.70 GeV, search for Λc+ → Σ+ γ, Σ+ → p π0, π0 → γγ, with the oppositely charged anti-Λc− reconstructed in single-tag hadronic modes. Use the seven energy points 4.59953, 4.61184, 4.62800, 4.64067, 4.66122, 4.68184, and 4.69857 GeV (real data and inclusive MC); generate 500k-event exclusive MC at each point for e+e− → Λc+ anti-Λc− with anti-Λc− → anti-p K+ π− and Λc+ → Σ+ γ, Σ+ → p π0, π0 → γγ. Only three tag modes are used: anti-Λc− → anti-p K+ π−, anti-p KS0, and anti-Λ π− (the other seven paper modes are unavailable). Tag-side selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vxy|<1 cm and |Vz|<10 cm (except KS0/Λ daughters), PID via dE/dx+TOF probability, KS0 → π+π− with |Vz|<20 cm, secondary-vertex χ²<100, decay length >2σ, and M(ππ) ∈ (0.487,0.511) GeV/c², Λ → anti-p π+ with M(pbar π+) ∈ (1.111,1.121) GeV/c², and tag candidates in M_BC ∈ (2.275,2.310) GeV/c², keeping the minimum |ΔE| candidate. On the signal side, require exactly one additional proton with |Vz|<20 cm, one radiative photon with E>0.65 GeV, and a π0 from two photons with angle >10°, 25 MeV < E < 0.45 GeV and M(γγ) ∈ (0.115,0.150) GeV/c², forming a Σ+ candidate with M(p π0) ∈ (1.176,1.200) GeV/c² and ΔE_sig ∈ (−0.038,0.026) GeV, keeping the minimum |ΔE_sig| candidate. A 4C kinematic fit enforcing four-momentum conservation is applied with χ²<200.
