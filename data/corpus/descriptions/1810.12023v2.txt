On the ψ(3686) at 3.686 GeV, study ψ(3686) → π0 h_c with h_c → p pbar π+π−, π+π−π0, 2(π+π−)π0, 3(π+π−)π0, or K+K−π+π−, using the 3.686 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generating 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the five modes with the tag π0 taken as the lower-energy π0 from ψ(3686)→π0 h_c. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, net charge 0, and positive/negative track multiplicities of 2/2 (p pbar π+π−, 2(π+π−)π0, K+K−π+π−), 1/1 (π+π−π0), or 3/3 (3(π+π−)π0); photons must have EMC timing 0–14, energy above 25 MeV (barrel) or 50 MeV (endcap), be at least 10° from any track, and satisfy nγ ≥2 for the p pbar π+π− and K+K−π+π− modes or nγ ≥4 for the π+π−π0, 2(π+π−)π0, and 3(π+π−)π0 modes. For p pbar π+π−, identify protons/antiprotons against π/K by the probability method with prob>0.001 and require at least one p and one pbar; for K+K−π+π−, identify K± against π/p similarly and require at least one K+ and one K−; the remaining charged tracks are assigned as π±, while the pure-pion modes use no PID. Reconstruct π0 candidates from photon pairs by minimizing the γγ mass difference, trying all combinations when extra photons are present, and for modes with two π0’s fit separate tag and h_c π0’s. Then perform a 4C kinematic fit to the π0(’s) plus the charged final state, with χ²<200, and impose the tag-π0 recoil-mass signal window |RM(π0)−3.525|<8 MeV/c² plus a competing-hypothesis veto against ψ(3686)→γχ_c2 and post-fit J/ψ, η, and ω mass vetoes (e.g. |RM(π+π−)−M_J/ψ|>18–74 MeV/c², |M(π+π−π0)−M_η|>14–16 MeV/c², |M(π+π−π0)−M_ω|>6–32 MeV/c² depending on mode).
