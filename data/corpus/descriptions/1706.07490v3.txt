On the six BESIII energy points from √s = 4.008 to 4.600 GeV (4008, 4226, 4258, 4358, 4416 and 4600), we measure e+e− → φφω and e+e− → φφφ using the corresponding real data and inclusive MC; both signals are generated flat in phase space with the standard KKMC ISR and ψ(4260) top-mother convention, 500k exclusive events per mode per energy point, together with 100k-event samples per point for the peaking backgrounds e+e− → K+K−φφ, K+K−K+K−φ, K+K−φω and K+K−K+K−ω. We reconstruct two φ mesons in φ → K+K− and tag the third particle (the ω, or the third φ) only through its recoil mass against the φφ system, so no kinematic fit is applied and this is a partial reconstruction. Event selection requires four charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 10 cm and Vr < 1 cm, two positive and two negative, net charge zero; kaon identification uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, separating K from π/p and requiring exactly 2 K+ and 2 K−. The two φ candidates are formed from oppositely charged kaon pairs with at least two track-disjoint φ's per event (the 1.01 < M(K+K−) < 1.03 GeV/c² window is applied at the ROOT level), and when more combinations exist only the φφ pairing minimising ΔM = √((M_φ1 − M_φ)² + (M_φ2 − M_φ)²), with the PDG φ mass, is retained and the two φ's are randomly labelled. No recoil-mass window is imposed, since RM(φφ) is the fit observable.
