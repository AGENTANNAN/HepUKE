At ψ(3770) (3.773 GeV), measure the CP-even fractions F_+ of D0→π+π−π0 and D0→K+K−π0 with the double-tag method, tagging the recoil anti-D0 in about twelve modes (e.g. K+K−, π+π−, K_S ππ, Kπ, K_S π0, K_S π0π0, K_S ω, K_S η, K_S η′, K_L π0, K_L π0π0, K_L ω, plus CP-mixed modes). Use 3.773 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate exclusive MC for the two signal modes: 200k events for D0→π+π−π0 and 100k events for D0→K+K−π0, both through ψ(3770)→D0 anti-D0. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, at least one positive and one negative track, net charge 0; photons with TDC 0–700 ns, angle to any track >10°, E>25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap), at least two. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, separating π from K: the π+π−π0 mode requires ≥1 π+ and ≥1 π−, the K+K−π0 mode requires ≥1 K+ and ≥1 K−. Then a γγ kinematic fit constrains the pair to the π0 nominal mass with χ²<25, keeping at least one π0 with γγ mass in 0.115–0.150 GeV (both photons in the barrel EMC), followed by a 4C kinematic fit to π+π−π0 (or K+K−π0) with nominal four-momentum constraint and χ²<200. For the π+π−π0 mode, veto K_S0 by rejecting any π+π− pair with invariant mass 0.481–0.514 GeV; tag-side D0 candidates use MBC and ΔE (±3σ) selection, with MBC 1.86–1.87 GeV for K_L0 tags, and the double-tag yield is extracted from a 2D MBC(tag)–MBC(sig) fit.
