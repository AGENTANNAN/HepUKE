Measure the e+e− → K+K− Born cross section at 22 R-scan energy points spanning √s = 2.00–3.08 GeV (BOSS 713). Use the real data and inclusive MC at all 22 points, and generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC (ConExc mode 45, vpho → K+K− via PHSP) at each energy point. Event selection requires exactly one K+ and one K−, net charge 0, charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm. Kaons are identified by the probability method with a 0.001 cut against pion and proton hypotheses, with no lepton requirement. A 4-momentum-constrained kinematic fit on the K+K− pair is performed with the nominal flag and a χ² cut of 200. Additional optimized cuts are applied: energy-dependent E/p<0.7–0.8, cosθ<0.8 for the positive track and cosθ>−0.8 for the negative track to suppress e+e− background, track opening angle >179° in the CM frame, |ΔTOF|<3 ns to reject cosmics, and a ±3σ momentum window on the negative track. The dominant (γ)μ+μ− background is subtracted using MC shape, the ISR correction factor is determined iteratively with ConExc, J/ψ interference is treated via additional J/ψ data near √s≥3.0 GeV, and |F_K|² extraction plus a Breit-Wigner fit to the ~2.24 GeV structure are done at ROOT level.
