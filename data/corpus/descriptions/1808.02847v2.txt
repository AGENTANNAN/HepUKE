Study e+e− → π+ D0 D*− between 4.05 and 4.60 GeV across 84 energy points, analysing the five high-luminosity points (4.2263, 4.2580, 4.3583, 4.4156, 4.5995 GeV) with real data and matched inclusive MC; generate 500k-event exclusive MC of the π+ D0 D*− phase-space mode at each of these points. The D0 → K−π+ is fully reconstructed, while the D*− is not reconstructed directly but inferred from the recoil mass against the D0π+ system. Event selection requires at least four charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, with particle identification by the probability method (cut 0.001) identifying K− and π+ against all other hypotheses (π±, K±, p±, e±, μ±). The D0 is formed from the K−π+ pair with invariant mass closest to the nominal m(D0), within |M(K−π+) − m(D0)| < 15 MeV/c², and the leftover oppositely-charged track is taken as the bachelor π+. Using partial reconstruction, the corrected recoil mass RM_cor(D0π+) = RM(D0π+) + M(K−π+) − m(D0) is required in the D*− region (1.85–2.15 GeV/c²), with the signal window |RM_cor − ΔM − m(D*−)| < 20 MeV/c² and sideband 1.91 < RM_cor < 1.95 GeV/c², while e+e− → D*D* background is vetoed by rejecting events with M(D0π+) < 2.03 GeV/c². No kinematic fit is applied; the signal yield is extracted from an unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to RM_cor in the ROOT stage.
