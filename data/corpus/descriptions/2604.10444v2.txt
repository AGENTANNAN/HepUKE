Using 20.3 fb⁻¹ of ψ(3770) data at 3.773 GeV (dataset 712_3773) and the corresponding inclusive MC, perform a double-tag measurement of D⁺ → π⁺π⁺π⁻η and D⁺ → π⁺π⁰π⁰η with η → γγ, tagging the D⁻ in five hadronic modes: K⁺π⁻π⁻, K⁺π⁻π⁻π⁰, K_S π⁻, K_S π⁻π⁰, and K_S π⁻π⁻π⁺. Generate 500k-event exclusive MC for each signal mode, with ψ(3770) → D⁺D⁻, the chosen D⁺ signal decay, and D⁻ → K⁺π⁻π⁻ in the decay card (π⁰/η → γγ). For the three-pion channel require 2 π⁺, 1 π⁻ (net charge +1), and two photons with angle >10° and energy >25 MeV, then fit with four-momentum conservation plus a γγ mass constraint to the η, χ² < 100. For the π⁺π⁰π⁰η channel require 1 π⁺ (charge +1), six photons with angle >10° and energy >25 MeV, and fit with four-momentum conservation, two γγ constraints to π⁰ and one γγ constraint to η, χ² < 50. Signal regions are 1.863 < M_BC < 1.877 GeV/c², with ΔE(sig) in (−0.033, 0.032) GeV for the three-pion channel and (−0.052, 0.038) GeV for the π⁺π⁰π⁰η channel, and mode-dependent ΔE(tag) windows applied later. The three-pion channel applies K_S and η′ vetoes (M(π⁺π⁻) outside 0.478–0.517 GeV/c² and M(π⁺π⁻η) outside 0.8–1.0 GeV/c²), while the π⁺π⁰π⁰η channel applies a K_S veto on π⁰π⁰ (outside 0.448–0.534 GeV/c²), η′ vetoes on π⁰η, and rejects D⁺ → π⁺π⁰π⁰π⁰ background with ΔE in (−0.1, 0.1) GeV and M_BC in 1.83–1.89 GeV/c²; best candidates are chosen by minimum |ΔE_sig| (and minimum |ΔE_tag| for the three-pion channel), with a later amplitude analysis using an additional D⁺ mass constraint at the ROOT stage.
