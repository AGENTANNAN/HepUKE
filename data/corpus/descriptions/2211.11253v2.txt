Measure normalized differential cross sections of inclusive π0 and KS0 production at six energy points: 2.2324, 2.4000, 2.8000, 3.0500, 3.4000, and 3.6710 GeV, using the closest available real-data samples and the corresponding inclusive MC; no exclusive signal MC is generated, since the signal e+e− → π0/KS0 + X is embedded in inclusive hadronic events produced with the luarlw generator. Event selection requires at least two charged tracks with |cosθ| < 0.93; photons with EMC time in [0, 700] ns, angle to the nearest track > 10°, and energy > 25 MeV (barrel) or > 50 MeV (endcap); π0 candidates from photon pairs with invariant mass constrained to the π0 mass via a Kalman fit (χ² < 200), requiring at least one π0; and KS0 candidates from π+π− secondary vertices obtained by minimizing the mass difference. No kinematic fit is applied; instead, additional offline π0 angular cuts (|cosθγ| < 0.8 for pπ0 < 0.3 GeV/c, < 0.95 for pπ0 > 0.3 GeV/c) and KS0 daughter-track cuts (|cosθ| < 0.93, |Vz| < 30 cm, |Vxy| < 10 cm, decay length > 2σ) are imposed, with no PID on the KS0 daughters. QED backgrounds are simulated with babayaga3.5, and τ+τ− at 3.671 GeV with KKMC. Signal yields are extracted by fitting the M(γγ) and M(π+π−) spectra in momentum bins.
