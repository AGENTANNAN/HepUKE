On ψ(4040) at √s = 4.009 GeV with 482 pb⁻¹, measure the semileptonic decays Ds+ → φ e+ νe, Ds+ → φ μ+ νμ, Ds+ → η μ+ νμ, and Ds+ → η' μ+ νμ using a single-tag/double-tag analysis. Use the 4.009 GeV real data and inclusive MC, plus one 500k-event exclusive MC sample generated with ψ(4040) → Ds+ Ds− (VSS). The tag side is reconstructed as Ds− → K+ K− π−. The signal side requires, mode by mode, K+ K− e+ with net charge +1 and one missing νe; K+ K− μ+ with net charge +1 and one missing νμ; two photons plus μ+ with charge +1 and a missing νμ (η → γγ); and two photons, π+π−, plus μ+ with charge +1 and a missing νμ (η' → η π+π−, η → γγ). Each mode is fitted with a 4-momentum constraint, the φ mass constrained to its nominal value for the K+K− pair or the η mass constrained to its nominal value for the γγ pair, and a χ² cut of 200. The same tag-side requirements and fit framework are applied to data, inclusive MC, and exclusive MC for all four signal modes.
