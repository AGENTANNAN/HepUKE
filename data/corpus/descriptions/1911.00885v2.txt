We measure e+e− → η' J/ψ using about 11 fb−1 of BESIII real data at the 14 energy points from √s = 4.178–4.600 GeV and the corresponding inclusive MC, and generate 100,000-event exclusive MC at each point and for each mode with ψ(4260) → η' J/ψ, Mode I η' → π+π−η, η → γγ and Mode II η' → γπ+π−, both with J/ψ → e+e−. Event selection requires exactly two positive and two negative charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm and net charge zero; photons must satisfy 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap energy thresholds, TDC 0–14, and a >20° separation from tracks, with at least two photons in Mode I and at least one in Mode II. Particle ID uses the probability method: tracks with p>1.0 GeV are treated as leptons, with EMC energy >1.0 GeV taken as an electron and <0.4 GeV as a muon, while other tracks are separated as pion versus kaon/proton, requiring one ℓ+, one ℓ−, one π+ and one π−. In Mode I the two photons are first mass-constrained to the η with a Kalman fit (χ²<25, at least one η), followed by a 5C kinematic fit to ℓ+ℓ−π+π−η with χ²<50; in Mode II a 4C fit to ℓ+ℓ−π+π−γ is performed with χ²<40. The J/ψ must fall in M(ℓ+ℓ−) ∈ [3.07, 3.13] GeV/c², with sidebands [3.00, 3.06] and [3.14, 3.20] used for background, and ψ(2S) backgrounds are vetoed by requiring M(η J/ψ) outside [3.67, 3.70] GeV/c² in Mode I or M(π+π− J/ψ) outside [3.66, 3.71] GeV/c² in Mode II. Photon conversion is further suppressed with cosθ(π+π−)<0.95, charged-track helix parameters are corrected before the fits, and when multiple combinations survive the one with the smallest χ² is chosen. Both decay modes are processed over the same real data, inclusive MC, and their respective exclusive MC samples.
