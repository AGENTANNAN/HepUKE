Study e+e− → K+ Ξ0 Σ̄− (continuum production with ψ(4260) as the KKMC mother per BESIII convention) via a partial-reconstruction technique, with Ξ0 → Λπ0, Λ → pπ−, π0 → γγ, and Σ̄− → n̄π+ treated as the missing recoil. Use 44.55 fb−1 real data at c.m. energies 3.773, 4.180, and 4.230 GeV from a 56-point scan spanning 3.51–4.95 GeV, inclusive MC at 3.773 GeV, and signal exclusive MC generated at 100k events per energy point for each of the three energy points. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93 and no impact-parameter requirement (since Λ gives a secondary vertex), requiring at least two positive and one negative tracks; photons with 25 MeV (barrel)/50 MeV (endcap) energy threshold, EMC timing 0–14, angle to the nearest track >10°, and at least two photons. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 cut, identifying protons against kaons/pions, kaons against pions/protons, and pions against kaons/protons, requiring at least one proton, one K+, and one π−. Perform a Kalman 1C kinematic fit constraining the two photons to the π0 mass with χ²<500, a secondary-vertex fit for Λ → pπ− minimizing the mass difference, and partial reconstruction of the K+Ξ0 system requiring the recoil mass between 1.10 and 1.30 GeV, choosing the Ξ0 candidate with mass closest to 1.31486 GeV. Apply mass windows |M(pπ−) − m_Λ| ≤ 5 MeV and |M(Λπ0) − m_Ξ0| < 10 MeV, and require Λ flight significance L/ΔL > 2. Cross sections are extracted with ISR and vacuum-polarization corrections.
