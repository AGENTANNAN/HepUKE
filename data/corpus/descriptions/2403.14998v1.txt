Starting from threshold up to 4.95 GeV, measure the e+e− → Ds+Ds− Born cross section with a single-tag method, tagging Ds− → K+K−π− and identifying the recoiling Ds+ via its recoil mass. Use the full 3.94–4.95 GeV real-data sample (138 energy points, ~22.9 fb−1) and the matching inclusive MC, and generate 100k-event exclusive signal MC at each scan point from ψ(4260) → Ds+Ds−, Ds → K+K−π (flat Born cross section as the initial input). Tag the Ds− through two intermediate modes: Ds− → φπ− with φ → K+K− inside 1.005 < M(K+K−) < 1.035 GeV/c², or Ds− → K*(892)0 K− with K*(892)0 → K+π− inside 0.832 < M(K+π−) < 0.928 GeV/c² and helicity angle |cosθ(K+)| < 0.52. Apply a recoil-mass window 1.945 < RM(Ds−) < 1.990 GeV/c², using the mass-constrained form M_recoil(K+K−π−) + M(K+K−π−) − m(Ds−) to sharpen the resolution, and perform a four-momentum-constrained fit with χ² < 200. Subtract the peaking background from e+e− → Ds+Ds*− (estimated from exclusive MC, normalized to luminosity and cross sections) at Ecms > 4.6 GeV, and extract the cross section via a five-iteration MC-weighting procedure that updates the ISR correction (1+δ), efficiencies, and the lineshape, using σ_Born = (N_fit − N_bkg) / (2 × B(Ds→KKπ) × ε × (1+δ) × (1/|1−Π|²) × L) with the factor 1/2 accounting for both Ds± single tags.
