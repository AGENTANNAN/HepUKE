On the J/ψ (3.097 GeV), study J/ψ → γ η_c, η_c → Λ Λ̄ (phase space), Λ → p π−, Λ̄ → p̄ π+. Use the 3.097 GeV real data and inclusive MC, and generate 100k exclusive MC events for the signal mode. Charged tracks: at least two positive and two negative, net charge 0, |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<100 cm, Vr<10 cm; photons: EMC TDC in [0,14], angle to any track >10° (20°/30° for p/π in ROOT), energy threshold 25 MeV barrel / 50 MeV endcap, at least one photon. PID by the probability method (prob>0.001) separating protons from kaons and pions, requiring at least one proton and one antiproton, with remaining tracks assigned as π+/π−. Reconstruct Λ → p π− and Λ̄ → p̄ π+ through secondary vertex fits minimizing the mass difference, then perform a 4C kinematic fit to γ Λ Λ̄ with χ²<200. Additional ROOT-level criteria: Λ mass window [1.008,1.124] GeV, |M(γΛ)−M_Σ0|>9 MeV veto, p/π separation by momentum >0.4 GeV/c, and space-like separation 1/S ≤ L1/L2 ≤ S with S=(1+β_Λ)/(1−β_Λ).
