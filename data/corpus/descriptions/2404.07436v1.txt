On the 2.125 GeV R-scan point (part of a 22-point scan from 2.000 to 3.080 GeV), study e+e− → ωη′ with ω → π+π−π0, η′ → γπ+π−, and π0 → γγ. Use the corresponding real data and inclusive MC, and generate 500k exclusive signal MC events for this ωη′ final state with the ω Dalitz decay and η′/π0 phase-space decays. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly two positive and two negative tracks and net charge zero; photons with TDC 0–14, energy >25 MeV (barrel) / >50 MeV (endcap), more than 10° from any track, and at least three photons. PID uses the probability method with a 0.001 probability cut, pions identified against kaons and protons, requiring two π+ and two π−. Reconstruct π0 from the photon pair with the smallest |M(γγ)−mπ0| in a Kalman fit constrained to the π0 mass with χ²<25, requiring at least one π0, then perform a nominal 4C fit on π+π−π+π−π0γ with χ²<60; also fit competing 4C hypotheses π+π−π+π−γ (two photons missing) and π+π−π+π−γγ (one photon missing), and require the nominal χ² to be smaller than both to veto e+e−→2(π+π−)π0 and e+e−→2(π+π−π0) backgrounds. Choose the ω candidate as the π+π−π0 combination closest to mω, retaining |M(π+π−π0)−mω|<0.029 GeV/c², and select η′ via the remaining γπ+π− with |M(γπ+π−)−mη′|<0.025 GeV/c², using 0.05<|M−mη′|<0.10 GeV/c² as sideband. Born cross sections are extracted with iterative ISR and vacuum-polarization corrections.
