At the J/ψ(3.097 GeV), study the Dalitz decays J/ψ→γη/η′ with η/η′→γe+e−, giving a γγe+e− final state. Use the 3.097 GeV real data set and its inclusive MC; generate 100k-event exclusive MC for each of the two modes, J/ψ→γη, η→γe+e− and J/ψ→γη′, η′→γe+e−. Event selection: charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm, Vr<1 cm, exactly one positive and one negative track and net charge zero; photons with TDC 0–14, barrel energy >25 MeV and endcap energy >50 MeV, angle to any track >10°, and at least two photons. Particle ID uses the probability method, treating high-momentum tracks (p>1.0 GeV) as leptons, with electron identification if EMC energy >0.6 GeV, requiring one positive and one negative lepton; the electron/pion likelihood cut is >0.5 for the η channel and >0.95 for the η′ channel. Perform a 4C kinematic fit to γγ e+e− with nominal flag and χ²<100, then apply post-fit vetoes: reject photon conversions with R_xy>2 cm when cosθ_eg>0 and |Δ_xy|<0.8 cm, veto γγ masses within ±0.03 GeV of the η(547) and η′(958), require the low-energy photon E>0.15 GeV, and require opening angles >20° between the radiative photon and e± and >10° between the low-energy photon and e±. Both decay modes share the same selection chain.
