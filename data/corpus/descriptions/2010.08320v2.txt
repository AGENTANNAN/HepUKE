Measure the Born cross section for e+e− → Ξ− anti-Ξ+ using single-baryon tagging at eight BESIII scan points: 2.644, 2.646, 2.900, 2.950, 2.981, 3.000, 3.020 and 3.080 GeV (33.7, 34.0, 105, 15.9, 16.1, 15.9, 17.3 and 126 pb−1). Use real data at each point and an exclusive MC sample of 100k events per point for the Ξ− anti-Ξ+ final state, with Ξ− → Λ π−, Λ → p π−, anti-Ξ+ → anti-Λ π+ and anti-Λ → anti-p π+. Event selection requires charged tracks with |cosθ|<0.93, |Vz|<10 cm and Vr<1 cm, at least two positive and three negative tracks; photons are selected in the EMC TDC window 0–14. PID uses the probability method with prob>0.001, separating protons from kaons/pions and pions from kaons/protons, requiring at least one proton and at least two π−, with proton candidates matched to positive charge. Reconstruct Λ → p π− and then Ξ− → Λ π− through secondary-vertex fits that minimize the mass difference and remove used particles. Apply a nominal four-momentum-conserving kinematic fit to the Ξ− and π− candidates with χ²<200, infer anti-Ξ+ from the recoil mass M_recoil = sqrt((E_cm − E_Λπ)² − |p_cm − p_Λπ|²), and extract the signal yield from an unbinned maximum-likelihood fit to the M_recoil spectrum. The single-tag method includes a ~19% double-counting correction at ROOT level, an ISR correction iterated to the 1% level, and 90% CL upper limits at the two threshold points 2.644 and 2.646 GeV.
